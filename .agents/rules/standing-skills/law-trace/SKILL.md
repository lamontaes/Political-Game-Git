# Law trace

A law is explained only when its authority, recorded changes and consequences can be followed at a named source head. Use the current assignment and claims; this skill grants no additional writer or merge authority.

## Trace

Pin main and any candidate branch separately. Inventory assigned production catalog IDs and out-of-catalog modules. Cross-link overlapping topics to their owner instead of duplicating implementation. Read the current feature-walkthrough skill for the broader hand-back and run its why-chain before reporting.

Follow enactment to canonical law-in-force records, effective dates, producers, affected people, money or services, knowledge and player consumers. Ask why each transition occurs until it reaches recorded circumstances, researched behavior, an authored stand-in, a draw or an absent producer. A catalog entry or passing fixture alone does not establish a working effect.

Enumerate every distinct effect supported by research, including secondary effects and evidence of no effect. Give each size/range, unit, denominator, delay, affected cohort and source limits. Distinguish observed inactivity, a recorded nobody-noticed outcome, a research finding of no effect and missing wiring. Do not turn missing evidence into an inert outcome. Count wired effects and inspect single-effect laws for omitted consequences; do not invent extra effects to increase the count.

Check federal, state, county, city/township, D.C. and territory authority as applicable. Research which may act and undo the law. Trace floors, ceilings, permitted ranges, full preemption and home-rule constraints through existing authority and law machinery. An unsupported level is an explicit legal limit, not automatically an implementation gap. Verify repeal and effective-date behavior without erasing historical records.

For variable nonlegal effects, trace the existing per-world size draw, evidence range, stable identity and saved continuation. Exact legal amounts and dates retain their sourced jurisdiction values. Do not replace researched variation with a universal midpoint or redraw values each turn. Keep research estimates distinct from actor decision rules and causal proof.

## The chain is the acceptance (owner, Oct 14, 2026)

A trace is finished only when it follows one dollar or one rule to a named
person and back out: the law in force changing, the payment or view that
reached that person, the choice that moved, and the director's line. Name each
step by record id. Most people are untouched; name the ones who were.

## Hand-back: six lines per law

Start with law ID/name, owner, source head and candidate head, then:

1. **Enactment:** exact file/function and canonical change, including date.
2. **Effects:** every supported reaction, people reached, sizes/ranges/units/delays, research or stand-in, and wired/total count. Identify inert evidence explicitly.
3. **Authority:** acting and repealing levels, preemption, floors/ceilings/ranges and jurisdiction gaps.
4. **Terminal:** where each why-chain ends: people and records, stand-in, draw or no producer.
5. **Gap and fix:** smallest existing-system number, wiring or record repair; separate new features for owner review.
6. **Proof:** the law's row from `scripts/law-wiring/check.ts` (or another executed run) naming whose money or outcome changed and by how much; otherwise NOT RUN with the missing check.

Finish with one status: works, fixed tonight, needs research or needs a feature. Qualify source-only versus watched proof, and candidate versus landed. Include exact changed-file checks, failures, ownership dependencies and next action. Open each fix as a ready PR against main; the mergers merge it.
