# Research before building

When work touches X, look up real examples of X across the country before
writing the rule: paychecks need real pay data and stubs, businesses need real
business records, an inflation feature needs how it actually hit real
businesses in a real place. This research sets the shape and range the
simulation should produce, and checks the totals it does produce; it never
becomes the die roll that decides one actor's outcome (see `no-dice`), and it
never appears on a player's screen as a citation or source — players see the
outcome, not the research trail.

## Check your own work

Before writing the rule, name at least one real source or example you
actually checked — a specific pay stub, a specific business record, a
specific place's rate. If you can't name one, the research isn't done yet.
After writing it, grep the player-facing strings you added for anything that
looks like a citation, dataset name, or "source:" and remove it; that
information stays in data and docs, never on screen.
