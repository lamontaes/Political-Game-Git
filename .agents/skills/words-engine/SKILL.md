## The target: scenes like Wildermyth (owner, Oct 24)

Scenes "that come out of what is actually happening in the world, narrated into a moment, played out with real dialogue and choices, the outcome back into the game; the current opening is the right shape, the words are wrong." That is the measure of every piece of this line: a record fires it, the narrator sets it, people with purposes play it out with four real replies, and what the player chose goes back into the records.

## The one rule that explains everything

Words come from the English engine; every bank part plays unless the owner's grades hold it, hand-written (`origin: authored`) parts included (owner, Oct 24; this replaces the Oct 20 "held until graded" hold, which no longer applies). Words come from the English engine's banks (`data/english/parts/`) and control names. The banks are built at development time: pieces are written to calibrated patterns, drawing on any source for the patterns (books, other games, transcripts, the internet), and copying no one's copyrighted text. At play the engine arranges the pieces; the director decides which moment is happening and who is in it. Neither types a line. Public-domain records remain a fine source of pieces, not a requirement. The old "two documents, one public-domain" attestation rule was never the owner's and is withdrawn (OWNER-DECISIONS.md, "The text rule", Oct 15, evening). Research and real data calibrate how pieces are arranged; they never decide a person's outcome.

## The three layers, and where each lives

1. **Situations (the director, `src/director/`).** `src/director/data/situation-types.json` is the catalog: 34 situation types (`departure`, `celebration`, `news-arrives`, `first-meeting`, `visit`, ...), each with roles (who fills it: `subject`, `counterpart`, `household`, `kin`, `present`, `classmates`, `coworkers`, `absent`, `authority`), the **moves** each role may make (`farewell`, `promise`, `comfort`, `tell`, `request`, `blame`, ...), what each role **wants**, a **bearing** (tender, solemn, ...), a setting, a timing, and **causes**: the record labels that fire it (a `money:job-started` record, a death, an eviction). A cause whose producer the core does not have yet names it in `awaitingProducer` and matches nothing. No type holds a sentence. The director (`src/director/scene.ts`, `scheduling.ts`, `ledger.ts`) watches the player's circle, fires a situation when a record matches a cause, stages it (home, work, town, the council room), and runs it as moves. `conversation.ts` runs talks: opens from the place and occasion, then the biggest recorded event of either person in the ledger, keeps to that topic, chains to one related topic, closes when done; four replies ranked by the player's traits, lies as the same moves made false, "say nothing" beside them (`src/director/data/conversation-rules.json`). `journal.ts` writes the first-person "My life" memoir from records. `opening-story.ts` is the opening.
2. **Moves (the grammar).** A move is one thing a person does with a line: greet, deliver news, ask, answer, condole, congratulate, deflect, lie, agree, close, farewell, comfort, request, blame. Moves are situation-blind: a condolence serves a funeral, an assassination and a lost election. The director picks the next move from the situation's arc and the speaker's traits and relationship; the engine fills it. The move names in the catalog and the `kind`/`stage` names in the banks must agree; that mapping is the seam between director and engine and is the first thing to check when a line comes out wrong.
3. **Pieces (the banks, `data/english/parts/*.json`).** One bank per register: `conversation.json`, `journal.json`, `address.json` (floor, hearing, public comment, campaign, victory, concession), `interview.json`, `minutes.json`, `legislation.json`, `notices.json`, `meeting.json`, `hearing.json`, `newscast.json`, `news.json`, `headlines.json`, `newspaper-ledes.json`, `opening-speech.json`, `counter.json`, `scene-words.json`, `bring-it-up.json`, `choice-names.json`, `winning-losing.json`. Each part is a sentence with slots, its move (`kind`, `stage`), its register, the record facts it needs (a sentence that claims something about the speaker, "I'm a nurse.", names the fact and is said only when the record holds it), and optionally a source note (document, date, address, license) or `origin: authored` with author and date. `src/english/english-composition.ts` (`composeAddress`, `composeFromBank`), `grounded-english.ts` and `speech-registers.ts` compose; `english-calibration.ts` and `move-fit.ts` weigh pieces; `english-grades.ts` applies the owner's grades (a failed part is never used; a conditional one only where its condition holds); `runtime-text-origin.ts` was meant to guard that every shipped string has a bank origin, but its audit (`tests/e2e/runtime-text-audit.spec.ts`) is not on main and its baseline lists strings from `src/player`, which no longer exists (checked Oct 26). Nothing enforces it; check by hand with `player-screen-text`.

## How a bank is made (authoring to patterns)

Full history of the existing mined banks: `docs/research/english-corpus/README.md`. The method now:

- **Authoring.** The `civic-prose` skill is the process. A piece is drafted to a move, register, key and slots; reviewed for grounding (a sentence that claims something about the speaker, "I'm a nurse.", names the record fact it needs and is said only when the record holds it) and terminology; then added to the bank with its move tags. Mark new parts `origin: authored` with the author and date. Every part plays unless the owner's grades hold it: the grade ledger is the one gate, and a part carries no flag of its own. After adding or regenerating a bank, run `node --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/apply-grades.ts`, which folds the grades and rebuilds the reach table.
- **Patterns.** Counts, shapes and wording come from any source: English books, other games, transcripts, council minutes, news, the internet (`scripts/english-corpus/shapes/<bank>.json`), copyrighted or not (owner, Oct 24: "There's plenty of stuff, even copyrighted, out there. You don't have to cite stuff."). Where wording came from never decides whether it plays, and no line names its source. No real politician's slogan.
- **Mining.** Where any record or text offers good pieces, mine them with `scripts/english-corpus/mine-shapes.py`. The `source` fields on a part are optional notes.
- **Tests.** `<bank>-bank.test.ts` composes a whole exchange through the real engine in a generated place (every record id resolving, no feeling words, both chairs where a chair exists). Add a bank by adding a shape or an authored set, a script and its test.
- **What "calibration" means.** Research sets how pieces are arranged and how often: the length of a public comment, how a concession opens and closes, which moves follow which. It never picks which line a particular person says: traits, relationship, the record and the owner's grades do.

## Grading: scenes with voice notes (owner, Oct 24; the batch tooling deleted Oct 28)

The owner does not grade batches. He plays or watches a scene or a scenario and reacts with a voice note; the session turns each note into grades on the parts the scene used and into fixes at the root. What he liked ships; what he rejected never plays again. The batch apparatus is deleted (owner, Oct 28, 18:15 UTC, `docs/coordination/OWNER-DECISIONS.md`; `docs/reports/words-director-plan-2026-10-28.md`, decision 4): no batch generators, no page builders, no table read, no files under `data/english/batches/`. Do not rebuild one.

- **One grade file per set of voice notes**, `data/english/grades/<set>.json` (`voice-notes-batches-11-19.json`, `voice-notes-oct15-28.json`): `{"batch": "<set>", "grades": [{"i", "grade", "by", "line", "note", "parts"}]}`. Every row carries the part keys of the line it grades (`parts`), so the file stands on its own. A voice note names a line by its label or number; the session writes one row per line.
- **`node --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/apply-grades.ts`** folds every grade file into `data/english/part-grades.json`, rebuilds the reach table (`data/english/grade-reach.json`: a grade moves every piece that shares its pattern, voice and register, `src/english/grade-reach.ts`) so a part graded bad or fix stops playing everywhere and the grade reaches the whole game at once. Run it after a grade file changes. `data/english/calibration.json`, which the engine reads, keeps the weights the earlier numeric grades set; nothing new feeds it.
- **The in-play grade control** (`src/bench/grade-line.ts`) writes a grade on a line the owner is looking at to `data/english/grades/play-grades.json`, naming the pieces that said it; `apply-grades.ts` folds it like any grade file.
- **A scene shown to the owner answers why by record** (`ask-why`, `player-screen-text`): no raw id in a line, no body named by a member's title, no line repeated, no relative asked after with no recorded cause, no scene staged in the wrong household. Fix the engine defect at the root and replay in a fresh drawn place.
- **Proof scenes** are welcome: a situation the director does not yet fire may be composed from real records in a played world and shown, marked as a proof, so the owner reacts to what the game can say and about what. `scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed>` plays a drawn place's year and lists every scene with the record that fired it; `npm run words:coverage` lists the zero cells of the coverage table.

## What the owner saw on Oct 26 (read before writing)

- **No newer English exists.** Batch 22 was the Oct 15 held lines laid out for grading; #5942 released them. The owner believed batch 22 was better English than what he heard; it is the same English. Better words come only from new pieces written per scene to `scene-standard` and his voice notes turned into grades. Tell him so plainly.
- **Live news is never worded.** `composeNewsStory` runs only for the pre-game archive and the opening; every `press.printed` story reaches News as a bare record, so laws, votes and results never appear in words.
- **Narration that restates the screen** ("It's a {weekday} in {month}.", "You're at home in {place}.") was rejected by the owner; scenes follow `scene-standard` rule 6.
- **The opening address reads as a stat readout** (one sentence per number); journal lines are single facts ("I voted for H.R. 2.") with no context, and the game cast that vote for the player.

- **A "say it differently" assertion needs a witness.** `expect(textA).not.toBe(textB)` for two opposite speakers or trait profiles is false in any drawn place where the facts give both the same line. Draw places in a bounded loop until the two differ, name the places passed over and throw if none does, or assert the difference over the whole bank; run it on 10 fresh draws before the PR (Oct 27: #6296 at 15:59, #6420 at 22:24 sent back for it).
- **Director code keeps no numbers, and its counts are not hand-typed.** Before opening a scene PR run `npx vitest run src/director/director.test.ts` on the head with main merged in: its "keeps numbers in data" guard rejects every numeric literal in `src/director/*.ts` (a day length, a `b[1] - a[1]` comparator, an age cutoff) and its situation-type count must name the new type. Put values in a `src/director/data/` table tagged SOURCED, ESTIMATED or TUNABLE and read them through a named accessor (Oct 27: #6237 and #6260 sent back for it, #6273 fixed red main).

## The owner's standing rulings on this line

- **Grades tune the engine; they never gate it** (owner, Oct 24: "Quit worrying about my grades ... Get the English engine in the game. That should make every single piece of dialogue available. If I find pieces I don't like or I keep grading, you can keep updating it, and that will keep tuning the engine."). Every piece in every bank plays in ordinary play, scenes included, unless a grade holds it; a grade run reaches the whole game at once. Nothing waits for the owner to grade it, and nothing asks him to approve lines one by one.
- Scenes are the narrative: consequential dialogue happens in scenes; the day screen carries dealing. Day advances time; a scene stops it. A quiet day fires nothing.
- A talk starts from the place and occasion, then the biggest recorded event of either person; one topic with side remarks, chaining to one related topic; news only as received; no cold "How is X?" about someone absent unless something recorded happened to X; the other person closes it; the listener remembers the last talk; the old subject loop (every known person in id order) is gone.
- Four replies always, ranked by the player's traits; lies are the same moves made false about the player's own record; "say nothing" is the smaller control beside them. The player's choices are the player's: no auto-hire, no auto-vote.
- The narrator is settled: second person in scenes, first person in the memoir, grounded in records; the journal frames a childhood loss by age; school and work are chapters, not entries.
- A family scene is staged in the player's household from that household's records (a lost job, an eviction, a divorce); the people in the room show in the picture and the talk, never as a roll call of names.
- Knowing is a read: a person cites a law, a vote or an act only through a record of how they learned of it (news received, a talk, a notice), and the stance cites that source.
- No feeling words unless a record carries the feeling; grief has a cause record.
- **Scene PRs collide on four shared files; land on fresh main.** Every scene edits `data/english/parts/scene-talk.json`, `src/director/data/situation-types.json`, `src/director/purpose-scene.ts` and the situation-type count in `src/director/director.test.ts`, and mergers never hand-merge a bank. Right before marking ready, `git fetch origin main`, merge it, re-add your pieces by piece key and your count bump on main's files, and push within minutes; a PR that is stacked or held waits for its base to merge, then redoes this. Oct 27: #6201, #6296, #6298 (twice), #6303 and #6309 were each sent back for this one conflict.

## The checks a Words session runs

In-session, on changed files only: typecheck, eslint, prettier, the bank's own test (`scripts/english-corpus/<bank>-bank.test.ts`), the director's tests for changed files (`src/director/*.test.ts`), `npm run spelling` (American English; `tests/guards/english/`), `npm run zero-dice`, `release:check --mode pr` with a declaration in `docs/release/changes/`. The played-world proof is a year in a drawn place with the record ids that fired each scene (`played-world-proof`; `scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed>` plays the year and lists them). One line at the top of the PR body with the result; the first lines of the PR body say what the owner can play now.
