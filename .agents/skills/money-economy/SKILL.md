# Money: the economy and the dollar

The design is `docs/design/country-tier.md` (owner, Nov 8: no hard-coded baseline; ranges are checks, not targets; Nov 10: the clock has two speeds, results first). Run scripts from the repository root and, in a cloud session, with `OCD_STORAGE_OVERRIDE="cloud session, owner approved Oct 14"` set. A builder runs only the tests it wrote or changed; a checker runs the gate. No seeded roll or fixed percentage decides what a household, firm or treasury does (`no-dice`).

Method that worked on Nov 8:

1. **Find why before building.** The national month was once one damped linear formula (`stepMacroMonth`) with no source of motion, so every world went flat; it was deleted on Nov 10. The national row is now the sum of the states, each kept as counts and stepped by the month (`country-model.ts`), so motion comes from the states' households, employers and books, never a dated shock or a dice roll.
2. **Prototype on the pure model.** `src/core2/country-model.test.ts` builds the tier without a world. The cycle prototype script (`macro-cycle.ts`) was deleted with the formula it ran. Compare recession counts with real data using one definition (unemployment up 1.5 points inside 24 months: real UNRATE 1990-2026 gives 4 episodes, 20% of months, depth 2.1 to 5.3). FRED CSVs come from `curl https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=<SERIES>`.
3. **Check the bands.** `scripts/money/band-exits.ts` runs the 12-month bands test on module worlds opened on published and drawn months; a swing that starts from one noisy month's change exits the band (smooth the opening momentum).
4. **Prove in a played world.** `scripts/money/outside.ts` (outside money by owner and treasury balance by year), `scripts/money/played.ts` (the national economy by year in a played world), `scripts/money/collected.ts` (receipts by government and year), `scripts/money/pay-paths.ts` (where pay flows), `npm run speed:year` (compare the same seed and place, main against your branch). Units: ledger amounts are minor units (cents); a billion dollars is 1e11.
5. **A flat number may be a reading artifact.** Compare the number a screen reads with the number the flows pay. Household income stayed flat because `unplacedPayPerWorker` ignored the pay level the paychecks used.
6. **Surplus piles come from the data.** A state budget row can have revenue far above spending (Census year); nothing spends it, so the treasury hoards and the outside account runs negative. Treasuries keep a reserve and invest the rest (`SG-GOV-reserve-sweep`).
7. **Tests red on main.** `budgets.test.ts` "every dollar lands" failed at the US treasury on clean main (Nov 8). Rerun it on clean `origin/main` with the same seed before blaming your branch; never loosen it. If it is still red there, say so on the pull request with the test, place and seed.
8. **Find who asks before capping a flow (Nov 15).** When a monthly cap exists because "everyone asked at once" (for example `creditHouseholdMonthlyLendingShare`), print the applicants by household, place, employer, income, costs and cash before replacing the cap. At Matagorda TX in early 2025, 89 of 129 personal-loan applicants were officials and aides filled in with no cash who lived outside the town, because `borrowingHouseholds` had no place filter. Compare the same run on the base: a change elsewhere (a savings spread) can multiply the applicants too. Rank by the lender's own judgment of each applicant (`approvedShare`), never by the order households were made.
9. **Measure money by kind of household before naming a cause (Nov 15).** A place's average opening cash of $430 a household read as "counted households open broke"; split by town or elsewhere and tenure, the town opened at the survey's median and the average came from 21,798 relatives elsewhere at $0, whose accounts the town's never share (group accounts are per place). Split any average by place and kind first.
10. **Crediting a price change to its causes (Nov 15).** A business carries the outcome web's price factor from one review to the next (`lastWebPriceFactor` in `finance-state.ts`). To say which law raised what it charged over a period, read the web's month row that review stood on (`webReachInMonth`), never the current row: reading the current row credited a tariff whose link had just started with a bottle deposit's dollars (Eveleth, Minnesota, seed 9ea5b0b1). Split a factor among its links by each one's share of its logarithm so the parts sum to the whole (`law-reach/web-price-trace.ts`). A trace that only reports must leave every other table's digest the same (`recordProof` and `compareProofs`, `src/bench/dev-proof.ts`, same seed and date).
11. **A treasury's cash or an outside owner's net that grows without end is a surplus with no use (Long game, Nov 17, #9835).** Log the treasury month by month: its cash, what it paid out and took in, and each budget's spending and revenue. At Inarajan, Guam federal spending stayed near $7.1 trillion while receipts grew with the wage bill, receipts passed outlays in July 2031, and nothing used a federal surplus (`sweepReserves` skipped the federal treasury), so the U.S. Treasury's cash and the outside market's net grew until the net passed 2^53 cents in year 13. A surplus now repays the debt held by the public (`repayFederalSurplus`). A test makes the surplus with the budget engine's own call (`amendBudget` halving every federal line) rather than waiting years for one.
