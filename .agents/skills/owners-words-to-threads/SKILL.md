# The owner's words

## Verbatim (owner, Nov 14)

"When I say stuff and it goes to threads, you don't summarize it. You send it verbatim." Attach his message whole, or quote it word for word. Your own notes go beside it, marked as yours. This holds when a thread passes his words on to a helper.

## Examples are generalities (owner, Nov 14)

"When I give examples, don't just limit it to those examples. I'm giving generalities. Your job is to see the bigger picture and build that as a system... no dice, all to the specifications of the game." Say in the brief that it is an example. Name the class (his "unemployment rate" is every figure a person might say), build the one system for the class, and say in the report what you took the rule to be.

## Standing rules for words

- A scene is only dialogue. No count, tally or status line spoken in a scene (Nov 14, 00:50Z).
- People say how it feels, never the figure; the record's value decides which line, by a sliding scale and never a draw. A figure may be said where real people say it: a speech, a paper story, a budget hearing. Money a person pays or is paid is their own fact.
- A scene is a tree: after the ask, two more turns on two branches, endings that differ, each writing something a later scene reads.
- Lines differ by speaker's traits, bearing and tie to the player, not by synonyms.
- Everything shown is simulated. Real life may inspire; never offer "source it from real data". No real person, name or sentence enters a bank.
- Do not wait on his grades. Questions for him go on a decision card or a Q line.
