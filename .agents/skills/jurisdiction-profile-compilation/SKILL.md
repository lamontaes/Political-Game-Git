## Keep two different questions separate

- Evidence: what a dated primary source establishes about the real jurisdiction.
- Gameplay: which approved versioned rule this saved fictional government uses.
  Unknown evidence must not become a fabricated fact. It also must not alone
  block a playable approved game profile. Never label a simulated fallback as a
  verified constitutional rule. An unavailable implementation is a third, distinct
  state; do not hide it by changing an admission flag.

## Procedure

1. Reuse the existing research before acquisition. The Census Individual State
   Descriptions is an organizational baseline with a vintage, not a complete
   current-law certification. Verify material exceptions with appropriate
   primary instruments. Record the field, scope, vintage and actual source.
2. Build shared mechanisms and compatible modifiers; avoid one bespoke engine
   per state or making every locality inherit one example city. Keep municipality,
   county/county-equivalent, township/town and D.C. semantics distinct.
3. Bind identities and overlapping territories explicitly. Similar names, or
   geographic overlap, do not prove identical governments or rule inheritance.
4. Separate structural anchors from fields approved to vary at Begin. QAR1-01
   is resolved: defining institutions and terms/limits/veto/local forms usually
   start close to the recognizable modern baseline; later supported reforms and
   coherent policy/condition variation remain. Persist the resolved rule/version
   and prior history. No read-time reroll. Old preplayable lives may be retired
   through the explicit QAR1-09 process, not silently rewritten while read.
5. Compile all states and applicable general-purpose local coverage required by
   the brief, using already approved progressive materialization. A handful of
   worked examples are tests, never a geographic product ceiling. QAR1-02
   excludes school boards and special districts for now. D.C. is a separate general-
   government context, not a school-board exception. QAR1-03 prioritizes agency/
   cabinet appointments and party/campaign-committee leadership as distinct
   domains of power, selection and finance, using common people/staff primitives.
6. Wire office creation, eligibility/entry, actual procedures, consequences and
   readable normal-player routes. A government catalog row or refusal message
   is not a completed playable government.

## Validation and return

Validate structural/profile coverage across all required jurisdictions. Play
representative materially different structures, not every possible permutation
before receiving any useful work. Check actor authority, real funds, rule dates,
local boundaries, incompatible modifiers, no-action and save/reopen. Test the
actual composed source. Report counts separately for identity, profile coverage,
implemented mechanism and verified ordinary gameplay; never collapse them into
one inflated playable count. Include open/fixed/deferred bugs and next owners.
