2026 GUBERNATORIAL ELECTIONS

There will be 39 gubernatorial elections in 2026 with 18 incumbent Governors running for reelection and 21 incumbent Governors who are either term-limited or not seeking reelection. The general election is scheduled to be held on November 3, 2026. Click a state or territory below for election information.
