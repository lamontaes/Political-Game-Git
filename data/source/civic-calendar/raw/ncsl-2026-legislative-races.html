Summary 2026 Legislative Races by State and Chamber

Updated June 25, 2026
A Summary is a quick read to increase your knowledge of a topic.
Related Topic: Elections

Legislative races in 46 states will be conducted in November 2026. These include races in 88 of the nation's 99 legislative chambers. The table below indicates which states and chambers are holding legislative elections, and how many seats are up for reelection this year.  

A total of 6,139 seats are up for regularly scheduled elections in 2026, representing 83% of the nation's 7,386 legislative seats. This page does not include any special elections that may be held in conjunction with the general election or at other times throughout 2026.

No legislative races will be held in 2026 in Louisiana, Mississippi, New Jersey or Virginia because they hold state elections in odd-numbered years. In 2025, House races will be held in New Jersey for all 80 assemblymembers and in Virginia for all 100 delegates. New Jersey and Virginia Senators have four-year terms and were last elected in 2023. In Louisiana and Mississippi, all members of both chambers have four-year terms and were last elected in 2023.  

The table below also provides the size of each legislative chamber and length of terms. Terms in all chambers are either two or four years; no state lawmakers have six-year terms.
