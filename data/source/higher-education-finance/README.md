# State higher education finance, fiscal 1980 to 2025 (SHEEO)

`SHEEO_SHEF_FY25_Report_Data.xlsx` is the State Higher Education Executive
Officers Association's State Higher Education Finance (SHEF) report data, all
values unadjusted (nominal dollars). For each state, D.C. and the nation, each
fiscal year from 1980 to 2025, it gives public colleges' gross tuition and fee
revenue, net tuition revenue, enrollment and state support.

- Source: https://shef.sheeo.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SHEEO_SHEF_FY25_Report_Data.xlsx
  (listed at https://shef.sheeo.org/data-downloads/)
- Citation: State Higher Education Executive Officers Association. (2026).
