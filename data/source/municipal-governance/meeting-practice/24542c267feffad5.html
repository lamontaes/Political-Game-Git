City Council Meetings Schedule

Information about upcoming City Council meetings.

  • View all upcoming council events
  • View committee meeting schedule

In accordance with the Council Rules of Procedure, the following is a list of the upcoming Council meetings. Most meetings occur on a Monday, unless otherwise noted below.

Meeting Location & Time

Meetings are held in the Vincent E. Griego Council Chambers, basement level of the City of Albuquerque Government Center, 1 Civic Plaza NW, Albuquerque, New Mexico 87102. 

All meetings begin at 5:00 p.m., unless another time is indicated on the agenda.

All meetings are open to the public.

CONTACT

The 311 Community Contact Center is a centralized call center for the City of Albuquerque. The 311 service is a single telephone number for all non-emergency City of Albuquerque inquiries and services.

Call: 311 or 505-768-2000
Report Online
Emergencies: 911
Non-Emergency Police Calls: 505-242-COPS
