For each City Council meeting, the Office of the City Clerk provides live stream video, including captions, on our Council News Central Page.

Video Archives

Archived City Council Meeting videos can be found on the Council Meeting Calendar page.

Attending City Council Meetings

Chicago City Council meetings are open to the public. Meetings typically begin at 10:00 am in the Council Chambers located on the second floor at City Hall, 121 North LaSalle Street.

For more information on attending a Chicago City Council meeting, please contact the Chicago City Council Sergeant-at-Arms at 312-744-6800 or via email at CityCouncilSeating@cityofchicago.org.

Disability Access

Any person with a disability who would benefit from a reasonable accomdation in order to attend a meeting of the City Council of attend and/or address one of its committees can contact the Sergeant-at-Arms at Alvin.starks@cityofchicago.org or 312- 744-6000. Any such request should be made as far in advance of the relevant meeting as possible to provide City staff with sufficient time to make any appropriate accommodations.
