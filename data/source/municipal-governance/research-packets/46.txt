# Part 2 — 50-State Municipal Completeness Matrix — 2026-08-25


## Purpose
This is the control artifact for national municipal-environment research. It prevents opportunistic city research from being mistaken for nationwide coverage.


The matrix distinguishes:
- `RESEARCHED`: current official institutional/location material exists in the Part 2 library.
- `ROOM VERIFIED`: a current formal meeting room/location has been verified.
- `PARTIAL`: at least one useful municipal family is researched, but the state's major/medium/distinctive coverage is incomplete.
- `COVERAGE STRONG`: three or more complementary municipal families are researched well enough for the reference-library stage, although photo packs may still be incomplete.
- `GAP`: no adequate municipal family yet.
- `ART READY`: requires several usable real visual references plus stable current-location/era understanding; this matrix does NOT imply art readiness unless explicitly stated.


## Important selection caveat
The `major / medium / distinctive` entries below are research-control candidates based on the work already done. They are NOT yet the final algorithmic selection from the 2025 Census Government Units Listing + current population data. The later GUS/population crosswalk may replace a candidate. Hawaii and some New England/local-government systems require topology-aware selection rather than blindly choosing three incorporated cities.

## Matrix-design correction
`major / medium / distinctive` is insufficient by itself in states with materially different local-government forms. Final selection should be multi-axis:
1. population/urban scale;
2. government-unit/legal form;
3. legislative method (council/commission/board/open town meeting/representative town meeting/SB2/etc.);
4. physical environment family;
5. historical/visual distinctiveness;
6. gameplay relevance.


A state may therefore warrant more than three reusable reference families even when only three are needed for an initial art tranche.
