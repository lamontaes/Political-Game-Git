# Official annual office salary facts

`facts.json` records factual annual amounts independently read from official statutes, enacted appropriations, compensation commissions, courts and government publications. Each row identifies its office, jurisdiction, amount, precise locator, historical reference period and source URL. The access date is October 11, 2026. Reference years intentionally vary; these are calibration references, not a complete current-law salary register.

The exporter rounds cents to the nearest whole dollar and projects only salary facts and citations into `src/simulation/office-pay.generated.ts`. It does not copy publisher tables, personal identity records, portraits or source prose. No runtime import reaches this journal or captured research. Legacy official hosts are explicitly allowed in the exporter: Arkansas government media, Guam's Office of Public Accountability, the CNMI Law Revision Commission, Kansas Legislative Research, the Virgin Islands Office of Personnel, and legacy state legislature domains.

Only single annual salary figures are accepted. Per-day, per-week, chamber-dependent, allowance-dependent and unverified rows are absent. The unchanged salary reader supplies an explicitly labeled estimate from official same-office salary facts and Census reference incomes. An estimate is not statutory authority. For example, Mississippi's official legislative audit reports an annual salary plus payments for full out-of-session months; this journal does not invent a single annual total from that formula.

The preserved CSG captures in `data/source/book-of-the-states/` are research-only historical evidence. They and their lock hashes are not inputs to this exporter and must not enter the game bundle. The production build runs `check:data-shipping` after all output preparation. Its scan covers emitted files, including maps and server output, and rejects the salary capture URLs, filenames, hashes and captured bytes.

Run `npm run export:office-pay` to regenerate; run `npm run export:office-pay -- --check` to check freshness. `tests/source/official-office-pay.test.ts` reconciles every shipped salary and citation with the journal and verifies all three categories in all 56 jurisdictions. `docs/reports/2026-10-data-fix-salary-diff.json` records the before/after data swap, including newly estimated rows.
