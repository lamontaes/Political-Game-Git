# Official annual office salary facts

`facts.json` records factual annual amounts independently read from official statutes, enacted appropriations, compensation commissions, courts and government publications. Each row identifies its office, jurisdiction, amount, precise locator, effective year and term and source URL. The access date is October 11, 2026. All 122 facts were reviewed for the office entitlement in force on October 11, 2026. Statutory bases include applicable adjustments; current published compensation schedules supersede old bases. Locators cite both the base and adjustment, with formulas where needed. Future-term raises, housing, expenses and leadership supplements are excluded.

The exporter rounds cents to the nearest whole dollar and projects only salary facts and citations into `src/simulation/office-pay.generated.ts`. It does not copy publisher tables, personal identity records, portraits or source prose. No runtime import reaches this journal or captured research. Legacy official hosts are explicitly allowed in the exporter: Arkansas government media, Guam's Office of Public Accountability, the CNMI Law Revision Commission, Kansas Legislative Research, the Virgin Islands Office of Personnel, legacy state legislature domains, Florida’s official rules publisher, the Virgin Islands Legislature, and the University of Tennessee’s publication of the courts’ compensation memorandum.

Only single annual salary figures are accepted. Per-day, per-week, chamber-dependent, allowance-dependent and unverified rows are absent. The unchanged salary reader supplies an explicitly labeled estimate from official same-office salary facts and Census reference incomes. An estimate is not statutory authority. For example, Mississippi's official legislative audit reports an annual salary plus payments for full out-of-session months; this journal does not invent a single annual total from that formula.

The preserved CSG captures in `data/source/book-of-the-states/` are research-only historical evidence. They and their lock hashes are not inputs to this exporter and must not enter the game bundle. The production build runs `check:data-shipping` after all output preparation. Its scan covers emitted files, including maps and server output, and rejects the salary capture URLs, filenames, hashes and captured bytes.

Run `npm run export:office-pay` to regenerate; run `npm run export:office-pay -- --check` to check freshness. `tests/source/official-office-pay.test.ts` reconciles every shipped salary and citation with the journal and verifies all three categories in all 56 jurisdictions. `docs/reports/2026-10-data-fix-current-pay.json` records this current-pay review against the original PR head. `docs/reports/2026-10-data-fix-current-all-56.json` records every resulting salary and estimate. The original dated swap receipt remains in `docs/reports/2026-10-data-fix-salary-diff.json`.
