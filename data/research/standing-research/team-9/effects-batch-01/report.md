The stand-your-ground interval comes from a pooled observational model of forty-one states during 1999 to 2017. It is uncertainty around a pooled estimate, not the distribution of state effects. The study reports substantial state differences and possible residual confounding. Its law definition covers statutory public-place self-defense, not every local self-defense rule.

The pollution range combines the paper's reported design and subsample elasticity spread. It is not a confidence interval. Dividing by one hundred matches the cause's percentage-of-start unit near its starting value. The pollutant is TSP; this does not establish a PM2.5 or air-quality-index coefficient. The outcome is infant deaths from internal causes per live births.
