## 2. Research

Measured observation from [ALA’s national data page](https://www.ala.org/bbooks/book-ban-data): for 2025, ALA reports 713 documented censorship attempts involving library materials and services; 487 targeted books. It separately reports 4,235 unique titles challenged. Attempts and unique titles have different denominators. ALA collects professional reports and news coverage and warns that unreported challenges make this a snapshot. No staff-hours figure was found in this source.

Measured observation from [NCES Table 236.60](https://nces.ed.gov/programs/digest/d23/tables/dt23_236.60.asp): the textbook row reports $55 per pupil in annual, unadjusted dollars in 2020–21, compared with $51 in 2019–20. The table covers the 50 states and D.C.; territorial coverage cannot be assumed. These annual expenditure observations do not establish a one-time adoption cost or the incremental cost of a particular curriculum law.

Measured observations from [California DMV licensing fees](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/licensing-fees/) and its [ID card eligibility page](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/identification-id-cards/). Regular ID application fees are $40; reduced-fee ID cards cost $11; senior ID cards are free for applicants age 62 or older. Qualifying homeless applicants may receive no-fee cards. These are applicant fee categories, not a production-cost study or a national range. Application rules and eligibility affect who actually pays.

Lobbying counts, size and revenue remain unverified. A public-relations establishment proxy must not be labeled lobbying registrants; payroll must not be labeled revenue.
