# Transportation rules need more jurisdiction evidence

The first transportation research tranche records 21 legislative decisions across ten topics. It corrects several claims in the worked example and keeps unsupported effects unsized. It is a research draft. Transportation is not ready for approval: 1099 availability cells remain unresearched, and the remaining verification work is listed below. No game behavior has changed.

## What the evidence establishes

California enacted transit-oriented housing legislation in 2025, with provisions beginning on different dates. LA Metro reports that its 2016 transportation measure received 71.15 percent support. The source records distinguish enactment from a rule already operating at the game start. See [SB 79](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB79) and [Measure M](https://www.metro.net/about/measure-m/).

The fluoridation review provides an effect range that includes no benefit. It studies starting fluoridation; it cannot establish a certain increase in cavities after stopping it. [Cochrane review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD010856_does-adding-fluoride-water-supplies-prevent-tooth-decay).

## Wider consequences and missing links

The draft separates direct financial or legal mechanisms from measured behavioral effects. A bond authorization does not prove construction, and a rail acquisition does not prove operating service. Public grants do not establish unlimited local taxing authority.

The two initial system redesigns split financing, governance, ownership, contracts, rates, and service safeguards into negotiable pieces. England provides models for bus franchising and water regulation. Further U.S. ownership redesign measures and effect evidence remain open.

Special districts, tribes, counties, cities and school districts require fuller authority records. The organization and staff requirements are handoff requirements; this research has created no simulated organizations or people. The federal area batches and the remaining 177 topics have not been delivered.
