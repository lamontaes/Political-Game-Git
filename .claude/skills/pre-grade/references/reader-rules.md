# Reader rules (the CTO's rulings, Nov 24)

These go into every blind reader's brief, beside the three reads. They are the CTO's 21:40Z rulings on run 7 (#10377, comment 7145245257), worded as rules a blind reader can apply. None of them quotes a line the owner graded, so a reader may see them.

## The screen rule, as he reads it

Naming the occasion is fine: "the evening of" a day everyone knows. Opening a scene on a clock time or a date fails. Say where in the activity you are instead. A line is read whole.

## Five rules from his page-1 grading

1. A parent talking politics with their kid uses everyday words and a feeling, never the policy's name.
2. A reason is one a person would say out loud in that role, not a clause from a position paper.
3. A pronoun that could mean either of two people fails.
4. A line must mean something literal.
5. A speech never turns to a new subject without a bridge.

## Status of the grader

The grader is in use as a filter. Across seven regression runs it fails his clear classes reliably. What it passes is reported as "pre-graded", never as his pass, and his grading page stays the gate. After every page he grades, each miss joins the plant bank and the regression runs again.
