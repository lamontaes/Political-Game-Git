# A scene builder's first report

No lines come first. The Words lead reads this report and rules before a single line is written.

Report, for each variant of the scene:

1. **What stages it.** The record that makes it due, with its file and function, in the engine that owns it (`scene-producers`). One situation type with variants by data, never a path per office or place.
2. **How often.** Run a played year (`played-world-proof`) in three drawn places and say how many times it staged and when. A scene that never stages is the finding.
3. **The packet.** Every fact the scene may say, from records (names, jobs, the tie to the player, the town, the office). Nothing else may be said: no he or she, no history, no "always" that no record holds.
4. **What it writes back.** Each ending writes a record a later scene or screen reads (`scene-standard`). List them.
5. **Who can be asked and why**, from records, as the existing list is.
6. **What the engine cannot do today.** A seat or event with no engine record is listed, not built; say what is missing.
7. **What the player's offered replies are** and how they differ by traits.

Then stop and wait for the ruling while other work continues. The owner's example in the ask is a generality (`owners-words-to-threads`): name the class of cases the scene serves.
