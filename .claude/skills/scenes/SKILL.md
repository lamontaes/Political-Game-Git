# Scenes are places, not backdrops

A scene is four things together: a background picture; narration shaped by
the actual state of the world (the same place in two different worlds reads
two different texts, not the same boilerplate); anchors where people sit,
stand or work; and the people who are really there right now, posed and
clickable — not decorative, not a name-swapped stand-in. Places are multi-use
by default: a campaign office is also where decisions get made, speeches get
written, and a victory speech gets given. Don't build a second location when
the existing one already fits.

## Check your own work

Change the world state under a scene you're building — a different law, a
different day, a different occupant — and confirm the narration text actually
changes. If it reads identically regardless of world state, it's boilerplate,
not a scene. Confirm every person rendered is someone the simulation actually
placed there (check the anchor's source), not a hardcoded or generic figure.
