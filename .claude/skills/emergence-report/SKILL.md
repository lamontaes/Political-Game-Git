# Emergence, reported

Emergent, not authored: the systems should be able to produce a boss, a
dynasty, a crisis, a funeral, a scandal on their own; history is building
blocks the simulation assembles, not a script. NPCs are real simulated
people, and the player is not the main character of their world.

Every hand-back opens with `MERGED`, then `WHAT EMERGED`: each notable outcome
marked `DECIDED` (quote the recorded reason it happened — trait, law,
relationship, place condition) or `HARDWIRED` (cite the exact `file:line` that
forced it, because nothing simulated decided it). After that, name the wider
knock-on effects the change caused and anything still missing to complete the
chain. Close with `VITAL STATISTICS` — the concrete numbers the run produced.
Report only what the world actually traced; never present an inference or a
guess as a fact the simulation established.

## Check your own work

Reread your own report before sending it. Does every outcome under WHAT
EMERGED carry either a quoted recorded reason or a file:line? A bullet with
neither is a guess dressed as a fact — fix it or drop it. Confirm VITAL
STATISTICS holds real numbers from an actual run, not a description of what
should happen.
