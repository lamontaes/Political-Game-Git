# Pinned tests

A test draws its place (`drawn-place-tests`). A test pinned in its file to named places and seeds is added only when a CTO line on the crew's thread approves that exact pull request (CTO, Nov 16, `done-proof`). With that line, the rules below say how the pin is written; an env seed, or a retry over fresh seeds, is still a draw and counts as unpinned. The staging lessons below hold for every test, drawn or pinned. Drawing places is still how a feature is proved in play: `played-world-proof`.

## The rules

- **With the CTO's line, pin the place and the seed in the file.** Name two or three places with their seeds. They were found once by drawing and waiting (`drawn-place-tests` shows how); the file records the answer so the next run is the same run.
- **Stage a law by the game's own enactment and start from the row the game reads (Nov 15).** A test that waits played days for a bill to pass took 352 seconds and was red on a tie. Pin the place, take the starting amount from `billValueInForce` (the floor doctrine keeps the federal row on a tie, so "the local row" is the wrong start), and enact through `enactLaw`. Say in the comment why no played days are needed.
- **Account lead rulings of Nov 15 (all tests, every PR).** No skipped or conditional tests and no hand staging: the case is reached through the game's own path. A pick found by a script outside the test is capped at one hour, and the test pins the answer only with the CTO's line. A long replay is a script that writes the played file, not a test. A meeting, vote or filing that a script or test makes for someone is not play: the player's acts go through the action their press takes (`campaignAct`, `askLeaderForHearing`), and nobody calls `scheduleVote`, `fileForOffice`, `gatherSignatures` or another writer by hand to stand for a player's act. A test that calls a writer (`vacateSeat`, `setPartnersV1`, `holdVote`) stages with a record the game may not make: label it a writer check in its name and the PR body, and show a played case beside it, or say none was reached. A builder's report names every engine function its test calls. A record set by a call the player never makes is not staging: reach the case through the player's own acts (meet, visits, Ask out, Propose), as `src/director/proposal-played.test.ts` does.
- **A place or territory a note names only by label (Red Hook, Virgin Islands) has its key in the test that drew it.** Rerun drawPlace("<seed>:<draw>", <purpose>) from scripts/core2-year/place.ts for the draw numbers and read `.key` and `.seed` (territory keys look like `territory:VI:red-hook`). Pin that key and that world seed; a guessed GEOID throws `Unknown place key`.
- **`seatAct(core, id, { kind: "draft", officeId, question })` takes the question as the law's full id** (`us-policy-positions:environment-energy.ban-new-gas-hookups`); a bare `environment-energy.ban-new-gas-hookups` drafts a bill that comes out `blocked` ("no-state-law") and never reaches a floor, so a test reads a blocked bill as nothing happening.
- **`campaigns.traits.test.ts` draws its seed at random unless `GOLDEN_PATH_SEED` is set.** Compare a red on it on fixed seeds (s1 to s3), with `expect(` changed to `expect.soft(` in a scratch copy to list every failing assertion on both sides.
- **More Nov 16 rulings (Account lead).** A band picked to make the test pass is tuning, not a check. Acting for other candidates and reading their scene as the player is staging. A scene "composed, not pressed" is not played. The limit is near twice the slower run on every test file the PR touches. A tracer's stated cause can be wrong: replay it on the current head before building the fix.
- **Measure a test's limit beside the PR's other changed tests (maxWorkers 2), not alone.** On Nov 15 proposal-played ran 56 s alone and 64.5 s beside the others; the limit is near twice the slower run, so a limit eight times the measured time (a 300 s limit on a 37 s test) is too loose. A test that spends hours right after a day opens must resume the day's opening stop first, or spend returns nothing and the break-it run passes when it should fail.
- **Stage a rare event through the game's own path.** If the case is a recall in progress or a filing open on a day, drive the world there with the engines' own calls. Do not insert a fixture row, and do not wait for a draw to land on it.
- **About a minute.** Over two minutes is a different kind of test. Set an explicit timeout when the run is near vitest's 30 second default.
- **Fail by name.** When the case is missing, fail at that point with the place, the seed and what was passed over.
- **Never loosen a test, raise a baseline or widen a number to get green** without a CTO line approving that pull request (`done-proof`). If a builder respells a line or a number to slip past a check, send it back.
- **A test that needs a rare case that does not exist is a finding**, not a license to skip: file an item.
- **A late start changes who runs.** A test that opens a world later in the year to save time changes who files, who is in office and what is under way (the views thread, Nov 14: a moderate files on a cause from a Feb 3 board meeting at Marshfield MA, and in a world opened Aug 1 or Sep 1 only two liberals file; at Monument Beach MA opened Jun 1 the conservative never files). Check that the case still exists in the late world before moving the start date.
- **A pinned case must fail on the old behavior.** Put the old line back once and see the test go red (the warm-up test: without the hold the played person acts 69 times in the window, with it 0) before calling the case pinned.
- **A test that files for office sizes the place by its vote date** (campaign events builder, Nov 15; the filing fee it also staged cash for was taken out of the game on the owner's word the same day). Assert the filing was not refused. A big place whose vote is nine months out takes about 190 seconds of played days after a 38 second build; pin a small place where the vote follows the filing window by weeks, and measure its build time, days to the vote and the debate or other event it needs before pinning it (a place with a council race can still hold no debate).
- **A pinned case must fail on the old behavior.** Swap the one changed source file for the base branch's copy (`git checkout <base> -- <file>`), run the test, put the file back; the layoff test was first written at a place where the old code already printed the story and would have proved nothing. A test that passes on the old code is deleted, not kept (Account lead, Nov 15). Put the old line back once and see the test go red (the warm-up test: without the hold the played person acts 69 times in the window, with it 0) before calling the case pinned. Read which assertion went red: a fix that changes anyone's choices changes the world after it, so on the parent a pinned pair or row may not exist at all (Nov 15: a lost-hours test went red on the parent because the pinned row was missing, then because its count of cases was 0, never at the defect). Make one assertion read the defect over every case the condition finds, and name in the comment the line that fails on the parent and why.
- **A test that files for office stages the filing fee's cash, and sizes the place by its vote date** (campaign events builder, Nov 15). The player starts with $0 and a place that charges a filing fee refuses the filing (`filing-fee`, `status: "refused"`), so set the player's `liquidMinor` before `fileForOffice` and assert the filing was not refused. A big place whose vote is nine months out takes about 190 seconds of played days after a 38 second build; pin a small place where the vote follows the filing window by weeks, and measure its build time, days to the vote and the debate or other event it needs before pinning it (a place with a council race can still hold no debate).
- **A test that takes "the first ask" breaks when an ask moves earlier (Nov 15).** Moving a party's ask before the day's weighing made the first ask an ask to a member who weighs the seat, so the player's-answer test registered the wrong person. Pick the case by what the records say (the ask came after the contest's weighed row had `filedOn` set), never by order, and say why in a comment. Narrowing which case a test stages is not loosening it; removing or weakening an assertion is, and goes to the CTO (`done-proof`).
- **Check that a break-it edit changed the file (Nov 15).** A `sed` that matches nothing leaves the code intact and the test green. Print the changed line, or count matches, before reading the result.
- **A test's played days are a cost; say why in a comment and use the smallest named place.** A test that plays months must say what the months are for (the federal income tax test plays 40 days, then 62 after each law change, because a law reaches the tier at its next month step and the treasury at the close after that), and it runs at the smallest place that holds the case (Roy, Montana is about 28 seconds a year; Klamath Falls and Delta City about 190). A first version at Klamath Falls took 152 seconds and was sent back; at Roy it takes 23. The explicit limit is near twice the measured test time.
- **A case that stays red is fixed at its cause, never marked to pass (CTO, Nov 16, `done-proof`).** No `it.fails`, skip or new known-red entry without a CTO line approving that pull request. After three real attempts at the cause, post "for the CTO" on the crew's thread with what you tried.
- **A test that draws its own seed prints it and reads `GOLDEN_PATH_SEED` when it is set (acting CTO, Nov 16, 11:19Z).** Otherwise the golden-path job's table names a place the test never played and the red cannot be replayed.
- **A test that stages a death, a payment or a settlement on a record picked by state ("the richest household") must ask whether the game already acted on that record this month.** The estate test (law-wiring-taxes, Dennis MA) picked a record the game had just killed in part: its second settlement reused a transfer id (the accounts engine keeps ids for the month and the one before) and the failure looked like a game bug in the PR that happened to be on the head. Before blaming the PR on the head, run the same pinned test with that PR's change put back (`git checkout <earlier head> -- <file>`); if it fails the same, it is not that PR's.
- **A test that waits out a game timeline is slow by the game's own days, and says so (estate heirs, Nov 15).** Probate is 270 played days, so a played-world case that kills a person and runs to the close takes about 190 s at Dennis, Massachusetts (key 2516740, seed 9b87fa9d713b54ecc27f071016da6e4ecd4f220b5f459bbd5c86bc14e9fc9f97); give it a limit near twice (420 s) and a comment that names the timeline. Stage the deaths through `api.updatePerson(id, { alive: false })` and `settleEstate`, never by waiting for a death. Read anything that keeps only the living (`willOf`, `heirsOf`) before the death you stage, not after: after it, the dead are gone from the answer.
- **A world opened late has none of the causes lived before its opening** (no campaign under way, no speech, no rival's platform built from a season of events; item `opening-world-holds-causes-lived-before-its-start`). Until that is built, a case that comes out of a campaign plays from the game's start (Jan 20) to the month it comes, and the comment says the time is the game's own played days (Nov 14: the pledge and rivals tests).
- **Typecheck does not read most test files** (item `tests-typecheck-covers-src-test-files`). After changing a function's arguments, run its tests: on Nov 14 a test calling `speechAct` the old way threw at every seed with typecheck green.
- **Playing a pinned test longer keeps each assertion at its own date (Nov 15, views thread).** Moving the outlet endorsement test from Oct 1 to Oct 8 to see the printing turned its follower count red (10 against 11): followers had changed in the extra week. Advance to the first date, assert what was measured there, then advance again for the new assertion; do not move the old assertions to the later date.
- **Stage what a case rests on through the player's own record, not the world's luck** (Account lead, Nov 15). A case that rests on a person's view (a no that rests on the player's stance, a yes near the line) moves whenever anyone changes how stances are made: on Nov 15 the country's view of each question joined every opening stance (#9323) and three serve-ask cases at Guam seeds turned. Pin the player's side of it instead, the way a player sets it: the filing's platform (`${question}=${value}`), a seat the player holds, their own vote (the reporter's call after a yes: drawn runs of press-comment-context always vote against the town's lean, so press-comment-after-vote casts the vote with `chooseOwnVote` at Bagdad, Arizona). Re-pinning a moved case needs the old premise, the cause and a CTO line on that pull request (`done-proof`), and the PR body names the old seed, the new pin and why.
- **A roll call months away is held today through dev mode's route.** `holdVote(api, billId, { anyDay: true })` (what dev mode's hold-vote calls) runs the body's real vote, committee included; a territory's floor calendar can put a drafted bill eleven months out. A bill can still die in committee: pin a bill that reaches the floor, and say which way it was drafted.
- **Before a re-pin for drift, print the rates before and after across the scan (Account lead, Nov 16),** so the move is shown as a rate, not a swapped place.
- **Count how many cases an assertion rests on (Nov 15).** A counted-buyers test at Bastrop passed for weeks because one July renter in 125 had the down payment; a Train fold that regrouped counted households removed that one, and the test went red with no change on its branch. Print how many cases pass the condition (not only that one does); an assertion that passes on nothing (zero of N) is replaced by one that can fail, and a cap or count set to what the world happens to give is a tuned number, not a bound. If the count is one or two, the test is luck, so stage a named case through the game's own path instead, and get a CTO line before re-pinning.

- **Look for the story the game already tells before staging one** (Nov 15, the paper's looking-for-work line): probe the pinned place month by month and word each record the game wrote (`wordPrintedStory`); Washington, Kansas told its jobless every month from February to June, so nothing needed setting. Only if no month does, move the earlier reading the story compares against, say why in the test comment, and assert that the printed figure comes from the game's own row, never the one set by hand. A record the game wrote proves the route; a hand-built one proves only the wording.
- **A screen that shows "now" reads the latest kept month, not the live count.** Compare the Metrics tab with the kept month row on its own day, never with a fresh read after the month closed (Washington, Kansas, July 21: 35 live against the tab's 28 from July 2).
- **Finding named places where a season is played.** A test that files a candidate and plays to election day needs places where the player gets onto the ballot (some races want thousands of signatures). Probe once: loop `drawPlace("<fixed-prefix>:<n>", "campaign-season")`, file, gather signatures, and write each place's key, name, race and petition result to a file (vitest swallows `console.log` from a long test, so a probe that only logs shows nothing). Pin the three that pass by seed string and assert the drawn key equals the recorded one. Millston WI, Grafton VT and Bicknell UT on `rwprobe:0`, `:5`, `:6` are such places (Nov 15). A season takes about 50 to 60 seconds a place, so give the test a limit near twice that.
- **A second compose of one moment is skipped.** To show a scene for other kinds of record (a mailer, an ad, a bigger move), write the one fact onto the real cause record's facts and the one stance row it reads, compose again, and restore both. Say in the test that this is an edge case, not a played one, and keep the played cases for what the world made.
- **When a merge stops a pinned place drawing its case (Nov 15).** First find what changed (the world still makes the case, or the director stopped staging it), say so on the crew's thread, and swap the place only with a CTO line on that pull request (`done-proof`). Scan ten drawn seeds in a throwaway test that logs to a file (never commits), pin the one that plays every case, and write the old premise and why it stopped in the test's comment and the PR body. A lone place that covers every case is better than three that each cover some.

- **To play a different person than the world's player, finish the day first.** A test that needs an eligible entrant (a housemate with a race open) plays to a whole day (`minuteOfDay` 0) with the world's player, calls `retirePlayerV1(core)`, sets `core.playerId`, calls `director.watch` and `core.focusPersonIds.add`, then plays on with the new person; retiring mid-evening throws "Finish the controlled day before retiring its clock". `advanceCore` alone never feeds the director's ledger, so a home-talk test must play through `advancePlayerV1` (`src/director/home-talk-own-race-only.test.ts`).

- **Before you pin a person, read who they are.** A pinned "yes" or "no" must be someone the engine should offer at all. On Nov 15 the governor's pinned "no" at Fern Prairie, Washington was a sitting U.S. Senator from Rhode Island, offered only because the list did not filter by state; fixing the list broke the pin. Print the pinned person's place, job and any official row before pinning, and state the old premise in the PR body when a fix moves one.
- **Stage a test in the order play has (Nov 15).** The game keeps an event's record only when it names someone in the player's circle at the moment it is written. A test that makes the person the player inside the event's own handler gets a record the director cannot read, and calling the keeper by hand hides that. Put the person in the circle first (`core.focusPersonIds`), then let the game write the event. When a pin changes, write the old premise and the new one in the test's comment, and time each place twice: alone, and beside the PR's other changed tests. A place's first days can cost minutes (Denver, Pennsylvania took 137 s for eight days). Set the limit near twice the slower of the two runs (Account lead, Nov 15: West Hartford, Connecticut took 56 s alone and 68 s beside them, so its limit is 135 s).
- **A scene's lines are proven by playing the call, not composing it (Account lead, Nov 15).** A test that says which lines a player hears takes the scene the game staged (`purposeScene` on the schedule entry), taps replies by their move (`replyInScene`, `playedNode`) and checks what the last reply said. Calling `composeSceneTalk` with the purpose swapped to another variant or `askerTraits` set by hand shows lines no player meets, and a colleague's bill drafted with `seatAct` for that colleague is the test acting for them: wait for a colleague's bill to come up in play (the legislature's `bills` table, `billStage(...).yours.mayChoose`), and find the place and the tap path by a script outside the test (press-comment-after-vote.test.ts).

## Lessons from the public-action tests (Account lead, Nov 15)

7. **A bound a builder wrote is a premise, so state it.** A share bound ("no action holds a tenth of the adults") went red when the train's world shifted by a few people. Do not nudge the number silently: say in the test's comment what the number means (here, the renters an issue touches, not the town), change it once with the new measured figure beside it, and name the change in the PR body.
8. **A test that waits for a case it may not get is a drawn test even with a fixed seed variable.** A rally test and a movement test drew a place by a random seed and passed only where a rally or a founding existed. Draw and wait for the case and fail by name (`drawn-place-tests`), or, with a CTO line, pin the place (`drawPlace(<fixed seed>, <label>)`, then `expect(place.name)`); say in a comment why a run over a minute is the game's own days.
9. **Stage the id shape the engine really makes.** A recruiter's id may hold colons (a newspaper's owner is `newspaper:...`), and a parse written for a person's id lost the action. Put an id of that shape in the test.

## Debugging a test (Nov 15, Visit/Call thread)

`console.log` is swallowed by vitest here. A throwaway debug test writes its tally to a file and the file is deleted when the answer is found.

## Lesson of Nov 15 (the paper's hold for comment)

- **A Journal line is checked by its sentence, not its whole text (Nov 15).** The Journal opens a line on the time since the line before ("One month later, ..."), so a pinned exact-text assert goes red as soon as the Train writes any earlier line in that world (#9477 failed its fold this way on a head four Train pushes old). Assert `line.plain ?? line.text` equals the sentence and `line.text` ends with it, and merge the newest Train before the final gate.
- **Break it before you trust it.** Two press tests at their pinned places passed with the change and without it, because the place's tip never ran, so the asserts sat inside `if (ran)`. Undo the line under test and run the file. If it still passes, stage the case through the game's own path (here: the player takes the bribe in the offer scene, the offerer leaks it with `leakToReporter`), and assert outside any branch.

## Lesson of Nov 15 (an at-large race has one result record per seat)

- **A race is not one filed contest and one result.** A test that looks up `election-result:<the contest the player filed for>` and judges a win by that contest's winner calls an at-large win a loss and finds no Journal line (New Llano, Louisiana, a three-seat race: filed under `council:0`, elected to the seat counted as `council:4`). Read the result events whose `contestId` or `race` is the filed contest and that name the player, and take a win from any of them. Pin the place and say in the comment which premise changed.
- **A pin read before a merge is not a pin on the merged head.** A test pinned to a place's outcome (a win, a count) moves whenever any merged change touches that world. #9545's New Llano win was seen 3 of 3 on the commit before the train was merged in, then reported on the merged head without a rerun; CI showed the player losing there. After every merge of the train into a branch, rerun each pinned-outcome test on the merged head before any sha is reported, and say in the report which head the run was on. Prefer a pin whose margin is wide: New Llano's win rested on the third at-large seat, 51 votes to 63, and fell to 27 votes after unrelated train changes.
- **A played step to a distant date is the cost.** Playing a new life to April 2026 took 8 minutes at one place, so a test of a rule that sits inside it stages the rule on the opening day (a minute) and leaves the played-year read to the PR body, with the break-it run on the staged rule.

## Playing a controlled person through days

`advanceCore` refuses a controlled person. To play days for the player in a test, build the world with `buildBenchWorld`, wrap the person in `BenchPlayer`, `select` them, and loop: on a `stop` packet vote their floor holds with `voteOnStateFloor(api, id, bill, "own")` and `resume`, on a `choice` packet `resume` with `own-choice`, otherwise `advance("day")`. `src/core2/bill-floor-date-holds.test.ts` is the worked case (Holiday Lakes, Texas, seed aa11, about a minute); a fix test should be run once with the fix stashed to see it fail where the defect was.
