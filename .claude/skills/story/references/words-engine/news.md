# Words engine: news stories

Part of the `words-engine` skill. Headline, lede, body and briefs.

## News stories: headline, lede, body and briefs (owner, Oct 29: "headlines and then a little bit of story. Nothing is actually on the paper")

- **One composer, one call.** A printed story's facts come from the source record by a builder in `src/bench/printed-words.ts` (`BUILDERS`, read through `Reader`). `composeNewsStory` (`src/english/english-newscast.ts`) words the headline, the lede, then one sentence per `context` role in `STORY_KINDS` whose facts are present. `wordPrintedStory` returns `{headline, lede, paragraphs, outlet}`, and the lane's paper prints exactly that (CREW.md seam): never a second composer on the page side.
- **Measure before writing:** `node --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/printed-kinds.ts --seed <seed>` plays a drawn place's year and lists every printed kind with its count, how many print a body, the unworded records and one example with its facts. A failing printed-words test's `GOLDEN_PATH_SEED` reproduces there.
- **A body sentence says something the record holds and the lede did not:** the bill's purpose, the sponsor, the chamber, what follows a veto or a committee vote, the national rate, the rate before. Add the fact to the builder from an engine record; never invent one. A sentence whose facts the lede already named is skipped by the composer.
- **Pieces live in two banks:** `data/english/parts/news.json` (14 words at most) and `headlines.json`, gated by `requiresFacts`. Words like passed, failed, vetoed and won add their own requirement (`variants()` in english-newscast.ts).
- **The paper's style:**
  - headlines in sentence case;
  - sums of a million or more rounded ("$228.7 million"), and a trillion in trillions ("$7.1 trillion", never "$7204.5 billion");
  - a law's measure named by the English engine's subject for its series (`storyTopicName`), never the key read as words ("snap maximum").
- **Measure what one reader meets.** `node --max-old-space-size=8000 --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/paper-year.ts --seed <seed> --draws 3 --played` plays the player's year and counts the sentences one paper prints more than three times in its year, the item's measure, and the same across all the town's outlets. A national story prints in every outlet, so the town-wide count says little: within one paper, Blunt, South Dakota went from 54 to 0 (#7022).
- **Print what the body did, not that it sat** (the CTO, Nov 1, under the owner's "real ones with some good fluff"). A sitting with business is told by its votes; one with nothing before it is a dated line in the week's meetings brief. A weekly line needs a date, or the same sentence prints every week.
- **The news picker takes any usable line, not the most specific.** `composeNewsStory` draws evenly among the lines whose facts are present, so a generic line plays beside a specific one. To keep a generic line out, set a different flag in its place: a repeated exposure sets `again`, not `conflict`.
- **Make a kind's pieces exclusive by flags.** Until the newscast reads `excludesFacts` (#7098), a piece that needs fewer facts still plays beside a specific one. So the builder sets exactly one flag from each set, and every piece of the kind requires one from each: the law pieces need `took` or `enacted`; `here`, `nationwide` or `federal-here`; and `up`, `down`, `same` or `new` (#7127). An old piece of the kind without the new flags stays eligible, so give it the flags or remove it.
- **A law is news only when it changed what stood, told at its own level** (owner's Nov 1 playtest, #7127). The archive keeps a law whose row differs from the row it replaced at its level and place (`rowReplaced`, laws). Its story tells that level's change, names the government that set it, and says whom it reaches from `amounts` in `data/english/bill-names.json`. The paper carries a law or a budget only while the news engine still calls it news (`stillNews`).
- **A word in a piece can add a hidden requirement.** `variants()` in english-newscast.ts makes "night", "tonight" or "evening" need an `evening` fact, "morning" a `morning` fact, and "won", "lost", "passed" and "failed" their outcome. A concession line that said "on election night" never played (Lane 3, Nov 3). Read that list before wording a news piece.
- **A body sentence needs a fact of its own in its words.** The composer drops a context sentence whose fact texts all appeared earlier. A sentence that names only the opponent after "{name} beat {opponent}." is taken for a repeat, so give its flag a text the sentence says ("concede"), not "yes" (#7151's follow-up, the concession in the race story).
- **A fact no piece reads is no fact.** Before counting on a builder's fact for a story's body, find a piece with its slot. The parent's death gave `relation` and `survivor`, no piece read either, and archive-words failed in three of five worlds (#7131).
- **Never print the outside account as an employer.** "Buyers and sellers outside the town" (an organization with `outsideFlow`) is the market's stand-in for work and trade out of town: say the person worked outside the town.
- **A government's treasury is not an employer either.** A public worker's work record names the organization `organization:treasury:<government>` (kind `government-treasury`, named "Arizona treasury"). Say the government they worked for, "the government of Arizona" or "the federal government", read from the budgets engine's governments row; a printed obituary said "had worked for Arizona treasury" (#7022's follow-up).
- **Briefs** (owner, Oct 29): the paper's side column carries short local stories for realism (a car stolen, a business opening, a student's win). A brief is a story kind marked `brief: true`, with a headline and one line only, not clickable. It comes from records the world keeps, chosen by the one press desk (`src/core2/press-desk.ts`, the Words session's per the CTO, Oct 29): by the outlet's space, the record's reach and its recency, never a roll.
