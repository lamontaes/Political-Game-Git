My fellow citizens, it is good to be with you today, and better to say that I think the best of this country is still ahead of us. I stand before you more hopeful about this country than I have been in a long while. Paychecks have grown faster than prices this year, and that is real progress for working people. The economy has grown this year, and the question now is whether that growth reaches the people who do the work. Not everyone has shared in it. Work has grown harder to find, and more Americans are looking for it than a year ago. Hope is not the same as being satisfied. It is the belief that next year can be better than this one, and the willingness to work for it. Every American who wants to work should be able to find it, and until that is true, it is the first thing I will answer for.

This country has never been made great in this building. It is made great every day by people who will never stand on this platform.

A new day is beginning, and I would like us to meet it with a little more faith than we have lately allowed ourselves. I took the oath with a light heart, because I believe this country is capable of a good deal more than it has been asked to do lately.

I believe free people, given room, build things no plan could have imagined, and I am eager to see what they build. I promise to govern for all of you, in the hope that someday many of you will say I earned the right.

To the members of Congress: the people sent us here together, and they will judge us together. I mean to work with you, and I will come to you often. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

I would also be plain about what I will not do. As federal law stands, the answer on legal cannabis sales is no. Bring me a bill on legal cannabis sales and you will have my veto. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

Vice President Allison Molina has my trust, and in the years ahead the country will come to know why. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

To the governors and legislators of every state: much of what this country needs will be built in your capitals, not this one. I will be your partner. GOVERNOR LINDA MOORE · STATE REPRESENTATIVE MARK GONZALES · STATE SENATOR LAWRENCE GOODWIN

The best of this country is still ahead of it. I can see it from here, and I hope you can too.

To the families doing better, I want it to last, and to any who are not, I will not forget you. The road ahead is long, and we will not walk all of it in my time. But we will walk it together, and we begin today. God bless you, and God bless the United States of America.
