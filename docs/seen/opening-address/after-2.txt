Chief Justice, members of Congress, honored guests, I thank Vice President Allison Molina, and I begin this term with real hope for what this country can do. In the year now behind us, take-home pay moved in the right direction, and that is a credit to the people who did the work. From Montana to the coasts, wages have climbed faster than prices. Let me state it directly: the country produced more than it did a year ago, as the economy grew steadily. Consider where we stand: the number of people out of work has still climbed slightly since a year ago. The people of Montana and every other state have shown they can do hard things, and inflation is the next one. My commitment is this: unemployment rose slightly, and I will not rest until more people are back at work.

Across this country, I heard about take-home pay from people who had seen it in their own towns and in their own work.

Vice President Allison Molina and I are here to share our hope for the years ahead and to ask for your help in making it real. As I stand here, the oath I just took is a beginning, and I take it with real hope for what this country can do on inflation and much else.

Here is what I believe: the good news on take-home pay belongs to free people and free enterprise, and the more room we give them, the more of it we will see. Above all, Vice President Allison Molina and I see those who voted against us as fellow citizens with the same hopes, and we mean to earn their trust.

To the House and the Senate, Speaker Cindy Chambers, the first matter I will bring before this House is a plan on inflation. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

Here is what I intend: where legal cannabis sales is concerned, I have drawn a line, and I will not cross it. Let me state the facts: the law of the United States makes no provision for legal cannabis sales at present. Let me be plain: on legal cannabis sales, my answer is no, and I am prepared to veto it to keep it that way. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

Allow me a word of gratitude: Vice President Allison Molina will be a full partner in this work, and I would accept nothing less. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

To the governors of every state, Montana taught me what a state can do when its leaders act without waiting for permission. GOVERNOR LINDA MOORE · STATE REPRESENTATIVE MARK GONZALES · STATE SENATOR LAWRENCE GOODWIN

Here is my hope: a young person in Montana or anywhere else will grow up believing their best years are ahead of them.

To those making hard choices at home, the gains on take-home pay mean little until they reach your household, and I intend to see that they do. Finally, I thank the people of Montana, who set me on this road, and every American I now serve.
