Chief Justice, members of Congress, honored guests, I thank Vice President Allison Molina, who will work with me, and I welcome the chance to serve. In the year now behind us, progress on wage growth reached kitchen tables in Montana and well beyond. Across the country, wages climbed steadily, giving working families real ground over prices. Americans from Montana to the coasts produced more than they did a year earlier. Still, the jobless rate moved up somewhat, and that matters. The progress on wage growth shows what this country can do, and we can bring that same effort to sticker shock. Finding work is the work I will answer for first.

Across this country, the questions about sticker shock came from every kind of family I met.

To the House and the Senate, I will need your help, Speaker Cindy Chambers, to move this agenda.

On one matter I will hold firm, I will ask Congress to set legal cannabis sales aside.

I will ask Vice President Allison Molina for honest advice, and I will listen. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

In closing, may the good news on wage growth be the first of many, and God bless you.
