Chief Justice, members of Congress, honored guests, I thank Speaker Cindy Chambers, and I expect much from this Congress. In the year now behind us, this was a year when take-home pay gave families something to build on. Across the country, from Montana to the coasts, wages have climbed faster than prices. The country produced more than it did a year ago, as the economy grew steadily. Work is still harder to come by, in Montana and elsewhere. We start the next stretch of work with take-home pay going our way. I will measure every plan I sign against finding work.

Across this country, the people I met spoke most often of inflation, and I have not forgotten what they told me.

Vice President Allison Molina will work with me on the good years ahead. The oath I just took is a beginning, and I take it with real hope for what this country can do on inflation and much else.

Lower taxes and fewer rules will carry the gains on take-home pay further. If you voted against me, the work on inflation is yours too.

To the House and the Senate, Speaker Cindy Chambers, the first matter I will bring before this House is a plan on inflation. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

Where I will not bend, legal cannabis sales is not a step I can support. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

Vice President Allison Molina will work with me on every hard choice. VICE PRESIDENT ALLISON MOLINA · U.S. REPRESENTATIVE MIKE LANE · U.S. SENATOR SUSAN WOOTEN · U.S. SENATOR KELLY SIMPSON

To the governors of every state, where your states have made progress on take-home pay, I want to learn from it and build on it. GOVERNOR LINDA MOORE · STATE REPRESENTATIVE MARK GONZALES · STATE SENATOR LAWRENCE GOODWIN

The good news on take-home pay will be the start of something larger, and every family will have a share in it.

To every household, I will measure take-home pay by what it does for your family. In closing, let us carry take-home pay forward together, and may God bless you.
