West Vero Corridor, Florida, seed w8s-7, Dennis Guzman

Distinguished guests, my fellow Americans, with Vice President Heidi Long here, I mean to spend this moment on plans rather than promises. I am here to ask Congress for action on grocery inflation. I took this oath as Alaska taught me, meaning to keep it.

From the first stop to the last, parents asked me what grocery inflation will mean for their children.

Over the past year, from Alaska to every other state, rising pay gave us a footing. Across the country, earnings improved considerably, which means raises that prices did not cancel out. But the jobless rate rose marginally, and that is where we begin. I will sign no budget that ignores finding employment.

The progress on rising pay should be protected by keeping taxes low and spending honest, not spent on new programs we cannot afford.

First, I will ask the leaders of both chambers to schedule a vote to ban members of Congress from trading stocks.

Next, I will press Congress to pay for raising the debt limit until it does.

And third, the Congress should raise tariffs, and I stand ready to work with any member toward that end.

Where I will not bend, I urge Congress to reject forgiving student loans.

I will ask Vice President Heidi Long to tell me when I am wrong.

To every state capitol, I will ask your offices to report to mine on a ban on stock trading by members of Congress.

Finally, thank you, and may we meet again with better news on grocery inflation.
