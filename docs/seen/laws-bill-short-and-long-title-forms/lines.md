# Act short title on the bill paper: lines as composed

Words lead ruling (Nov 14): the long title is dropped; an act reads heading, short title, sections. Each bill below shows its enacting words from the body's data row and the name billTitleOf returns (the same function the Journal, office screens and the bill screens call), which the short title quotes.

Three drawn places, 250 days of the played world from 2026-10-12, composed through billForm and composeBill. Before (train) is the same text without the short-title line. Text only, no screen pictures.
