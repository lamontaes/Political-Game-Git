# Your staff on the office screen

Delta, Louisiana, seed d4fa7fd5-1815-4859-98f8-7003d7f56523 (a new game stepping in as the Mayor from the Advanced start), 1440x900, Politics, Your office.

- `your-staff.png`: "Your staff" lists the staff engine's candidates, each with the reasons it weighs, and a Hire button.
- `after-hire.png`: after Hire, the person serves as "Aide" under Your staff and leaves the candidates; each candidate is listed once.

Governors' offices in the draws tried showed no staff section (the office view lists staff only for a post with a job record); not changed here.
