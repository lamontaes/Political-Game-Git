# People in a talk, and who is here: mock-ups for the owner

Mock-ups, not built. Each picture is the running game at 1440 by 900 in Fort Duchesne, Utah (seed `4d7f9a5b-4255-42f4-9fd1-95750e09d9a1`) with a state forced in the page. The busts are round 2 pieces, so the faces are stand-ins; the names are the player's own household.

The owner, Nov 10: "Remove all the people. And yes, I think the speaker as a large bust... Maybe people can be on top of the text box and, you know, the picture of their portrait can resize based on how many people there are, and maybe, you know, get a little bigger when they're talking or something. And I think there needs to be ... some kind of screen or mock-up or something to see who's in the room, I guess, when you enter a place if we're not going to have people."
