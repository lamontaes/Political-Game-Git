# Bill paper: repeal headings and enacting words, lines as composed

Words lead findings (Nov 14): a mixed bill's heading stated the opposite of its repeal section, and enacting words joined their section badly ("Ohio. That", "assembled That", "as follows That"). That ...". Text only, no screen pictures.
