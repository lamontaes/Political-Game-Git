3. The player filed at the clerk's counter (the filing visit's choose route). The platform had a stance on each of the 71 platform questions: odd-numbered questions for, even-numbered against. Races refused on age were passed by.
4. The filing's own scene (at home, the next morning) was played live through Day. It is not pictured.
