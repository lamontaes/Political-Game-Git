## St. David, Illinois, seed 7febec3d252cef05

**Wednesday, January 22, 2025**

_First thing on January 22nd finds you at home. Last night you watched Governor Lori Lopez's State of the State address on WMNH-TV._

> WMNH-TV: Lawmakers of Illinois, and all who call this state home. This past year, collective bargaining for public workers took effect, and people across the state are already living under it.

> WMNH-TV: Why would we change course, when workers who get the contract chip in for what it costs? Because I hold that the right to organize built the middle class in this country, I am against right-to-work rules, and if the legislature passes them, I will veto them.

> WMNH-TV: I have always believed a worker standing alone has a lot less say than workers standing together. Teachers and other public workers have a seat at the table. I will keep collective bargaining for public workers, and any bill in this legislature to undo it will meet my veto.

> WMNH-TV: On unions, let us say no to the rules. Thank you, and good night.

**Tuesday, February 11, 2025**

_First thing on February 11th, you stay in. Last night you watched Mayor Sara Mullen's yearly address on WMNH-TV._

> WMNH-TV: Members of the board, and neighbors. Tonight I want to talk with the people of St. David about how we keep people safe and treat them fairly. For 2024, we are taking in more than we spend here, and I mean to keep it so.

> WMNH-TV: I believe an officer should be judged by people who have done the job. As things stand, the department reviews complaints against its own officers. I will oppose any ordinance to give civilians oversight of the police.

> WMNH-TV: I am for a bigger police budget, and I ask the board to give the police department more to work with this year. That is because I believe people who break the law should answer for it. At this moment, calls for help wait longer than they should for an officer.

> WMNH-TV: As we stand here, the jail runs short of guards on most shifts. Because I believe the first job of government is to keep people safe, I will ask the board to staff the jail fully, and I will not let it rest.

> WMNH-TV: I ask the board to say no to civilian oversight of the police this year. Thank you.

## Olympia Heights, Florida, seed bbe1df4166577b65

**Wednesday, January 22, 2025**

_First thing on January 22nd finds you at home. Last night you watched Governor Sally O'Connor's State of the State address on WJQC-TV._

> WJQC-TV: Lawmakers of Florida, and all who call this state home. I am against letting cities set their own minimum wage, and if the legislature passes it, I will veto it. That is because I believe a business or a family should know the rules from one place to the next. As we stand here, one minimum wage covers the whole state, wherever a shop opens.

> WJQC-TV: I believe some rules ought to be the same no matter where you live. As we meet tonight, every town makes its own rules, and they change at the town line. I will sign a repeal of the law giving towns more power when the legislature sends it.

> WJQC-TV: On local rules, let us say no to letting cities set their own minimum wage. Thank you, and good night.

**Tuesday, February 11, 2025**

_First thing on February 11th, you stay in. Last night you watched Chair Courtney Murray's yearly address on WJQC-TV._

> WJQC-TV: Members of the board, my fellow citizens. I have come to speak to Miami-Dade County about how we help people through hard times, because it bears on every family here. For 2024, we are taking in more than we spend here, and I mean to keep it so.

> WJQC-TV: I have always believed the best way out of hard times is a job. As matters stand, too few of the dollars spent on assistance lead to a job. I am against more assistance spending, and I will ask the board to vote it down.

> WJQC-TV: I ask you to tie more of our aid to getting back to work, because aid should lead back to a paycheck. Thank you, and good night.

## Twin Lakes, Minnesota, seed 6d38f6639bfb0096

**Wednesday, January 22, 2025**

_First thing on January 22nd finds you at home. Last night you watched Governor Christopher Stephenson's State of the State address on KMSZ-TV._

> KMSZ-TV: Members of the legislature, and citizens of Minnesota. Should we go on like this, when our police don't work with federal immigration agents? Because I believe our laws mean something only if we enforce them, I will ask the legislature to partner with federal immigration enforcement, and I will not let it rest.

> KMSZ-TV: On immigration, let us partner with federal immigration enforcement. Thank you, and good night.

**Tuesday, February 11, 2025**

_First thing on February 11th, you stay in. Last night you watched Chair Andre Mason's yearly address on KMSZ-TV._

> KMSZ-TV: Members of the board, and my fellow citizens. I have come to speak to Little Elbow Township about what health care costs, because it bears on every family here. For 2025, we are spending more than we take in, and I won't hand that bill to whoever sits here next.

> KMSZ-TV: I have always believed a family shouldn't go broke because somebody got sick. As things stand, the clinic is full, and folks without insurance wait the longest. I am for a bigger health budget, and I ask the board to fund the clinic and the hospital this year.

> KMSZ-TV: I ask you to fund the clinic and the hospital, because nobody should have to drive an hour to see a doctor. Thank you, and good night.
