# Busts on the talk box and who's here, second mock-ups (Nov 11, 2026)

Mock-ups for the owner's answers to calls 8 and 9 (Nov 10, about 11:08 p.m. ET). Nothing here is built. Each picture is the running game at 1440x900 with a state forced in the page; `make-these.mjs.txt` is the script.

His words:

- Call 8, people on top of the talk box: "it's a little too big I think and I'd like to see this in multiple different scenes."
- Call 9, who's here (he picked the busts along the bottom of the room): "it just looks like they're thrown in there. I'd like to see some way to delineate them. Also similar with a chat box."

What changed from the first mock-ups (`docs/seen/talk-and-whos-here`):

- The busts are smaller: about 200 pixels for one person, 180 for two and 150 for four. They were 330, 270 and 190.
- A soft glass strip holds them. It has no box: its ends fade into the room and a light runs along its top and fades before the ends. The names are set on the strip.
- In a talk the same strip is joined to the top of the talk box, so the people and the box read as one thing. The speaker is lit and a little larger; the others are dimmed. The small round face in the talk box's header is gone.
- A crowd shows the people you would know by name, then "and 412 more".

The faces are round 3's busts.
