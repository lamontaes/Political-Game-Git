# Visit and Call on a person's card: lines as played

Instruction: the Account lead's assignment of Nov 14, 2026, 07:06Z (no owner quote for this exact ask; it comes from the promises, family-life and eviction-talk threads, which each found that "I'll come by" and "I'll call" have no act that keeps them). Owner's Nov 14 06:38Z rule applied: plain American English, no jargon.

Every new string, all in `src/bench/data/controls.json` under `person`:

- Section heading: **Reach out**
- Buttons: **Visit**, **Call**
- Place name after a home visit: **{name}'s home** (workplaces show their own name)
- Reasons an act is closed, shown as "Visit: ..." or "Call: ..." under the buttons:
  - here: They are here with you.
  - elsewhere: They live too far away to visit.
  - away: You can't get to where they are right now.
  - held: Finish what you're in the middle of first.
  - silent: They have nothing to say right now.
  - gone: They're gone.

No bank lines changed. The talk that opens uses the existing greetings, so a call opens as a visit does ("Good to see you"); call-specific openers are the Words lead's to write.
