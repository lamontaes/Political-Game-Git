## Meaning for engineering teams and remaining gaps

Measured collector limit: the nine receipts observe canonical stamps in crisis records and, in some worlds, town business books. Unobserved stores do not establish absent effects. A completed payment without a matched service event remains a downstream delivery gap. Reader-present without an attributable record remains a trace investigation, not a diagnosis of its cause.

Required team handoff: connect each new saved consequence to its canonical law, operative date, actual person or place, prior/after value and source record IDs. For transit, retain payment authority and transfer lineage, then identify delivered service and riders separately. For groundwater, preserve real allocation/withdrawal records rather than treating an aggregate projection as delivered water conservation. Current engineering teams own these producer changes; Audit/Systems continues the single campaign and trace diagnosis.

This is the first partial, not final nationwide coverage. Forty-seven selected worlds were unsealed at this snapshot. No browser, scene, full-suite or visual acceptance is claimed. Final totals and explanations will be recomputed from sealed inputs as the same campaign completes.
