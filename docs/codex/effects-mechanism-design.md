## One recorded chain, distinct beneficial and adverse channels

Proposed contract: the domain producer reads the operative law's saved term, attempts an actual activity and records what occurred. Its record cites the governing law stamp, source activity/payment IDs, actual exposure, capacity/budget basis, jurisdiction and date. An outcome reader consumes that delivered exposure. It does not independently create the service or pay the same money again.

For diminishing returns, record attempted units, delivered units, remaining reachable need and the binding constraint. Units stop contributing when no eligible recipient or usable capacity remains. Whole-unit restoration may be discrete because a vehicle is a whole unit; it is not a discretionary actor decision selected by an authored threshold.

For negative returns, save a second cause chain with its own actual exposure and outcome. More staff may improve service while actual overcrowding, maintenance delays or conduct complaints worsen another channel. Neither channel requires the other to precede it. No common turning point, timing or inverted-U equation is proposed.

Measured existing arithmetic uses the earlier operational count and limits restored units to both idle units and affordable declared restoration at governing/public-program.ts:1166. That is a capacity ceiling from records. Its declared unit cost may still be an authored fixture; production cost requires actual inputs or a sourced term.

Required integration: match unit, date, jurisdiction and affected population before composition. A statewide rate cannot use a watched-town numerator. Research calibrates supported response strength and measured turning points; a real capacity limit is not fitted from a generic research range. Missing workload, denominator or unit cost remains an explicit contract gap.
