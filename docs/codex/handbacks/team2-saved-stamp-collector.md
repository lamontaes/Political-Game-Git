# Nested stamped costs were outside the audit reader

Before: The audit read sequenced history arrays. Saved business privacy costs and budget months could carry producer stamps that the reader never observed.

After: The collector walks every saved object carrying an array of law stamps. It validates the canonical law and application date, preserves named records and dates, and deduplicates repeated stamps and flat aliases. Starting-law observations have their own source and no invented enactment. An empty observation never proves an absent effect.

The collector is HARDWIRED to accept only the exact canonical law key, source and operative date at application. It rejects wrong keys and dates. It reads arbitrary nested paycheck and transfer fixtures without requiring a history sequence. No actor decision or downstream delivery is inferred.

## Wider effects and missing links

Starting-law costs are separately labeled `source=starting`. Their counts do not enter enacted-law totals. A stamp without a consequence payload stays unproven. A law enacted before the observation window can still have a recorded consequence inside it.

## Why-chain

Saved record → canonical stamp → law in force at application → exact record locator and saved payload. This terminates at the recorded mechanism. Aggregate cash, revenue and cost fields are retained as named values; they are not summed into a supposed total law-caused cost.
