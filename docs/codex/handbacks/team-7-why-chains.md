## 1. Why-chain from the code

The placement reader excludes the player, gives scene participants grouped or raised seats first, and assigns workers remaining spots. Missing staging returns nobody; excess people are omitted from view. Seat assignment bottoms out in an authored ordering, not each person's recorded seat choice (`src/presentation/backdrop-people.ts`, placeBackdropPeople).

A grouped seat becomes desk activity because the spot has a group. Desk activity becomes reading or writing through a stable seed draw. Waiting can become a phone pose through the same mechanism. **Finding: stand-in.** This path does not establish a book, document, phone, task or personal decision. Comfort, mobility needs, relationships and attention are absent from this choice (`src/presentation/appearance-engine/pose-chooser.ts:119`).
