# Eviction ends a home, but the world does not yet choose where the family goes

A family can lose its tenancy without getting a same-day destination. Population research supports town counts, but many household, job, rent, and election behaviors still stop at authored rules. The correction replaces the exhaustive numeric assignment with these causal findings. Existing receipts remain preserved. The eviction draft is held for the owner's morning review.

## 2. Source research

The primary author-hosted [Collinson et al. paper](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w30382/w30382.pdf), revised July 2023, was read at its introduction and residential-mobility/shelter results. The published QJE identifier is `10.1093/qje/qjad042`. Its judge-assignment design measures incremental effects among court-filing tenants. The first-year clock is case filing. The 3.4-point shelter and eight-point new-address effects are provisional cohort checks, not absolute shelter quotas or outcome draws. Team 9 owns broader corroboration.
