# Education and civil laws have several effects that stop short of people

The first source audit covers eleven policy questions. Eight counted paths end
in aggregate school outcomes; two questions have no counted path. Preschool's
employment effect and abortion restrictions' infant-mortality effect have no
producer. Births still use a seeded outcome draw. Teacher pay, tuition growth
and nondiscrimination pay use fixed sizes. These findings need research and
coordinated repairs before any claim that every supported effect works.

## Why-chain

Measured at main `54930d427555034f3a92f335064586247985784d`: enacting a
measure supplies proposition answers. The law reader resolves operative dates,
authority and rank. The outcome reader subtracts the opening baseline and
applies delayed link sizes. The monthly writer records aggregate measures.
This chain bottoms out in a multiplier on a place's outcome, rather than a
student's recorded decision or school resources.
Evidence: `src/simulation/outcome-web/place-outcomes.ts:advancePlaceOutcomes`.

The separate birth chain reaches `reviewTownFamilies`, where the multiplier
changes a chance and `rng.fork("child").next()` selects a birth. That is a
measured seeded actor-outcome draw, not permission to keep it. The teacher and
fairness chains reach money-flow terms, but their amounts use fixed factors.
The press chain reaches accurate, high-confidence event knowledge for all
selected readers, without a saved attention decision.

## Research

New primary read: [20 U.S.C. 7907](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7907&num=0&edition=prelim),
HTTP 200. The section prohibits officers or employees of the federal government
from using this chapter to mandate, direct or control state, local educational
agency or school curriculum. It also prohibits Department funds under this
chapter from endorsing or requiring curriculum. This is a specific federal
constraint, not proof of every jurisdiction's curriculum authority.

The attempted EEOC sexual-orientation guidance URL returned HTTP 404; no new
employment-floor evidence is claimed from that read. Existing code cites Burn,
Gilpin/Karger/Nencka, Dench/Pineda-Torres/Myers, Gemmill and school studies.
Their encoded estimates below are source observations, not newly verified
research. Every supported real effect cannot yet be certified from this packet.
Team 9 breadth, primary effect sources and subject-specific jurisdiction law
remain dependencies. No new rate or range was invented.

## Revisions and authority limits

All six education questions and the four statewide civil/library questions
are mapped to state reach. D.C. reads state/county/city reach; territories read
state reach but their powers cells remain unresolved. The local fairness
question maps to county and city. Township has no distinct powers column;
`computeLevels` sends known kinds not identified as counties or consolidated
governments to city. That is source behavior, not verified township authority.

The powers catalog leaves territory powers and many local subject preemptions
unresolved. `mayAnswerQuestion` permits an `unknown` verdict because it rejects
only `no`. This must not be reported as researched legal permission. Blanket
home-rule status cannot establish every local subject's authority. Federal
grant and civil-rights floors must remain separate from statewide program
questions; copying a state question to Congress would change its meaning.

`lawInForce` selects later operative answers at each level and resolves rank.
Its comment explicitly retains blanket rank for enacted floor preemption.
`outcomeFactor` stops a delayed law effect immediately when the current answer
returns to baseline. That reversal is source-verified, not a watched repeal
test. It also cannot prove that past completed schooling or birth outcomes
should reverse. Each trace below retains the distinction.
