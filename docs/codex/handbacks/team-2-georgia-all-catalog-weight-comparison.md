# Weighted law arguments changed 76 Georgia House decisions

After all catalog batches landed, 76 member decisions changed from present to yes. No vote switched between yes and no. The comparison held each bill, person and world constant. Cameron Salas moved from present to yes on legislative term limits because the weighted arguments stopped canceling each other. This is a measured comparison through the production chamber decision reader. The bills are explicitly authored development probes. No generated House bill, recorded floor vote or passage is claimed.

## 1. Why-chain

Cameron's decision changed because the chamber reader found a reason for voting yes. Why did it find that reason? The weighted principle score became positive. Why was the unweighted score zero? His two cited principle contributions canceled at equal argument weights. Why did weighting break that tie? The law assigns different importance to the supporting and opposing arguments. Why does that affect Cameron? The consumer multiplies those weights by his own recorded stances and continuous strengths.

The terminal is a person's saved principle records and explicitly authored argument weights. Principle formation sizes, score scale and decision thresholds remain authored stand-ins. The comparison does not establish all real legislative influences, including constituent requests, donors, groups, party leadership and news. It adds no convictions to force a changed result.

## 2. Research

The 509 catalog argument weights are authored relevance choices, not empirical estimates of votes. Their catalog batches cite the political positions that ground each argument. This measurement tests the actual merged catalog and decision consumer together. It does not validate those weights against real Georgia voting records.

All 92 questions had weighted argument rows in this build. The existing authority reader admitted 68 questions at the state level. Those 68 questions supplied the probes. The receipt lists all 92 canonical keys and 509 arguments, along with the exact 68-question state coverage.

## 3. Revisions

One arm preserves catalog weights; the control sets them to one. Both arms share the same people, principle history, bills, date and chamber inputs. Principle formation runs once before the arms separate. The evaluator and procedural thresholds are identical.

No revenue, spending, price, rate or cost level is drawn by this runner. It measures decisions. Research outcome ranges do not supply an invented outcome. Its seed belongs to the existing world-opening mechanism. The run does not certify that every mechanism in that opening is researched.
