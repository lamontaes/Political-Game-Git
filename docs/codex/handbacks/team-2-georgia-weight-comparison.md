# Georgia House votes kept their direction when argument weights changed

The paired comparison changed 810 principle scores across 1,620 member decisions. No vote changed direction, and no member changed disposition. Cameron Salas opposed Medicaid expansion in both versions, with a weaker negative score after weighting. The build contained weighted arguments for nine health and technology questions. No House bills were generated during the seven-day observation. Nine clearly labeled development probes supplied the questions for this bounded comparison. These results establish that the merged consumer reads weights. They do not establish a changed recorded vote, passage, or a nationwide outcome.

## 1. Why-chain

A chamber member decides from their recorded positions, principles, commitments and other available cues. Why can an argument weight change that decision? The chamber evaluator reads the principle consideration. Why does that consideration change? Each law argument multiplies the person's saved continuous strength by its catalog weight. Why did the measured scores change without changing votes? The signed contributions retained their direction in these comparisons. Why did Cameron's direction stay negative? His single cited principle contribution remained negative after multiplication by a positive weight.

The terminal is the person's recorded stance and strength, with an authored argument weight. The formation weights, score scale and importance thresholds remain authored stand-ins. Constituents, donors, groups, party leaders, news and reintroductions are not established by this comparison. No conviction was invented to force a flip.

## 2. Research

This is a source and decision measurement, not an empirical estimate of legislators' voting behavior. Catalog weights are authored design choices grounded in the catalog's cited positions. The opening uses the existing Georgia chamber, with 180 simulated members. The paired comparison uses all of them.

Only nine questions and 47 weighted argument rows were present in the pinned production build. The raw receipt lists every canonical question, bearing and weight. Later catalog batches are outside this result.

## 3. Revisions

The control changes every argument weight to one. The measured arm preserves the catalog weights. Both arms share the same people, history, bills, date and voter inputs. Each uses the actual chamber decision reader. The measurement also records principle scores separately from final dispositions.

This comparison adds no policy effect sizes, tax rates, person convictions, thresholds or draws. A zero flip count is an observed result for this sample. It does not imply that weights can never change a vote. The existing focused consumer checks separately demonstrate a flip with competing authored arguments.
