# Fairness pay uses a stable research range and states its limits

The existing pay mechanism now uses a stable world/state wage ratio instead
of one universal gap. Pay notes show the actual reduction after the minimum
wage floor. Missing job-place records leave pay unchanged. This repairs the
mechanism's units and variation; federal coverage and the study's applicability
after Bostock remain unresolved.

## 1. Why-chain

A hire's pay differs because the existing reader applies a wage share. The
reader selects the existing recorded partnership cohort. It reads protection
where the job is, through the state and town law readers. The wage share comes
from the world seed, state ID and research interval. The interval comes from
Burn's estimated log-hourly wage coefficient and standard error. The chain
bottom is a researched aggregate association translated into a hardwired pay
mechanism; an employer decision and federal coverage are missing world pieces.
Evidence: `src/simulation/fairness-pay-law.ts:uncoveredPayShareAt`.

## 2. Research

Burn (2018), Journal of Labor Research, Table 3 reports a log-hourly-wage
coefficient of 0.027 and state-clustered standard error of 0.012 for men in
same-sex cohabiting couples. The normal 95% interval is 0.00348 to 0.05052
log points. Wage ratios use the exponential, not one plus the coefficient.
The uncovered share therefore lies between exp(-0.05052) and exp(-0.00348).

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12122-018-9272-0.

The sample uses the 1990/2000 Census and 2008–2014 ACS, ages 22–65.
This is sampling uncertainty, not measured state heterogeneity. State-law
estimates do not establish a municipal effect or the incremental effect after

for its hourly-wage estimate. No new effect for women is asserted.

## 3. Revisions

The fixed coefficient becomes one stable world/state parameter. Towns in the
same state share it; serialization reproduces it. Seedless fixtures retain
the central log coefficient. A missing canonical job-place record causes no
pay reduction. Federal Title VII coverage needs employer and employment-history
records; absence of a state law does not prove discrimination is lawful.

## 7. Worked example

The player-shift test pays the same recorded four-hour shop shift in both law
branches. The uncovered amount is the offered minor-unit amount times the
world/state share, rounded to cents and bounded by the same four-hour minimum
wage. The covered amount equals the offered amount. Exact actor names and
month-by-month paid transfers were not captured by this check and are not
invented here. The source's mathematical comparison is a check, not a full
worked life or nationwide example.
