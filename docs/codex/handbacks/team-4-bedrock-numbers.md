## Wider knock-on effects and missing links

The source paths connect fictional residence dates to qualification, intake dates to trait/decision records, candidate decisions to filed fields, fallback seat lean to winners, and work demand to scheduling. Those are source connections, not new watched outcomes. Replacing an unsupported setting must preserve identity/history and use actual jurisdiction authority or researched nonlegal anchors; this inventory authorizes no replacement.

Numbers in the imported qualification catalogs, nomination plans, candidate decision writer, national mood shift and ballot counter are dependencies, not silently treated as research. Their owner must report their own strengths, sizes and dates. Team3's outcome reader seams are excluded. The complete Team4 ledger remains open until the remaining released-area boundaries are reconciled with central ownership.
