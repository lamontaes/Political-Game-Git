The conversion covers all fourteen assigned laws. Two disabled cost proposals use existing rural-transit and fare-relief payment evidence. No row is admitted to run because the supplied contract does not name the required registry bindings. The remaining effects retain their exact missing records and terms. Existing stamps remain intact. The mileage monthly caller request is superseded.

## 1. Why-chain

An authorized commitment fixes an installment plan. A completed cash outcome must match its amount and currency. The existing government outlay records that real payment. Its cost stamp preserves the operative measure and payment chain. This chain ends at saved cash bookkeeping. It does not establish driver work, fuel purchases, delivered hours or a person's ride. The shared engine must resolve those actual inputs before it can follow the chain.

## 2. Research

This conversion uses the existing law traces and published payment receipts. It adds no study estimate, coefficient, price or outcome level. The preserved Ohio fare-relief receipt records 266,666,666 USD minor units paid after its canonical operative date. That amount stays receipt evidence; it is not a constant in a consequence expression. NTD operating-cost observations remain calibration only.

## 3. Revisions

The old effect list's payment-derived hours were removed from the conversion. The golden-rule repair already removed them from production. The mileage caller patch remains historical and must not be applied under the new contract. Unsupported binding names cannot become runnable merely because they fit a string field in the shared type. Candidate source references stay disabled in the separate missing-capabilities list.

## 4. What gets built

1. A question-keyed consequence input covers the fourteen canonical assigned laws. Its runnable lists are empty until registry admission.
2. The separate missing-capabilities list includes two disabled proposals with the exact shared row shape. They use price-cost, preserve-completed and actual completed-payment minor units. Their selector, writer and field strings identify existing source references for binding, not registered engine capabilities.
3. Each of the fourteen entries retains known inputs, supported effect requirements and exact missing terms, units, selectors, actions or actual records.
4. A reproducible check validates canonical coverage and disabled admission, checks the published shared type's source hash, and typechecks the input and proposals against that exact type without writing a shared production file.

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

SIMULATED: No new decision, payment, trip or person consequence is produced.

RECORDS: Existing measure, appropriation, commitment, installment, flow, outcome and budget identities are retained as input requirements. The shared migration must preserve completed history and idempotence without running both old and new cost paths.

WORLD PIECES: Exact transit cash-cost evidence exists. Admitted cost selector/action/input/guard keys and the appropriate payment activity contract remain dependencies. Actual service and person exposure remain separate missing records. Other laws need the filed terms and real covered bases listed in their entries.

CHECKS: Empty runnable lists mean missing admission, not zero world effects. No service row or onward exposure row is fabricated. Source field references in disabled proposals must never be interpreted as executable object paths.
