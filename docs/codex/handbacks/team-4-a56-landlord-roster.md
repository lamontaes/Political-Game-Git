# First landlords follow recorded ownership and the home roster

First leases now use the home's recorded person or organization owner. Homes without recorded ownership receive the existing ownership mixture across their saved roster, instead of a landlord draw. Five places passed the first-lease checks and the existing anniversary checks. Household owners and ambiguous co-owners still need a recipient contract; they receive no invented landlord proxy.

## 1. Why-chain

First landlords changed when the old landlord fork changed. That happened because startTownLeases picked an ownership category from the home's seed. The category chose a real actor, but the actor did not have to match recorded legal ownership. Recorded ownership was ignored because the lease writer never read ownership tenures. The chain ended at a seeded category draw, a finding.

The replacement reads active ownership at the lease date in src/simulation/living-world/town-rent.ts:1206. A unique living person or open organization is the actual recipient. Where no title exists, landlordKindsByHome spreads the existing mixture across actual homes at src/simulation/living-world/town-rent.ts:1156. The terminal is recorded legal ownership or an explicitly retained representative profile. This is bookkeeping, not a landlord's decision.
