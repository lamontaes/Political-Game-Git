# Curriculum purchases now follow the pupils recorded for that month

An enrollment entered later could previously add a curriculum charge to an earlier month. The bounded repair excludes it and keeps overlapping enrollments from charging for the same pupil twice. The reader also exposes the governing law and enrolled pupils when a yes/no law lacks purchase terms. Such a law remains visible; its missing purchase price does not establish that every curriculum effect is inactive. Monthly law stamps and watched education effects still need integration.

## Why-chain

The held curriculum caller adds the purchase total to state school spending. The total comes from active schooling enrollments and their dated school locations. The old reader checked the enrollment start date, but not when that enrollment became known. A later-known enrollment could therefore change an earlier month's purchase. The new reader checks the recorded date and history sequence before counting it. The terminal is an actual enrollment/state/profile record, followed by the governing bill's filed purchase terms. No amount is inferred from a plain yes/no answer.

This fixes purchase bookkeeping. It does not create adopted standards, distribute materials, train teachers, or prove students learned differently. Those producers remain separate.

## Research

The received adoption evidence is California's 2013 appropriation of $1.25 billion, described as approximately $200 per pupil for materials, professional development, and technology. Its source is https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/ca/commoncore.asp. The held data also cite https://www.cde.ca.gov/re/cc/ccssfaqs.asp for standards.

The held source contains authored purchase bounds of $100–$400 per pupil and a 12–36 month phase-in. These are not a researched national distribution. This delta neither changes those numbers nor endorses their nationwide use. Team9 owns the breadth and per-world sizing repair. Only the controlling law's already-filed terms are consumed here.

## Revisions

The reader now returns the controlling law, month, pupil IDs, enrollment IDs, school IDs, and the calculated purchase total. Absent terms yield a null total and a recorded limit. The existing spending wrapper retains its zero additional purchase entry for that case. This preserves budget behavior without claiming zero authority or zero supported effects.
