# Separate non-money changes retain their own news records

A later recorded rule or service change could disappear from public news because an earlier section of the same law had already been reported. The repair separates actual non-money sections and preserves their source IDs. It adds no effects, amounts, speech, people, or legal authority. Nationwide firing and noticing remain unmeasured.

## 1. Why-chain

1. A second section could lack a public story because its stable news key matched the first section.
2. The keys matched because exposures used step `0` regardless of their recorded section.
3. A sweep suppressed the matching key because history already contained it.
4. The public story also omitted the source and section IDs, leaving downstream causal readers without that connection.
5. The terminal is HARDWIRED grouping and provenance loss in `src/simulation/press/law-effect-news.ts`. The repair reads existing IDs; it does not decide what a person believes.

The source already accepts job-rule, business-rule and public-service exposures. Missing producer records and the non-money reflection path remain separate owner-controlled gaps. Team2's published audit also excludes event records from its news collection pattern and cannot match prefixed law tags through exact-ID equality. That adapter issue is distinct from this reader defect.

## 2. Research

This is a record-preservation repair. No empirical effect size is added. CTO's daily brief requires every supported fired effect to remain traceable through noticing and public news. Existing source records establish the law, section, channel and affected person. The fictional regression fixtures establish reader behavior, not real legal powers or nationwide prevalence.

## 3. Revisions

Money and null-section exposures retain their existing step keys. A non-money exposure with a recorded section uses that section as its step. Repeated exposure to one section remains one story under the existing reporting rule. Existing event rows are immutable. No guessed rates or universal place sizes are introduced.

## 4. Numbered parts

1. Retain source record IDs and section keys while projecting a touch.
2. Group distinct recorded non-money sections separately.
3. Preserve source and section references in the resulting public event tags.
4. Exercise two successive sections for each of three non-money channels in three places.

The only production writer claimed is `src/simulation/press/law-effect-news.ts`; its test is also exclusively released to Team8. Team2 owns the shared law-effect stamp/helper. This patch does not substitute its tags for that forthcoming contract.

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

SIMULATED: no new person decision occurs here. The reader consumes already recorded own exposures.

RECORDS: public events retain their law, section, actual source and involved person IDs. A source without a person produces no touch. A law without a measure produces no story. No absent source effect is manufactured.

WORLD PIECES: exposure producers, journal noticing, close-friend reactions and shared law-effect provenance require their existing owners and contracts. This patch establishes neither a new producer nor a complete noticing chain.

CHECKS: distinct sections must produce distinct stories, no money may appear in the non-money summary, actual affected people remain event readers, and an unchanged repeated sweep writes no duplicate story.

## 6. Proof run

The bounded fictional fixtures reuse three previously logged places: Quantico, Maryland; Rockland, Idaho; and Tab, Indiana. Quantico and Tab are unincorporated opening cases. Seeds use each actual place ID. Each fixture authors one law and records two successive sections through the canonical event and exposure writers for job-rule, business-rule and public-service channels.

Before the repair, all three new cases failed because the first story omitted its recorded section. After the repair, the changed test file passed all 12 tests. The three new cases preserve 18 distinct section stories in total. The nine retained tests cover outcome findings and actual resident readers. Receipt logs remain in ignored `test-results/team8/law-news-before.log` and `law-news-after.log`.

These are controlled reader fixtures. A watched nationwide 24-month run, five random state openings, journal/friend noticing and fired-effects denominator are NOT RUN. The unit counts cannot be substituted for those acceptance measures.

## 7. Worked example

In each fixture, the recorded person's first public-service exposure points to the first authored source event and section `first`. The news event retains both references and includes the affected person as a reader. A later exposure for section `second` points to its own source and produces a second story. Both amounts are null. Repeating the sweep returns the same world without another story. No intervening facts or spoken reaction are inferred.
