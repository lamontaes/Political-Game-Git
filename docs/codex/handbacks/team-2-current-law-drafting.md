# Automatic drafting refuses missing current-law terms

The compiler no longer draws a configuration, appropriation or service choice from bank defaults. It reads a compatible saved governing law and preserves its complete parameters; absent or incompatible terms refuse compilation. The whole filing pipeline remains held pending the producer repair and actual after-proof.

## 1. Why-chain

A sponsor's principles propose a direction. The old compiler chose a registered configuration by seed, multiplied its authored amount by one of five factors, and drew service or other enumerated choices. Why those terms? They came from the bank and dice, not current law. The new read follows the governing enacted measure to its saved configuration, parameters and current clauses. The terminal is that exact saved law record; missing records mean nothing files through this compiler. No new sponsor decision, constituent pressure, donor influence or calibrated actor rate is invented.

## 3. Revisions and breadth

The same rule applies to each supported federal/state/county/municipality configuration. It does not infer territory or township authority. Starting-law rows lacking quantitative/structured terms refuse, even if their answer is known. No range or new effects link is added; legislative amounts are saved bill terms, not causal rates.

Only the current governing measure at the same jurisdiction supplies a single compatible unbundled lineage. Its bank version and all parameters must exist. Amounts are copied exactly, never clamped or rounded. Current saved clauses must match the source configuration; amendments, changed clauses, future records, ambiguous lineages and unsupported values refuse. This deliberately does not reconstruct structured parameters from amendment prose.
