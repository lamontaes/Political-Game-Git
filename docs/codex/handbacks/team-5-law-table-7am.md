## 2. Research

The published eleven-question audit supplies the encoded effect inventory and primary federal/library/abortion reads. The direct-range candidate supersedes only its teacher and tuition midpoint findings. The facility audit supplies three separate six-line traces. Exact published references appear in Method.

New primary read: Ian Burn, 2018, [Not All Laws are Created Equal](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12122-018-9272-0). The PDF is now available; the earlier HTTP 403 finding is superseded for this read. Its Table 3 reports a log-hourly-wage coefficient of 0.027 with a state-clustered standard error of 0.012 for cohabiting gay men. The baseline annual-income, employment, and weekly-hours coefficients are 0.017, 0.005, and 0.196; they are not statistically significant.

Table 4 reports an employment coefficient of -0.017 with standard error 0.007 and weekly-hours coefficient of -0.733 with standard error 0.255 for cohabiting lesbian women. Employment is a linear-probability coefficient, not automatically a relative percentage. The hourly-wage and annual-income coefficients are not statistically significant. These findings establish additional outcomes to trace; nonsignificance does not prove an effect is inert everywhere.

The study uses the 1990 and 2000 Census 5 percent samples and 2008–2014 ACS 1 percent samples. It compares cohabiting same-sex couples with married heterosexual couples, older than 22 and younger than 65. The ACS excludes recent interstate movers. Money is in constant 1999 dollars. Its design is difference-in-difference-in-differences, with legal-provision and full/part-time heterogeneity. This is a state-law study predating Bostock, not a universal municipal or post-2020 causal estimate.

A sampling interval derived from the standard error would not be an empirical per-place effect range. No runtime range is inferred from it. Employer thresholds, complaint periods, damages, and attorney fees matter in the paper; the current pay reader does not consume those legal distinctions.

The primary Chicago Fed working-paper revision in the merged audit reports capital-investment gains of 21 percent in visits, 21 percent in children's checkouts, and 18 percent in children's event attendance. Usage persisted at least ten years; reading rose 0.02 standard deviations over seven years, with no math effect and no local housing-price effect in that study. Its library data cover 1992–2018, with nearby-school analysis using 2009–2018 data. Those are capital-shock results, not a causal operating-floor range. The later published abstract uses different thresholds and estimates; versions remain separate.

### Authority, repeal, preemption, and all supported places

All six education questions and the statewide civil/library questions have existing state reach. D.C. reads state/county/city reach in the audited catalog. Territory power cells and local subject powers remain unresolved; they are not permission. The local fairness question reaches county/city in code. The audited township mapping falls through to city rather than establishing township power. All 50 states, D.C., and the five inhabited territories require real subject-specific authority; no all 56-place certification is made.

[20 U.S.C. 7907](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section7907&num=0&edition=prelim) bars the named federal curriculum control under its chapter. Federal aid is a distinct instrument. [20 U.S.C. 9122](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section9122&num=0&edition=prelim) names all 56 jurisdictions for library assistance; it does not establish a local library board's powers or a state funding floor.

[42 U.S.C. 2000e-2(a)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000e-2&num=0&edition=prelim), [Bostock](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/19pdf/17-1618_hfci.pdf), and [42 U.S.C. 2000e(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000e&num=0&edition=prelim) establish a separate federal employment floor and covered-employer rules. The fifteen-employee test includes twenty calendar weeks, with exclusions; a current staff count is insufficient. [42 U.S.C. 2000e-7](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title42-section2000e-7&num=0&edition=prelim) preserves state/local liabilities subject to its exception. State/local repeal cannot eliminate federal coverage. The statutory State definition names D.C., Puerto Rico, USVI, American Samoa, and Guam; CNMI needs its separate extension authority. Federal employee routes remain separate.

[20 U.S.C. 1412](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1412&num=0&edition=prelim) supplies specific federal disability-assistance conditions, including a free appropriate public education for eligible children aged 3–21 subject to exceptions. An authored repair authorization cannot establish compliance or repeal these requirements.

The aggregate reader's immediate reversal on repeal does not prove past completed schooling should reverse. Teacher repeal removes a future floor without cutting existing terms. Tuition retains past frozen-year gaps. Facility duties remain due after repeal in the inspected generic writer. These are distinct source behaviors; only teacher and tuition candidate checks have runtime proof in this packet.
