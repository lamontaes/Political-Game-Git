# Automatic drafts preserve the law's saved terms

The draft compiler preserves a verified current law's amount and service rule. An explicit sponsor change can replace either value. The starting-law parameter producer and the filing caller still need to supply those inputs. This repair remains a draft until that integration works without silencing ordinary legislation.

## 1. Why-chain

An automatic draft carries its amount because the compiler reads the governing measure's saved parameters. Why that measure? The existing law-in-force reader resolves the exact question and jurisdiction. Why trust its parameters? The compiler checks the lineage, version and current operative clauses. Why can the proposed amount differ? The caller explicitly supplies a sponsor change. Why that change? The sponsor producer must derive it from the person's principles, constituents and budget room. That final producer input is missing here. The terminal is a saved legal record; the proposed personal decision remains a missing contract.

## 2. Research

The owner's golden rule supplies this repair's contract: sponsors write from current law. No empirical rate or public spending level is supplied by this change. Amounts in the focused fixture are authored test inputs. They are not researched costs or observed government payments.

## 3. Revisions

Configuration, amount, service-window and enumerated selection no longer use seeded picks. Verified current values survive unchanged unless the caller supplies a valid change. Missing or stale terms return a refusal. That refusal is an input gap to repair; it is not legislative pipeline acceptance.

## 4. What gets built

1. Resolve the governing enacted measure and its unique saved draft lineage.
2. Match its exact eligible family and configuration, then verify current provisions against the saved terms.
3. Accept optional `sponsorParameterChanges` over those verified values. Validate money, enumerated, integer and duration parameters without rounding or clamping.
4. Keep authority resolution, effect validation and provision writers unchanged within the released compiler boundary.

The remaining producer contract is precise. Starting law needs its actual jurisdiction, question, family key and version, variant, complete typed parameter values, operative dates and source. The compiler currently accepts enacted lineage only. A yes/no starting answer cannot supply an amount or service schedule.

The filing caller must preserve the same sponsor-authored changes through compilation and introduction. `introduceAutomaticLawMeasure` currently recompiles without forwarding this optional field. Team 1 owns that producer seam. No defaults substitute for either missing input.
