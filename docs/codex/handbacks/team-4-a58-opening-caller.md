assigning the adult's starting job. Five generated openings save a job and weekly

This completes the normal adult-opening caller portion. Other callers of the
legacy business engine, its fixed revenue and actual paycheck settlement remain

staff role; the unchanged hiring writer saves the player's work and pay flow.
The terminal is saved employer and role evidence plus the existing starting-job
assignment rule, not a newly modeled employer's hiring decision.

wrapper at line 436 now uses ensureTownResidents. The canonical producer is
