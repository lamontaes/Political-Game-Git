# Homes carry place-sourced building facts

Recorded homes now carry building-year and structure-unit estimates from their own place's Census tables. The existing price handler reads a saved home's age through the lease's actual records. A controlled bill signed at the governor's desk then limits a named tenant's renewal. This is a partial rent-cap repair. Real statutory CPI windows, complete legal coverage and the dated-reader dependency remain open, so the candidate stays draft.

## 1. Why-chain

A rent restriction can exempt newer buildings because the legal rule defines which homes it covers. The handler previously had no construction fact to read. A dwelling's record date cannot supply that fact: recording an existing home does not construct it. The opening housing writer now saves sourced stock estimates once. The handler joins the priced flow to its obligation, tenure and dwelling. Its terminal is the saved physical building fact and the law row's age rule.

## 2. Research

The Census Bureau's 2020–2024 ACS five-year tables B25034 and B25024 describe housing units by construction band and units in their structure. The source compiler matches exact seven-digit place GEOIDs. It retains estimates and published margins of error. Of 32,350 roster entries, 41 have no exact match. Puerto Rico is included; the other territories' ACS coverage is absent. No county or national replacement is used.

The tables are marginal distributions, not a joint age-by-structure table. Finite bands yield seeded opening estimates. Structure bands are conditioned on the already recorded home classification. Open-ended bands, suppressed cells and unavailable categories remain null. Pre-2024 game dates do not borrow the later survey. The source lock preserves downloaded bytes, URLs and hashes under data/source/acs-place-housing-structure.

The existing official rent-cap packet is data/research/laws/rent-cap-terms-2026.json. The new row predicate accepts its age rule as data. This does not establish every statutory exemption, certificate date, rent concession or notice.

## 3. Revisions

The approved core change adds only optional builtYear and unitsInBuilding fields to Dwelling and CreateDwellingInput. The existing writer validates and copies them. Old saves remain absent. Root's canonical-pay stamp is preserved. Town-home creation records existing stock even when a newcomer arrives later; it does not backdate construction.

A missing building year is explicitly unsupported; establishedAt is never substituted. The shared registry and catalog remain coordinator-owned.
