# Starting jobs use employers already recorded in the town

Five controlled hiring fixtures select existing town employers and save the same
work identities after reload and repeat. The earlier opening caller still runs
before those employer records are available. Keep this adapter in draft until
that caller's producer order is released and proved. The old business engine's
other callers and fixed revenue remain unfinished.

## 1. Why-chain

An adult starting job previously selected a player-only legacy business because
adultStartEmployer read localBusinessesIn. That business belongs to the old fixed
revenue engine. Its existence did not establish a current town employer with an
actual paid staff role. This chain ended at a stand-in business.

The replacement reads townBusinesses, then requires an existing organization and
an active paid staff role with a dated local workplace and a supported wage.
The terminal is an actual saved employer and staff role. The existing family
vouching, previous occupation preference and wage ranking remain. This is still
a starting-job assignment rule, not a newly modeled hiring decision.

The adapter is in src/simulation/local-economy.ts:848. Its actual hiring consumer
is hireAtAdultStart in src/simulation/job-market.ts:1581.

## 2. Research

Audit gap A58 authorizes migration away from player-only legacy employers.
No new empirical revenue, wage, staffing rate or sales amount is introduced.

## 3. Revisions

The adapter keeps the same organization and worker role property names used by
the hiring consumer. It narrows the role type to the two fields that consumer
actually reads. It preserves existing vouching and experience preference.
No pay expense, transfer settlement, revenue or owner draw hunk changes.

A missing person or eligible recorded employer returns null. This intentionally
removes the legacy fallback from this selector. Earlier opening order therefore
needs a separate caller repair before this draft is ready to land.

## 4. What gets built

1. Select only existing open town businesses and their active paid staff roles.
2. Refuse future, unpaid, owner, professional, unlocated and unsupported-wage roles.
3. Keep the existing hiring writer, identities, preference and wage readers.
4. Prove selection and hiring in five places after town records exist.
5. Hold ordinary opening proof for its exact producer-order and caller release.

Deleting local-economy.ts is not part of this checkpoint. Other seating and
refresh callers remain. Team3's released payroll and settlement hunks stay intact.
