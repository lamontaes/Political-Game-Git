# Team 1 bedrock numbers: laws and life-formed principles

Several current laws and principle producers still turn authored assumptions
into individual choices, bill terms or costs. This ledger identifies those
numbers and their downstream consumers. It changes no calibration. The held
law stack and the independent officeholder candidate are separate source
snapshots; neither inventory nor focused checks establish full-world acceptance.

## Scope and status

This covers the ten assigned direct-law modules, their bill-term producer,
opening estimates, and the newly assigned life-principle producer/caller.
Shared budget, people, money, press, governance and population internals remain
with their current writers. Their external inputs are named where consumed.
The listed generation distributions are not researched individual outcomes.

## Open actions and numerical design boundary

No number was replaced by this inventory. Team9 has the exact broad-anchor,
unit and legal cells. Team2 owns governance filing/backing thresholds and
receives the continuous consumer proposal. The source already permits a party
public-role pull of 3 to form a principle by itself; removing a seed does not
prove the remaining weights are realistic. Duration, depth, reinforcement,
conflict and legacy transitions still require the numbered CTO design decision.

for the declared source families, not an assertion every proxy is approved.

are not silently treated as reviewed behavior coefficients. The source family
scope above is the inventory boundary, not ownership of every imported writer.
