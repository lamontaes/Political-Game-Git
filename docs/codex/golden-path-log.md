# The golden path, part 1: an eighteen-year-old files at the clerk's counter, then campaigns alone and meets nobody

A new life can now go from the eighteenth birthday to an active campaign for the local board, filed in person at a clerk's counter, in 52 of the 56 places. Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands have no local board on record, so the path stops there. Of 15 breaks found, 7 are fixed in this package, 1 was fixed on main, 1 is eased by the speed change this branch carries, 5 are open and 1 is recorded as unsupported. The CTO has two calls to make. The speed change should merge before this package. And a week-old save that runs out of an 8 GB heap needs an owner.

## The story

**Ardsley, New York (seed golden-path-1).** Cynthia Lane is seventeen on January 5, 2026, living with both parents in the village. Her birthday is January 7. The player presses the next-day button twice and she turns eighteen. Nothing on her screens changes, but the record now shows both parents' authority over her ended that morning. The Campaigns list shows "Trustee" with "Eligible · Minimum age: 18 (estimated)".

Places offers "Westchester County", with "County clerk" beneath it. The player chooses it, and a visit goes on the calendar for 9:00 a.m. that day with the county clerk, Evelyn Sears. The player attends: Cynthia leaves at 8:40 a.m. and reaches the counter at 9:00. The counter offers three questions, "requirements", "filing" and "filed", and the player asks each. The clerk answers for the eight seats her office takes filings for. The village's Trustee and the county's Commissioner ask 18. The village's Mayor and the county's Sheriff, District attorney, County clerk, County treasurer and Coroner ask 21. For Trustee the election is November 2, 2027, the deadline is June 1 of that year, and the fee is $75. Nobody has filed for any seat. The player presses "Trustee" and Cynthia is a candidate; asking and filing take no time on the clock. She presses "Back" and is home at 9:20 a.m.

For the rest of the week the plan offers no door canvass, phone shift, fundraiser or town hall; its reason reads "needs-host", because nobody works for her campaign and no party chapter backs it. So each afternoon she spends on outreach. After five afternoons her support reads 50.01%, up from 50%, and she has met nobody new by name. January 10 took 109 seconds to pass.

**Hope Valley, Rhode Island (seed golden-path-2).** Kian Haynes turns eighteen on January 7, 2026, in a village inside the town of Hopkinton. Rhode Island has no county governments. Places offers "Town of Hopkinton" with "Town clerk". At 9:00 a.m. the town clerk, Darius McDaniel, answers for the one seat his office takes, "Township board member": 18, an election on April 2, 2026 (estimated), a June 1 deadline and a $75 fee. Kian files and is home at 9:20 a.m.

## What happens next

This package fixes breaks 4 to 10, with tests over all 56 places and a generated-world test of the counter. Its next part takes breaks 11 and 12: a candidate alone should knock on doors and meet the residents who answer. It also takes the filing-officer and Rhode Island data in break 13 and the knock-on above. Breaks 2, 3 and 15 go to the speed lane. The rules sweep takes the hand-written lines and the first two knock-ons. Break 14 stays unsupported until territorial local governments are read.

## Method

The playthrough in `scripts/golden-path/` plays the path through the functions the shell's buttons call and prints each break; later parts add their steps after the campaign week. Each walk stays within seven simulated days, and places are drawn from all 56 by seed. A third run, seed gp-1, drew Bryant, Washington, and was used only to measure the crash and the day's length. Checked on main 40398a6b and 2506a16a with #3769 merged in, October 8, 2026.
