# The golden path, part 1: an eighteen-year-old can now file for the town board, but not at the clerk's counter

An eighteen-year-old now reaches an active campaign for the local board in 52 of the 56 places. Two walks of a new life, from the seventeenth year through the eighteenth birthday, filing and the first campaign week, found eleven breaks. Five are fixed: the clock no longer crashes the day after the birthday, the birthday ends the parents' authority, the local board's age is the qualified-elector age, and a village or county resident can stand for the board that governs them. Six are open. Filing is still a button on the Campaigns screen, because no clerk's office exists to visit. A lone candidate cannot book a door canvass. Some days take minutes.

## The story

**Ardsley, New York (seed golden-path-1).** The character is seventeen on January 5, 2026, living with both parents in the village. Their birthday is two days away. The player presses the next-day button twice and the character turns eighteen on January 7. Before the fixes, nothing in the household changed: both parents still held authority over an adult. The Campaigns list offered "Trustee" with "Minimum age: 21 (estimated)", so the new adult could not file for anything. Places offered one thing, "Inspect: Village of Ardsley". There was no clerk's office to go to.

With the fixes, the birthday ends both authorities, and the Trustee row reads "Eligible · Minimum age: 18 (estimated)". The player files from the Campaigns screen. The week's plan offers no door canvass, phone shift, fundraiser or town hall, because nobody is there to host one, so the player spends an afternoon on outreach each day. On that walk January 10 took 134 seconds to pass.

**Hope Valley, Rhode Island (seed golden-path-2).** Hope Valley is a village inside the town of Hopkinton. Rhode Island has no county governments. The Campaigns list offered only the state House (minimum age 21) and Senate (30). The town council that governs Hope Valley meets in the game, but no seat on it was offered. With the fixes, the list offers "Township board member" with "Eligible · Minimum age: 18 (estimated)", and filing records a campaign for March 31, 2026 (estimated).

**Bryant, Washington (seed gp-1, probe).** Bryant is unincorporated, so its local board is the Snohomish County commission. Before the fixes the clock threw "A view of an official names another person." on January 8, the day after the birthday, and the game stopped. The county commission row read "Qualifications: not on record" whatever the character's age.

## Method

The headless playthrough in `scripts/golden-path/` plays the path through the functions the shell's buttons call and prints each break; later parts add their steps after the campaign week. Each walk stays within seven simulated days, and places are drawn from all 56 by seed. Checked on main 40398a6b and 2506a16a with #3769 merged in, October 8, 2026.
