# The golden path, part 1: an eighteen-year-old files at the clerk's counter and meets 178 neighbors, but support barely moves

A new life can now go from the eighteenth birthday to an active campaign for the local board, filed in person at a clerk's counter, and spend its first week meeting neighbors by name at their doors, in 52 of the 56 places. Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands have no local board on record, so the path stops there. Of 20 breaks found, 11 are fixed in this package, 1 was fixed on main, 1 is the game working as designed, 6 are open and 1 is recorded as unsupported. One thing needs the CTO: someone must be named to read those four territories' local governments.

## The story

**Ardsley, New York (seed golden-path-1).** Cynthia Lane is seventeen on January 5, 2026, living with both parents in the village. Her birthday is January 7. The player presses the next-day button twice and she turns eighteen. The Campaigns list now shows "Trustee" with "Eligible · Minimum age: 18 (estimated)".

Places offers "Westchester County", with "County clerk" beneath it. The player chooses it, and a visit goes on the calendar for 9:00 a.m. that day with the county clerk, Evelyn Sears. The player attends: Cynthia leaves at 8:40 a.m. and reaches the counter at 9:00. The counter offers three questions, "requirements", "filing" and "filed", and the player asks each. The clerk answers for the eight seats her office takes filings for. The village's Trustee and the county's Commissioner ask 18. The village's Mayor and the county's Sheriff, District attorney, County clerk, County treasurer and Coroner ask 21. For Trustee the counter reads "November 2, 2027" for the election, "June 1 (estimated)" for the deadline and "$75.00 (estimated)" for the fee. Nobody has filed for any seat. The player presses "Trustee" and Cynthia is a candidate; the counter now lists her under Trustee, with the date she filed. Asking and filing take no time on the clock. She presses "Back" and is home at 9:20 a.m.

For the rest of the week the plan offers no shift. It reads "Host: none" beside a link to "Party and community work", because nobody works for her campaign and no party chapter backs it. On the same screen, each afternoon the player presses "Spend a session on the doors". Each session takes Cynthia to 22 doors. On Wednesday, January 7, 17 people answer, among them Austin Richards and Miriam and David Villanueva; on each of the next four days, 37 to 42 do. By Sunday she has met 178 neighbors by name. Her recorded share of support in the race, with nobody else filed, reads 50.01%, up from 50%. Saturday, January 10, took 105 seconds to pass.

**Hope Valley, Rhode Island (seed golden-path-2).** Kian Haynes turns eighteen on January 7, 2026, in a village inside the town of Hopkinton. Rhode Island has no county governments. Places offers "Town of Hopkinton" with "Town clerk". At 9:00 a.m. the town clerk, Darius McDaniel, answers for the one seat his office takes, "Township board member": 18, an election on "April 2, 2026 (estimated)", a deadline of "June 1 (estimated)" and a fee of "$75.00 (estimated)". Kian presses "Township board member" and is a candidate. He is home at 9:20 a.m. Over five sessions on the doors he meets 176 neighbors by name, 21 on the first afternoon and 38 to 40 on each after it, and his share of support goes from 50% to 50.01%.

They belong to the rules sweep's hand-written-text rows.

## Method

The playthrough in `scripts/golden-path/` plays the path through the functions the shell's buttons call and prints each break; later parts add their steps after the campaign week. Each walk stays within seven simulated days, and places are drawn from all 56 by seed. Each place was walked four times. The first walk came before any fix and the second after breaks 4 to 10 were fixed. A short third walk went to the clerk's visit, to read whether each clerk already held the office, and the final walk ran on this branch's last build. The stories quote the final walks; the walk reads what each screen is given, not the rendered page, so the counter's and the plan's wording is read from their code. The final walks applied #3769's changes locally, uncommitted, so that the day after the birthday did not take more than 10 minutes; this branch no longer carries #3769. A probe run, seed gp-1, drew Bryant, Washington, and was used only to measure the crash and the day's length. Checked on main 03ffce4f, October 8, 2026.
