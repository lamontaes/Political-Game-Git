# The golden path, part 1: an eighteen-year-old files at the clerk's counter, then campaigns alone and meets nobody

A new life can now go from the eighteenth birthday to an active campaign for the local board, filed in person at a clerk's counter, in 52 of the 56 places. Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands have no local board on record, so the path stops there. Of 18 breaks found, 10 are fixed in this package, 1 was fixed on main, 1 is eased by the speed change this branch carries, 5 are open and 1 is recorded as unsupported. Two things need the CTO. The speed change this branch carries should merge before this package. And someone must be named to read those four territories' local governments.

## The story

**Ardsley, New York (seed golden-path-1).** Cynthia Lane is seventeen on January 5, 2026, living with both parents in the village. Her birthday is January 7. The player presses the next-day button twice and she turns eighteen. The Campaigns list now shows "Trustee" with "Eligible · Minimum age: 18 (estimated)".

Places offers "Westchester County", with "County clerk" beneath it. The player chooses it, and a visit goes on the calendar for 9:00 a.m. that day with the county clerk, Evelyn Sears. The player attends: Cynthia leaves at 8:40 a.m. and reaches the counter at 9:00. The counter offers three questions, "requirements", "filing" and "filed", and the player asks each. The clerk answers for the eight seats her office takes filings for. The village's Trustee and the county's Commissioner ask 18. The village's Mayor and the county's Sheriff, District attorney, County clerk, County treasurer and Coroner ask 21. For Trustee the counter reads "2027-11-02" for the election, "06-01" for the deadline and "$75.00" for the fee, with neither of the last two marked as an estimate. Nobody has filed for any seat. The player presses "Trustee" and Cynthia is a candidate, but the counter shows nothing new; only the "Trustee" button is gone. Asking and filing take no time on the clock. She presses "Back" and is home at 9:20 a.m.

For the rest of the week the plan offers no door canvass, phone shift, fundraiser or town hall; its reason reads "needs-host", because nobody works for her campaign and no party chapter backs it. So each afternoon she spends on outreach. After five afternoons her recorded share of support in the race, with nobody else filed, reads 50.01%, up from 50%. She has met nobody new by name. January 10 took 109 seconds to pass.

**Hope Valley, Rhode Island (seed golden-path-2).** Kian Haynes turns eighteen on January 7, 2026, in a village inside the town of Hopkinton. Rhode Island has no county governments. Places offers "Town of Hopkinton" with "Town clerk". At 9:00 a.m. the town clerk, Darius McDaniel, answers for the one seat his office takes, "Township board member": 18, an election on "2026-04-02 (estimated)", a deadline of "06-01" and a fee of "$75.00". Kian presses "Township board member" and is a candidate. He is home at 9:20 a.m.

## What happens next

This package fixes breaks 4 to 10 and 16 to 18, with tests over all 56 places and a generated-world test of the counter. It also takes the Rhode Island town data in break 13 and the third knock-on, New York's filing officer. Breaks 2, 3 and 15 go to the speed lane. ## Method

The playthrough in `scripts/golden-path/` plays the path through the functions the shell's buttons call and prints each break; later parts add their steps after the campaign week. Each walk stays within seven simulated days, and places are drawn from all 56 by seed. Each place was walked three times: a first walk before any fix, a final walk after breaks 4 to 10 were fixed, and a short walk to the clerk's visit to read whether each clerk already held the office. The stories quote the final walks, before breaks 16 to 18 were fixed; the walk reads what each screen is given, not the rendered page. A probe run, seed gp-1, drew Bryant, Washington, and was used only to measure the crash and the day's length. Checked on main 40398a6b and 2506a16a with #3769 merged in, October 8, 2026.
