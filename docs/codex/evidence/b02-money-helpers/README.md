# Campaign money and helpers proof

## Random new-game places

- Small town: Lamont, Oklahoma. Seed `session27-b02-asks-random-place-2026-10-06`. The campaign screen estimated 121 households.
- Large city: San Antonio, Texas. Seed `session27-b02-large-city-random-2026-10-06`. The campaign screen estimated 573,850 households from the 2020 Census place population, converted at 2.5 residents per household.
- Both screens use the same estimated unit prices. The estimated place-coverage totals were $741.48 for Lamont and $1,366,203 for San Antonio. The exact minor-unit figures are in `campaign-unit-prices-and-place-totals.json`.
- Each new game played three distinct asks and one helper request. Their recorded views, means, estimated limits, answers, and reasons are in the donor and helper screenshots and text files. These generated characters had no recorded personal funds, so their answers were refusals.

## Paid purchase and manager screen

The random games opened with no campaign money available and no known experienced manager candidate. The paid-purchase and manager screenshots therefore come from a funded fixture rendered through the existing campaign screen. It buys one palm card for $0.15, offers the manager job only to an experienced person the candidate knows, records the acceptance and payroll, and shows that manager's proposed plan. The fixture supplies an explicit $5,000 candidate contribution and is not represented as another random new game.

All campaign material prices are estimates, not vendor quotes. The household total uses ACS or Census place population where compiled, then recorded household locations as the fallback.
