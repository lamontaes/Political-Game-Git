## 1. Why-chain

An enacted law supplies spending authority. An office commits funds against that
authority. Cash must cover an installment when it falls due. The existing
payment producer debits the government and credits the operator. The Treasury
reads that completed transfer as expenditure. Spending not covered by its
receipts contributes to its existing deficit and borrowing identity. Bedrock is
legal authority, actual cash transfer and the accounting identity. Authorization
and a planned installment are not paid costs.

## 2. Research

No new empirical price, rate or cost level is introduced. This connection reads
existing saved appropriation, commitment, installment and transfer records. The
fixture's $150,000 authority and $100,000 payment are explicitly authored test
inputs, not researched national spending estimates or production defaults. The
Treasury's inherited fiscal2025 modeled baseline remains outside this repair;
its inherited yes/no cost proxies are listed in golden-rule-outcome-audit.json.

## 3. Revisions

The first fixture used malformed synthetic legislative IDs and failed integrity.
It now passes the controlled authority through the canonical Congress procedure
with explicitly authored ballots. A second fixture set a date without its
canonical moment and failed integrity. It now advances the canonical world clock
and lets the registered program transition post payment. Neither integrity
check was weakened. Failed transfers and unposted installments are excluded.

## 7. Worked example

The federal account starts with explicitly authored test receipts of $150,000.
Its controlled enacted rail appropriation allows $150,000. Before any installment
posts, the new Treasury cost journal is empty. After the existing producer pays
$100,000, the account has $50,000 and the operator has $100,000. Transportation
outlays, deficit and debt each rise by $100,000 against the same unpaid world.
The Treasury preserves the exact payment's cents and law stamp across JSON
reload. This is not a researched per-resident cost or a generated political
choice.
