# Federal privacy costs saved on business books

MERGED: this candidate is not merged. The shared stamp is approved on main.

WHAT EMERGED: HARDWIRED — quarterly accounting subtracts the existing researched
privacy production cost from quarterly earnings and saves that amount with its
controlling federal law stamp. This is accounting, not a new actor decision.
The proof uses controlled enacted yes/no laws in real seeded observer worlds;
it does not establish that an ordinary legislature naturally passed them.

## 1. Why-chain

A privacy duty changes firms' data handling. Compliance consumes staff and
systems. Paid resources add production costs. Quarterly accounts subtract the
cost from net earnings and cash, or require more borrowing. Bedrock is the legal
duty and the paid resources needed to comply. Hiring changes require an actual
employment decision; this repair does not invent one.

## 2. Research

The NBER page for Demirer and colleagues, working paper 32146, was read directly.
It says: “This increase translates into only a 0.1–0.6% rise in overall
production costs because data plays a relatively small role in firm production
compared to computation.” The mechanism is data/computation complementarity.
The owner-approved European adaptation remains provisional for American firms.
This range is not a confidence interval, statutory rate or layoff probability.
The numeric coordinator payload retains the quote, source URL and artifact hash.
