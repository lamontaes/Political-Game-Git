## 1. Why-chain

A law must name a consequence because the shared engine needs an action to
apply. The action needs legal terms because a yes/no answer does not specify an
amount. Terms need actual covered records because an appropriation alone does
not establish a paid worker, rider or recipient. The engine needs registered
bindings because arbitrary strings cannot authorize a saved change. Bedrock is
the legal rule and actual person's activity or completed consequence record.

## 2. Research

## 3. Revisions

Unknown terms and bindings remain missing rather than becoming zero, default
amounts or executable paths. Each law lists its actual required base and
prospective repeal treatment. Direct and onward changes require the same shared
row shape; onward inputs must read a saved exposure, not a binary law flag.
Capacity and exposure are allowed by the shared type, but no binding is invented.
