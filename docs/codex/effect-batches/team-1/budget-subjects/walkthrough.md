# Governor budget requests use recorded spending lines

Before: the governor's budget season picked one or two program families from the whole catalog using a seeded draw. A saved priority could appear without any recorded spending authority.

After: the existing season handler lists only families joined to this government's actual appropriation and its saved source-measure lineage. The governor's recorded priority is retained when that family is backed. A government without a recorded spending line gets no invented program candidate. This changes budget-request subjects; it does not spend money or pass a budget.

## 1. Why-chain

A governor can request a change to a program because the government has recorded spending authority for it. That authority points to the source measure. The measure's saved lineage identifies the family that produced its text. For a bundle, only the component that wrote the saved appropriation edition is admitted. The governor's saved agenda decision can prioritize a backed family. The chain ends at actual saved authority and the officeholder's recorded choice. Constituents, donors and other policy pressures are outside this bounded selection repair.

## 2. Research

This uses existing bookkeeping rather than a researched rate or outcome curve. Budget categories, including otherPrograms, cannot establish a family. No quantity, multiplier, filing rate or success rate is introduced.

## 3. Revisions

The random pick and its fixed one-or-two limit are removed only from the released budget-season selection hunk. Every recorded family is eligible. Missing or ambiguous source lineage yields no candidate. The existing priority reader, matter writer, options, decisions, calendar, other RNG uses and bill intake remain unchanged. Recorded authority can be renewed after its availability window; eligibility here establishes a historical budget subject, not current permission to spend.

## 4. What gets built, in numbered parts

1. A read-only recordedBudgetProgramFamilies selector joins this jurisdiction's saved appropriations to their exact measure lineage. Local-government authority is excluded from a state governor's request. Bundled components join the edition written by program-governing.
2. governingSeasonHandler passes those families to the existing openMatter writer. No second budget writer is added.
3. Focused proofs cover all 56 jurisdictions with no recorded line, single and bundled lineage, unsupported priority, an actual governor's saved request and canonical Save/Continue replay.

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

SIMULATED: the existing officeholder and staff decision paths consume the saved request and priority. RECORDS: the existing appropriation and budget-matter event remain the source of truth. WORLD PIECES: a current officeholder, a recorded appropriation and exact source lineage must exist. With no appropriation, there are no program candidates; the existing hold-spending option remains. CHECKS: no unbacked family, only the funded bundle component, no duplicate budget matter after reload. This repair does not prove a new appropriation, cash payment or enacted effect.
