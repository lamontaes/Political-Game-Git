# A finding subject's recorded resignation reaches office continuity

Before: Press findings had no bounded adapter for a respondent's later voluntary office response.

After: An actual respondent can supply a saved selected decision and their exact words to the existing resignation writer. Missing or undecided inputs leave the office unchanged. Audit must identify the canonical player or autonomous caller for admission before this becomes live behavior.

## 1. Why-chain

A finding alone does not resign its subject. The subject must hold the actual office, choose to resign in a saved decision scoped to that finding, and record their own words. Source inspection: src/simulation/press/finding-subject-response.ts:167 calls the canonical office writer after these guards. That writer ends the actual tenure. ## 3. Revisions

The saved finding supplies the respondent and public evidence. The saved office supplies the options. A selected remain decision records the words without ending work. Missing people, offices, words, decisions or matching scope produce no response. Blocked and undecided decisions produce none.

## 4. What gets built

1. Project an actual public finding and held office into two response options.
2. Consume a matching saved subject decision and exact words.
3. Append the existing press response and event, then call the existing office writer for resignation.
