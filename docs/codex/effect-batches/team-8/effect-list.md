# Health and technology effects need named records

The existing map covers all nine assigned questions, but an entry does not establish that anyone experienced its effect. Medicaid already changes named people's coverage. Other rule, service and data-rights consequences need their own records. This inventory separates those direct consequences from measured population outcomes and the still missing figures.

## 1. Why-chain

A passed law needs operative terms. Terms govern an actual eligibility decision, service, payment, business duty or legal restriction. That consequence must name its person or place and carry the canonical law stamp. News reads the consequence; journal and friend reactions need their own recorded knowledge. The terminal is the actual legal decision, delivered service or physical exposure, not the existence of a map link.
