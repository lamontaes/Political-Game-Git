## Why and source limits

Measured primary source: [Census poverty measures](https://www.census.gov/topics/income-poverty/poverty/guidance/poverty-measures.html) defines official poverty using money income before taxes. Therefore a tax change has zero direct arithmetic effect on that measure. The proposed range is exactly zero to zero. This says nothing about indirect earnings, migration, food insecurity, take-home income or supplemental poverty.
