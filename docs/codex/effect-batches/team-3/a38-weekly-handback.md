# Weekly pay keeps the law that raised it

The job-market weekly caller now lets the common compensation writer raise terms before paying. Four sampled state callers previously wrote their own higher terms first, leaving the common writer nothing to raise or stamp. Removing that private helper restores canonical law stamps on terms and actual paychecks.

MERGED: Published for coordinator receipt. No team merge or whole A38 completion.

WHAT EMERGED: HARDWIRED bookkeeping from saved work, dated law terms, period dates, actual tax payments, and employer cash. No new decision, wage estimate, schedule, or random outcome.

## 1. Why-chain

The state wage stamp was missing because the weekly helper raised terms without the canonical stamp. The common writer then saw sufficient pay and returned without raising it. The helper existed because weekly jobs once had a separate floor path. Weekly settlement already delegates to the common writer, so that extra write is unnecessary. Removing it leaves the common law dispatch before terms and payment calculation. Bedrock: the legal floor, the saved contract, actual work identity and recorded payment period.
