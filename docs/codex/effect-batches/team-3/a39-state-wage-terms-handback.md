# State wage bills use their adopted hourly amount

MERGED: This state-reader change has not merged. A state minimum-wage bill now supplies its exact adopted hourly target. A plain yes answer supplies no increase. The state average raise and its invented yearly steps are removed. Federal and city fallbacks remain separate unfinished parts of A39.

WHAT EMERGED: HARDWIRED legal bookkeeping read fictional clauses through the canonical lawmaking procedure. No employer decision or named person's paycheck was simulated. The reader preserved the law's date and jurisdiction and survived canonical Save/Continue.

## 1. Why-chain

1. A state wage setting changes because the adopted legal text contains an hourly target.
2. That text counts because its actual saved enactment governs the state's wage proposition on the queried date.
3. The final-term reader selects the adopted target with the exact question, key and minor-per-hour unit.
4. The wage reader returns that target and actual measure identity without adding a research average.
5. The legal bedrock is the adopted clause. No amount means no new numeric setting from that proposition.

The old reader added a median raise in yearly steps even when the bill carried no amount. Those state fallback constants and the step function are removed. The existing statutory rule-field reader remains.

## 2. Research

The removed research average described past state wage increases. It did not determine the dollar amount of any new bill. This repair introduces no researched level or rate. The test clauses explicitly author $17.75 and $18.25 hourly targets, and a separate existing fixture authors a $17.00 statutory clause.

These are fictional legal inputs, not real 2026 rates. Dated starting-law data remains coordinator-owned. The legacy starting-rate reader is unchanged and these checks do not establish that its starting values have correct phases.

## 3. Revisions

A future read cannot borrow a later numeric term. Its temporary miss is not cached, so the same saved enactments activate when their effective date arrives. Historical cache keys remain unchanged; advancing the current date does not add a second cache dimension.

The existing long state fixture now files its explicit statutory amount. Its long payroll and poverty assertions remain in the file and were not executed. No skipped test was permanently disabled.
