MERGED: This piece is published for review and remains a draft. It has not been merged into main.

Federal wage bills now supply their adopted hourly amount. Dated state reads use the existing legal phases, so D.C.'s July rate does not leak into January. ## 1. Why-chain

A wage floor must follow the operative legal clause because employers owe the amount the law sets. That clause must carry a number and unit because a yes answer does not specify dollars. The number must match the activity date because a later phase has no earlier legal force. The existing law reader selects that phase; the wage consumer had instead read a later snapshot. The chain bottoms out at the dated legal rule, rather than an averaged increase or assumed $15 amount.
