# Saved criminal cases reach their own dated review

Before: Criminal cases waited for the newspaper’s weekly sweep. A case without a judge could stay pending after its due review.

After: Saved referrals, charges, and mistrials supply their own review dates. An actual local trial-bench change can wake an already-due case. Opening and load recovery have an explicit export for Audit’s caller. The weekly fallback remains while the complete replacement is proven.

## 1. Why-chain

A case advances because its saved stage reaches its existing date. The date comes from that stage and the unchanged charge or hearing delay. The targeted adapter calls the existing case writer. A pending case without a sitting, unrecused judge stays pending until an actual bench write or explicit recovery boundary. Bedrock: saved case and judge records. The inherited 60/120-day delays remain declared stand-ins, not newly researched legal timing.
