# The governor’s saved desk opening reaches existing clemency decisions

Before: An NPC serving a sentence could wait for the newspaper sweep to consider a request after a new governor took office.

After: The actual saved desk opening reaches the defendant’s existing request decision. A request is written only when that defendant selects petition. The old request remains bound to its original holder, and weekly fallback remains available.

## 1. Why-chain

A new petition exists because the defendant selects petition. Why? The existing decision reads their sentence, traits, public record, and relationship to the actual authority. Why can they ask this office? The saved case venue reaches its recorded clemency rule. Why does this run now? The actual governor’s saved opening due item calls the existing opening writer and then the adapter. Why that governor? The due item must match the current saved term. Bedrock: the person’s own durable decision and the legal authority record. Missing authority or a required holder leaves the request unsupported. Counsel and the separately assigned unseated-board gap remain outside this change.

## 2. Research

This change introduces no rate, wage, bail amount, sentence length, or timing. It reuses the existing jurisdictional clemency rules and decision considerations. Existing case delays remain declared stand-ins, not newly sourced legal timing. The test’s successor election and term dates are explicit authored inputs, not a natural election or constitutional-calendar proof.

## 4. What gets built

1. Resolve the actual office from the saved term ID.
2. Reach only saved running cases whose venue reaches that office’s clemency power.
3. Reuse the defendant’s durable request decision and existing petition writer.
4. Preserve original petitions, repeated delivery, stale-term refusal, and canonical reload.
5. Keep weekly request creation and both court fallbacks until the complete replacement is proved.
