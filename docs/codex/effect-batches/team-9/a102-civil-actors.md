# Eviction cases wait for an actual judge

An eviction filing cannot become a judgment while its trial court has no seated judge. A tenant's request for counsel and a right-to-counsel law also cannot establish that a lawyer represented them. Actual civil representation remains a missing record producer that needs a CTO decision.

## 1. Why-chain

The old rent path could issue a judgment without a judge because the facts substituted “the court” for a missing seat holder. The decision then used the tenant's answer and rent arrears. The new caller requires the existing court, dated seat, tenure and actual judge before that decision. The chain ends at a saved legal appointment, rather than an unnamed decision-maker.

The old representation stamp came from the tenant's answer and the statute. Neither recorded an actual attorney or admission to the case. The candidate removes that inference; it does not create a lawyer, fee or civil case schema.

## 2. Research

This bounded repair consumes Audit's October 1 actor contract and existing judicial records. It adds no researched rate or legal term. State sentencing ranges remain with X5. Federal case venue remains deferred under A100.

## 3. Revisions

Read the judge at the actual hearing date. Preserve the filing date for the tenant's answer. A judge who takes office after filing can hear a later rent hearing; the former holder cannot silently remain its judge.

## 4. What gets built

1. Retain the actual court, seat, tenure and person join from the existing judicial readers.
2. Leave the existing filing pending when that join is absent.
3. Remove inferred counsel, representation prose and the unsupported representation stamp.
4. Preserve the selected judicial identities on the existing housing judgment event through the narrowly released writer.
