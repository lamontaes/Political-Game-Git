Before: An unseated pardon body answered from the case record and could produce a grant. A board containing the executive combined that person's choice with invented votes for the other seats.

## 1. Why-chain

The petition stays open because its required body has not answered. Why? There are no saved members or member votes to read. Why does the case record not count? It describes the defendant and sentence, not a body member's decision. Why can an executive not supply the missing votes? Their own recorded choice cannot establish another person's consent. Why is consent required? The case's existing jurisdictional clemency rule names the body. Bedrock: a legal consent requirement and missing actual decision-makers. This is an explicit missing mechanism, not measured zero support for clemency.
