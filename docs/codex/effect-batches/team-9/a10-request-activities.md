# Saved office openings reach the defendant’s request decision

Before: An NPC could wait for the newspaper sweep to consider clemency.

After: A saved sentence and an actual saved governor-desk opening call the existing request decision. The defendant decides from the same sentence, traits, relationship, and public-record considerations. The weekly fallback remains pending complete replacement proof.

## 1. Why-chain

The request exists only if the defendant selects petition. That selection comes from the existing durable decision. The decision reads the actual sentence and its legal authority. An actual sentence or desk opening supplies the activity that calls it. Bedrock: the saved person’s decision and the legal authority record. A missing authority or actual holder remains unsupported.

## 2. Research

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

WORLD PIECES: an actual defendant, running sentence, legal authority, and any required seated executive. CHECKS: the selected option controls whether a petition exists; canonical reload and replay must preserve old records and create no duplicates. No new actor, officeholder, rate, or outcome is authored in production.
