# Town adoption records whether the executive receives the ordinance

Before: The town-profile meeting enrolled and enacted an adopted ordinance directly. It recorded no explanation for bypassing the mayor.

After: Town and compiled councils share their passage handler. An explicit no-presentment profile records that outcome. A council requiring presentment retains its actual executive route. Unread presentment rules leave the ordinance pending.
