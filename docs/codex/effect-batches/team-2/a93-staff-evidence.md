# Executive staff candidates keep their actual records

Before: Offering staff candidates wrote randomly selected careers and degrees into their histories. A person without recorded posts counted as steady.

After: Candidate offers supply no career or degree. The office reads existing records and leaves steadiness unknown when there are no posts. Four changed-file cases pass, including the actual D.C. mayor's candidates and repeat/Continue checks. This is one office-evidence piece of Running the Government; the full playable slice remains unfinished.

## Why-chain

Measured: The candidate producer creates the offered people without adding work or education history (state-governing.ts:1214).

Measured: The assessment reads canonical posts and completed education records (staff-evidence.ts:96). Without posts it records limited evidence and unknown steadiness (staff-evidence.ts:106).

Measured bedrock: A job or degree needs an actual saved record. An offer by an office does not establish that experience (staff-evidence.test.ts:152).
