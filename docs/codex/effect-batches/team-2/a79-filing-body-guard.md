Before: A filing review formed member principles before checking the actual chamber's saved institution. Its preliminary vote still projected the combined state vote share onto both chambers.

After: The review validates the actual state bodies first. A closed body or missing roster stops that formation. The scaled preliminary verdict remains unfinished until the proposal-ordering contract is resolved.
