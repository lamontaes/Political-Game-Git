Main Content

​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​Kentucky Revised Statute Chapter 141 requires employers to withhold income tax for both residents and nonresidents employees (unless exempted by law). Employers must withhold the income tax of the employees receiving "wages" as defined in Section 3401(a) of the Internal Revenue Code.


2026 Electronic Filing and Paying Requirements

All employer filing frequencies are required to electronically file and pay the income tax withheld per 103 KAR 18:150​. 

To register and file and pay online, please visit the MyTaxes portal at MyTaxes.ky.gov​. 


​

  2025 Withholding Statement Reporting

   Withholding statements include W-2, W-2G, and 1099 forms.

​Due Date: January 31, 202​6

The withholding statement information (Forms W-2, W-2G, and 1099 Series) must be reported to the Kentucky Department of Revenue (DOR) on or before the January 31 due date. 

​​Withholding Statement Filing Options

  • EFW2 electronic file – by web filing or on CD
  • Publication 1220 electronic file or on CD​
  • Form K-5 – online filing (complete and submit online)
  • Form K-5 – online fill-in form (complete, print, and mail to DOR) (can't exceed 25 withholding statements)

​Attention K-5 Filers

A MyTaxes portal account is required to electronically file Form K-5. 

To learn more about logging on to your MyTaxes account, or opening a MyTaxes account, please visit; https://revenue.ky.gov/Pages/MyTaxes.aspx

Withholding accounts submitting this information in an electronic file format EFW2 or Publication 1220 do not file Form K-5.​

​Form K-5

Form K-5 is used to report withholding statement information from Form W-2, W-2G and 1099 and can be completed and submitted online in the MyTaxes portal at MyTaxes.ky.gov. Once in the portal, you will click on "Transactions" in the page header. From this drop down box, choose, "File A Form".  When entering information on this page, choose your account "reconciliation" option.  This will allow you to choose Form Type, K-5.  Upon completion of the K-5, you can "Calculate" and then "Submit". Print a copy of your K-5 for your records, but DO NOT mail. 

You can also print and mail the K-5.  The print and mail option is only for those reporting fewer than 26 withholding statements. ​If you qualify for the mailing option, enter the portal at MyTaxes.​ky.gov and navigate to the K-5 form as described above. Once your information is entered, hit "calculate", then "print", then the "Save and Exit" button. Make a copy for your records before mailing.  Mail your K-5 to:  501 High Street, Sta. 57, Frankfort, KY 40601​.​

​

Electronic Submission of W2s, 1099s and W2Gs


To file W2s, 1099s and W2Gs, electronically, you must first request access to become a Transmitter. Once completed you will be able to submit these in the MyTaxe​s.ky.gov portal.

To become a Transmitter, please refer to the How to Guide for Transmitters. If you have questions, please email us at: WHTransmitterRequest@ky.gov

Transmitter passwords expire every 90 days. Failure to update the credentials by logging in to MyTaxes will result in failed transmissions.
​
​

Format Specifications of W2s, 1099s and W2Gs files

​

W2s:


Kentucky follows the SSA’s EFW2 specifications with DOR defined RS Records for filing W-2 information.

Specifications are available h​​e​​re.

CD submission is another filing option. CD submissions must accompany a Transmitter Report, Form 42A806-CD.

1099s and W2Gs:


DOR follows the federal Publication 1220 specification format for filing W-2G and 1099. DOR defined B Records are required when reporting Kentucky statements.

Specifications are available her​e​.

CD submission is another filing option. CD submissions must accompany a Transmitter Report, Form 42A806-CD.

Only report 1099 data to the DOR when Kentucky tax has been withheld.
​


Please note: At this time, corrected W2s, 1099s and W2Gs must be submitted via paper.


Requesting a transcript of forms W-2, 1099, or W-2G


To request a transcript of forms W-2, 1099, or W-2G you must request it in writing to our office and provide the following information:
  • Employee’s first and last name
  • Social Security number
  • Tax year(s) requested
  • Where the recipient would like DOR to send the completed transcript
  • Contact information if there are any questions, and
  • An IRS-provided Federal Wage and Income Transcript.
    • IRS Form 4506-T (mail) 
    • IRS Transcripts (web)

Requests should be made to the Withholding Tax Branch at: 502-564-1900, or dorwithholdingtaxcompliance@ky.gov​
