Maryland Tax Connect for Individual Taxpayers is now live.
Maryland Tax Connect for Individual Taxpayers is now live, replacing iFile as Maryland’s online tax portal for individuals.
File returns, make payments, check refund status, and manage your account, all in one place. Creating a new account is the best way to track your filing history, view notices, and manage your account over time. For more information, please visit our
