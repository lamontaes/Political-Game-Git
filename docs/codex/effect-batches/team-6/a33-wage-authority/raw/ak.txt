Does Alaska have an Employment Security Tax?
Yes. The
Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development
administers the Employment Security Tax, also known as Unemployment Insurance Tax. For more information, go to the department’s website at
labor.state.gov
.
The Department of Labor’s
Employment and Training Services Division
houses the
Unemployment Insurance Program
, and
Employment Security Tax
unit.
