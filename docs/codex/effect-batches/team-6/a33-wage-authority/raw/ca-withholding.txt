In California, there are four state
payroll taxes
. Two are paid by employers, and two are withheld from employee wages.
Employer-Paid Taxes
Unemployment Insurance (UI)
Employment Training Tax (ETT)
Withheld from Employee Wages
State Disability Insurance (SDI)
Personal Income Tax (PIT)
Your SDI and PIT
deposit due dates
depend on your federal deposit schedule and the amount of PIT you withhold. Your UI and ETT payments are due every quarter.
Use
e-Services for Business
,
Express Pay
, or other
electronic file and pay options
to meet the
e-file and e-pay mandate
for employers.
