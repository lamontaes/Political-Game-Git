The Texas Comptroller's office serves the state by collecting, processing, administering, or overseeing
100 separate taxes, fees, assessments
, and program applications, including local sales taxes collected on behalf of more than 1,800 Texas cities, counties, and other local governments.
News and Announcements
Franchise Tax Notices – July/August 2026
Form 05-211, Texas Notice of Intent to Forfeit Right to Transact Business
If you receive this notice, you failed to meet 2026 franchise tax filing requirements. To resolve this issue, you must file and pay your 2026 franchise tax report and pay late filing penalties and interest. If you are at or below the no tax due threshold, simply file your 2026 Public Information Report or Ownership Report.
Learn how
to resolve this issue and ensure your franchise tax account is current.
Form 05-213, Texas Notice of Forfeiture of Registration
If you receive this notice, you failed to meet 2025 franchise tax filing requirements. To resolve this issue, you must file your 2025 franchise tax report and pay any tax, penalty and interest. If you are at or below the no tax due threshold for 2025, simply file your 2025 Public Information Report or Ownership Report.
Learn how
to resolve this issue and ensure your franchise tax account is current.
Postal Service Policy Change Affects Postmark Timing
A U.S. Postal Service policy change could mean delays in when mail is postmarked. This could cause date-sensitive payment and report submissions to the agency to be late.
