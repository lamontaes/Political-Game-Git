August 31, 2026
LDR to close for system updates and Labor Day holiday
BATON ROUGE - Due to critical system updates and the Labor Day holiday, the Louisiana Department of Revenue (LDR) will be closed from 2 p.m. on Friday, Sept. 4, 2026, through Monday, Sept. 7, 2026.
August 27, 2026
Hunters get a tax break during the 2nd Amendment Weekend Sales Tax Holiday, Sept. 4-6
BATON ROUGE - Consumer purchases of firearms, ammunition and a wide range of hunting supplies are exempt from state and local sales tax during the 2026 Louisiana Second Amendment Weekend Sales Tax Holiday, Friday, Sept. 4 through Sunday, Sept. 6.

LDR partners with OMV in Bossier City to resolve driver’s license suspensions
BATON ROUGE – The Louisiana Department of Revenue (LDR) is partnering with the state Office of Motor Vehicles (OMV) to help Shreveport/Bossier-area taxpayers resolve driver’s license suspensions.
