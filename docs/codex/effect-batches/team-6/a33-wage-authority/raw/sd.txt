We provide you with a variety of tools and services to help you navigate tax laws and regulations. Here you will find information and updates about various taxes for individuals in South Dakota.
Taxpayer Bill of Rights
How Do I File?
Individuals have a responsibility to file and pay use tax on items they purchase from unlicensed vendors. If you need to self report use tax, please click on the link below that will take you to our online form.

Income Tax
South Dakota is one of seven states that does not impose a state income tax. If you have questions regarding your federal tax return, W-2, or stimulus checks please contact the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) at (800)829-1040 or visit their website at:
https://www.irs.gov/
Inheritance and Estate Taxes:
South Dakota does not have an inheritance tax. The voters of South Dakota repealed the state inheritance tax effective July 1, 2001.There is also no estate tax.
