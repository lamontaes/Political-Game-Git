Do you pay estimated income tax?
The last two estimated tax payments for 2026 are due September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. Before you pay, remember to review your tax account balance in Online Services.
Review tax account balance
Did you file a 2025 personal income tax extension?
If you received an extension of time to file your 2025 income tax return, you must file your return by October 15, 2026. The fastest way to file is electronically.
Find e-file software
Want to switch to the STAR credit from the exemption for next year?
You can register to switch to the STAR credit from the STAR exemption anytime during the year. But the timing of your first STAR credit will depend on whether you register before or after the deadline to switch.
