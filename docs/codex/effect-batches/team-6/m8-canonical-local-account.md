# Local budgets require their recorded account

Before: The local budget reader accepted an account by its name without
validating its saved government profile. Settlement could also use a forecast
when recorded cash was missing.

After: County and city budgets validate their existing public account and
require recorded cash. A missing, mismatched or ambiguous account leaves the
budget unchanged. No account is created or consolidated. The change follows
the CTO's approved local-account repair.

## 1. Why-chain

A government's month must reflect its payments because its budget describes
money it can use. Payments belong to a saved organization, so the reader must
identify that account. Its stable key names a government, but a name alone
does not validate its profile. The canonical account accessor verifies the
saved government classification and jurisdiction. If no unique account and
cash record remain, there is no supported settlement. Bedrock: recorded
account ownership and completed transfers, rather than forecast permission.

## 2. Research

This repair adds no rate, cost, revenue estimate or legal power. It reuses
the existing compiled government relation, saved organization profile,
resource position and completed transfer records. An account does not prove
tax authority. Historical account selection and individual tax-family
admission remain separate reviewed contracts.

## 3. Revisions

The local registration path calls the existing government identity and
public account accessors. Named local identities retain their actual saved
government key. State and federal registration remain unchanged. The common
settlement writer requires both the recorded-flow map and local account cash.
It preserves ambiguous accounts instead of choosing one or merging assets.
Reserve still earmarks the same account cash under the existing budget rule.

## 4. What gets built

1. Validate county and city account registration in the existing month reader.
2. Leave local settlement unchanged when recorded cash is unsupported.
3. Retain saved account, position, flow and transfer outcome identities.
4. Prove five sampled local governments, all 56 jurisdiction controls,
   ambiguous accounts, forecast rejection and canonical Save/Continue.

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

Simulated: no new person decision, tax, service or court payment is produced.
Records: actual account cash and completed transfers feed the existing budget.
World pieces: the compiled local government relation and canonical profile
reader already exist. Missing accounts remain missing.
Checks: settlement preserves actual cash, exact source identities and repeat
behavior. Cash earmarked as reserve remains part of the same physical asset.
Held bail is a separate pending spendability dependency from Team 9; this
repair does not claim that connection is complete.

## 6. Proof run

The selection seed is m8-canonical-local-five-places. The sampled governments
are Echols County, Georgia; La Grange, Missouri; Bryan County, Oklahoma;
Glenville, West Virginia; and LaGrange County, Indiana. Only fixture identity
selection uses the seed. No money or person outcome is drawn.
All 77 focused checks passed on the owned source before current-main
composition. These include five positive settlements, ten invalid or missing
account checks, five forecast rejections, 56 state/DC/territory account
controls and one ambiguity check. Positive cases also assert source IDs,
canonical reload and repeat settlement.
Initial fixture errors used an unsupported transfer reason and compared
balance alone instead of balance plus reserve. Their outputs are retained
and are not product failures. Eleven initial negative assertions exposed
the account-validation and missing-cash defects. No full suite, browser,
speed, nationwide law-effect or named-person watched run was performed.

## 7. Worked example

Each sampled local government receives an explicitly controlled account
with $100.00 and a completed $5.50 fixture receipt. The reader retains the
actual organization, position, flow and transfer outcome IDs. Settlement
records $105.50 across balance and reserve, using its existing reserve rule.
Saving and reopening preserves those IDs. The cursor prevents counting the
receipt twice, and repeated settlement preserves the budget. This controlled
receipt asserts accounting behavior without claiming tax or bail authority.

## Remaining contracts

M5 needs a historical-cutoff account/profile binding without a local-to-state
alias. M7 needs reviewed legal term keys, units, taxable occurrence, marginal
annual threshold handling, exemptions, currency, effective timing and actual
recipient. Existing completed liabilities are preserved. Held bail needs its
published accessor and approved common spendability seam. CTO review remains
required for this core financial change.
