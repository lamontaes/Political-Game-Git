# Federal budgets use one account and one monthly settlement

Before: The monthly pass recorded actual federal payments and also advanced a forecast treasury that could create deficit borrowing. New saves opened both sets of books.

After: Only the common government account settles federal cash. New saves open its budget row without creating forecast books. Older forecast records remain unchanged. Missing cash cannot create a payment or loan.

## MERGED

This renewal is a branch candidate for the existing A47 pull request. It is not merged or CTO-approved at this source. The 988 standing-appropriation opening hook remains unchanged.

## WHAT EMERGED

HARDWIRED: The monthly caller uses the common government account and no longer invokes the forecast settler (`src/simulation/public-budgets/index.ts`). In all 56 controlled place contexts, the scheduled monthly handler records the saved partial payment and the actual cash balance. No person receives a service merely because this accounting pass ran. No official decision is claimed.

## 1. Why-chain

An appropriation grants permission to spend. It cannot establish cash paid because it lacks a completed transfer. A government's budget must read its saved account because receipts and outlays move that account's balance. Two independent settlements can count the same government differently. Removing the forecast caller leaves one saved-payment writer. Old forecast bytes remain unchanged because replacing them would rewrite prior saved history. Bedrock: actual resource positions and completed or partial resource-transfer outcomes.

## 2. Research

No new money level or rate is introduced. The opening debt export is exactly the existing rounded December 31, 2025 Treasury value. The existing effective interest rate remains unchanged. An older save's debt and rate supply its one-time federal row admission when needed. These are metadata; neither creates cash. The existing seven receipt and thirteen outlay categories remain.

## 3. Revisions

The monthly forecast invocation is removed. Both new-world calls to the forecast opener are removed. The canonical federal row uses the existing debt metadata directly. Historical forecast fields remain optional and are never advanced. The exported archive APIs remain for explicitly historical compatibility tests. The common cash settlement, category mapping, source IDs and bail reserve consumer remain unchanged.
