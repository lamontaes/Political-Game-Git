# Saved crisis authority reaches the existing service contract

Before: The crisis appropriation writer saved actual sourced authority with no
legislative measure, while service resolution required an enacted-law identity.

After: The owned reader supplies the published StandingProgramAuthority shape
from that actual saved appropriation. It preserves its account, government,
source basis and availability, without inventing a measure or policy question.
The existing service handler remains Team5's consumer; payment alone cannot
establish delivery.

## 1. Why-chain

Why can this authority fund a service without a simulated enacted measure? It
is an actual sourced adopted appropriation recorded through the common writer.
Why retain its account, government and window? Those identify who can commit
which money, for what program, during what period. Why cannot the payment prove
care? Cash transferred to an organization does not establish an operator's
completed activity or a person's attendance. Why must the same service handler
consume it? The actual commitment, operating payment and recipient activity
checks remain necessary under either legal authority. Bedrock: a written legal
spending ceiling, an actual cash transfer and a separate completed activity.

## 3. Revisions

standingCrisisAuthority(world, appropriationId, onDate) returns the shared
StandingProgramAuthority or null. It reads the exact saved record and rejects
other record kinds, other programs, enacted-measure authority, missing sourced
basis, a not-yet-recorded appropriation and dates outside its actual window.
The account and government identity remain exactly those on the record.

The consumer must add the actual appropriation ID and its eventId to the source
chain; the shared dispatcher validates both against the saved record. The
reader does not create commitments, payments, staff, services or stamps. There
is no second schema, registry, dispatch engine or per-law month path.
