# Missing paid-leave rates use sourced peers instead of seed draws

Before: An unread or newly adopted program's employee premium and benefit rate
varied with the world seed around other programs' averages.

After: The two existing fallback readers use sourced same-program peers,
ranked by Census region and household-income distance with the approved authored
reciprocal-rank rule. Known rates, caps, eligibility and the existing claims,
payments and stamps keep their paths. Estimated rates reach the existing
premium and benefit readers.

## 1. Why-chain

Why does an unread rate need an estimate? The existing reader lacks a published
starting rate or an admitted numeric bill term. Why use other read programs?
They supply actual employee premium and lower-wage replacement observations.
Why rank region and household income? Those are the owner's accepted peer
comparison dimensions. Why give nearer references more weight? Reciprocal rank
is the accepted authored estimate, not an empirical program-cost coefficient.
Why remove the seed? A person's identity or the world seed changes neither the
source rate nor the comparison facts. Bedrock: sourced program rates, an
explicitly labeled estimate, and an actual lost-pay input to the existing writer.

## 2. Research

The existing 2026 premium packet has thirteen read employee shares; the benefit
packet has twelve read lower-wage replacement rates. These are percent of wages
and percent of lost pay, respectively. Both packets retain each official program
source or the matching benefit source quote. D.C.'s actual employee share is
zero because the employer pays; that observed zero remains in the peer facts.
An absent or unread rate is excluded, never turned into zero.

The Census CPS 2023 H-8 packet supplies current annual household income for
ranking places, not a worker's pay or an enacted rate. Existing Census regions
are reused. When that comparison is unread for a territory, all program peers
have equal weight and the result is a labeled plain mean; no territory income,
region or program eligibility is invented. The #1369 weighting rule is an
authored design choice, not a research estimate of causation.

No employer premium, tier boundary, statutory start date, weekly maximum,
2026 wage cap, claim eligibility or bill parameter is changed. A law's own final
premium and benefit-rate bindings remain a separate A45/M7 admission gap.

## 3. Revisions

A single paid-leave source helper supplies both released fallbacks. Each rate
kind selects its own read program observations, then ranks same-region first
and household-income distance second. Equal closeness shares a competition
rank and weight; alphabetical order only stabilizes display. Immutable source
estimates are cached by rate kind and place; repeated paychecks do not re-sort.

Premium percent becomes employee millionths once, retaining the existing
rounding. Replacement remains percent for the existing benefit arithmetic;
lost pay and the unchanged read maximum determine actual minor units. These
are sourced weighted means with no rate-scale draw, new curve or outcome-level
roll. The helper changes no saved schema or decision engine.

## 4. What gets built

1. Remove only the released premium and benefit fallback RNG paths.
2. Reuse sourced program peers with the approved ranking method.
3. Preserve read rates, caps, collecting dates and current-law checks.
4. Preserve the canonical claim, cash, budget and stamp writers.
5. Verify exact units, seed independence and a funded saved recipient payment.

## 5. Simulated, records, world pieces, checks

Simulated: an explicitly authored claim and cash receipt, not natural illness,
work absence, earned premium revenue or a lawful spontaneous claim decision.
Records: existing public account, receipt, benefit flow, transfer and law stamp.
World pieces: a saved Oregon recipient and actual account cash; the full
upstream absence/eligibility producer is not established by this direct fixture.
The canonical benefit writer and payment amount computation are unchanged.

and canonical Save/Continue preserve the same saved flow, outcome and stamp.
