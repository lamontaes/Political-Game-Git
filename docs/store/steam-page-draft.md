## Short description (Steam limit 300 characters)

Live a life in any real American town, run for the offices that govern it, and change the law. Every neighbor, business and official is simulated, every vote has its reasons, and the town remembers what you did.

## About this game

You turn 18 in a real place: one of the 50 states, Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the Northern Mariana Islands. Your town is built from its real records: its population, its schools, its businesses and jobs, its laws and its elected offices.

The people around you live their own lives. They work their shifts, spend their pay, grow closer or drift apart, and form their own views. When you skip ahead, your character lives on their own too, until something needs you.

When you're ready, walk into the office that takes filings in your town, whether that's the county clerk, the registrar or the city clerk, and run. The rules are that place's real rules: age, residency, fees, signatures and deadlines. Knock doors, recruit volunteers, raise money under real contribution limits, give speeches and take stands. Every voter you reach changes their mind for their own recorded reasons, and on election night you see every race and how your neighbors voted.

Win a seat and govern. Draft an ordinance with real amounts, learn where each colleague stands and why, trade favors, and see it pass, fail, or meet the mayor's veto. Laws take effect on their dates and change people's records.

## Features

- **Any real place.** Every town in the 50 states, D.C. and the five territories, built from public records.
- **A whole world, running.** Your neighbors, employers, the bank and the national economy all keep going whether you watch or not.
- **Real government.** The law in force where you live, at every level, and every office over you with a holder from day one.
- **Real elections.** Real filing offices, dates and rules. Primaries follow each state's method, and election night has exit polls.
- **Everything has a reason.** Click anyone and see why they did what they did.
- **Talk to anyone.** Conversations come from what people actually know, and a lie you tell can come out later.
- **Your record follows you.** Promises and votes are on the record, and the next election weighs them. **(planned for early access: people bringing it up in conversation)**

## Early access FAQ

**Why early access?** The life, the town, filing, campaigning, elections and passing local laws run today. We want players in those towns while we build out state and federal careers, the courts and the rest.

**What's in early access?** Local and state offices: council, mayor, county, state legislature and governor, including the executive's side. **(planned: state legislature and governor play are in progress)**

**How long?** Set once the owner has played the full path end to end.

**Will the price change?** The owner's call.
