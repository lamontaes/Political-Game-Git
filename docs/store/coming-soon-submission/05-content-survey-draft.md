## 2) Mature content

**Adult Only Sexual Content:** No.

If later content adds explicit sexual material, the survey must be updated **before** that build is reviewed. Adult-content pages require store **and** build review together and take longer.

## 3) Store screenshot age flags

Valve asks at least four screenshots marked suitable for all ages when the images have no gore, violence, or suggestive themes. Requested A shots (home, talk, calendar, people, title-in-play) are intended to be flaggable. Do not flag a shot that shows graphic injury if one is ever captured.

## 4) Political / real-world people

The game uses real place names and sourced institutional rules. It generates fictional people after Begin (OCD-SIM-003). Do not put real living private individuals in capsules. Do not imply Valve or any party endorses the game’s politics.
