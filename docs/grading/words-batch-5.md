# Speeches, hearings, public comment, interviews and headlines in real Americans' words: grading batch batch-5

These 100 lines are what the story director writes when its words come from the mined speech, interview and headline banks instead of hand-written lines. Each sentence was said in a public-domain federal record, with the names, employers and towns filled from a generated person's records.

## How to grade

Grade one thing per line: does it fit the line before it, the way a person would say it? Write good, rewrite or kill, and a note where it helps. A line you kill is never said again; another real sentence takes its place. Grades never block a merge. The lines come from 5 drawn places: Broadview Park, Florida; Hartford, Michigan; Hydetown, Pennsylvania; Carrizozo, New Mexico; Loraine, Texas.
