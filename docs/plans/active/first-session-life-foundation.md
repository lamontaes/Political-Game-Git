# First-Session Life Foundation

## Objective

Recover PR #34 after human-play rejection by replacing the forced legislative-office opening with a saved, variable-age life start while preserving the accepted campaign and election primitives behind an explicit regression route.

## Binding constraints

- One canonical `World`; only player-chosen, played, accepted generated, or
  already-canonical biography may become truth.
- `Play From Here` leaves prior life sparse. `Build My History` records only
  the dated anchors the player explicitly authors in the ordinary history
  store. The canned quick-history generator remains development scaffolding.
- Birthplace, hometown, and current residence are independent real supported places with recorded provenance.
- Friendly place names, source identities, and local political capability are
  separate. A place does not gain invented political rules merely by being selectable.
- Normal play begins in age-appropriate ordinary life. Politics is optional.
- Situational setup answers create subtle, low-confidence, append-oriented mind
  evidence. Direct proposition answers create private beliefs and never infer party.
- Browser saves store the exact serialized World and validate it on every load.
- The accepted office/campaign implementation remains intact at an explicit regression route and is not the default launch.
- Human visual acceptance remains outstanding even after automated proof passes.
