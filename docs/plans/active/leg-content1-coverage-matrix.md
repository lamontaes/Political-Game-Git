This is that matrix. It is deliberately short: what the corpus supports, what
was built from it, and what was not built and precisely why.

## What the corpus actually contains

Read for this pass: the packet's declared items 1–8, in particular the
federal legislative-content compiler pilot (document 71), the
legislative/historical content-compiler research plan (document 69), the
research-utilization inventory's C01/C02/D03/D04/D05/D06/L05/L06/L07 rows, and
this repository's own registered source `MANIFEST.json` on the tree under test.

Two different things are called "source" here and the matrix keeps them apart.

**Structural provenance** is a real legislative episode that demonstrates a
clause _shape_ exists. The corpus supplies exactly one substantial example —
the infrastructure package the pilot analyses — and that example is rich: it
establishes separate identities for measure, text version, provision,
amendment, procedural action and analytic artifact; it separates authorization
from appropriation and both from revenue; and it contains a targeted amendment
that was ordered to lie on the table, which is the corpus's own proof that
proposed text and adopted text are different records.

**Runtime source grounding** is whether this game has a registered production
data domain that could ever supply a baseline for the thing a bill is about. It
does not license any claim about the world; it decides whether a family's
declared intended outcome names a series that could in principle be measured
here, or one that could not.

Nothing in the delivered content reproduces a value, an amount, an outcome or a
speech from either kind of source. Every number a player can move is authored
fiction and says so in its own record.

revenue amount lands in a field nothing adds to a spending total, and an

elsewhere; a limit somebody else publishes and revises; a category qualifying
outright; and what an applicant operates.

## Two limits inside what was delivered

- **Forecasts.** Every family declares the series an estimate of its effect
  would need, and in a new game none of those series has a baseline. The
  analysis surface names the missing series rather than producing a number. The
  available branch is real and proved, but it is reached by recording a
  baseline, not by the content bank asserting one.
- **Autonomy.** Vote behaviour outside the modelled members remains authored,
  as it was before this work. Nothing here makes the legislature dynamic, and
  the delivered content does not claim to.
