## Architecture Integrity Audit

- Open-set fidelity: the 92N matrix remains a complete 50-state disposition;
  production remains evidence-bounded at twelve supported Alaska records with no
  arbitrary jurisdiction cap.
- Semantic integration: tests cross acquisition, compilation, query, the
  canonical seat chain, normal Work persistence, and replay rather than asserting
  serialized data against itself.
- Context and history: jurisdiction level, seat, observation date, foundational
  date, and unknown amendment intervals remain explicit. Unknown history is never
  rewritten as continuous authority.
- Primitive reuse and ownership: the implementation reuses the source substrate,
  fiscal query, canonical seats, and Work system. It adds no treasury, economy,
  fiscal forecast, law engine, municipal execution path, or global UI root.
- Provenance and determinism: parent and child identities, hashes, retrieval
  receipt, rights boundary, page order, proposition gates, disposition check, and
  byte-identical replay are inspectable and deterministic.
- Adversarial coverage: missing, tampered, altered, conflicting, wrong-level,
  intervening-date, and future-date cases fail closed without converting absence
  into a prohibition.
