## LEARN

- A player-facing day skip cannot use object identity as proof that time moved: migration or other append-only preparation may return a distinct World at the same moment. Clock harnesses must inspect canonical time and explicitly answer each exact interruption.
- Optional activities may have the controlled person as a participant without naming them as the responsible person. Decline authority therefore follows controlled participation plus access, while performance authority remains stricter.
- Headless evidence runners must not import browser-only scene modules indirectly. Keep time-neutral scheduled-activity choices in a server-safe boundary and let visual venue execution depend on them, not the reverse.
