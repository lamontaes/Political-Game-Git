## LEARN

New saves can predate the economic provider's retrieval frontier, so a mounted
normal-player route must treat honest unavailability as a first-class success
state. Supported-date graph coverage belongs in a deterministic browser fixture;
the normal-save proof should preserve the earlier canonical date rather than
manufacture data or advance time just to make the chart nonempty.

## Stop conditions retained

- No new source acquisition, chart framework, budget engine, policy forecast,
  allocation controls, or operative fiscal action.
- No fallback from an unmatched place to Lexington or from a locality to a
  containing county, metro, or state.
- No edits to the paused source workspace or another owner's worktree.
