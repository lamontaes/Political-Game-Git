## Core contracts

The expression inventory `campaigns-kernels.json` gives the original file/function, exact source span and expression, export name, and the original source operand for each named input. Numbered exports follow source order. A local intermediate input must be an earlier kernel's result; core must not reimplement the calculation. Compound assignments become pure new-value calculations; the caller updates only its local traversal accumulator. Preserve the original enclosing guards, traversal order and short-circuit record reads. Nullable facts remain nullable, and no missing fact becomes a new permission. Old reader names in the operand map describe the fact to provide; they do not authorize importing old execution.

`offices` supplies contest, jurisdiction, officeholder and effective-rule facts. `journal` supplies indexed dated records, joins and source provenance and persists the resulting effects. `calendar` supplies resolved instants, activity state/conflicts and chronological traversal, reserves/releases holds and posts follow-up items. Each coverage row names its remainder. These are integration contracts, not new core implementations or permission to duplicate policy in those modules.
