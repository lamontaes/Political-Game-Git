The class is drawn by length (three words or fewer, no slot, no fact gate). Length is not the rule. The rule (owner, rule 155): a reply that says nothing is not a reply. A short line stays when it answers the question asked, states an event on the record, takes a stand, or asks something the record can answer. Keys are `conversation.<part>.<slug>`; parts and texts below. Deleted texts stay in this file.
