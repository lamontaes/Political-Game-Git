# Addresses: turn lines, closes and event lines (Words lead, Nov 16 06:40Z)

For the desk thread's step 3. Add as written. Each is shown said in an address the player opened or attended by presses at the final sha, or deleted. No figure in any of these; a figure belongs to the budget and record lines that already carry one.

## Turn lines (fact turn-to; slot {topic} is the next point's plain name, lower case)
- "Now, {topic}."
- "Let me turn to {topic}."
- "The other thing I came to talk about is {topic}."
- "That brings me to {topic}."
- "I also owe you a word on {topic}."
One turn line between two points; never before the first point, never before the close.

## Event lines (fact place-event, by kind; slots from the event's own record)
Closure ({business}, {place}):
- "{business} closed this year, and {place} felt it."
- "We lost {business} this year. People here lost their jobs with it."
Disaster ({hazard} as the record names it, {place}):
- "The {hazard} hit {place} hard this year."
- "Nobody in {place} has forgotten the {hazard}."
Law ({law} by its name, took effect in this realm this year):
- "{law} took effect this year, and people here are living under it now."
- "This was the year {law} became law here."
Election (the realm voted this year):
- "{place} voted this year, and I heard what you said."
- "You went to the polls this year. I'm standing here because of it."
The second election line plays only for a speaker elected at that election (fact from the term record). The second closure line plays only when the closing's record holds jobs ended.

## Closes
Plain, any party, any level:
- "Thank you. Let's get to work."
- "That's the work. Thank you for hearing me out."
- "Thank you, {realm}."
Evening only (the address's own hour, rule 141):
- "Thank you, {realm}, and good night."
After an ask the speaker made in this speech (fact: an ask was said):
- "I've told you what I want done. Now I'll go do it."
- "You know what I'm asking for. Hold me to it."
For a speaker whose party holds the body (fact party-holds-body):
- "We have the votes to do this. Thank you."
For a speaker whose party does not (fact party-lacks-body):
- "I can't do this alone, and I won't pretend I can. Thank you."
No two addresses in one world on the same day share a close.

## Rewordings, Nov 16 08:05Z: every close and event sentence carries a record slot (option a)
The variety tests stand as written. No bare "Thank you." sentence and no slotless sentence in a close or event line. These replace the lines above with the same uses and facts:
- "Thank you. Let's get to work." becomes "There's work waiting in {realm}, so let's get to it."
- "That's the work. Thank you for hearing me out." becomes "That's the work in front of {realm}, and I thank you for hearing me out."
- "I've told you what I want done. Now I'll go do it." becomes "I've told you what I want done on {topic}, and now I'll go do it." ({topic} is the ask's own question name.)
- "You know what I'm asking for. Hold me to it." becomes "You know what I'm asking for on {topic}, so hold me to it."
- "We have the votes to do this. Thank you." becomes "The {body} has the votes to do this, and I mean to ask for them." ({body} is the body's name from its record.)
- "I can't do this alone, and I won't pretend I can. Thank you." becomes "I can't do this without the {body}, and I won't pretend I can."
- "You went to the polls this year. I'm standing here because of it." becomes "{place} went to the polls this year, and I'm standing here because of it."
- "We lost {business} this year. People here lost their jobs with it." becomes "We lost {business} this year, and people in {place} lost their jobs with it."
"Thank you, {realm}." and "Thank you, {realm}, and good night." stay.
