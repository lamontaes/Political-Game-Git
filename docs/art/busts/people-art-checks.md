# People art: his recurring complaints, checked before he sees anything

The `codex-test/` tool folder some lines below name is no longer on this Mac (checked Nov 16); rebuild a tool from its description before you rely on it.

He has said each of these more than once. Nothing goes to him until every check below passes. Never send a picture with a "what isn't right yet" list. He ruled on Oct 13 and again on Oct 28: "Don't tell me what isn't right yet. Don't send me anything that isn't right."

## How people are made (the engine does it all)
Owner, Oct 28: "You're literally just pasting one image or replacing one image with another... If you're building everything on the same three bases, then you should just be able to mix and match." Then: "All the images are made. You just have to attach them and make it to where you can pick between them."
- The character creator is `build-rally/engine.py` + each person's master color sheet (`kit/<person>/meta.json` palettes, `master.py`). A person is a **stack of finished part images**: body (the person's own painted face and skin), shoes, trousers, top, hair. Nothing is decided pixel by pixel when a person is assembled.
- Colors change through the part's **tone map and a palette**. A garment is repainted as a whole image, so its colors never reach a hand or another garment.
- The kit lives in `cto-notes/scenario/build-rally/`. `export_kit.py` builds the parts once per pose sheet. `engine.py` stacks and paints them. `kit_check.py --repair` then `kit_check.py` checks them. `sheet.py` makes the loud-color eye check.

## Before sending anything
0. People parts: `python3 lego/check_angles.py` from `codex-test/fit`: must print `0 flags`, and every sheet it writes gets looked at.
1. `python3 kit_check.py --repair` then `python3 kit_check.py`: must print `0 flags`.
2. `python3 sheet.py <dir>`: look at every person in every pose in loud colors (orange top, sky trousers, white shoes, blond hair) beside the drawing. Hands, hair edges, hems, shoes, faces.
3. For a scene: look at every person in it at full size (not a few crops): facing, hands, clipping, scale, halos at the edges.
4. Only then send, and describe what it is and does. No caveat list.

When he names a new pain point, add a row to the table the same turn, and add a check to `kit_check.py` if a check can catch it.
