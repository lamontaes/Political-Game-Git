# People sit on the painted furniture

The audit found chairs tagged as standing positions, figures placed between diner stools, and missing desk and dais seats. The repair records those seats, chooses poses from furniture and current activity, and lists present people when a room runs out of supported positions. Chairs facing away from the camera remain recorded capacity gaps because the current people pack has no rear view.

Alternate-light camera agreement was inherited from the existing staging record and was not newly verified.

The completed room captures use an explicitly labeled placement proof with real identities from one random-place game; they are furniture/contact checks, not evidence that the same people visited every room. The normal random-place game opening was captured separately. All 19 browser checks passed. Human artwork approval remains outstanding.
