Aldermen split 4–2 on drainage bond
Mayor Ida Banks calls the vote at Tuesday's meeting at City Hall.

The Latimer Board of Aldermen voted 4 to 2 on Tuesday to borrow $1.2 million to rebuild the ditches on the north side of town, after a meeting that ran past ten o'clock.

Alderman Walter Greer, who voted no, said the city should wait for state money. "We'll be paying this off for twenty years," he said.
