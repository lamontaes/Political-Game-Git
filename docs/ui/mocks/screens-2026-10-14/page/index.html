Journal

Your memoir, in chapters, first person, on a plain page.

My life
Chapter one · Latimer

I was born in Latimer in the spring of 2006, the second of Ruth and Earl Spencer's three children. Dad worked nights at the catfish plant on Highway 8; Mom kept the books for the Methodist church and kept the rest of us in line.

Evelyn was two years older and never let me forget it. Our house on Pecan Street had a porch that leaned toward the road, and on summer evenings half the street ended up on it.

The winter I turned seventeen, the plant cut Dad's shift. He came home at six in the morning for the last time and sat at the kitchen table without taking his coat off.
