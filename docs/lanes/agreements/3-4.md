# Lanes 3 and 4: who shows what in the paper and the Journal

Owner: Lane 3 (what prints and what is told); Lane 4 (the screens that show it). Stand-in: records in test files. Check: the paper's browser check.

## Proposed by Lane 3, Nov 4 (Lane 4 to confirm or change)

### The first paper prints only fresh news (`words-opening-paper-only-fresh-news`)

- **The rule (Lane 3):** the paper's front page and its foot print only stories dated within the news engine's own `newsMaxAgeDays` of the paper's date. Older stories the player heard stay in the archive, so the news screen is not empty on day one (OWNER-DECISIONS, Oct 14). The paper keeps no list and no cutoff of its own; it reads the parameter from the news module.
- **Lane 4's file this touches:** `src/bench/calendar.ts`, only `heardItems`. It splits what the player heard into `fresh` (in the window) and `archive` (older), each newest first, with the same wording calls as today. Lane 3 writes that change, its test (`src/bench/paper-fresh.test.ts`) and one filter line in `play.mjs`'s `nightsNews` (the paper keeps rows not marked stale). Lane 4 owns `/api/news` in `handler.ts` and the screen: the paper prints `fresh`, and the archive lists `archive`.
- **Fresh January records:** where a place has fewer than six fresh stories of three kinds on day one, the missing records (an inauguration as a news record, a legislature convening, January meetings and filings) belong to Lane 1's records, filed as a pool item with `lead:` set, never filler.
- **Who updates on a clash:** whichever PR merges second updates to the other (ruling 5).
- **Check:** the item's done-when. In three drawn places, named with seeds, the first paper has no story older than `newsMaxAgeDays`, and every story names a record dated in the window. The PR quotes the front page before and after, and the paper's browser check is run.

### The Journal (Lane 3 alone)

- `src/director/journal.ts` and its rows decide every Journal line and chapter. Lane 4's Journal screen shows chapters and lines as given and words nothing itself.
