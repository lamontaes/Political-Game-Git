## Proposed by Lane 3, Nov 3 (Lane 1 to confirm or change)

### The night you lose: a relative's answer counts at the next race (`words-family-loss-scene-the-part-they-played`)

- **Lane 3 reads (Lane 1's records, unchanged):** the `election-result:<contest>` record's `totals` and `winnerId`; the campaign row `filing:<contest>:<person>` and its `volunteerIds`; the `gifts` row `<filing>|<person>` (the candidate's own money).
- **Lane 3 writes:** a `scene.reply` event from the talk at home after a lost race (`typeKey: family-loss`) with `backing: "behind" | "doubts"` when the candidate says they'll run again and the relative answers. No other change.
- **Lane 1's file this touches:** `src/core2/modules/candidacy.ts`. Its `onEvent` keeps that answer in a `backing` table (one row per candidate and relative, the latest), and `candidacyReasons` adds `householdBacking`: plus for a relative behind the run, minus for one who doubts it, more the closer they are. The weight is `candidacyHouseholdPull` in `src/core2/data/candidacy/parameters.json` (TUNABLE, spread 0.25 to 1, under `SG-P15-candidacy-weights`). This is the reference build on `claude/scene-family-loss-worked-it`, lifted by hand.
- **Who updates on a clash:** whichever PR merges second updates to the other (ruling 5). If Lane 1 would rather own the read, Lane 3 ships only the reply's `backing` fact and Lane 1 adds the table and the reason.
- **Check:** in a played world (`buildBenchWorld`, drawn place, seed printed), a lost race with a relative on the volunteer list stages the scene. A "run again" reply writes the backing row, and the next contest's `candidacyReasons` for that person shows `householdBacking` by the row's id.
