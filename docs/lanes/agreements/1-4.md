- **Controls Lane 4 adds** (buttons, panels) call the engine's acts and never write campaign or filing records themselves.

- **Controls Lane 1 adds in `src/bench`** (Lane 4 amendment, Nov 4): a button or panel for one of Lane 1's acts (filing, a vote, a desk decision) is built from the shared controls (`screen-to-mock`), sits on the screen that already shows that record (never a new menu beside the old one), calls the engine's act, and is named in the PR body under "Review: Lane 4". Its words come from the English engine's banks.

- **The path check** (`src/bench/path-check.ts`, Lane 1 entry, Nov 4, for Lane 4 to confirm): it files the player through the clerk's answer like the tests above, so when Lane 1 changes the filing rule Lane 1 updates it in the same way, named under "Review: Lane 4".
