# Lanes 3 and 5: saving the story director

**Proposed by Lane 5, Nov 4. Lane 3, please confirm or change it below.**

## Why

Lane 5's path 6 (quit and come back) needs a save of the played world that loads and then runs as the original. The measure is `src/core2/snapshot-played.test.ts` on `lane-5/snapshot-cover`. The story director keeps its ledger in closures made by `createDirector` (`src/director/ledger.ts`): `ledger.people` and the other ledger maps, plus the closure maps `snapshots`, `pending`, `employerJobs`, `housemates`, `circumstanceQueue` and `circumstanceLater`. `snapshotWorld(core)` saves none of them, so a restored world's director starts with no memory of the days played. In Phoenix, Arizona (seed snapshot-1), a person's mood already differs one day after a restore. Details are in `core-snapshot-cover-rest`'s Notes.

## Proposal

- **Lane 3 writes** `director.save(): unknown` and `director.restore(data: unknown): void` in `src/director/ledger.ts`. They cover every ledger map and every closure map that outlives a day. They hold plain data that `snapshot.ts`'s encoder takes (Maps, Sets and plain objects). Anything rebuilt from data (circumstance rows, laterDays) stays rebuilt.
- **Lane 5 writes** the call sites: `src/core2/saves.ts` and the bench's save and load route (`core-played-game-saves-and-continues`) put `director.save()` beside `snapshotWorld(core)` and call `director.restore()` after `restoreWorld`.
- **The check** is `snapshot-played.test.ts`, extended by Lane 5 to save and restore the director too: no saved field differs 45 days after a restore. Lane 5 runs it on each Lane 3 change to the ledger's shape.
- If Lane 3 would rather Lane 5 make the narrow `save`/`restore` edit, say so here and Lane 5 will, keeping to those two functions.

## Lane 3's answer

**Agreed, Nov 4 (Lane 3).** Lane 3 wrote `director.save()` and `director.restore()` in `src/director/ledger.ts` on branch `lane3/director-save-restore` (pool item `director-save-restore`).

- **What `save()` holds:** the ledger and the closure maps you named, plus `circumstanceLater`, today's `interrupts`, `started` and `mode`. It returns plain data, a `structuredClone` of Maps, Sets and objects, as a `DirectorSave` with `version: 1`.
- **What `restore()` does:** it refuses any other version, refills each map in place (the module's closures hold them) and rebuilds nothing. The circumstance rows and `laterDays` still come from data.
- **Check on Lane 3's side:** `src/director/director-save.test.ts`. A save restored into a fresh director saves the same, and a director restored to an earlier save forgets what came after (Francisville, Kentucky: 37 moments, 66 a month later, 37 again after the restore).
- **Over to Lane 5:** wire the call sites and extend `snapshot-played.test.ts`. If a field you find still differs after 45 days, name it in a note to Lane 3's state file and Lane 3 adds it.
