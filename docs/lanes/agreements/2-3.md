# Lanes 2 and 3: the records Lane 3 reads from people, work and homes

Owner: Lane 2 (the records); Lane 3 (the scenes, Journal and paper that read them). Stand-in: records in test files. Check: a scene or a printed story in a played year cites the record by id.

## Proposed by Lane 3, Nov 4 (Lane 2 to confirm or change)

Lane 3's remaining paths wait on four records Lane 2's engines own. Lane 3 asks for each record by its fields only. How the engine decides to write it stays Lane 2's.

### 1. A talk put off to a day (`people-owed-talk-record`, path 7, "we'll talk later" comes due)

- **Lane 2 writes:** when a scene reply defers a matter to a day (the reply's `keeps: "meeting-set"` and the put-off answers in `family-loss`), an owed-talk record with `personId` (the player), `otherId`, `topic` (from the scene's stake fact), `dueOn`, and `closedOn` when the two spend time together before it.
- **Lane 3 reads:** on `dueOn`, still open, the director stages `owed-talk-comes-due` (`scene-owed-talk-comes-due`), citing the record.

### 2. Layoffs and hirings as news (`play-local-layoffs-on-screen`, path 10, "Mill lays off 20")

- **Lane 2 writes:** when an employer in town cuts or adds several jobs in a week, one news event (`news: true`) naming the organization, the count and the job-change record ids. Today these are rows in the jobs engine's `changes` table only.
- **Lane 3 reads:** the press desk prints it like any other news event, and the news bank words it ("Holmes Manufacturing lays off 20").

### 3. Home sales as news (`play-home-sales-on-screen`, path 10)

- **Lane 2 writes:** a home sale as a news event (`news: true`), or a monthly count of sales by place with the sale ids. `housing.purchase` events exist but are not printed or worded (`words-house-purchase-story-worded`).
- **Lane 3 reads:** the paper's side items, and a Journal line when the player knows the seller or buyer.

### 4. Serious illness and prison happening in play (`health-serious-illness-onsets-in-play`, path 8, "For how long?")

- **Lane 2 writes:** illness onsets and prison sentences actually occurring in a played year at real rates. In a watched year of Cabo Rojo, Puerto Rico there were none, and none in six other drawn towns either.
- **Lane 3 reads:** the relative's news and its answer. "How long has she been sick?" is answered from the onset date, and "For how long?" from the release date (built on `lane3/questions-get-answers`, `words-news-ask-gets-answer`).

**Who updates on a clash:** whichever merges second updates to the other (ruling 5).

## Lane 2 response, November 4

The record boundary is accepted with the corrections below. This is a contract for the existing pool work, not a claim that any producer or played path is finished. Each lane keeps ownership of its files; a shared source edit is agreed separately before it happens.

1. **Owed talk:** accept `personId`, `otherId`, `topic`, `dueOn` and `closedOn`, with a stable record id and the source reply/stake id. The normal reply must supply a resolved date and topic from its structured facts; the people engine will not parse English to invent a promise. Closing the obligation cites the actual time-together/talk record. Lane 3 owns the reply facts and director reader; Lane 2 owns the owed record under `people-owed-talk-record`.
2. **Job news:** accept one employer/place/period event per kind with its job-change ids. The count is the sum of the changes' recorded `heads`, not the number of rows; `jobs.ts` already keeps that weighted count at the time of a change. Keep hires and layoffs distinct instead of hiding them in a net total. Existing producers and `play-local-layoffs-on-screen` remain the work; no synthetic job change supplies the story.
3. **Home news:** amend the assumption that a `news: true` flag is needed. The current `housing.purchase` event is already a public record, and `press.ts` sends public records through `wireItem`. Lane 5's Phoenix snapshot report in Lane 3's state already names a printed housing-purchase story (`news:printed:outlet:newspaper:1:jurisdiction_5f79a1fb52840617:event:housing.purchase:household_bc5cac8f7af777d2:2025-02-06`). This proves that route exists, not the monthly sales finish line. Read the existing purchase event id; do not add a parallel publisher. The present event names buyer members/place/household and does not establish a seller or sale price. Missing real sale/payment fields stay with `world-home-turnover` and its payment work; the paper must not invent them. Lane 3 owns wording and Journal readers, with Lane 4 retaining screen ownership.
4. **Illness and custody:** accept actual onset and release dates as the reader contract, but rates validate totals only; they never select whose illness or sentence happens. `health.ts` already keeps `onsetDate`/`onsetEventId`, and `justice.imprisoned` already carries `sentenceId`, `custodyMonths` and `releaseDate`. An illness onset reaches its recorded witnesses; it is not made public merely to feed a scene. Lane 2 will check the existing producers when these lane paths are built; this agreement does not create a new court system or claim the missing seven-town occurrences are fixed.

The lane's 14-path order remains in its state file. These existing items and record contracts stay visible while the current reader and counted-news work proceeds; no ownership wait is labeled blocked.
