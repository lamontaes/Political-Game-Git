# Lanes 2 and 3: the records Lane 3 reads from people, work and homes

Owner: Lane 2 (the records); Lane 3 (the scenes, Journal and paper that read them). Stand-in: records in test files. Check: a scene or a printed story in a played year cites the record by id.

## Proposed by Lane 3, Nov 4 (Lane 2 to confirm or change)

Lane 3's remaining paths wait on four records Lane 2's engines own. Lane 3 asks for each record by its fields only. How the engine decides to write it stays Lane 2's.

### 1. A talk put off to a day (`people-owed-talk-record`, path 7, "we'll talk later" comes due)

- **Lane 2 writes:** when a scene reply defers a matter to a day (the reply's `keeps: "meeting-set"` and the put-off answers in `family-loss`), an owed-talk record with `personId` (the player), `otherId`, `topic` (from the scene's stake fact), `dueOn`, and `closedOn` when the two spend time together before it.
- **Lane 3 reads:** on `dueOn`, still open, the director stages `owed-talk-comes-due` (`scene-owed-talk-comes-due`), citing the record.

### 2. Layoffs and hirings as news (`play-local-layoffs-on-screen`, path 10, "Mill lays off 20")

- **Lane 2 writes:** when an employer in town cuts or adds several jobs in a week, one news event (`news: true`) naming the organization, the count and the job-change record ids. Today these are rows in the jobs engine's `changes` table only.
- **Lane 3 reads:** the press desk prints it like any other news event, and the news bank words it ("Holmes Manufacturing lays off 20").

### 3. Home sales as news (`play-home-sales-on-screen`, path 10)

- **Lane 2 writes:** a home sale as a news event (`news: true`), or a monthly count of sales by place with the sale ids. `housing.purchase` events exist but are not printed or worded (`words-house-purchase-story-worded`).
- **Lane 3 reads:** the paper's side items, and a Journal line when the player knows the seller or buyer.

### 4. Serious illness and prison happening in play (`health-serious-illness-onsets-in-play`, path 8, "For how long?")

- **Lane 2 writes:** illness onsets and prison sentences actually occurring in a played year at real rates. In a watched year of Cabo Rojo, Puerto Rico there were none, and none in six other drawn towns either.
- **Lane 3 reads:** the relative's news and its answer. "How long has she been sick?" is answered from the onset date, and "For how long?" from the release date (built on `lane3/questions-get-answers`, `words-news-ask-gets-answer`).

**Who updates on a clash:** whichever merges second updates to the other (ruling 5).
