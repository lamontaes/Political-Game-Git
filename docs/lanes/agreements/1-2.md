# Agreement 1-2: Lane 1 (run and govern) and Lane 2 (the world)

Owner: Lane 1. Written Nov 4, before Lane 1's next change to `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts` (`legislature-ask-skips-bills-the-committee-will-hold`).

## What each side reads and writes

- **One engine moves every bill.** A council, county board, state legislature or Congress bill moves through `legislature.ts`, from filing through committee, the floor and passage. The body, its seats and its rules are data rows. Lane 1 owns the file. Lane 2's governments that pass laws in the background (`world-legislating.ts`) call its public functions and never keep a second path for a bill.
- **How the crowd votes.** A member's vote is `legislature.ts`'s roll call, reading the member's lean, district, party and asks. Lane 2 supplies the world's inputs it reads (district facts, the laws in force, the bill catalog). It does not write votes or committee decisions itself.
- **What Lane 1 changes next.** The committee stage for a bill on the next floor day now runs the evening before (`committeesBeforeTheFloor`, through the same `holdCommittee`), so a sponsor asks members only about bills the committee reports. A bill's committee decision now carries the evening-before date instead of the floor day. Lane 2 code that reads a committee record's date reads it as "decided by the floor day", not "on the floor day".
- **Who updates on a clash:** whichever PR merges second updates to the other (ruling 5).
