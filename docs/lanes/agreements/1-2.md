# Agreement 1-2: Lane 1 (run and govern) and Lane 2 (the world)

Owner: Lane 1. Written Nov 4, before Lane 1's next change to `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts` (`legislature-ask-skips-bills-the-committee-will-hold`).

## What each side reads and writes

- **One engine moves every bill.** A council, county board, state legislature or Congress bill moves through `legislature.ts`, from filing through committee, the floor and passage. The body, its seats and its rules are data rows. Lane 1 owns the file. Lane 2's governments that pass laws in the background (`world-legislating.ts`) call its public functions and never keep a second path for a bill.
- **How the crowd votes.** A member's vote is `legislature.ts`'s roll call, reading the member's lean, district, party and asks. Lane 2 supplies the world's inputs it reads (district facts, the laws in force, the bill catalog). It does not write votes or committee decisions itself.
- **What Lane 1 changes next.** The committee stage for a bill on the next floor day now runs the evening before (`committeesBeforeTheFloor`, through the same `holdCommittee`), so a sponsor asks members only about bills the committee reports. A bill's committee decision now carries the evening-before date instead of the floor day. Lane 2 code that reads a committee record's date reads it as "decided by the floor day", not "on the floor day".
- **Who updates on a clash:** whichever PR merges second updates to the other (ruling 5).

## The check

In a played year, a council bill, a state bill and a Congress bill each reach a roll call through `legislature.ts`, each named by its bill id and roll call id.

## Bill values at the bill's jurisdiction (Lane 2's request; accepted by Lane 1, Nov 4)

Item `laws-bill-before-reads-bill-level`. Lane 2 may wire `currentAmount` and `billSections` in `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts` to one law-or-budget-in-force reader at the bill's own jurisdiction, and use the same reader in `bill-catalog.ts`. The reader returns the record it read as well as the value. Floor or additive treatment comes from the existing doctrine data. A later phase of the same bill keeps its `earlier` value. Nothing else in `legislature.ts` changes: not voting, the calendar, committees or enactment. `voting.ts`, `campaigns.ts`, `staff.ts` and `src/bench` are out of scope. A wider need is agreed here first.

The check: the Brookside regression and two drawn worlds, named with replay numbers, where the catalog's and the paper's values match the law or budget record they cite by id; `legislature.test.ts` passes; New Game opens.

Pool item: `people-counted-readers-stances-from-stories`. Lane 2 owns the story-delivery and views changes. Lane 1 owns `campaign-views.ts`, `electorate.ts` and `modules/voting.ts`; no change in those files has been made for this proposal.

Audience counts must follow the existing press delivery records, not choose individual outcomes by a roll or rate.
- Requested shared scope: add an optional per-question shift to `StanceProfile` and have `groupStance` add it to the unclamped opening stance and then clamp once, exactly as `viewOn` does. Preserve that profile in counted ballot rows and their existing explanation reader; invalidate any cached district view when a stance changes. No new election engine or changed vote/turnout formula.
- Check: a naturally printed story in a drawn played year changes a counted stance with its news source; fill-in retains the stance; an actual election reads it and saved ballot explanations match the cast result. Removing the story step must fail the regression. Run the same four-year town against base and head, each year within 20%.
