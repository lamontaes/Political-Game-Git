# Playtest records — what each one is, 2026-09-22

Written for a reader who was not there. Every record below is a walk through
the actual game in a browser, by a person-shaped route: the ordinary creator, a
named town, a stated age, no fixtures, no injected records and no developer
menu. Where a route does not exist, that is recorded as the finding rather than
forced.

Each record names the branch and commit it was walked on, and the browser.
**Chromium 141 locally, which is not the version CI runs** — a browser count
from a walk is evidence about the game, not a CI verdict.

Three states are kept apart throughout and should not be read as one: a defect
found, a thing built but unreachable, and a thing genuinely not built.

## How to read a claim in any of these

Every claim names the tree and commit it was measured on, and the input. Two
opposite-sounding claims can both be true of one game: "Alaska opens" and
"Alaska refuses" were both correct, of Sitka and of Anchorage. Where a record
says something was inferred rather than measured, it says so, and that
distinction is the point.
