# A full year campaigned, and the same sentence at the end

Walked 2026-09-22 11:00Z on `main` at `c659f256`, in Chromium 141. Ordinary
creator, Phoenix, Arizona, age 44. Two hundred presses of the week control,
committing every week the planner offered one and clearing every stuck
session, from filing to election day and past it.

This closes the question the earlier campaign walk left open: a campaigning
life had never reached election day, so no outcome had ever been compared.

## What happened

- Filed for governor: **"Robinson for Governor of Arizona · 302 days to go"**.
- **38 weeks committed. 152 sessions cleared.** That is 38 × 4 exactly — every
  single one cleared by _giving up_, not one performed.
- Treasury: **USD 0.00** at every check across the whole year.
- Campaign log at the end: **one entry**, "A field shift · January 5, 2026".
- Election day arrives on schedule, and the result is:

> Veronica Robinson lost. That is a thing that happened to them, not the end
> of them — tomorrow is still there.

Word for word the sentence the life that never campaigned got.

## What that does and does not establish

**It does not show that campaigning fails to change the result.** It shows the
same thing as the Denver enumeration, at a year's scale: the player never
campaigned. 152 cleared out of 152 booked, and `no-campaign-session-can-be-
carried-out-2026-09-22.md` explains why — no campaign session is performable
in any of the three towns walked.

So the two identical losses are not a finding about the election model. They
are a finding about the door: **two lives that differ only in how many
bookings they gave up end the same way, because neither one ever did anything
a campaign consists of.**

Whether a performed session would move the treasury, the log, or the result is
**still untested**, and there is still no way to test it from inside the game.

**It does close one thing.** The race resolves whether or not the player
engages with it, and the game does not stall or lose the election date across
two hundred presses and a year of simulated time. The countdown ran 302 → 238
→ 174 → 110 → 46 → decided, at a steady 64 days per 40 presses, which is the
four-hour crawl measured earlier holding constant over a full year rather than
degrading.

## The single log entry is the sharpest detail

One line, dated the day of filing, for a field shift that was never carried
out. The campaign log is real, it is wired to something, and in a year of
committing every available week it recorded one event — the first booking. A
booking is not a doing, and this is the one place in the game that says so out
loud without meaning to.

## Not established

- Whether a player can ever win. Two results reached, both losses, both by
  lives that performed no campaign work.
- Whether the result responds to anything at all. Untestable from the player's
  side until a session can be performed.
- Whether the same holds for a legislative seat rather than a governorship.
  Only governor's races have been walked to a result.
