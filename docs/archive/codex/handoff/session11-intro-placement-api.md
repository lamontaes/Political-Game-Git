# Intro proof reads the spot the engine actually selected

The capture lane can read the chosen staging spot and the saved actor selection
without guessing either from pixels. Public-office illustrations remain separate
from recorded attendance. Missing physical staging stays visible in the receipt.
New scenes remain candidates until the owner has seen all six fresh-game sets.

## Capture seam

Session 4 owns tests/e2e/session4-intro-placement-proof.spec.ts and the one
combined six-place receipt. Session 11 owns the placement adapter and source
lineage. No second capture run or central room-presence writer is introduced.

WorldOrientationPanel exposes JSON in data-placement-trace on its existing
world-orientation test node. The attribute is recomputed for the current card.
introPlacementTrace(placements, actors) consumes actual BackdropPerson results
and the existing LivingSceneActor projection. It reads slotId, slotRole,
resolvedPose, resolvedView, facing, depth and personId from the chosen placement.
It joins exact person ID and title to the actor's recordIds, provenance and
presenceBasis. Missing or ambiguous joins are explicit gaps. Its art field names
the actual recipe, resolved files and pack version.

The returned unstagedActors carry their real selection records, plus the reason
no-physical-placement. Existing portrait cards that use fixed presentation
boxes have no physical staging contract and are not assigned invented depth,
facing or slots. Congress/state/locality portrait layouts therefore cannot be
called tag-placement proof merely because they display the right officials.
Legislature placements have no matching living-scene chapter and report
missing-actor. Parents have no chapter either: an empty trace does not establish
that no parents are visible. Additional state portraits outside the chapter
cast also lack selection telemetry. The capture must retain these gaps.
The existing Oval Office's standing spots require turned views. The current
pack lacks matching turned faces/hair, and those people remain missing-art
overflow. This is an existing source-availability gap, not new approval or a
reason to fabricate a turned face.

## Candidate coverage

The published workflow is scripts/art-asset-factory/scene-coverage.mjs. It reports
117 places and 870 spots, including per-place totals, role totals, pose-family
and pack posture/view entries per physical slot kind, measured surface counts
and registry hashes. It rejects unresolved surface references, geometry drift,
missing role tags and missing pose tags. It does not count an art contract as
approved or available pixels.

All 870 spots have additive IDs and role tags. Most ordinary furniture remains
neutral rather than being assigned invented authority. Seven pose-family
contracts and 30 pack posture/view entries declare fit. There are no leaning
pose contracts; the 133 leaning spots do not become standing slots. Surface
references reuse 56 measured quads across 19 places; 33 permit live content.
The existing separate surface registry audits 59 places. Eleven places retain
explicit unmarked-surface notes; other un-audited places remain coverage gaps.
All prior staging coordinates, scale, furniture groups and facing are preserved.
All prior pack fields and family contacts/approval statuses are preserved.

## Presence boundary

presentAt(world, {jurisdictionId, place, organizationId}, moment) filters the
existing work roster by exact employer, whereabouts and alive-at-time. It returns
computed-work-schedule provenance and never writes attendance. Session 4 retains
recorded-room-presence.ts ownership. Its current room/event guards remain the
central attendance contract.

recordWorkAbsence appends one life.whereabouts.absence-recorded event through
recordWorldEvent. It records a half-open interval, person ID, reason, recorded
moment and provenance. whereaboutsAt checks that record first. This does not
change pay or job status, assign a destination, or decide illness/vacation.
The reader indexes the unchanged absence-event group and avoids rebuilding it
for unrelated history writes. Old saves need no additional history arrays.
