# The opening: questions that put you in power, a quiet first day, a light childhood, and the Creator's county and population (bank id b33, phase P1 council journey, unlocks the golden path "18 -> clerk -> council")

Verified against origin/main 40d67708d (Oct 6).

## What the player experiences

Before the life starts you are asked ten to twelve questions, and none is a trivia quiz. You are the mayor of a made-up city and the vendor agreement for license-plate cameras lands on your desk: sign for 90 days of stored plates and police can chase the hit-and-run cars, sign for 14 days and some leads go cold, or decline. A different player, or the same player on another day, meets a governor with a clemency file, an oversight chair with a disaster contract, a trial judge with a sentence to weigh, a council member one vote short. Each question says what office you hold, what your authority really is, who is hurt or helped by each choice, and never a guaranteed outcome. Your answer changes which question comes next ("why 14 days?"; "what if the police chief now says the cars were found?"), and branches rejoin on related subjects. Nothing you answer writes a fact about the life you are about to live.

Then the fade, and a quiet first day. You wake in your own house at seven. Nobody has asked you for anything yet. A morning note (what the paper, the kitchen table or a text from home says, composed from your world's records) tells you where you are, who is in the next room, what the town is like this week, and what is on the calendar later in the month. You walk the house, the block, the school or the workplace and learn who is who, and the world makes its first request only after you have looked around.

If you start young, ages 5 to 17 play as a few light scenes, not a chore list: a school friend, a rival, a teacher, a move. Some come back years later: the rival files for the same ballot, the friend sits across the clerk's counter. In the Creator, every town in the list shows its county and its population, and the birth years offered are only the ones that can start a life.

- Fixed rules: zero dice; nothing blank or placeholder, estimate and mark it; one rule for all 50 states, D.C. and territories; emergent not authored; one writer per record kind; delete what you replace.

Convert `MAYOR_PLATE_READER` and its follow-up into rows (keep key and ids so saved answers replay). Write rows for the six owner-rated roles (mayor, trial judge three-way, governor clemency, oversight chair, president, council member one vote short), then widen toward about 50 nodes. `Replaces:` the two const items in `setup-curated-question-bank.ts` (delete the file when empty). Must not: invent a number; every figure cites a research row or is marked "ESTIMATED FROM AVERAGE: ...".
2. **Prose from the packet.** Render each prompt and option through `composeGroundedLine` (Session 4) from the packet's facts; the plate-reader text becomes the first regression fixture. `Replaces:` the fixed `prompt`/`text` strings of curated items, and `setup-playtest65-copy.ts` and `setup-text39-copy.ts` once the old bank keys they serve are gone from `SETUP_QUESTIONNAIRE_BANK` (grep first; delete only what is orphaned). Same seed + version + answers must give the same text.
3. **Selection fills the route with power questions.** In `nextQuestionnaireStep` the deep route draws 10-12: 3 lived items first (`CURATED_LIVED_BEFORE_WIDENING`), then dilemma rows, branching by recorded choice id, rejoining on related roles, never two from the same role in a row. No RNG, no world write. Question availability reads `mayAnswerQuestion` so a judge is never asked to enact a law. `Replaces:` the `policy-docket` items that carry no place and people (15 in `setup-questionnaire-bank.ts`) once packets cover their subjects.
4. **The quiet first day.** One rule in the day projection: on the player's first day (and until their first conversation or first leaving the house) the list shows nothing owed; no deadline, summons or notice is created for that day by the notice writers (they check one `firstDayQuiet` read from the world's start date). `Replaces:` nothing; add the read once in `ordinary-life.ts` and call it from the notice writers' gate. 5. **The morning note.** A document on the player's table at seven, composed by `composeGroundedLine` from records only: place, who is home (`buildLifeIntroduction`), one local fact from `projectOpeningTown`, one line of the state's present business from `projectOpeningLegislature`, and the next dated thing on the calendar. 6. **Childhood scenes that return.** On top of `projectChildhoodMoment`: schedule scenes by life event (term start, move, sibling), not by a monthly beat (follows the pacing research); scenes pull a friend or rival from the school's recorded students (Session 6/b23 school roster) and write a relationship record through `appendChildhoodEntry` and `recordRelationshipInteraction`.
