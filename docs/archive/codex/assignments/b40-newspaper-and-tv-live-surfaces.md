# The newspaper and the TV as live surfaces (bank id b40, phase P3 looks / P2 equip and inform; unlocks "the news looks like real newspapers", "screens tagged in every place", election-night TV)

Verified against origin/main 40d67708d (Oct 6).

## What the player experiences

You are in the diner and the TV over the counter is on: the local station's evening segment, drawn to fit the set's glass, with a ticker along the bottom. You click it and the segment opens as a story. On the counter lies the town daily; you click it and see a newsstand of mastheads (the national daily, your state paper, the town weekly, a sports-and-entertainment outlet), each set in its own type. You open one: a lead story about three paragraphs long, a column of smaller stories, the date. Every name in a story (the council member, the senator, the athlete) is a button that opens that person's record. Two outlets covering the same vote frame it differently, because their owners and editors differ, and you can tell which is which from the masthead.

The clerk's office has a monitor on the wall; the home has its coffee-table papers and living-room TV. All of them open the same reader, fed by the same published stories. A neighbor reads the sports pages and talk radio, a retiree the daily; the player's own habits shape what the room's paper leads with. On election night the TV in your room shows the count (b03's scene) through this same set.

## Owner decisions it rests on

- "screens and the newspapers are supposed to be interactive" (9/27 15:26, owner-words-opus-oct1-3.md item 40); the news video is "flat and doesn't actually fit with the TV screen" (9/29 15:30).
- OCD-UI-005 (Register): "Its presentation must look like authored newspapers, magazines and publication front pages, with recognizable mastheads, editorial hierarchy, typography"; "Article text, dates, identities and numbers remain live; a flattened generated newspaper image is not a dynamic publication"; "A substantive story may be about three paragraphs when the represented facts support it"; "Do not manufacture crises, quotations, motives, numbers or outcomes".
- OCD-UI-005: "The default news view should foreground interesting, consequential national developments"; "Outlet spin follows the existing represented claims and editorial model"; "Link actual people, places, offices, bills".
- Register (art): "Dynamic names, news text, dates and statistics stay data-rendered rather than baked into generated pixels"; "Physical frames, textures and empty visual shells may be authored".
- Roadmap: "Screens, posters, newspapers and TVs tagged in every place"; "Every person named anywhere ... is a real person you can click, and news includes lighter outlets."
- Fixed rules: zero dice; nothing blank or placeholder, estimate and mark it; one rule for all 50 states, D.C. and territories; emergent not authored; one writer per record kind; delete what you replace.

2. **Newsstand and masthead pages.** Replace the plain `SceneSurfaceReader` news body with the `NewsDesk` model: from a paper, the newsstand of mastheads then one publication's front page (lead plus column, date, `place`); from a TV, the segment then the story. 3. **Three-paragraph stories from records.** Extend `editorialParagraphs` with a third paragraph built only from records already tied to the event: the actors and their offices, and what is unresolved or next (the pending step of the measure, the next vote or filing date) read through `storyLawReader`. Must not: invented quotes, motives, numbers.
4. **Outlets with their own slant, and a lighter outlet.** Slant is the record, not a label: framing words and which claim leads come from the outlet's owner directives and editor (`ownership.ts`, existing claim and editorial model). 5. **TV segments that fit the set.** Draw `RoomTelevision` inside the measured screen quad (`television-screen-slot`, `quadMatrix3d` in `backdrop-surfaces.ts:840`) with the painted bezel on top, so the picture takes the set's perspective and glass: anchor graphic, lower-third with the outlet name and date, ticker `projectRoomMedia` already builds. 6. **Every name is a person.** Each name in a story or ticker renders as a button via `people` (already `NewsDesk.tsx:364`); in the reader and on the TV segment the same. 7. **Each person reads by their own habits.** Consume b30's `newsHabit` and b22's `newsHabitOf`: the room's paper and TV choose outlets from the resident's habit (`roomStation`/`roomPaper` today pick by place only), and a NPC's exposure is recorded by `recordStoryHeardExposure` when their habit reads it.
