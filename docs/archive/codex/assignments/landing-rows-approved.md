# The approved landing rows (Outcome Web Proposal, Oct 5 6:45 p.m.; owner approved parts 1–7 and all 36 for 1.0)

Row = (kind, record, ranking inputs). Zero = write a zero record with the month and cause. Nobody is notified by the landing itself; exposure writers do that.

## Four kinds

- **FLAG:** rank residents by recorded risk inputs; the top share equal to the change in points flips state.
- **AMOUNT:** scale every recorded amount of that type by the change.
- **EXPOSURE:** every address gets the place value, accumulated.
- **EVENT:** rank by recorded exposure; the top share gets the event through the existing writer.

## Measures by kind

- **FLAG:** uninsured, poverty, SNAP, homeless, food insecure, child asthma, teen cannabis, has AC, rents vs owns, works (older, mothers, married women), graduated on time, math/reading proficient, has degree, voted, payday loan, medical debt in collections, in prison, recycles, rides transit, took Amtrak, park nearby.
- **AMOUNT:** median earnings, prices, electricity price, property tax, corporate tax, drug out-of-pocket, spending per student, farmland value, borrowing cost / loan rates.
- **EXPOSURE:** air particulates, hot days, water violations, litter, emissions, road condition, transit service, speed limit, officers per 1,000.
- **EVENT:** violent crime, firearm homicide, overdose death, infant death, low birthweight, traffic death, birth, moving in/out, renter moves, farmland sold.
