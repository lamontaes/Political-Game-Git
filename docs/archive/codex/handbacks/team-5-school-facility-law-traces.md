## Research

Primary read: [20 U.S.C. 1412](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title20-section1412&num=0&edition=prelim),
HTTP 200. As a condition of assistance under this part, a state's plan must
assure a free appropriate public education for children with disabilities
aged three through twenty-one, subject to the section's exceptions. It requires
child identification, location and evaluation, including children attending
private schools. It also requires education in the least restrictive
appropriate environment and bars funding mechanisms that cause placements
contrary to that requirement.

These are specific federal conditions and protections. A general state
repair authorization does not replace them or establish compliance. The
section does not supply a causal repair-to-attainment estimate, local capital
borrowing authority or the powers of all fifty-six jurisdictions. Repeal of
an authored repair program does not repeal these federal requirements.
