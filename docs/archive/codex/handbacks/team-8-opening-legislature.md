# Your recorded state representatives stand on the opening card

The legislature card stages the saved members representing your home, with
their names and offices above their heads. It is a public-role illustration;
opening the card creates no attendance, acquaintance, travel or knowledge.

## 1. Why-chain

The card formerly showed an empty chamber because its people array was empty.
The names existed in projectGovernmentBrowser's representedBy reader. That
reader resolves the home's recorded district membership against the saved
state seat plan and actual member records. The new projection uses only those
member rows, and the existing figure recipe uses each saved person's appearance.
The illustration spaces those figures across the chamber floor. Bedrock is
district and member bookkeeping, saved appearance and authored presentation
layout, not an actor's decision to attend. A missing district/holder stays absent.
