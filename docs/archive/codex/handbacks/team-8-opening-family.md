# The family card shows the saved parents in the home region

The family opening now illustrates the saved parents and guardians beside their
recorded names and relationships, with the same home-region selection as the
state card. It creates no people, travel or claim of present attendance.

## 1. Why-chain

The old parents card read projectOpeningFamily but supplied no cast; its backdrop
always selected main-street. The new pure openingFamilyPeople reader projects
only that canonical parents roster, using saved personName and introduction.
The panel supplies SavedPersonFigure and labels from those identities. The
regional picture uses the same supplied regionalPlate or selected regionScene
as the state card. A miss is neutral, never a guessed Midwestern street.
Bedrock is saved parent/guardian relationships and authored regional coverage;
there is no attendance, relationship or appearance writer in this path.

coverage and the production useWorldOrientation reader decide which picture is
admissible for the saved home. The family illustration is a role illustration,
not a photograph or claim that a deceased or separately housed parent is in the
room today. The current saved figure reader remains unchanged.
