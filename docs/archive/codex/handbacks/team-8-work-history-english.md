# Work history states the job starts actually on record

The life-so-far paragraph now begins work history with the saved active job's
start, then states earlier saved job starts. It no longer uses an open-ended
past-job phrase that suggests a career gap or continuous employment.

## 1. Why-chain

The old opening copied life-introduction work grounding: an ended job became
You worked at name from year, while current work became You work at name, and
have since year. A distant teenage job and current adult job therefore looked
like an unexplained39-year career gap. The new pure reader follows actual work
relationships, current work status and organization names, excludes future
starts and unnamed employers, then renders a grounded English tell bank. Active
jobs read Since year, you've worked at name; other saved jobs state only their
start year. Bedrock is saved job start/status/name bookkeeping and authored
wording. No unrecorded job or unemployment interval is inferred.

## 2. Research

CTO7:38 permits truthful sparse-record wording when real intervening history is
not available. Current history supplies a teenage part-time job and a current
adult job in these cases; it does not establish the intervening employers.
The owner's about-four-year observation is not evidence for a fixed actor churn
rule, so no interval, invented job, fabricated employer or outcome rate is added.
The new reader preserves all saved named job records and their source IDs.
