**What.** Source the numbers behind the deep past, peer networks, profiles and partnerships. Each row below is a registered stopgap whose replacement waits on sourced or estimated data, collapsed here from 6 pool item(s) on Oct 19, 2026 so one researcher takes the engine family at once. For each row, find a public source with a year and a unit, record the figure as a SOURCED or ESTIMATED parameter row with a citation and a spread, put the research file under data/research/core/ with a reader, and resolve the stopgap row. Where a row's replacement is a mechanic rather than a number, source what that mechanic will read and leave the mechanic to the area's builder (name the pool item in the PR).

- `SG-P15-partnership-model` in src/core2/opening-partnership.ts: Opening household members are aged from the place's records by household type: the chance of having a partner now comes from a yearly chain of first marriage (ACS 2024 never-married shares by age), divorce (NSFG women's first-marriage survival, used for every marriage), widowhood (SSA 2023 life table, with the spouse's age from the CPS 2023 spouse age gap) and remarriage (NCFMR 2022); a partner's age follows the national CPS 2023 spouse age gap for every couple, including same-sex couples; parents of children under 18 follow national CPS 2023 parents by age and living arrangement; a household of related housemates is always one parent and one grown child; parents' grown children at home are a seeded count from the parent's births and the place's share of adults living in a parent's home (ACS B09021); homes of unrelated housemates take the place's nonfamily household sizes (B11016), each housemate's sex and age drawn on their own. Replacement: Place-level ACS 5-year sex-by-age, marital-status and household-relationship tables for every seated place and territory, cohabitation and second-marriage survival from NSFG, the spouse age gap and parents' ages by place, and siblings, grandparents raising grandchildren and older parents living in a grown child's home seated as their own household types. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/parameters.json:spouseAgeGapOpenRowYears)
- `SG-P8-civic-inputs` in src/core2/data/civic-inputs.json: Current indexed institution and canonical generated employer classifications stand in for historical public directories. Replacement: Era-specific directories with explicit institution access, membership and authority records.
- `SG-P8-deep-past` in src/core2/data/deep-past.json: Generated summarized school cohorts reuse common grade stages, present institution names and current-locality proxies where past places are unrecorded; this is not sourced personal attendance or historical institution existence. Replacement: Era- and locality-specific sourced institution histories and validated cohort distributions; preserve existing personal facts. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/parameters.json:deepPastMiddleSchoolOffsetYears, src/core2/data/parameters.json:deepPastHighSchoolOffsetYears)
- `SG-P15-kin-light-past` in src/core2/deep-past.ts: Relatives living outside the town (husk-tier kin) keep only their family and move facts at the opening; their own schooling, work and era history is not generated. Replacement: Generate a relative's deep past when the game first simulates them (promotion from husk tier), from the same deep-past rules residents use.
- `SG-P8-opening-peer-network` in src/core2/data/opening-peer-network.json: Who became acquainted in an estimated historical school cohort and who still remembers the other's name. Replacement: Admit sourced personal acquaintance histories where available; otherwise calibrate explicit generated school/community social priors and recognition persistence against appropriate network/cohort observables, retaining source uncertainty and named provenance. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/parameters.json:openingPeerGroupSize)
- `SG-P15-opening-profile` in src/core2/opening-profile.ts: Each adult's education and faith at the opening, read from their state's CES 2024 shares by age band with a household share; education and faith are treated as independent within a state and age band, and children carry no attainment. Replacement: Attainment from the person's own schooling history (deep past) checked against ACS B15001 by state, age and sex; faith from upbringing and the Pew Religious Landscape Study.

**Rules.** Research before building: real data checks the simulation and never decides an outcome by itself; a sourced rate calibrates the band the proof reports against, never the chance one person acts. Estimate, never UNKNOWN: a value the source lacks for a place starts from the real average with a per-world spread, marked ESTIMATED with its source in data, never shown to the player. One rule, all 56 places: a figure that varies by place is a per-place table in data, never a state named in logic. Sliding scales and causes: if an engine reads the number as a cap, a cutoff or a chance, file a bug for the builder instead of sourcing it. Follow .agents/skills/researcher/SKILL.md.

**Done when.** Each listed row is SOURCED or ESTIMATED in its parameter table with a citation (publisher, year, URL) and a spread (low, high, unit, citation); each stopgap row above is resolved in src/core2/data/stopgaps.json with the source named; the research-data-reader guard (tests/guards/research-data-has-reader.test.ts) is green; the tripwire and calibration-guard tests pass; and a one-year played world (buildBenchWorld, a drawn place from all 56, seed named in the PR) reports each band and where the town landed.
