**What.** Prepare a trailer shot list and a price proposal for the owner to sign off. Both are owner decisions: build the simplest proposal and flag it.

**Cause.** Design: docs/roadmap/later-specs-draft.md section M7-06 (approved by the owner Oct 12) and docs/packages/M7-early-access.md.

**Fix.** Write the shot list as moments the running game plays (each naming its screen or scene) and a price proposal with the comparable prices it rests on. Put both under OWNER DECISIONS NEEDED.

**Time box.** 4 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note and stop.

**Rules.** Owner decisions are flagged, never waited on: write the question under OWNER DECISIONS NEEDED in the PR body and in OWNER-DECISIONS.md. Look before you ask: search OWNER-DECISIONS.md for a price ruling first.

**Done when.** A second session plays a drawn place on main and finds every shot in the list playable as written, by screen or scene, and finds the price proposal with its comparables ready for the owner.

**Waits on.** Early-access preparation (AGENTS.md: saves and release work wait for it) and the weekly reschedule.
