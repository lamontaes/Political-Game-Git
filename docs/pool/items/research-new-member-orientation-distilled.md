**What.** On Sept 22 the owner asked for real orientation programs for newly elected officials so he could decide what goes in the game: "have GPT search up orientations for legislators. If it can get examples from all 50 states at levels of government, maybe five representative samples for each state at the local level. But I feel like state and federal level, those orientations, have it distill those orientations to me and I'll decide what should go in the game." The research question `new-member-transition-and-orientation` was only partly answered (Congress's allowance and programs, plus several states) and never reached him. On Oct 29 he ruled that learning the job is "a mix of short scenes in a real orientation", so the game needs the real content: what a new council member, county official, state legislator, governor and member of Congress is actually taught, by whom, when, and for how long.

**Cause.** Design: docs/research/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md (new-member-transition-and-orientation, partly answered 2026-09-22 with the Congress and several states) and the owner's Sept 22 request quoted in this item; the Oct 29 ruling is in docs/coordination/OWNER-DECISIONS.md.

**Fix.** Finish the research for every state legislature, D.C. and Puerto Rico and about five local samples per state from pages read, then write one report in docs/research/ that ends with a one-page summary of which parts would be a played stop or scene and which would only be summarized, for the owner to choose.

**Time box.** 6 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note and stop.

**Rules.** Research before building: every program is cited from a page actually read, with its date and the body that runs it, and nothing is invented to fill a state. One rule, all 56 places: the samples cover local programs from every state plus D.C. and the territories where a program exists, and state and federal programs, named in data, never in logic. This is a game, not a spreadsheet: the distillation names which parts would be a played stop or scene and which only summarized, for the owner to choose. Look before you ask: the CTO brings the choices to the owner in one sitting with the reading line.

**Done when.** A second session opens the report in docs/research/ and finds, per level (town or city, county, state legislature, governor, Congress), the real orientation's topics, schedule, length and who runs it, with at least five cited local samples from different states and every state legislature that publishes one, ending with a one-page summary the owner can pick from. The report passes `npm run report:check` as its test, and the CTO has brought it to the owner in a sitting with the choices recorded in docs/coordination/OWNER-DECISIONS.md. A second session picks a drawn place and finds its state legislature, and a local sample from that state, in the report.

**Waits on.** Nothing.
