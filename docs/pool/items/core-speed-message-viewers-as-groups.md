**What.** emitMessage sends each paid ad or mail as one event with every viewer as a witness, so the views, drives and life engines respond per viewer. Viewers nobody watches take it through hearAsGroups (the speech path, core-speed-hearers-as-groups); the message row keeps every viewer for word passed on. A world candidate's ad campaign runs every month of an election year, so the cost grows with the town's size. Grouping viewers by like hearers cuts that cost without changing who sees what.

**Rules.** Level of detail (owner, Nov 9): the player's circle and focus and officials stay individual; the player's own campaign is untouched. One model, two tiers. No dice. Timed in alternating pairs.

**Done when.** In a played world at Gunnison, Utah (key 4932660, seed n8f6) over four years, the branch is timed against core-speed-hearers-as-groups in two alternating pairs of both orders, with year 4 faster in both; the records proof matches by record id, and town race winners are compared against both main and the speech branch in a test.
