**What.** A sales tax raise falls on businesses, most of the money leaves town, and the government that raised it gains little or loses. Measured in the law sweep in Marion, Mississippi (seed 7df73e53cd67), against a twin world with no bill. The town board added 10.59 points: businesses ended $277,900 lower and households $24,100 lower, governments gained $19,600, and $283,600 was recorded as leaving to the outside. When the county board passed the same raise, governments ended $7,700 lower. The sweep ran 120 days for local bodies. In LaGrange, Wyoming (seed 63355aee286d) the result held: businesses ended $80,000 lower, households $400 lower, and $72,000 left town. The audit did not trace whether shoppers went elsewhere or the tax was paid outward, and it lists the county result among those that point the wrong way. Trace the tax by the payments' own labels from the till to the treasury, and fix it at the cause for every government that levies a sales tax: the tax collected on sales in the place is paid to the treasury of the government whose law levied it, a government that raises its rate ends with more unless its sales fell for a recorded reason, and money that leaves to the outside has a named cause. Seen: the county's treasury, which ends lower after it raises a sales tax, and the Metrics tab's money result.

**Cause.** Measured: Marion, Mississippi (2845160), world seed 7df73e53cd67, twin worlds for 376 days to January 31, 2026 on the train at 53e4b29fe8, one with a raise passed through the real body on the first day and one with no bill, every journal post summed by its own label (the command is in Notes). Three things add up to what the audit saw, and none of them is the payee choice in `syncSalesTax`: the tax reaches the treasury of the government that levied it.

1. The seller pays all of it. `taxInside` (src/core2/modules/purchase-tax.ts, line 178) takes each level's share out of a price that does not move, so a raise changes only what sellers remit. Town board raise, 0.062 to 10.65 points: the city collected $1,931,400 against $12,500 in the twin, and businesses remitted $1,800,800 more. County board raise, none to 10.59 points: the county collected $1,918,300 and businesses remitted $1,787,000 more. This is `prices-sales-tax-shifts-to-shelf-price`, which has its cause.
2. Every other level's take falls. `syncSalesTax` (src/core2/modules/budgets.ts) sets each level's share as its rate over 100 plus the total, inside the same price. When one level raises its rate the total grows, so Mississippi's 7 points brought in $1,304,400 against $1,421,700 in the twin: $117,300 less after the town's raise and $118,500 less after the county's. The county's raise also left the city $12,200 short: the city's 0.062 points were not a law of its own but the state's average local rate, which stands at the city level only while no city or county in the chain has its own row (src/core2/laws-in-force.ts, the `localAverage` step), so it ends the day the county's law begins.
3. The reserve sweep sends the gain outside the month it arrives. `sweepReserves` (src/core2/budgets-treasury.ts) moves whatever a treasury holds above its reserve to the outside account, and the reserve is sized from budgeted spending, which a tax raise does not change. The county swept $1,738,000 more than its twin, 91 percent of what the raise collected, and ended $180,800 higher. The city swept $724,000 more, paid $333,300 more in wages and $277,900 more for purchases it had been too short of cash to pay in full (both go to the outside account), and ended $490,100 higher. This is the same sweep as in `money-town-tax-raise-leaves-its-own-treasury-no-richer`, which has its cause.

Taken together after the county's raise: county up $180,800, state down $31,100, federal down $97,400, city down $900. The audit's "governments ended $7,700 lower" over 120 days is this same sum earlier in the year. Every dollar that left to the outside carries one of three labels: reserve-sweep, treasury-wages or treasury-purchases.

**Fix.** No third fix. Build `prices-sales-tax-shifts-to-shelf-price` (the shelf price carries the tax, so the seller and the other levels are not the ones who pay a raise) and the sweep change in `money-town-tax-raise-leaves-its-own-treasury-no-richer` (a raise is kept or spent by the budget of the government that levied it, not swept the month it arrives). `sweepReserves` is one function for every treasury and every tax, so that change covers the sales tax too. Then this item is its own Done when at Marion and LaGrange.

**Time box.** 8 hours for one Opus session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** The chain is the acceptance: one tax dollar is followed from a named buyer's purchase to the seller that collected it, to the treasury that levied it, by record id. Ask why: the proof says, by record, why $283,600 left town, and names the cause of every dollar a government lost after raising a tax. One model, two tiers: counted households pay the tax on what they buy by the same rule as full people, times their count. Sliding scales and causes: shoppers buy less or elsewhere by how far the price moved, for a recorded reason, never all at once.

Compare every cash account in the two worlds: full people in town, counted households, businesses, each government's treasury and the outside account. The treasury of the government that raised the tax must end higher than in the twin, by the extra tax its sellers collected less any sales lost for a named reason; every dollar that left to the outside is named by the payment's own label; and one purchase is followed by record id from buyer to seller to treasury. Repeat with the county board as the Body: the county's treasury must end higher, not lower. Then the same check as a named repro in Marion, Mississippi, seed 7df73e53cd67 (10.59 points, town board and county board), and in LaGrange, Wyoming, seed 63355aee286d.
