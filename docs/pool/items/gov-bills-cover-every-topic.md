**What.** The bills the played world proposes come from a narrow set of topics: a mayor's year brings a right-to-counsel ordinance and a governor's a grocery-tax exemption, but no bill on transit, health care, schools or crime. Owner, batch 20 voice notes: "the bills are only odd topics". Bills should come from what a place's own conditions call for, across the catalog's subjects.

**Rules.** Wire to the world: a place with long commutes, closing clinics, crowded schools or rising crime proposes bills on those, read from its records. No dice: the topic follows conditions and members' views, never a roll. One rule, all 56 places.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of `buildBenchWorld` at three drawn places, the bills introduced span at least five of the catalog's subjects, and each names the condition record that prompted it.
