**What.** Dev mode has a control that answers "who did this law reach". It reads one family of records and misses most of what laws do, so the proof a tester needs after passing a law comes back empty or names the wrong tier of people. Every measured case from the audit:

- As Matuis, Northern Mariana Islands (no seed given): a minimum wage raise from $7.25 to $11.60 passed through dev mode, the House 13 to 7 and the Senate 7 to 2, and the governor signed. Two hundred days later the control said 0 records, 0 people, and Trace of the law said "record-not-found". Pay was not checked in that world.
- Bigelow, Arkansas (no seed given): a town property tax raise from 0.487% to 1.23% passed 3 to 2. Four months later reach reported 0 people, and Trace said "record-not-found". In Denver, Missouri the same kind of raise moved about $380,000 out of people's accounts, "so a property tax acts and the control cannot see it."
- Woods Cross, Utah (the audit's twin-world tax test there used seed f61d987b2e0e): reach reported 72 people reached by a state income tax change, out of about 1,200 full people, and nobody from the counted population.
- Marion, Mississippi (seed 7df73e53cd67), on the 147 state laws the sweep enacted: reach gave a number above zero for 23 of them, named a full person for 9 and a counted person for 2. Raising the state income tax rate from 4.4% to 6.6% took $479,000 from counted households and $7,000 from full people in town; reach reported 86 full people and no counted ones. A state minimum wage raise from $7.25 to $10.88 left households $18,200 higher and businesses $14,100 lower; reach named four people.

Read in code on Nov 10. `reach` (`src/bench/dev-mode.ts`, line 515) walks every table of three modules (the laws module and the two law-reach stores), keeps rows whose law id matches, and counts a person only from a `personId` field. It never reads the table where the engines themselves record whom they applied a law to, with the dollars moved and the head count of a counted group (`applied`, written by `lawApplied` in `src/core2/law-reach/applied.ts`, line 55, under the module `core2-law-effects-v1`). The first reach store names its target in `targetId`, not `personId` (`ReachRow`, `src/core2/law-reach/group-a-records.ts`, line 93), so its rows add to the record count and never to the people. No screen calls the control at all: it is served at `GET /api/dev/reach` (`src/bench/dev-api.ts`, line 141) and returned by the direct law write, and a search of `src/bench/ui` and `src/bench/play.mjs` finds no caller. The code marks itself a stopgap, `SG-PLAY-dev-law-reach-full-only` (`src/core2/data/stopgaps/play.json`). Fix it at the cause, for every law at every level: one reader in the core answers who a law reached from every effect record, for full people and for counted groups times their count, with what changed (money, eligibility or a price) and the dollars; an effect that leaves no record naming the law (a tax bill, a raised wage) is recorded by the engine that pays or prices it; and the control sits on the screen beside the law.

**Time box.** 10 hours for one session (sized for Sonnet; the lead is Fable). When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** An effect is a read, not a record: reach must show the people whose dollars, eligibility or price another engine changed when it read the law, and a law that moved $479,000 cannot read as reaching nobody. One model, two tiers: a counted group is read by the same rule as a full person, times its count, so the control reports both tiers from one reader. The chain is the acceptance: the proof follows one dollar from the law in force to a named person and back out, by record id. Replace, never layer: `reach` is replaced by the core's reader and the stopgap row is closed, with no second count left beside it. Owner, Oct 13, standing rule: an effect is a read; a record nobody else reads is a placeholder. Owner, Nov 8, on dev mode: "It should be a fast track to prove whatever needs to be proved in the game in order to call it working, or prove like knock-on effects and stuff."

**Done when.** A second session, in dev mode in a drawn place: it starts the bench (`npm run bench`, `/play`), leaves the Place field empty so the place is drawn, writes down the place and seed the title shows, and turns Dev mode on in Options. On Government it introduces a property tax raise at the town's own body (the county's where there is no council) from the Bills panel, presses Pass with the executive set to Signs, runs until that law takes effect and then 90 days more. It opens the law's reach from a control on the screen, beside the law. The control shows a number of full people and a number of counted people, both above zero where the town has both, and the dollars taken from each tier. It names at least one full resident of the town with the dollars the law took from them, and that person's card shows payment rows since the law's date that add up to that figure, by record id. The dollars are then followed in a twin world: the same place and seed run with and without the bill (`scripts/dev-tests/twin.ts`), cash compared by account. Every person or counted household whose account differs by more than a null bill moves in the same pair is on the reach list, and the reach's dollar total for each tier agrees with the twin's difference for that tier, or the PR body names each gap and its cause. It repeats with a minimum wage raise and an income tax change at the state legislature, with "Chair hears the bill" checked. It then runs the audit's four cases as named repros: the wage raise from $7.25 to $11.60 in As Matuis, Northern Mariana Islands; the property tax raise from 0.487% to 1.23% in Bigelow, Arkansas; a state income tax change in Woods Cross, Utah; and, in Marion, Mississippi on seed 7df73e53cd67, the income tax raise from 4.4% to 6.6%. In each the reach is above zero, names people who paid or were paid, and in Marion shows counted households as the larger share, near the $479,000 the audit measured. A test in a drawn place with its seed named fails when the fix is undone. A screenshot of the control for each case is saved with the place and seed under it. The owner plays it: with Dev mode on he passes a tax or a wage law, waits for it to start, opens it and reads the names of people it reached and the dollars it took from or gave to them.
