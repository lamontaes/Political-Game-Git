**What.** This is the effects lead's acceptance. Pick one researched outcome-web link that reaches people, for example unemployment to rent arrears or dirtier air to infant deaths, and follow it in a played year. The cause is a real engine record, the owning engine reads the factor and a named person's record changes, the person learns of the change or lives it, their view or choice moves with that as the cited reason, and the director stages a scene or the journal says so. Every step is named by record id.

**Rules.** The chain is the acceptance: cause record, factor read by the owning engine, the named person's record, the view or choice moved, the scene or journal line, each by record id. Ask why: the view cites the news received or the lived event. No dice: the effect changes rates the person's own reasons weigh, never a roll.

**Done when.** A test (`src/core2/effects-end-to-end.test.ts`) plays one year of `buildBenchWorld` with every module in a place drawn from all 56, seed named, and records by id the cause record, the web month that carried the link, the owning engine's record for one named person, the news item or lived event, the stance or choice that moved citing it, and the scene or journal line; a screenshot of the scene goes in the PR body.
