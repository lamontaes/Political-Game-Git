**What.** Plays a year as a mayor and as a governor in several drawn places and lists the records that name the executive and the scenes the director fired from them. It maps each of the owner's executive scenes (a department head's bad news, an appointment and the person who wanted it, a budget line cut, a disaster's first hours, a meeting with another executive) to the record that is missing, and files one engine item per gap with its lead. It also lists the scenes the existing records already support and the checks not yet made.

**Rules.** Look before you ask: the map quotes the engine's own records and files items, and puts no question to the owner. Wire to the world: every row names a record kind and a count from a played year, not a guess. One rule, all 56 places: the places are drawn and named with their seeds.

**Done when.** docs/design/scenes/from-life/executive-record-gaps.md lists the records naming an executive in a played mayor's and governor's year in drawn places with their counts, maps each executive scene in the owner's Oct 27 brief to a missing record or an existing scene, and names a filed pool item with a lead for every missing record; npm run pool -- --check reports no error for those items.
