**What.** A person who learns that a law changed their money has a note in their journal at most, because the scene waits on a teller the records never name. Create a moment for each law a person learns about, and open a scene when a news story taught them, with the head of the outlet that carried the story as the teller. An heir told by a probate notice has no one to tell them, so it stays a journal line. The owner decides who tells a person that a law changed their money; the simplest version is built and the question is registered.

**Rules.** An effect is a read, not a record: the teller is read from the landed change that cites the story and the story's outlet. Every number lives in the parameter table: `directorLawLearnedImpact` is ESTIMATED with its source and spread. Owner decisions are flagged, never waited on: registered as SG-DIRECTOR-owner-decision-law-teller.
