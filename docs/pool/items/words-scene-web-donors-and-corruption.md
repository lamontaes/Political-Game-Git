**What.** The scene web (`npm run scene-web`, `docs/reports/scene-web.md`) lists every situation type in the donors and corruption domain, one row per cause that fires it. This domain covers donors and corruption: a donor's ask, an offer left for an official, a contract and the person behind it. A row is complete when a real life calibrates it, its lines vary by speaker, and it fires in a played year. The owner's donors and corruption requests with no type are listed under Missing in the report and become rows here.

**Rules.** Serves `scenes-from-life`: every row cites at least one episode packet from a real life, and no real name or line ships. Serves `scene-standard`: roles cast from the world, lines vary by trait and tie, and a scene writes something back. One rule, all 56 places: each played year is a drawn place with its seed named.

**Done when.** In `docs/reports/scene-web.json`, regenerated by `npm run scene-web` on a seed the builder did not choose (buildBenchWorld, place and seed named), every row in the donors and corruption domain names at least one episode slug in `docs/design/scenes/from-life/`. Every speaker role has at least three lines. Every row either fired in the played year or names the record or producer it waits on as a pool item. Every owner request for this domain listed under Missing has a row or a pool item.

**Time box.** 4 hours per pass; a domain may take several passes, each with its own PR.

**You will see.** In the morning scene-web report, the donors and corruption rows filled in: an episode, lines for each speaker, and a fired count from a drawn town.

**Screenshot.** The domain's section of `docs/reports/scene-web.md` in the PR body.

**Files.** docs/design/scenes/from-life/, data/english/parts/, src/director/data/situation-types.json, docs/reports/scene-web.json
