**What.** Congress has authority over cannabis commerce, but the catalog only allows state bills. Add the federal prohibition as a separate starting answer and let Congress repeal or restore it through the existing bill engine. Keep state retail permission independent, including when federal law permits sales but state law does not.

**Rules.** One law system with levels as data. Cite 21 USC 841 and 903 and Gonzales v Raich; no blanket federal preemption. Keep state licensing and price effects with the state permission. No changes to protected campaigns, electorate, views or wage bars.

**Done when.** A generated-world test finds the federal opening prohibition, drafts and signs repeal and restoration through Congress, and preserves both state prohibition and state permission without creating state retail eligibility from federal permission. Break-it, ten fresh draws and a new game pass, with place and replay in the PR. Disclose that staged desks do not prove votes and criminal enforcement is not added.
