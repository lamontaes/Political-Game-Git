**What.** Full people form partnerships from age, closeness, traits, money and housing, and the law in force. Today no couple forms in a played year, because the opening seeds partnerships and nothing makes new ones. Read each unpartnered adult's closeness to other adults, their traits, whether they can afford a home together and the marriage and partnership law where they live, and form the partnership when those causes weigh enough. The new couple moves in together through the core's household change.

**Rules.** No dice: no roll decides a partnership; age, closeness, traits, money, housing and the law decide it. Sliding scales and causes: the pull to partner grows smoothly with closeness and shared means. Ask why: every new partnership names the records behind it. One rule, all 56 places: partnership law is place data. The chain is the acceptance: follow one new couple to the household, the lease and the accounts.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a drawn place and a seed the builder did not choose, at least one full pair forms a partnership with the causes recorded by record id, the household joins through the core's call, and a test in src/core2 proves the chain. The owner plays it: two people in his circle become a couple and move in.
