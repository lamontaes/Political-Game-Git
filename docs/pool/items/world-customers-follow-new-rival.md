**What.** When a new seller of a kind opens, its share of the place's customers builds up over months from the incumbents instead of arriving in one step at the opening. The Oct 16 attempt moved customers by price and capacity and found almost no movement, because every seller's price follows the same wage and price indexes (median spread 0.06 percent). This item names one cause that does differ between sellers and moves customers by it, contract by contract for counted purchase flows (the pattern of shiftToOpenSellers) and in full households' living plans, keeping every household's total unchanged.

**Rules.** No dice: the move follows the cause, never a roll. One model, two tiers: counted and full buyers move by the same rule, times their count. One rule, all 56 places: no place named in logic. The chain is the acceptance: a lost customer is followed to the firm's shrinking sales and its layoff, by record id. OWNER DECISIONS NEEDED if this cause is not the one he wants: register a stopgap of kind owner-decision.

**Done when.** An incumbent loses customers over the months after a rival opens and sheds staff when its sales fall below its payroll, shown by the rival's opening record and the incumbent's purchase flows. In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a drawn place and a seed the builder did not choose, layoffs at shrinking firms follow their lost customers by business and purchase-flow record ids, and the monthly layoff rate is reported against JOLTS.
