**What.** Source the numbers behind the core parameter tables (distance, time, past, speech, corruption, moves). Each row below is a registered stopgap whose replacement waits on sourced or estimated data, collapsed here from 6 pool item(s) on Oct 19, 2026 so one researcher takes the engine family at once. For each row, find a public source with a year and a unit, record the figure as a SOURCED or ESTIMATED parameter row with a citation and a spread, put the research file under data/research/core/ with a reader, and resolve the stopgap row. Where a row's replacement is a mechanic rather than a number, source what that mechanic will read and leave the mechanic to the area's builder (name the pool item in the PR).

- `SG-P15-distance-by-place` in src/core2/data/parameters.json: How far a relative lives changes how often they are visited only by whether they are in the household's county (full pull) or elsewhere in the state (0.39 of it, from Pew 2005 daily contact by same or different town). Replacement: Travel time between the two people's places once relatives carry a place, from Census Gazetteer place centroids, with visits falling with travel time as measured in a contact-by-distance study. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/parameters.json:farKinContactShare)
- `SG-CORE-past-undated-law-age` in src/core2/data/past/parameters.json: A law the research records only as in force at the opening (a state's policy answers, a law carried back to the start date) is given an enactment date between one and twenty years before day one, fixed by the world's seed. Replacement: Enactment years from the research for each policy answer (NCSL and state code histories), read by the law history in place of the seeded age. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/past/parameters.json:pastUndatedLawAgeYearsLow, src/core2/data/past/parameters.json:pastUndatedLawAgeYearsHigh)
- `SG-P8-work-time-reserve` in src/core2/data/parameters.json: Reserves guessed sleep hours and gives waking recovery a separate guessed duration. Replacement: ATUS age/employment primary activity totals and participant durations using compatible crosswalks and observed individual spreads. (parameter rows: src/core2/data/parameters.json:workSleepReserveHours, src/core2/data/parameters.json:workWakingRecoveryHours)
- `SG-P15-speech-reception` in src/core2/data/speech/parameters.json: How much a speech moves listeners and who retells it; retelling reaches only the listener's household. Replacement: Reception calibrated to persuasion experiments by audience and message (Coppock 2023), retelling through friends and the news module, and memory by age (M4-04). **Rules.** Research before building: real data checks the simulation and never decides an outcome by itself; a sourced rate calibrates the band the proof reports against, never the chance one person acts. Estimate, never UNKNOWN: a value the source lacks for a place starts from the real average with a per-world spread, marked ESTIMATED with its source in data, never shown to the player. One rule, all 56 places: a figure that varies by place is a per-place table in data, never a state named in logic. Sliding scales and causes: if an engine reads the number as a cap, a cutoff or a chance, file a bug for the builder instead of sourcing it. Follow .agents/skills/researcher/SKILL.md.

**Done when.** Each listed row is SOURCED or ESTIMATED in its parameter table with a citation (publisher, year, URL) and a spread (low, high, unit, citation); each stopgap row above is resolved in src/core2/data/stopgaps.json with the source named; the research-data-reader guard (tests/guards/research-data-has-reader.test.ts) is green; the tripwire and calibration-guard tests pass; and a one-year played world (buildBenchWorld, a drawn place from all 56, seed named in the PR) reports each band and where the town landed.
