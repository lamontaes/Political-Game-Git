**What.** Replaces the 2026 law that was carried back to the January 20, 2025 start with the law in force that day: the federal income tax schedule and Social Security wage base, every state's income tax, state and average local sales tax, SNAP maximums, the Medicaid match and unemployment weekly bounds. Six research files under data/research/laws/ (irs-2025-federal-tax, taxfoundation-2025-state-income-tax, taxfoundation-2025-sales-tax, fns-2025-snap-maximums, hhs-2025-fmap and dol-2025-ui-weekly-bounds) are written into src/core2/data/laws/starting-law.json as each series' first phase by scripts/research/build-starting-law.py, and scripts/research/check-start-law-2025.ts exits 1 on any difference; the engines already read that table (withholdingFor, syncSalesTax, allotments, payCoverage and unemploymentTerms). Three more files carry the mechanic bands and re-base two ESTIMATED rows: stateFlatIncomeTaxPercent (4 to 4.25, spread 2.5 to 5.695, from taxfoundation-2022-2026-flat-tax-conversions) and pricesCannabisUsePrevalence (0.22 to 0.2317, spread 0.1594 to 0.3757, from samhsa-2024-cannabis-past-year, a table for all 56 places), plus bls-2025-union-public-sector; scripts/research/check-law-reach-sources.ts checks them. It updates the `replacement` of SG-GOV-start-law-carried-back, SG-GOV-REACH-B-flat-tax-replacement, SG-GOV-REACH-A-cannabis-market, SG-GOV-REACH-A-bargaining and SG-GOV-REACH-A-union-dues, and it reports one played year in Hanapepe, Hawaii. Split from research-law-reach-sourced-parameters, which stays open for the other stopgap rows, the mechanics and the series still carried back.

**Rules.** Research calibrates, never decides: no source text reaches a player and no number picks one actor's outcome; the union band only calibrates what the proof reports against. Estimate, never UNKNOWN: the flat rate and the cannabis share stay ESTIMATED with their source and spread, because one national figure stands in for each state's own. One rule, all 56 places: the 2025 law and the cannabis shares are per-place tables in data, with no state named in logic.
