**What.** A scene plays again every time its cause recurs, and nothing asks what the last one settled. Measured by the audit with the crew's scene tool, a 17-year-old for one year in each of Ninnekah, Oklahoma (seed 3dac8ffb3f3c), Tesuque, New Mexico (seed 8a5d1fba28fc) and Odessa, Washington (seed fd7e212758b7): 45 of 64 scenes were the same job-offer call. In Ninnekah it was 15 of 16: ten employers called, Woodward's Market three times, and he had no job at the end. Measured in the audit's browser play (Blodgett Landing, New Hampshire, ten weeks): the "someone has news" scene played five times, and one stranger walked up three times with the same question, "Have you heard about Compass News Network?" I ran the three seeds again on Nov 10's main (commit 8ac2262853) and the count is the same: 45 of 64 (15 of 16, 16 of 24, 14 of 24). In Tesuque one caller phoned four times, and the same relative's letter made the same ask ("Come and see me in Santa Fe County") three times in six months. Read in code, not run: `offerToPlayer` (src/core2/modules/jobs.ts:4357) refuses a second offer only for the same position id, so a new opening at the same employer calls again whatever happened to the last offer. The director's only limits are day counts in `scheduleMoment` (src/director/scheduling.ts:741) and `produceCircumstances` (src/director/ledger.ts:1451): the same kind with the same people once in 30 days (`repeatDays`), six of one kind in 91 days (`seasonCap`, src/director/data/situation-types.json), one scene from circumstance a week and one street scene every 14 days. None of them reads what the earlier scene settled. Fix the class: how often one kind, one caller and one subject may reach the same person is set from records. The limit reads what the last one settled (the offer was declined, lapsed or accepted; the news was already told; the ask was already agreed to) and what has changed since (better pay, a different job, a new story). With nothing changed, the cause goes to the journal.

**Time box.** 8 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** No dice: which repeat plays is decided by the records of the earlier scene and of what changed, never by a roll, a random skip or a hash. Sliding scales and causes: the limit is not a fixed count that flips at a number; a repeat needs a cause on record, and how soon it may come slides with how much changed and with the tie to the caller. Replace, never layer: if the record-based limit covers what `repeatDays` and `seasonCap` did, they are deleted in the same change and the PR counts them. One model, two tiers: an employer's memory of its own offer to a person is the same rule for a counted applicant, times the count. Owner, Nov 8, 2026: "There's lots of irrelevant scenes popping up."

**Done when.** A different session starts the bench (`npm run bench`, play at /play), begins a new game with the Place field empty so it is a drawn place, writes down the place and seed, and plays a jobless 17-year-old for one year with dev mode's Run. When the first job offer comes it declines, and it keeps a screenshot of each later call. It runs the crew's scene tool on the same seed (`node --max-old-space-size=6144 --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed> --out <file>`) and counts scenes by kind, by caller and by subject from its log. It must see: every repeat of a kind to the same person from the same caller or on the same subject names the record that changed since the last one (an offer row with different pay or a different position, a new story id, a new event), listed by record id, and a repeat with no changed record is a failure. No employer the player turned down calls again with the same job at the same pay. No person brings the same news twice. Then the same on the audit's seeds 3dac8ffb3f3c, 8a5d1fba28fc and fd7e212758b7: in Ninnekah, Woodward's Market does not phone three times with nothing changed, and the list shows why each remaining offer call was allowed. In a second run where the player accepts the first offer, the calls stop and the player's own record shows the job. A director test fails when a kind repeats to one person with no changed record. The owner plays it: after he turns a job down, that employer leaves him alone until something about the offer changes, and no stranger asks him the same question twice.

**Notes.** From the Fable audit, Pillar 4 ("Three more played lives, a year each") and root cause 6: "A scene plays because something in the world matches it, and plays again every time that recurs. Nothing asks whether this scene belongs in this person's story now."

Nov 10 re-run on main (commit 8ac2262853), scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts, one year each. Ninnekah, seed 3dac8ffb3f3c: 16 scenes, 15 job offers from ten employers; Woodward's Market (caller Rowan Tyson) on 2027-02-22, 2027-06-14 and 2027-08-09; Pollard's Market, Frazier's Market and Monroe Supply Company twice each; the 16th scene is the father's cause invite. Tesuque, seed 8a5d1fba28fc: 24 scenes, 16 job offers from nine employers, Edward Rowland four times (2027-03-08, 05-31, 07-12, 08-23); five letters, three of them from Cora Sinclair with the same ask (2027-01-01, 04-01, 06-01). Odessa, seed fd7e212758b7: 24 scenes, 14 job offers from nine employers; five letters, three from Nadia Robertson with the same ask. The player had no job at the end of any of the three.

What the audit did not verify, and one thing to know about its tool: the scene tool walks each offer's reply tree but records no reply, so in these runs every offer lapsed unanswered. Nobody has tested in the running game whether declining or accepting stops the calls. The cap has to hold for an offer that was answered and for one left to lapse. The audit also did not trace which records fired the five "someone has news" scenes in Blodgett Landing (no seed kept).

The jobs engine's half (an employer reads its own earlier offer to the same person before offering again) is in src/core2/modules/jobs.ts; ask the Jobs session per docs/coordination/CREW.md before editing it.
