**What.** An audit of every subject in the conversation bank found five more whose answer moves all said one thing in other words: the greeting reply, the goodbye reply, the school question, the reply to where someone lives (home-reply) and the reply to how long someone has worked somewhere (tenure-reply). Each gets moves that differ in meaning: detail (the player's employer or town), deflect (a later time) and change (raise their-work, their-school or home), and the goodbye gets later and wish.

**Rules.** Declared slots only; evasion points to a later time; American English; no dice (traits and calibration rank the moves).

**Done when.** In a drawn place and a seed nobody chose, a talk that reaches the home reply shows replies that differ in meaning, named by place and seed; words:rules shows no new pulls; conversation tests pass. Still open: answerMoves.school must list detail, deflect and change; follows needs greet: their-work, school: their-school, tenure-reply: home; the greeting and tenure-reply never reach the player's choices in a fresh world.
