**What.** The bounded part of traits-wire-integrity that its open PR delivers. The integrity decisions that run in an ordinary played year (constituent-cases.bring-case, constituent-cases.handle-case, staff.follow-through, staff.hire, staff.dismiss and corruption.offer) read their case through the shared two-way rule with the temperament term on record: the official's own call decides what the office does when they have staff, and a bribe's money pull is bounded so honesty can weigh against it. Every other decision in the family (taking or telling about a bribe, the three recall decisions, a reporter's tip, the three justice decisions and appointments) waits for a record nothing writes in an ordinary year, and each has a gap row naming the cause and the open item that writes it.

**Rules.** No dice: a trait weighs one reason among the person's recorded reasons and never rolls an outcome. One model, two tiers: counted people decide through the same shared formula. Wire to the world: shown in played years by record id.

**Done when.** scripts/traits/trait-wiring-audit.ts (played, flat and mirror twins) and scripts/traits/trait-wiring-matrix.ts on one year of buildBenchWorld in each of two places drawn from all 56, seeds named, show constituent-cases.bring-case, constituent-cases.handle-case, staff.follow-through, staff.hire, staff.dismiss and corruption.offer with calls above zero in both years, each with its traitWeight row cited to its study in data/research/trait-effect-sizes.json or a gap row; every other integrity decision has a gap row in data/content/trait-decision-gaps.json naming the cause and the open pool item that fixes it; and the family test reads from the log the person.weighed records of two people who chose differently for their traits, naming both record ids.
