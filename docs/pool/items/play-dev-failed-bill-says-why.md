**What.** A bill that a tester forces through still fails, and the tools do not say why in words a tester can read. Two measured cases. With every member voting yes, a sales tax bill, a federal wage bill and two repeal bills in American Samoa failed (run with the crew's harness; no seed given). The lead replayed one repeal: the record says "held in committee", 0 to 0, by the labor committee's chair, on the day it was filed, and a held bill is marked failed the same day. In the sweep in Marion, Mississippi (seed 7df73e53cd67), at narrow margins all 40 bills Congress may pass failed, 219 to 216 in the House and 52 to 48 in the Senate, because Congress keeps the Senate's 60-vote rule; at 233 to 202 and 65 to 35 all 40 passed. The audit's fix line asks that dev mode "say why a bill failed". It also found that dev mode has a switch that makes the chair hear a bill and the crew's test harness does not. Read in code on Nov 10. `countVote` (`src/core2/modules/legislature.ts`, line 5092) marks a bill failed the day its committee does not report it (line 5109), and for a bill the chair never heard it writes a "held in committee" record (`committeeStage`, line 4274) but no "bill failed" record, so neither Run until "A bill fails" nor the news has anything to read. The Senate check (line 5154, the share `senateClotureShare`, 0.6) fails a bill that has more yes than no; the failure record (`recordFailure`, line 4842) carries the stage and the tally and no fact naming that rule or the votes needed. Dev mode's `decideAt` (`src/bench/dev-mode.ts`, line 802) does return each committee's hold with the chair's reasons, and the Bills panel on Government prints it (`src/bench/ui/dev-bills.mjs`, lines 174 to 190: "Held by the chair", "Chair left it unheard, no discharge route here" or "Lost in committee", then two bare numbers); but that panel is the one drawn under the Members cards. The dev card on the bill's own page (`decide` in `src/bench/ui/dev-bill-card.mjs`, line 155) prints only the number, the status and the tallies, has no "Chair hears the bill" switch, and its widest margin is 10 (`src/bench/data/controls.json`, `devBill.margins`), which in a Senate of 100 is 55 to 45, so that card can never pass a bill through Congress. In the harness, `pass` in `scripts/dev-tests/law-run.ts` (line 67) and the same step in `scripts/dev-tests/twin.ts` cast every vote yes and hold the vote with no chair hearing; only `scripts/dev-tests/bodies.ts` (line 51) asks for one. Fix the class: every failed bill, at every body, carries one worded reason on its own record (held unheard by the chair with the chair's recorded reasons, lost in committee, short of a majority on the floor, short of the chamber's higher bar with the votes needed and the votes it had, vetoed and not overridden), written by the legislature engine and read by every view; dev mode shows it wherever it shows a result; and the harness passes bills through the same function dev mode uses, chair hearing included, and prints the reason when a bill still fails.

**Time box.** 10 hours for one session (sized for Sonnet; the lead is Fable). When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** Ask why: a failed bill answers why by record, in words, on the bill itself, so a tester and a player read the same reason and nobody has to replay the bill to learn it. One rule, all 56 places: the votes a chamber needs come from that chamber's own rule in data, so the reason reads right for a council, a legislature and Congress, with no body named in a screen. Replace, never layer: the harness's own copy of "pass a bill" is deleted and both tools call one function; the bill page's result line and the Bills panel's line are built from the bill's one stage view (`legislation-screens`), never counted in the screen. No dice: the chair still decides from their own position and temperament; the switch casts the chair's decision, as it does today. Owner, Nov 8, 1:30 a.m.: "when it finds these errors or finds that these laws don't pass, I want them fixed. I don't just want a report of everything that was broken." Owner, Nov 8, 12:50 a.m.: "you could force pass things and you could choose the margin."

**Done when.** A second session, in dev mode in a drawn place: it starts the bench (`npm run bench`, `/play`), leaves the Place field empty so the place is drawn, writes down the place and seed the title shows, and turns Dev mode on in Options. First, it opens a bill's page in the state legislature, and on the page's Dev mode card (which now carries the "Chair hears the bill" switch the Bills panel has) presses Hold the vote at "Passes by 3" with that switch off, on bill after bill until one fails in committee. The card's result line then says in words that the chair of the named committee held it unheard, or that it lost in committee by its tally; the bill's page says the same to a player; and Trace on the bill opens the chair's recorded reasons, by record id. It turns the chair switch on for a fresh copy of the same bill and the bill reaches the floor. Second, it introduces any bill Congress may pass and holds the vote at "Passes by 3": the result line says the Senate had 52 yes votes and needed 60, in words, and the card offers a margin wide enough to pass it; at that margin the bill passes. Third, it introduces a bill and leaves it to the body, picks "A bill fails" in the Calendar's Run panel and presses Run: when a chair holds a bill of the place's own chain unheard, the run stops that day on that bill's record id. It saves a screenshot of each result line with the place and seed under it. Fourth, the harness: `scripts/dev-tests/law-run.ts` on the same place and seed, at the state level, passes the same held bill with the chair hearing on, and with it off prints the same worded reason the screen showed. A test in a drawn place with its seed named asserts the reason on a held bill, a bill lost in committee and a bill short of the Senate's bar, and fails when the fix is undone. It then repeats the audit's cases as named repros: a repeal bill in American Samoa that its labor committee's chair holds, and a bill in Congress from Marion, Mississippi on seed 7df73e53cd67 at 219 to 216 and 52 to 48. The owner plays it: with Dev mode on he forces a bill, it fails, and the bill's page tells him who stopped it and what it would have taken to pass.

What was run: six law bills against a twin world with the crew's own harness, in American Samoa and Denver, Missouri (four passed and two were held in committee; of the four repeals, two passed and two failed), the bill records replayed for the wage bills in As Matuis and American Samoa, and the Marion sweep, where bills were passed with dev mode's Introduce and its own vote at a margin of three or four. The audit gives no seed for the American Samoa runs and does not name the town. Where the code on Nov 10 differs from the audit's account: dev mode is not silent about a committee hold. `decideAt` returns it and the Bills panel prints it; the tester could not read it because that panel is drawn under the Members cards (`play-dev-bills-and-bribe-panels-drawn-under-members`), and the bill page's card drops it. Nothing anywhere prints the Senate's bar. The audit did not check whether the player's own bill page says why a bill failed. The audit also found a forced bill is not a clean test because the body adds a rider at the same step (`floorAmendments`, called inside `countVote` at `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts` line 5126, the line the audit cites); that is a separate defect, filed from the same audit as `gov-riders-added-when-every-vote-is-forced`, and is not fixed here. The `dev-tester` skill already warns that "a state or federal bill can still die in a committee" and tells testers to redraw places; when this lands, that line is replaced by the switch.
