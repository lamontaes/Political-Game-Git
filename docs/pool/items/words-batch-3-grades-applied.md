**What.** The owner graded part of batch 3 by voice, skipping around, and named lessons for every line. His note is read into a grade file the map reads, with the lines he read with their slots filled checked by hand. His lessons are applied across the banks: no line without its context, no stock tags like "That's real", the place's real name instead of "this town", varied openers, placeholders that read as real words, journal entries told as moments, sympathy that fits who says it, and a job offer that names the pay.

**Rules.** No hand-written player text: every change is a bank line, edited in place under its key. Sliding scales and causes: the grades move weights on the map, and a line he liked is never deleted.
