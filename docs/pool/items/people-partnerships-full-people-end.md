**What.** Full people separate and divorce from the closeness between them, strain, money and the law in force. A couple whose closeness has worn down under strain, or who cannot afford one home, may part; the law decides how long it takes and what it costs. The household splits, the home and money divide, and the children's household follows. The player would see it as people they know ending a marriage, told through the director.

**Rules.** No dice: a separation follows closeness, strain, money and the law, never a roll; An effect is a read, not a record: the housing, finance and family engines read the split and the household, money and links change; The chain is the acceptance: follow one couple from the strain to the filing, the law read, the split money and homes, and the director's scene or journal line; One rule, all 56 places: the divorce law is state data; No hand-written player text: words come from the English engine.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a place drawn from all 56 with a seed the builder did not choose, named in the PR body, a full couple separates, the household and money split by record id, the divorce law in force is cited, and a test in src/core2 shows it.
