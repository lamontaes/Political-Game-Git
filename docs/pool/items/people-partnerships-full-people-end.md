**What.** Full people separate and divorce from closeness, strain, money and the law in force. Today no partnership ends in a played year. Read the pair's closeness, the strain between them, their money, their housing and the divorce law where they live, and end the partnership when those causes weigh enough, splitting the household through the core's household change. Each end is a record the people and the news can read.

**Rules.** No dice: no roll decides a separation; closeness, strain, money, housing and the law decide it. Sliding scales and causes: the pull to separate grows smoothly with strain and falls with closeness. Ask why: every end names the records that weighed. One rule, all 56 places: divorce law is place data. The chain is the acceptance: follow one end to the household split, the lease and the accounts.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a drawn place and a seed the builder did not choose, at least one full pair separates with the causes recorded by record id, the household splits through the core's call, and a test in src/core2 proves the chain. The owner plays it: a couple in his circle separates and the town knows why.
