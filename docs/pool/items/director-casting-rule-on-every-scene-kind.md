**What.** A scene goes to whoever its cause record names or whoever heard of it. Nothing asks whether that person could be in that scene. Measured in the audit's browser play (Blodgett Landing, New Hampshire, a 17-year-old high-school student, ten weeks, no seed kept): a legislator "comes down the aisle to find you" and asks, "Did the governor's speech sound like your district to you, Ray?"; the mother, who lives with the player, "stops in at the house"; and "You knock on Susan Kent's door. Your aunt has come to say goodbye." Measured again on Nov 10's main (commit 8ac2262853) with the crew's scene tool: in Ninnekah, Oklahoma (seed 3dac8ffb3f3c) the 17-year-old's father, who lives in his household, is met "on the street", and the scene's own narration says he "called your office about firearms and weapons rules all week. Today there's a knock at your door."; in Tesuque, New Mexico (seed 8a5d1fba28fc) and Odessa, Washington (seed fd7e212758b7) the mother in the player's own household "is at your door" and "comes to the house". Read in code, not run: `recordAddressResponse` (src/director/address-response.ts:52) names the player and the player's household as witnesses of every answer to a governor's address; the row `governor-answer-hallway` (src/director/data/circumstances.json:434) says place "seat" and player "heard-it"; `playerHolds` (src/director/circumstance.ts:312) passes "heard-it" on the witness list alone; and `circumstancePicks` (line 607) never reads the row's place or the player's age, and reads an office only where a row's player rule asks for one. Of 44 circumstance rows, 14 take "anyone". `composeScene` (src/director/scene.ts:75) words the arrival from the setting and a name only, so "stops in at the house" plays for someone who lives there, and the opening line ("has come to say goodbye") is picked without reading the arrival ("you knock on"). The narration "called your office" in data/english/parts/scene-talk.json requires only the fact "same-day". Fix the class, not these scenes: every situation type and every circumstance row declares who may receive it (age band or the record that stands for it, office held or sought, tie to the cause, where the player is, where the other person lives), as data, and the director refuses a cast that breaks the rule. A refused cause goes to the journal or to the next person with a reason. The arrival and opening lines read the same facts, so nobody who lives with the player "comes to the house".

**Time box.** 8 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** One rule, all 56 places: the receive rule is data on each situation type and circumstance row, read the same way in every place, and any age it uses comes from a record the place keeps (the voting-age row, the office's own age rule, school enrollment), never a typed number or a named town. No dice: a refused cast falls to the next person the records give a reason, or to a journal line, never to a roll. No hand-written player text: the arrival and opening lines stay bank pieces, held back by facts (`excludesFacts`, per the words-engine skill), not rewritten in code. Scene standard rule 2: every role is cast from the world, and a role that can't be filled means the scene does not fire. Owner, Nov 8, 2026: "There's lots of irrelevant scenes popping up."

**Done when.** A different session starts the bench (`npm run bench`, play at /play), begins a new game with the Place field empty so it is a drawn place, writes down the place and seed, and plays a 17-year-old for one year with dev mode's Run, opening every scene that stops play and taking a screenshot of each first line. It runs the crew's scene tool on the same seed (`node --max-old-space-size=6144 --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed> --out <file>`), which logs every scene with its kind, cast, setting and cause record id. From the played scenes and that log it fills one list by kind: the kind, who received it (age, office held or sought, household), who came and where they live, and the rule row that allowed the cast. The list has zero casts outside the rule. It then does the same on the audit's seeds 3dac8ffb3f3c, 8a5d1fba28fc and fd7e212758b7 and must see: no person in the player's household "comes to the house", "is at your door" or "stops in"; no line says "your office" to someone who holds none; no scene set at a seat reaches a person with no seat. A second played year as a seated state legislator (Advanced, Play as) still gets the governor's-answer scene in the chamber. Scenes from circumstance may not show in the scene tool (see Notes), so those kinds are judged from the browser play. A director test fails when a situation type or a circumstance row has no receive rule. The owner plays it: as a 17-year-old he is never walked up to in a chamber, and his mother at home is simply there, not arriving.
