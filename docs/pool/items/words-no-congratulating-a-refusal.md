**What.** In the owner's Oct 27 sitting, a person answered "Not right now" when asked about work and heard "Good for you." Nine follow-ups in the conversation bank that assume the person works ("Good for you. Is it busy?", "Have you been there long?") had no stage, so they played after a no-job answer too. Seven reactions to news carried a feeling ("That's great.", "Oh, no.", "That's a shame.") and played for any news, good or bad, because a news subject carries no record of which it is.

**Rules.** No dice: the scene fires from its cause record and the asker's lines are chosen by their traits through the calibration table, never a roll. One rule, all 56 places: the proof draws its place from all 56 and names the place and seed. Serves `scene-standard`: the reply writes a scene reply back and two opposite speakers say different lines.

**Done when.** The nine job-assuming follow-ups play only in the working stage; the seven feeling reactions no longer react to news of either kind; the count of conversation pieces that play is given before and after; `english-parts-banks` and `conversation.test.ts` pass.

**Time box.** 2 hours.

**You will see.** When you tell someone you're not working right now, they no longer say "Good for you."
