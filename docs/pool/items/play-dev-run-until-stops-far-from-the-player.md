**What.** Dev mode's Run panel on the Calendar runs the world "until" an event: a bill passes, a bill fails, a person dies, a law takes effect and others. It stops on things far from the player and misses the ones in town. Measured in San Roque, Northern Mariana Islands (the lead's own session; no seed given): "until a law takes effect" stopped on a Hawaii law, "until a death" stopped on a Minnesota legislator, and "until a bill passes" ran 400 days and never stopped. Measured through the screens in Ten Broeck, Kentucky (no seed given): "until a law takes effect" stopped on laws in Colorado, Hawaii, Delaware and the Virgin Islands, and never on the law just passed in town. So a tester who passes a law cannot run to the day it takes effect, which is the first step of proving what it did. Causes, read in code on Nov 10 (the audit named none). `runUntil` (`src/bench/dev-mode.ts`, line 1473) stops on the first new record of the event's kinds anywhere in the world (`RUN_EVENTS`, line 94, counted from `core.logByKind`), and narrows to one person only when a caller names one; the Run panel names none (`devRunPanel`, `src/bench/ui/dev-run.mjs`, the request at line 72). Another state's law writes the same "law took effect" kind under a national place id (`lawTookEffect`, `src/core2/modules/laws.ts`, line 1012), and officials from every other place are full people, so their deaths write the same death kind. "A bill passes" waits on the presentment record, which the legislature writes only when a passed bill lands on the played character's own desk as the executive (`executiveAction`, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts`, line 5272; the record at line 5318); a bill that passes and is signed by anyone else writes none, so the run can never stop. Fix it for every event in the list: each stops first on the player's own places and people (the town and the governments in its own chain, read from the place's law chain; the town's residents and the player's circle), with the bill or law just introduced in the Bills panel ahead of everything; "a bill passes" reads the bill's own status; the whole country is a choice the tester turns on; and the result line says in words what stopped it and where.

**Time box.** 8 hours for one session (sized for Sonnet; the lead is Fable). When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** One rule, all 56 places: what counts as the player's own governments is read from the place's law chain in data, so the same rule holds in a territory, a district and a state, and no place is named in the code. Ask why: a stop names the record that caused it, by record id, and the place and body it belongs to, so the tester knows why the run ended. Speed: the check each day reads the day's new records by index, never the whole log. Owner, Nov 8, 12:50 a.m., on dev mode: "Being able to simulate X amount of days at a time, or until a certain thing has happened, or a certain bill has passed, etc. Or you could force pass things and you could choose the margin."

**Done when.** A second session, in dev mode in a drawn place: it starts the bench (`npm run bench`, `/play`), leaves the Place field empty so the place is drawn, writes down the place and seed the title shows, and turns Dev mode on in Options. First, on Government it introduces a bill for a law amount at the town's own body (the county's where the place has no council) from the Bills panel and presses Pass with the executive set to Signs. On the Calendar it picks "A law takes effect" and presses Run. The run stops on the day that law's own row takes effect: the result line shows that law row's record id, its Trace shows the town's own jurisdiction and the new value, and the run did not stop earlier on another state's law. Second, it introduces another bill at the same body and leaves the vote to the members, picks "A bill passes" and presses Run. The run stops on the day the first bill of the place's own chain passes its vote, names that bill's record id and its body, and the bill's page shows it passed. Only when no bill of the chain passes before the cap may the line say the cap was reached, with the days run. A test in a drawn place with its seed named runs the same world to the cap without the stop, lists every bill of the chain that passed in that time, and fails unless the run stopped on the earliest of them; it fails when the fix is undone. Third, it picks "A person dies" and presses Run: the stop names a resident of the played town or someone in the player's circle, and never an official who lives elsewhere unless the whole-country choice is on. It saves a screenshot of each result line with the place and seed under it. It then repeats the three runs in San Roque, Northern Mariana Islands and the first run in Ten Broeck, Kentucky (the audit's places; no seeds were given, so the seeds drawn are written down). The owner plays it: with Dev mode on he passes a law in town, presses Run until a law takes effect, and the game stops on the day his law starts.

A bill its committee chair never hears is marked failed with a "held in committee" record and no "bill failed" record (`countVote`, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts`, line 5092; the hold at line 5109), so "A bill fails" cannot stop on it either; `play-dev-failed-bill-says-why` carries that. This is one of the five items for the acceptance rule (what a second session must do in the running game before anything is called done): a tester cannot follow a law to its effect if the clock will not stop on it.
