**What.** On current main Montpelier, Vermont (5046000, seed 5a1c0de7a11ce5e1) ran out of memory in year 4 (FATAL heap out of memory, exit 134), where on Nov 6 it finished four years (7.58 GB process). Years 2 and 3 now keep far more: relationships 77,839 (was under 60,000) at year 2 and 125,603 at year 3, the log 142,112 at year 2 (was 101,791), folded causes 103,686 (was 99,070) and 153,412 at year 3 (was 109,139). Commit by commit on year 1 alone (news.heard 26,820 before, 29,550 after): adcae6b5 is good and 4095b2fb is the first bad one.

**Rules.** Speed and memory: no year may keep what no engine reads; the owner's Oct 29 speed ruling; measure on the same machine, one process.

**Done when.** A four-year Montpelier run on main finishes under the 8 GB limit again with relationships and the log at or below the Nov 6 counts, and the PR names which table grew and why.

**Files.** data/research/laws/starting-law-2026/health-human-services.json,data/research/powers-catalog/question-powers.json,src/core2/laws-in-force.ts

**Notes.** State Sessions Nov 8. 4095b2fb (fix: preserve sourced SNAP scope and avoid repeated law sorting, PR law-levels-federal-rule-reversal) changed 6 lines of health-human-services.json, 7 of question-powers.json and laws-in-force.ts. Upper Witter Gulch is not affected (year 2 log 41,260 against 41,393). Bisect: adcae6b5 good (year 2 log 101,791, folded causes 99,070), b5ea1bd5 and 4095b2fb bad on year 1. A change to who is subject to a federal work rule probably moves many people at once; read which records grew (relationships, news.heard, the log) at year 2 and whether an engine reads them. Not caused by my folded-causes narrowing: it gave identical counts to plain main, so it was dropped.

Nov 8, later (State Sessions): the blame on 4095b2fb is for the trigger, not the cause. Dumping every table's row count at the end of year 1 on Montpelier at adcae6b5 (good) and 4095b2fb (first bad) shows the two worlds almost the same: the biggest differences are votes 18,892 against 19,309 (+417), news.heard 16,965 against 16,631, actCounters 13,669 against 13,558, relationships 25,450 against 25,476 (+26); no table moved by more than 2 percent. So the commit does not add a kind of record; it nudges the world onto a different course (a different race result or contact), and by year 2 that course keeps 3 times the relationships and 40 percent more log. Montpelier's memory is therefore path-dependent: one course finishes, another runs out. The fix is not in the SNAP rule; it is in what a large town's election year keeps whatever its course (relationships formed at rallies and doors, the event log, folded causes), and Montpelier should be run on at least two seeds before it is called fixed.

Nov 8 (State Sessions), the circle cut for folded causes, tried and reverted. Keeping a folded cause only when `keepsWhy` is true for its voter or candidate changed nothing: Upper Witter Gulch year 4 kept the same 962,745 records, and Montpelier's year 2 kept the same 84,003 folded causes (year 4 not run). Every folded cause kept today is already for a candidate the player runs against or a person they know, so the rows are the player's own race (a voter hears of each rival and the trace still finds how), not other people's. The 595,258 in Montpelier's year 4 are therefore the player's race across a large town's voters and its candidates. Cutting them needs the CTO's Oct 30 08:15 ruling (keep only a cause a known fact names) narrowed again, for example to the voters in focus, or the first-hearing fact pointed at the summary row: a decision about what the trace can still show, not a code cut.
Nov 8 (State Sessions), where Montpelier's year 4 goes, with other states' officials counted (#7359). Montpelier, Vermont (key 5046000, seed 5a1c0de7a11ce5e1, `speed:year --years 4 --exclusive`): it finishes four years, in 105.4, 260.4, 197.4 and 1,011.8 s, process 7.7 GB at the end against the 8 GB heap limit. Years 1 to 3 grow gently (477k, 929k and 1.20 million records); the election year adds 1.45 million: `core2-campaigns-v1.foldedCauses` 94,150 to 595,258, `core.relationships` 43,366 to 241,231, `core.durableLog` 78,831 to 338,267. Reading `foldCauses` in `campaigns.ts`: a folded cause is kept as its own row when a voter's known fact names it as its source, and `reach` writes that fact (`candidate:<id>`, `sourceId: cause.id`) the first time a voter hears of a candidate. So one kept row per voter and candidate pair, which is bounded by voters times candidates but large in a place with many races and many candidates each. Next cut to try: keep the folded cause only for the player's circle and the people in focus (`keepsWhy`), the way `keepCause` already does for the live row, and let everyone else's first-hearing fact point at the summary row (`causeSummaries`, one per voter and candidate) instead. The records proof will then differ in `foldedCauses` only. Not started.

Nov 8 (State Sessions), the rows counted by writer, as the last note proposed. Montpelier, Vermont, seed 7e57ab1e0000beef, four years (counter on every put to `impressions`, `foldedCauses` and `causeSummaries` and on every `api.relationship` call, by the four nearest callers; the counter is not committed). Year 4 took 442 s with the counter on, 1.34 million records at the end. Puts (a put to an existing row counts too, so these are writes, not new rows): `impressions` 953,792 from an endorsement being heard (`endorsementHeard` < `askForEndorsement` < `askForSupport`), 626,250 from retelling a statement at an event (`retell` < `makeStatements` < `holdEvent`), 278,130 and 128,636 from the event's own audience, 213,081 from mail, 101,002 from ads. Relationship writes: 150,774 `contact`, 102,647 `care`, 95,493 `shareHomeTime`, 124,599 `meet` at a join, all from ordinary daily life (`life.ts` plans, `people.ts` home time), not from the campaign. So on this seed the election year's cost is writes to the same voter and candidate impression rows many times (one row per pair, so the row count is bounded), and the relationship rows come from daily life in a larger town, not from door knocks. A cut that keeps the result and does not rewrite the row on every hearing of an endorsement (`endorsementHeard` reaches every voter in the room for each endorsement) is the place to look, and the weekly cause fold's `causeSummaries` put (23,226) is small. On the first seed (5a1c0de7a11ce5e1) the folded causes stay the largest table (688,917); that seed's rows were not counted by writer.
Nov 8 (State Sessions), the door-contact cut tried and reverted. Keeping a door knock's "contact" relationship only when the player is one of the two (a volunteer's or a rival's knock keeps its cause and impression only) changed little: Montpelier, Vermont, seed 5a1c0de7a11ce5e1, with the strangers' summary rule of PR "gov-trace-summary-for-strangers" in place: years 101.6, 251.0, 252.8 and 1,140 s, 8.2 GB process, 3.07 million records at the end, of them `core2-campaigns-v1.foldedCauses` 688,917, `core.durableLog` 399,060 and `core.relationships` 324,487; at year 2 relationships were 52,563 against 55,327 without the cut. So the election year's relationship and folded-cause rows do not come from volunteer knocks or from strangers' first-hearing facts. `keepsWhy` of the voter is true for most voters in a town where the player runs (the player's candidacy puts the town in focus), so the summary rule keeps them. The next measurement is by writer, not by guess: count the rows `core.relationships` and `foldedCauses` gain in the election year by the calling function (a counter in a scratch copy of `api.relationship` and `folded.put`) on Montpelier's seed 7e57ab1e0000beef, which finishes in 357 s. Not started.
Nov 8 (State Sessions), the CTO's narrowed trace rule built and measured (PR: gov-trace-summary-for-strangers). Only the player's circle and people in focus (`keepsWhy` of the voter) keep a folded cause as its own row; the first-hearing fact of anyone else names the voter's summary row. Upper Witter Gulch (seed 5a1c0de7a11ce5e1, three runs, `--years 4 --exclusive`): year 4 was 110.7, 112.4 and 104.9 s, every year under 120 s. Montpelier, Vermont, seed 5a1c0de7a11ce5e1: years 102.7, 251.6 and 248.6 s, then out of memory in year 4. Seed 7e57ab1e0000beef: 111.1, 294.4, 164.4 and 357.1 s, process 6.1 GB, 1.33 million records at the end, the most in `core.relationships` 171,335, `core2-campaigns-v1.impressions` 86,311 and `core2-law-effects-v1.applied` 52,819. So on the second seed folded causes are not the growth: it is the campaign's relationship and impression rows (about 80,000 and 40,000 added in the election year), which are the door contacts and meetings of the player's own race. Next: the door-contact focus cut (CTO, Nov 7: only people the player personally met count as contacts in focus; a volunteer's knock keeps only the result), which is `core-speed-door-contact-weighed-rows`. The Montpelier bar of 300 s a year is not met in year 4.
