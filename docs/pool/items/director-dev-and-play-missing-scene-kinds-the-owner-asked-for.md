**What.** On Nov 8 the owner named things a politician does that the player should be able to do, and five of them have no kind of scene. Read by the audit on main as of Nov 9, in code and not played: starting a movement, forming a task force, calling a hard meeting, a campaign trail around the state and a 15-person debate have no scene kind; two doubt scenes exist. Checked by me on Nov 10's main (commit 8ac2262853), read only: src/director/data/situation-types.json holds 103 kinds and none of the five. The engines under three of them already keep records nothing stages. A study committee or task force is a row in the body engine, formed when a body cannot settle a bill (`referToStudy`, src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:4917), and its only reader is its own test (`studiesOf`, src/bench/study-committees.test.ts); nothing forms one by an executive's order. A resident in a crowd can found a standing group (src/core2/modules/public-action.ts) and the player can join one through the cause-invite scene, but the player cannot start one. A debate is a sitting the player takes part in (kinds debate-invite and debate-sitting), and nothing in the elections engine makes a field of 15. Build each as a kind of scene through the engine that already exists: a cause record, a cast from the world, a purpose with four replies, and an ending another engine reads. Each must be reachable in play by the office or campaign that would really do it, and causable from dev mode by running that same game code.

**Time box.** 10 hours for one Sonnet session, one kind per PR, starting with the two whose record already exists (the task force, then the movement). When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** This is a game, not a spreadsheet: each of the five is here because the owner asked for it by name on Nov 8, and none adds a step the player must take. Wire to the world: each scene's cause is a record an engine already keeps or a registered stopgap naming the engine that should, and each ending moves a bill, a group, a race or a tie that another engine reads. No dice: who joins, who carries, who shows up and how many file follow their records and reasons, never a roll. One rule, all 56 places: which executive may form a task force, and how a statewide race travels, are read from the place's data. Owner, Nov 8, 2026: "can you start a movement?"; "Starting committees and task forces to research things? Having a contentious meeting with a secretary or a legislator or an executive"; "campaigning around the state"; "there's 15 people at the debates. Maybe sometimes, there's just 2 or 3". The night Coordinator's call on task forces (docs/coordination/OWNER-DECISIONS.md, Nov 8 to 9, 2026; the owner may overrule): "a record in the one body engine. One is formed when a body refers a split bill to study, or by an executive's order... Story writes the scenes on top."

**Done when.** A different session starts the bench (`npm run bench`, play at /play), begins a new game with the Place field empty so it is a drawn place, writes down the place and seed, and opens dev mode's scene list: each kind built under this item is listed with a way to cause it, not "No game code to attach to". Through Advanced, Play as, it takes the seat that fits (a mayor or governor for the task force and the hard meeting, a resident or officeholder for the movement, a candidate for a statewide office for the trail and the debate), causes each scene from dev mode, answers it, and keeps a screenshot. For each it names the cause record id, how each person was cast, and the record id the ending wrote with the later record that shows another engine read it: the task force's report read by the next bill's positions and by the press; the movement's group and its members in the public-action records; the meeting's result on the bill or budget line it was about; the trail stop's effect on views in the place visited; the debate with every candidate on the contest's record answering. It then plays a year without forcing anything and lists, from the crew's scene tool on the same seed (`node --max-old-space-size=6144 --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed> --out <file>`) and from browser play, which of the kinds fired on their own. A kind that could not be built in the time box has a pool item with its lead and the engine record it waits on. The audit named no place for this reading; its dev-mode screenshots are from Ten Broeck, Kentucky, so repeat the dev mode scene list there. The owner plays it: as a mayor he can order a task force and sit down with a department head who disagrees, and as a citizen he can start a movement instead of only joining one.
