**What.** The thirty most-played spots that still held a single line after the first batch (the job-offer hold-it promise, the welcome-aboard close and the offer-good-until close lead the list) now hold three lines each. Each of 60 new pieces sits in the same spot as the line it varies (same subject, step, move, role, purpose, key and after), says the same fact in other words, and carries a bearing so the speaker's traits pick the tone. The existing lines stay.

**Rules.** Declared slots only; no name tag on most lines; "okay", never "all right" or "alright"; every fact is one the spot already has; American English.

**Done when.** `npm run words:grade` shows no pull and nothing waiting from the new lines and no silent spot; english-parts-banks and the English guards pass; a rerun of scripts/words/one-line-spots.ts lists none of the thirty spots as a one-line spot; the new scene-talk replies that follow a line are judged as pairs by a session that did not write them.

**Time box.** 3 hours for one Sonnet session.
