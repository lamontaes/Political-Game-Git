**What.** The first pass of the legislating domain of the scene web. Two packets from real lives cover a member pressed to switch a close vote, which is the owner's model of consequential dialogue, and residents testifying at a hearing. Where the engines keep the answers from mattering, it is filed for the right lead. The section-bargain narration stops assuming a city council.

**Rules.** Serves `scenes-from-life`: a packet per episode, no real name or line ships. Ask why: each gap names the record that exists and the code that doesn't read it. One rule, all 56 places: a line about a body never names one kind of body when the type plays in several.

**Done when.** Two packets in `docs/design/scenes/from-life/` name their types, and `npm run scene-web` (buildBenchWorld, drawn place and seed named) links them for floor-vote, vote-asked and public-hearing. The vote switch is filed as `legislature-ask-before-the-roll-moves-the-vote` (lead laws-effects), and the member's side of a hearing as `scene-member-hears-testimony` (lead director). Section-bargain has three narrator lines, none naming a council. `english-parts-banks` and `scene-talk.test.ts` pass.

**Time box.** 3 hours.

**You will see.** In the morning report, the legislating rows with real lives behind them. In play, after the items land, a colleague's ask the night before a vote that can change the roll.
