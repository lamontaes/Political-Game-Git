**What.** Speeches should cite real figures, lines should fit the kind of speaker, and rhetorical craft such as threes and call and response should be earned by the speaker's skill. Today a speech's lines do not depend on who speaks or on the numbers in the town. Give the English engine the speaker's kind and skill and the record figures to cite.

**Rules.** No hand-written player text: words come from the English engine. No dice: craft follows the speaker's recorded skill, never a roll. Ask why: each cited figure names its record id.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a drawn place and seed the builder did not choose, a speech by a skilled speaker uses a three or a call and response and cites a figure with its record id, an unskilled speaker's does not, and the batch page grades both; a test in src/english states it.
