**What.** The simulated inaugural address reads "My fellow citizens. We are one people. Unemployment is at 4.1 percent. I want to thank the Governors. We see you. The best is yet to come." Speeches should weave in the world's real figures (inflation, Social Security, jobs, the place's own numbers), give lines to kinds of speakers and speaking styles, and keep rhetorical craft (threes, call and response, a callback) for skilled speakers.

**Rules.** No dice: a speaker's craft reads their traits and record, never a roll. Figures come from the records at the moment of the speech. Craft reads the speaker's traits and record (experience, skill), never a roll. Lines come from the English engine's banks. Graded on its own curve on the review page.

**Done when.** In a played world built by buildBenchWorld, two speeches on one unchosen seed, by a skilled and an unskilled speaker, differ in structure by their records, each cites at least two figures by record, and the owner grades a speech page.

**Files.** `src/english/`, `data/english/`, the speech producers' callers in `src/director/`.
