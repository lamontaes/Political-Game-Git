**What.** The core owns disasters. The question: do they come only from real dated hazard records (FEMA), from causes in the world's conditions, or both? `core-disasters-ss-prices` builds the real-records path. No ruling found in OWNER-DECISIONS.md or the register.

**Rules.** Look before you ask; world events happen from causes, never a monthly chance.

**Done when.** A ruling line is written into OWNER-DECISIONS.md with a quote and `core-disasters-ss-prices` follows it, shown by a disaster record id in a one-year run of the played world in a drawn place with its cause named.

**Files.** docs/coordination/OWNER-DECISIONS.md

**Notes.** core.md line 225, checked Oct 15.

Found Oct 17 (claude): `data/research/core-figures/P0-12/storm-2024-<state>.json` (51 files) holds NOAA Storm Events 2024 annual hazard totals by state with deaths, injuries and property and crop damage, retrieved Oct 11. It is the data a disaster hazard (and the outcome web's "severe disaster", 100 or more deaths in a county) would be calibrated from; no engine reads it. Once the owner rules how disasters arise in play, the first reader is the hazard rate by state from these rows.
