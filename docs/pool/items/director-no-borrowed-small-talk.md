**What.** A scene whose own lines are missing hands the player to everyday talk, and everyday talk says whatever is biggest on record, which has often been the President's address. Measured by the audit (As Matuis, Northern Mariana Islands, a serious illness forced on the player through dev mode): "You've been seriously ill. Your mother sits down with you." "We need to talk." "I've heard those promises too many times." The replies were about the President, in March 2026, 13 months after the address. Measured by the audit with the crew's scene tool on main as of Nov 9 (Ninnekah, Oklahoma, seed 3dac8ffb3f3c; Tesuque, New Mexico, seed 8a5d1fba28fc; Odessa, Washington, seed fd7e212758b7): 17 of 64 scenes fell back on small talk: ten letters, four visits after a missed family gathering, two pieces of news and an address. I ran the same three seeds on Nov 10's main (commit 8ac2262853). The letters and the gathering visits now play lines of their own, after Story's Nov 9 merges. Two of 64 still fall back, both the eviction news at home (kind news-arrives), and the governor's address in Odessa played as narration alone. Read in code, not run: `showStory` (src/bench/play.mjs:2058; the branch is at line 2113) plays a scene's own exchange when it has one and otherwise calls `talkTo`, which opens `openConversation` (src/director/conversation.ts:2116), the everyday-talk engine, whose subjects (`subjectsBetween`, line 966) include the address, a person's political lean, the school and the news. Five kinds have no purpose and take that road whenever someone is there to talk to (departure, news-arrives, visit, hearing, verdict), and any kind whose purpose cannot be worded (`purposeScene` returns "skipped", src/director/purpose-scene.ts:166) takes it too. Fix the class: remove the fallback. A scene whose own lines are missing does not play; its cause becomes a journal line; and the missing bank is reported by kind, purpose and step, so Words can see what to write.

**Time box.** 8 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** Replace, never layer: the hand-off from a scene to everyday talk is deleted in the same change, in the bench and in the director, and the PR counts what was removed; no second fallback takes its place. No hand-written player text: nothing typed in code fills the gap; a missing bank is reported, not papered over. An effect is a read, not a record: a scene that only makes small talk writes nothing another engine reads, so it has no reason to stop the player. Owner, Nov 8, 2026, on the scene that came from this fallback: "this is a terrible scene. And this should have been taken out of the game a long time ago." The audit's fix list, grading section, item 5: "Remove the fallback. A scene whose own lines are missing does not play."

**Done when.** A different session starts the bench (`npm run bench`, play at /play), begins a new game with the Place field empty so it is a drawn place, writes down the place and seed, and plays one year with dev mode's Run, opening every scene that stops play. It runs the crew's scene tool on the same seed (`node --max-old-space-size=6144 --import tsx scripts/dialogue-batch/scene-year.ts --seed <seed> --out <file>`); in its output file a scene has lines of its own when its `exchange` is filled. It must see: every scene that stopped play has its own exchange, zero scenes open an everyday talk, and no scene line names the President's speech unless the scene's cause is that speech. It then forces a serious illness on the player through dev mode's scene controls, as the audit did, and sees either a scene about the illness or no scene and a journal line, with a screenshot. The same on the audit's seeds 3dac8ffb3f3c, 8a5d1fba28fc and fd7e212758b7: the eviction news either plays its own lines or does not stop the player. A command or dev mode panel prints the missing-bank report (kind, purpose, step) for the year, and a test fails if the bench can open an everyday talk from a scene. The owner plays it: no scene ever turns into "Did you catch the President's speech?" again; a moment with nothing written for it stays in his journal.

One trap in the tool: its console log prints the everyday talk it opens with each scene's cast even when the scene has an exchange of its own, so the log shows small talk ("I should tell you, I lean liberal.") under scenes that the game plays with their own lines. Read the `exchange` field in the output file, not the console.

Not verified: I did not reproduce the forced illness in As Matuis. `subjectsBetween` keeps the address as a subject for 14 days from the day it was given (`directorTalkAddressDays`, src/director/data/director.json), so a line about the President 13 months after the opening address needs explaining: either a later address was inside the window, or the reply came by another road. `openConversation` also carries a rule that a scene's talk keeps to its occasion and does not turn to small talk (near line 2287); the audit's runs show small talk got through it, and I did not find how.
