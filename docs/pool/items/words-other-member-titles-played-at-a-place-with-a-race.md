**What.** The race seat is said by the member of a body named for its members through `memberOfBody` (data/english/seat-words.json: aldermen, trustees, selectmen, commissioners, supervisors, councilmen, councilors, councillors, burgesses, jurors, freeholders, directors, assessors). Only alderman has been played (Shelby, Aberdeen and Abita Springs). The others are untested in play. Seen: the interview invitation and the paper's filing, petition, debate and result stories at a town for each member word. Two Illinois places drawn for trustees (Aroma Park 1702258, Beach Park 1704303) had no race the player could enter within a year.

Seen: the interview invitation and the paper's race stories at a town for each member word.

**Cause.** Needs cause. Nothing is known to be wrong; this is the missing check, and any wrong word found goes to its own item.

**Fix.** None until the cause is found.

**Time box.** 3 hours for one Sonnet session.

**Rules.** One rule, all 56 places: choose places by the body's name in the office record, never by naming a place in code. Say the place and seed of each town played.

**Done when.** A second session, in the running game on main, plays one drawn place for each member word the table holds that some place's office record uses (trustee, selectman, commissioner, supervisor and the rest), names the place and seed, and reads the reporter's invitation and the paper's filing, petition, debate and result stories there; each reads as that place says it, and the lines are in a file for the words read.
