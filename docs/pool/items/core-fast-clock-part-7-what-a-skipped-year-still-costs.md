**What.** The owner asked for twenty years in about five seconds. Parts 1 to 4 bring a skipped year down from day-by-day time; nobody knows yet to what. This part measures it in three towns of different sizes and names, engine by engine, what still costs more than a second a year. Feeds: core-fast-clock-twenty-years (it names what still costs more than a second a year, so the 20 years button reaches about five seconds).

**Cause.** Measured: Chumuckla, Florida (953 people; world seed aa4665b284a797eb671c6eafef8865086489b881e3eb81e1116a200598fb6482): a year of only dated work took 14.6 seconds, so twenty would take about five minutes in the smallest town before parts 2 to 4 add their monthly steps. The largest costs were the press week 1.8 seconds, other governments' floor weeks 1.6, the housing review 1.4, the jobs review 1.3, counted renewal 0.9 and local meetings 0.9.

**Fix.** With parts 1 to 4 on main, time twenty skipped years in three drawn places (a small, a middling and a large town), one process at a time (skill speed-work and skill heavy-runs). From a profile of one skipped election year in each, list every engine that costs more than a second a year. Write the table into this item's Notes and the parent's, and file one pool item per engine on the list, at the item standard, each with its cause in code.

**Time box.** 4 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note and stop.

**Rules.** One run, one number, named with its place, seed and commit. An inclusive profile share is not a saving (skill speed-work). No fix is built in this part.

**Done when.** A second session reads the table in this item's Notes, then on main runs the 20 years control in a drawn place it names with its seed and in one of the table's three places: the second time is within a fifth of the table's, and the drawn place's time sits between the smallest and largest town's or the report says why not. Every engine on the list has a pool item that names its file and function.
