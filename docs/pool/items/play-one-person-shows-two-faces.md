**What.** Measured by the audit on Nov 9 in Blodgett Landing, New Hampshire (a random world; the audit gives no seed), playing a 17-year-old girl the game named Ray Kent: "One person, two faces. The You screen's bust has light brown hair and pale skin. The Your life tab shows a full figure with black hair and tan skin. Her mother's bust resembles neither of them." The audit's screenshot of the second face is `docs/audits/fable-vision-2026-11-09-screens/01-your-life-four-lines.jpg` (branch audit/fable-vision). The audit names the cause as duplicate drawing code: "Six person cards and three ways to draw a face. The cards are `memberCard`, `leaderBlock`, `personCardNode`, `candidate`, `townRow` and `savedLifeRow`; the faces are `headshot`, `bust` and `fillArtwork`, all under `src/bench`. This is how one character showed two faces on one screen." Read in code on main, Nov 10: the three face parts are `headshot` (`src/bench/ui/headshot.mjs`, line 11), `bust` (`src/bench/ui/strips.mjs`, line 38) and `fillArtwork` (`src/bench/ui/picture.mjs`, line 12). The You bust is fed the card's face record (`faceOf` in `src/bench/scene.ts`, line 932), while the Your life figure is fed `portraitUrl()` (`src/bench/play.mjs`, line 3167), which picks among three pictures: the creator's own portrait, the figure the current scene draws, or the card's face. Which of those gave the second face was not traced. Fix the class: one face part and one picture source per person, read from that person's one looks record, used by every card, list, bust and menu; the other face parts and the duplicate cards are deleted.

**Time box.** 8 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** Replace, never layer: the face parts and person cards that are not the one kept are deleted in the same change with their styles, and the PR body counts them. One model, two tiers: a counted person filled in on a click gets their face from the same rule and seed as a full person, so the same person comes back with the same face every time. Owner, Oct 28 (AGENTS.md, where the game is going): "It's just different ways of displaying the same information." Owner, Oct 26 (screen-to-mock skill): "There's a bunch of independent UI things... This all needs to be unified... there's too much duplication." Owner, Nov 8, 2:10 p.m.: "I'm Mexican and my son is white. I don't know how that happened."

**Done when.** A different session starts the bench (`npm run bench`), starts a new game through the creator in a drawn place (place and seed named in the proof), and takes screenshots at 1440×900 of every place the player's own face appears: the player card in the corner, You and its two other tabs, the talk box in one scene, People, the title's Continue and Saved games after returning to the title. Hair color, skin tone and the pack person must be the same in all of them, and the proof names the one looks record id they were read from. It then opens the cards of each household member from People and from a scene and checks each of them the same way, and checks that a parent and child share the family's origin on their looks records. A search of `src/bench` must find one function that draws a face and one that draws a person card; a test fails when a second is added. The same screenshots are then taken in Blodgett Landing, New Hampshire, the audit's place (the audit gives no seed), as the named repro. The owner plays it: his character has one face everywhere he looks, and his family looks related.

**Notes.** From the audit's Pillar 4 (measured in the browser life), Pillar 6 ("There are six ways to draw a person card and three ways to draw a face", read) and "Duplicate systems" item 4 ("I checked each item below in the code myself"). What the current code contradicts in the audit's account: `townRow` (`src/bench/ui/town-row.mjs`, line 19) draws a town in the creator's place list, not a person, so it is not a person card; a sixth place that does draw a person with its own layout is the Play as row (`src/bench/ui/play-as.mjs`, line 60). The others are as the audit says: `memberCard` (`ui/member-card.mjs`, line 15, three callers in `play.mjs`), `leaderBlock` (`ui/strips.mjs`, line 236, three callers), `personCardNode` (`play.mjs`, line 2217), `candidate` (`ui/strips.mjs`, line 545, a wrapper around a member card and stance rows) and `savedLifeRow` (`ui/strips.mjs`, line 314). Also read: `packPersonOf` in `src/bench/whole-figures.ts` (line 82) is written to give "the same person... everywhere" from one seed, and both the card and the scene build the person through `whoOf` (`scene.ts`, line 898), so the two faces may come from the creator's own portrait (`/api/portrait`, drawn from the looks recipe) standing beside the pack person; trace that first, in the audit's place, before deleting anything. The family's shared origin landed after the Nov 8 playtest (`src/core2/opening-origin.ts`; stopgap SG-PEOPLE-origin-skin-tones narrows the pack to three skin tones per origin), which may be why a mother still resembles neither.
