**What.** Dev mode's Trace is meant to open any record and say why it happened. Measured in Savage Town, Virginia (the lead's own session; no seed given): after 45 days of play the lead asked Trace about 19 records that the journal and the news name as their own sources. Ten came back "record-not-found" and nine came back "no-saved-reason". Trace answered why for none of them. In two other sessions the Trace of a law just passed said "record-not-found" as well (As Matuis, Northern Mariana Islands, a minimum wage raise; Bigelow, Arkansas, a town property tax raise). So the game cites sources its own tracer cannot find, and the ones it finds have no reason attached. Read in code on Nov 10: `trace` (`src/bench/dev-mode.ts`, line 1629) and the trace box on the screens (`traceNode`, `src/bench/dev-trace.ts`, line 224) both take their answer from `resolveSavedTraceV1` (`src/core2/saved-trace-v1.ts`, line 1539). It answers "record-not-found" when its lookup has no row for the id (line 1552) and "no-saved-reason" when the row carries no saved reason, score or choice (line 1584). A law row lives in the laws module's own table, which `trace` reads only to fill its `record` field after the resolver has already answered. Which of the 19 ids were stories, law rows, bills or events was not recorded. Fix the class: every id that any screen, journal entry or news story names as a source resolves to its record in Trace, and each answers why: the saved reasons where they are kept, and otherwise the chain of causes the record itself names (a story to the event it reports, the event to the decision, law or dated cause behind it), one click at a time, down to a person's decision or a law.

**Time box.** 10 hours for one session (sized for Sonnet; the lead is Fable). When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note, and stop.

**Rules.** Ask why: every proof answers why, by record, so a source the game prints must open to its cause in the game's own tracer, never to "not found". The owner's ruling of Oct 14, morning, as the CTO recorded it in `OWNER-DECISIONS.md`: "Anything the tracer cannot answer is a dev-mode item. A legislative vote shows each member's reasons; a business closing shows its books; a budget cut names the law and the line's history; a person shows job, pay, hours and spending." Ruled by the CTO from standing rules, Oct 14: dev mode's Trace is the ultimate trace, "recorded reasons where kept, recomputed from the same seeded history where not." Keep it simple, owner, Oct 11: bookkeeping is a debug switch and "why" reasons are saved only for the player and the people they deal with, so the fix follows cause links and recomputes on a click; it does not start saving reasons for everyone. Speed: nothing is added to a played day.

**Done when.** A second session, in dev mode in a drawn place: it starts the bench (`npm run bench`, `/play`), leaves the Place field empty so the place is drawn, writes down the place and seed the title shows, turns Dev mode on in Options and runs 45 days from the Calendar's Run panel. It opens the Journal and the News and, for the first ten source ids each of them names, opens Trace on that id (from the Watch panel's news table, a person's Records section, or `/api/dev/trace/node`). None answers "record-not-found". Each shows its record and either saved reasons or a cause that can be clicked, and following the causes ends, within a few clicks, at a person's decision with its reasons, a law row or a dated cause such as a term ending. It also passes one bill from the Bills panel, runs until its law takes effect, and opens Trace on the law row's record id: the bill, the vote and each member's reasons are there. It saves a screenshot of one full chain with the place and seed under it. A test in a drawn place with its seed named runs the same 45 days, gathers every source id the journal entries and the news stories name (from the same views those screens read), traces each one, prints how many were found and how many answered why, and fails on any "record-not-found" or any record with neither a reason nor a cause; it fails when the fix is undone. It then repeats the 45 days in Savage Town, Virginia (the audit's place; no seed was given, so the seed drawn is written down) and reports the count beside the audit's 0 of 19, and repeats the law trace in As Matuis and in Bigelow. The owner plays it: with Dev mode on he clicks the source under a news story or a journal line and reads, step by step, who decided what and why.
