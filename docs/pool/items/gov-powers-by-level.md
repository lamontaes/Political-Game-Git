**What.** What a town, a county, a state and Congress may each legislate, tax and spend on, from data per place (home rule or Dillon's rule, each state's grant to counties and towns, federal enumerated powers), read by the authority check before a bill is introduced and by the executive before an order.

**Rules.** One rule, all 56 places; no state named in logic.

**Done when.** A bill outside a body's powers is refused for its recorded reason in a drawn place.

**Files.** src/core2/modules/legislature.ts, authority data

**Notes.** None.
