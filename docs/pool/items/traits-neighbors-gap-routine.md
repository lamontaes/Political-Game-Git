**What.** The matrix's opposite-neighbors gap (how much further apart the daily mixes of the most opposite adults in a town sit with personality than with traits wiped flat) is 0.00 to 0.04 in six drawn towns on the family branches, against 0.10 on October 20, and the lead's proof asks for twice that. The mix is mostly routine personality barely touches: going to scheduled work is 35 percent of picks with 0.7 percent turned, shelving a business idea each month 25 percent with none turned, school and staying out of races most of the rest. The main daily outlet, seeing someone against resting, is about a tenth of picks, and in some pairs temperament cancels a difference circumstances made, which narrows them. Find what would let temperament show in more of a day without adding a mechanic the owner has not asked for, measure it on main after the families merge, and report the gap.

**Rules.** Wire to the world: measured in one year of buildBenchWorld per drawn place, played against flat and mirror twins. Never loosen the check: the gap is the matrix's existing neighbors measure, not a narrower one. This is a game, not a spreadsheet: a new daily activity is the owner's call, not a fix here.

**Done when.** scripts/traits/trait-wiring-matrix.ts on main with every traits family merged, in two places drawn from all 56, seeds named, prints the opposite-neighbors gap with each action's share of the difference, and either the gap reaches twice the October 20 value or the report names the cause in numbers and the owner question it leaves.

**Notes.** Filed Oct 24 by the traits lead. Gaps measured: Holualoa, Hawaii -0.002; Peacham, Vermont +0.040; Ora, Indiana +0.035; Arbela, Missouri -0.097; Jerry City, Ohio +0.014; New Britain, Connecticut +0.029. Measured Oct 24 by the traits lead in the same six twin runs: flipping a person's own traits moves their year's mix 0.20 to 0.41 apart (the matrix's meanDistanceSelfMirrored: Arbela 0.368, Ora 0.203, Jerry City 0.205, New Britain 0.283, Peacham 0.407, Holualoa 0.270), so temperament does reshape a person's year. The opposite-neighbors gap stays near zero because neighbors picked only for opposite traits already differ by job, age and household, and the distance saturates: Ora's opposite neighbors sit 0.79 apart with traits flat and 0.82 with them. Picks at chooser sites temperament turns at least 5% of the time are 13.5 to 23.8 percent of all picks. Measure again on main once the families merge before concluding.
