**What.** An officeholder's records should fire scenes: an address is a speech scene, a signing brings staff and press into the room, and some endings open a game screen. Today the director watches the player's life but not what an officeholder's own act records write. Have the director read the office-day act records and open the matching scene for the person who acted.

**Rules.** No hand-written player text: words come from the English engine and screens show record data and control names. An effect is a read, not a record: the director reads the act record at the moment it is written.

**Done when.** In a one-year run of the played world (buildBenchWorld) in a drawn place and seed the builder did not choose, an officeholder's address and a bill signing each open a director scene citing the act record id, the scene names the staff and press present from the records, and a test in src/director states it.
