**What.** Draft the Steam store page text and the early-access FAQ from what the shipped game does. Every claim must be something a player can do in the game on main.

**Cause.** Design: docs/roadmap/later-specs-draft.md section M7-06 (approved by the owner Oct 12) and docs/packages/M7-early-access.md.

**Fix.** Write the store text and FAQ in one document under docs/, claim by claim, each claim naming the screen or play step that shows it. Leave the price and trailer to part 3.

**Time box.** 5 hours for one Sonnet session. When it runs out, push what passes, set the state honestly with a one-line note and stop.

**Rules.** Look before you ask: read ROADMAP.md and docs/packages/M7-early-access.md first. This is a game, not a spreadsheet: claim only what the golden path plays. Owner decisions are flagged, never waited on.

**Done when.** A second session plays a drawn place on main and checks each claim in the store text and FAQ against what it can do in the running game, finding every claim true, each claim naming its screen.

**Waits on.** Early-access preparation (AGENTS.md: saves and release work wait for it) and the weekly reschedule.
