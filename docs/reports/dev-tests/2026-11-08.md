# Dev Tester report, Nov 8 night (newest first)

Replay any line: `tsx scripts/dev-tests/law-run.ts <seed> <city|county|state> [policy substring] [days]`, then `node scripts/dev-tests/summarize.mjs <raw file>`. Raw lines are in `docs/reports/dev-tests/raw/`.

## Status at the first checkpoint

**Proved so far (Phase 1, in progress, 0 of 89 laws fully graded):** the harness drives the game's own route: draft a bill at the body that has the power, every member of every chamber votes yes, the vote is held, the executive acts, the law engine is read after the lag, then a second bill repeals. A control world from the same seed is run for the same days, so differences are the law's, plus any chance chains it sets off. When a vetoed or failed bill changes nothing, the twin matches the control exactly (cash and organization cash differ by 0), so the twin check itself is clean.

**City level, Prairie Village, Kansas (seed 8aadc4eadb2fce04f3477fe846529b657c108aa220a4db232645bdb8486b87b8), 9 policies so far:** income, sales and property tax and shift-highway-funds-to-transit show the law in force at city level after the lag, and sales tax repeals. The four budget-line bills pass but the law engine reports no row for them (they move through the budget engine, so my law-row check does not apply; their money moved, so they are not graded broken yet). Fare-free transit was vetoed by the mayor.

**Findings so far**

- Five random draws had no city body at all (Buccaneer Bay NE, Schofield Barracks HI, Gantt SC, Schurz NV, Union NH). Seats exist only for statewide offices there. Needs the owner's call on whether a place with no local government should be playable as one; owner: Laws and effects.
- A passed city bill takes effect 30 days later by the state's rule, so reading the law the same day looks like "no change". The harness reads after the lag.
- Executives veto many bills even with every member voting yes. Dev mode's forced pass needs a veto-override step (Dev Mode session).

**Not done:** county and federal levels, the other 80 laws, the second place per law, effects against research size and timing, news and memory checks, phases 2 and 3.
