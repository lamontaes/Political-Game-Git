## What it means

- **Real numbers steer the world after day one.** The nation drifts back to 4.5% unemployment and 2.451% inflation with no shocks, and every level below copies the one above. Counted households, most of every town, have births, deaths, illness, crime, school results and partnerships set by a real yearly rate with a carry; their circumstances only pick who. Outside elections replay 2024 with flat midterm and incumbency numbers. Six checks fail runs that leave a real range.
- **It got past every check** because the guards test a number's form (sourced? random? copied on day one?), not what it does each month. The pass marks I wrote into the Oct 10 work orders rewarded staying inside real ranges. The Sept 30 golden rule never reached the rule files, and #4030 (Oct 12) brought back anchors #881 (Sept 28) had removed.
- **The rest of the country moves only thinly.** Other states pass yes-or-no laws, but neighbors see only starting laws, and those laws reach no economy or news. National opinion resets to 2024, there is no presidential approval, no national event engine, no courts, and the President is idle unless played.
- **Most screen gaps are display work.** The records behind "What people say about me", quotes on cards, "What he wants", department heads and "Asked" already exist. So do closeness, why a tie changed, political views, world panels and the scene-stop setting; no screen shows them.
- **The code is spread thin.** About 246,000 lines of game code and 204,000 of tests. A household's spending is built in 5 places, paychecks run 3 ways, a person's lean lives in 5, named and counted people follow different rules, four screen kits sit side by side, 8 scene protocols, money formatted 7 ways.
- **Markers:** 775 registered stopgaps (485 open, 9 stale, 11 unregistered, 13 resolved but still called, 265 open with no pool item); 2,852 ESTIMATED or TUNABLE numbers (185 set a result directly; 12 core decision weights say "blocks prototype release"); 48 researched links that run nowhere; 835 live pool items. No TODO or FIXME in shipped code. Two found bugs: the affectionate trait never loads, and governors' term limits are never enforced.

## The proposed guard: `no-steering`

- **Label every parameter row** coefficient, level, opening or law. An unlabeled row fails.
- **Scan code that runs every day or month** for four patterns:
  - PULL: L + k*(x-L), L - gap/k, or k*(T-own), where L or T is a level row, research data or a higher place's figure;
  - FIXED RATE: a real rate applied with no term from the world, or a rescale to a published total;
  - CLAMP on an outcome;
  - BAND ASSERTION: a test or tool comparing a played measure to a real range.
- **Identities stay allowed,** such as money balancing and counts adding up. The baseline list only shrinks.
- **A behavior test backs it:** push a value off in one of two identical worlds on day one, run a year, and fail if the gap closes with no recorded cause.
- **Where it would fire today:** `macro-model.ts:306-317, 330-345`, `regional-model.ts:315, 325-329, 340-350`, `jobs.ts:922-968, 979-1009, 2012-2015`, `counted-moves.ts:143, 544, 729-770`, `medical-bills.ts:464-500`, `school-outcomes.ts:350-380`, `outcome-web-model.ts:368-381`, and every band test above.
