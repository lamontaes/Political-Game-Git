## 3. History

### The one history every reader should pull from

**The pre-start history store, `preStartHistory` in `src/core2/pre-start.ts`, and the durable log after the start.** It is the only history the engines run. Six module passes write it over the years before day one (`runPreStart`, called once at `src/bench/world.ts:518`): `macro.ts`, `laws.ts`, `business.ts`, `budgets.ts`, `offices.ts` and `people.ts`. It already holds everything else as copies: `peopleHistory` (`modules/people.ts:228-255`) copies every `pastFacts` row of every person into it as `person.<kind>`. So the store exists. The readers do not read it: no file in `src/director` or `src/english` reads `preStartHistory`, and the only screen that does is the person card's `seats` (`src/bench/card.ts:81-93`), which `play.mjs` never shows.

Two more facts change what must move:

- **H1, four play-time callers of the generated stand-in.** `buildDeepPast` (`deep-past.ts:215`, stopgap SG-P8-deep-past) runs for the whole town at `src/bench/world.ts:337`, for each newborn at `births-people.ts:156` (`buildNewborn`), for each counted person who is met at `state.ts:2236` (`fillInPerson`), and for each named official at `state.ts:2679` (`fillInIdentity`). The last three run after `runPreStart`, so a person filled in during play gets stand-in facts that never reach the history store (inferred from the call order). The director's grading tool and four core tooling scripts also call it.
- **H4, the working life is not on main.** `src/core2/past-work.ts` is on open pull request #6452 only. Until it merges, every reader of a person's working life reads the stand-in `work:tenure-summary`, which is built from a typed table of median years with an employer (`town-workplaces.ts:513-519`).

### Which helper carries a number

Five carriers can hold a number:

- **The per-world draw:** `seededUnit` (`src/core2/past-module.ts:72`; open #6468 moves it to `src/core2/seeded.ts`). #6446 uses it in a pattern: a `published…` function for calibration and a world function drawn from the seed on top of it, with the spread in a tagged parameter row. - **`countedSpread`** (`modules/residents.ts:278`) reads a spread row from `data/counted/parameters.json` for the counted tier. It has two callers (`residents.ts`, `counted-demotion.ts`) and is not a per-world draw. It carries a share that spreads across counted people.
- **`quotedLineRate`** (`modules/macro.ts:533`) prices a credit line off the national rate. It carries only interest rates on loans and lines.
- **The `estimate-not-unknown` rule:** a fallback when a law or data row is blank becomes the real average with its source and spread, never a typed number.
- **The module's own parameter table** (`src/core2/data/<module>/parameters.json`, `src/director/data/director.json`) for everything that is a rule, a weight or a duration.

A rule set by statute (a voting age, a minimum age for an office, a term length) is law, not a statistic: it belongs in the law or office data row, the same one in every world, and read from that row, not typed.
