**The biggest gaps:**

1. **The economy is held to fixed numbers.** It always drifts back to 4.5% unemployment and about 2.45% inflation, and nothing in the world can push it. Six tests fail any run that leaves a real range. Money starts on this today.
2. **Your own town is still made event by event.** Every town hall, every listener, every weekly interview. Relationship records are never folded down. That's why 20 years takes minutes and big towns hit 8 GB.
3. **Scenes have one version each, and none of the lines are yours.** Story is adding versions per kind, lines from your voice notes, and the 6 kinds that can't start.
4. **The player can only react, never start politics.** You can't start a movement, form a task force, or call a hard meeting with a secretary. And the other 49 states never change a law.
5. **Screens are close but not clean.** The quotes and "What he wants" lines you approved are missing, because no record holds them yet. Desk scenes keep the menu rail. The meeting still has a colored tally bar, and Options has no color-blind mode.
