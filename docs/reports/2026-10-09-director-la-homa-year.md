# Margaret's 2021 holds one moment: the day her store closed

The owner's answers to the director's seven questions are applied, so nothing here waits on the owner. The story director now runs a full year on the new simulation core in La Homa, Texas. It turns what happens to a watched person into moments, the changes big enough to keep, and threads, that person's view of each other person. Margaret Harrell, 51, her household's only earner, has one moment in 2021: her store closing on March 14. It ends her household's pay, so it takes over the screen as news she brings home. Her recorded past adds seven moments to her journal. For the game, a year now yields the few moments worth playing.

## Why it reads this way

**One moment from 2021, against 2,529 small changes.** Measured: across her 20 classmates combined, Margaret's closeness rose 2,500 times during the year; to her daughter Ava it rose 29 times. Each rise was at most 0.05. Inferred from the rule: 0.05 times the tie scale of 0.35 is 0.018, under the floor of 0.11 (owner answer 1). Each change moved its thread but stored no moment. The tie rule is at `src/director/ledger.ts:653`.

**The job loss takes over the screen.** Inferred from the rule at `src/director/ledger.ts:712`: her pay was all of the household's earned pay, so the share lost is 1.000. That share times the money scale of 0.470 (the stress scale's "fired at work", 47 points) and her risk weight of 1.083 gives 0.509. She avoids risk, recorded as −1. That is above the owner's interrupt line of 0.50, the stress scale's marriage anchor. Read from the code: the core's closure event carries no feeling (`src/core2/finance-state.ts:1083`), so the feeling channel adds nothing.

**Her recorded past is in the journal, not on screen.** Measured: seven facts dated before 2021 became moments. Her three school stages score 0.26 each, her partnership with Peter in 1994 scores 0.50, and her three children's births score 0.39 each. These are the stress scale's rows for beginning school, marriage and gaining a family member, labeled ESTIMATED (owner answer 5). Read from the code: past moments are journal lines, never scenes (`src/director/ledger.ts:426`). The 2001, 2008 and 2020 public events are background only, because her record does not say any of them hit her.

**Her family's threads rise after the closure.** Measured: on January 1, Peter's thread reads 1.300, the standing tie of 0.800 for a partner in a shared home plus their 1994 partnership. Each child's reads 1.190, the same tie plus a birth. Each rises by 0.509 on April 1, the first month boundary after the March 14 closure. Measured in the run: the closure stored a money moment for each relative in their own records. Read from the code: Margaret's threads count a broad event that hit both people as the pair's shared history (`src/director/ledger.ts:535`). Measured: the closure stored 564 personal moments, 218 for the workers it named and 346 for household members. It reached 348 household members; why 2 of them stored nothing was not checked.

**Her classmates come back with nothing at stake.** Measured: all 20 first contacted her on January 12 or 13, 2021, 36 years after they started high school together in August 1984. Each thread renewed and stayed at zero importance. Read from the code: the core records the classmates as a generated school group with remembered names (`src/core2/opening-peer-network.ts:118`). It records no event between Margaret and any one of them, so a return adds nothing. Their closeness reached 0.502, above her husband's 0.462, because the core raises closeness only on recorded contact.

**Erica's year is two moments that take over the screen.** Measured: her husband's serious illness reached her at 0.643, and his death at 0.921. Both are above the interrupt line. The funeral record links back to the illness news; that link is the callback. Inferred from the rule at `src/director/ledger.ts:560`: a moment's hindsight value is its impact times (1 + John's thread importance now). His importance is now 2.864: the 0.800 tie, their 1990 partnership at 0.500, the illness and the death. So the illness reads 0.643 × 3.864 = 2.486 and the death 0.921 × 3.864 = 3.560. The callback's strength, 1.894, is the illness moment's hindsight as stored on January 2. John's importance then was 0.800 + 0.500 + 0.643 = 1.943, so 0.643 × 2.943 = 1.892, with the difference from rounding. Measured: Erica's household holds only John, and her record keeps one person she knew, John, so the funeral lists no mourners.

**Anniversaries had no first anniversary to fall on.** Measured: town-wide, 10 moments between people who shared a broad event were linked back to it. All fell more than half a year after the event and well before its first anniversary, so each link is weaker than 0.001 (owner answer 7).

## What happens next

- **For the owner:** nothing. The answers are applied: the floor of 0.11, the interrupt line of 0.50, the pace, fading, the recorded past, watching, and anniversaries. Each is one number in `src/director/data/director.json`.
- **For SOL-1258, who maintains the core:** closeness never rises above 0.502 through contact (`src/core2/state.ts:579`).
- **For SOL-1258:** no contact is recorded between Margaret and her husband or two of her children all year. The "last contact" of January 1, 2021 on those threads is the date the core created the family relationship, not a contact.
- **For SOL-1258:** Margaret's household has no income on record after March.
- **For SOL-1258, the recorded past:** shared history between people who knew each other, so that someone from the past can come back with something at stake.
- **Hooks approved and passed to SOL-1258:** the core's feeling calculation for each person who hears an event, passed with the event; and a notice when a tie changes.
- **Fixed in this pull request:** a partner was recorded as known since the older partner's birth. A partner is now known no later than the opening, or from an earlier dated record naming both, such as Erica and John's 1990 partnership.
- **For the life-replay harness (P9):** it replays lives on the old core only, so no replayed life could be added here.
