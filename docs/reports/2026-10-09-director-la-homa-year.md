# Margaret's 2021 holds one moment: the day her store closed

This report adds no new question; the owner's answers to the seven questions in the director design note set its numbers. The story director now runs a full year on the new simulation core in La Homa, Texas. It turns what happens to a watched person into moments, the changes big enough to keep, and threads, that person's view of each other person. Margaret Harrell, 51, her household's only earner, has one moment in 2021: her store closing on March 14. It waits as news she brings home. Twenty high school classmates come back in January, but nothing they shared is on record, so they weigh nothing. For the game, a year now yields the few moments worth playing.

## Why it reads this way

**One stored moment, against 2,529 small changes.** Measured: across her 20 classmates combined, Margaret's closeness rose 2,500 times during the year; to her daughter Ava it rose 29 times. Each rise was at most 0.05. Inferred from the rule: 0.05 times the tie scale of 0.35 is 0.018, under the recommended floor of 0.11 (owner question 1). Each change moved its thread but stored no moment. The tie rule is at `src/director/ledger.ts:523`.

**The job loss is the year's strongest change.** Inferred from the rule at `src/director/ledger.ts:582`: her pay was all of the household's earned pay, so the share lost is 1.000. That share times the money scale of 0.470 (the stress scale's "fired at work", 47 points) and her risk weight of 1.083 gives 0.509. She avoids risk, recorded as −1. Read from the code: the core's closure event carries no feeling (`src/core2/finance-state.ts:1083`), so the feeling channel adds nothing.

**Her family's threads rise after the closure.** Measured: each of her four relatives reads 0.800 on January 1, the standing tie for a partner or child plus a shared home. Each reads 1.309 on April 1, the first month boundary after the March 14 closure. Measured in the run: the closure stored a money moment for each relative in their own records. Read from the code: Margaret's threads count a broad event that hit both people as the pair's shared history (`src/director/ledger.ts:405`). That is why her family threads show 0 moments of their own but importance 1.309. Measured: the closure stored 564 personal moments, 218 for the workers it named and 346 for household members. It reached 348 household members; why 2 of them stored nothing was not checked.

**Her classmates come back with nothing at stake.** Measured: all 20 first contacted her on January 12 or 13, 2021, 36 years after they started high school together in August 1984. Each thread renewed and stayed at zero importance. Read from the code: the core records the classmates as a generated school group with remembered names (`src/core2/opening-peer-network.ts:118`). It records no event between Margaret and any one of them, so a return adds nothing. Their closeness reached 0.502, above her husband's 0.462, because the core raises closeness only on recorded contact.

**Erica's year is two moments that take over the screen.** Measured: her husband's serious illness reached her at 0.643, and his death at 0.921. Both are above the recommended interrupt line of 0.63 (owner question 2). The funeral record links back to the illness news; that link is the callback. Inferred from the rule at `src/director/ledger.ts:430`: a moment's hindsight value is its impact times (1 + John's thread importance now). His importance is now 2.364, so the illness reads 0.643 × 3.364 = 2.164 and the death 0.921 × 3.364 = 3.099. The callback's strength, 1.572, is the illness moment's hindsight on February 1, when John's importance was 1.443: 0.643 × 2.443. Measured: Erica's household holds only John, and her record keeps one person she knew, John, so the funeral lists no mourners. Her record shows John known since the opening because the core does not say when partners met.

## What happens next

- **For the owner:** the seven questions in the design note set the floor, the interrupt line, the pace, fading, the years before the game, watching, and anniversaries. Each answer changes one number in `src/director/data/director.json`.
- **For SOL-1258, who maintains the core:** closeness never rises above 0.502 through contact (`src/core2/state.ts:579`).
- **For SOL-1258:** no contact is recorded between Margaret and her husband or two of her children all year. The "last contact" of January 1, 2021 on those threads is the date the core created the family relationship, not a contact.
- **For SOL-1258:** Margaret's household has no income on record after March.
- **Hooks requested from SOL-1258:** the core's feeling calculation for each person who hears an event, passed with the event; and a notice when a tie changes.
- **For SOL-1258, the recorded past:** shared history between people who knew each other, so that someone from the past can come back with something at stake.
- **Fixed in this pull request:** a partner was recorded as known since the older partner's birth. A partner is now known no later than the opening, because the records do not say when partners met.
- **Where to answer:** the seven questions open the design note's pull request, number 3926, and the note itself, `docs/design/director-on-core2.md`.
