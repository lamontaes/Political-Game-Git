## What costs the time in year 4 (CPU profile, 163 s sampled)

- Campaigns engine: 62 s (38%). Inside it, town halls (`holdEvent`) 24.8 s, the day's campaign choice 24.2 s, statements to an audience (`makeStatements`) 20.7 s.
- A speech's event fan-out is the largest single piece: `deliverSpeech` 14.9 s, of which `emit` 10.9 s (each listener observes the event, then the views engine moves their stance, the drives engine decides a response, the life engine appraises).
- Garbage collection: 15.5 s (9.5%). Module-table `get`/`put`/`insert` and their copies: about 23 s self time (14%).
- Volume, counted by instrumenting `holdEvent` and `makeStatements` (temporary counters, not committed): year 1 held 3 town halls (98 listeners); year 2 held 1,281 (64,700 listeners); year 3 held 6; year 4 held 1,134 (326 people checked per event, about 62 listeners per event, 78,700 listener reaches in all).

So the election year is cost per listener (about 240 microseconds each, most of it the shared event fan-out) times 79,000 listeners. The code on that path is already cached and micro-tuned by earlier speed work (parameters are read once, table facades are reused); another few percent each is left in single functions, but a cut of 21% needs a change in how much is done, not how fast.
