# A reporter running for council stops the game, and town votes repeat one split

A player can reach council and board votes, but the votes barely depend on the bill. Seats changed hands with no record. Races with no candidates left nobody in office. State and federal vacancies never became elections. One North Carolina town stopped on September 29, 2025, when a local reporter ran for council. These come from eleven towns run for a year: one in each of New Mexico, New York, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island and South Carolina, and two in North Carolina. Ten finished the year. The owner has nothing to decide; the CTO's five fixes come first below.

## The five things to fix first

1. Stop a candidate from being their own interviewer, and keep press staff off ballots and opening councils (defect 1).
2. Rebalance local votes so the bill's question decides them, after measuring how much each member's fixed lean outweighs it (defect 2).
3. Record every seat change, veto, failed vote and budget vote as an event that news and scenes can use (defects 3 and 7).
4. Give a race with no candidates a fallback, and hold special elections for state and federal seats outside the town (defects 4 and 5).
5. Organize the whole U.S. House, not one state's members of it, and give townships a town budget (defects 8 and 9).

## The defects, ranked by how much they break play

"Measured" means seen in the year's records. "Inferred" means read from the code at the file and line given.

1. **The game stops when a reporter runs for council.** North Carolina, Scotland Neck, seed d48dca5fc9c0, stopped on September 29, 2025 with "Invalid meeting endpoints." (measured). The paper's reporter `person:press:outlet:newspaper:0:jurisdiction_9a4f0415d6301cb0:reporter:0` filed for council seat 2. The paper's interview row named them as both candidate and reporter (measured). The press picks the reporter closest to the candidate and never leaves the candidate out (inferred, `src/core2/modules/press.ts:1312`). The game then makes that person meet themself, which it refuses (inferred, `src/core2/state.ts:1222`). Anyone in town may run, press staff included (inferred, `src/core2/modules/candidacy.ts:699`). The same gap shows in Pennsylvania, where a council bill had a reporter as its sponsor (measured), so reporters also sit on councils from the opening (inferred, `src/core2/modules/offices.ts:256`).
2. **Town votes repeat one split whatever the bill.** Ohio, Utica, seed 92e8bf7f65cc: every board vote was 1 yes, 2 no, for example `bill:office:gus2025:191153:board:2025-02-04:person_2653256c3e966db1:0` (measured). The same repeat shows in New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Oklahoma (6-1 and 1-6), Pennsylvania and South Carolina (measured). Each member carries a fixed lean drawn from their personality traits, the same on every bill (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:1091`). The member whose lean favors bringing bills sponsors nearly every bill at a meeting (inferred, `src/core2/modules/world-legislating.ts:480`). The sponsor gets a full extra point toward yes on their own bill (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:1268`). Each other member votes on their own fixed lean plus their view of the question (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:1296`). Where the leans outweigh the views, the same members vote no on every bill and the split repeats (inferred; the sizes were not measured). This is the golden path's "negotiate, then pass or fail" step.
3. **A seat changes hands with no record.** Ohio: `seat:gus2025:209071:council:3` was vacated on February 1, 2025, and no appointment record follows (measured). The same shows in 8 of 10 towns (measured). The seat is refilled the same day, and no event is written (inferred, `src/core2/modules/offices.ts:861`). So no news item or scene can tell the player who joined the council (inferred).
4. **A race with no candidates ends with nobody in office.** Ohio: `contest:seat:gus2025:209071:mayor:0:2025-11-04` has no winner and no totals (measured). South Carolina, Aynor, seed 1391d978f6a2: `contest:seat:gus2025:134414:assessor:0:2025-05-02:special` is the same (measured). With no winner the game schedules the seat's next regular election and does nothing else, so a special election's seat stays vacant until then (inferred, `src/core2/modules/voting.ts:2060`).
5. **State and federal vacancies outside the town never become elections.** Oklahoma, Langley, seed 8c47744a9b21: `seat:US-OK:lower:021:0`, `seat:US-OK:lower:032:0`, `seat:US-OK:lower:066:0`, `seat:US-OK:lower:063:0` and `seat:US-OK:us-house:03` were vacated for a special election (measured). None was held by year end (measured). New York, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island and South Carolina show the same (measured). A special race opens only for seats that cover the town (inferred, `src/core2/modules/elections.ts:399`). The year's records show no new holder for these seats (measured). The code would seat a generated official on the date the election was due (inferred, `src/core2/modules/offices.ts:1000`). That shortcut is registered stopgap SG-GOV-special-election-appointee (measured, `src/core2/data/stopgaps.json:1049`).
6. **A legislature's year happens on one day, and senates never start a bill.** Ohio: all 53 lower-chamber bills were introduced, heard and decided on June 30, 2025 (measured). In each state the session's bills are drafted and decided on its adjournment date, and in Congress on December 15, 2025 (inferred, `src/core2/modules/world-legislating.ts:613`). Every bill starts in the lower chamber (inferred, `src/core2/modules/world-legislating.ts:638`). So a state senator never sponsors a bill, and a bill has no days between introduction and vote for anyone to lobby (inferred).
7. **Vetoes, failed bills and budget votes leave no record.** Oklahoma: 11 of the 54 lower-chamber bills ended "vetoed", and the year has no veto record (measured). North Dakota has 2 vetoed federal bills and no veto record (measured). A veto only changes the bill's status (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:2133`). A failed vote does the same (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:2019`), and budget votes are stored but not announced (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:316`). So none of them can become news or a scene.
8. **The U.S. House also gets a council president.** All 10 towns that finished the year recorded a "council-president" for a House member on December 15, 2025, for example `official:term:seat:US-OH:us-house:08:2025-01-20` (measured). At the end of the federal session the game organizes only the House members from the town's own state, for example Ohio's delegation, not the whole House (inferred, `src/core2/modules/world-legislating.ts:648`). The leadership code then organizes that delegation like a town council (inferred, `src/core2/modules/leadership.ts:136`).
9. **A township never gets a town budget.** New York, Felts Mills, seed 1ccb545e9d35: no town budget was recorded all year (measured). A town government and its budget are built only for a city or village, not a township (inferred, `src/core2/modules/budgets.ts:412`).
10. **A federal balanced-budget law cut the veterans line by $110.8 billion and a retirement claim by 29 percent on December 15, 2025.** The law passed Congress in its one-day session on December 15, 2025, and the same cut shows in 7 of 10 worlds (measured). In Rhode Island, Charlestown, seed 3ac088ae75a4, it cut the veterans line by $110.8 billion: `budget-line-changed:budget:US:2025:veterans:law:US:federal:us-policy-positions:fiscal.balanced-operating-budget:general:2025-12-15:2025-002#balance#2025-12-15` (measured). In New Mexico, Red Rock Ranch, seed da4f30ef438d, the same cut lowered the retirement claim `retirement-claim:person_5f4f1daa964d2f48` by 29 percent, from $1,708.32 to $1,206.29 (measured). Only the 29 percent drop was measured; whether the amount is paid monthly or yearly was not printed. The line's size before the cut was not printed. The cut applies to the federal fiscal year starting October 1, 2025, which the budget id labels 2025 (inferred, `src/core2/modules/budgets.ts:331`). The law cuts every line except interest in proportion until spending fits revenue (inferred, `src/core2/modules/law-reach/group-b-money.ts:427`).
11. **A new president "succeeded as vice-president," and the vice president stayed.** Oklahoma: `term:seat:US:president:2025-12-18` seats a generated person who "succeeded as vice-president" (measured). `term:seat:US:vice-president:2025-01-20` is still serving (measured). The succession rule seats a new person instead of moving the vice president (inferred, `src/core2/modules/offices.ts:912`).
12. **The player held the public works director post with no record of a hire or a choice.** Oregon, Tillamook, seed 19a393194ce9: the player `person_07ace693acfa8811` held the post at year end, and the year's records show no hire or choice by the player (measured). The mayor fills town posts from every resident 18 or older, and the player is not left out (inferred, `src/core2/modules/executive.ts:229`).
13. **Oklahoma's Corporation Commission has 1 seat; the research data says 3.** The research row is titled "3 members, statewide, staggered 6-year terms" (measured, `data/research/offices/state-offices.json:9336`). The office is built with a fixed seat count of 1 (inferred, `src/core2/offices-state.ts:494`).
14. **One measure number names several bills.** Every body numbers its measures from 2025-001 (inferred, `src/core2/modules/legislature.ts:750`). In Ohio, number 2025-001 named a board bill and a council bill on February 4, 2025 (measured).

## Not established

- Why New Mexico and Oklahoma held no local election in 2025. Oregon, North Dakota and Rhode Island hold local elections in even years, so their quiet year may be right. Each place's calendar was not checked.
- Why 7 of 10 towns recorded no town budget. The cause was found only for New York.
- Year speed. Years took 74 to 195 seconds per town. Ohio took 132 seconds, North Carolina 126 and South Carolina 195, all over the two-minute limit. Three worlds ran at once on four cores, so these are not clean measurements under the speed rule.
- Why the New York player, `person_ef16c977a35b5a37` in Felts Mills, seed 1ccb545e9d35, ended the year with $0 in cash and no job (measured).

## What the viewer misreads

Some suspicious lines come from the viewer, not the game. Its "deaths" line counts only deaths kept in the log, and most deaths are not kept. It asks for tables that do not exist, which prints "Unregistered module table". It hides local contests and offices and cuts news at 200 items. Items R1, R16 and R24 in `verification-TRACE-4.md` list each one.

## What happens next

Nothing is fixed here; the audit only reads. All 14 defects are open. Defects 2 through 9, 11, 13 and 14 sit in the government engines (OPUS-GOV). Defect 1 sits in the press and candidacy code. Defect 12 sits in the town executive. Defect 10 sits in the law effects on budgets. The CTO routes each one.

## Method

The viewer ran each town for one year from January 20, 2025, three worlds at a time. The stopped North Carolina world was rerun on its seed to read the interview rows. A second North Carolina world (Warsaw, seed d48ede07ab82) ran the full year. One Haiku agent per town wrote the town's report. One Sonnet agent checked every item against the code, and this session checked the main causes again. The raw viewer files are in `audit/`.
