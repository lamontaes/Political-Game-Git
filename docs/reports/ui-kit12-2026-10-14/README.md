# The whole play page now wears Kit 12

Every screen of the game now uses the approved Kit 12 type, glass, frame and controls, and the old theme is deleted, not covered. The title, the play screen, every menu, the conversation box, the dossier, the filing office and election night all share the dark glass, the brass frame, Cinzel names and plain small-capital actions. The scales sit in the conversation box's upper right; switched on, they tip and glow red and the lying reply turns soft red. Nothing needs a decision. Four screens (news, budgets, what changed, and money) are styled but do not yet match their mocks, because the data they receive is raw or incomplete; the last section names who supplies what.

## Before and after

Before: two strips of boxed tab buttons, gray button faces with a gold corner chevron, corner ornaments on every panel, and a menu that opened as a narrow box over the scene. Developer controls (seed, world settings, "Play" beside every officeholder, mood and stress numbers) were in the open.

After: the player card in the bottom left holds the portrait, the name, and the date with the time under it, the only place either appears. Six brass rings sit above it. A menu opens as the Kit 12 menu list on the left with the screen on the right, and related screens are tabs across the top. Conversations show the speaker's portrait top left and numbered replies. Developer controls sit behind one faint ring in the top right corner.

## What was removed

The stylesheet was written again from the kit. No old color, token, component or control style remains (measured with `git diff --numstat` against main and a search for the old palette, which finds nothing).

- The old stylesheet had 640 lines and 116 rules. Git counts 384 of the 640 lines as deleted; the other 256 are lines the new file happens to repeat, mostly closing braces and the scene and figure placement that the kit does not restyle. 66 of the 116 old selectors no longer exist. The 50 selector names still in use (the scene, figures, calendar cells, map) now carry kit values.
- Gone: the gray button face, button outline and button shadow tokens; the brass corner ornaments; the gold corner chevron on pressed buttons; the boxed tab strips at the top left and the bottom right; the "×" close button; the boxed reply buttons; the image-based scales.
- The page markup lost 67 of its 173 lines and the client code 198 of its 1,679. The two tab strips are replaced by one menu list and one ring row. The five sub-screen buttons inside Politics are now tabs. The "Election night" button that sat disabled is now a tab that appears only once there are results.
- Player-facing text removed: record ids shown as citations on hover, the "(est.)" tag on laws, and bare record ids in the news.
- Moved behind the developer ring rather than deleted: seed, opening date and world settings on the title; the golden-path step; mood and stress numbers; "Play" beside officeholders; how a tie last moved in numbers. The version number stays small under the title menu and in Options.

## Still not matched to a mock, and why

What each screen shows was measured in the shots and the page code. Who should supply the missing data is inferred from the team table in `AGENTS.md`, and is shown in parentheses for OPUS-PLAY to carry into its "Needs from other sessions" list.

- **News** (`src/bench/play.mjs:1377`) lists each record's date, kind and raw fields, such as `post: city-clerk` (measured). The newspaper mock needs a headline and a story, which would come from the English engine (OPUS-PEOPLE, inferred).
- **What changed** (`src/bench/play.mjs:1454`) shows place codes such as `county:39007` where a place name belongs (measured). The server's changes data carries the code only (measured); a name would come from the government engines (OPUS-GOV, inferred).
- **Budgets** (`src/bench/play.mjs:1248`) shows line keys such as `individualIncomeTax` (measured). Readable line names would come with the budget engine (OPUS-GOV, inferred).
- **Money and property** (`src/bench/play.mjs:1065`) shows cash, housing and withholding only (measured). The mock's came-in, went-out and three-month table needs the player's ledger rows, which the server's money data does not include (measured). Adding them changes the server's data shape, which this order excluded (OPUS-PLAY with OPUS-CORE, inferred).
- **Title** (`src/bench/play.html`): the mock shows Continue, Saved games, Patch notes and a hero figure. The game has no saved games or patch notes yet (measured), so those items are left out rather than shown empty.
- **The radial row**: the mock shows six rings without naming them. The page uses six rings for journal, calendar, money, politics, people and travel; the other screens are on the menu list (measured).
- **Glossary underlines** (`src/bench/play.mjs:223`) appear only where a whole label is a Guide term, never inside prose. The Guide holds 38 legislative terms, and in this run only the calendar's "Filing deadline" matched a label (measured).

## Method

- Build: branch `ui/kit12-whole-game` against main at fd3574c9, on October 14, 2026.
- World: seed `kit12-proof`; the game picked Geneva, Ohio at random; the opening date was January 20, 2025.
- Shots: 1920 × 1080, Chromium through Playwright, driving the page the way a player would. Election night was reached by pressing Until needed and answering each stop until the November 4, 2025 results opened.
- Checks run: eslint and prettier on the changed files, the typecheck, `release:check --mode pr` (OK). `zero-dice` reports 4 new list-picks in core files this change does not touch; it reports the same 4 on clean main.
- No building sub-agents ran. One read-only report reviewer (civic-report-reviewer) read this report until it passed.
