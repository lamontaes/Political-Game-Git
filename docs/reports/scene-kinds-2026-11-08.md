## How this was made

The table is generated from the director's list of scene kinds, the purpose arcs and the scene word banks. Wordings counts the bank pieces that can say the kind: the talk pieces of its purpose, which every kind with that purpose shares, plus its own opening narration. Dead in the last column marks a kind with no cause that can start it today.
