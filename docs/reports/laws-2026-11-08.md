# Every law in the game, Nov 8, 2026

Tonight Congress gained a route on every federal question, other states' laws began to move people, and players in consolidated city-counties gained a local government. This is the list of every law: who can pass and repeal it, and whether its effects reach people.

## Three plain lines on tonight

- Congress can pass and repeal a law on every federal question in the powers catalog. Each starts as cited law. They are tariffs, a voucher for every eligible family, a power plant carbon limit, Medicare drug price negotiation, the debt limit, immigration levels, student loan forgiveness, the disaster cost share, national data privacy, D.C. statehood, mandatory minimums and a members' stock ban.
- Congress can also raise or cut 11 federal budget lines. The campaign contribution limit is now a law federally and in 50 states.
- What other states do now reaches people. Movers read every state's income tax from the law in force, and a state legislature leans toward what its bordering states already have.
- Players in Honolulu, Nashville, Louisville and the other consolidated city-counties now have a local government to run for. A school class too small to measure no longer reads zero. A town's unemployment rate counts its own residents.

## The count

165 laws are listed. 89 are wired end to end. They exist in every place, can be passed by bill, reach named people and money, and can be repealed. 76 are passable by bill but not yet checked in a played world. Of the 246 researched effects, 177 reach people. The research puts 21 at about zero. About 46 are read by no engine.

A level in parentheses is who can pass and repeal the law. The higher level governs where real law says so.

## Added tonight, with the effect that reaches people

- Medicare drug price negotiation (Congress): a Medicare member's bill falls 8%, by the medical bills record. Baker, Louisiana.
- Tariffs raise prices 0.8%. A voucher for every eligible family cuts homelessness 30%. A power plant carbon limit cuts emissions 2.6%. The outcome web reads each. La Boca, New Mexico.
- A bigger federal deficit raises a state's bond payments, 25 basis points per point of output. Fair Oaks, Oklahoma.
- National data privacy (Congress): each employing business pays 1.0% of its other costs a month, once. Stuart, Oklahoma (PR open).
- Campaign contribution limits, federal ($3,500 an election) and each state's by office: campaigns follow them. Ladera Heights, California; Gantt, South Carolina (PRs open).
- Debt limit, immigration levels, student loan forgiveness, disaster cost share, D.C. statehood, mandatory minimums and the members' stock ban can be passed and repealed. No engine reads them yet (stopgap SG-LAWS-federal-position-no-reader).

## Not wired yet

- 46 researched effects have no engine to read them. Most wait on a record that does not exist: litter, riders, asthma, weather, loan balances, an immigration flow, sentencing or members' holdings.
- Only the player's state and town sit in a played world. Other states' laws on amounts and other towns' councils do not change by themselves yet.
- A city sales or income tax passed in play is printed in the paper, but no price or payment engine reads the city rate yet.
- Sentencing, water, flood control, bonds, and marriage and custody have no policy a bill can enact. Each needs researched law for all 56 places.
