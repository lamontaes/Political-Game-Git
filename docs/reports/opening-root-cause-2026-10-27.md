# The opening is a second, hand-built scene system that ignores records the world already keeps

The simulation works at the opening: the President, the officeholders, the household, the economy and every state's laws exist on day one. The screen shows a fixed slideshow instead. The opening has its own stage list, picture table, cast rule and renderer, beside the director's scene path. Four kinds of records exist and go unread: the town's businesses, the people who work there, each resident's stored look, and the laws in force with who holds each power. Three must be built: a per-world economy, looks and home-state pasts for officials, and the inauguration's venue. The fix is to delete the opening's path and play the address as a director scene, about 31 hours of new work across five leads.

## What the player sees, and why

Measured in a played world: Iowa City, Iowa, seed `28c0b8150290`, drawn at random from all 56 places, with every module installed (`buildBenchWorld`, two levels). The opening ran 11 pictures over about 67 seconds. Each item below says whether it was measured in this world or inferred.

1. **The same numbers in every world.** Measured: the President says "The national unemployment rate is 4.1 percent." The figure is a real record (`macro-month:2024-12`). It is the published December 2024 row with no per-world spread, read at `src/core2/macro-model.ts:106-136` and `src/director/opening-story.ts:245`. Every game starts on January 20, 2025, so every world says 4.1, 2.9, 2.6, 4.1 and 6.6 percent. Measured: the pull request that added the other numbers (#6103, October 25, 2026) states in its own body that the figures are the same in every world.
2. **A stat readout, not a speech.** Measured: this address gave five number sentences in a row, one per figure. The sections are fixed at `src/english/english-opening-speech.ts:92-125`, and `src/director/opening-story.ts:369-393` sets the count to at least three numbers besides unemployment, because the done-when of `words-opening-address-numbers-order-implementation` asked for three. Measured: none of the five sentences ties its figure to an ask or to the listener; the sections have no slot for one.
3. **"I was in Pennsylvania."** The mined fragment "I was in {place}." is said alone, with no reason after it (`data/english/parts/opening-speech.json:2704`). The owner heard it in his Albion, Pennsylvania playtest on October 26, 2026. Its place comes from a real campaign-visit record. Inferred from the code: every President's visits are the same 2024 battleground states plus their home state (`src/core2/officials-president.ts:148-153`). Measured: this President visited nine battleground states and Wyoming, not Iowa, so this world said no place line.
4. **"Congress must remove the duty to retreat before using force."** Inferred from the code: the President's asks come from their views alone (`src/director/opening-story.ts:323-338`). Measured: stand-your-ground is a state power only, and Iowa already has it by statute (Iowa Code § 704.1(3)). The second ask, "States should restrict abortion.", is also a state power and already Iowa law (Iowa Code § 146C.2). Both facts sit in the research data the game ships (`data/research/powers-catalog/question-powers.json`), and the asks never read it. Measured: the address names no law in force, because the only reader of laws in force returns 10 money series (`src/core2/modules/laws.ts:584-598`). Measured: the same powers file lists 121 policy questions, 58 of them state powers only.
5. **Empty stock rooms.** Measured: each number cuts to a picture chosen by topic from a fixed table (`src/bench/data/opening-pictures.json:24-71`): unemployment shows an unemployment office, wages a factory floor, prices a grocery store. Measured: all four cutaways stand empty, because the topic rules carry no cast. The town's own businesses and the people who work there are records, and the opening never asks for them.
6. **People change faces between pictures.** Measured: Representative David Saunders (`official:term:seat:US-IA:us-house:01:2025-01-20`) wears figure 38 on the House floor and figure 02 in the next picture. Inferred from the code: a look is a hash of the person's id among the looks still free in that picture, rerolled per picture (`src/bench/whole-figures.ts:263-269`). The player has a stored appearance (`app_94e9f3308c6f8e1f`) that the figure chooser never reads. Measured: officials have no stored appearance at all, only a gender (`src/core2/state.ts:2664`).
7. **Night, then noon, in the same living room.** Measured: the household watches at 8:30 p.m. The family stage then shows the same house at midday, because every stage picture uses the table's fixed hour (`src/bench/data/opening-pictures.json:84`, `src/bench/opening.ts:66`).
8. **The inaugural address at the White House.** Measured: the stage table gives the President's stage the White House for an inaugural address (`src/bench/data/opening-pictures.json:3-6`). The inauguration record exists (`inauguration:2025-01-20`, `src/core2/modules/offices.ts:627`), but, inferred from the code, its place is the player's town, so nothing says where the oath was taken.
9. **"John Holt won." on the night of the inaugural address.** Measured: the news has no inauguration story, so the address is worded as an election story (`src/director/opening-story.ts:779`).
10. **The President went to school in Iowa but comes from Wyoming.** Measured: the President's school records sit in Iowa (`official:term:seat:US:president:2025-01-20:p8-deep-past-v1:school:elementary`), while the home-state record says Wyoming. Inferred from the code: national officials are filled in with the player's state as their place (`src/core2/modules/officials.ts:711`), and the deep past builds schools there.

**Why it ended up hard-coded.** The opening was built in one night (October 14, 2026) to an owner ruling on its sequence, before the director could cast a scene from records and before the town's businesses had pictures. Its pull request said what it lacked: no cabinet, no inauguration line, no relation words, and a stopgap list of goal words. It worked around each gap with a table in the bench, and later items asked for more lines in that path instead of moving it onto the director. Inferred from the pull request bodies and the git history. No ruling asks for a separate path: the owner called the address the model for every scene, where the director reads the records and plays the one that fits (October 14, 2026, night; `docs/play-first/LAW.md` section 3).

## How long it takes

Measured in Iowa City, seed `28c0b8150290`, one process, debug bookkeeping off.

- **Loading a new game:** 33.6 seconds, with each part rounded (people 17.3 seconds, the past 3.6 seconds, the economy 5.4 seconds, the core 1.1 seconds, government 5.6 seconds, the opening 0.7 seconds). Building the opening's pictures took 0.07 seconds.
- **The opening on screen:** about 67 seconds over 11 pictures, at the screen's own pace (1.8 seconds plus 45 milliseconds a character per line, 2.4 seconds per person pointed out, `src/bench/play.mjs:263-265`).
- **The first week:** day one 4.7 seconds, then 0.09, 0.05, 0.05, 0.03, 0.05 and 1.0 seconds.
- **A year:** 117.7 seconds (`npm run speed:year`), against the 120-second limit.

The three slowest steps, from a CPU profile of the same run:

1. **Making the town's people, 17 seconds.** Inside it, the families take 8.3 seconds and kin take 4.8 seconds (`buildOpeningKin`, `src/core2/opening-kin.ts`). Inferred from the build order: kin are built in the people stage, before the town is collapsed into counts in the economy stage (`src/bench/world.ts:233-330`), so kin are made for households that end up counted. Old-core upbringing adds 1.5 seconds (`src/simulation/people-upbringing.ts`). Garbage collection takes 7.5 seconds of the whole run.
2. **The first day, 4.7 seconds.** Every legislature's opening session votes on day one (1.4 seconds, `src/core2/modules/world-legislating.ts`). Town halls and debates are weighed for every counted resident (1.4 seconds), and so is candidacy (1.8 seconds).
3. **Opening the government and the economy, 11 seconds.** The opening economy takes 3.0 seconds. The press hires its staff by filling in town residents one at a time (`hireStaffMember`, `src/core2/press-staff.ts`, 1.3 seconds). Seating the officials takes 0.9 seconds.

Grade: works with the gap named. The year runs inside its 120-second limit with 2.3 seconds to spare; loading has no limit on record; each cause is named above.

## Work orders

Each order is a pool item with its lead set, a time box and a done-when a merger can check in a played world. Existing items get this report's evidence instead of a twin.

**Director engineer (`lead: director`)**

- `director-opening-as-a-scene` (new, 8 hours): the opening is one situation type cast by the director, its settings and casts read from records; the stage list, the picture table, `castOf` and `cutawayFor` are deleted.
- `director-president-asks-within-powers` (new, 3 hours): the President asks Congress only on federal powers not yet law, and asks the states only where the player's state's law differs.

**Lane 4 (`lead: play-first`)**

- `play-opening-plays-in-the-talk-box` (new, 4 hours): the address plays through the shared talk box and scene parts, with "Skip scene"; the opening box, name tag and their timers are deleted.
- `play-stop-35-people-drawn-from-their-own-look` (existing): add Representative Saunders changing from figure 38 to figure 02 between two pictures, and the unread `appearanceSeed`.
- `play-opening-address-empty-waiting-room` (existing): add the four empty cutaways and their cause.

**Words builder (`lead: words`)**

- `words-address-flows-from-records` (new, 4 hours): the address is passages, each figure joined to the ask it bears on, a visit line with its reason and an inauguration news story; the per-number moves and the bare place fragments are deleted.

**Missing records**

- `core-macro-opening-month-per-world` (new, `lead: m0-m2`, 4 hours): the opening month is a per-world estimate around the published row with its sourced spread.
- `core-officials-appearance-and-home-past` (new, `lead: m0-m2`, 4 hours): officials are filled in with an appearance and a past in their home state, and the inauguration record gets its venue.
- `laws-in-force-every-question` (new, `lead: laws-effects`, 4 hours): one reader returns every question's law in force at each level for a place, read by the address and the Issues screen.

## Method

Read-only on game code: no file under `src/` changed. One world was built through the bench's own staged loader on a random seed, and the opening's pictures were read as the server serves them. The first week was timed a day at a time and profiled with Node's CPU profiler. Introducing commits were found with `git log -S` and matched to pull requests. Read: `OWNER-DECISIONS.md` (the October 26 and October 27, 2026 sections), `docs/play-first/LAW.md` section 3, the October 26, 2026 assessment and the pool items named above. Searched: `DECISION-REGISTER.md` and the story director's design for the opening.
