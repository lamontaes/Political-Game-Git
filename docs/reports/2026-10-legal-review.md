## Data permissions depend on the actual work and publisher

**Measured inventory:** The tracked source and research directories contain 884 files. The coverage tables below include every top-level directory and standalone research item, including sources outside the central manifest. The citation register records individual URLs found in metadata and corpora. A URL, an acquisition hash, and an owner approval are not a license.

[17 U.S.C. § 105](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/105) excludes qualifying U.S. government works from domestic copyright protection, subject to statutory exceptions. It does not make every government-hosted work public domain. Contractor works, third-party photos, and works transferred to the government can retain copyright. It does not automatically apply to state governments, foreign distribution, trademarks, or access contracts.

[17 U.S.C. § 102(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/102) distinguishes unprotected ideas and systems from protected expression. Factual values can be used differently from reproducing tables, questionnaires, explanatory prose, or original compilations. Attribution does not replace permission. A transformed simulation parameter is not automatically exempt from an acquisition contract.

The [government-edicts decision](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/19pdf/18-1150_7m58.pdf) supports reuse of lawmaking and judicial work within its doctrine. Do not extend it to every state website, agency handbook, vendor annotation, or municipal meeting packet. Use official statutory facts and independently written explanations where possible. Preserve section numbers, jurisdiction, effective date, and source citations for accuracy.

### Commercial sources, polls, and papers remain partly unresolved

**Verified restriction:** [Book of the States terms](https://bookofthestates.org/terms-of-use/) limit licensed data use to noncommercial purposes and restrict substantial copying. They require conspicuous Council of State Governments source acknowledgment. The stored legislative, governor, and judge salary tables therefore lack a demonstrated paid-game license. Obtain written commercial rights or independently source the underlying salary facts from official records. Counsel should assess existing capture and redistribution rights as well as compiled outputs.

**Measured:** The movement research cites Gallup’s Biden approval series and a Berkeley IGS Newsom poll. [Gallup’s permissions page](https://www.gallup.com/corporate/198173/gallup-permissions.aspx) requires permission for covered republication. Do not ship its charts, branded tables, question text, or report prose based on a citation alone. Counsel should distinguish using facts for internal calibration from redistributing protected material and review applicable access terms.

The [Berkeley IGS item](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/26b6t6k0) was blocked by a browser challenge. Its item-specific license was not verified. Public access through eScholarship does not establish CC licensing. Other poll references, including Pew or YouGov where present, remain item-specific and unresolved unless an express license or written permission is saved.

**Measured:** Research citations include NBER, AEA, Oxford, SAGE, Chicago, INFORMS, Springer, and PubMed Central papers. The item register below includes the detected paper links. Their individual text, figure, questionnaire, and replication-data licenses were not established. A paper’s presence on a federal host does not make its authors’ work a federal government work.

Use independently written factual research summaries with authors, title, publication, year, DOI or URL, and a precise locator. Obtain rights before distributing paper text, tables, illustrations, or supplements. Check replication-data and code licenses separately, including noncommercial clauses. Citation-only research and shipped content require different clearance decisions; the build inventory must establish which applies.

NGA is an association, not a federal agency. Its HTML captures include copyrighted presentation, biographies, and portrait links alongside factual office information. NCSL, Ballotpedia, LegiScan, CSG, Tax Foundation, KFF, NAR, Zillow, MIT Living Wage, news outlets, and other cited organizations likewise need their own terms review. State website access does not confer a blanket redistribution license.

Wikipedia text and Wikimedia images have different licenses. If Wikipedia prose is reproduced or adapted, verify the applicable text license and contributor attribution. If it is merely a locator for official facts, cite the primary source. Municipal code vendors’ interface, editorial materials, and access terms remain separate from the laws they display.

The owner-provided judicial-selection source expressly states task authorization without broader redistribution terms in [its lock](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/data/source/judicial-office-selection/artifact-lock.json). Internal Drive research also lacks public distribution evidence. Obtain the author’s rights grant and third-party source lineage before shipping their expression. Authored estimates require contributor ownership records and source citations where derived.

## Generated artwork does not clear its reference inputs

**Measured:** The [art provenance registry](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/art/manifest/provenance.json) has 134 entries. Eighty-eight record unknown rights; 46 record owned rights. Several generator names and executed settings are unrecorded. An approval field establishes creative approval, not legal ownership. One claimed Firefly return records an embedded Google AI credit, so filenames cannot establish the executed model.

### Firefly terms and evidence to retain

[Adobe’s Firefly FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/get-started/learn-the-basics/adobe-firefly-faq.html) permits commercial use of non-beta outputs. Beta output is generally permitted unless the product states otherwise. Record the actual product, model, generation date, account, plan, applicable terms, prompts, reference inputs, export, and human edits. The asset registry also lists Gemini, Scenario, and OpenAI; those assets need their own applicable terms.

The [Generative AI Product Specific Terms](https://www.adobe.com/go/adobe-gen-ai-addl-terms), effective April 23, 2026, place input and output responsibility on the user. They prohibit removing or altering generated watermarks or Content Credentials. They do not warrant noninfringement, uniqueness, or protectability. Non-Adobe models invoke separate third-party rules.

The same terms restrict indemnity to specified eligible teams or enterprise plans and qualifying features, surfaces, and export events. Exclusions include modifications, combinations, certain reference inputs, and context. Do not assume that cropped, composited game assets carry indemnity. Counsel must review the actual agreement and workflow; the public terms alone do not establish coverage.

The [AI user guidelines](https://www.adobe.com/legal/licenses-terms/adobe-gen-ai-user-guidelines.html) prohibit infringing inputs and deceptive impersonation. Preserve original metadata and source-to-derivative lineage. Audit conversion and packaging for removed credentials. No general obligation to print an Adobe credit was identified in these materials; contractual metadata duties and source-photo credits still apply.

The [Copyright Office’s AI report](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf) distinguishes human authorship from AI-generated expression. Commercial permission does not establish exclusive copyright in every output. Document human contributions and contributor assignments where protection or ownership matters.

## Real institutions and places need accurate, limited depictions

**Measured:** The [institution types](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/src/simulation/local-institutions-data.ts#L1) expressly model sourced real names. Their data include NCES schools and colleges, CMS hospitals, and Census places. The CMS input names Mayo Clinic Hospital and Cleveland Clinic Hospital. The campus manifest names universities including Harvard, Cornell, and the University of Alaska Fairbanks.

Institution and place names are factual identifiers; they can also function as trademarks. Descriptive use in an expressive game can differ from using a mark as a badge, endorsement, or selling point. [15 U.S.C. § 1125](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1125) addresses confusion over affiliation or sponsorship and related claims. A federal directory does not grant the institution’s logo rights.

**Inferred risk:** Assigning fictional malpractice, corruption, discriminatory conduct, or crime to an identifiable real school, hospital, employer, or college can create defamation or disparagement concerns. It can also misleadingly describe real services. Label the simulation clearly and have counsel review harmful institution-specific events and marketing examples.

**Measured:** The [school-row comment](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/src/simulation/local-institutions-data.ts#L8) identifies current names carried backward before the directory vintage as estimates. **Inferred:** Historical accuracy needs source verification wherever those names are presented as real past attendance.

**Measured:** The [campus manifest](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/art/campuses/manifest.json#L16) records named flagship artwork as standing in for other institution kinds. **Inferred:** A recognizable Harvard or Berkeley landmark used for an unrelated private college can create affiliation confusion. Prefer accurate institution binding or generic, independently created scenery. Obtain permission for prominent branding where counsel determines it is needed.

**Measured:** The [civic-symbol manifest](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/art/manifest/civic_symbols.json) says symbol artwork has not been acquired. Some backdrop [surface metadata](https://github.com/lamontaes/Political-Game-Git/blob/3ea466ae8e0c69d2b41b21bb1f352a4fe1e63185/art/backdrops/surfaces.json#L484) excludes seals from specified surfaces. The thumbnails nevertheless show flags, round wall emblems, and recognizable government interiors. Metadata exclusion does not prove that generated pixels contain no protected seal.

[18 U.S.C. § 713](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/713) regulates uses of specified federal seals and their likenesses, including endorsement implications. State seals have separate statutes. Flags, institutional crests, mascots, uniforms, vehicle markings, campaign logos, commercial signage, and store capsules need a rendered-image check. A photo’s copyright license does not settle those uses.

No obvious commercial wordmark or current-officeholder portrait was identified in the contact sheets. This limited finding is not a logo clearance. Complete an asset-resolution and in-game review, especially oval-office, governor-office, university, rally, television, hospital, and storefront scenes. Counsel should also check the game title and marketing marks before commercial use; no trademark registry search was completed here.
