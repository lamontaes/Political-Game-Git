title: A day of calls puts your own money into your campaign too
---

When you spend a campaign day raising money, you back your own campaign first by the same reasons a rival candidate does, so a candidate with savings can pay for an event venue. The campaign day test now holds the interview and the event for a candidate who holds no seat, in a drawn town.
