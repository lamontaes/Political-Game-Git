title: A candidate can hold an event and give an interview from the campaign day
---

Your campaign day now offers an event and an interview beside knocking on doors and calling donors. The event is held in a hall and the people who come become part of the race; the interview asks a local outlet for a sit-down, and the outlet's answer decides whether it runs.
