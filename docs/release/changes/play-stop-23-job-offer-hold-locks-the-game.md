title: A job offer no longer locks the game
---

When an employer's offer holds your clock, the play screen now shows the offer itself — the employer, the job, the pay and the day to answer by — with Take it and Turn it down. Either answer releases the hold and Day and Week work again; an offer that just arrived can also wait at the Jobs tab until its deadline.
