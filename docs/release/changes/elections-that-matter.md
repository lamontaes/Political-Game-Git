title: Campaigns raise and spend money, and voters have reasons to choose
---

Every candidate's campaign now raises money from people who would give and spends it on ads, mail, events and polls that reach voters. Candidates answer from their own views and their party's. Every voter decides on one set of reasons, how likely each is to vote slides with what's at stake and their own habit, and filing deadlines, election calendars and tie rules follow each state's law.

In a primary, voters who know nothing that separates two candidates of the same party still choose one, so the primary counts the party's voters across the whole race.
