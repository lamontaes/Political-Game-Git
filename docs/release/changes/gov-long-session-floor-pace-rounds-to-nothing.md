title: A legislature that sits all year takes bills to the floor from its first floor week
---

A state or territory legislature that sits all year now takes up its first bill in its first floor week and spreads the rest through the year, so a legislator has floor votes to work for and the paper has floor news; before, no bill reached the floor until late summer.
