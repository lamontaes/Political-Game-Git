title: Fewer, better-timed disasters: only larger storms strike a town, each on its own day.
---

Every storm and flood the world produced was dated the first of its month,
so several could strike one town on the same day. Each one now arrives on the
day its recorded source episode began.

Only the larger recorded episodes now become a disaster for a town. The storm
records count every wind report, and treating each one as a damaging event
gave one county about fifteen disasters a year. Which reports a town really
lives through is filed as a research question; until it is answered, only
episodes the game rates major or catastrophic are declared.
