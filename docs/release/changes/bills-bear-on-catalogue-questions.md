title: A bill now says which policy question it bears on
---

Before, no bill in play was about anything in the game's list of policy
questions. A bill could record the question it concerned, and nothing that
files one ever said.

Now the Transit Access Pilot a Kentucky staffer opens on, the bill the Kentucky
legislature files on its own, and a player's own draft of fare relief for
assistance enrollees all bear on "Should local transit be free to ride?", and
a drafted bridge repair programme bears on funding upkeep before new
construction. The bill's page and the docket show the question under the
sponsor.

Most bills still bear on nothing the list asks — signage, ferry notice,
dredging schedules — and are left unlinked rather than pinned to the
nearest-sounding question. A save made before the questions shipped keeps
filing bills; they are simply not linked.
