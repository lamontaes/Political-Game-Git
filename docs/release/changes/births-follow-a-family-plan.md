section: Changed
title: A child is born when a couple decides on one
---

Children in town are now born when two people who share a home decide on it.
On each quarterly town review, a couple weighs having a child against their
own lives: their ages, the children they already have and how young the last
one is, how long they have been together, what they earn and pay in rent, the
room in their home, their health, their temperament and beliefs, the law where
they live, and how many people in town are out of work. If one of them raises
it and the other agrees, the baby arrives on the plan's own date. The draw that
used to give town women a baby by chance is gone, so the player and everybody
else follow one rule. A birth from a plan now also carries that plan, so the
plan shows the child it led to.
