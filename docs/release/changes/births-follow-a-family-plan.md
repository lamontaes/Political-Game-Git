---
id: births-follow-a-family-plan
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A child is born only when a couple decided on one
---

A child in town is now born only when two people who share a home have a
recorded family plan: one of them raised it, the other agreed, and the baby
arrives on the plan's own date. The quarterly draw that used to give town
women a baby by chance is gone, so the player and everybody else follow one
rule. Nothing yet lets a town couple raise a plan by themselves, so until that
arrives no town couple has a child. A birth recorded from a plan now also
carries that plan, so the plan shows the child it led to.
