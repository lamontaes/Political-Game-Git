title: Your county's clinics, roads and fair run on what its board voted
---

Before, a county board voted a budget but nothing it voted for reached a person. Now the health, highways and parks lines of a voted county budget become the county's clinics, road repair and fair. The county manager commits the money to a department already working in the county, it is paid in installments, and residents who are unwell ask for the clinic and others spend a day at the fair. Each visit is recorded on the person. A county with no such department commits nothing, and no provider is made up.
