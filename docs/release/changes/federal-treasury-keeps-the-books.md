title: The federal government now keeps its own books, and a top-rate tax law reaches them
---

The United States government now keeps a monthly account beside the state
and local budgets. It opens from the government's real 2025 receipts and
outlays and its real debt at the end of 2025, borrows each month's
shortfall, and pays interest on what it owes. A law raising the top income
tax rate now brings in about 1.5% more income tax from the next tax year,
and the government borrows that much less.
