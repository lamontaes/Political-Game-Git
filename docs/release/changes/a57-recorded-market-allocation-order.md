impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Housing prices follow recorded markets and affordable allocations fill in order
---

Market rents and home prices now use the existing recorded market model without
fixed citywide law multipliers. Enacted renewal caps and recorded housing permit
consequences retain their existing writers and source chains.

New affordable leases follow the same dwelling date and ID order as their
set-aside targets, so enough eligible private households can fill the recorded
share. Unknown income and public housing do not become private eligible capacity.
