id: veto-override-read-from-each-constitution
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Every state's veto override bar is its own constitution's
---

Before, 38 legislatures the game builds for itself (37 states and Puerto
Rico) drew their veto override bar from a list of fractions. In 21 of them
the draw was wrong. California needed only half of each chamber, though its
constitution requires two-thirds of the membership. Hawaii needed three-fifths
where its constitution says two-thirds, and Indiana two-thirds where its
constitution says a majority of the members elected.

Now each of those legislatures overrides a veto by the fraction its own
constitution sets, counted the way the constitution counts it: members
elected, members present, or members present and voting. Each bar cites its
section and quotes it. Virginia, whose reading was already on file, now
applies its two-thirds of members present instead of only its second
condition, a majority of the members elected.

Georgia, Mississippi and New Hampshire were read from a search engine's
excerpt of the official text, because their official pages refused the
connection.

The days a governor has to act on a bill, during the session and after the
legislature adjourns, were drawn the same way. Each is now the state's own
figure from The Council of State Governments' Book of the States 2023. Where
that table gives no count of days (Maine, New Jersey and South Carolina after
adjournment), the game uses the most common figure it gives for other places
and marks it estimated.
