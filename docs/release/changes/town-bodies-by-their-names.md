title: A town's council is named for what it is, not "governing body"
---

Every town's council seat used to read "Member of the governing body", on a
body called "City of Seattle governing body". The office list, the campaign, the Who
governs page and the day's summary now name the body. Where the game has read
the town's own government, that name is used, as in the Anchorage Assembly or
the Bowling Green Board of Commissioners. Everywhere else the body is named by
the kind of town: Seattle's reads as the Seattle City Council, whose members
are Council members; a village's is its Village Board, whose members are
Trustees; a town has a Town Council and a borough a Borough Council.

Those kind-of-town names are the game's placeholder until each town's own body
has been researched, and some towns really use another name, such as a
commission or a select board. Saved campaigns and seats carry on unchanged.
