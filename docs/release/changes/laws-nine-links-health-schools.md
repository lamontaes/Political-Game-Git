title: Health, assistance and school laws are paid and read by the treasury, the pay floor and the outcome web
---

Medicaid, SNAP, welfare assistance, preschool and private-school vouchers are now paid at the treasury's monthly close, which reads the law in force and each household's records when it pays; a raised SNAP maximum raises every household size's allotment in proportion. The teacher minimum salary is held by the public pay floor: it raises every public school teacher paid under it the day it takes effect and each month after, and a new teacher is hired at it. The health and hospitals, higher education, schools and SNAP changes now reach infant deaths, college degrees, graduation and food insecurity through sourced outcome links, and the cash assistance the welfare line pays counts as income when the town's poverty is measured.
