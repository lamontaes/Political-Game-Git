title: Someone stands at every filing counter
---

Where a town's races file with a county board of elections, or with a clerk's office its records name no one for, the counter now has a person at it: an election commissioner or clerk drawn for the world, on the county's or the town's payroll. They introduce themselves, take your papers and tell you about the petition in the scene that follows, instead of leaving you at an empty counter.
