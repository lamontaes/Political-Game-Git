title: Press secretaries talk the way the briefing room does
---

A bank of 62 short turns from White House press briefings, such as "Go
ahead.", "I'm not going to get into hypotheticals." and a reporter's "Two
questions.", is now in the game's data for the dialogue engine to run a press
briefing with.
