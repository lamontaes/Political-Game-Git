title: People can now fall ill, recover, and die in the course of ordinary life.
---

Everyone in the world now ages against published United States mortality
rates, including officeholders and your own character. Nobody is protected by
office or fame, and a death happens on its own day however you choose to pass
time. When the cause is not known, the game says so instead of inventing one.

People can also go through serious health episodes: they may be limited or
unable to work for a while, most recover, and what they share stays private
until they or the people around them disclose it. When a public official dies
in office, it becomes public news, and when an official can no longer do the
job and the people responsible know it, their office is told.
