title: An interview you accept is one you sit for
---

When you say yes to a newspaper or station that asks for an interview, you now sit down with the reporter, who asks each question in turn. You pick how to answer each one (plainly, with your record behind it, by turning it on a rival, or briefly), or say nothing, and the story prints what you chose.
