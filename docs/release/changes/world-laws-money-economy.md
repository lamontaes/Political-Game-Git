id: world-laws-money-economy
section: Fixed
title: New jobs pay at least the minimum wage, the federal budget moves the economy, and unpaid bills are owed
impact: patch
---

A new job now pays its role's rate for the region and never less than the minimum wage in force, instead of copying whoever holds the role now. A change in the federal budget pushes the national economy. A household's mortgage balance falls only by what it paid, a buyer's loan is limited by the down payment actually made, and unpaid property tax or sales tax a business couldn't pass on is owed and paid first from the next bill.
