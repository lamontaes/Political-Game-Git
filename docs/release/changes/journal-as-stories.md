---
id: journal-as-stories
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Your journal tells a job found, lost or left as a small story, not a one-line statement.
---

Where the journal used to say "After four months out of work, I found work in Crewe," it now says when you went back to work, who had paid you last, and what the new job paid against the old. A job lost says how long you had been there and what it paid; a job left says what the next one paid.
