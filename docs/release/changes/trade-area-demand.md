title: Small-town firms keep their staff because their trade area is counted
---

A small town's shops, restaurants and offices sell to people beyond the town, as they do in real life. Their sales now meet their books at the opening, so they no longer lay workers off toward half their receipts and the town's out-of-work count no longer climbs month after month.
