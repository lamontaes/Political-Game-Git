title: A calendar hold that ran out still shows up in your life record
---

A hold you were never shown and a hold you turned down are two different
things, and the game now records them as two different things — which is
right, and is what a refusal you never made was quietly costing you before.

Drawing that line moved every lapsed hold out of the one kind of record the
life record knew how to read, so time running out on a meeting left nothing
behind at all. Your own account of your life simply skipped it: the hold was
on your calendar, then it was not, and the record between those two days said
nothing.

Both now appear. A hold that ran out reads as one that ran out, in its own
words, and nothing in it claims you decided anything.
