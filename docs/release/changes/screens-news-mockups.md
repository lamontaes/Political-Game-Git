title: News is printed under an engraved masthead, with a photo from the council room or statehouse
---

The News front page and the evening news set the paper's name in an engraved masthead: a city daily under the eagle, a small-town weekly beside its courthouse. When a city, county or state government's story leads the paper, a photo of that room runs beside it: the council at a vote or lawmakers applauding a signing.
