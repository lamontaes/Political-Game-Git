section: Changed
title: People look their age
---

Faces now age with the people who wear them. Someone in their twenties or
thirties looks young. From their mid-forties they show lines at the eyes and
mouth. From sixty-five they have deeper wrinkles and gray brows. Each person
keeps the same face for life, so a neighbor you knew young is recognizably
the same person years later. In the creator, the Face arrows choose among six
faces, and the game ages the one you pick.
