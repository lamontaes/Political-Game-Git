title: The Money sheet lists everything you paid out last month, by reason
---

The "what went out last month" list on the Money sheet now shows every payment your household made in the month, grouped by what it was for, including one-off payments such as a fee, a move or a premium that the list missed before. A payment that came up short shows the unpaid part. The list waits until a full month has been watched, so it never shows part of a month as the whole.
