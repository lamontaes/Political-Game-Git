section: Changed
title: Homeowners without a mortgage pay property tax on their place's due dates
---

Homeowners without a mortgage now pay their property tax in installments on the due dates of their state, instead of a twelfth every month. Owners with a mortgage still pay a twelfth each month through the lender's escrow, so a county's treasury now receives its tax in the lumps real counties do.
