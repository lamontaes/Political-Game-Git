title: More news stories print a full story
---

Stories about bills signed or vetoed, floor and committee votes, veto overrides, bills introduced, hearings, the state jobs report and the Federal Reserve's rate now print more than a headline and one sentence: what the bill is about, who sponsors it, what happens next, the national rate, and where the rate stood before. Headlines are set in one style, large sums read "$228.7 million", and a law is named by what it covers ("the maximum monthly food assistance benefit"), not by an internal key.
