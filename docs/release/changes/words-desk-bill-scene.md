title: A bill on your desk is a scene
---

When a bill the council or legislature passed is presented to you as mayor,
county executive, governor or president, an aide you hired brings it to you to
sign or veto. A mayor or county executive with no aide gets it from the town's
or county's clerk. The presentment is on the record, and the scene names the
bill as a clerk reads it ("Ordinance 2025-1").
