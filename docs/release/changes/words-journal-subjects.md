title: Journal lines say who and what they are about
---

Your "My life" journal now names a child at birth ("My daughter Jane was born."), names the office when someone you know wins or loses a race ("My father was elected to Mayor."), and says "Little did I know" or "That was a turning point" only when something later in your story follows from it.
