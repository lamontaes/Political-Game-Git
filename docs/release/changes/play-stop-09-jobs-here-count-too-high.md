title: News's jobs line counts your own town.
---

The "Jobs here" line in News now counts the people of your town who are at work, including those who work outside it, and those looking for work, and the openings its own employers have posted. It used to add every job held by an official or other named person anywhere in the country, so a town of 2,570 showed 2,036 employed.
