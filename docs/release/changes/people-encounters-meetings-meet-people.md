title: Residents who speak at public comment now know the council members who heard them.
---

A resident who stands up at a council's public comment and the members who hear them now know each other by name, and the speaker's time at the podium starts a tie. No screen shows how they met yet: the effect is that they can now be visited and asked for a vote.
