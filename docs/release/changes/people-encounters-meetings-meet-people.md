title: Going to a town hall or speaking at public comment now meets the people there.
---

The people who come to a town hall now know the official who held it by name; the official does not learn every face in the room. The residents who stand up at public comment and the council members who hear them now know each other. No screen shows how they met yet: the effect is that these people can now be visited and asked for a vote.
