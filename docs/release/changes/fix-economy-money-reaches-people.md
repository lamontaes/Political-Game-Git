title: Homes have prices, new businesses start small, and owners get paid
---

Homes in your town now have real values, and renters pay rent each month; homeowners with a loan pay their mortgage. A new business starts the size its founder can afford, with the founder's own savings and a bank loan sized to what the business can repay, and its owner takes home part of the profit. When a family runs short of money, the game records who paid the bills, what went unpaid and which relatives helped. Workers whose employer closes now look for new jobs instead of vanishing from the count.
