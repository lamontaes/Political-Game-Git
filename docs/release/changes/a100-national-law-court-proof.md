title: National laws use their saved federal court
---

A court test now carries an authored federal tax bill through Congress and the President's actual desk, then binds the enacted law to the saved Supreme Court and its seated judges. The same binding survives saving and loading; removing that court leaves no substitute court.

This proves court selection. It does not add a judicial challenge or a national tax-law precedent, and does not claim that a national judicial ruling occurred.

The complete changed judicial-review test file passed five tests. Scoped types found no diagnostics. The A100 static checks increased from seven of eight on the pinned main source to eight of eight on this branch.
