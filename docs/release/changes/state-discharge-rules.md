section: Added
title: In eleven more states a bill stuck in committee can be brought to the floor by petition.
---

Where a committee will not report a bill, members of a chamber can take it from the committee by signing a petition, and the number they need now follows each chamber's own rule (a majority of its members, or a fraction of them) for the U.S. House and eleven state chambers, from the rule text in each case. Before, only the U.S. House had a petition.
