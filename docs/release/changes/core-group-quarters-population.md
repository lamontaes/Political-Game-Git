title: College towns, prison towns and base towns house their residents in group quarters
---

The students in a college's dormitories, the people in a prison or a nursing home and the service members in a base's barracks now live in group quarters, counted from the Census, instead of renting homes in town, so a college town's rents, renters and evictions follow the households that really rent there.
