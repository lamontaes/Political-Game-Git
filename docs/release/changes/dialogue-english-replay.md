title: Spoken replies stay in conversations
---

When bargaining over a bill, replies show the words your character says, using
the bill's current designation and proposed section, wherever those words are
written; every move stays available. Ordinary talk can now open with a spoken
greeting and can ask about a known governor death by the title recorded in
the save, then tell someone who has not heard. Opening News records that you
read the story, and only a story you read or took part in can come up in talk.
Talking about what is going on, or suggesting something to do, needs something
actually going on in the scene. After Save and Continue,
these conversations show the words you chose.
For a new child, a parent or guardian who appears in the room opens their
portrait and details card once. Save and Continue remembers that introduction.
