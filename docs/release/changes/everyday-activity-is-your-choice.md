id: everyday-activity-is-your-choice
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Reading, drawing or resting at home waits until you ask for it
---

At home, the next scene was often a fifteen-minute everyday activity: pick
something to read, draw or rest. It arrived on its own each new day, in
place of something that might actually be happening in the life.

Those everyday activities are now something you choose. Beside "See what
happens next" there is a button, "Spend a little time on your own", while you
are at home and have not already taken that time today. "See what happens
next" goes on to whatever the life has waiting instead. The note about
activity durations under the scene is gone too, because the choices still
show their own times.
