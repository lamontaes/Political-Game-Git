title: Mayors, governors and presidents weigh a nominee by their own traits
---

An official who appoints a department head, a cabinet member or a federal secretary now weighs loyalty (how close the nominee sits and whether they know them) against merit (how qualified they are) by their own traits, so one who prizes loyalty picks a friend and one who prizes merit picks the better qualified.
