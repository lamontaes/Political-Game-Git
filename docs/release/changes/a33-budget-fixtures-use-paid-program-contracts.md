id: a33-budget-fixtures-use-paid-program-contracts
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Budget regression fixtures settle saved payments and preserve their source records.
---

Pension, carry-forward, corrections and governor-disposition regression fixtures
use recorded government cash and existing payment writers. Pension and program
costs retain the measure, appropriation, commitment, installment and paid-transfer
chain. Controlled funding, recipients and contract prices are explicit test inputs;
they do not claim automatic pension or corrections delivery in ordinary play.

The borrowing assertion remains intact and separately blocked on a public issuer
and disbursement contract. No forecast is converted into cash and no account is
allowed to overdraw.
