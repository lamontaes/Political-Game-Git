title: A grown-up's family is drawn from real American shares
---

A new life that starts as an adult no longer has one parent and nobody else.
About seven in ten now grew up with two parents, the share the Census Bureau
counts today, and the rest with one. Brothers and sisters come in the numbers
real families have them, from none to four, older or younger and a few years
apart. Grandparents are recorded on each parent's side, and the older ones may
have died before the life began.

Every place in the country follows the same rule, so families differ by draw,
not by state. Saved games keep the family they already had.
