title: Scenes with a purpose sound like real people and keep moving
---

A reporter changes the angle instead of repeating the question; a vote counter asks what would change your mind; a volunteer opens with something from the rally and your character answers first. After the first exchange the replies offer new moves, including a way out, and a lie can be owned with an edge. Every branch of a scene ends with the other person's closing line, never on an unanswered question. The lines are calibrated on real interviews, oral histories and casework reports, studied, not copied.
