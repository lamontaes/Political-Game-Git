section: Fixed
title: Tuition price consequences leave unrelated payday payments alone
---

Price-cost consequences ignore statutory tax payment activities and resource flows whose basis does not match the row before requiring a person payer. Ordinary town paydays continue with the tuition row registered. A referenced resource-flow terms record whose flow is missing still reports the integrity error; tuition caps and recorded backfill retain their existing behavior.
