title: A speaker no longer asks "Why would we change course" about a complaint
---

Governors, mayors and the President who defend what is in force no longer ask "Why would we change course, when ..." and then name something wrong with how things are. The question is asked only of a fact the speaker is glad of, and a way of opening a point is used only where the point fits it.
