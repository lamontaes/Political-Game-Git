title: Filing for office is a petition you have to fill
---

Filing for office now takes out a petition. Your name goes on the ballot
only if enough voters have signed it by the filing deadline. The number you
need comes from your state's filing rules for that office. The people you
know, the doors you knock and the evenings you hold all add signatures, and
each person decides for their own reasons whether to sign. The morning after
the deadline, the clerk checks the names and strikes anyone who has died,
moved away or may not vote. You make the ballot or come up short. The other
candidates in town go through the same petition.
