title: Police, fire and school pay follows the budget where public-sector bargaining is law.
---

Where a state or town has a public-sector bargaining law in force, the pay of police, fire and school workers now rises at each budget date by the growth of their employer's own budget line, and it stops rising that way when the law is repealed.
