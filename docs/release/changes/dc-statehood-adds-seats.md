title: A statehood law for D.C. adds two Senators and a voting Representative
---

When Congress passes statehood for the District of Columbia and the law takes
effect, the District gets two Senate seats and a voting House seat. The Senate
grows to 102 and the House to 436, and every vote count and majority reads the
new seat count. The Delegate in office becomes the Representative, and each
Senate seat goes to the party the District's own 2024 result favors. Before,
Congress could pass statehood and nothing changed. The seats come from a data
file, not from code.
