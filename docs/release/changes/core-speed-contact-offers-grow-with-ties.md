A town's later years run a little faster: when a person weighs what to do, the parts of each choice that depend only on the kind of act are worked out once per kind, and the trait-lean bounds once per parameter table. Every choice comes out the same.

No choice or payment rule changes: action-kind terms are reused only within one scoring call; bookkeeping continues to read trait bounds through the parameter table.
