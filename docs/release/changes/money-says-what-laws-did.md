title: Money and property says what new laws did to money
---

Money and property now has a section called "What new laws did to money". It
lists the five most recent times a new law reached the player's own pay,
taxes, benefits or rent, or a family member's, with the date and the amount.
It also says what each law did across the player's town, such as a minimum
wage raise that added a total each month to the pay of a number of residents.
Each law's name opens its page. Before any new law has reached anyone's money,
the section says so.
