title: The talk at home after you file comes from someone with something at stake
---

After you file for office, someone at home talks to you only if the records give them a stake in the run: the household's savings against how long the race will last, or a young child at home while you work. They open with that in their own words, you answer one of four ways (own it, bring them in, bound it, put it off), and what you say counts: a relative who agrees to help is counted among your campaign's volunteers, and putting it off costs you with them. A household with nothing at stake no longer gets a scene.
