title: The opening no longer seats a prison inmate on a town board.
---

The opening no longer seats people an institution houses. When a town office was filled without an election, its holder could be drawn from anyone in town, including people living in a prison or a nursing home. At Richfield, Utah, an inmate sat on a special district's board. Now the draw leaves out every resident of the Census's institutional group quarters (correctional and juvenile facilities, nursing homes, other institutions), full and counted alike. College dormitories and military quarters are not institutions in the Census's sense, so their residents can still be drawn.
