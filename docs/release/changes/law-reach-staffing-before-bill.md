title: A budget cut now lets the newest public hires go first, named or counted
---

When a city or county cuts the line that pays a department (schools, police, fire), the layoffs now follow one order across the employer's whole staff: the newest hire goes first, whether the game follows that worker closely or counts them in the wider town.
