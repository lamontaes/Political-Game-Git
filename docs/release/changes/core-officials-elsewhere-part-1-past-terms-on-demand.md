title: Other states' officials' past terms are filled in when you open them
---

A new game no longer writes out the earlier terms of every legislator, governor and statewide officer in the other states before you start, about twelve thousand records nobody had looked at. Their earlier terms are filled in the first time you open one of their cards, and they are the same every time you open them.
