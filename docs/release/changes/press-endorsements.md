title: Newspapers and stations endorse in the final weeks of a race
---

In the last weeks before an election, each local paper and station settles whom to back. Its editor and owner weigh how they regard the candidates, and the outlet endorses the one they hold highest, or no one if none stands out. Readers of that outlet think better of the candidate it backs.
