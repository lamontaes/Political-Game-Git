impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A person who works for a public employer now shows that employer on their card and in the campaign screens.
---

A worker at a public employer that pays its staff from its government's own funds, such as a town's post office, was shown as having "a job outside town" on a person card, and in the campaign and closing screens. The card and those screens now name the employer and the job, the same way scenes do.
