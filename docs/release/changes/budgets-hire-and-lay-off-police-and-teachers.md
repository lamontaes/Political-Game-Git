title: Budgets hire and lay off the town's police officers and teachers
---

A town's police officers and teachers are now the positions its budgets fund.
Each quarter, when the police line of the government that serves the town
rises in real terms, the town hires officers from its own job seekers; when it
is cut, officers are laid off, the newest first. Teachers follow the state's
school line the same way. Those laid off are recalled first when funding
returns. A teacher needs a bachelor's degree wherever the person's schooling
is on record.
