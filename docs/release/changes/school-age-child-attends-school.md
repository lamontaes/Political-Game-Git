title: A school-age child goes to school again
---

A child old enough for school was starting life attending nothing: no school, no classmates and no enrolment on their record. Every opening scene set at school or with a classmate quietly stopped appearing, and the town the child lived in had no school in it. School-age children are enrolled again, and those scenes come back with them. An adult's summarized earlier life is unchanged: it still declines to assume a school, employer or credential the record does not actually hold.
