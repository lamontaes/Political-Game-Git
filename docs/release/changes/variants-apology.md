title: A quarrel over a missed payment now plays four different apology scenes.
---

When you miss a payment and the person you live with is wronged by it, the talk that follows depends on how things stand. A close housemate is hurt and asks what happened, someone you have quarreled with over money before is tired of hearing sorry, and someone short on money themselves asks for a plan. The old lines about a council meeting no longer play for a household quarrel.
