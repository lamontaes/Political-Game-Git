title: A bill's floor date holds for bills drafted between sittings and goes away when the leader refuses the bill
---

A bill drafted while no legislature sits is put on the floor leader's calendar the day the next sitting opens, so the date its paper names holds, and a bill the leader refuses to put on the floor stops naming a floor date.
