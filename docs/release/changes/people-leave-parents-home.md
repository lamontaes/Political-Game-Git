title: Young adults now leave their parents' homes when their age, pay and cash allow it.
---

An adult living with a parent weighs staying against a home of their own each month: their age, whether their pay covers the rent, a crowded home, how close they are to the parent and how much of their pay the rent would take. If they go, they sign a lease and start a household of their own, and the family's everyday spending is split with them.
