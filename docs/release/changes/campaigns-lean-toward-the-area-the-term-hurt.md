title: A campaign now leans toward the area the last term hurt or helped.
---

A challenger running after a term where pay, jobs or prices slid now spends more of the campaign on the questions that bear on them, and an incumbent leans toward what the term improved. A seat with no sitting incumbent campaigns as before.
