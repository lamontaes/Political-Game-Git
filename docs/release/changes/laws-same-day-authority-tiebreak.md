section: Fixed
title: When a state repeals and grants a city's power to set its own minimum wage on the same day, the later law now governs.
---

A state's answer on local wage authority always beat a statute taking effect the same day, even one enacted after it, so a council could be refused under a repeal the state had already undone. Laws enacted in play now carry the order they were enacted in, and the later one governs.
