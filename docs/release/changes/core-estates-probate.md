title: Estates pay their debts, wait in probate and follow a will
---

When someone dies, what they owed on their standing payments is paid from their estate first. The estate then waits in probate for about nine months before the heirs take it. A person whose nature and age led them to make a will leaves their money to their family in shares by how close each was; anyone else's passes by the state's order of kin, or to the state. A pension or other income paid to the person stops at their death, so nothing more reaches the estate after it.
