title: You can run for one seat on your town's council, and only while filing is open.
---

Before, the clerk and the Campaigns screen offered you every seat on the council at once, and even races whose filing would not open for years. Now you are offered one race of each local body: the seat for your own district where it is elected by district, or the at-large race once where its seats are elected at large. A race shows up only while its filing period is open, and once you have filed for one of a body's races, its others are no longer offered.
