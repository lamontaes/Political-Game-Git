title: Answer a reporter's interview your own way
---

When an outlet invites you to an interview during a campaign, the campaign screen now shows the reporter's questions and lets you choose, for each one, how you answer: plainly, with a fact from your record, by turning it on your rival, briefly, or with something that is not so. Your answers then appear as a conversation.
