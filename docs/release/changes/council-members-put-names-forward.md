title: A mayor fills a council vacancy from names the council puts forward
---

When a council seat falls vacant, the mayor still names someone they know
first. Now the other council members also put forward the people they know,
and the mayor weighs those names too. Before, a mayor whose only acquaintances
were the council itself had nobody eligible, and the council drew a resident
from the town roster instead. In Tchula, Mississippi, the mayor now appoints a
resident one of the council members knows.
