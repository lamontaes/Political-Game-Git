title: Your journal tells the count of a petition to recall you
---

When a petition to recall you is counted, your journal now says how it went: "519 people signed to recall me. It went to a vote." Where you heard the petitioners out and fewer signed for it, it says so: "Hearing the petitioners out cost them 79 signatures, not the vote." Before, the count never reached the official it was about unless they happened to know the organizer.
