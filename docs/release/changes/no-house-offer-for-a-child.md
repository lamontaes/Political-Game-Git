title: A child is no longer offered a house
---

A child was offered a house. In Alaska, Camila, 10, was offered a $250,000 home
with nothing behind it. Buying a home now needs age 18, and the offer appears
only when the game keeps the person's money. The Money screen also shows its
date in words instead of year-month-day.
