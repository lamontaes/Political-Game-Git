title: A child is no longer offered a house or a romance, and place figures read as plain dates
---

A child was offered a house. In Alaska, Camila, 10, was offered a $250,000 home
with nothing behind it. Buying a home now needs age 18, and the offer appears
only when the game keeps the person's money.

A 12-year-old saw "Pursue a relationship" among the things they mean to do,
with a refusal under it. The aim is no longer listed for anyone under 18.

The place figures on the Money screen read like "Unemployment rate, Alaska:
7.0%, up from 4.3% in July 2026" instead of naming "the last published figure"
and a code such as "2026-M07" or "FY2025", and a footnote asterisk after a
place name is gone. The Money screen also shows its own date in words.
