title: A House member's own seat is no longer shown as their home district
---

When a home place lies in two House districts, the Government screen now keeps
the home's district unresolved after the player wins one of them. Before, it
showed the seat the player holds as the district that contains their home,
which the game has not recorded. The note naming both districts stays.
