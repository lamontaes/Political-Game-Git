title: A neighbor who loses a job holds it against the governor
---

When a neighbor is laid off or their workplace closes, they think it over a
few days later and come away holding it against the governor of their state
or territory. That view counts in the town's support for the governor, and
when you ask them about the people in office, they tell you why.
