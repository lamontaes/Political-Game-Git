title: Intelligence on an international crisis reads the record, not a roll
---

What intelligence judges about a foreign counterparty in a crisis, and how
sure it is, no longer comes from a roll. A first look at a counterparty with
no history rests on how tense the standoff is. Intelligence grows surer as it
watches the counterparty respond, and it reads a past habit of escalating or
stepping back into the next dispute with the same counterparty.
