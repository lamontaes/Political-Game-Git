title: A new life opens with a close family and a readable earlier life
---

A new ordinary life can begin with parents, a sibling and grandparents in the record. Older adults may also have a partner and grown child. Shared moments appear in the Journal across childhood and adult years, and the people in those moments are the same people the world knows as family.

Newly generated close relatives have distinct first names. Earlier shared moments, moves and jobs are spread through the year; school starts in late summer and school completion falls in spring. A birthday remains a birthday.

An adult with recorded work and active pay terms starts with a personal balance shown in Personal. That opening balance is a seeded game savings profile, not a claim that every past wage was paid. The local employer and monthly wage are still the game's stated business placeholders. Pay actually earned after the life begins is recorded separately, month by month. A life without known pay keeps its starting money unknown.
