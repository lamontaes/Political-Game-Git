title: A state law that raises the minimum wage now raises it, even when its bill names no dollar figure.
---

A state law that answers yes to raising the minimum wage now adds the average
real state raise to the state's rate: $0.90 the day it takes effect, another
$0.90 each year after, up to $2.00, measured from the Department of Labor's
table of state rates from 2013 to 2024. A bill that names its own dollar figure
still uses it. Every town job paid below the new rate is raised on the first pay
period after each step, and the state's poverty rate follows after its lag. A
later law that answers no takes the rate back to where it was, and the effect on
poverty ends with it.
