title: Saying yes to a Saturday invitation now counts
---

Saying yes to a Saturday invitation now counts. The afternoon goes on your calendar with a short trip there, and when the day comes you go, and it is remembered as time spent with the person who asked. Before, a yes was recorded and then the day "passed without an answer".

Invitations now say who asked and when, from your side: "Rachel Kim asked you over on Saturday afternoon. Going is optional." The Invitations panel has a button to say you will come as well as to decline, and it is no longer the same neighbor asking every time.
