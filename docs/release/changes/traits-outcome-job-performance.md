title: Employers value how a person works
---

When a business hires or lets someone go, it now weighs how diligent each worker is, at a size taken from research on conscientiousness and job performance, and the people not yet named in your town are judged by the same rule. A layoff's record says whether how the worker worked, not only how long they had been there, decided it.
