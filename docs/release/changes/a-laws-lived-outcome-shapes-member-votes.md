section: Improved
title: A law's lived effects now inform later member votes
---

When people had saved views about a law's effects and the members responsible
for it, those views did not inform a later vote about changing the same
policy. Ordinary bills and state or federal constitutional policy proposals
now use those recorded experiences when members decide. Their other recorded
reasons still count in the same vote.
