title: Panels move and resize freely, the opening fills the screen, and Attend holds a meeting you arranged
---

A meeting you arranged with somebody through Contact can now be attended.
Pressing Attend used to do nothing, because the game looked for a route to a
place called "Arranged in person" and found none.

Panels move by their title bar and resize from any edge or corner. After a
change, "Keep this size" saves it; otherwise the panel opens at its saved or
default size next time, and double-clicking the title bar resets it. The
Maximize and Reset layout buttons are gone, and panels now fade out when they
close.

The opening cards fill the screen on each place's approved picture, without
the camera drift or its Pause motion button, and one title fades out before
the next fades in. Politics opens on Government. Contact opens as its own
screen over whatever is open, and "Talk to somebody here" lists only people
who are actually in the room. Scrollbars are thin and styled. The table papers
in the room and the minutes notice after a day passes are gone, and the
desktop app opens maximized, with the hub's toolbar stepping aside in full
screen.
