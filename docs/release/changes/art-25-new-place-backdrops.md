title: Twenty-five new places to be in, from county clerk offices to convention floors
---

The game gains 25 painted places, each staged for people:

- rural and urban county clerk offices, a registrar of voters and an election warehouse;
- five rally sites (a county fairground, an airport hangar, a courthouse square, a downtown plaza and a union-hall parking lot);
- the national convention floor and stage;
- five kinds of apartment and four regional houses;
- school and campus rooms.

Light and weather versions fall back to midday for now.
