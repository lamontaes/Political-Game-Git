title: A paper prints its own endorsement in its own pages
---

When a newspaper or magazine endorses in a race, it now prints the news of that endorsement in its own pages the next press week, filed under its editor's name. Before, a paper's own endorsement competed with every group's endorsement of the same race for one story a week, and lost each time, so its readers never read it. The story takes no reporter's slot.
