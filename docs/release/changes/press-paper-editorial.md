title: A paper prints its own endorsement as its editorial
---

When a newspaper or magazine endorses in a race, it now prints that endorsement in its own pages the next press week, under its editor's name, as its editorial. Before, a paper's own endorsement competed with every group's endorsement of the same race for one story a week, and lost each time, so its readers never read it. The editorial takes no reporter's slot.
