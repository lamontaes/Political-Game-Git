title: School talk says the right years and fits its replies to the question
---

A schoolmate now says how long it has been since you were last at school together, not since your first year there, and only people who were at the same stage of a school in the same years count as schoolmates. "We were at school together, weren't we?" is no longer answered "Yes, I do.", and someone who works at the school today no longer asks whether you remember it.
