title: Nobody can ask out the person who raised them, and the dead are not offered Talk or Meet
---

Before, a grown child whose guardian had no family record could ask that
guardian out, and the date went ahead. A parent who had died still showed
Talk, Meet, Travel to and Contact on their card.

Now anyone who ever held a parent's or guardian's authority over the other,
or ever cared for them, can never be asked out, whichever of the two asks.
The card for somebody who has died offers no actions.
