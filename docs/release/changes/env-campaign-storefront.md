title: A campaign storefront field office for campaign strategy and voter phone banks.
---

Calling voters from a desk and organizing campaign strategy now place your character in a campaign storefront field office rather than defaulting to home or an empty screen. The room features work tables, phone banks, strategy whiteboards, and dynamic slots displaying campaign branding and volunteer call targets without baking text into the underlying art. The swept community park pavilion is also brought through the authoring pipeline under candidate isolation, preserving strict separation between candidate previews and production play.
