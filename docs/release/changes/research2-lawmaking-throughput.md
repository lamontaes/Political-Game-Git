Official records now provide bounded evidence for congressional, state, and local lawmaking volume. The packet keeps introduced bills, active carryover pools, passage, enactment, vetoes, overrides, and local ordinances separate. It covers the 118th Congress, Kansas, New Mexico, Florida, and Kentucky 2024 regular sessions, Texas's 2024 off-year, and three local 2024 ordinance records.
