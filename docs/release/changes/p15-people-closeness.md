In the separate core prototype, how close two people are now follows the time they spend together, and fades when they stop meeting, more slowly between relatives. People who live together share home time each week. When someone reaches out, they turn to the ties that are fading most. Every resident now remembers classmates from school. Relatives' homes now depend on whether each person moved away, and on whether their parents' marriage lasted. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build. The closeness model and its tunable numbers are registered stopgaps that block release.
