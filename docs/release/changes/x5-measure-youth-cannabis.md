title: Each state now tracks teen marijuana use, and legal cannabis sales move it
---

Every state and D.C. now keeps the share of 12- to 17-year-olds who used marijuana in
the past month, starting from the 2023-2024 national drug use survey. Legalizing
cannabis sales lowers it about 4% once the law is in force, and ending legal sales a
state began with raises it by the same amount. The studies disagree, so each world
draws its own size, from a 13% drop to a 10% rise.
