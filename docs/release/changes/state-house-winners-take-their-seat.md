title: A state House winner can take the district seat they won
---

When a candidate's home district is saved but the filing screen did not ask
them to name it, winning the election now connects that result to the district
seat. The winner can appear as its member and vote during the term, including
in older saves with enough recorded district information. The game leaves a
seat unresolved when its district cannot be established from the save.
