title: Your office has On and Off switches for what stops you
---

Your office has a "What stops you" strip with one On and Off switch for each kind of vote your seat has: meetings of your body, floor votes, and bills on your desk. Every switch starts On, so you are held at every vote. Switch one Off and you are away from that kind of vote: the record shows you absent, a bill on your desk waits, and nothing is cast or signed for you. What you have done now reads "Yes", "No" or "Abstained".
