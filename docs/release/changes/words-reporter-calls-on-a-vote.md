title: After you vote on a bill, a reporter calls to ask why, and the paper's story of the vote quotes you or says you wouldn't comment.
---

When you vote on a bill as a member of a council or board, a reporter from an outlet that covers the body asks why you voted the way you did ("You voted against Ordinance 2025-1. What should people know about why?"). If you answer on the record, the paper's story of that vote quotes you. If you refuse, it says you would not comment. A reporter who already called about the bill's introduction calls again about the vote.
