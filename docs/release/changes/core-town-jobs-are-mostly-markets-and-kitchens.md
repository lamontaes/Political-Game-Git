title: Your starting town now has the jobs its county really has, and some are open from your first week.
---

Most towns used to open with almost nothing but stores, kitchens and warehouses, because a town without its own hospital or bank sent those jobs to the biggest employers instead. Now every town has its clinic and care home, and its jobs follow its county's mix. Neighbors who work out of town do real work (a nurse, a teacher, a truck driver), not "works outside the town". Employers also start with the openings a real town has, so a job can come your way in the first weeks instead of after two months.

An employer also no longer raises the pay of an opening before anyone could have taken it: a posting counts as standing unfilled only once its industry's usual hiring wait has passed, so new jobs are posted at the going rate instead of jumping in their first month.
