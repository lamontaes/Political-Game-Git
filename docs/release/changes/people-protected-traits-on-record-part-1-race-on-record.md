title: North Carolina towns now look like their own people
---

The people in a North Carolina town are now drawn from the town's own mix of residents by race and Hispanic origin (the 2020 Census counts for each of its 776 places), not the state's. A town that is mostly Black in a state that is mostly white now has mostly Black households, and the faces you meet follow. Every other place still draws from its state until its own counts are added.
