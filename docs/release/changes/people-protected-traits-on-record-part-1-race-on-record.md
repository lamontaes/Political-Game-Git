title: Towns now look like their own people
---

The people in a town are now drawn from the town's own mix of residents by race and Hispanic origin (the 2020 Census counts for each of about 31,800 places in the 50 states, DC and Puerto Rico), not the state's. A town that is mostly Black or mostly Hispanic in a state that is not now has mostly Black or Hispanic households, and the faces you meet follow. Island-area villages still draw from the nation's mix.
