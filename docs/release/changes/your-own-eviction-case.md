title: You can be taken to court over your own rent
---

Your household was never evicted. Now, when you hold the lease and fall two
months behind, your landlord takes you to court, and you learn what you owe
and the day the case is decided. Before then you can pay it all if you have
the money, offer a payment plan, take the lawyer a right-to-counsel law
provides, or move out first. An unanswered case is decided on the next first
of the month and can end in eviction. Skipping ahead stops the day before.
Tenants without a lawyer now lose about 40% of cases, not 50%, matching
national court records.
