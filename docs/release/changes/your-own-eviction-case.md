title: You can be taken to court over your own rent
---

Your household was never evicted, and everyone else's case was decided by a
roll. Now, when you hold the lease and fall two months behind, your landlord
takes you to court, and you learn what you owe and the day the case is
decided. Before then you can pay it all, offer a payment plan, take the
lawyer a right-to-counsel law provides, or move out first. Every case is
decided by how far behind the tenant is, whether the landlord follows
through, what the tenant does, and the state's trial judge.
