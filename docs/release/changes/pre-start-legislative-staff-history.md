impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A selected staff start no longer keeps its earlier local job active
---

The versioned prior-year staff start now ends the character's generated local job and its future pay terms when legislative staff work begins. The earlier job remains in their history and can explain their starting savings, while only the selected staff job is active at Begin. No salary is claimed to have been paid during the prior year.

This staff option remains gated in the New Game pilot until a staff salary rule and first-pay caller are connected. Legislative staff is employment, not an elected seat or a campaign victory.
