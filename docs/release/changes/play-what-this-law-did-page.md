title: A law's page shows what it did
---

After a law passes, its page in Politics, Issues has a "What this did" section. It names the people the law reached, with the money it moved for each: people in town, people elsewhere, and the households in town as a count. When a later law replaces it or the rate goes back, the page says until when, and the day it stopped for each person.
