section: Changed
title: Residents who bring a problem to an official's office stay part of the wider town until you meet them.
---

Every resident who took a problem to an official's office hours used to become a fully tracked person, with their whole household. In a year that was hundreds of people the player never met, and it was the largest part of a long game's growing memory use. Now their case is kept and decided by the same rule as anyone's. They become fully tracked only when the player is the official who hears them, or opens them. Their card then shows the case, their tie to the official and how it changed their view of them.
