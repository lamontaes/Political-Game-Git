section: Changed
title: A donor who just gave to your campaign says how much, the bill they care about and which way they want you on it.
---

When someone who gave to your campaign comes to ask for something back, they now say what the records hold: what they just gave (and what they have given in all, if they gave before), the bill before your body they care most about, and the side they want you on. Your answers name the same things: you will take another look, hand it to your office, say a gift buys no position, or put them off. A small giver, a large one, a friend and someone you put off before still sound like themselves. Stock lines that said the same thing to every candidate are gone.
