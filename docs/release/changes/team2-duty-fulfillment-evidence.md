section: Fixed
title: Staffing no longer proves fulfillment of an enacted duty
---

A covered body remains compliance-unknown when no qualifying fulfillment record
connects it to the duty. Employing someone no longer creates a finding that the
body complied. Unknown coverage, previously saved findings and repeat handling
remain unchanged. Positive fulfillment still needs an approved receipt binding.
