title: Four more federal laws change the world
---

A federal tariff law now raises what households pay, Medicare drug price
negotiation lowers what enrollees pay out of pocket, a national cap on consumer
loan rates cuts high-cost borrowing where a state has no cap of its own, and a
higher retirement age keeps more people 62 to 66 at work. Before, Congress could
pass each of these and nothing in any place changed. Each place now keeps that
outcome from the start of the game, and the law moves it after its lag.
