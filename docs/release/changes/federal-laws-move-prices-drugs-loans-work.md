title: Three more federal laws change the world
---

A federal tariff law now raises what households pay, Medicare drug price
negotiation lowers what enrollees pay out of pocket, and a higher retirement age
keeps more people 62 to 66 at work. Before, Congress could pass each of these
and nothing in any place changed. Each place now keeps that outcome from the
start of the game, and the law moves it after its lag. The delegates' 2024
results now come from a data file, not from numbers typed into the code.
