title: The news page lists the laws that reach you and what each one did
---

Before, a law that passed showed up only on its own bill page. The news page
for your town said who held office and what happened lately, but nothing about
the laws in force where you live.

Now the news page has a "Laws that reach you" section: national law, your
state's, and your town's, newest first. Each one says when it was enacted and
what it changed in the world, such as a tax and what it has collected, or
money it provided and how much has been paid out. A law with no part the game
acts on yet says so plainly. Laws from other states never appear.
