title: Explaining lines under headings and controls are gone
---

Removed the lines that explained or captioned a control: the filing sentence and each card's reason line on Travel, the fit line under a party membership, "Something needs you" over Day and Week, the age line and the waiting-for-a-gender button text in the creator, and the version number on the title. City seats now read as districts, never wards, and pressed buttons are a lit fill with no corner mark.
