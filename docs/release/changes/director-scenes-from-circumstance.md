title: Things that happen in the world come to you where you are
---

What happens in your town and beyond now reaches you through the people around you, not only when it names you. A neighbor who read that a law took effect stops you in the street. Someone at home brings up a story they read, and a coworker brings up the national news. You hear of a death from someone who knew the person, and a new hire is introduced at work. If you serve, a colleague catches you in the hallway after the body meets, and an aide calls when a group starts organizing in town. Each comes from a real record and a real person with a reason to raise it, and these moments come at most every few days.
