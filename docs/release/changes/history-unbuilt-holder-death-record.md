An official known only by name (another state's member of Congress) who dies has a death record, a public record as anyone's death is, and the seat they leave names it. It names them by id in its facts, since a record lists only the people the world holds, and is kept at the player's places, as the seat's vacancy is. At Meadow, Utah, all 20 vacated seats name their death, from 2.
