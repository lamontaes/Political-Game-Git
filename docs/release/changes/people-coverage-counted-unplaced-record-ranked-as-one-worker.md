title: About half of a town's working households hold a job's health plan, month after month
---

The share of a town's households that hold a health plan through work could swing from one in twenty to nine in ten between one month and the next, and a small change in pay could set it off. A wage law could leave the town's households tens of millions of dollars poorer in premiums. The count of who holds a plan now weighs each group of households for the workers it stands for, so the share stays near the national one and a pay change no longer moves it.
