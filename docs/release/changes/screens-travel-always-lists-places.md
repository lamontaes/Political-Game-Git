title: Travel always lists somewhere to go, and says what is open now
---

Travel lists the places you have a reason to go, then the town's diners, shops, post office, library and the like, so the list is never empty, even at night. Each place says "Open now" with who is there, or "Closed now". The "You are at home" line under the title is gone, a relative's workplace no longer says "You work here", and the cards are smaller so more fit on a page.
