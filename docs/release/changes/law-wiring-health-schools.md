section: Improved
title: Health, food aid and school laws reach people and budgets
---

Medicaid expansion now splits each covered adult's payment between the state and the federal government by the Medicaid match in force, and the work requirement ends coverage for the adults it reaches. Food aid reads the SNAP maximum and the assistance work requirement for each household and person, universal preschool and private-school funds record the households they pay, and raising the schools or health budget line now moves per-student spending and infant deaths in the outcome web.
