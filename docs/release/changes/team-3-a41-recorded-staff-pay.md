impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Office staff pay uses recorded work and occupational wages
---

Civil-service, executive-staff and agency-director jobs with a mapped occupation now initialize pay from their recorded workplace, weekly hours and the existing occupational wage reader. An unmapped occupation no longer receives the generic office salary. Existing salary agreements and enacted office salaries remain intact.
