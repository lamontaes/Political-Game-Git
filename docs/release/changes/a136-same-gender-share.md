id: a136-same-gender-share
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: New couples in town are two women or two men as often as the town's first couples
---

Before, every resident had their own 6% chance of looking for a partner of
their own gender. That share was set by hand and is about four times the
share of same-sex couples a town opens with.

Now each town's women, and its men, have exactly the town's share of people
looking for a partner of their own gender: the same 1.5% the opening uses
for its couples, rounded to whole people. Nothing is drawn. Who among them
it is stays the same from season to season, so nobody changes from one
quarter to the next.

No place has its own figure on file yet, so every place uses the national
estimate.
