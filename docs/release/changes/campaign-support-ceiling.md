title: Campaigning no longer carries a candidate to 96% of the vote, and your own polling can be wrong
---

Every afternoon on the doors used to move the same share of the vote whether a
candidate stood at 40% or 90%, so a candidate who kept canvassing could run from
48% to 96%. Campaign work now moves support fully up to half the field, less and
less above it, and not at all past three-quarters. The same rule applies to
rival campaigns.

The campaign's own read of where you stand is no longer always close. How far
off it can be depends on who on the campaign has done survey work, and for how
long: a volunteer's count can miss by as much as twelve points.
