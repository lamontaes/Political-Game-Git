title: The journal tells your own race even against a rival you never met
---

A candidate's journal now tells how their own race went the day after the count, won or lost, even when they never knew their rival by name ("It wasn't close."). Before, a race against a stranger left the journal at "I ran for Commissioner." with nothing about the result.
