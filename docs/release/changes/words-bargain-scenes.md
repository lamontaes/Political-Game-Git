title: A vote trade is a scene for both legislators
---

When a bill's sponsor wins a holdout's vote by adding a section the holdout
wants, the holdout is asked for their vote by the sponsor, and the sponsor
bargains with the holdout over the section. Both scenes name the bill and the
section from the trade's own record and are staged in the chamber it was made
in.
