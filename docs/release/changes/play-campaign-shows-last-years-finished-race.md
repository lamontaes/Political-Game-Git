title: Running for office again opens this year's race, not the finished one
---

Once an election is over, the Campaigns screen and the trail no longer show that race. Filing again, after winning or losing, opens the new race: you can knock on doors, collect signatures and make the ballot, and the clerk's counter knows which filing is yours.
