title: A bill's sections show the law they change and keep a tax's shape
---

A bill now lays out each section as the law it changes: the current value and the statute it comes from, the new value, and the date it takes effect, with the value, date and shape (flat or graduated) as the fields a sponsor can edit. A bill that sets a graduated income tax's new top rate now scales every bracket by the same share, rather than replacing the schedule with one rate; a section marked flat sets one rate for everyone.
