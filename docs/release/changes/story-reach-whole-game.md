section: Improved
title: A grade you give now reaches every line like it, across the whole game
---

When you grade a line, every other line that makes the same move in the same voice and register is weighed by it, in every kind of scene and every part of the game, not only in the kind of scene you graded it in. A line still plays only where its own scene calls for it.
