title: The game now opens under the taxes and benefits actually in force in January 2025.
---

Paychecks, sales, food assistance, Medicaid and unemployment benefits start under the January 2025 law for the federal government and every state, instead of the 2026 figures carried back to the start, and change to the 2026 law when it takes effect. A state that repeals its graduated income tax now replaces it with the middle of the real flat-tax states' rates, and the share of adults who use cannabis matches the latest national survey.
