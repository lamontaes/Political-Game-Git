title: The economy and every place's numbers move only for a reason you can point to
---

Before: the national economy and every state's measures (uninsured share,
poverty, graduation, crime, air quality and the rest) were nudged a little every
month by chance. The economy also took random jumps into a new era and random
price shocks, and every place's numbers now and then rode a random society-wide
wave. Two worlds with the same laws and the same history could end up far apart
for no reason anyone could name.

After: nothing is drawn. A place's numbers hold where they start until a law, a
condition or a dated, recorded crisis moves them, and the record names the cause
that did. The national economy moves only from its own causes: the credit cycle
and the central bank's board, and recorded shocks such as an energy
disruption, a disaster, a trade break or public money paid out. A price spike or
a recession now has a date and a cause in the record; it is never just bad luck.
