title: A mayor, county executive or governor can send a bill to their council or legislature through a member.
---

Your office now shows the same Draft a bill list for a mayor, county executive or governor that a council member gets. Pick a policy and the game asks the member most inclined to carry it. You are told who was asked and whether they agreed to carry it; if they did, the bill goes before the body as brought at your request.
