section: Fixed
title: Government and news staff are paid the pay their post lists
---

A government post or a news outlet's staff job was paid for the full hours of the work schedule it followed, which could run past the hours the job lists, so staff were paid up to a fifth more than their post says and small outlets ran short. The rate is now set over the scheduled hours, so the pay is the pay the post lists.
