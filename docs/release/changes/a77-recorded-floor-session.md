title: Recorded floor ballots obey the shared session-end rules
---

Player and council floor callers retain the shared bill driver's session-end
decision. Supplying a recorded roll call cannot move a bill after adjournment;
the saved procedure still decides whether it dies or remains pending. Open
sessions retain the supplied members, ballots, provenance and sponsor control.
