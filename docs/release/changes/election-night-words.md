title: Election night speaks: the winner's aide, the loser's call, election morning and the returns have words again.
---

On election day the narrator now says the morning, your ballot, your polling place and its poll worker, offers a canvass before you vote and says how it went, and on the night reads the returns place by place to the call, whether you won, and the other states' governor races. After a win, your campaign aide steers you aside before the victory speech and asks what to say about your opponent; after a loss, the aide urges you to call the winner, and answers, presses or pleads as you agree, ask, refuse or stall. Before, these scenes had no lines, so election morning offered nothing to read.
