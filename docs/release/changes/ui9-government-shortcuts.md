title: Keep a government one click away
---

A government you are looking at can be pinned, and it stays on your shortcuts through a save and a reload. Opening it again brings that government straight back up. It does not move you, change where you live, or give you any standing there — it is a way back to a page, and where you live and what you may do are still whatever the world says they are.

People you know can be pinned from the people list as well, so anyone you want to hand can be kept there whether or not they are in the room with you.
