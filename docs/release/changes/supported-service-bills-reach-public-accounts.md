title: Supported service bills carry spending terms into government accounts
---

Lawmakers can file supported service bills with a recorded amount and a named
government, and their chambers record the vote. An enacted bill records what
the government may spend. The current officeholder can decide how to use that
authority; actual payments draw from the saved public account. The game keeps
the bill, spending decision, payment, and service result as separate dated
records, so a bill passing does not itself claim a service was delivered.
