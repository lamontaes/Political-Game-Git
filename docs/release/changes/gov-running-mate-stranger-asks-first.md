title: Someone you have never met asks you one thing before agreeing to be your running mate
---

When you ask a stranger to be your running mate and they are inclined to say yes, they first ask you something: why them, or where you stand on the question they care most about. You answer from what is true (the seat they hold, the view you share, your own stance), or you can dodge their question, which may cost you their yes.
