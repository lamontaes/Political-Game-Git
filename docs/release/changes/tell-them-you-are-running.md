title: Tell the people at home you are thinking of running
---

Once you mean to run for office, you can tell whoever is at home with you. The
talk runs three to eight exchanges: when the election is, the news, something
the two of you shared before, whether they would help and what worries them.
Their answers come from their own temperament and your history together, and a
date that has already passed is never spoken of as ahead.
