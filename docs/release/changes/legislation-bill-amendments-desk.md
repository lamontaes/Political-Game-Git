### Added

On a bill's paper you can choose your own vote before the floor takes it up, and the floor result reads in words ("Adopted, 4 to 2.") above the names of everyone who voted each way.

### Added

You can amend a section, lowering it to split the difference with the law in force or raising it. The amendment shows as a struck clause and the clause that replaces it, with its own estimate; the body votes it member by member, and the bill is rewritten only if it is adopted.

### Added

When a passed bill waits on your desk as an executive, its paper offers Sign it and Veto it, and once decided it names who signed or vetoed it and the reasons that weighed.
