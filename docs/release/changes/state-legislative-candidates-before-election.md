title: State legislative candidates enter races before election day
---

Background state legislative races now record fictional candidates and their choices before the general election. New prospects receive seeded prior residence histories, and supported known age and residence requirements are checked before they enter a slate. Sitting members with incomplete older residence records retain a labeled provisional game-profile route. Results name living candidates from saved slates, and a seat with no survivor records a vacancy when its term begins. Candidate timing, generated seat views, and unresolved qualifications remain labeled game profiles; state filing deadlines and primaries are not represented.
