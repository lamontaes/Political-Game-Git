title: Island villages open at their real Census size
---

The villages of Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands now come from the 2020 Census (158 villages instead of 44), and each opens with its real population, up to the 10,000 people the opening sample keeps for a place.
