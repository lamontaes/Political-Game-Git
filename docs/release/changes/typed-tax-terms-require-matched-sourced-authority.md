title: Typed tax terms require a matching enacted law and sourced authority
---

Typed tax-term questions now share one binding table across federal, state,
county and city levels. A saved proposal binds only when its question, enacted
law, public-government identity and sourced taxing authority match. Missing or
unsupported authority leaves the tax policy unavailable instead of supplying
an assumed power or taxable base.
