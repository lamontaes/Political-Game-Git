title: People connect a law to what it cost them the day they hear about it
---

When a law changes what a household pays, the person who pays the bills now connects the change to that law as soon as they hear a story about it, even if the household's bill already changed before they heard. Before, they could only make the connection on the monthly billing day, and someone who heard the story after the first changed bill could miss it entirely once the news stopped carrying the story. A person who heard the story before the law took effect also remembers hearing it, so their view of the law still moves when the first bill arrives.
