title: An argumentative defendant weighs contesting the charge over a plea
---

A person who treats a disagreement as a contest over who is right now counts
that habit as one reason to take a charge to trial instead of accepting the
plea on offer. It weighs beside the evidence, the sentence, their principles and
everything else they carry, so the same defendant with the same facts and no
such streak still takes the plea.
