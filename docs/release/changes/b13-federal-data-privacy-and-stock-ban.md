title: A national data privacy law costs businesses
---

A national data privacy law now costs the businesses in every town a share of
their yearly costs from the day it takes effect, and a repeal takes the cost
off. The size is a world's own draw between 0.1% and 0.6%, the European
compliance cost (marked as an estimate). The ban on stock trading by members of
Congress carries a sourced about-zero row: the game keeps no member holdings,
and the study finds members' trades matched or trailed the market.
