id: minors-and-childhood-friends
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Children are no longer offered adult meetings or party work
---

Before, an eight-year-old could be offered party work, see a party
organizer among their contacts, or ask an unrelated adult to meet, and
the "friend over the years" childhood story could cast a teacher as the
friend. Now party work and party organizers are for adults, a child asks
an adult outside the family through their parent or guardian, and the
childhood-friend story casts only people near the player's own age.
