title: The president lives in the White House and governors in their residences.
---

A sitting president now lives at the White House and works in the Oval Office, at the Resolute desk, in four lights. A sitting governor lives at the governor's residence and works in the governor's office. Other full-time officeholders work where their office sits: members of Congress in a congressional office, mayors in the mayor's office, sheriffs, prosecutors and county clerks in their own offices as those are painted.
