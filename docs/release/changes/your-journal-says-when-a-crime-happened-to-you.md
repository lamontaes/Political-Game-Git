title: Your Journal says when a crime happened to you
---

A crime in your Journal now reads as what happened to you: "You were
assaulted, and police in Fairbanks took the report." It used to repeat the
town's police log word for word. That read like a stranger's crime showing up
in your own life story. Crimes that did not happen to you stay in the news.
