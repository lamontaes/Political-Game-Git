title: Party bodies use recorded opinions instead of seeded stances
---

Party strategy and procedure questions have their own canonical subjects. The existing political belief formation pipeline saves their selected options and reasons alongside policy beliefs, while preserving older policy saves. Party bodies read those saved views. Members without a formed view abstain, and an all-abstaining review leaves the platform unchanged. The controlled person still chooses explicitly. Fresh party views require recorded reasons; party membership does not supply an opinion.
