title: When the executive wants a bill filed, they come to the member they want to carry it.
---

An executive who can't introduce a bill in their own name asks the member most inclined to carry it. That ask is now a scene for the member: the asker comes in with the bill, and the member can agree, decline, bargain for help finding the votes, or put it off.
