section: Changed
title: The trip to an abortion provider is the distance from where she lives
---

When a woman in the background weighs an unplanned pregnancy, the trip to the nearest provider is now her state's or territory's own distance (from county-level driving distances, before and since the recent closures, depending on the law in force), not one of two national averages.
