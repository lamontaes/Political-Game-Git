title: The trip to an abortion provider is as far as it really is where people live
---

When someone in town has an unplanned pregnancy, the distance to the nearest abortion provider now comes from where they live, before and after a restriction there, for all 56 places. The trip's cost, which their household pays, and whether they carry or end the pregnancy both follow from that distance.
