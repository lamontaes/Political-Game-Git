An official's acts in office and a senior's graduation result are history the one reader tells: the office day's acts (`office-act`, one kind with the state office's log, each act told once) and the school engine's graduation results (`school.graduation`). The Journal's office acts and school year's end, and the director's graduation reads, now come from the reader instead of the engines' tables. A player seated as a state legislator and played 120 days at three packet seeds (433 acts) has the same Journal as before, though no act reaches a line on either; the Journal tests that tell a school year's end give the same results as before.
