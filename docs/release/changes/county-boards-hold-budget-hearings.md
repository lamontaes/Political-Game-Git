title: Your county board holds a budget hearing and votes the property tax levy
---

Every year, about three months before its fiscal year opens, your county board
holds a budget hearing on one of its sitting days. The hearing sets the levy
the county's books call for, as a property tax rate on homes, and puts it on
the board's agenda. The members vote it at their next reading, each from their
own reasons, including how close the county's offices are to closing without a
budget.

A levy that passes becomes the property tax your household's home or rent is
assessed for, on the day it takes effect, and the county's budget for the year
expects exactly that levy. A levy the board turns down leaves the county
collecting at its current rate.
