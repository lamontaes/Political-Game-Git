title: Who counts as family or a housemate comes from the records, and the new pace is sourced
---

Whether a contact is a relative or someone who shares your home was decided by
matching the words in the sentence describing them, so rewording that sentence
would have silently changed who rings. It now reads the kinship and household
records directly. The pace at which relatives ring and new people turn up is
set from published averages: weekly contact with close family, and about one
new acquaintance a week for a typical adult, three days apart for the most
outgoing and twelve for the most reserved.
