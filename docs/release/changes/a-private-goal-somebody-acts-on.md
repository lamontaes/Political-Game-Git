title: People act on private goals of their own
---

Before, every generated person in the world was given a private goal when the
world was built — to learn something, to keep up with people, or to keep some
time for themselves — and no simulated person ever acted on one. The goals were
read only to vary what the player could say in a scene, so the player was the
only character in the world with an agenda.

Now, when somebody decides on their own whether to get back in touch after a
long gap, their own goal is weighed alongside their temperament. A person who
has been meaning to keep up with people is likelier to be the one who rings. It
leans on the decision rather than making it: the same person may still leave it,
and a person with no such goal decides exactly as they did before.

When they do ring, that is recorded as a step toward the goal, against the
message they actually sent. The goal stays live, because ringing one person is
not finishing it, and a goal nobody acted on records nothing at all.

Nothing about this is shown to the player. An agenda is something to notice in
what somebody keeps doing, not to read off a card.
