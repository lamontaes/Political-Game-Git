title: Keep life requests concrete and charge time for activities
---

Requests retain the person, task and any agreed condition when saved. Answer a favor first, then carry it out or withdraw the commitment. Dialogue and reading dialogue take no time; actual activities keep their disclosed duration. Small conversational choices no longer skip large stretches of life. Family labels and child conversations follow established relationships, and repeated completion cannot grant another reward.
