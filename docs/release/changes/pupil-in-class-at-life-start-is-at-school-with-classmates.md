title: A pupil whose life begins during the school day is at school with their classmates
---

Before, a child whose life began on a school morning was put at home, and
nothing in the world ever put a child in a room with a classmate, so the school
conversation could not open.

Now a pupil who begins in class is at their school, in the room with the pupils
in the same grade at the same school that the enrollment records hold. A school
with nobody else on record leaves the room empty, which is the truth. A weekend,
an evening and the summer are not class, and an adult is not placed at school.
