## Fixed

### A city that is a whole state no longer empties itself into a place that does not exist

In a place where the counted households are the entire state, such as Washington, D.C., people were leaving for a "rest of the state" that has no people, so the city lost 7.1 percent of its residents in one year. It now loses 3.6 percent; a smaller loss to other states remains and is being calibrated.
