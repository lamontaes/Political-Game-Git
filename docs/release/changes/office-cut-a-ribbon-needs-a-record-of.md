title: Cut the ribbon on a finished public work
---

A mayor or council member can cut the ribbon on a road, park, housing or school work the town paid a local firm for, in the weeks after the fiscal year it was built in ends. The ceremony is kept as a public record naming the firm and the official; the news line for it is not worded yet, and the finish day is the next budget year's start, not a recorded completion.
