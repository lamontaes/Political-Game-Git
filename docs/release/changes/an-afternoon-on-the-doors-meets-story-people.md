title: An afternoon on the doors takes the time you give it and meets neighbors as story people
impact: minor
---

A session on the doors now takes two, three or four hours, as the player chooses, the range of real volunteer canvass shifts. The neighbors who come to the door are kept as story people: a name, a look, where they were met and what they said, with no household or daily life added to the world. Somebody met a second time is written out in full. Each neighbor raises the problem where their state ranks worst among the states for someone in their circumstances, and decides from it, their party and their own traits whether they take to the candidate. Support moves by how much a candidate's own visit moves a canvassed voter in field research, and recognition is counted against the town's adults rather than only those written out.
