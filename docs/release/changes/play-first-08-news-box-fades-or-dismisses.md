title: The news and Journal boxes fade or close
---

The night's news no longer sits over the room from the first evening until you open it. It shows as a toast top right, fades by itself after about eight seconds, and goes at once when you click its close. The story stays in News either way. The Journal line at the bottom right is a toast too: it fades after the same time and closes with its own X.
