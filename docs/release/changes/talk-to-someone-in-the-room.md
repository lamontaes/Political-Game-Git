title: You can talk to a coworker or housemate the room shows
---

Before, picking a coworker in the room and choosing Talk opened a blank box
that said "This person is no longer here with you."

Now Talk opens the conversation with that person: their face, what you can
say, and their reply, through the same ordinary-talk writer the rest of the
game uses.
