title: Townspeople without a local job commute
---

When you meet someone who works but is not on a local employer's books, they now hold a real job at the place's outside employers, with regular paydays and tax withheld like anyone else. When they drop out of your life and go back into the town's counts, the job ends and the pay returns to the town's pool.
