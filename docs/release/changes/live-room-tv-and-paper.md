title: The TV and the newspaper at home change every day
---

The television in your room now shows a broadcast from a real station in your
world: its logo in a corner, the day's program or its own top story along the
bottom, and a ticker of the day's headlines, each naming the paper that ran it.
The newspaper on the table is a small front page with your town's paper's
masthead, the day's date and its lead story when it has one. Both change with
each day, and clicking either still opens the story to read.
