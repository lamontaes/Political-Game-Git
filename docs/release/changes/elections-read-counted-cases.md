id: elections-read-counted-cases
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A case you handled counts at the next election for the town's people too
---

When you help a resident with a problem (or turn them away), the people in town who are only counted, not yet met, now carry that into the voting booth. Their feeling toward you after a decided case is read the same way as for a person you have met, so a town where you helped many people leans toward you, and one where you turned them away leans against you, without anyone being filled in first.
