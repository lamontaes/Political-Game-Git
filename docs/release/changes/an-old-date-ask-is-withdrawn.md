title: A date asked for before you were with somebody does not go ahead after
---

Before, a date asked for just before becoming a couple with somebody else
could still be answered yes and go ahead. Now it is withdrawn when its
answer would come, the People screen says the date did not go ahead, and
nothing is put on the calendar.
