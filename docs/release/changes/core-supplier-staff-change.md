---
id: core-supplier-staff-change
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A hotel or public supplier keeps its contracts when a worker leaves
---

The year no longer stops when a worker named on a hotel's visitor lodging agreement or a public supplier's agreement leaves their job, moves away or dies. The agreement now follows whoever still does that work at the business, including people hired since, and it ends only when no one there does that work anymore.
