title: The title screen no longer says you have no saved lives while it is still opening them.
---

A long life can take a moment to open. The title screen and the saved games list now say your lives are being opened instead of "None yet". If they cannot be read, they say so, say that nothing was deleted, and offer to try again, instead of claiming the browser will not store anything.
