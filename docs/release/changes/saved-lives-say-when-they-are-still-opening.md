id: saved-lives-say-when-they-are-still-opening
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: The title screen no longer says you have no saved lives while it is still opening them.
---

A long life can take a moment to open. The title screen and the saved games list now say your lives are being opened instead of "None yet". If they cannot be read, they say so, say that nothing was deleted, and offer to try again, instead of claiming the browser will not store anything. The list of saved lives no longer opens every whole life to show it, so a very long life can no longer stop the list from appearing; if one cannot be read at that moment, it is listed as kept and the rest still show.
