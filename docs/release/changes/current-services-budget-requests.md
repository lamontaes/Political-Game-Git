title: Governor budget requests reach the legislature
---

The current governor's saved budget request can become a general appropriations bill at legislative intake. Its first year keeps the adopted amounts and dates; a missing second biennial year is labeled flat current services. Requests from departed governors lapse with a saved reason. Passage creates spending authority for the bill's final lines, without creating cash or forcing passage.
