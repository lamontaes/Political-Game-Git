title: Cost-law fixtures respect legislature session windows
---

Cost-law regression fixtures file on the existing legislature calendar before
their intended operative date. They retain the actual executive decision and
refuse a filing opportunity after the session closes. Chained fixtures can defer
the final observation advance without rewinding the world or bypassing a legal
gate. Budget cash and recorded-flow assertions retain their existing contract.
