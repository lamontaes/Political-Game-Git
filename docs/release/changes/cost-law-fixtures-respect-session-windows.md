title: Cost-law fixtures respect legislature session windows
---

Cost-law regression fixtures file on the existing legislature calendar before
their intended operative date. They retain the actual executive decision and
refuse a filing opportunity after the session closes. Multiple fixture bills
file at one existing opportunity and share a forward clock through scheduled hearings, floor readings and the actual executive desk.
Independent runs retain the single-law baseline without rewinding the world or
bypassing a legal gate. Budget cash and recorded-flow assertions retain their
existing contract.
