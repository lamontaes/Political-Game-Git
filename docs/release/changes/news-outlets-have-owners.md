title: News outlets have owners, and an owner decides for every outlet it holds at once
---

Every newsroom in a new life now belongs to somebody: a private equity chain, a
family newspaper group, a broadcast group, a nonprofit trust, or its own
independent publisher. The press desk shows who owns each outlet.

Owners review their holdings on their own schedule and act across everything
they own in one decision. A chain can eliminate newsroom jobs at all of its
outlets in one round, and the reporters who lose them are gone from the desk,
so there are fewer people to cover the places those papers served. A chain can
also buy an independent outlet, which then falls under its next decision.

Owners can already decide to share stories across their outlets, send them all
the same commentary to run, or combine newsrooms into one. Those decisions go on the
record, but they do not yet change what the outlets publish.

The owners and what each may do are data that a mod can replace. All of them
are fictional.
