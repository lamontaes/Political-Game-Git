title: Somebody who is not here is on the phone
---

Opening a conversation with somebody who was not in the room, such as an old
contact asking to meet, drew them in the conversation as if they were standing
there. The room draws only the people actually present, so the conversation
now does the same: somebody who is not here is marked as on the phone, their
face is drawn as a call rather than a presence, and nobody absent is added to
the people around the player.
