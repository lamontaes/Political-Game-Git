title: When a missed payment causes a quarrel at home, the person you wronged now comes to you about it.

A missed payment that strains the closest grown person in your home writes a quarrel, but the apology scene that should follow was refused every time since Nov 11, so the quarrel went to the journal with no talk. Now the person you wronged opens the talk, and it plays by how close you are, how often this has happened and how tight their money is.
