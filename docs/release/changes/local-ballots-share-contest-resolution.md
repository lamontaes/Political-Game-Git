section: Changed
title: Local and presidential elector ballots share the contest count
---

Town and county elections now count residents' recorded candidate views through
one contest resolver. Ward ballots admit their own residents. A candidate who
dies before election day stays on the ballot with no votes. Local results still
follow the office's rules for advancing from a primary and taking office.

Presidential elector-unit elections also use the shared count. Numbered
district ballots require recorded congressional residence; statewide
residence cannot supply it. Popular totals still require certification and
electoral ballots before the national constitutional count.
