title: Knocking on doors is a scene at the door
---

When you knock on doors for your campaign, the person who opens the door is someone with a stake in the race. You introduce yourself, say what you're running for and ask for their vote, and they answer from where they stand: some are with you, some aren't sold yet, and you can press them or thank them and move on.
