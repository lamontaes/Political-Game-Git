title: People in a scene no longer say the opposite of how you voted
---

After a vote, a reporter, a neighbor or a recall organizer now knows how you voted and how the vote went. You are no longer offered "I support it" after voting no, "They'll see when I vote" after the vote is over, or "it works" about a bill that failed, and nobody tells you that you voted for something you voted against. A reporter no longer asks why a bill cuts a program when it cuts nothing.
