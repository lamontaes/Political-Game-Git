title: A governor's briefing room when a disaster strikes the state
---

When a disaster strikes your state and you are the governor, the head of the department that leads for it meets you in your office and says what their department sees, and you decide whether to declare a state of emergency. If you leave it unanswered, the briefing meets you again a week later, and after 30 days from the disaster the chance to ask for federal help closes, with a public record that nothing was declared.
