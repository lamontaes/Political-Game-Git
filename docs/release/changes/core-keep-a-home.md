title: Losing a home makes people want to work
---

A person who is evicted or loses their home can now resolve to secure a home again, and that resolve makes them more likely to look for work and apply for jobs. Most people form it; some, by their nature, carry on as they were. The engine that ends an officeholder's town job when their term ends now does it the next morning, so the town no longer stops when a term ends on a day the officeholder worked.
