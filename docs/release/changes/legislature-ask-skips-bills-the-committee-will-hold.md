title: Sponsors ask for your vote only on bills that will reach the floor.
---

Before, a sponsor could ask you the evening before a floor day about a bill its committee was about to hold, so the answer you gave went nowhere. Now a bill on the week's floor goes before its committee the evening before, as a scheduled bill already did, and a bill the committee holds leaves the agenda before anyone is asked. Every ask is about a bill that comes to a vote the next day. A bill's second chamber, which votes the same day only if the first passes, is not asked about the evening before.
