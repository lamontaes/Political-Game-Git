title: Governors in California, Colorado, Illinois, Indiana and New Jersey now take office on the day their state sets.
---

The terms of the governors you meet in those five states now end on the right January day, the Monday after January 1 in California, the second Tuesday in Colorado, the second Monday in Illinois and Indiana, and the third Tuesday in New Jersey.
