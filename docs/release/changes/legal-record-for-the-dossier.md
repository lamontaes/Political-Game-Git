title: The player's court cases, sentences and clemency requests in one record
---

The player's own legal record is ready for the Legal tab of their dossier. It
lists each case once the player is charged, with where it stands: the hearing
date, a plea entered, a retrial after a hung jury, or how it ended. It lists
each sentence with its term and whether it is being served today, and each
clemency request with every answer given so far. It offers a plea or a
request only when the court or the clemency authority would take one, and
otherwise gives the reason in the same words the action would. Reading the
record spends no game time and records nothing.
