title: The night's paper prints only news that is still news on its date; older stories stay in the News archive.
---

The first paper used to run votes and races from months before its date as if they were today's news. Now the paper prints only stories from the last 30 days, the window the news engine itself uses. What you heard earlier is still listed in News.
