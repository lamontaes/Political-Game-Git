title: Fund public transit service with a tax you pass, and see what it delivered.
---

As an Alaska state legislator you can now carry a service decision all the way
through. File an added transit service appropriation and a tax, move both
through the legislature, and let the tax take effect and collect real public
cash. Then request the service and watch each period come due. The Transit page
shows which periods were delivered and paid, how many vehicle-service hours the
money bought, and what is left in the public account. It also says plainly what
the game does not model, such as ridership or travel times. If no collected cash
is available, the page tells you before you ask for service, and a refused period
pays nothing. Taxes and public receipts now has its own page under Politics,
where you can follow a tax bill through its legislative steps. The legislative
sittings and votes for these bills are fictional game content and are labeled as
such.
