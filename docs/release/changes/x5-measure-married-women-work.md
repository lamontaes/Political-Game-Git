title: Each state now tracks how many married women work, and home broadband moves it
---

Every state, D.C. and Puerto Rico now keeps the share of married women who are working
or looking for work, starting from the 2024 Census survey. Each point more of homes
with broadband than a state began with raises it about 0.07%, and losing broadband
lowers it the same way.
