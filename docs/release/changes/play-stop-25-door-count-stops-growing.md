title: Knocking on doors keeps counting after every door in town has been knocked
---

Once a campaign had knocked on every door in town, Knock on doors stopped adding to its door count and then grayed out with no reason. The canvass now goes round again, the doors knocked longest ago first, and every door knocked counts. A grayed campaign action (Hold an event, Ask for an interview or Call donors) now says why on its button.
