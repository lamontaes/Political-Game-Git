title: Ask people you know to help your campaign
---

You can now ask people you know to help your campaign. Each person answers from
what they think of you, how they are related to you, the hours they have free
and their own temperament. Those who agree join your campaign staff and add to
your reach when they are on shift. A person who says no is remembered as
having said no.
