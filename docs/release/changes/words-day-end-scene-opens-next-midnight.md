title: A scene from something that happened during the day never opens at midnight.
---

Something that happens during your day (a call, a filing, a law you hear about) now opens its scene the next morning, when your day starts, and the scene's first line names that morning's date, the same day the clock shows. No scene stops the clock at 12:00 AM, even in a place whose hours the game doesn't list.
