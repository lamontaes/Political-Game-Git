title: Party work, weekly campaign plans and rival campaigns that act on their own.
---

Local party organizers now come back with real work: an organization meeting,
a canvass or a phone shift from home, a talk about running for office, a
fundraiser, a request for the organization's support, or a town hall. Each one
is on a real evening with a real host, travel is shown before you go, and you
can go briefly without changing what happens. The people you meet stay in your
life, and meeting the same volunteer again makes them a regular contact.

Once you are a candidate you plan each week: field shifts, fundraising calls
and advertising, with the channel, amount and area chosen by you. A plan the
committee cannot afford is refused and nothing is spent. Without staff you plan
alone; with staff you see their proposals. Sessions you cannot get to can be
let go, so a busy week never stops time.

Your opponents now campaign too, once a week, with their own money: field
events, paid messages, fundraising and requests for party support. What they
do in public reaches you.
