title: A classmate or neighbor answers from who they are and how you stand
---

When the player asks a classmate to split the unfinished part of a project, or
asks a neighbor to go to a posted public meeting, the answer now depends on the
person's own temperament and on how they stand with the player from their side:
warmth, trust, respect, what they feel bound to, and anything unsettled between
them. Besides yes and no, a classmate can say they need to see what is left
before agreeing, and a neighbor can say they have not decided, or suggest the
player go instead. Those answers leave the question open, so the player can
take it up again. The tone of every line in these two conversations also comes
from that same reading rather than from a count of good and bad exchanges.
