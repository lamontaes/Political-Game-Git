title: A worker whose pay a law raised now knows the law did it
---

When a minimum wage law raised town pay, the paycheck rose, but nothing
recorded that the law had reached the worker, so no one formed a view of the
lawmakers or told a friend. Now each raise a law made reaches the worker it
raised, with the monthly difference, and their partner hears of it at home. In
Abbeville, Mississippi, a federal raise lifted 14 workers' pay by $260 to $650
a month, and all 14 were reached, along with 5 partners.
