title: Floor debates are heard before the vote, in the voice of the body
---

A member who sits on a board now hears the floor debate on the meeting day, before the roll is called. A small town board speaks plainly by first name, a council has the chair call on people, and a state or territory chamber speaks formally. The speaker gives a reason in plain words, and standing your ground against a speaker who presses leaves a strained tie that later talk remembers.
