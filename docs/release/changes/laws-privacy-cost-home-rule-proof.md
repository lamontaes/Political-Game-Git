title: A consumer data privacy law now costs the town's businesses
---

When a state's consumer data privacy law is in force, every business in town
that employs people sets aside a share of its operating costs to comply, and
pays it each month. Repeal the law and the cost stops. A state's home-rule
grant also decides whether a town council may draft ordinances on matters the
state has not settled.
