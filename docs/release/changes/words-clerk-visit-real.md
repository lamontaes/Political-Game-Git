title: The clerk answers in connected sentences
---

At the clerk's counter you now ask for the papers on a first visit, and only a return with your petition says you brought it, read from your filing record. The clerk answers in one turn of connected sentences and names the place's own council or board. Filing is a reply you say ("Let's do it. Where do I sign?"), and the transcript shows only the lines you chose.
