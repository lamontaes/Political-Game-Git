title: Missing office salaries use sourced peer estimates
---

An office with no published annual salary uses comparable sourced office
salaries instead of a random point in a range. The estimate uses the state's
region and recorded household-income comparison; where that income is unread,
it uses the same-office average. Published salaries and enacted salary terms
keep their existing readers.
