title: A relative put out of their home tells you what happened, from the records.
---

When a relative or a housemate is put out of their home, they tell you when, what they owed or how many months behind they were, and, when you ask, where they are going (a relative's home, a shelter bed, nowhere) and the job loss behind it, all from the housing and jobs records, in several wordings. They no longer repeat the same figure in their answer or close with a stock goodbye.

A relative put out who takes shelter at your own home now says so ("I'm coming to stay with you all"), a relative put out tells you first before any other news.
