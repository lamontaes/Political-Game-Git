title: Crime victims decide whether to call the police
---

Whether an ordinary local crime reaches the police is now each victim's own
decision instead of a fixed share. A victim weighs what was done to them
against the trouble of reporting, whether it has happened to them before,
their own past with the police, their temperament and their age. The same
victim in the same circumstances always decides the same way.
