title: A household that just paid its rent or mortgage buys its month of groceries and everyday goods
---

A household whose rent or mortgage came due on the same day as its everyday shopping used to set the home's payment aside a second time after paying it, so it bought far less than it could afford, and in a tight month nothing at all. It now sets aside only what its home will cost by its next shopping day beyond the pay it has coming before then, so the home still comes first. The town's shops sell what households can really pay for, and the monthly money review records what the household held and set aside when a month falls short.
