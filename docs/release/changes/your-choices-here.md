title: The room's "What's happening" button is now "Your choices here," and you can reach it
---

In a room, the button that opens who is with you and what you can do now
reads "Your choices here" instead of "What's happening." It also sits above
the bar at the bottom of the screen: before, the bar covered its lower half,
and a click on it could land on the bar instead. Once the choices are open,
"Return to the room" sits clear of the bar too.
