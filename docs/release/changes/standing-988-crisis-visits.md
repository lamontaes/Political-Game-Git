title: A state's 988 crisis money now reaches people who need it.
---

A state's standing crisis-response money is now carried out by a health department, clinic or hospital already working there, never by an unrelated company. Once the provider is paid, a resident who is acutely or seriously unwell decides whether to call the crisis team. Another adult at home counts against calling. The visit counts as service only after it has taken place. Money paid to an organization that cannot run a crisis team buys no calls.
