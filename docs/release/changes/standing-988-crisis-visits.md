section: Added
title: A state's 988 crisis money now reaches people who need it.
---

A state's standing crisis-response money is now carried out by a health department, clinic or hospital already working there, never by an unrelated company. Once the provider is paid, a resident who is acutely or seriously unwell decides whether to call the crisis team. A family member at home with a recorded duty to look after them counts against calling. If no state provider can run a crisis team, the money is not committed and no provider is invented. The visit counts as service only after it has taken place. Money paid to an organization that cannot run a crisis team buys no calls.
