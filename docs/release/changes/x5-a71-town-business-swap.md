title: A town business borrows on a credit line sized for its trade, and shop sales catch up with the town's pay over years
---

Town businesses now borrow on credit lines sized for their trade: a restaurant's line covers
about five weeks of its sales and a realty office's about four months, from what small firms
really hold. Shop sales in town also follow a change in the town's pay more gently, catching up
over a few years instead of all at once.
