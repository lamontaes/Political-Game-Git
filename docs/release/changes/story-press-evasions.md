title: Reporters hear the stock lines real briefings use.
---

When a reporter presses you, your put-offs and stalls now include the stock lines real briefings use ("I'm not going to get ahead of the vote." "It's too early to say.").

A speaker at a hearing's public comment can now greet the members first and close on an ask, as real council comment does.
