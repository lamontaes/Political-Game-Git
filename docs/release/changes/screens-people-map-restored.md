section: Changed
title: The People map is back to round faces in colored rings, with lines you can see
---

Each person on the People map is a round face in a ring colored by how you know them: family, household, friends, work or politics. A line in the same color runs to you, and people who live with you are joined to each other by a dashed line. Names sit on the far side of each face, so no line runs through them, and the key shows each color as a line.
