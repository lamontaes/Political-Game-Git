title: Four more kinds of scene can start in play.
---

A person released from prison comes home to the family waiting for them. After a floor debate, the member who argued the other side comes to you about how it was put. When the press carries a bribe taken by an official, someone close to them asks to their face whether it is true. The day after a party asks you to run, an officeholder you know weighs in on whether you should.
