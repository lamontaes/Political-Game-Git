title: An offer of work now says it is waiting for your answer
---

Asking an employer for work writes the offer straight away, and until now only
the career panel that made the request ever said so. Everywhere else the day
said "You do not hold a job or an office right now", which was true and left out
the part that mattered: there was an offer on the table waiting to be accepted
or refused. A player could ask for a job on their first day, let three months
pass checking regularly, and conclude that nothing had ever come of it.

The day's own summary now names an offer that has not been answered, and it
appears under what is waiting on you alongside everything else that needs a
decision. It is still not a job, and it is not counted as one until you accept
it.
