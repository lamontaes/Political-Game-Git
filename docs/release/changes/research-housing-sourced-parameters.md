section: Changed
title: Evictions follow more places' own law, and one more territory's home prices follow its own price index.
---

In 21 more states and territories, the eviction notice, the right to pay what is owed and stay, the wait for a hearing and the days before removal now come from that place's own statute, so a renter behind on rent gets the time to pay the law there gives, or none where the law gives none. Puerto Rico's home prices now follow its own house price index instead of general prices.
