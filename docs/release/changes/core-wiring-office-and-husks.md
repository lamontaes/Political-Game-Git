title: Town offices answer residents, and cause groups spend what they raise
---

A resident who goes to the town office about a cause now hears back within a week and can go back again. Relatives living out of town no longer act in town. Cause groups spend their donations on ads that reach people. People met later in the game have health records, and appointments no longer skip everyone under 25.
