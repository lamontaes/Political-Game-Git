title: Each world's 2024 vote is its own
---

Each new world opens with its own 2024 presidential vote: a national swing and a swing in each state move every state's and county's result, and the turnout and the town's share of citizens are drawn around the published figures. The races outside the town, the officials' sense of their state and district, the town's starting lean in a campaign, recalls, ballot measures and the closest states of the last election all read the world's vote.
