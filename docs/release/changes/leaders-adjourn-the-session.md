title: A legislature ends its session when its leaders decide
---

Before, a legislature in play sat until the last day its law allowed, and
only Kentucky's sessions ever ended. A new law that waits a number of days
after the session counted from that legal limit.

Now each chamber's majority caucus adjourns the regular session once the
budget has passed both chambers and none of its own members' bills is still
pending. Bills other members carry die with the session where the state's
rules say pending bills do. The legal limit still ends a session the leaders
have not closed, and it now comes from each state's own limit wherever the
game has read one. A new law's waiting period counts from the day the leaders
adjourned.
