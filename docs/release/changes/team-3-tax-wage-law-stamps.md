title: State tax payments and minimum-wage pay terms retain their governing laws
---

State income-tax assessments, paid-leave premiums and their positive allocated payments retain the governing law and actual assessment and transfer identifiers. State and city minimum-wage pay terms retain the law at their own jurisdiction. Existing federal and teacher-floor attribution remains intact. This changes saved provenance without changing tax rates, wage floors or payment amounts.
