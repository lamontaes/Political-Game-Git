title: The President's address reads like a real inaugural
---

The opening address no longer reads out statistics. It follows the shape of real inaugural addresses: the oath, what the President believes, the country's place in its long story, the year told in words, the people, then a turn to Congress, the Vice President, the governors and legislatures, and the people themselves, with hope before the close. It runs eleven paragraphs, one for each scene of the opening montage. Lines that read clunky are gone, including "government closest to the people", "everyone who did, and everyone who did not", both "Too many people" and the "To every family listening" close.
