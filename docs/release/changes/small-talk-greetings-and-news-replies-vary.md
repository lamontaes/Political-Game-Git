id: small-talk-greetings-and-news-replies-vary
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: People greet you again and hear your news in their own words
---

Someone you already talked with no longer answers every greeting with "Hi
again." They may use your name, mention that you talked earlier the same day,
sound glad to see you when you get along, or a little short when things are
tense, and children talk like children. Telling someone news they had not heard
gets one of several plain answers and often a question back, instead of the
same line each time. A person avoids repeating the words they used with you
lately. News you mention no longer ends with two periods.
