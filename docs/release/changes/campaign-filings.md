title: The campaign screen shows what the committee must file
---

The campaign screen has a Filings section. After you file for office, it
shows when the committee's first statement is due with the state and lets
you file it on the spot. A filed statement becomes a public record, and one
not filed by its deadline is shown as missed. Kentucky uses its own
statement of spending intent, due within 5 days. Every other state asks for
a statement of organization within 5 to 15 days, a placeholder drawn once
per state until the real rules are researched. In Minnesota, trying to run
a second time for the office you are already running for is now refused in
the words of the state's one-committee rule.
