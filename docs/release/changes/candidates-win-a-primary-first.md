title: Candidates for Congress and the state legislature win their party's primary first
---

Each state now holds its primaries on its real 2026 date, using its own
method: a primary for each party, or one primary for all parties that sends
the top two or four on, as in California, Washington and Alaska. Runoffs
follow where the state holds them, such as Texas in May and North Carolina
when the runner-up asks. Only the winners reach the November ballot, so a
sitting member can lose their own party's nomination, and a seat a party is
favored to win can draw candidates nobody recruited.

A state law passed in play can move its primary or change its method. The new
rule governs the next election whose filing opens after it takes effect.
