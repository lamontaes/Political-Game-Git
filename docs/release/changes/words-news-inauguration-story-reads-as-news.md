title: The first night's news reports the swearing-in as a news story
---

On Inauguration Day the news no longer says the President "won the election" and no longer reprints the whole speech. The story's headline is the swearing-in. It opens with who took the oath and when, gives the country's unemployment rate and pay growth, and quotes one line of the address. In other years the story is the address to Congress.
