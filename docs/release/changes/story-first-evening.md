title: The first evening is told, not listed.
---

The President's inaugural on January 20 is an inaugural again, given in the shapes real inaugurals take, and it changes with the President and the world. The household's first word on it, the loading screen's story of your life, the Journal's first evening and the news of the address are told the same way. The evening opens on what is on the news, not on the date. A law that changed nothing no longer prints as a story.
