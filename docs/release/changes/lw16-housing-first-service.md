section: Fixed
title: Housing-first assistance reaches residents after an eviction
impact: patch
---

Funded housing-first programs now offer a recorded assistance appointment to residents whose eviction ended their primary occupancy and who still have no replacement home. Requests use the existing service decision, attendance, and law-exposure path in all 56 places. Assistance has no treatment or sobriety prerequisite. The completed appointment records service hours; it does not claim a housing placement. Appointment timing is a labeled estimate with a research-ledger entry.
