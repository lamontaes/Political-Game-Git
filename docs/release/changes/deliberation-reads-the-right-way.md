title: Someone who thinks things through no longer behaves like someone who acts on impulse
---

People described as acting on impulse were the ones insisting on an answer before they would agree to anything, and people described as thinking things through were the ones going along with a change on the spot. Each person's card said the opposite of how they behaved. They now behave the way they are described, in the places their temperament is read: arrangements being renegotiated, plans to study together, family plans, and keeping in touch.
