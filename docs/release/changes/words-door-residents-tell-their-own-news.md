title: People at the door with no view on the race tell you what's new in their life
---

A resident with no strong view on the race now tells you what is going on with them this year when you ask: a new job they just started, or a new baby at home. You can ask what they do there, or the baby's name, and they answer from their own life. Someone who just gave you their vote answers kindly even with nothing on their mind, instead of shutting the door.
