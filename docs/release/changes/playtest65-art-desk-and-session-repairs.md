title: Clearer art review and reliable return to title
---

Art requests have clearer categories and stable labels, with current versions and review decisions kept together. Removed revisions stay removed without bringing their earlier images or older alternatives back into review. Incoming revisions and clothing preparation stay with the art team; only an explicit handoff puts a finished revision in your review queue. Verified artwork uses follow the selected game version; artwork outside the checked consumers remains unconfirmed.

Return to title waits for saving and preserves earlier saves when unsaved changes are discarded. New lives use contextual household history, clearer optional questions, readable dates and correctly owned invitations. State information appears together, hometown lists scroll continuously, and windows fit smaller screens.
