id: gov-bribe-unrewarded-surfaces
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A bribe that buys nothing can come back on the official.
---

When an official takes a bribe to vote against a bill and then votes for it, the person who paid loses trust in them, and may tell someone, who may take it to the press. Before, a taken bribe could only come out if a falling-out happened for some other reason.
