title: Crimes now happen to households in town
---

Each month a town's households are burgled, robbed, assaulted and robbed of property at the national rates, scaled up or down by what is happening in town, such as unemployment and police staffing. Renters, families with children and lower-income households are hit more often, and voters react to the crimes that actually happened to people in their town.
