title: The Government screen follows the approved look
---

Government now sets each part on a gold-edged header strip: a body's seats as large numbers in each party's color, its recent votes typed out with the result and count, and the executive as a bust with the name large and the party and post under it. The party bar, the seat picture and the colored vote bars are gone.
