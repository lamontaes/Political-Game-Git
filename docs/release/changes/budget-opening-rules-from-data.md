---
id: budget-opening-rules-from-data
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Budget openings read D.C.'s shape and the median reserve from the data
---

A state NASBO reports no rainy-day balance for, such as Georgia, now opens
with the median share of the states NASBO does report, computed from the
table and marked as estimated from the average. Before, the share was typed
in by hand. A state whose Census state column is empty because one government
serves as both state and local government now reads its local column by that
rule. Before, the District of Columbia was named in the code.
