title: Townspeople you haven't met can take up a cause when they lose their job
---

When a business lays someone off or closes, the people who lose their jobs can now come away with a cause to prove themselves or to hold the employer to account, even if you have never met them. Whether they do follows their own temperament, the same way it does for the people you know. If you meet one of them later, they already carry that cause and act on it.
