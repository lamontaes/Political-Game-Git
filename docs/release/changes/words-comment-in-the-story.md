title: What you tell a reporter, or that you declined, is in the paper's story.
---

When a reporter asks you about something you did, such as a bill you just introduced, your answer now goes into the paper's story of it ('Connie Gallagher told reporters: "People who work two jobs and still come up short. It's written for them."'). If you refuse or walk off, the story says so ("Timothy McKay declined to comment."). The reporter's scene about a newly introduced bill now plays: it names the reporter's own outlet and opens on the bill.
