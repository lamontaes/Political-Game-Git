impact: patch
section: Changed
title: What people think of officials is a view they hold
---

When a law reaches someone, what they make of the officials behind it is now
decided the way every other political view is: what the law did to them is
weighed against the view of that official they already hold, and the result is
kept as their own view. A second law that cuts the other way can leave them
torn, rather than adding points to a tally. What they say about an official in
conversation, how a town votes on a candidate, and how a legislator reads the
people a law reached all read that same view. Saves made before this change
still load and keep what their people had already decided.
