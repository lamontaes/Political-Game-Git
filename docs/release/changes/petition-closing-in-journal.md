title: The Journal says how your petition closed, with the count
---

When a petition you filed closes on its recorded deadline, your Journal now says
whether it qualified and how many valid signatures were counted against how
many the place required. Before, the closing was recorded but never reached
your Journal, because the filer was not listed as having taken part. The count
comes from the signatures people were asked for and decided to give, as before.
