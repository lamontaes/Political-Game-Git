title: People a law costs band together, and residents contact officials and attend meetings
---

When a law costs at least six residents of a town a tenth of a month's pay or
more, a group forms there against it, and the people hit hardest are the most
likely to join. The group presses legislators to change the law and works
against the candidates its members blame in the next town election.

Each year, about a quarter of a town's adults contact an elected official and
a little more than a quarter attend a meeting of the town's government. People
who react quickly, and people with strong feelings about an official, do it
more often.
