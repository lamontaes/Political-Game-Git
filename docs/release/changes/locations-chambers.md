title: Councils, county boards and state houses meet in rooms that fit them.
---

A small town's council now meets in a small council room and a big city's in a large chamber. A rural county's commission meets in a plain commission room. A state's lower house meets in its own state house chamber, apart from the senate. Committee hearings have a wood-paneled hearing room, and lobbying a legislator happens in the capitol hallway.
