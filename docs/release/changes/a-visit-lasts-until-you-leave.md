title: When you go somewhere, you stay there until you leave
---

After you traveled to a place and talked to someone there, the game put you back in your own home, with the people you had just left standing in your kitchen. Now you stay where you went, through talks and waiting, until you go somewhere else, choose Home in Travel, or the day ends. The card's clock also moves when a talk ends.
