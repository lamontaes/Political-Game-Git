title: A scene is no longer dropped to the Journal because the season already had six of its kind
---

Scenes of one kind used to stop after six in a season, whatever they were about. Now a scene is held only when it repeats a record that nothing has changed since, so a second bill, a second law in the news or a second invitation about a new cause gets its own scene. In four played years that adds a handful of scenes (a desk visit, a stop in the street, a donor's ask, three invitations) and takes none away.
