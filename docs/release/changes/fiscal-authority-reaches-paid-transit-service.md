title: Rural transit funding reaches paid service
---

An enacted rural transit appropriation creates one saved spending authority
from its effective date. Executive commitments and transit payments share
its amount. Payments still require cash.

A posted operating payment records exact modeled vehicle-service hours at
the game's provisional cost and publishes those hours. Maintenance payments
do not create service hours. A separate modeled local ride is recorded in
one existing resident's history. Only a later encounter with a public report
supports that resident's private reaction to the funding decision. No car
status, travel-time change, or election effect is inferred.
