title: Rivals run attack ads on an officeholder's record
---

A challenger running against someone with a record in office now spends part of their ad money showing it: the votes and broken promises the voters they reach disagree with. How much depends on the challenger's temperament. When you run for re-election, the ads against you name the real votes you cast, and the voters who saw them weigh those votes. Before, no rival ever ran an attack ad.
