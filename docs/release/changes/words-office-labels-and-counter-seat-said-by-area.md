title: Town job titles, vote labels and the filing counter now say the right place and office.
---

A clerk in a town is now a town clerk, in a township a township clerk, and in a city the city is left off instead of said twice. Your office lists a vote as Yes, No or Abstained, a charter township's board is the township's own, and the clerk at the filing counter names the whole body and the state for a Senate race instead of "the senate in" your town.
