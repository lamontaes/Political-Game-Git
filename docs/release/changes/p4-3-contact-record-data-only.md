id: p4-3-contact-record-data-only
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The contact rows and endorsement requests show record data only
---

A party chapter's contact row and the endorsement requests a former official
receives now show what the records hold and control names, with no written
sentences. A contact row has the person's name, who they are to you, the day
you were last in touch, how you can reach them (a way that is closed now is
drawn dashed), and a request waiting on you as its name, day and purpose with
Accept, Decline, Another day and Offer. An endorsement request has the
candidate's name, the saved request, the people there by name and the recorded
replies as its buttons. Raw record numbers no longer appear on screen.
