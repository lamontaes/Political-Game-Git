title: The paper's police log says how many more than usual and what rose most, and gets less room in an ordinary month
---

A month's police log is read against the town's own usual month. A month over it says by how many reports and what kind rose most; an ordinary month is one short line; a month well under the usual loses its place in the Briefs column to other items.
