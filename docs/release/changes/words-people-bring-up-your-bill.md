title: People who read about your bill bring it up with you.
---

When the paper prints the vote on a bill you sponsored, the people who read it bring it up when they talk with you ("I read in the Windsor Leader about transportation."), even after newer stories from the same paper. A bill that failed is now remembered by what it would have changed.
