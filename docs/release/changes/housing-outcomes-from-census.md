title: Rent stabilization and multifamily zoning move the share of homes rented, renters' moves and new apartment buildings
---

Every state, D.C. and Puerto Rico now keeps three housing figures from the
Census Bureau's 2024 surveys: the share of homes that are rented, the share
of renters who moved in the past year, and new apartments in buildings of
five or more per 10,000 people. A rent stabilization law cuts the share of
homes rented 15% a year after it takes effect and renters' moves 20% at
once, as San Francisco's did. A law allowing small multifamily homes in
single-family zones doubles new apartments in large buildings a year later.
Each moves the other way when a law the place began with is repealed.
