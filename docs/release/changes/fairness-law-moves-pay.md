title: A fairness law now sets the pay of men partnered with men
---

A state fairness law, or a town's own ordinance, used to change nothing
anyone was paid. Now a man whose partner is a man is hired at 2.7% below his
job's rate where no such law covers him, and at the full rate where one does,
which is the gap such laws closed in the research. The rate is set when he is
hired, so a new law or a repeal reaches later hires. Women, and everyone else,
are paid the same either way. In one opening, two partners in Charlotte, North
Carolina were each paid 2.6% less a pay period than they would be under a
fairness law.
