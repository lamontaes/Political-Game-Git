title: Farmland protection holds back as much building as the research found
---

A law protecting farmland from development used to close every acre of farmland to new houses, so a town's single-family starts fell about a quarter. It now holds back the share of farmland's loss that preservation programs did in the research (47% of the farmland built on), so starts fall about a tenth, from the day the law takes effect.
