title: A bill's page keeps its vote on screen, and Play as lists only offices this world runs
---

A bill opened from Your office or Bills now shows where it stands, the staff and your vote in its first column, and each column scrolls inside itself, so the vote buttons no longer fall off the foot of the sheet. Your own members' votes read as a name and a vote. An empty Staff heading is gone. In Advanced, Play as no longer offers other states' legislators and governors, whose offices had nothing in force.
