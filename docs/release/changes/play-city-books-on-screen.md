title: Budgets shows what your town has actually taken in, paid out and holds.
---

Politics → Budgets now opens on your town's own budget. Beside the adopted plan it shows what the town's treasury has actually taken in, what it has paid out and the cash it holds, all read from its books. If the town has paid out more than its whole year's budget, the screen says so.
