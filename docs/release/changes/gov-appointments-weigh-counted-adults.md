title: A town's department heads are chosen from the whole town.
---

The fire chief, city clerk and the town's other department heads were chosen from the few people the game tracks one by one, plus three adults drawn at random. Now the mayor or council weighs the whole town's best suited adults for each post, and the one they name from the wider population becomes a full person with their real job and household.
