title: Unplanned pregnancies happen across the whole town, not only to the people you know
---

The households in town that are counted rather than named now have unplanned pregnancies by the same rule as the people you know: the same exposure, the same weighing of the law and the miles to a provider, and the same trip paid from their own cash. A pregnancy carried to term adds a birth to the town's count.
