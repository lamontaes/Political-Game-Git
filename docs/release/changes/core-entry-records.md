title: Everyone and everything new in the world has a record of arriving
---

A person who comes into full view, an official who takes a seat, or a business, group or office that opens after the first day now leaves a record of it: who or what arrived, why, and where. In the town's own places it is a public record. The town's counts and the news no longer find people and businesses appearing from nowhere.
