title: A fiscal note follows a state wage-tax rate bill
---

The Tax Work draft preview shows the player's selected proposed rate. After
filing, the note shows the rate in the matching saved levy. The note keeps the future taxable wage base and aggregate
cash change from current law UNKNOWN while those inputs are unavailable. A
lawful 0% rate would assess zero tax on each covered wage amount once operative,
without calling an unknown change in total receipts zero. A recorded policy
supplies the effective date after enactment. Reading the note neither files a
bill nor moves public money.
