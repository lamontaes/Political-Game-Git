---
id: congress-races-counted
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Every race for Congress records its nominees and a vote count
---

Congress's elections chose each seat's winner from a national total, so no
race for a House or Senate seat had a ballot, a named field or a count anyone
could read. Now each seat whose nominees come from different parties gets a
ballot 45 days before election day, and on election day it is counted from
that seat's own voters: the seat's recorded 2024 ballots split by its
starting partisan lean, with the incumbent's usual edge and a share for
candidates in neither party. The winner of that count is the member who
takes the seat. The world report lists the place's own state's races in full
and sums up the rest of the country.
