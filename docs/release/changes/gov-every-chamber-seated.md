title: Every state, D.C. and territory now has its whole legislature, governor and statewide officers seated.
---

Every other state, D.C. and each territory now opens with every seat of its legislature, its governor and its statewide officers held by a named official with a career behind them, in the party on record for the seat. Each state's and D.C.'s legislators and governor come up on their own calendars, and the voting engine seats each winner in the office as someone from that state; a governor who loses is replaced by the winner, not kept on. The territories' races and the statewide officers below governor aren't run yet, so those officials stay on past their terms for now. The old handful of eight sample members per chamber is gone. These officials are names on the record, filled in as full people only when the player meets or opens one. Those legislatures don't pass laws yet; that comes next.
