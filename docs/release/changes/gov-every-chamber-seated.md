title: Every state, D.C. and territory now has its whole legislature, governor and statewide officers seated.
---

Every other state, D.C. and each territory now opens with every seat of its legislature, its governor and its statewide officers held by a named official with a career behind them. Their seats come up on their own calendars, and the voting engine seats each winner as someone from that state. The old handful of eight sample members per chamber is gone. These officials are names on the record, filled in as full people only when the player meets or opens one. Those legislatures don't pass laws yet; that comes next.
