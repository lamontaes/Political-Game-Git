section: Changed
title: Every meeting, floor vote and bill on your desk now stops you, and nothing is voted or signed for you
---

When you hold a seat, every meeting of your body, every floor vote and every bill that reaches your desk now stops you by default. Before, only your own bills and close votes stopped you, and the rest were voted for you from your character's views, shown as "Decided for me" in Your office. A governor or mayor with the desk stop off had bills signed or vetoed for them the next day. If you switch a stop off, you are recorded absent for that vote and bills wait on your desk until you act or their deadline passes, when they become law unsigned. A member of Congress who spends the week at home in the district is recorded absent too. Pressing "Decide for me" at a stop still votes your character's own position, and staying silent at a stop still abstains.
