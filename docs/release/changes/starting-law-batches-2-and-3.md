title: States begin with their real tax, bail, energy, voting and privacy laws
---

Every state, D.C. and territory now begins the game with the law it really
had at the start of 2026 on ten more questions: whether its income tax rises
in steps, whether it has ended cash bail, whether its cities may run broadband
or set their own minimum wage, whether it charges drivers by the mile, whether
it bars cities from banning gas hookups, whether voting rights return
automatically after a sentence, whether it limits abortion after a set point,
whether bottles carry a deposit, and whether residents can see, correct and
delete the data companies hold about them. Where a place's law couldn't be
confirmed, the game treats it as unknown rather than guessing.
