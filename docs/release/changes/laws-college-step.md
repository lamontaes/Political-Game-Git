title: Graduating seniors now go on to college, and more state higher education spending helps them finish
---

Each high school graduate in a town now enrolls in college or does not, and an enrolled graduate completes a bachelor's degree or does not, decided by their own record and household income against the national college-going and six-year completion rates, which differ a little from world to world. When the state spends more on higher education, enrolled graduates in the town are a little more likely to finish, and a graduate who finishes is hired and paid as a college graduate once their four years are up, while one who enrolled and did not finish is hired as someone with some college.
