section: Added
title: A passed city, county or state sales tax taxes what a household really spends
---

A passed sales tax, where the state lets that level levy one, now taxes the food
and bills a household actually pays each month, to the person who paid, on the
day the month is settled. A month the household could not pay for is not taxed,
and spending from before the tax took effect is never taxed.
