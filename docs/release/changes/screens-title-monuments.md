title: The title shows the Capitol, the White House and the Lincoln Memorial in their lights, not conventions or rallies.
---

The title screen now turns through the national monuments only: the U.S. Capitol in the morning, at midday, at night and in the rain, the White House at midday and the Lincoln Memorial at night. Conventions, rallies, chamber floors and the Oval Office no longer appear there.
