title: The grown-ups in your town go to work
---

Before, most of the adults living around you had no job at all. In a new
game in Lexington, 17 of about 90 working-age neighbors worked anywhere,
mostly at the market and the schools.

Now nearly everyone of working age in your town has a real job, with a title,
a workplace in town and weekly hours. The mix of work follows your own
county: a farm town has farmhands and truck drivers, a city has more
accountants and nurses. Every town also has the people a political life runs
through: a city clerk and a planner at City Hall, a principal at each school,
a pastor at each church, police officers and firefighters, a nurse at the
hospital, a community organizer, a union representative, staff at each party
office and a few campaign workers. A few neighbors are still in school,
retired, at home with small children or looking for work.
