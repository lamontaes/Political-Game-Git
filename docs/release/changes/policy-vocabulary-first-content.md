The first policy content ships as a loaded pack: 13 domains and 127 issues of
American state, county and municipal government, with the level that
ordinarily decides each. No player-facing surface reads the policy catalog
yet, so nothing on screen changes; a note claiming otherwise would be false.
The proposed attention weights are deliberately not included, being a
director's proposal awaiting the owner rather than a measurement.
