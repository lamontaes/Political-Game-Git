title: Homes come on the market when their owners die, all over town
---

When the last member of one of the town's households you have not met dies, the home they owned is now sold as a known household's would be: the lender is paid first and what is left stays with the town's residents. Homes left by the dead come back on the market across the whole town, not only among the people you know.
