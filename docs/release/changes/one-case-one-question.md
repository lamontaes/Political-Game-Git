---
id: one-case-one-question
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A reporter asks you about a case once, not three times in a day.
---

When several records about one case arrived in the same week, a paper opened a
separate story for each, and its reporter asked the subject the same question
once per story. In a playthrough, the Alaska governor was asked the same
question three times on the same day and had to deny it three times. Now a
week's developments on one case become one story that draws on all of them.
While a paper is still working a story on a case, later developments wait for
that story to run and then become its follow-up.
