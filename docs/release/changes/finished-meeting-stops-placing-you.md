title: A meeting you finished no longer keeps you in its room after you reload
---

After a reload, the corner of the screen named the last place you arrived, no
matter how long ago. Someone who went to a chapter meeting in January still read
"Community room" a year later. An arrival now stops naming where you are once
every meeting or journey it was made for has ended. It still counts at the
moment the meeting ends, so the walk home reads the room you are leaving.
