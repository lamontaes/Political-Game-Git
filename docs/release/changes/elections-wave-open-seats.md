title: A bad year for the president's party draws more challengers, and open seats draw more candidates
---

When the national economy turns or a midterm comes, people outside the president's party are drawn to run for partisan seats and people inside it are less so. Open seats pull harder than before. In two drawn places, races with no candidate fell to none and filing fields for state and statewide offices reached 5 or 6 before primaries thinned them to the general election's 2 or 3.
