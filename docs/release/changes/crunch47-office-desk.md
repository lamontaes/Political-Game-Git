title: Your office now shows what it is answerable for — programs, staff, your bills and casework.
---

Under Work, "Your office" now reads the rest of the officeholder's desk beside the staff briefing: the public programs your government has on record, what those services have to work with, what money has been put to the office and what the office has actually committed, who works there and since when, the bills you sponsored and where each one stands, and how the office handles constituent casework. An amount put to the office is plainly not a decision the office has taken. Where your government has no record — no program, no staff, no bill, no workflow — the panel says so in words instead of showing a zero or an empty post.
