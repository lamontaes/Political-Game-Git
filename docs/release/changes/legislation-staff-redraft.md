### Added

On your own bill, you can ask staff to redraft a section: it is shown as the clause struck and the clause inserted, and you keep the staff's version or your own. A new Bills tab under Politics follows bills before your state's chambers and Congress, with where each stands and how your own members voted.

### Changed

The bill paper and Your office no longer print how many members are for, against and undecided before a vote; they show what members have told you.

### Changed

As a member of Congress you now stop for the vote on a bill on tomorrow's floor: you read the bill and vote Yes, No or Abstain, or leave it to your character, instead of the vote being cast for you.
