title: When a reporter stops you about a bill, the talk names the bill, the vote and the paper
---

A reporter who catches you after a vote now asks about that bill by its number, and your answer on the record says how you voted and how the roll came out ("The roll was 5 to 2, and it passed."). Your other replies each name something different: the paper you're declining, the people in your town you still want to hear from, or the reporter by first name when you push back. A stall is no longer offered once the vote is held, and the paper keeps the line you gave it.
