title: More transit service brings more riders
---

When a law adds or cuts transit service, ridership follows a year later. Each
1% of service a law adds raises rides by 0.3% to 1.0%, and a cut lowers them
the same way. A state that lets its highway money pay for transit now gains
riders, not only buses and trains. Changes in service that no law caused are
already part of how ridership moves on its own, so they are not counted twice.
