After you file, the relative with a stake in it talks about your own household: the months left in the race and whether you can afford it, or who looks after your child on the nights you are out. Your four replies are each about that stake, and asking for help can bring them onto your campaign. The clerk's petition scene and this talk now play after the counter instead of being skipped.
