id: no-sit-and-talk-invitation
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The evening "sit and talk" invitation is gone
---

Somebody you live with used to say they would be home this evening and ask
whether you would like to sit and talk. Saying yes put two hours on your
calendar and nothing else: no conversation ever came of it. It is no longer
offered. An invitation already waiting in a saved game still reads and can
still be answered.
