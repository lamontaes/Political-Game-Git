section: Fixed
title: A council or board meeting you attend now decides the same bills the same way as one you do not
---

Every town and county body now files its agenda the day before and votes at its meeting, whether or not you are in the room. When two bodies meet the same day, the earlier hour votes first. "Decide for me" now casts your own recorded position and shows it on the roll call as your vote, where it used to record an abstention.
