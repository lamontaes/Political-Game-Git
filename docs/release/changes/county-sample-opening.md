title: The people of the county outside town now weigh running for county offices.
---

A county seat is no longer weighed by the town's people alone. The county outside the town now has its own households, weighted to its real count of voters, with years in the neighborhood, workplaces and neighbors they know, and the ones drawn to run are filled in as full people when the seat is weighed.
