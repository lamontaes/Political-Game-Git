title: A resident who stops you on the street greets you, names the measure by what it does, and lets you answer only what is true
---

When a resident stopped an official on the street about a measure, they said "I read about Ordinance 2025-1." and then "I keep reading about Ordinance 2025-1." in the same box, and the first answer offered was "I voted the way I thought was right" even when the official had not voted. Now the resident greets you by office and name in one of many ways, says the measure once by what it does, and asks about the vote you actually cast. If you voted, your answers say how you voted. If you were not in the vote, your answers say that. Their next line picks up what you said.
