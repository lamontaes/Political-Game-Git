title: Buttons, headlines and scene lines now read in plain American English
---

Twenty-six lines that no American says now read the way people here talk. The new-life screen says "to continue" instead of "to go on", the menus say "jobs" and "school" instead of "posts" and "study", and a few scene lines and headlines drop British turns such as "in the street", "glad of" and "fell ill".
