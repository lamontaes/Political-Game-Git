title: The evening before the vote on your bill, you ask a colleague for their vote
---

When a bill you sponsor would fail, the evening before its vote you ask a colleague the count says it needs. They answer from where they stand. One already for it says yes. One a favor from you would carry says they're not there yet, and a plea or a trade brings them over, while pressing them doesn't. One even a favor wouldn't carry says no. Their answer counts at the roll call, and their vote cites it.
