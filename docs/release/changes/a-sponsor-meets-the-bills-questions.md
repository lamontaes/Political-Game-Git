title: Whoever files a bill has met the questions it is about
---

A bill names the policy questions it bears on, such as whether the state should
levy an income tax. Until now nothing read that list, so nobody in any save
had ever come across a policy question except in the demo. The person who
sponsors a bill now has each of its questions on record as something they have
encountered, dated to the day they filed it. That says only that they met the
question, not which way they lean. It is the first input the pass that forms
people's political views will read.
