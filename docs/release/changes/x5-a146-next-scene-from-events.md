title: The next everyday moment follows what just happened in your life
---

Before, which everyday moment came next in a life was picked by a hash of
the world, so the same day could open on a different moment in another
world with the same history.

Now a reply to the moment you just finished still comes first. After that
comes the moment tied to the most recent thing that happened to you,
either the same kind of moment or one with somebody who was part of it.
When nothing ties, the moments come in their usual order.
