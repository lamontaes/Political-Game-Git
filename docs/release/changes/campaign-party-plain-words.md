section: Changed
title: Plainer words on the Campaigns and Parties screens
---

The Parties screen no longer opens with "Coming to any of this is not joining,
endorsing or voting." A trip to a meeting says "There is no fare." instead of a
note about travel costs not being represented. An office you may run for says
"You can run for this office." A campaign with no poll says "There is no poll
yet.", and a candidate whose own money cannot be used reads "None of your own
money can go into the campaign yet."
