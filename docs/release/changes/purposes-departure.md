title: Before you go into prison, someone who stays at home asks you for something.
---

The day a sentence takes you away, someone in your home asks you to write, to call when you can, to tell the child yourself, or to come home on the day you get out, naming the term and the date from your record. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
