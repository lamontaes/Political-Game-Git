title: Whether you concede on election night is printed in the race's story.
---

On the call after a lost race, conceding or refusing now counts. The press puts it on the record, and the race's story in the paper says so by name: "Vivian Ramirez called Hugo Osborn to concede." or "Jessica McGee would not concede the race." A concession made after the paper has printed is not told in that story.
