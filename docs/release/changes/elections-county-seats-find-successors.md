section: Changed
title: County races where the officeholder steps down no longer go to no one
---

When a county officeholder stands down and nobody files, the outgoing holder now asks the residents most drawn to the seat to succeed them, and the first who agrees files. In two drawn places this took county races with no candidate from 3 of 9 and 3 of 10 to 1 of 9 and 0 of 10.
