title: Eight drawn people that nothing could reach
---

Eight finished adult figures, four standing and four sitting, had been drawn, cleaned up and then left in no catalogue at all, so nothing in the game could reach them however far along they were. Six of them now sit in the review set with the rest, each one tied back to the artwork it was cut from. The two left out are turned a way the game has no sitting or standing view for yet, and they are recorded as waiting on that rather than quietly dropped.
