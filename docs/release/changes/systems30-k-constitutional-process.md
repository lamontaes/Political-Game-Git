title: Constitutional and charter process records
---

Track Congress-proposed Article V amendments, California legislative
amendments/revisions, and Carson charter changes through Nevada measures.
Retain proposal text, recorded bodies and denominators, authenticated state
ratification, filing/effective/operative dates and historical rule versions.
Supported prospective proposal-threshold changes affect later proposals.
Open-ended text effects and unsettled procedures remain explicitly unavailable.
The feature-local Politics tracker supplies A's mount; integration, independent
review and installation remain separate delivery states.
