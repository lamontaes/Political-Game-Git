title: A finished high school now counts when an offense points to someone
---

When the town works out who an offense points to, a resident's own school
record now counts. Someone on record as finishing high school is a little
less likely to be the one named, and someone who left without a diploma is
more likely. A resident whose schooling is not on record is weighed like the
average resident, with nothing guessed. How large the difference is remains a
placeholder until the research is in.
