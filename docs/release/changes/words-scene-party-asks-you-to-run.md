title: A party with no one running for a seat asks one of its members to run
---

When filing opens and a party has no one on the ballot for a partisan seat, its local chair asks one of its members to run, starting with the people the chair knows best. If you've joined the party and the chair comes to you, your day stops for it: they say which seat it is and why they came to you, and you can ask what it takes, say yes, say no, or ask for time. A yes puts you in the race. Members the party asks who aren't you weigh it like any other reason to run, and the party stops asking once one of them files.
