title: A small territory town with no businesses on the books no longer fails to open.
---

A town whose businesses keep no books, such as some in the Northern Mariana Islands, now opens its account for money from outside the town when its governments' treasuries open, the same way a campaign's first outside purchase opens it. Before, building that town stopped with an error.
