title: A housemate holding you to paying on time names the payment
---

After a quarrel over a missed payment, when you promised a housemate you would pay on time, they now name the payment when they bring it up: "You said you'd pay the mortgage on time. It's late again." or "You kept your word on the payday loan. Thank you." The payment comes from the loan behind the missed payment (the mortgage, the loan payment, the payday loan, the medical bill or the business loan). The line that never said what it was about ("We talked about this already.") is gone.
