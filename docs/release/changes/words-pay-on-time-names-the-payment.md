title: A housemate holding you to paying on time names the payment
---

After a quarrel over a missed payment, when you promised a housemate you would pay on time, they now name the payment when they bring it up: "You said you'd pay the loan on time. It's late again." The payment comes from the loan behind the missed payment (a personal loan from the bank); a missed payment on any other kind is not brought up. The line that never said what it was about ("We talked about this already.") is gone.
