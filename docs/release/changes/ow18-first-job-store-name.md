title: Your first job's store is named for your town
---

Before, the store where a generated past began work carried the town's postal
form, such as "Dry Ridge, Kentucky Market", or the stand-in "Neighborhood
Market".

Now it carries the town's own name, such as "Dry Ridge Market", in every
place.
