title: The federal government borrows from named lenders and pays them interest
---

When the federal treasury runs short it now borrows from groups of lenders (foreign holders, the Federal Reserve, funds, banks, households and the rest), and its net interest is paid back to each group by how much of the debt it holds. Before, the money came from and went to an unnamed outside account.
