title: A skip stops at a meeting posted while it runs
---

A skip used to run past meetings posted while it ran. A fifteen-year-old in
Washington, D.C. lost four party meetings between February and June 2039 that
way, each marked as passed without an answer.

The skip now stops at the first meeting or campaign shift posted during it,
with the meeting still open to attend. The same holds for a meeting someone
agreed to while the skip ran, such as a contact who said yes. Pressing again
from that stop lets the meeting go.
