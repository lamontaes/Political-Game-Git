section: Improved
title: A town's poverty rate comes from its people's incomes
---

A town's poverty rate is now counted person by person: everyone whose household's yearly pay and benefits fall under the Census line for its size and children is poor, so a layoff, a higher minimum wage or a later retirement age changes it through real paychecks, and it differs by household kind and income. Places with no recorded jobs keep an estimate instead of a made-up zero.
