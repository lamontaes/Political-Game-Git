title: The Journal says when a law reached you
---

The Journal now says when a law changed your money. It shows a raise that a
wage law gave your pay, with the old and new amounts. It shows a rent change
that a housing law set, and income tax or a leave premium that a new law took
from a paycheck or stopped taking. Any other time a law's record names you
shows up there too. In a watched Vicksburg, Mississippi world, a federal
minimum wage law raised 39 workers' pay on July 1, 2028, and each of their
Journals now says so.
