---
id: mayors-most-common-rule
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Towns whose charter is unread elect a mayor for four years
---

A town whose own charter the game has not read now follows the most common
real rule: its voters elect the mayor directly, and the mayor serves four
years. Before, each such town drew how its mayor is chosen and how long the
term runs from national survey shares, so some towns had no mayoral race and
others a one-year term for no reason of their own. Towns whose rules have been
read keep them.
