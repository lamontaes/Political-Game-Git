section: Changed
title: Retirement claims follow Social Security's ages in the law
---

When people claim Social Security, the earliest age, the full retirement age for their birth year and the age delayed credits stop now come from the law in force on the day they decide, so an enacted change moves who claims and when. The separate table of ages the game kept is gone.
