---
id: team-5-pension-liability-measured
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Each government's pension debt opens at its state's measured size
---

A government's opening pension liability is no longer 1.2 times a year of
its spending everywhere. It now uses its state's own measured ratio: every
public plan's actuarial liability against the state's combined state and
local spending. A place with no listed plan opens at the states' median,
marked as an estimate.
