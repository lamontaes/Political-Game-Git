title: The meeting says who spoke, numbers its ordinances in words, and leaves the people in view
---

At a meeting, each item now reads "Ordinance 2025-6" under its name, where a bare "2025-6" looked like a date. The people who spoke are listed under "Spoke for" and "Spoke against" with a count, apart from the vote, where every name used to be followed by "Against". The people in the room stand beside the meeting panel and are no longer half hidden behind it.
