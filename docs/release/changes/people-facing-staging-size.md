title: People face who they're with and are sized for the room
---

People in a scene now turn toward the person they are with, and everyone in a chamber or meeting room turns toward its front. Companions stand near each other without hiding one another, nearer people are drawn in front of farther ones, and people in the council chamber and the U.S. House are drawn at full size. Four home stand spots moved from the open floor to a counter, a doorway or a window.
