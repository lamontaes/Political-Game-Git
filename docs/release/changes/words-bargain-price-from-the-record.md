title: A colleague you ask for a vote can name what they need, and taking it up is a real deal
---

The evening before a vote, a colleague who will not give you their vote for a favor may mention their own bill waiting on the floor, or the one section of yours they can't take. Taking it up promises your vote on their bill, or cuts that section in half for everyone, and their vote at the roll reads the deal. You can also ask a colleague just to stay out of the room, and if they agree they are recorded absent. A yes given as a favor is now a debt they can call in later, and a colleague no longer reminds you of a vote they never gave you.
