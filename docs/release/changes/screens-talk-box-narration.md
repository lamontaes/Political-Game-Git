title: When there is nothing to answer, the talk box shows one Continue
---

When the narrator speaks alone or a conversation has ended, the talk box shows a single Continue at its bottom left and nothing else; Continue moves the game on. A conversation shows its replies with Say nothing, and Leave only where walking away is a real choice. The line under the speaker's name is gone.
