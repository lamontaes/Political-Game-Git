title: A raise or cut to a public-school budget line is checked end to end in a drawn town
---

A new test follows a mayor's schools-line change through the council's budget bill to the district's teachers. A place where the council recorded its own amendment, rejection or veto is skipped by that record; a place where the line was not adopted with no such record now fails the test.
