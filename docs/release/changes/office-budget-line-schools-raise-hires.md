title: A cut or raise to a public-school budget line now changes the district's teachers, including those counted in the town's wider population.
---

When a city's budget for the year changes a service line, the people it funds follow the change from the line as it stood before that year's bill. Teachers, police and other staff who are counted workers are laid off or hired alongside the named staff.
