title: Universal pre-K now moves eighth-grade reading and teenage violent crime
---

A state that starts universal pre-K now sees two more effects years later. Eighth-grade
reading moves a little, most likely slightly down, about nine and a half years on,
because studies disagree, from no change in Boston to a loss in Tennessee. Violent
crime falls about 1% fourteen years on, once the first enrolled children turn 18, and
ending the program reverses it.
