title: News names how the town's government is organized
---

Under the town's government in News, below its legislative body, News now shows the government's form, such as "Council-manager form of government" or "Consolidated city-county government", in the term public sources use. It shows only where the government's form is on record and its name does not already say it, which today is 26 of the 35,616 playable towns and cities; elsewhere News shows nothing new.
