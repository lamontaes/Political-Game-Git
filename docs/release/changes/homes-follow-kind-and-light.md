title: Home is a painted room that follows the time of day and the weather
---

Your home is now one of the new painted rooms, and it changes with the
clock: morning light, midday, night, and rain on rainy days. The people who
live with you stand in it, and the TV and the papers on the table are in the
right places. Six kinds of home are painted (a small apartment, a rowhouse, a
suburban house, a large house, a mobile home and a farmhouse), each sized for
people from its own picture. Until new lives record what kind of building
they live in, every home is the suburban house.
