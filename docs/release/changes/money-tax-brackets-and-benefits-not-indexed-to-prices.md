title: Tax brackets and the Social Security wage base now rise with prices and pay, as the law has them do.
---

Over a long game, pay rose with prices but the tax brackets, the standard deduction and the Social Security wage base never moved, so every worker slowly drifted into the top bracket and the federal government piled up a surplus. Now, each January, the federal brackets and standard deduction rise with prices and the Social Security wage base rises with average pay. The 16 states whose law indexes their brackets do the same, and so do the states that index their standard deduction. Other states keep their dollar amounts until a law changes them. Nothing changes in the first two years of play.
