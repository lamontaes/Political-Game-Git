title: A bribe can buy a vote, and a scandal reaches voters through the news
---

An employer who would lose money on a minimum-wage raise may offer a bribe to the council member closest to voting for it, and a member who takes it votes with that payment as one of their reasons. If the bribe comes out and someone takes it to the press, the local news carries it, and each voter holds it against the official only once the story has reached them, weighed by how much they trust the press.
