impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Private aims say what they can and cannot offer
---

An aim you set for your character now tells you the truth about itself. "Run for
office" is offered where the game actually knows of an elected office you could
stand for, and when it has nothing to put under it, it says why in the same
words the candidacy rules use — that it has not recorded how long you have lived
here, or that it has not read this state's offices yet — instead of leaving an
empty space. Where the aim is unavailable it no longer blames your age when your
age has nothing to do with it.

Someone you have not spoken to in over a year is no longer offered as both
somebody to get back in touch with and somebody to pursue a relationship with.
Getting back in touch comes first, and the relationship aim becomes available
once you have actually been in contact again.
