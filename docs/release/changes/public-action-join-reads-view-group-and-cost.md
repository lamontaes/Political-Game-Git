title: Strikes, protests and rallies draw the people who care, not the whole town
---

A bad term used to put nearly every adult in a town into the street. Now each person, the neighbors you know and the wider town you have not met alike, decides by their own view of the problem, whether it is their own group (the renters in a rent protest, the jobless in a protest over jobs, the school staff in a strike), and what going costs them in the week they are awake. A protest in a small town is a handful or a few dozen, it differs by issue, and a town can have a protest nobody you know is in. A scene about a protest or rally now says how the turnout looks (a few people, a group, a crowd) and never the number; the paper still prints it.
