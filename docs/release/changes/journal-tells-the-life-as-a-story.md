title: The Journal opens with the life told as a story
---

The Journal's Chapters view now opens with the life told in sentences, one chapter for growing up and one for adult life: where the person was born and whether they were raised there, what a parent did for a living, the high school they finished, the first job after school and the one they hold now, and whom they married and at what age. Each clause comes from a new life-story bank mined from public-domain federal oral histories and congressional testimony, with the person's own records filled in, and a clause the owner grades down is no longer chosen. The dated entries follow as before.
