title: A bill no longer quotes a real statute or a research source as the law it amends
---

A bill that changes a law in force when the game began says the old amount and the new one and cites nothing, since the real code section or survey behind that law only set where the world started. A bill that changes a law passed in play cites the bill that passed it, such as "amending HB 9" or "amending Ordinance 2025-3".
