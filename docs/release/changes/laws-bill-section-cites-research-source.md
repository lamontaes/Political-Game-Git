title: A bill cites the law it amends by its statute or the bill that passed it, never a web page or research note
---

A bill that changes a law in force when the game began cites that law's statute where the starting law records one ("amending Wis. Stat. § 104.001(2)"), and nothing where it records only the web page, survey or estimate behind it. A bill that changes a law passed in play cites the bill that passed it, such as "amending HB 9" or "amending Ordinance 2025-3".
