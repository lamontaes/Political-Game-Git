id: rent-follows-home-prices
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Rents in town follow the town's home prices, and an evicted household rents
---

A private landlord's renewal is no longer drawn at random. It follows the
town's home prices over the year, worked out each month from the economy the
world records, and rent stabilization holds it to the general price rise plus
five points. A new lease's rent follows the same home prices. A household
evicted within seven years rents its next home rather than buying one. A
household named for one person reads "Ana Ruiz's household," not "The Ana
Ruiz's household."
