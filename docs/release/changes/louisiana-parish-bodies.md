title: Louisiana's parishes are governed by their police juries and parish councils
---

A Louisiana parish's governing body now carries its own name: the police jury
in the 38 parishes that keep one, whose members are police jurors, the parish
council in the 21 home-rule parishes, and the parish commission in Caddo.
Before, every parish board was called a board of commissioners, the national
average name. The number of seats on each body is not read yet and stays the
national average, marked as an estimate.
