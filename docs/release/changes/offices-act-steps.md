The office day's two longest functions are now named steps: `officeActHandlers` (the acts a local officeholder takes, 356 lines) is one function per act (`officeHoursAct`, `townHallAct` and seven more), and `actOn` (a state officeholder's entry for the day, 270 lines) is one function per kind of entry with a short function that runs them. The bodies moved as they were, in the same order; the records proof over two played years (Old Orchard Beach, Maine, seed offices-fill) is identical before and after.
