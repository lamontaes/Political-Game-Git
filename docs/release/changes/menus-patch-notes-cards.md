section: Changed
title: Patch notes show one release at a time
---

Patch notes open on the newest release, and its name is a list you can pick any older release from. Its changes are cards by their headlines, and the change you click reads in full beside them, marked Added, Improved or Fixed, instead of every release down one long page. In the Guide, the term you picked is now marked on its card.
