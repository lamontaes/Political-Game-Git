title: Donors no longer give past what they can afford in a month
---

A donor asked by several candidates used to be offered their whole month of spare money by each one, so a donor in a busy election season could give far more than they had left after their bills. Each gift now comes off that month's spare money, so the next candidate to ask sees what is left, and campaigns raise less from the same donors.
