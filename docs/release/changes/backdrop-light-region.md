title: Places look like their region, and like the hour
---

Main Street, the town square and the doorsteps are painted in the town's own regional character: New England, the Mountain West, the Southwest, the Plains, the Deep South, Alaska, Puerto Rico and the Pacific islands. Fifty-eight more places have morning and night paintings, so an evening meeting or a morning shift looks like the hour it is.
