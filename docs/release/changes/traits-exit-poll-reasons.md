title: Who tells the exit pollster depends on why they voted
---

Voters who follow politics closely and cared about the race now tell the exit pollster more readily, and temperament tips only the close calls. About 45% of voters tell, as real exit polls find; before, a voter's temperament alone decided, so the eager and the private split by personality and nothing else.
