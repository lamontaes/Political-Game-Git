title: Count minor offenses and let police staffing move their arrests
---

The justice case flow now records minor quality-of-life offenses (disorderly conduct, drunkenness, liquor law violations, loitering, vagrancy and drug possession) as cases, and the police staffing a place funds moves how many of those arrests happen.
