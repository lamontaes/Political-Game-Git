title: Run for office from day one, and sim to the day filing opens.
---

You can announce for a coming race the day your life starts. Your campaign opens at once: raise money, go out on the trail and gather the signatures the race will ask for, all before filing opens. When the filing window opens, file at the counter, and everything you gathered carries into the filing. Every race whose filing hasn't opened has a "Sim to" button on Elections that runs the world to the day it opens, stopping on the way only for what needs you.
