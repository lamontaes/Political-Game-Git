title: Paid leave uses the adopted replacement share
---

An operative paid-leave law's final adopted replacement share now sets the
benefit paid through the existing claim and government-account writers.
Filed terms alone do not apply. Wrong units and shares outside zero through
one retain the existing sourced fallback. Weekly caps, eligibility, payments
and provenance keep their current paths. Employee-premium terms remain a
separate catalog contract gap.
