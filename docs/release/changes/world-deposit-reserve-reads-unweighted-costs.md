title: Businesses in a big town keep enough cash for their bills
---

In a town opened at its full Census size, a business now keeps cash on hand for all the bills it pays at the town's scale, not only its payroll, before putting the rest in the bank. A wholesaler that used to bank fourteen times its share and run dry on payday now keeps its cash.
