title: You can run for your own town's governing body
---

Every town with a government of its own now has a seat you can stand for. In
Paducah, Boise or any of about nineteen thousand others, the list of offices
under Campaigns now shows the town's governing body beside the state's seats,
and filing for it runs the same campaign to the same election day.

Win, and you sit on that body. Your office says so and says since when. Where
the game has read the town's government in depth, as in Bowling Green, the seat
also shows under that city's local meetings and records. Where it has not, the
screen says plainly that the town's meetings, votes and powers are not
established yet, rather than telling a winner they hold nothing.

The game does not invent what it has not read. The number of seats, wards,
term length and who may stand are left unknown, and a separately elected mayor
is not offered, because which towns have one is exactly what has not been read.
