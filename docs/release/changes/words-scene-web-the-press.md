section: Changed
title: When a reporter calls, you can apologize, explain, send a statement or go off the record
---

A reporter's call about a story on you can now be met with an apology, an explanation or an attack on the story, and any call for comment can be answered with a statement from your office or off the record; the call no longer names a time of day the game doesn't know.
