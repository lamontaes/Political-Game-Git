title: Taxes keep being withheld, and passed budgets change spending
---

Paychecks kept only Medicare tax after January 1, 2027, because that year's income tax and Social Security rates were not published yet. Each tax rate, minimum wage and benefit amount now stays in force until a law changes it, so federal income tax, state income tax and Social Security keep coming out of every paycheck. A budget that a council, county board, legislature or Congress passes now changes what that government spends, line by line, and next year's budget starts from those lines. A budget that fails keeps the lines already passed.
