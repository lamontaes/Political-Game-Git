title: Lawmakers' principles are not re-read from their whole lives several times a day
---

Before each bill step and agenda filing, every seated member's principles are formed again from their recorded life. On the same day, a member whose life records have not changed since an earlier reading that wrote nothing is now not read again. The check covers their own principle rows, those of the parents and confidants it read, the crimes against them and their seat roll. Anyone whose principles changed earlier in the same pass is read again, and a new day, a reloaded save or another world reads everyone afresh. Crime victims are found from an index that follows new events instead of rereading every event. Saved results are unchanged: a watched year from South Fork, Pennsylvania, gives the same saved fingerprint as before.
