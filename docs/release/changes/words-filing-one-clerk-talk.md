title: After you file, the clerk tells you what the petition needs in a scene of its own.
---

Filing at the counter now ends when you file. The clerk then tells you what comes next in a scene you can answer: how many signatures the petition needs and the day they are due, or that the office takes no petition. You can ask what happens if you come up short, push back on the number, or head out to start. Before, the clerk said it at the counter, and a player who lived alone then got a second, empty visit to the clerk's office.
