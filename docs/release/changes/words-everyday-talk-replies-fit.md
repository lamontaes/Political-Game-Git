title: People answer what you actually said
---

When you answer someone in town or at home, what they say back now fits what you said. Ask a family member to sit with you a minute and they say "Of course." Tell them you need some quiet and they say "Okay. I'll leave you be." and let you go. Disagree with them and they say "Suit yourself." instead of "That's what I thought too." Asked whether you saw the President's speech, you answer first ("I saw it. What did you make of it?") and never just ask back. Nobody asks you about a speech they never saw. These answers come from the same scene engine and the same lines your grades tune, so a grade on one reaches all of talk.
