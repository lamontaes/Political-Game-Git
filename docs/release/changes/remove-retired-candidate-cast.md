The retired Visual4 candidate cast was permanently removed: the images, their manifests, the two developer review screens that showed them, and the generators and tests that were only about them. No player-facing surface ever drew from them, so nothing a player sees changes. The Wave A bodies, their wardrobe and their admission route are untouched — a committed manifest names those plates, so they are used material and stay.
