title: People you deal with keep why they said yes or no
---

When the player or someone they deal with weighs a yes-or-no choice, such as opening the door to a canvasser or giving to a campaign, the game now keeps a record of the reasons, including their temperament, whether they said yes or no. A played year shows two residents with opposite temperaments, in the same situation, giving opposite reasons at the same decisions and ending the year with different friendships, money, civic acts and moods.
