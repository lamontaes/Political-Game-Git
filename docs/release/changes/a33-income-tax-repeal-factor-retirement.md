id: a33-income-tax-repeal-factor-retirement
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Keep income-tax budgets tied to collected payments
---

Income-tax adoption, repeal and restoration no longer multiply a government's revenue forecast. The existing statutory paycheck writer applies the operative law; the budget continues to read saved tax payments and their law attribution. A law answer alone does not create a collection or erase a completed payment.
