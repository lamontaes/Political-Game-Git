section: Changed
title: A legislature's first bill number follows its own filing pace
---

Before, the bill number a legislature had reached when a new game opened was
drawn at random between 12 and 639.

Now it follows how many bills that legislature files in a year (The Book of
the States 2023, Table 3.19) and how far into the year the game opens. A
game that opens in early January meets bill 1 or close to it, and one that
opens in July in a busy legislature meets a higher number. A legislature the
table does not count takes the middle state's pace, marked as an estimate. A
saved game continues from the highest number its legislature has already
filed.
