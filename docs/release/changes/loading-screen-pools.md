title: The loading card has a pool of dry lines for every step of building the world
---

Each of the thirteen steps the loading card runs (building the town, writing everyone's past, opening the economy, seating the government, the two months the world runs before the first evening, and the rest) now has a pool of fifteen short, dry lines in the controls bank, the joke always on the machinery of government and never on a named person, party or group of voters. The Menus lane's cycling reads a step's pool while that step runs; the step names stay as they were.
