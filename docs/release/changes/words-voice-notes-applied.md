Every line you judged in your voice notes of October 15 to 28 now counts across the whole game: a line you liked is said more, and a line you rejected stops, along with its look-alikes. The lines you banned by pattern are gone and cannot come back: nobody "is home" in narration, nobody signs off with "Talk to you soon" or "See you later", nobody says "must" for "have to", "that's settled", "the doors" or "gaps", narration never names the day or the town, and a date is said without the year. In a scene the narrator now speaks alone first; Continue, then the person talks. The talk box sets your earlier lines, the other person's earlier lines and what is being said now apart.
