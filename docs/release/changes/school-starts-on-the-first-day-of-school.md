title: A new character starts school on the first day of a school year
---

A new character's first school was recorded as starting on their fifth
birthday. A child born after September 1 was a year ahead of the school
calendar, spent seven years in elementary school and moved up a year late.
Now each school a new character has attended starts on the first day of a
school year and ends on the last. A five-year-old not due in kindergarten
until the fall has a place waiting and starts that fall.

This applies to new games. A game already in progress keeps its dates.
