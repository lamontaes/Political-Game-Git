title: State officeholders follow their session calendar
---

State legislators and governors use the shared office day for session work, district hours, speeches and decisions. Legislators receive per diem through their state's treasury, and governors can nominate named appointees and act on budget items under their state's veto rule.

Governors can reduce appropriation increases where the place’s rule allows it. Override votes use exact fractions, attendance and joint totals from the legal data.
