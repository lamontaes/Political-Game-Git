title: News names who holds office again
---

The News screen's look around your town names who holds office again: each
office's title over the person who holds it, their employer where it says more
than the title, and the date their term began where it is recorded. Your
town's government and its council, and the public institutions in your town,
are listed by name. The menu reset had removed this section together with its
sentences; it now shows the records alone.
