title: A reporter or moderator says back where you stand, and names the race.
---

In an interview or a debate, the reporter or moderator opens by naming the race you are running in, takes each answer in by saying back the side you took (or that you and a rival split on it), and closes without stock thanks; a short answer is taken as short.
