title: The opening's state card and the rent figure never show a blank
---

The state card at the start of a new life now shows an estimated population
and an estimated share of citizens who voted right away, and swaps in the
exact figures when they arrive. Under "How the place is doing", the
two-bedroom rent now shows its number on the card instead of only inside
"Exact values", and a place with no rent figure of its own shows its state's
average, marked as an estimate. A face that fails to draw is now tried again
instead of staying as initials.
