title: A chamber no longer re-elects its whole leadership when one member leaves
---

When a seat changed hands in the middle of a term, the chamber chose every leader and every committee again and the paper printed that it had organized. Now only the posts and committee seats the departing member held are filled, a new member takes the committee seats of the one they replaced, and a chamber organizes in full only at a new term or when the majority changes hands.
