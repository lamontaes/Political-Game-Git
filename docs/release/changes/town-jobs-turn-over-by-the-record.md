title: Who in town quits, is laid off or is hired comes from their record
---

Each quarter, the town's jobs still turn over at the real national pace: 2.0%
of jobs a month are quit and 1.1% end in a layoff. More are laid off and
fewer quit when unemployment is high. Nobody is picked by chance anymore.

The most recently hired are laid off first. The youngest quit first, then
whoever is newest in the job. A quitter walks straight into another job when
somebody they know from a congregation, club, council or a child's class works
for another employer in town. Among the residents looking for work, the ones
out of work the shortest time are hired first, and somebody who has never
held a job comes last.
