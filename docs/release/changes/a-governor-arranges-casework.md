title: A governor can arrange how the office handles casework, and "What came of it" shows only their own term
---

A governor's office said "The office's workflow is set up when you take the
office" for the whole term, because the choice was only ever offered alongside
a legislator's voting workflow, which a governor does not have. The office desk
now lets a governor choose how constituent requests are handled from the first
day in office.

The governor's "What came of it" list also showed the predecessor's budgets
and bills. It now lists only what came of this governor's own term.
