title: A parent whose child leaves school mid-year holds it against who answers
---

When a family moves in the middle of the school year and a child has to leave
school, each parent who moved with them thinks it over a few days later. They
come away holding it against the head of their local government, or the
governor where no local government is recorded. When asked about the people in
office, they name the child and say so.
