title: A dead senator's seat is filled by appointment, then by a special election
---

When a U.S. senator dies, the seat no longer stays empty until the end of the
term. The governor appoints a senator of the same party, and a special election
at the next November election chooses who serves the rest of the term. If the
term ends at that election anyway, the appointee serves until the regular
winner takes office.
