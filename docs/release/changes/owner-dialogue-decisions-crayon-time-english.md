title: Plainer choices and American English in everyday scenes
---

The blue-crayon scene no longer appears. Choices that take no time no longer
say "No time passes". Several lines now use plain American English ("in line",
"called", "favor", "schedule", "a manager"), and four growing-up
memories no longer claim an outcome nobody saw happen.

Drawing, reading and resting no longer interrupt the day as a scene. They
sit in a Free time section beside the moment, for the player to choose.
