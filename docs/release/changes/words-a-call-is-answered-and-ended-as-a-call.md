title: A phone call is answered and ended the way a call is
---

On a call the other person answers ("Hello?"), you say who it is ("Hey, it's me" to a relative or a close friend, "Hi, it's" and your name to anyone else), and they go on by how well you know each other and how long it has been. A call ends with "Thanks for calling" or "I'll let you go", and a close tie may add "Call anytime". Nothing that needs the two of you in one room, such as "Good to see you" or "Stop in anytime", is said on a call.
