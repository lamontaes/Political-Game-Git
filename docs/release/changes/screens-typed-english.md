title: Screen words come from the controls table, and stored codes no longer show
---

The Money sheet's bank labels, the campaign act buttons and results, the filing form's For and Against, the Play and Draft buttons and the new-life summary now read their words from the controls table instead of being typed in the screen code. A vote, a campaign act or a travel reason with no worded name now shows nothing instead of its stored code.
