title: People's opening stances are kept as records with their reasons
---

On the questions the national survey asked, a person's faith and schooling now set their opening stance once, as a record that keeps those reasons, instead of being worked out again each time someone asks. Two neighbors of different faith and schooling start out holding different stances.
