title: State and federal races draw candidates from the whole state
---

Statewide and federal candidates now come from everyone with standing in the state, and a party asks its strongest person when a side has no one. Every U.S. House seat and the legislature are on the ballot each even year. Winners take office on the day the place's law sets, every post has a replacement, and seats outside the town get their special elections. Reporters and outlet owners can't run or be appointed while they work for the outlet.
