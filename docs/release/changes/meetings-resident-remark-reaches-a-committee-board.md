title: Speaking at a board meeting now reaches the members even when the board's committee took comment the day before
---

When you spoke for or against an item at a meeting of a board large enough to send items to a committee first, your remark went on the record but none of the members heard it: nobody met you and nobody's view moved. The committee had already taken public comment the day before. Now the board's members hear what you say at their meeting, the same way a smaller board's members always have, and each of them knows you afterward.
