title: Guam's villages and the Northern Marianas' municipalities now have a government, a budget and a payer for their council and mayor
---

A town in Guam or the Northern Marianas opened with no government of its own: the budgets engine looked its units up by the place's Census code, which an island place's key is not, so its council and mayor had no payer and no office job, and its departments no treasury. The town's own record now names its units, the municipality opens with a government and a budget in force (the area's own local aid a resident over the residents of every village the municipality governs, taken as its revenue and spent on its administration, a registered estimate, so its treasury meets its payroll), and its council and mayor are paid from that treasury. Every mainland place opens exactly as before.
