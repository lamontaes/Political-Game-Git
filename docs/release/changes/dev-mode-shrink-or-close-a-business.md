title: Dev mode can cut a business's orders or close it, and shows what came of it
---

With dev mode on, Metrics has a Businesses panel. It lists the town's businesses by staff. You can cut a business's outside orders to a share you choose, using the same limit a law's operating rule sets, or close it through its owner's own closing. "What came of it" lists everyone the business let go: how they went (a layoff, a staff cut or the closing), why, the record they heard, and whether they're looking for work, working again or moved away. It shows the town's jobs, people, job seekers, open claims and sales by industry month by month. Every id opens in Trace.
