title: Townspeople who lost a race before the game began now say so on their card.
---

The town's past races used to name their losing candidate with a made-up name, so nobody you could meet had ever lost an election. Now, where one of the town's own people fits the loser (old enough, on that side of politics, and among the most involved), the race names them, and their card says they lost the race for that office. Statewide and federal races still name an outsider, and someone who moved to town after the race keeps the name but not the line on their card.
