title: See how each chamber of Congress divides, and who sits against their own party
---

The Government screen now shows how the Senate and the House divide, by party
and by caucus, and names the members whose caucus is not the party they belong
to — an independent who counts with one of the two, or a member of a party too
small to have a caucus of its own. Each name opens that person.

A chamber whose save records no caucus for anyone still shows its parties and
says so plainly, rather than disappearing.
