---
id: pension-costs-from-reported-plans
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Each government's pension grows and pays out at its own plans' rates
---

A government's pension now grows each year by the employer's normal cost its
own plans report to the Public Plans Database, and pays out the benefits they
report. Illinois' state plans owe 0.92% of their liability a year and pay out
5.51%; Chicago's owe 0.51% and pay out 5.60%. A government whose plans do not
report a rate, or whose reporting plans hold less than half its liability,
takes the median of every plan: 0.83% and 5.59%, marked as estimated from the
average. Before, every pension owed a hand-set 2% and paid out 8%: more than
all but one state's plans owe, and more than any state's plans pay out.
