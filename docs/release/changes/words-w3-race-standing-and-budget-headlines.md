title: The Campaigns screen names your race, the day voters decide and why no ad ran
---

Right after filing, the Campaigns screen says which seat you are running for, the day voters decide and when filing closes, instead of the same sentence in every town. Trying to buy an ad with no money says why from the campaign's books: nothing raised since you filed, all of it spent, or money left over. The paper's federal budget headline names its fiscal year or its total, and a budget hearing's headline names whose budget it is.
