title: The recall organizer says only what the petition holds: your vote
---

At the recall petition table, when you ask what the petition says you did, the organizer now names your vote ("That you voted for Ordinance 2025-10. That's all it needs to say.") instead of claiming you broke a promise no record holds. Lines about a broken promise, a mailer or office hours no longer play unless a record of them exists.
