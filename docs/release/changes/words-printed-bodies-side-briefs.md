title: More stories print a body, and the paper carries side briefs
---

Layoffs, the town's month, board meetings, profiles, vacancies, new officeholders, party leaders, sales of a business, closings, deaths, campaign gifts and the rules that took effect now print more than a headline and one sentence: how many people a business plans to keep, how many residents are out of work, who held a seat before, how a vacant seat is filled, what a business does, and more, each read from the town's own records. The stories from the year before the game starts (elections, votes, budgets, closed businesses, a parent's death) print the same way. A member who agrees to carry a bill a citizen asked for makes a story. Births, homes finished, home sales, new businesses, school graduation and test results, and crimes the town recorded print as short items with a headline and one line. Dates in a story are now the day it happened, not the day it ran.
