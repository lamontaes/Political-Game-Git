title: You can turn down a personal invitation again
---

Somebody asks whether you will come to something on Saturday. It goes on your
calendar as a tentative hold, and you can say no to it.

That last part had stopped working. The hold a personal invitation writes now
names you as the person responsible for it, because an invitation you mean to
keep is one you have to be able to walk to, and the game refuses to perform an
activity nobody is responsible for. The screen that offers the refusal was
still looking for a hold with nobody's name on it, so it found none and
offered nothing: every invitation sat on the calendar until its day came and
went.

The refusal now recognizes a hold in your own name, and still refuses one that
somebody else owes the answer for.
