A town or county body now has to give the public notice its state's law asks for before a regular meeting: an ordinance put on the agenda with less notice than the law requires waits for the meeting after, and the record keeps the posting day, the hours of notice and the law that set them. A place whose law sets no number uses the middle of the states that do (ESTIMATED).
