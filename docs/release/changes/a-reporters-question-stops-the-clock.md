title: When a reporter asks you to respond before a story runs, a long skip stops so you can answer.
---

A reporter gives you two days to respond, and the newsroom decides what to
cover once a week. A skip of a week or more used to carry you straight past
the question, and the story then ran saying you did not respond. A longer skip
now pauses at each weekly newsroom review and ends there when a reporter has
just asked you something.
