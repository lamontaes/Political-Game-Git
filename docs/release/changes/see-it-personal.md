section: Changed
title: Money and property shows your rent, pay, debts and legal permissions
impact: patch
---

The Money and property page now lists what you are paid and what you pay, each with who it goes to or comes from, the amount, how often, and the last payment with its status and date. Loans show their lender, balance, payment, rate and standing. A permission or prohibition a law put on you shows under the law's own name. A law your place began with now appears in the list of laws that reached your money, and in the town's list, named but with no page to open.
