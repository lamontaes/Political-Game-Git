title: A member of Congress's share of the vote last time is their real race's result
---

A sitting U.S. House member's or senator's share of the vote last time now comes from their real race (the 2024 House, the Senate class last elected), moved slightly per world, instead of a number drawn inside a typical range. Races with no published two-party result, such as an uncontested seat, and state and local seats keep the typical range. Dev mode's "How this world opened" says which it is for each seat.
