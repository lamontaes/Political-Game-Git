title: A donor's ask now plays as a small donor, a large donor, a friend or a donor who was put off before
---

A donor who comes to you after a gift no longer always says the same thing. Someone who gave a little and wants to be heard, someone whose large gift expects something back, a friend who gave, and a donor you put off before each speak in their own lines and offer you their own replies. Which one comes is read from what the records show: the gift against the donor's own year, its share of what others gave, how close the two of you are, and earlier gifts and asks you put off.
