title: A bargaining sitting describes the colleague in front of you, not a town in Kentucky.
---

When a bill you configured reached the members' room, the sitting argued about
your bill but introduced the member arguing for it as representing Ashland and
the counties around it, whichever state you were sitting in and whatever the
bill was about. The subject of the negotiation was already drawn from your own
bill; the reads of the two colleagues were not, so a broadband bill in Nevada
seated an advocate who wanted what a Kentucky transit authority needed.

The member who wants the narrower section is now introduced by the section they
want and who they want it written for, taken from the bill on the floor. The
member counting the cost reads as before. The authored Kentucky transit sitting
is unchanged and still names Ashland, because that sitting is about Ashland.
