title: The dialogue engine knows how news stories are written
---

Counts from 51 Voice of America staff stories now sit in the game's data: a
median sentence of 15 words, a lead of 24, most paragraphs one sentence long,
about one sentence in nine quoting someone, and "said" as the usual attribution
word. No sentence from a story ships; the counts shape the game's own news
writing.
