Politics → Budgets now shows a government's year and total in large type, where its money comes from and where it goes as rings with the amount of each of its largest lines, and the treasury's books beside them, on framed plates. A mayor or other executive still steps spending priorities up or down. Government names read "County of Gila", not "County Of Gila".
