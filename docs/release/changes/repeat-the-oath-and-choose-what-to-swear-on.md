title: You repeat the oath of office phrase by phrase, and choose what to swear on
---

Swearing-in is now a ceremony you take part in. A new legislator or governor
chooses what to place a hand on: a Bible, a Hebrew Bible, a Quran, a copy of
the state constitution, a copy of the Constitution of the United States, or
nothing but a raised right hand. They also choose whether to swear, ending
"So help me God", or to affirm. The officiant then says "Raise your right hand
and repeat after me", and the player clicks each phrase of the oath to repeat
it in their own name. The oath is recorded, with the choice, only when the
last phrase is spoken.

The oath opens with the words federal law requires of every state
officeholder, to support the Constitution of the United States. The rest is a
general wording shared by every state until each state's own oath is
researched. Nothing in the world reacts yet to what the oath was taken on.
