A research question can now carry `readFirst`: the owner's own words when he
reads a finding and says to put it at the top, with who said it and when. The
rendered document lifts those into its lead, his words quoted, above the
questions he filed himself.

It lifts rather than reorders. The bands stay sorted by what an answer would
change, because who asked a question should not move it out of the band it
belongs in — but what the product owner weights is worth more to whoever is
instructing us than our ordering is, and a lead that only says "read this
first" without saying why he thinks so throws away the useful half.

Also adds the connectivity map: `npm run connectivity:map`, with entries in
`docs/connectivity/entries/` and a rendered document at
`docs/connectivity/WHAT-REACHES-WHAT.md`. Three things are recorded separately
for each subject — a system underneath, something to see, a control that moves
it — because this game has every combination of the three, and the ninth
combination is the one nothing else catches: a subject named in shipped
content with nothing behind the name, which loads, resolves and accepts a bill
about it while there is nothing there to affect.

No player-facing behaviour changes: both are authoring documents, and nothing
in a running game reads either.
