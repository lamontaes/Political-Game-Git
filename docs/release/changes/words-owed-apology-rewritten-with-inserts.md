title: The apology after a missed payment names the loan, who was not paid and when the next one is due
---

When you miss a payment and someone at home has to hear about it, they now talk about that payment: the loan it was on, the lender who called, how far behind you are and when the next one is due, instead of the same general words about "the missed payment" in every house. If you promise to pay on time, you promise it for the next due day, and the game checks it against the next payment: kept when you pay, broken when you miss.
