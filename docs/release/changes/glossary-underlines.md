title: Civic words are underlined and explain themselves when you rest on them
---

Words like primary, filing deadline, cloture, veto override, quorum and
appropriation now carry a quiet ink underline wherever they appear: in
conversations, the Journal, the news, bills, dossiers and the Guide. Resting
the pointer on one, or moving keyboard focus to it, opens a short plain
definition. "Got it" removes that word's underline everywhere; the Guide keeps
the entry, and it can take the mark back. Each word is marked once per
paragraph, and never inside a button or a field. Ten terms were added,
including committee assignment, germane amendment and the three interview
ground rules.
