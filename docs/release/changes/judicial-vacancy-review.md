id: judicial-vacancy-review
impact: none

An explicit review action for a player serving as President can open a recorded federal judicial selection when the seat is actually vacant. It draws chosen candidates only from living judges already seated in the public court roster, spends 30 game minutes under a labeled placeholder duration, and saves the review and the President's accurate knowledge. It does not infer private candidate qualifications, open a vacancy, hold a hearing, or cast Senate ballots. The ordinary player UI and a naturally occurring vacancy remain pending.
