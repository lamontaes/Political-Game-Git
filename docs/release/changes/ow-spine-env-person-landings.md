# Environmental outcomes reach residents

Carbon pricing, bottle deposits, power plant carbon limits, and clean electricity effects now produce estimated person outcome records through the shared outcome path. The same matcher covers all 56 modeled places and schedules the normal official view reflection.

DECISION: These outcomes are place averages, so they are reflected to all modeled residents of the affected jurisdiction as estimates. The power plant particulate estimate names people downwind of coal plants, but the simulation has no individual plant proximity records; the jurisdiction average is used as a proxy and is not a claim of personal exposure.
