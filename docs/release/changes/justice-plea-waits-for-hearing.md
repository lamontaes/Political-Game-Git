title: Someone charged with bribery now waits for the hearing before a guilty plea is entered.
---

A guilty plea no longer ends a bribery case on the day of the charge. Everyone
charged waits for the hearing, as long as cases take in that state: at home if
they post bail, in jail if they cannot, and at home without bail where the
law has ended cash bail. Posted bail leaves the defendant's own money for the
court and comes back in full on the day the plea is entered or the trial opens,
so a defendant has less to spend while the case is open.

A prosecutor with a full desk still charges the strongest cases: a busy
office weighs its caseload against a new charge only up to its capacity, so an
official caught taking a bribe is charged even while other cases wait.
