section: Changed
title: Asking someone for their vote plays through the same conversation engine as every other scene
---

When you ask a colleague for their vote the evening before a roll call, or a resident for theirs at the door, the scene is now built by the same conversation engine as every scene someone brings to you. The answers still come from where the other person stands, never from a roll. The scene's ending is named by the same rules as every other ending, so a colleague who is still deciding when you let it go reads as still undecided, not as a scene left there. The wording of a line may differ from before.
