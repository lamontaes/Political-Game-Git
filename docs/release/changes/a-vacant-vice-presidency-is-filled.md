title: A vacant vice presidency is filled, and the Vice President succeeds a President who dies
---

When the Vice President dies, the office no longer stays empty for the rest
of the game. The President nominates a successor, both houses of Congress
confirm them, and the new Vice President serves out the same term. The
nominee is a member of the House from the President's own party. Their House
seat is then vacant and filled by a special election, like any other House
vacancy.

When a President dies in a new game's opening term, the Vice President now
becomes President for the rest of the term. The vice presidency then falls
vacant and is filled the same way. Before, the presidency stayed empty.

How long the nomination and the vote take, whom a President picks, and
whether Congress can refuse are the game's own stand-ins until the research
comes back: ten days to nominate, 75 days to confirm, and Congress always
confirms.
