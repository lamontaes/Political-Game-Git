title: A vacant vice presidency is filled, and the Vice President succeeds a President who dies
---

When the Vice President dies, the office no longer stays empty for the rest
of the game. The President nominates a successor, both houses of Congress
confirm them, and the new Vice President serves out the same term. A nominee
who sat in Congress leaves the seat, which is then filled like any other
vacancy.

When a President dies in a new game's opening term, the Vice President now
becomes President for the rest of the term. The vice presidency then falls
vacant and is filled the same way. Before, the presidency stayed empty.

The amendment lets a President nominate anyone eligible. Whom a President
picks, how long the nomination and the vote take, and how Congress votes are
the game's own stand-ins until the research comes back: an even draw among
everyone old enough other than the player, ten days to nominate, 75 days to
confirm, and both houses confirm.
