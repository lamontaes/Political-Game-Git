---
id: gov-public-pay-floor-school-districts
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A city or county pay floor no longer raises school staff; a state floor still does.
---

School districts are governments of their own under the state, not part of the town. A public pay floor your council or county board passes now reaches the town's and county's employees but not the school district's teachers and staff, while a state or federal floor reaches all of them. The pay-floor bill card's list of who the law reaches shows the difference.
