title: What is waiting on you now opens the thing that answers it
---

"Waiting on you" told the truth and stopped there. It said the posted meeting
was waiting for a decision, and that an offer of work was waiting for an
answer, and then left the player to go and find the screen that takes one. A
list of things you cannot act on is a reminder, not a day.

Each of those lines is now the way in to the record that answers it. The
posted meeting opens the commitment on the calendar. An unanswered offer of
work opens Work. A thing that is with somebody else opens that person. An
errand you do with the time you have stays a plain sentence, because it is
answered right below, with the time; and a line the record carries no route
for also stays a plain sentence rather than becoming a control that guesses
where to send you.

Following one of these is still reading. It opens the record and the day does
not move: no evening is spent by looking at what an evening would cost.
