title: The Lie button sits beside the replies in every conversation
---

Every conversation box now has a Lie button to the left of the replies, as
the owner's design asks. Pressed, it shows the knowingly false replies the
conversation offers in place of the ones they replace. Where the conversation
offers no lie, the button stays in its place, disabled. It replaces the scales
icon that sat in the corner of recorded-scene conversations, and it now also
appears in ordinary talk with people in the room and in office conversations.
