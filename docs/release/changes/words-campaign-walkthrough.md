The first time you file, the clerk tells you the signatures and the day they are due, then asks whether you have run a campaign before. Say no and the clerk walks you through it: the signatures and how to gather them (doors, a table where people shop, friends carrying a sheet), money and what it buys (mailers, a poll, ads in the town's own paper), and the press. Say you have and the clerk lets you go. After filing, the status reads "Collecting signatures".
