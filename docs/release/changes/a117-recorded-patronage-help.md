title: Patronage support reads recorded campaign help
---

Town patronage support counts distinct adult residents in outstanding debtor
households who actually donated, volunteered, or provided chapter backing to the
candidate's current campaign. Debt bands and household membership no longer
invent vote weights, and a fixed half-share cap no longer clips recorded help.
The reader exposes the people and favor records it counted. These are campaign
help records, not evidence of secret ballots or a new support producer.
