title: When someone tells you how a race came out, they give the count and where they stand
---

When someone tells you how a race came out, asking about the count gets the margin or the winner's share from the result, with a reason that depends on whether they backed the winner. Your replies each name something different: the seat, the winner, the person telling you, or your town. Standing by someone who lost is remembered in your journal.
