title: A state bill to repeal or adopt the state income tax now changes the tax.
---

Your state's income tax opens as it stands today until your world's history votes tax rates, and the law screen now says the state has one. A state bill repealing the income tax now ends it, and a bill adopting it brings it back. Before, the law screen said no income tax was ever adopted while the tax was still withheld, and a repeal changed nothing.
