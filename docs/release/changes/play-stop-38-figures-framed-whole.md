title: Heads stay in frame, and you and the clerk stand apart at the counter
---

The President's portrait in the talk box now shows his whole head. In a close-up room such as the bedroom, you are no longer drawn with your head above the top of the picture or cut off at a side. At the clerk's counter the clerk is seated where you do not stand in front of them.
