title: The title menu has Continue again and changes its national scene each visit
---

The title menu offers Continue, New game, Options and, on the desktop, Quit, with no tagline. Behind it a national scene changes with each visit: the White House, the convention, a rally, the House and Senate floors, the debate stage or the Oval Office, with your character standing in it once you have a life to continue.
