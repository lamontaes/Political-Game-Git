The title menu is back to its established form: the subtitle, then New game, Continue, Saved games, Options and Patch notes, each with its line, and Quit on the desktop. Behind it a national scene changes with each visit: the White House, the convention, a rally, the House and Senate floors, the debate stage or the Oval Office.
