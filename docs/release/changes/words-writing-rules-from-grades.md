title: Lines with filler or stock phrases come up less often
---

What the owner's grades and voice notes taught, and what shipped games teach, are now writing rules every line is weighed against. Lines that open with filler, ask a question that names nothing, give in at once or reach for a stock phrase come up less often everywhere; lines that name who, what, where and when come up more.
