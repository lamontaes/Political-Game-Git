title: Hardship now shapes what people do next
---

A layoff, an eviction or a missed loan payment now leaves stress that the person carries into what they do next, and how hard it lands depends on who they are. A worker who has just lost a job is more likely to file for unemployment, a household in a bind is more likely to ask for a loan, and a person under strain is more likely to start leaving town, each by a size taken from research and each fading as the stress wears off. Missing a loan payment now weighs on a person as much as being laid off.
