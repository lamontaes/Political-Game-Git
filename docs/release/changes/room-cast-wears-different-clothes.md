title: Two people in the same room no longer arrive in identical clothes
---

Before, two people standing in the same room could be dressed in exactly the
same outfit, which looked like a copy of one person.

Now each person placed in a room is given an outfit nobody else in the room is
already wearing. People on shift in a job with a uniform still share the
uniform, as they should.
