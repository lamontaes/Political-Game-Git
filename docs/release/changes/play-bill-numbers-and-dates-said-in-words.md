title: A bill is called by its name and number ("Ordinance 2025-1") everywhere, and dates read in words.

Your office, the Bills list and a bill's page showed a bill's bare number, "2025-1", which read like a date. They now say "Ordinance 2025-1" (or the body's own word for its bills). The debate card and the line for a seat you had to leave now say their day in words.
