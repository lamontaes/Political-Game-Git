section: Improved
title: Conversations drop the lines that read wrong, and keep the ones that landed
---

People no longer open a talk with "Do you remember" and the name of your old
school, or close one with "Talk to you soon." The President's address says
"Thank you very much" rather than "Thank you so much." The lines that read
best in review, such as "You'll need friends after this vote. I won't be
one.", are ready for the scenes where colleagues ask for your vote.
