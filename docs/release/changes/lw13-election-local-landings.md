title: A saved council term-limit bar now reaches the officeholder it affects.
---

The local council term-limit path now records an election-rule exposure for the
actual officeholder whose saved, law-stamped event says the law barred another
candidacy. The exposure points to that event and carries no invented financial
amount. Independent ward commissions and all-mail voting remain without
named-person landings until their existing records identify an individual's
changed eligibility or actual ballot delivery.
