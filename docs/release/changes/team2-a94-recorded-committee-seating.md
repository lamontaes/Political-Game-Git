impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Committee seating follows recorded member tenure
---

Committee rosters now order actual seated members by their saved seating date,
then stable member identity for equal seniority. State members use validated
opening or late-entry events; Congress uses its recorded seat-roll event.
Changing a caller's seed no longer changes who gets a committee seat.

Missing seating evidence or duplicate member identities leave the committee
roster unavailable. Generated employment dates never establish seniority.
The existing compiled committee sizes and round-robin distribution remain the
disclosed game assignment profile; this change does not invent legal committee
appointment procedures, requests, or party quotas.
