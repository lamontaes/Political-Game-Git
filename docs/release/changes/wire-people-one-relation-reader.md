title: Everyone in the game reads how two people are related the same way
---

How a person is related to someone is now read one way everywhere: the director's scenes, the talk, the journal, the person card and the calendar all say the relation the family record holds today, so a former partner is no longer spoken of as a partner in one place and a former partner in another.
