title: A wage bill can raise the floor in two dated steps.
---

A bill drafted from a seat on the council or board can now raise the minimum wage in steps: one amount from one date and a higher one from a later date, written as sections of the same bill. When the body passes it and it becomes law, the town's wage floor rises on the first date and again on the second, and each new law names the bill. A step whose date has already gone by when the bill becomes law takes effect by the town's usual rule instead of reaching back into the past, and the bill's paper no longer claims one date for a bill whose sections name two. The draft screen does not offer the second step yet; that control comes in a later change.
