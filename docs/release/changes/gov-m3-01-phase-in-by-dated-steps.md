Nothing a player can reach yet: a bill drafted from a seat on the council or board can now raise the minimum wage in dated steps, written as sections of one bill, and once it becomes law the town's wage floor rises on each step's date, each new law naming the bill. A step whose date has gone by when the bill becomes law takes effect by the town's usual rule, or gives way to the next step when that one comes first, so the floor never falls back to an earlier step. The bill's paper names no single date for a bill whose sections name two, and its second step reads as raising the first step's amount. The draft screen offers the second step in a later change.
