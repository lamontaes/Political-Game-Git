title: A cocky defendant weighs going to trial over taking a plea
---

A person who overestimates their own prospects now counts that confidence as
one reason to test a charge at trial instead of taking the plea on offer. It
weighs beside the evidence, the sentence, their principles and everything else
they carry, so the same defendant with the same facts and no such streak still
takes the plea.
