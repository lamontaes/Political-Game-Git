title: The journal tells each vote once, and says when your bill failed.
---

"My life" no longer prints each of your votes twice ("I voted for Ordinance 2025-1, the Parks Funding Ordinance." and "I voted for Ordinance 2025-1."), and when a bill you sponsored fails it says so ("Ordinance 2025-1 died in committee." or "The vote on ... went against me.").
