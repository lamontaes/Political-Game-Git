title: An afternoon you said yes to always has a way there
---

In Elko, Noah said yes when Gia invited him over, and going still said "You
have no way to get from Home to" her house. Saying yes is meant to book the
short trip there, but some route left the afternoon with no trip, and then
nothing could book one. Going to an afternoon you said yes to now books the
trip itself when it is missing. The afternoon is then kept as usual.
