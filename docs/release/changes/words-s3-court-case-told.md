impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Someone you live with who is charged, convicted or cleared tells you what it was for.
---

When someone in your house is charged, convicted or found not guilty, they tell you what for, from their own side of it (taking a bribe, paying one, offering one), with the plea or the trial day, the bail, or the sentence. They answer what you say back, briefly and without repeating what they just told you.
