title: A party founded in a world's history fades away when it keeps losing voters
---

In a world whose past runs from 1925, a party founded there that loses share in each of several straight presidential elections now joins the national party nearest it, and its ticket ends. Some worlds then found a new one later. The dev opening view lists each ending with its year.
