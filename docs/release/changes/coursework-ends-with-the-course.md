title: Coursework you shared with a classmate ends when you leave the course
---

After a person left school, the Journal could still say a shared piece of
coursework "is due, it counts as one piece, and the other half is not done."
The coursework also kept one of the two slots a life has for things that are
still going on. Once the course is left, whether it ended, the person withdrew
or transferred, or they completed it, the coursework is no longer open.
