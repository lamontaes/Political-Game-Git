title: A federal budget Congress adopts now moves the whole country, not only your town.
---

When the game's Congress raised or cut a federal budget line, only payments in your town changed. Now every state's households and employers receive the budget's dollars, line by line and in each state's share, so a bigger Social Security line pays more to retirees everywhere and national jobs and output follow on the Metrics tab.
