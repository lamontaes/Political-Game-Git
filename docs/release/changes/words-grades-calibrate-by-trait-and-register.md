title: One graded line now tunes the whole voice
---

When a line of dialogue or a speech is graded, the lines built the same way, in the same voice and in the same kind of setting, now come up a little more or a little less often everywhere, not just the one line. A good opening speech line makes a concession speech or a hearing's thanks sound more like it; nothing is ever removed because a neighbor was graded.
