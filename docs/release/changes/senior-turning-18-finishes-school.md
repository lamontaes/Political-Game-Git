title: A high school senior who turns 18 during the year still goes to school and can graduate.
---

Whether someone goes to school now follows their class year, not their age on the day. A senior born in the fall or winter, which includes many players who start at 18, used to stop going to school on their 18th birthday and then fail to graduate in June. They now finish the year and graduate on the same terms as their classmates.
