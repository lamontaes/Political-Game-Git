section: Changed
title: A statewide race with an independent in it is decided by the voters
---

A governor's or the D.C. mayor's race with a candidate on neither major
party's line, such as a sitting executive with no party on record, no longer
falls back to a drawn count of a few thousand votes. That candidate now
shares the part of the place's 2024 ballots that went to neither major party,
and the major-party nominees split the rest by the place's own lean. In
Washington, D.C. the Democratic nominee now wins about nine votes in ten,
where a Republican used to be able to win on a drawn count.
