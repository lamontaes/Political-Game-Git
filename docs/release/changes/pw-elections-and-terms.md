title: Every election and term follows its state's rules, and a skip stops on Election Day
impact: minor
---

Each office's term length, election years, primary, filing deadline and the day its winner takes office now come from a rule for that kind of office in that state or territory, in all 56. A governor elected in November takes office on the state's inauguration day, and a county row officer on the county's start day, not on Election Day or January 1 everywhere. An official whose term runs out with no one elected leaves the seat on its last day, and the seat is filled the way the office's vacancy rule says until the next winner takes office. Every seat decided at an election gets its next election, a full term on, as soon as the result is in. When you skip or jump the calendar across an Election Day you can vote in, the game stops there when the polls open. If nobody is playing your character, it votes or stays home on its own reasons, and its ballot is kept with those reasons.
