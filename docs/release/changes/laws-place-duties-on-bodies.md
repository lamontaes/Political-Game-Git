id: laws-place-duties-on-bodies
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A law that places a duty on a body now does something
---

A passed law that requires a class of body to act, such as a utility keeping a
plan for continuing service or a state agency publishing how its posts are
classified, now records that duty. On the date the law sets, the law's page
says how many of the bodies on record met it. Where no one is on record as
working at a body, or the law covers only bodies above a size the world does
not record, the page says that part is not known rather than guessing. Before this
change, the law's page said nothing acted on these sections.
