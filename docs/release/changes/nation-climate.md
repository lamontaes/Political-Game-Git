title: The climate follows the country's carbon laws
---

The country now keeps one warming level and a level for each kind of hazard (heat, flood, storm, wildfire, drought). They move only with the carbon price and power-plant carbon rules in force in every state, so a world that repeals a rule warms a little faster and one that prices carbon warms a little slower. Nothing in town reads the levels yet.
