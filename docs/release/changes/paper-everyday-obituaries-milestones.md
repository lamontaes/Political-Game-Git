title: Obituaries, round birthdays and business anniversaries in the local paper
---

An obituary now tells what the records hold of a life: where the person was born, the office they served in, where they worked and for how long, and who survives them. A resident who turns 80, 90 or 100 gets a birthday notice, and a local business marks its 10th, 25th or 50th year. A council member who votes again for a family firm is reported as the second or third time, with the firm named.
