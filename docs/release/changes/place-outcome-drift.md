title: States' uninsured and poverty rates now drift over the years, and no two games end alike.
---

A state's share of uninsured people and share in poverty no longer hold near
where they began. Each drifts month by month, partly with the whole country
and partly on its own, and now and then a nationwide wave moves every state a
long way. Laws and the economy still push them up or down on top of that, so
over a long game each state's story is its own.
