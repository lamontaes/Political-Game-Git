title: The clerk tells you how many signatures your petition needs and the day they are due
---

When you file for office at the counter, the clerk now says what comes next: the number of signatures your petition needs from voters in town and the day they must be back on the clerk's desk, read from the petition the filing opened. Where the office takes no petition, the clerk says so. The narrator's line that sets the scene at the counter now comes from the same story director as every other scene.
