title: People in the wider population now retire, quit and look for work the way people you can click do
---

Workers who are only counted in the town's population used to retire, quit and start or stop looking for work by a separate summed-up rule, and applied to every opening nearby. They now weigh staying, looking around, leaving and retiring by the same reasons as full people: their age, savings, how their pay compares with the going rate, how long they have held the job, how scarce jobs are, and their temperament. Each applies to the openings worth more to them than waiting. Retirement now comes in the mid-sixties for most workers, with savings deciding who goes first.
