title: Winning an office no longer needs a Qualify button, and saves stuck outside an office they won are seated
---

A governor-elect used to have to press "Qualify for the term" on Campaigns
before the term began. A player who never saw the button lost the whole term,
with no warning, when time passed the start date. That step is gone. The
office's requirements, such as age and residence, are checked for the winner,
and the oath on the first day of the term is the ceremony.

Missouri House and Senate winners were never seated. The term limit counted
the member's own first term as one already served and refused the seat. That
is fixed. A won legislative seat also no longer appears as "An offer of work
... waiting for your answer".

A save already stuck, for example a governor whose term began without them or
a member whose first day was refused, is seated when it is opened. The seat
counts from that day. The campaign's closing line now gives a governor-elect
the date the term begins, instead of saying "the seat is theirs".
