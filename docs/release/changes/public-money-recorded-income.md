title: Public payment effects use recorded monthly personal income
---

Public spending and tax collection effects compare completed payments with
recorded aggregate personal income for the same jurisdiction and calendar month.
A fixed dollar amount, labor-income proxy, or divided annual total cannot supply
that denominator. A compatible positive income record must be available at the
read date. This consumer change does not create ordinary monthly income records;
the existing canonical metric writer and its producer coverage remain separate.
