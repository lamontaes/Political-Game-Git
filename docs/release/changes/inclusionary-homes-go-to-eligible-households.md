title: An inclusionary housing law's affordable homes go to households under the income limit
---

A town with an inclusionary housing law set aside every seventh or so new
apartment or rowhouse as affordable, but when the household that moved into
that home earned too much, the home was let at the market rent and the
set-aside was lost. The law now fills its set-aside with the covered homes
that households under the income limit rent, so its affordable homes appear
as those households move in.
