section: Changed
title: People are registered to vote, or not, at an address; a move leaves the old registration behind
---

Everyone in town is now on the voter rolls or not, at an address. Move across town and you stay registered at the old address until you register again, which people do sooner or later depending on how much they care about voting; where the state registers you automatically at a move, or lets you register at the polls, that decides what happens on election day. People who aren't on the rolls on election day register at the polls where the state allows it, and are turned away where it doesn't. Which polling place you go to no longer changes when other households move in or out.
