id: economic-figures-by-release-date
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Local economic figures appear on the day they were really published
---

The "How the place is doing" panel used to show nothing for the first eight
months of every life, because the game dated every figure by the day it had
downloaded the files rather than by when the government published them.

Figures now become visible on their real publication dates. A life that opens
in January 2026 sees the local two-bedroom Fair Market Rent from the first day,
because HUD published those rents in August 2024. County personal income for
2024 arrives on February 5, 2026, and state and metro price levels on February
19, 2026, the days those editions came out. A figure that describes an earlier
year is not shown before the edition that holds it was released.

Two things are still held back until their publication dates are established:
HUD's income limits and the monthly unemployment series. In New England, where
HUD sets rents town by town rather than county by county, rents do not reach a
town yet.
