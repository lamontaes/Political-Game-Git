title: Pay grows with time on the job and how a person works
---

Once a year a business now raises each worker's pay beyond the market's move: more in the first years on the job and less as the years add up, and more for people it sees as diligent, at sizes taken from research on tenure and merit raises. A business that keeps losing money gives less, sliding toward nothing, and the people not yet named in your town are raised by the same rule. New openings are still posted at the role's going rate.
