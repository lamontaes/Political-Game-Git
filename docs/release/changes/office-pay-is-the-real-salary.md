title: Governors, legislators, judges and members of Congress are paid their real salaries
---

A governor is now paid the state's published salary, a state legislator the
state's annual legislative salary where the state pays one, a judge the state's
general trial court salary, and a member of Congress $174,000. Before, every
office paid $60,000. Other offices still pay the same placeholder amount.
