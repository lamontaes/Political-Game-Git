title: At the filing counter your filing now carries your own stands, and every race can be asked for
---

A filing made at the counter takes your own views on the questions that seat decides, so the Campaigns row shows where you stand. When an office takes more races than the talk box holds, the clerk's replies include "What else is open?" and the next races follow, so no race is out of reach at the counter.
