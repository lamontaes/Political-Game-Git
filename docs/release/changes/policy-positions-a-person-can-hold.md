title: The game ships sixty-six policy questions a person can take a side on
---

Before, the catalog knew what politics is about — thirteen domains and a
hundred and twenty-seven issues — and held no question anyone could be for or
against. Every player-facing reader of that catalog reads propositions, and
the game shipped none, so those screens had nothing to show on an ordinary
first day.

This adds sixty-six authored positions in their own pack, `us-policy-positions`,
each naming a specific thing that could be done about one of the shipped issues
and asking it as a question. Direction lives in the answer, not in the question;
magnitude, where a position carries one, lives in its parameters.

They are declared authored fiction, not law. The issues they point at are
sourced, and the pack keeps its own provenance so the two are never confused:
a stance is not a finding, and nothing here is a claim about what any real
jurisdiction has done. Coverage is deliberately partial.
