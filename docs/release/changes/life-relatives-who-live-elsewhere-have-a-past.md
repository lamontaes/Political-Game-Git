title: A relative who lives somewhere else now has schooling and a working life on their card.
---

Opening the card of a parent, sibling or other relative who lives outside your town used to tell only that they had children. Their card now tells where they went to school, how long they worked and where, when they moved away, and whether they are working now, written the first time you open them and the same every time.
