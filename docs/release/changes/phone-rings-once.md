title: A call says the phone rang once, not twice.
---

When someone called, the scene's arrival line said the phone rang and who was on the line, and the call's own first line said it again ("The phone rings. It's Hope, from Thompson's Market."). The calls about a job opening, a job favor, an interview and a donation now leave the ringing to the arrival line.
