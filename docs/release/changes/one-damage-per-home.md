title: A family's storm damage matches their house
---

Before, when a storm or flood struck a town, a family and the house they lived
in were judged separately, so the record could say the house was destroyed
while the family inside came through untouched, or the reverse.

Now each home is judged once, and the family living in it shares that result.
A destroyed house means a family that lost its home, with the injuries and
deaths that follow from it.
