id: state-legislative-term-limits

section: Changed
title: State legislators stop running once their state's term limit is reached
---

The 16 states that limit their legislators' terms now enforce the limit: a member whose next term would pass it may not seek another, and the seat opens. A state that enacts a limit in play takes the most common real rule, eight years in each chamber, and a repeal lifts it. Opening legislatures in those states start with every member under the limit.
