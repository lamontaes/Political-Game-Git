title: A skeptical juror asks what supports the charge before voting to convict
---

A juror who asks what supports a claim before relying on it now counts that
habit as one reason to vote not guilty. It weighs beside the evidence in the
case, the burden of proof and what the rest of the room says, so the same juror
on the same testimony without that streak still votes to convict.
