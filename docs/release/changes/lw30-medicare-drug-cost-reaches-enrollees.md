impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Medicare drug negotiation reaches older people with estimated savings
---

When Medicare drug-price negotiation is in force, the monthly health coverage
pass records estimated average drug-cost savings for named people age 65 and
older. Each exposure links to the law and pass that produced it, and records
the CMS and HHS basis used because individual plan enrollment and drug use are
not represented.
