section: Changed
title: Letting highway money pay for transit changes transit service
---

Before, a law letting highway money pay for transit changed nothing in the
world, and no place had a starting answer on the question.

Now every state, D.C. and territory starts with its real rule: 39 keep gas
tax money on roads by constitution or statute, and 17 let it pay for
transit. A state or city that changes its rule changes its transit service
two years later, about 6% more service where the money is freed and 6% less
where it is locked away, with each world drawing its own size between 1% and
18%. The size follows how much highway money states moved to transit when
federal law let them.
