title: How a legislator bargains is read from several sittings, not one talkative one
---

A member who stated three commitments in a single sitting was being described
as strongly showing their hand, on the strength of one appearance. The game
now waits until it has seen them answer at three separate sittings before it
says anything about how they bargain, which is what it always claimed to be
doing. A member it has not seen often enough is described as exactly that.
