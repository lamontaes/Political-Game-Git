title: A recall names the bill that was actually voted, and a township has its own budget
---

A recall petition over an ordinance now finds the very bill that enacted it, so it can no longer name members of a different body whose bill happened to share its number. A place governed by a township rather than a city or village now has its own town budget, voted by the township board.
