title: After you concede, the winner takes your call, gracious or gloating as they are.
---

When you concede a lost race, the winner now picks up. What they say follows who they are: "Thank you for calling. You ran a hard race, and I mean that." or "Took you long enough. It was never that close, you know." You can wish them well, keep it short or answer back. A sharp exchange strains the tie between you.
