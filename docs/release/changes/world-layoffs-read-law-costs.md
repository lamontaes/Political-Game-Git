title: A business now cuts hours or staff when a law raises what it pays in taxes or energy
---

A business decided whether to hire, lay off or close from its wages, supplies and compliance duties alone; the payroll taxes, property tax on its premises and energy price law charges it paid never counted. Every month's books now list every cost by name with the law behind it, and hiring, layoffs, hours and closing weigh all of them. Profit tax comes out of the business's cash, never as a month's loss. Dev mode's Why for a business shows the month's full bill.
