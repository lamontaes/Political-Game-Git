title: Letting the weeks run on stops for meetings you were invited to
---

When the story has nothing to ask you, it offers "Let the weeks run on". That
button used to jump one to four months without looking at your calendar. It
walked past every posted public meeting, party meeting and campaign shift you
had been invited to, and each one was recorded as having passed without an
answer. A Nevada life took the button about forty times and lost five years
that way.

The button now runs only as far as the morning of the next thing on your
calendar: a public meeting, a party meeting, a campaign shift, a commitment or
a dated matter. Its description says which one and on what date. On the day
itself, the choices include "Attend" for that meeting next to letting the weeks
run on, so going or not going is your decision. An unanswered social
invitation still lapses during the stretch unless you asked to be stopped for
invitations.
