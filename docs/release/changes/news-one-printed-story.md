title: A printed story is one record
---

Each story a paper prints is now kept once, as the news engine's article, with the press's own notes (what it reports, who wrote it, how it reached its readers) beside it by the same id. Readers of the paper, the news screen and the director see the same outlet and slant for the same story. A year of two drawn places printed the same stories, with the same slant, reach and readers, as before.
