---
id: councilors-go-along
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Other councilors decide their own ballots, and one with no view goes along
---

When the player sits on a town council, the other councilors' ballots on the
player's ordinance are now their own decisions, from their principles, their
record, the ordinance's sponsor and the voters they answer to. Before, each was
a coin flip. A councilor with no view and nothing else to weigh on an ordinance
now votes for it, as most real council votes are unanimous, where before they
sat it out. This holds for every council vote, not only the player's.
