title: The opening cuts from the country to you between letterbox bars
---

A new life opens in six cuts, each filling the screen between black
letterbox bars: the President at the House rostrum for the State of the Union
(or outside the Capitol in an inauguration year), your two senators and your
House member in their seats in the chamber, your governor at work, your
town's government at its dais, your family at home, and you where your first
day starts. Plaques name the people who matter to each cut. Every number now
waits behind the Ledger in the top bar, closed until you open it. The whole
opening loads during the loading screen, so no cut fills in late. The old
introduction panel is gone.
