At the filing office the overlay names each rule in words (Filing deadline, Filed before, Age, Residency) instead of its record id, no longer lists the deadline twice, fits the screen with its platform questions scrolling inside it, and is no longer covered by the news card. Two offices with the same title now read differently: by the year of their election (U.S. Senator (2026) and U.S. Senator (2030)), and seats elected at large on the same day by their place in the list.
