impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Towns open with as many stores, restaurants, clinics and other businesses as their workers fill, not a fixed three
---

Every town used to open with the same count of each kind of business (three stores, three restaurants, one clinic) whatever its size. Now each kind's count is the town's workers in that kind over the county's usual business size from the Census 2023 County Business Patterns, so a city opens with scores of stores and restaurants and one closing takes a handful of jobs, not a third of an industry. Dev mode's Calendar shows the town's businesses by kind with the reason for each count.
