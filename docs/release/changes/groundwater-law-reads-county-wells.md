section: Improved
title: A groundwater limit now hits farms only where they irrigate from wells.
---

Before, a law limiting groundwater pumping cut every farm's output by the same amount wherever it was. Now it cuts a farm by the share of its county's irrigation water that comes from wells: a county that irrigates from rivers loses nothing, one that irrigates from wells loses the whole cut.
