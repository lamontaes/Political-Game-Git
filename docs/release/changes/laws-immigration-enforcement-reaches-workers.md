title: Local immigration enforcement changes who keeps looking for work
---

Where a city or county partners with federal immigration enforcement, a resident without work authorization is less drawn to look for or stay in work, by the size research measured (about 6 percent), and citizens a little less (about half a percent). Work authorization is part of who each noncitizen is, set when the world is made at the share of noncitizens Pew estimates are unauthorized, so the same person has the same status every time. A town with no such partnership is unchanged.
