title: A defensive defendant weighs trial over admitting the charge in a plea
---

A person who protects their self-image when criticized now counts that habit as
one reason to take a charge to trial instead of standing in court to admit it
in a plea. It weighs beside the evidence, the sentence, their principles and
everything else they carry, so the same defendant with the same facts and no
such streak still takes the plea.
