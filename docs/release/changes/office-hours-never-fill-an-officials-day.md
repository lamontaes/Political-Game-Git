title: An official's sessions, meetings and days at the post now take up their day.
---

State legislators, the governor and town board members now spend their office's hours. A floor day in session takes the day at the Capitol. A district weekday is a day at the post for an official whose office is their job. A town body's meeting takes its evening, on top of a member's own job. Before, these hours never counted, so every official had the whole day free even on a day they sat in session. Colleagues dropped by all evening, and nobody was ever too busy. A floor the player stays away from takes none of their day, and the day the player lives is counted once.
