impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A governor's or mayor's address tells a bad term's jobs, pay and prices, and a good term's, in plain words.
---

A governor's State of the State or a mayor's address now says it when work, pay or prices got worse over the term: "Good jobs are harder to find than when this term began, and families are feeling it." It did not before, because the lines for it named the state or town again and were dropped after the greeting. Each of jobs, pay and prices now has four ways to say it went wrong and four to say it went right, and the governor can open the address on jobs where the speaker holds a stand on it.
