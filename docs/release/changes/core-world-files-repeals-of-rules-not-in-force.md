title: Ordinances no longer repeal rules your town never had
---

A council member or county commissioner who traded a vote for a section of their own could win a repeal of a rule the body had never made, and the ordinance then told you it repealed "the rules now in force" on it. A member now asks only for a section that would change the body's law, by the same rule that decides which bills get filed.
