title: Take part in a Senate Judiciary hearing
---

After a judicial nomination reaches an appointed Judiciary Committee, the chair can announce a public hearing. A Senator you control can cast their own committee organization vote, and an appointed chair you control can announce the hearing. On hearing day, a committee Senator you control can attend or decline. The hearing records who was present and what the nominee chose to say.
