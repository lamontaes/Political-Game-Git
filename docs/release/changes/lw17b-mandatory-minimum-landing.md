id: lw17b-mandatory-minimum-landing
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A mandatory minimum now reaches the defendant it binds.
---

Where the mandatory-minimum law is in force and the judge's saved decision says
it required a jail term, the sentence now writes a recorded exposure to that law
in the named defendant's history, shown through the court-rule channel. The
judge's decision and the court's term range still set the sentence; the landing
only records what the law meant for the person. Where the law is not in force,
or the judge was not bound, nothing is written.
