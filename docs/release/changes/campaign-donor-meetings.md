id: campaign-donor-meetings
impact: minor
section: Added
title: Meet donors at a supporter's home, and send campaign mail.
---

A campaign day can now be a house party: the supporter closest to you who would host opens their home, invites their own friends and family and the people your campaign has reached, and you meet the room, speak, and ask each guest for money. Every gift is named on the campaign's books within the legal limit. The Campaigns screen also has a Mail button with an amount you set: mailers go to the town's next households, as many as the money buys, never more than the campaign holds.
