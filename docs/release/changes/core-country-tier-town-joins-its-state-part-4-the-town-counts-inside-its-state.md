title: Your town and county now count inside your state's figures, so what happens at home moves the state's jobs and population.
---

Each month the state's own counts give up the people, workers, jobs and cash your town and county hold, the rest of the state runs as before, and the state's figures are the rest plus your town. When a business in town closes, your state's jobs fall by its jobs the next month.
