title: The game opens with the President's address and the evening news
---

A new life now opens with the President's inaugural address, full screen and sentence by sentence. The camera moves from the President, with the Vice President and congressional leaders behind them, to Congress and your state's members, to your governor and your state legislature's leaders, and then to your family, naming each person and their office as it passes. Between those shots the picture cuts to whatever each sentence is about. Then you're home that evening with the night's news to pick up. All of it is composed from the world's records while the game loads.
