title: Trust, persuasion and news habits weigh temperament at the size research finds
---

How far a resident trusts a speaker, how far an argument moves them, and how closely they keep up with the news now lean on their temperament at the sizes studies of trust, political discussion and news use find, where before temperament weighed at a general size several times larger.
