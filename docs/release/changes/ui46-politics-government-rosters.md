title: Government shows who holds each seat, and Politics has one route to each place
---

Politics → Government now lists Congress seat by seat with each current
member, recorded vacancies with their date and reason, and seats with no
current record, with counts for each. A "Represented by" line names your
districts and senators apart from where you are and your home. Issues and
budget follows the place you chose in Government. Campaigns now holds every
campaign, Your office holds only the office, and transit and tax proposal forms
appear only when your office can use them, laid out as objective, proposal and
filing.
