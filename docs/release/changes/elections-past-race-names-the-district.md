section: Changed
title: An election story names the seat's district, or says the seat is at large.
---

On the opening's front page, every legislature race read the same: "won the race for State Senator". Now a story names the district where the seat's record numbers it ("won the race for State Senator in District 4", headlined "takes District 4"), and says "the at-large race" for a seat elected at large, such as a Virgin Islands senator's or a council's at-large seat. A district its records name by place, as in Massachusetts, New Hampshire and Vermont, or a ward still prints the race for the office.
