title: A bill with two steps on one law states its purpose once.
---

A bill that changes one law in two steps reads "An ordinance to set the minimum wage." instead of naming the purpose twice. The bill text also has the words for a section with its own date and for the sections the usual rule dates, for the governing work on raises phased in by dated steps.
