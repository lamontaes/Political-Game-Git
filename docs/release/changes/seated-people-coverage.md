title: Sitting figures now know where they meet the chair
---

Every sitting figure in the library now carries a measured point where it rests on a seat, so a room can place it in a chair instead of guessing. Figures drawn turned rather than square to the camera were the ones this was missing for, and they are also now recognised as the sitting poses they are rather than being set aside, which brings seven more of them, of several builds, into the set a room can draw from once they are approved for play.
