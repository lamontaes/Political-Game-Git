title: One filing deadline at the counter
---

Each race now has one filing deadline. The clerk, the filing panel, the petition's due date and the race list all read it from the same rule: the state's filing law for your side where it sets its own date before the race's first vote, otherwise the race's calendar. The clerk can no longer give you one date while the form shows another.
