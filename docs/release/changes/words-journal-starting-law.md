title: The journal tells a law you learned changed your costs, even an old one
---

When you learn that a law already on the books changed what you pay, your journal now says so by what the law is about: "I learned the law on electricity and the grid is behind what I pay now." Before, only a law a bill had passed in play was told, and the older ones went missing.
