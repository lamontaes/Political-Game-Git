title: A vote story names the law and whom it reaches, and the address's news story has a lede, what happens next and a quote.
---

A vote story in the news names the law, what it does and whom it reaches, in a lede and a body. The address's story quotes the President's own stand and says what happens next in Congress. A law that changed nothing no longer prints as a story.
