title: Fifty painted places now stand behind the opening and the places you go
---

Fifty painted places, 180 pictures in all, are now in the game. The opening
tour shows your state capitol (a tower in Florida, Louisiana, Nebraska and
North Dakota), the U.S. Capitol behind Congress, city hall behind your town,
and your town's main street behind your life so far. During play, places
that had no picture (school, work, the campaign doors and phone bank, the
governor's office, courtrooms and council rooms, and the street on the way
to a meeting) now show one, matched to the time of day and to rainy days.
Your job decides your workplace, and your home decides the street you
canvass. Home and the meeting room keep their current rooms.
