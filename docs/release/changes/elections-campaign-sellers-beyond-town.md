title: A campaign's polls, mail and halls are bought from real sellers
---

A campaign now buys from a business in your town when one fits, and otherwise from the region's pollsters, printers and halls outside it, paid from the town's outside account like every other purchase beyond the town's edge. Mailer postage is always paid outside, to the Postal Service. No made-up seller appears in town to take the money any more.
