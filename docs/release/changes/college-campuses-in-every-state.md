title: Colleges in every state, painted from their real landmarks
---

Fifty-two college campuses now have paintings, each drawn from a photo of its
best-known landmark: the flagship university of nearly every state and
territory, plus Harvard, Princeton, Penn, Cornell, Georgetown and Montana
State. A college without its own painting borrows one that
fits: a campus from the same state first, otherwise one from the same region
with the same climate and ground, so a Kentucky college never shows a desert.
The game can now pick these pictures; showing them where your character
studies comes next.
