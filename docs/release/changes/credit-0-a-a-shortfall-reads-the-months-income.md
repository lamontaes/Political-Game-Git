title: Households borrow only when the month's money really falls short, and retirees are judged on their pensions.
---

Nearly every household in town asked for a loan in the first month, because any household with less than a month of costs in the bank asked, whatever pay or pension was coming in, and a retiree read as having no income at all. The storefront lender was emptied in weeks and stayed empty. Now a household asks only when its cash and the month's pay, Social Security, pension and unemployment benefits cannot cover the month, and the bank judges every household on that same income.
