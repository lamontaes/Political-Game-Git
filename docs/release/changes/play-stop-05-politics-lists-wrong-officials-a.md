title: Politics lists your own members first and every chamber leader by post
---

Politics no longer lists a chamber's leader from another state as one of your own members, or every member of your state's legislature in one long column. It shows your own U.S. senators, U.S. representative and state legislators first, then the President, the Vice President and each chamber's leader named by post, chamber and the state they serve. Every other member is under the State and Federal filters.
