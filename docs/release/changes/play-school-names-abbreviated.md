title: School names are spelled out
---

Who you are names schools as people write them. A school record's abbreviations are spelled out: "El Sch" reads "Elementary School", "MS" reads "Middle School", and "CUSD" reads "Community Unit School District". "El" is spelled out only before "School" or at the end of a name, so "El Paso" stays as it is.
