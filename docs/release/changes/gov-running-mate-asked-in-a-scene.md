title: Running for governor, you ask your running mate in person, and they can say no
---

On the Campaign board, the people offered as your running mate now have an Ask button instead of Choose. Asking plays a conversation: the person answers from their own life, saying yes, or no with their reason (their own race this year, the seat they would give up, how far apart you are on what they care about most, something they heard about you, or that you are a long shot). A no is remembered, and you can ask the same person again after a month, or sooner once what they objected to has changed. Having met them, or having raised money since, can turn a no into a yes. "Choose for me" names someone who would say yes.
