title: New-life setup shows your town or state, not the White House
---

Before, every new-life screen showed the White House behind it, whatever place
you chose. Now the screens show your chosen town's main street, and your
state's capitol until you pick a town.

Before, making a new life showed the White House behind every setup step,
wherever the life was set.

Now the setup and the start of the life show the chosen state's capitol, and
once a town is picked, that town's main street or city hall.
