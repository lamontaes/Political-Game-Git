title: Heirs are paid an estate's cash when probate closes even if its house has not sold
---

An estate whose house was still for sale kept all its cash until the house sold, which in a quiet town could take years. The heirs now receive the cash when probate closes, and the house's proceeds follow to the same heirs when it sells.
