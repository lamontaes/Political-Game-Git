title: A scheduled law that a later statute overrides no longer makes news when its day comes
---

A law row scheduled in the starting law (Ohio's $11.00 wage on January 1, 2026) still reported "took effect" after a statute had ended the state wage, so the paper and the people's talk carried a rise that never happened. A row another statute overrides now records nothing.
