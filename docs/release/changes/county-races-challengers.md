title: County races draw challengers again
---

Townspeople now run for county seats such as commissioner, sheriff and clerk. A county's filing fee no longer keeps everyone out: people weigh it against what they earn while filing is open, and where the county takes petition signatures instead of the fee, against the signatures their friends can give. About a third of county races now have more than one candidate, close to how often real county races are contested (some counties see none, others most).
