section: Changed
title: The title screen shows your character
---

When you have a saved game, the title screen now shows your most recent
character standing in a public hearing room, looking the way they did when
you last saved and dressed for the room. People standing in rooms are now
drawn at the right size for each room. Furniture only hides the people
standing behind it. On the opening tour, the President and other officials
wear suits.
