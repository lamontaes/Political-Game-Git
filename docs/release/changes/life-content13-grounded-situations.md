title: New everyday moments at work and in local politics, only when your life has them
---

A few more ordinary moments can now come up: a break shared with a colleague,
being paired with somebody from your political group to knock on doors, and —
when the people at your job really do direct your work — your supervisor asking
you to pick up an extra shift, or to work one that falls on a class you have
booked.

These only happen when your life actually contains the people and the plans
involved. A job and a course are not treated as a commute or a clash, a
coworker is not treated as your boss, and the game no longer says you covered
a colleague's shift just because you agreed to. That comes up later only if
you actually worked it.
