title: State legislative races are decided on the state's real election day
---

A race for a state house or senate seat used to be decided 28 days after you
filed. It is now decided on the state's regular November general election: even
years in most states, odd years in New Jersey and Virginia, and every four years
in Louisiana and Mississippi. A filing made after the field closes, sixty days
before the election, stands in the next one. The campaign screen names the
election date before you file.
