title: Councils elected by ward have wards
---

A town council elected by ward now has a map of its wards. Each ward seat is
filled from its ward's residents. Only that ward's residents can run for the
seat, and only they vote for it. A vacancy is filled by someone who lives in
the ward. A player's council campaign runs for the seat of their own ward.

How many seats are ward seats comes from the town's charter where it has been
read. Elsewhere it is estimated from the national shares of councils elected
all at large, all by ward, or both, and the larger towns in each state are the
ones that elect by ward.

The map is redrawn after each census. A council draws it to keep each of its
members in a ward of their own, within the population deviation the courts
allow. An independent commission draws equal wards without regard to where
members live. A member whose home falls outside their seat's ward cannot run
for that seat again.
