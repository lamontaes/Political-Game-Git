title: Kids go to school, the town is a representative sample, and the counted population lives and dies
---

School-age people now decide each school day whether to go to school, and every town with a police department has a public school district that keeps the federal holidays. The town's full residents are a representative sample of the place, not just the player's circle. The counted population has births and deaths at the state's rates and people moving in and out, and newcomers carry names of their birth year. Counted voters start at their county's 2024 presidential lean and count only eligible citizens. The game keeps less in memory as years pass, and saves load from a world snapshot plus the moves made since.
