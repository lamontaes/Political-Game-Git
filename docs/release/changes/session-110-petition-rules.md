title: Petition forms have explicit legal terms
---

A shared petition rule gate reads local initiative and protest referendum terms from the compiled municipal packs and state initiative, referendum and constitutional initiative terms from per-place rows. Read constitutional provisions retain their signature bases, deadlines, distribution and refusal reasons; unread terms carry explicit median estimates. Proposition petition lifecycle dispatch remains pending the signer and ballot integrations.
