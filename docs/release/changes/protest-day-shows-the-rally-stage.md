title: A protest you organized or attended shows the rally stage on the day it is held
---

On the day a protest is held, the screen shows the rally stage for the person
who organized it and for each resident whose attendance was recorded. The
organizer and the recorded attendees stand in the picture; nobody is added to
fill the stage, and a resident who did not go sees no stage. The next day the
stage clears.
