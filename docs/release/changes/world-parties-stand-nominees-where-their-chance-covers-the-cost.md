title: A party no longer stands a nominee in a state or federal seat it cannot realistically win
---

Every seat elsewhere in the country used to be contested by both parties however lopsided. Now a party stands a nominee only where its chance of taking the seat is worth the cost, so a safe seat can go uncontested and a close one draws both.
