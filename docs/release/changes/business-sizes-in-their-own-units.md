title: Six business and bank sizes are written in their own units
---

Nothing changes in play. Six sizes in the town business books and the
nation's banks are now written in their own units, such as days of revenue for
a credit line and percent a month for loans that reprice, instead of as
shares. Each is still marked as a placeholder until it is measured.
