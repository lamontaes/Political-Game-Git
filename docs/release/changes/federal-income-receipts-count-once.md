title: Federal income tax has one receipt path
---

The federal top-income-tax law no longer has a second treasury-share estimate.
Income-tax receipts continue through saved paycheck liabilities and payment
allocations into the common government account and monthly cash settlement.
Replaying a paycheck or reopening its save preserves the same allocations,
transfers and receipts. Other federal-law sizing remains separate work.
