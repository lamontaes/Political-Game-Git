title: A neighbor's invitation to a cause asks you to a real town meeting, and you answer from where you stand
---

When a neighbor shares a cause with you, they now ask you to the next town meeting that is posted, name the body and the night, and you answer from your own view on the cause or the office you hold, instead of the same stock lines about phone calls and Thursdays in every town. If you say yes, the meeting remembers it: you kept your word by going and broke it by staying away.
