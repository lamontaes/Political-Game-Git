title: Skipping ahead no longer stops when a short-handed employer has to dip into its savings for two paydays in the same month.
---

When you skip time, a month's paydays are all settled together on the first of the next month. An employer short of cash on two of those paydays now draws its savings once for each, and the skipped month finishes.
