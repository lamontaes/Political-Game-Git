title: Veterans in town are now paid from the federal veterans line, and a raise to it shows who it reached
---

Veterans in town now draw benefits from the federal veterans line. When Congress raises it, their benefits rise by the same share, and the law's page and dev mode's bill card name them, count the counted veterans and show the extra dollars.
