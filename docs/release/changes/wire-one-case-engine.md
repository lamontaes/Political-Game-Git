title: Arrests follow the crimes people report, and one court keeps every case
---

Arrests for assault, robbery, burglary and theft now follow the crimes the town's households suffered and reported to police that month, so a quiet month in a small town brings no such arrest. Every case, sentence and term in jail or prison, for a named defendant or for the town's wider population, is kept by the one justice engine, and the county and state pay for each term by the days it actually runs.
