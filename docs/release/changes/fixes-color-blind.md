title: Options has a color-blind colors setting
---

Options now has "Color-blind colors", off by default. Turning it on swaps the game's signal colors (party colors, the lie scales' red, vote colors) to a color-blind-safe set, and a vote ring also says yes, no or abstain by its stroke: solid, dashed or dotted. The default look is unchanged.
