title: A candidate who knows you can ask you for money
---

A candidate who knows you (family, a friend, your party) now asks you for money instead of the game giving it for you. Their requests wait under Politics, Elections, with the race, the sum you would give and the cash you hold. Give that sum, give another whole-dollar amount, or decline. A candidate you turn down does not ask again.
