title: A legislative winner takes the seat when the term begins, in every state
---

Outside Kentucky, a player who won a seat in a state legislature was seated on
election night. No state works that way, and a result grants no authority of
its own. Now every legislative seat begins on the day its term begins. Kentucky
keeps its researched rule. Every other state uses one general rule until its
own is researched: the term begins on January 1 after the election, and it
lasts two years in a lower chamber and four in a senate, or whatever term the
game already knows for that office.

Between the result and that day, Your office shows "Before you take office",
and the campaign's closing line says when the term begins. When the day comes,
the seat is taken up and the legislature's work opens. When the term ends, the
seat ends with it.
