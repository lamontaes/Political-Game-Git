title: The News overview says what the laws in force changed where you live
---

The News overview now has a "What the laws changed" section. When a law in
force answers a policy question differently from where the place began and that
moved a condition where you live, News names the law, says which condition it
moved, and gives the value with and without it. Before, those effects were
worked out and never shown. A place where no law has moved anything shows
nothing, and no line names a source.
