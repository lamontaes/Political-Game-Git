title: Every made-up person gets their age and birthday the same way
---

Townspeople, the residents of a test world, your own character and the
officials and colleagues an authored start names now all take their age from
one table of age ranges by role and their birthday from one rule. Nobody's
birthday changes: the same world gives the same people the same birthdays as
before.
