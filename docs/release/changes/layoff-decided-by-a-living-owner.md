title: A business whose owner has died no longer lays people off in the dead owner's name.
---

A layoff, a raise or a hire at a business whose owner has died is decided by its best-paid living worker or the business itself, never by the dead owner, so a laid-off worker's grudge is aimed at someone who was there.
