section: Changed
title: A bribe offered to you names the money, the bill or contract it buys, and who is behind it.
---

When someone offers you a bribe, or presses you after you turned one down, they now say what the records hold: how much money, the bill whose vote they want or the contract they want steered, and where a lobbyist makes the offer, the group they speak for, the side it wants and what the bill costs its members a year. Your answers name the same things. A friend, a second ask, a small gift or a desperate offerer still sounds like one. Stock lines that said the same thing in every town are gone.
