title: A member you bargain with has a manner
section: Added
---

Members you bargain with now have a manner, read from the commitments they have
actually made rather than from anything invented about them. A member who has
answered plainly before has a reason to answer plainly again, and one who has
kept things open has a reason to keep them open. A member the game has not seen
answer often enough has no manner at all, and the sitting weighs what it always
did.
