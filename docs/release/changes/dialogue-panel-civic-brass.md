title: Conversations sit above the bar in a calmer green and brass panel
---

When you talk with someone, the panel now stays above the bar at the bottom
of the screen. Before, at 1280×800 the bar covered its last lines and the
replies under them, and a click on a reply could land on the bar instead.

The panel itself is quieter: deep green that fades at its edges so the room
shows through, one brass edge, and cream lettering. The faces of the people
you are talking with are larger at its top left. Your replies read as lines
you could say rather than a stack of boxes, and the Lie button stands beside
them, where it swaps in the false answers in place of the true ones. Nothing
in the panel moves on its own.
