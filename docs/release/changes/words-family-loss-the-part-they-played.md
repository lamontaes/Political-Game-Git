title: After a lost race, the talk at home comes from the relative who worked it or whose money went in.
---

The talk at home after you lose a race now comes only from someone in your household who put something into it: a relative on your volunteer list, or the household's money you gave your own campaign. They open on what they put in and how close the count was ("We lost by 12 votes. I put my time in too, so tell me what you want from me next."). If nobody at home helped, no scene is staged for it. When you say you'll run again, their answer is kept, and it counts the next time anyone in that household weighs a run. The old general comfort lines are gone, including a sister the records never named.
