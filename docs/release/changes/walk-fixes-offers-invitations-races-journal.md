title: Accepted jobs, past invitations, race results and the Journal read as they should
---

A job offer you have accepted no longer says it is waiting for your answer.
The day says when the work begins, and Jobs drops the Accept and Refuse
buttons once you have answered.

Party meetings and other optional invitations whose time has passed leave the
Places list instead of piling up there.

At a public meeting, the meeting's buttons sit in their own card above the Day
and Week controls instead of on top of them.

After a race, the result comes first. The campaign log says a repeated session
once, with how many there were and when, and your line in the results is
marked "(you)". The report on your campaign plan speaks to you, in dollars:
"You chose direct outreach in Presque Isle, with no money set aside for it."

The Journal says a sentence that recurs through a chapter once, after the
chapter's other events, with how many more there were and the date of the
last.

Employer names no longer end in "(fictional)", and a job's national pay reads
like "$17.03 an hour, $35,410 a year".
