title: A funeral in your family now plays, and thin scenes read differently each time you meet them
---

When someone close to you died, the scene where a mourner comes to ask you something could never be worded, so it never appeared. It now plays: the mourner speaks of the person who died as they were to them, and what they ask follows something that person did or cared about on record.
