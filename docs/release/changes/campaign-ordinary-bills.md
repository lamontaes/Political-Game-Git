title: Campaigns pay ordinary bills, and spending reports show what the money went to
---

Campaign committees now pay the ordinary costs of running: office rent,
signs and printing, postage, travel, events, phones and software, food and
bank fees. Each bill is paid on its own day, two to four days a week, to a
local business, and appears on the committee's monthly spending report with
what it was for and a running "Spent to date" total. Your rivals' committees
pay bills every week; yours pays them in the week after you did campaign
work, and never touches money set aside for an advertising buy you already
approved. The kinds of cost and how much each takes are placeholders until
the research on real campaign spending comes back.
