title: The people in a place stand at its turned spots and by its walls
---

The people a scene names, and the people at work there, now appear at every
spot a place picture marks for them. Before, a spot turned toward a desk,
a counter or a speaker, and a spot by a wall, stayed empty, because the
people art has no turned faces and no leaning pose painted yet. In a
generated world, four people put in each of the 138 painted places were all
drawn in 135 of them, up from 28. They now stand there facing the room, posed
for what they are doing. The executives on the opening tour now stand in the
Oval Office. Seats that face away from the viewer stay empty until a seated
person seen from behind is painted.
