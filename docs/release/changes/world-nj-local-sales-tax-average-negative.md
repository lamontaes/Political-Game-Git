title: New Jersey no longer starts with a local sales tax below zero
---

New Jersey levies no general local sales tax, but its starting law carried an average local rate below zero, taken from a table that folds Salem County's and the Urban Enterprise Zones' half state rate into it. New Jersey now has no local rate, like the other states without local sales taxes, and its sales carry the state rate.
