title: Hair sits properly on every head

Tall hairstyles such as buns, top knots and afros are no longer cut off at the
top of the picture. On fuller bodies, a band of bare scalp no longer shows
above the hair. Where hair falls on both sides of the face, the back of the
hair now shows behind the neck instead of the room. Ears are no longer faded.
