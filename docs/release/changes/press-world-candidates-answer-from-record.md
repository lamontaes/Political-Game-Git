title: Candidates answer reporters from their voting record, and some shade it.
---

A candidate who has voted on a question now answers a reporter from that vote, or shades the answer toward where the town stands. Who shades follows their honesty and how far the vote sits from the town: about a quarter of people would shade a vote the town opposes as strongly as a town can, fewer when the town is divided, and nobody when the town stands with the vote. A shaded answer names the vote it contradicts, so the town's reporter can check it and print the record against the candidate's word.
