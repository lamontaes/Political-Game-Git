title: The Senate and House floors in the opening are filled with their members
---

Before, the Congress card showed one standing figure on an empty House floor,
the year card showed an empty Senate floor, and members of Congress came
dressed in hoodies.

Now each floor holds members from its seat roster, with your own members
and your state's delegation first, facing the room. Officeholders dress
for business. Your life's card shows whoever the household record says
lives with you.
