section: Fixed
title: Automatic bills refuse missing or incompatible saved current-law terms
---

Automatic bills reuse complete parameters from a compatible saved law instead
of drawing amounts and service choices from authored defaults. Missing terms,
changed coverage or fiscal periods, and incompatible saved clauses refuse
drafting. Sponsor terms for first bills and their valid callers remain held.
