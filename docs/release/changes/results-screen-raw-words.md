title: Election night names a district as District 14 and an at-large seat by its state, never a code.
---

The places a race was counted in read as words on election night: a state legislative or U.S. House district as "District 14", a state's one at-large House seat as the state's name, and a state as its name. Blank and refused exit-poll answers read as words too.
