title: Governors and mayors give a yearly address too.
---

Your state's governor gives a State of the State each January and your town's mayor a State of the City each February. Each picks a kind (a candid reckoning, a railing, a hopeful lift, a victory lap, words for the dead or a policy push) from how the state or town is doing and who is speaking, tells its bad news as well as its good, and asks the legislature or council for what they believe in.
