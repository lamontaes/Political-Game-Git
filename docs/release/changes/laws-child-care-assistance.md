title: Child care assistance is state law in all 56 places
---

A state's child care income limit is now state law, researched for all 56 places, that a legislature can raise or lower from the bill paper. The state pays the average child care subsidy to working families under the limit with a child under 13, for the 20 percent of eligible children who receive one.
