title: The Northern Marianas' municipalities elect their mayors and councils.
---

A life in the Northern Mariana Islands used to open with nothing local to run for. Each village now belongs to its municipality: Saipan, Rota, or Tinian and Aguiguan. Each municipality has a Mayor and a three-member council, both held by people on day one. Both races are on the Elections page, filed with the Commonwealth Election Commission in an even-year November. A Mayor must be 25 or older and a council member 21 or older, and the Elections page shows the right age for each race.
