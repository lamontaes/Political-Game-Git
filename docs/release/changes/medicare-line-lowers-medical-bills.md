title: When Congress raises Medicare, its members' medical bills fall and their households leave less unpaid.
---

A change to the federal Medicare line now shows where it lands: each person on Medicare pays a smaller share of their care, and their household owes less on its medical bills, for named and counted residents alike. The law table can now follow the line to those bills.
