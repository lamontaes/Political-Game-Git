title: A bribe offer's record names the contract or bill it wants, for the scene to say
---

The offer, refusal and pressing records of a bribe now carry what the money is for: the bill for a vote, or the contract award and the work it pays for. The scene reads it so the person offering can name it; the lines that say it follow.
