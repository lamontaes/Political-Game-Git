id: legislation-own-vote-stops
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: As a legislator you are stopped to cast your own floor vote.
---

As a state legislator or member of Congress, the game now stops you the evening before a floor day for each bill on your chamber's agenda, and the vote on record is the one you cast. Before, your vote was cast for you on the committee's day, a week early, so the floor-vote stop never came.
