title: People's pronouns, past meetings and office names read correctly
---

A person's card uses their own pronouns ("She is your mom", not "They are your
mom"). A conversation recalls what you said in your own voice ("You said you
would go to the meeting"). Once a posted public meeting has happened, it no
longer waits on you, and nobody asks whether you are going to it. Offices read
the way people say them: "A seat in the Nebraska Legislature" in the list, and
"Put your name in for mayor" on the button.
