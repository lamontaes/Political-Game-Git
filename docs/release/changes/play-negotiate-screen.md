title: Choose the deal to offer a colleague about your bill
---

An officeholder can now choose, on the office sheet, what to offer each colleague who votes on a bill of theirs: nothing, a favor, or backing the colleague's own item. When they next talk about the bill, the colleague decides it from their own position and the deal is on the bill's record. This also brings back the outlet picker for an interview, which had dropped off the office sheet.
