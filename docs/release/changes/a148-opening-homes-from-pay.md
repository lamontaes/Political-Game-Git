title: Where a town household lives now follows what it earns
---

At the start of a game, each household in town lives in the home its pay
carries: an owner when the pay covers the town's monthly payment, in a house,
a large house, a farmhouse for a farm household or a mobile home, and a renter
in an apartment or rowhouse otherwise. The kind of home is no longer chosen by
chance.
