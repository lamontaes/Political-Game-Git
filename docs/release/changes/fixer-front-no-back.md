title: The front's panels have a close X and no Back buttons
---

New game, Advanced, Play as and the creator's three steps now share one close X that steps out one level, in place of a Back button on each. Each panel is named for what it is (New game, Advanced, Start a life) instead of carrying the game's name, and the gold rule under the heading is gone.
