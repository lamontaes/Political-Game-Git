title: Congress shows once in the opening
---

The opening's year screen no longer stands on the Senate floor with the
senators, and no longer counts the Senate and the House by party: it stands
outside the Capitol. Congress is told once, on its own stop on the House floor,
where its chart counts every seat in both chambers: each party's members, the
vacant seats and any seat with no recorded holder.
