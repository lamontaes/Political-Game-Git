In the separate core prototype, looking up a local institution now serves a person's cause instead of their need for company. Only people with a cause look things up, and only for the kinds of places they do not yet know: an office with staff, a place to meet or a group to join. Once they know one of a kind, they stop looking that kind up.

A death now reaches the relatives who learn of it as grief, starting at how close they were. Grief pulls them toward rest and toward their family and holds back organizing, and it fades at each person's own pace, spread so that about 46% have little grief by 6 months and about 16% still grieve at 18 months. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build.
