In the separate core prototype, looking up a local institution now serves a person's cause instead of their need for company. Only people with a cause look things up, and only for the kinds of places they do not yet know: an office with staff, a place to meet or a group to join. Once they know one of a kind, they stop looking that kind up. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build.
