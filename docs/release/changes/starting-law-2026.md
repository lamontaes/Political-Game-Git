title: Every state starts with its real laws on eleven policy questions.
---

A new game now knows what each state's law already says on eleven policy
questions: whether it has expanded Medicaid, is a right-to-work state, runs
paid family leave, sells cannabis to adults, requires a permit to carry a
concealed gun, limits rent increases statewide, offers universal preschool,
sets a minimum wage above the federal one, taxes wages, has ended cash bail,
and the federal work requirements for food and health assistance. Lawmakers
now argue from that starting point, filing bills to change the law rather than
to enact what is already in force, and a law passed in play replaces the old
one once it takes effect.
