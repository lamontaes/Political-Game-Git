section: Changed
title: News stories add what came before, name who died in office, and stop printing the game's own notes.
---

A story used to print its event's one-line record twice, once as the headline
and again as the body. Now a story adds what the world actually holds about it:
the earlier dated steps of the same development ("Earlier in this story"), the
previous reading of an unemployment, inflation or growth figure and whether it
rose or fell, the size and place of a flood or storm and how many that place has
had this year, and a dateline for readers outside the place.

A death or incapacity in office is now reported by name and office ("William
Rogers, Governor of Kentucky, died on July 1."), with what happens to each seat.
Those stories used to print the simulation's own ruling notes, including lines
such as "the game has not compiled NH's rule", which no longer reach the page.
Nothing is invented: no quotes, reactions or causes are written for a story.
