title: Between winning and taking office, a winner has a transition
---

A player who won a Kentucky House or Senate seat read "You hold no office in
this life yet" under Your office for the eleven months until the term began.
A governor-elect saw nothing between the result and the first day but the
Qualify button on Campaigns.

Your office now opens on "Before you take office" for anyone who has won and
not yet begun: what you are called until then (Member-elect of the House of
Representatives, Governor-elect), the day the term begins and how many days
away it is, and that the office and its powers are not yours until then. A
governor-elect is reminded to qualify under Campaigns.

Below that are the services open before the term: for a legislator, new
legislator orientation, the caucus meeting and committee requests, in the
weeks before the session; for a governor, assembling a transition team and a
briefing from the outgoing administration. Each shows when it opens and
closes, can be attended while it is open, and reads as missed if its window
closes without you. The game also carries the House's and Senate's own
November orientation, office lottery and party organizing meetings, ready for
the day a player can run for Congress. Every state and every locality uses one
general transition until its own is researched.
