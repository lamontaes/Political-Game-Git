title: As governor you decide a state of emergency yourself.
---

When a disaster is recorded in your state, the game stops you the evening before and the orders panel offers a state of emergency, which you declare or let pass. Before, the engine declared it for you. An executive order your staff brings, such as restoring voting rights after a sentence, is also yours to issue, revoke or set aside.
