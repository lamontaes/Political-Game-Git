title: Townspeople's views on rent, wages, Medicaid, preschool and public pay now come from their own lives
---

A renter and an owner next door no longer hold the same view on rent stabilization or a property-tax cap just because they lean the same way. Whether a household rents, its income, whether someone is working, has a private health plan, has a young child at home or works for a government now moves their stance on the questions those bear on, measured against the country as a whole, so a town of renters and a town of owners come out differently. How much each person weighs their own stake follows their character. When a life changes, such as a lost job, the view moves with it. Polls, the paper's read of where the town stands, door answers and votes all read the new stances. A candidate's own answers start from the same place, so what they say on rent or wages reflects their own life too.
