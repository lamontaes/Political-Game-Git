title: You say what became of your other parent
section: Changed
---

When a custom start puts a child in a home with one parent, the "At home" step
now asks about the other parent: living, living somewhere else, or has died.
The life records what you said. No parent is dead at the start of a life unless
you say so, and an unanswered question leaves no other parent on the record
rather than choosing one for you.
