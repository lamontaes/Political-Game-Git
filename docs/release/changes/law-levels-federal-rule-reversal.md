title: Federal Medicaid requirements can take effect and be repealed
---

A Medicaid work requirement enacted by Congress now governs under the same rule as the starting federal law. Repealing it restores the applicable state or local rule. When two acts take effect on the same day, the later enactment governs.

The same sourced precedence rule preserves federal SNAP work conditions in participating jurisdictions, without applying them to territories with separate nutrition-assistance programs.
