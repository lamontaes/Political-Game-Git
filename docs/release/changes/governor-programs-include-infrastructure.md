title: A governor's agenda can include infrastructure
---

A governor's first-year priorities and budget requests were drawn from
thirteen kinds of public program. The legislature drafts bills in twenty. The
seven missing were transit, bridges, broadband, water service lines, disaster
recovery, utility resilience and critical infrastructure, so a governor could
never set any of them as a priority, and money the legislature adopted for
one reached the office titled "public work". A governor now draws from the
same twenty the legislature does. Priorities already recorded in a save keep
their subjects.
