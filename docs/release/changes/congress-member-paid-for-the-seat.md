title: A member of Congress is paid for the seat
---

A player who won a seat in Congress earned only what their old job paid. From
January 3, the seat is now the member's job, paid weekly, and it ends with the
term. Other jobs are left as they were until the rules on outside work are on
file.

The Government screen told a new member only that their House district was not
recorded. It now shows the district of the seat they hold.

Pay is the same placeholder office salary every office uses until real salaries
are researched.
