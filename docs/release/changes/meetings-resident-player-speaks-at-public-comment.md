title: A resident can go to a council or county board meeting and speak
---

A resident who holds no seat can now mark a posted council or county board meeting on the Calendar, be in the room at its hour, and speak for or against any item before the body votes; the members who heard them then appear on People. At a board large enough to work through committees, a resident can attend and read the bills, and speaking there comes with a later change.
