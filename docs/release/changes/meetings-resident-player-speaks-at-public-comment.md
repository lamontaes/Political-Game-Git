A resident who holds no seat can now mark a posted council or county board meeting on the Calendar, be in the room at its hour, and speak for or against any item before the board votes; the members who heard them then appear on People. Every town and county body now files its agenda the day before and votes at the meeting, so the town hears of an item before the vote and a meeting the player is in the room for decides the same bills the same way as one they are not at.
