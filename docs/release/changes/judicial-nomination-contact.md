Adds a recorded Presidential interview and nomination path for a pending
federal judicial seat. An active interview spends 30 minutes and records the
candidate's own answer about privacy and government information requests as a
dated decision, event, and limited rights view when the candidate has an
affirmative recorded privacy value. Without that basis, the answer
and philosophy remain unknown. A nomination names that candidate but does not
fill the seat. An old calendar due item can no longer choose a Chief Justice
or manufacture Senate consent.

The selection adapter is available for a later player screen. Senate ballots,
the hearing producer, and browser acceptance remain separate work.
