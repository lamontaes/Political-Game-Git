id: fare-free-transit-raises-ridership
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Each state keeps how many transit rides its residents take, and a law making transit free raises them.
---

Every state, D.C. and Puerto Rico now keep how many transit rides their
residents take a year, starting from the Federal Transit Administration's 2024
count: 22.5 rides per resident nationally, from under 1 in Idaho to 171 in New
York. A law that makes local transit free to ride raises that about 42% a
month after it takes effect, where it is law: a state law across the state, a
city ordinance in that city. Before, a fare-free law changed nothing.
