section: Changed
title: Residents out of work find regional jobs, and the opening counts the region's rate
---

A resident who is out of work no longer waits for a job in their own town. Like people who live elsewhere, they find work in the wider region at a pace that follows their state's unemployment and their own traits, and the job pays through the same payroll as a commute. The opening also counts the region's whole expected number of job seekers instead of rounding away a fraction twice.
