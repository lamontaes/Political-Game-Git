title: A lawmaker asking for your vote gives the reason for the part of the bill they wrote
---

When a lawmaker asks for your vote on their bill, the figure they name and the reason they give now come from the part of the bill they wrote that matters most to them. A section they traded in to win someone else's vote no longer changes why they say they filed it. How they vote still weighs the whole bill.
