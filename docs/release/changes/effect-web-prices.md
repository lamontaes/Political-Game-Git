section: Improved
title: Prices, electric bills, bus trips and broadband follow the laws behind them
---

Shop prices in a town now move when a tariff, a bottle deposit or rough roads add to a business's costs, because each business sets its own price from them. A clean-electricity standard raises each household's electric bill by the researched amount once its cost is felt, a fare-free bus brings more riders without charging them for the extra trips, and a public broadband offer reaches more households that did not have a connection, most of all the poorest.
