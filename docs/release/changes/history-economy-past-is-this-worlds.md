title: The economy's past before a new life begins is the world's own, so past pay and layoffs follow the same path as the opening year
---

The months of the economy before a new life began were the country's published figures copied as they are, while the opening year already ran on the world's own path. People's past jobs were paid and lost by the real country's months, and the economy jumped between them on day one. Every month before the start now follows the world's own path, the published figures giving only its base and shape, so a person's past pay, layoffs and hires move with this world's economy and nothing jumps on day one.
