title: Voting outcomes reach recorded people
---

Six voting outcome estimates now use the shared person outcome, lived outcome,
and official-view path. Graduation effects use recorded adult school completion;
all-mail voting, automatic registration, right-to-work, and independent
redistricting use a sourced age-18+ resident estimate because individual
citizenship and registration are not universally recorded. Voting-rights
restoration reaches people with a recorded felony sentence whose post-sentence
right is restored by policy, excluding pardons and reversed convictions. The
restoration turnout-rate estimate notes that its percentage falls as the
eligible-voter denominator expands while ballots rise. The common path covers
all 56 places.
