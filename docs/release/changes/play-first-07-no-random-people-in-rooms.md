title: Only people with a reason stand in a room
---

A room now shows only people the records give a reason to be there: you, your household at home, coworkers at work, the person you went to see, the people a moment is about, the clerk at the counter and the voters you reach. Scenes no longer open by announcing that someone "is with you" or "is here".
