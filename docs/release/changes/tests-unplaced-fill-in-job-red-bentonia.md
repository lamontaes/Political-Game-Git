title: A household back in the counts brings its commuter's place among the town's unplaced workers
---

When a household the player had lost touch with went back to the town's counts, its commuter's pay went back to the town's pool of unplaced workers, but the commuter often did not: the household was counted with no unplaced workers, so meeting it again found no one to take an outside job. It now counts as many unplaced workers as the pay it brought back, at the town's pay per unplaced worker.
