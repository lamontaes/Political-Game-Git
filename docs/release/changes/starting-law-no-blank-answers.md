id: starting-law-no-blank-answers
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Every place starts with a known law on every researched question
---

At the start of a game, 412 of the 2,520 starting answers (45 questions in 56
places) were blank, so the law there read as unknown. Each has been read from
the place's own law or a named source, or, for the 52 whose law could not be
read, set to the most common answer among similar places and marked
ESTIMATED FROM AVERAGE.

A program already passed but not yet started, such as Maryland's paid family
leave, now reads as not in force until its start date. A repeal passed in play
also stops such a program before it begins.
