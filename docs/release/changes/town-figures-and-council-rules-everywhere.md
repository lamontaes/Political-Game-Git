title: A town's rent, income and jobs keep moving, and every town council has a size and a term
---

Once the last published local figures are behind a life, the "How the place is
doing" panel now shows where the world's own economy has taken them: two-bedroom
rent follows prices, income per person follows prices and output, and the
unemployment rate moves by as much as the world's own rate has. Each line gives
the last published figure beside it and says these are the world's conditions,
not an agency's release. Before this, a life in 2031 still read 2025 rents.

Every town with a government of its own now has a council size and a term.
Where the game has read the town's own rules, the office screen says so. Where it
has not, the town is given a size and a term drawn from the councils the game
has read, the same every time, and the screen says that too. A record of which
towns have read rules sits behind this, so other features can tell a town's own
rule from a typical one.
