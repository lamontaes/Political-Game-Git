title: A planned press exchange now shows the reporter's own newsroom, studio or booth
---

When you arrange an exchange with a reporter, the screen shows where it takes
place instead of staying blank. A spoken exchange with a broadcaster shows its
studio, a spoken exchange with an audio-only outlet shows its booth, and every
other exchange shows the outlet's newsroom. A reporter with no recorded outlet
still meets you in a newsroom. A check draws a generated world and confirms each
outlet type picks its own place and an unarranged key picks none.
