id: talk-delta-reply-moves-removed
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A scene's reply set no longer offers a reply that leaves, deflects or stalls; leaving is the screen's own Leave control.
---

The leave, deflect and stall replies are gone from every scene with a purpose, from the arcs, the arc variants, the situation roles, the veto, charge and acquittal answers, and the recall answers. A reply set holds as many replies as its arc has stances for, one to four, and a scene is skipped only when none of its replies can be worded. At the same scene-talk fixture, the scenes composed stay at 162 and 60 and the skipped stay at 89; no reply of those three moves is offered where 885, 146 and 73 were.
