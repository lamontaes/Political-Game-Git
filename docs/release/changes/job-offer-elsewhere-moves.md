title: Residents can be offered work elsewhere, and move for it
---

Once a year each adult in town weighs whether to look for work beyond it.
Being out of work, short hours, low pay, being early in a career or in a
profession, and a taste for risk push them to look. Steady full-time work and
being settled in later life hold them. Someone who looks applies to the state
government where their closest relative outside town lives, or in their own
state, for their own line of work. If they have done that work before, they
get an offer. Whoever takes the offer moves there and starts work; whoever
stays turns it down.
