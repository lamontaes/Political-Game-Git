title: Residents can be offered work elsewhere, and move for it
---

Once a year each adult in town weighs whether to look for work beyond it.
Being out of work, short hours, low pay, being early in a career or in a
profession, and a taste for risk push them to look. Steady full-time work,
being settled in later life and the life they have in town hold them. Someone
who looks applies to the state government where their closest relative
outside town lives, or in their own state: a professional in their own field,
anyone else for entry-level office work. A professional who has done the work
before gets an offer, as does anyone applying for the entry-level work.
Whoever takes the offer moves there and starts work; whoever stays turns it
down.
