The line that names the first evening at home ("It's the evening of January 20. You stay in.") played twice, once from the opening and once when the evening's scene began. It plays once now, as the scene's own first line. The President's address no longer bridges between its points with a stock turn ("Talking about {turn-from} brings me to {turn-to}."); each point opens on its own.

The President's inaugural address is no longer played as the opening scene: the game opens at home on the evening of January 20, and the address stays a record that the night's news and the family can mention. The owner's ruling of Nov 17: everything the engine did not write from the world is deleted until it is rebuilt from nothing; the address read as the same script every game.
