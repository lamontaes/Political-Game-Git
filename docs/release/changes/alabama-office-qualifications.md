title: Alabama's Governor, Senate and House ask for Alabama's own ages and residence
---

A 22-year-old in Alabama was told he could run for Governor, and the state
Senate asked candidates to be 30. Alabama now holds candidates to its own
requirements. A Governor must be at least 30 and have lived in Alabama for
seven years. A state Senator must be at least 25, and a state Representative
at least 21. Both must have lived in Alabama for three years and in their
district for one.

When a candidate is too young or has not lived somewhere long enough, the
refusal now says so plainly for every state whose requirements the game has
read.
