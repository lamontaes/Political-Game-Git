title: Pay acts, wage floors and other amounts a body enacts now change what is paid.
---

When a legislature, council or Congress passes a new salary, teacher minimum, minimum wage, paid leave premium, local seat pay or debt limit, the next payday or decision uses it. Before, these stayed at fixed real-world figures whatever the world's bodies did. Poverty lines, income fifths and new Social Security awards now rise with the world's own prices and wages each year.
