title: Follow modeled public cash and a delivered-service response across the same life.
---

The Budget page now keeps a separate Modeled account history for cash actually
collected and paid through the public receipts account. It shows the covered
dates, settlement periods, exact transfer records and a balance only when the
opening value and full modeled transfer history support one; government-wide
budget figures and published reference series remain separate. When the
controlled sponsor publishes a dated report for service that was both paid and
delivered, one recorded supporting colleague who can read that publication may
acknowledge it. The saved acknowledgment appears through People and the Journal
once, without adding a vote, approval score, ridership claim or broad public
reaction.
