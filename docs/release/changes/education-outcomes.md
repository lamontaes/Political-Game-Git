title: Every state now has a graduation rate and a math proficiency rate that laws, money and life move.
---

Each state starts at its real on-time high school graduation rate and share
of eighth graders proficient in math, and both change over the years. Laws
that equalize school funding raise achievement over the following years;
universal preschool shows up in graduation more than a decade later;
vouchers help some students graduate while costing others ground in math; and
poverty, health coverage and the economy push schools up or down. More
graduates, in turn, mean less poverty a decade on.
