title: A passed law that sets aside money in its own words now provides it
---

Some bills set aside money in their own words, such as a fund for replacing
water service lines or support for households' internet bills. When one of
these passed, the state still could not spend the money, and the law's page
said nothing acted on that section. The money is now available to spend like
any other appropriation. A section that only allows spending up to a limit
now says it provides no money itself, and how much later laws have provided
against it.
