title: Well-paid townspeople spend like their pay
---

A person living in the town who earns well now spends what a household at their pay spends, on the same curve officials use, never more than most of their pay. Before, every household bought the same basket at any income, so the best-paid kept most of what they earned. Households with no pay and rooms an institution houses still buy the region's basket.
