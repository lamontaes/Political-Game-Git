title: Talk at home knows who is in the room, the hour and what was already said today.
---

Talk at home is told who is in the room, what hour it is and what the household already said today: nobody is asked after while standing there, housemates no longer ask what you do, small talk waits a week and only comes once a day is done, closers fit the hour, two housemates never give the same sentence on one day, and the President is said by role after the first mention.

The people you live with now bring up your own news (a job got or lost, a filing, a race won or lost, your name in the paper) with their own stance from their tie to you. A reason from someone's own life is given only when it fits the side they take, a person's own pay is said to you once in four weeks, a shift is told only when it ran longer or shorter than usual, and someone under voting age takes a stand only when they follow politics closely.
