title: People meet new people during play, and closeness changes say why

A visit now meets the people the host lives with, joining a group meets its
members and staff, and an organized meeting meets the others who come. Each
meeting is a dated fact on both sides with a first tie from the hours spent
together. Every change in closeness saves its cause and the records behind it.
