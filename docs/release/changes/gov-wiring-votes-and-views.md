title: Speeches and lobbying change how council members vote
---

What a council member hears now changes how they vote. Speeches at public comment, witnesses at a hearing, a lobbyist's visit, a resident's problem brought to their office and a candidate's stated positions each move the listener's view on that question, more when the speaker is someone they trust, are close to, share a party with, or who has voted that way before. Residents leave an interest group when their view turns against it, and nobody belongs to both sides of a question. Grudges and family worries no longer push anyone to run for office, and unemployment benefits now follow each state's own law.
