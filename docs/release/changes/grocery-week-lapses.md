title: A week of groceries nobody bought lapses when the next week comes
---

In San Antonio, James's calendar said the groceries were "Still not done, 61
weeks on", and no new week ever came behind them. An unshopped week now lapses
when the next week starts, is recorded as "The week went by without the grocery
shopping.", and the next week's shopping opens. A week the player has put on
the calendar for a day still ahead is kept. Nothing is said about how the
household ate.
