---
id: a50-recorded-public-payroll-route
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Payroll uses the recorded government's canonical public account
---

A town job whose employer profile records a sourced government identity now
uses that government's canonical public account as its payer. Its workplace
remains the employer for pay periods and teacher salary rules; settlement and
law consequences validate the recorded worker against the dated public payer.

Existing account cash is reused. No cash estimate, tax rate, pay formula or
payment-cap rule changes. Employers with no recorded government identity keep
their existing payer; neither their name nor location invents a public owner.

City-office and county-clerk jobs now use the actual canonical municipal or
county government organization during setup. Existing government identity and
cash records therefore reach normal town payroll. Civic role counts reuse
those same organizations when another household is written. A place with an
ambiguous government join is not assigned to the first listed government.

School, police, fire, state-office and federal-office ownership is outside
this bounded producer repair until their employer ownership records are
admitted; a program's funding government is not treated as its legal employer.
