title: A voter who is not on the rolls meets the poll worker
---

A voter who goes to the polls and is not on the rolls now talks with a poll worker, who says why: an old address, never registered, or a form that came in after the deadline. In a state that registers people at the polls, the voter's own word registers them and the ballot opens; elsewhere the ballot is refused and the day is kept in the voter's Journal.
