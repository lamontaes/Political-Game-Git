title: Menus that go somewhere, and refusals that say what they mean
---

Who you are and Money and property used to open the same page; they are now two places, and your own name and age come before the regional figures rather than after them. The day no longer carries a second copy of study and work — it opens the one workspace those belong to — and the menu entry that only returned you to the room is gone, since closing a workspace already does that.

The list of people beside the room is now the people who are in it. Everyone you know is still in the people list, and can be pinned from there.

Walk home is no longer offered while you are already home, and a refusal says what is actually stopping you instead of telling you to check your commitments. When something does happen, the time it took and where it left you are shown, so you do not have to guess whether you went anywhere.

Patch notes list the releases the game has actually had, with the version and the date each one carries, and the version is on the title screen. Work in progress is no longer listed as though it had shipped, and a reserved version number is no longer shown as a release. Where a release records no date, the notes say so rather than inventing one.

In the office, the measure you are working on is named, and a different document that happens to be open is named separately as what it is.
