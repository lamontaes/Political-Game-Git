title: Who speaks at public comment and fills a town hall follows temperament at the size a study finds
---

Who stands up at a council meeting's public comment, and who fills a town hall's room, weighed each resident's temperament at 0.32, which ran high in random towns. Fitted on twenty towns drawn fresh, the weight is now 0.26: the third of residents leaning most toward taking part fills a room about three times as often as the third leaning least, as Mondak and colleagues found.
