title: Outgoing residents fill meeting rooms about three times as often as reserved ones

Who stands up at a council meeting's public comment and who fills a town hall's room now follows temperament at the size a study of public-meeting attendance finds: the most outgoing third takes part about three times as often as the most reserved, among people who feel as strongly about the question.
