title: A city's or county's own laws now change things in that place.
---

Once a city or county council passes a law, the world keeps that place's own
crime, schooling, health and other outcomes, so its ordinances act there: a
city that sets up civilian oversight of its police sees less crime in that
city, and towns that passed nothing are untouched. A state's figures are now
the average of its places, weighted by how many people live in each.
