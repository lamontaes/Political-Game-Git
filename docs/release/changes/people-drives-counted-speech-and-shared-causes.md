title: Anyone in town who feels strongly about a bill can stand up at public comment
---

Public comment at a council or county meeting now draws on every adult in town who feels strongly about the bill, not only the people you already know. Someone you have never met may stand up, become a person you can find and talk to, and leave the meeting with a cause of their own.
