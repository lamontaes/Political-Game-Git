section: Improved
title: Warm people make friends faster, and grudge-holders lose them faster
---

Time spent together now builds a friendship faster between warm, outgoing people and more slowly between reserved ones, and a quarrel costs more between people who hold on to it than between people who let it go. Two neighbors who spend the same evenings with the same person end up at different closeness because of who they are.
