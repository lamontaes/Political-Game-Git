Add review dialogue for legislators debating whether a private-sector union fee can be required as a condition of employment. Members can open with a reason for their vote and hear a related question about the workers or contract at issue. Follow-up questions can come from a shift change, a transfer between plants, a bargaining request, or a vote on a contract. Members can press a bargaining point, ask for the agreement before advising a worker, or speak up for the worker left outside a vote.
