title: A paper asks an official for comment before it prints an exposure
---

When a paper finds a bribe or a conflict of interest, the story no longer appears the same day. It runs in the paper's next edition, and it says whether the official admitted it, denied it or declined to comment, from the call they took or, for someone you are not playing, from how they tend to answer.
