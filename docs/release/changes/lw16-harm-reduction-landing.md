title: People with a substance use disorder ask for the funded harm reduction service
---

Before, a funded harm reduction law moved money to an operator, but no resident
had a reason to ask for a visit. Now a resident who holds the substance use
record on their private health record asks the service for a visit; full-time
work holds a person back, and people without the record have no reason to ask.
The shared funded-service test world also keeps its own wage questions, so the
service tests run again.
