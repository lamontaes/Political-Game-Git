title: A new governor no longer ends up with two chiefs of staff
---

A governor who took over an office whose chief of staff was still employed used
to hire a second chief. The old chief kept making recommendations, and the new
one was ignored. The first-day choice now lists the sitting chief with a Keep
option. Keeping them hires nobody new. Hiring someone else ends the old chief's
job.

Office staff hiring can also take applicants the officeholder has already met,
so the transition's staff interviews can put those people forward. Positions
with no one named still get generated applicants.
