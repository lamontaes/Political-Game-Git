title: The game knows about people sitting turned towards the room
---

Every seated figure the game had measured was drawn square to the camera, and
the one number that placed a person on a cushion came from where their
silhouette parts into two legs. That works on a figure facing you and means
nothing on a figure turned towards the room, because the near leg covers the
far one and the measurement runs on down to the shin. Ten bodies that had been
drawn and banked could not be described well enough to seat, so they sat
outside the catalogue.

Those bodies are now described. A turned figure's seat contact is read from the
lowest point of its own hip mass on the side away from its knees, which is the
part that rests on a cushion, and a figure the measurement cannot read is
recorded as unread with the reason rather than given a plausible wrong number.
The catalogue of candidate bodies goes from twelve to twenty-two, nine of them
seated, and a pose contract for a figure turned towards the room exists for the
first time.

None of this changes what a player sees yet. Every one of the twenty-two is a
candidate awaiting the owner's look at it, and no candidate is ever released by
a script. What changes is that the seated ones can now be looked at.
