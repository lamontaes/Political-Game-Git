title: A county sheriff makes the arrests and the county prosecutor decides the charges
---

When a county elects a sheriff and a prosecutor, the sheriff is now the
arresting officer named on the county's arrests, and the prosecutor makes the
charging decision on each referred arrest. A county with no sitting prosecutor
charges nobody. Portraits and business scenes show a sheriff in the city
police uniform until the sheriff's own uniform is drawn.
