title: Washington rent renewals read the announced annual cap
---

Washington's starting rent law now reads the officially announced 9.683%
annual cap for 2026 instead of the statutory 10% ceiling. Its next dated
phase reads the announced 10% cap for 2027. Both retain the sourced 12-year
construction exemption. Additional property and landlord exemptions still
depend on recorded facts; this correction does not establish those facts.
