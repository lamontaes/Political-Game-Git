id: lw13-council-term-limit-landing
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A town's term-limit bar now reaches the council member it affects.
---

When a town's council term-limit ordinance bars a sitting member from another
term, the saved retirement event now names that member and the law exposure
history records the cost for them, as the state legislative path already does.
The landing reads only the saved bar and the law in force on that date; it does
not decide who may run. Replaying it is idempotent.
