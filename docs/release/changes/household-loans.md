Loans can now carry a rate, a monthly payment and a term. Each month a loan
charges interest, takes its payment from the borrower's money, and adds a late
fee when a payment is missed. After enough missed payments the loan goes into
default, then to collections. A rate cap that is in force holds a new loan's
rate at the cap. Nothing in the game opens these loans yet. This change also
adds the 2026 research on budgets, public programs and debt, the Census
Bureau's 2022 state and local government finances for every state and D.C., and the math for
Social Security checks, food aid and loan payments.
