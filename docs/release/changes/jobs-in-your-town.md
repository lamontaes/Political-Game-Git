title: Jobs lists real openings in your town, and employers can say no
---

Jobs now shows openings at your town's own employers instead of the same three
jobs everywhere. Each listing gives the job, the employer, where the work is,
the pay by the hour or as a yearly salary, the usual weekly hours and the last
day applications are taken. Your county and city governments are employers in
every state and D.C., and a business hires for the work its staff already do,
at about what it pays them.

You can apply, or ask someone you know who works there to put in a word. The
employer answers a few days later and can turn you down. An offer waits a few
days for your answer and then lapses. Once you accept, you start on a set day.
If you don't come in, the employer may call with a new date or withdraw the
offer. A job you start pays weekly. Every date is fixed when it is set, so
saving and reloading never moves it.

The old three jobs are still under Other work for anyone already doing one.
How often openings appear, how often employers hire and the public-office pay
are placeholders until research comes back.
