### Improved

A bill's paper now shows where it stands: what the committee heard and how each member voted, what members have told you, and the floor result with the names of everyone who voted yes and no. Each name opens that person's card. The long paragraph of every member's vote is gone.

### Added

When you sit on the body that will vote a bill, the paper offers Yes, No and Abstain before the floor takes it up, and your choice is the vote that is counted.

### Improved

A floor result on a bill's paper now reads in words, such as "Adopted, 4 to 2." or "Defeated, 3 to 2.", above the names.

### Added

On a bill's paper you can amend a section, lowering it to split the difference with the law in force or raising it. The amendment shows as a struck clause and the clause that replaces it, with its own estimate; the body votes it, member by member, and the bill is rewritten only if it is adopted.

### Added

When a passed bill waits on your desk as an executive, its paper offers Sign it and Veto it with where you stand on it, and once decided it names who signed or vetoed it and the reasons that weighed.
