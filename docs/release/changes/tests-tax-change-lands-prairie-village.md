title: A person's felt change from a new tax counts only the law's own line of pay
---

Someone who feels a new income tax on their paycheck now feels exactly what that tax took, compared with their last paycheck of the same kind. A council stipend or a commuter's changing graduated tax no longer shows up as a gain when a city adds a tax.
