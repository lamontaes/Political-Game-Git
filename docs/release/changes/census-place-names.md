title: Towns are called what their residents call them
---

Eight consolidated city-county places were showing the Census Bureau's filing
label instead of their own name, so a life in Tennessee opened in
"Nashville-Davidson metropolitan government (balance), Tennessee" and its local
party was named after the same phrase. Nashville is Nashville now, along with
Athens, Augusta, Butte, Indianapolis, Louisville and Milford. The full legal
label is still on the place's record and still matches when you search for it.
