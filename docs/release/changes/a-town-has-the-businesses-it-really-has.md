title: A town has the kinds of businesses it really has, with staff and sales to match
---

The grocery stores, hardware stores, diners, garages, law offices, accounting
offices, contractors and salons in a town now follow the county's published
counts and the town's share of its population, instead of the same eight
businesses in every town. A town of under a hundred people may have one diner
and nothing else; a city of 240,000 has two of every kind in the game. Staff
per business follow the county's employees per establishment, and what a
business takes in follows the state's published sales per employee. A new life
seats at most two businesses of a kind and three staff in each, to keep it
quick. Puerto Rico and the territories keep the earlier list, marked as
unresearched, because their town populations are not held.
