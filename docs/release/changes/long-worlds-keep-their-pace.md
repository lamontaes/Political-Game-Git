title: A world that has run for years no longer slows to a crawl
---

Each day in a world got slower the longer the world ran, and by the fifth
year a single day could take a couple of seconds. Most of that time went to
paydays: every paycheck was checked against every paycheck the town had ever
paid, and every money record was looked up by reading all of them.

Days in later years are now many times faster. Five years of a watched world
that took most of an hour to run now take under three minutes, and opening a
save from a world's fifth year is about three times faster. Nothing that
happens in the world has changed: the same world plays out exactly as it did
before, only faster.
