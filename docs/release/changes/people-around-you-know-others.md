title: The people around you know people besides you
---

Your parents, housemates, old classmates and teachers now have a few neighbors
and friends of their own in their town. When they want to keep up with people,
they call them, not only you, and they may introduce you to someone they know.
Nobody new is made up for this: their friends are people who already live
there.
