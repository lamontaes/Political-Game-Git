title: Businesses, churches and clubs in your town open and close
---

Before, every store, diner, farm and garage in town lasted forever, and so
did every church.

Now, each season, a few of the town's businesses close and their workers
lose their jobs and look for new ones. About as many new businesses open,
of whatever kind the town is shortest of, each started by a neighbor who
was out of work, or who quits a job to start it when nobody is. When a business's manager is 65 or older, its closing is
the owner retiring. Now and then a church disbands, its members and staff
leaving with it, and neighbors who belong to no church found a new one.
Clubs, such as a garden club or a bowling league, are founded and disband
the same way.
Over five years in Columbus, Ohio, 13 businesses opened, 12 closed and 20
jobs ended with them.
