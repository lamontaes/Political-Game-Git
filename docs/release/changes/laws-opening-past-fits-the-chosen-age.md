title: A life started at 35 now has the past of a 35-year-old
---

When you pick a birthday in the creator, the town is built around someone who really is that age. A 35-year-old now has parents old enough to have had him, the schools he went through in the 1990s, the household he lives in and money on hand, instead of the family and school years of a teenager.
