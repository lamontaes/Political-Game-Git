title: The day no longer stops for a moment with nothing to show
---

A big moment the story had no scene for, such as hearing a parent's speech or a candidate at a debate, stopped the day under "Something needs you" with an empty box: only the day and the place, and no one to answer. Such a moment is now a line in the journal and no longer stops the day. Every stop left has a scene with someone to talk to.
