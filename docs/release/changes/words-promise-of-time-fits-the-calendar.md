title: A colleague asking you to carry a bill no longer promises a count for after the vote
---

When a colleague asks you to carry a bill and you ask where the votes stand, they promise a count for a day before the vote, from the bill's own calendar, and on the day of the vote they say you'll know tonight. They no longer say "by Friday" on the morning of a Tuesday vote. They also no longer say the vote is "next month" when it is later in the same month.
