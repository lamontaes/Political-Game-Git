title: Governor candidates run with a running mate where the law elects the lieutenant governor on one ticket.
---

In the states and territories whose law elects the lieutenant governor on one ticket with the governor, each governor candidate now runs with a running mate, named when that state's law names one: at filing, after the primary, or the winner of the lieutenant governor's own primary. Nobody runs for lieutenant governor alone there, the ballot shows the governor's race as one line for each ticket, and the two win or lose together on one count. Running for governor yourself, you choose your running mate from the people of your party on the Campaigns board, or let the game choose for you.
