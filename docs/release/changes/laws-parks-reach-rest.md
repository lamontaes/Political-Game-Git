title: Parks funding eases the stress of people who rest near a park
---

More money for parks, from the town's parks line or a dedicated parks tax, raises the share of residents who live near a park or recreation facility, as the research found. Each time a resident takes time to rest, it now relieves their stress by that much more, so a town that funds its parks is a little less strained.
