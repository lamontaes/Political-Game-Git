section: Added
title: Dev mode shows where a person's money went over the year
---

Dev mode now adds up the player's money from the start of the game, and anyone else's from the day they're watched: pay, taxes withheld, rent, purchases and benefits, each in and out, with their cash then and now, their job and what they did. It answers questions like why the player still has no money after a year.
