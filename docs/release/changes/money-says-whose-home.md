title: Money says whose home it is and how it is held
---

Money listed your household's home under "What you own" as "Owned … House", even for a 17-year-old with no money, and listed a rented apartment there too. The home now has its own part, named "Your household's home" when you share it, and says what it is and how it is held: "House … Owned" or "Apartment … Rented · $2,030 per month".
