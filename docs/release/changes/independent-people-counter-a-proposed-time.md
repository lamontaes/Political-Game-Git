title: Independent people counter a proposed meeting time
---

A person with a marked independent manner now leans toward offering another day
when someone proposes a meeting time, because they prefer to arrange their own
time. They still want to meet, so the lean argues for a different day and not for
saying no. The same person answers differently with and without the trait.
