id: x5-a135-recorded-employer-offers
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Job offers out of town come from real kinds of local businesses
---

A resident looking for work out of town is no longer offered the same office
clerk job by the state government. They now apply to a business the place
really has, such as a market, a diner or a builder, in the town where their
family lives or the largest town in their state. The job fits what they have
done before, and the pay is that town's going wage for the work.
