title: Someone who shares a cause with you now asks you along, and your family asks the money question when you file.
---

When someone who cares about a cause you share catches you on the street or at your gate and asks you to come along, the talk now has words: they say what they need, you can ask what it would take, say yes, say no or put it off, and they answer, thank you, press or leave the door open. When you file for office and money is the worry at home, your family asks plainly whether you can afford it, and you can ask them for help with phones and doors. Before, the volunteers' ask had no lines to say.
