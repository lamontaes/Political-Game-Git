title: Tariffs raise what households pay
---

An Act of Congress raising tariffs now lifts the price level in every state by
about eight-tenths of a percent, a year after it takes effect, and repealing it
takes the rise back. Each state starts at its real 2024 price level against the
national average, from the Bureau of Economic Analysis, so California pays about
11 percent above the average and Arkansas about 13 percent below. The territories
are unknown until their price levels are read.
