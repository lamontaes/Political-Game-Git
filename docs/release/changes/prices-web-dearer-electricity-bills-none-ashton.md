title: "Electricity's price is charged once where two electricity laws are in force, and each law's own share still shows"
---

Where a clean-electricity standard and retail electric choice were both in force, a rise in the price of electricity was charged to each household once per law and retail choice's cut was lost. The rise is now charged once, and each law applies its own share to the one price.
