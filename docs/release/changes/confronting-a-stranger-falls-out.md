title: Confronting someone you barely know now leaves bad blood.
---

When someone with a grudge confronts a person they had no tie with, such as the owner who laid them off, the two have now fallen out: the strain stays between them, and later time together has to make it up first. Before, a confrontation with a stranger left no mark at all.
