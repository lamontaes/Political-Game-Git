title: Raises, hires and layoffs reach both paydays in towns that pay twice a month.
---

In a town where employers pay twice a month, a raise, a new hire or a layoff of a worker in the wider population changed only one of the two paydays. Pay for those workers was then half right, or kept coming after a layoff. Both paydays now follow the change.
