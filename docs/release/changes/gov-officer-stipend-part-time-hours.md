title: Part-time town officers are paid a real hourly rate for fewer hours
---

A town's part-time officers, such as a city clerk or a police chief on a small stipend, were shown working a full week at a few cents an hour, and each one was offered a case about being paid under the minimum wage. They now work the part-time week their pay buys at the post's going hourly rate, so the pay is the same and the cases stop.
