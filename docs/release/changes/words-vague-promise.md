id: words-vague-promise
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A colleague who says they'll look at your bill has given you a vague promise.
---

When you ask a colleague for their vote and they say they'll look at it, the talk now ends as "You said you'd look at it" in the colleague's own story, and your journal tells it as "I told Pat I'd look at it." when it was you who said it, not as a promise kept. The scene's record marks whether a promise was firm (a vote traded, a yes given) or vague, for the promises the legislature will read.
