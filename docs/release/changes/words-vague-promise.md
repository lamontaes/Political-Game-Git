section: Changed
title: A colleague who says they'll look at your bill has given you a vague promise.
---

When you ask a colleague for their vote and they say they'll look at it, the talk now ends as a vague promise instead of "Still undecided". The scene's record marks whether a promise was firm (a vote traded, a yes given) or vague, for the promises the legislature will read. When you are the one who says it, your journal tells it as "I told Pat I'd look at it.", not as your word given.
