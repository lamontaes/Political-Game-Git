title: A jail term keeps a person from work
---

Somebody sentenced to jail no longer goes on working and drawing a paycheck.
When the term begins, every job they hold goes on leave, so its pay and its
hours stop. When the term ends, as handed down or cut short by clemency, the
job resumes and pay starts again. The employer holds the job through the term
for now; whether an employer lets a worker go while they are away is not yet
anyone's decision in the game. Probation changes nothing about work.
