title: Laws play out a little differently in every game, and Congress's laws reach outcomes too.
---

How strongly a law or a change in the economy moves an outcome is now drawn,
for each game and each state, from the range real studies report, so the same
law can do a little more in one playthrough and a little less in another. Laws
Congress passes now feed the same web: a federal minimum wage raise lowers
poverty where a state's own minimum is well below $15 and leaves states
already above it alone, and eleven more federal questions are ready to act as
the world starts tracking what they change.
