title: A debate you accept is one you take the stage for
---

When you say yes to a debate on the town's biggest outlet, you now take the stage with its moderator, who puts each question in turn. You pick how to answer each one (plainly, with your record behind it, by turning it on a rival, or briefly), or say nothing, and the voters who watch hear what you chose. If you never sit for it, the debate still goes on the night before the election.
