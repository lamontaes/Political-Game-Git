Gives every resident of the separate core prototype a family and a temperament of their own. Parents, siblings, children, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces and nephews who live elsewhere are now light records in the county or state, generated from the life table and Census fertility by cohort and age. Five temperament dimensions now follow personality norms by age and sex, with a share passed from parents to children. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build. The kin generator, the temperament mapping and their tunable numbers are registered stopgaps that still block release.
