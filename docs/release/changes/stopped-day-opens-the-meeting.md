title: A day that stops for your meeting opens it in the calendar
---

When a day stops because you said yes to a meeting, the notice now has an
Open in calendar button. It opens that meeting with going and staying home
ready. Before, the notice only said the journey came first, pressing Day
again did nothing, and the life could not move on.

A chapter meeting you attend from the calendar now also counts as attended,
so you meet the organizer, as you would from the chapter's own page. Before,
the meeting passed and was marked as missed.
