title: Request a trip from a paid transit operator
---

The transit screen lists funded operators that have actually been paid to run
service in your character's area. Request a trip and take the scheduled activity
to record your ride. Paying an operator or booking a trip does not count as
completed service.

Requested rides appear on Calendar as the saved trip itself. Ordinary commutes
stay attached to their destination, and a ride produces a service receipt only
after your character completes it.
