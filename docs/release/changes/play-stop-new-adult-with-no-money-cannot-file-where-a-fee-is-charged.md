title: You can file for office where a fee is charged even when you hold no money of your own
---

A new adult living with family holds no money of their own, and the filing counter counted only the candidate's own cash against the fee. In the places that charge one, that player could never file. The adults of your household now cover what you cannot, and the payment is recorded under the name of whoever paid.
