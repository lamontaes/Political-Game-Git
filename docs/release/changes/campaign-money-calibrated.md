id: campaign-money-calibrated
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Donors give from their monthly income.
---

When a candidate spends an evening calling for money, the people who want them to win now give from what is left of their month's income after their bills, not only from savings most of them do not have. A town race's call time now brings in about what local campaigns raise: gifts of $25 to $100 from most supporters and more from the closest and best-off, so a candidate can pay for an event or a forum after an evening or two of calls.
