title: Donors give from their monthly pay after taxes and bills.
---

When a candidate spends an evening calling for money, the people who want them to win now give from what is left of their month's pay after taxes and their bills, not only from savings most of them do not have. Whether someone gives now depends on how much they have to spare as well as how they feel about the candidate, so people living paycheck to paycheck usually say no and comfortable supporters usually say yes. A town race's call time brings in about what local campaigns raise: gifts of $25 to $100 from most supporters and more from the closest and best-off, so a candidate can pay for an event or a forum after an evening or two of calls.
