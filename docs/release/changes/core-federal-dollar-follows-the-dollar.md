title: The national economy now follows the federal dollar through real payments, not a hidden multiplier.
---

A change in the federal budget no longer moves the national economy through a locked-in multiplier. When Congress changes a budget line, the treasury's payments reach the named people, households and businesses of your town — a Social Security line change lands on retirees' awards, purchases reach the town's businesses — and they spend by the town's own rules; the national numbers move only by what those dollars actually do. In dev mode, pass a federal budget line and watch a named person in your town get paid and spend it, then the economy page move.
