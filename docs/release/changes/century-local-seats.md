title: The town's own offices are run from 1925 with the state legislatures
---

A mayor, a council or township board and the county's offices now have a history the century ran: how long each holder has served, who ran again and the share and votes of the race that seated them come from the century's yearly count for the seat, on the seat's own election year and term length. Before, they came from a separate career draw and a winner-share band, so a town's earlier races did not match the century's. Day-one holders of local seats can differ from before at every place; state and federal seats do not change.
