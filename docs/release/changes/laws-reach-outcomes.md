title: Laws on policy questions now change life in the towns they govern.
---

What the law in force says on a policy question, from voter ID and Medicaid
to rent limits and preschool, now feeds the world's causes and effects. The
first to reach people: a state law restricting abortion raises births in its
towns by about 2.3%, starting about seven months after it takes effect, as
births rose in states that enforced bans. More outcomes follow as the world
starts tracking them.
