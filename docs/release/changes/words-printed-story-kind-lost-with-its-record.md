title: A state official's new child from another town shows in News, said with the parent's office
---

A story the press printed about a record from a place you can't see, such as a state lawmaker's new child, is now worded from the kind the paper printed it as and shows in News. A birth from another town is printed only when a parent holds an office the paper covers, and the story says which office.
