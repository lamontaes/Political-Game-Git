title: People who move away leave town, and the journal says we moved
---

When someone moves away, they now leave town for real: they live in the place they moved to, their household goes with them (or they start a new one there if family stays behind), and they are no longer counted as residents or voters in your town. When a whole family moves together, each person's journal now says "We moved to" the new place instead of saying their own children or partner moved away. A journal no longer repeats "I started at" the same school for each school stage spent there.
