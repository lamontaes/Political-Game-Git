section: Fixed
title: People who drift back to the counts no longer get hired as full people
---

A person returned to the wider population could be picked for a job as if they were still a full person, which stopped the game when they were met again (a press week crashed in month 33 of a Juliaetta run). They now look for work as counted people do, and a paid withholding no longer keeps a second copy in memory.
