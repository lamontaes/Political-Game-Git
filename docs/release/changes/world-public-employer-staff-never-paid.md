title: Workers at a town's police, fire, public works, health and post offices, and at state offices, are now paid
---

The people who work at a town's police, fire, public works and health departments, at the state offices in town and at the post office used to go unpaid, because their workplaces held no money and no link to a government. Each is now paid from its own government's treasury: the town's, the state's or the nation's.
