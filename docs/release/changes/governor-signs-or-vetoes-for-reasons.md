title: A governor signs or vetoes a bill for reasons, and writes them down
---

A governor the game plays now signs or vetoes each bill for reasons it
records: their own principles on what the bill does, whether their party
carried it and voted for it, how they get along with the sponsor, whether the
legislature would override a veto anyway, and their chief of staff's advice.
Before, a bill no strong principle bore on was signed or vetoed by a draw.
