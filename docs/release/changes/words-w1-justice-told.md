title: A relative charged, convicted or cleared tells you what for, and answers you.
---

When someone in your house is charged with a crime, convicted or found not guilty, they tell you the offense from their own side of the case (taking bribes, paying one or offering one), along with the plea or the trial day, the bail, and the term, fine or probation. When you reply, they answer with what comes next instead of falling silent.
