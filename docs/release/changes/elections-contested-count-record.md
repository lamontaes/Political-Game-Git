title: A close race recounts by its own state's rule
---

A race whose count ends inside its state's automatic recount margin is now recounted. Where the state's law lets a trailing candidate ask, they may: people in the world decide from their temperament and how close it was, and the player asks by not conceding on election night. The paper carries the recount, the result is certified on the day it ends, and the winner is not seated before then.
