title: A tenant's lawyer the city cannot yet pay is owed, then paid
---

Under the right to counsel in eviction, a city whose treasury was empty on hearing day used to record a lawyer for the tenant and then never pay them. The unpaid cost is now a debt of the city to the legal services provider, paid day by day as cash arrives, and the tenant's record shows the full cost of the lawyer.
