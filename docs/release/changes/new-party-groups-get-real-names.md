id: new-party-groups-get-real-names
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A group that breaks away over a party dispute gets a real name, such as "Home Rule League", not "League for follow the wider party line".
---

When members walked out of a party body to found their own group, the group was
named by gluing "League for" onto the position they disagreed about, which made
names like "League for follow the wider party line". Each position now carries
a name a group could actually be called.
