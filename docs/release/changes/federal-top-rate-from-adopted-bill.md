id: federal-top-rate-from-adopted-bill
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Federal top-rate laws use their adopted rate
---

A federal law changing the top income tax rate now taxes the top bracket at
its adopted rate, instead of always using 39.6 percent. The change begins at
the existing tax-year boundary. The other brackets and deductions keep their
starting schedule. A missing, conflicting or unsupported adopted rate leaves
the withholding unsupported rather than inventing a rate. Repealing the
change restores the starting schedule.
