title: The paper prints who endorsed whom in a race.
---

When a party, a town group or a person endorses a candidate, the paper now prints it: "The Republican Party endorsed Kevin Stein for U.S. Representative." A group that keeps no name of its own is "a local group". Before, these stories were printed with no words at all.
