title: A state constitution that settles the shape of its income tax holds against a statute
---

Six states write the shape of their income tax into their constitutions:
Illinois, Michigan and Colorado require one rate. Pennsylvania and Washington
bar graduated rates as their supreme courts read their uniformity clauses.
Massachusetts writes its surtax on high incomes into its constitution. In
those states a bill that changes the shape of the tax no longer takes effect,
and paychecks stay as they were; only a ratified amendment changes it, as
Illinois voters were asked in 2020 and Massachusetts voters in 2022. Before,
an ordinary statute could graduate Michigan's tax or flatten Massachusetts's.
