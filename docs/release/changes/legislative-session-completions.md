id: legislative-session-completions
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Legislative sessions record their dated completion
---

Regular state sessions can now record completion on their statutory end date
or on a clearly marked estimate based on similar states' recorded session
rules. Each completion identifies the jurisdiction, session, chambers, date,
and reason so later simulation steps can distinguish a legal limit from a
procedural adjournment or a special-session scope ending.
