title: Join the public meeting in person
---

The posted public meeting now opens in its room when you arrive. You can read
the agenda, hear the people present, make a public comment, stay through the
discussion, or leave early. Your visit and words remain in your history; a
short visit does not count as full attendance. The discussion ends without an
official vote.
