title: News about vacancies, resignations and reporters' deadlines names the state and writes dates in words.
---

Some news lines printed a state's two-letter code, a raw date and the game's
own wording, such as "OR's governor calls a special election, held on
2031-08-17 in this game." They now read like news: "Oregon's governor has
called a special election for August 17, 2031." The same fix covers resignation
notices, a reporter's response deadline, and a governor's session with no bills.
