title: People remember whether you kept your word on a vote, a late payment or showing a new coworker around
---

When you promise a council member your vote, promise a housemate you will pay on time after a missed payment, or promise a new coworker you will show them around, the person now holds you to it. Your vote at the roll call, your next payment, or the next time the two of you talk settles it, and the next time you talk they bring it up once, warmly if you kept it and less so if you did not. Keeping it warms the tie; breaking it cools it. Scenes no longer ask for promises the game has no way to keep, such as visiting a relative or seeing a doctor; saying yes there now settles in the scene.
