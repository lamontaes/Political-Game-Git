title: A township board and a unicameral legislature have a prior term too.
---

Before day one, a Connecticut or Rhode Island town board, the D.C. Council, and the legislatures of Guam, the Virgin Islands and Nebraska now have the votes and laws of their last term, like any other council or county board; before, they listed none.
