title: Jobs describes an empty list without guessing about employers
---

When no current job listings are available, Jobs says, “No job openings are
listed here right now.” The sentence comes from the existing English engine
and the same saved listings that the screen displays. It makes no claim about
every employer in town. The wording check also catches several specific
awkward constructions and suggests plain American English repairs.
