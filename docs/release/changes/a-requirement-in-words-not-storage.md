title: A requirement the game reports without deciding is written in words again
---

Reading whether you could stand for office in Ohio said the game "requires true". Nevada's Attorney General required "MEMBER_STATE_BAR_OF_NEVADA". These are how the requirements are stored, not what they are, and neither sentence told a player anything. Ohio asks that a candidate be a qualified elector, and Nevada asks that its Attorney General belong to the state bar; that is what each now says. The game still declines to decide either one, because it records neither voter registration nor bar admission, and saying somebody met a requirement it never checked would be an eligibility it invented.
