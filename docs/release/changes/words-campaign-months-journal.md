title: The Journal tells your campaign's months: the clerk's count of your petition and your volunteers' evenings at the doors.
---

While you run, the Journal now notes the clerk's count of your petition signatures ("The clerk counted 32 good signatures on my petition for Council member. I needed 30.") and, at most every two weeks, a volunteer's evening knocking on doors for you ("A volunteer knocked on 37 doors for me.").
