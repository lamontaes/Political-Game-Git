title: Who follows the news and who hears about a law comes from the person
---

Whether someone follows the news closely, knows how a legislator voted, and
tells anyone what a law did to them was a roll of the dice. It now comes from
who they are. A curious person follows the news, and so does someone of
retirement age with no job to go to. A person knows a legislator's vote when
they follow the news or know the legislator. When a law reaches someone, they
tell up to three of the people closest to them, and someone who avoids
conflict keeps politics to themselves.
