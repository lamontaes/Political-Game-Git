title: People stand and sit where they belong in ten more rooms
---

The clerk's counter, the council chamber, the kitchen, the mayor's office, the coffee shop, the three doorsteps, the polling place and the election-night hall now place people where the painter put them: behind the counter, at the dais, around the table, in the doorway. Rooms that hold more people than were painted keep their other places, and the debate stage has its audience back.
