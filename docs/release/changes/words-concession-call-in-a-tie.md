title: After a tied race, the runner-up's call says how the tie was settled
---

When a race ties and the state's law settles it by a drawing or by a board's or legislature's vote, the runner-up who calls the winner now says so ("It came down to a drawing, and your name came out. Congratulations."), and the winner can answer that the drawing or the vote settled it. Before, the call had no line for a tie.
