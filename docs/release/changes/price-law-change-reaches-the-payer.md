id: price-law-change-reaches-the-payer
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A law that lowers a rent or tuition charge names the person who pays it
---

When rent stabilization holds a renter's rent, or a tuition freeze holds a
student's tuition, the saved price change now also records that the law reached
that person: the law, the monthly amount it moved (a saving when the price is
held below what it would have been), and their partner hears of it at home.
Officials behind the law can be seen in a better light by the people it helped,
and a repeal stops the record from growing.
