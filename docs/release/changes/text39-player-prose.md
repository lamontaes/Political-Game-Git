Household errands now name the tasks being discussed. Conversations distinguish
an agreement from completed work and keep the details recorded in your life.

Opening scenes and ordinary dialogue use clearer choices and replies. Revised
optional setup questions preserve earlier answers and distinguish requests from
guaranteed solutions. Older saved requests keep their original level of detail.
