title: Looking up which local office handles something takes about as long as it does in real life.
---

When someone looks up which local office handles a problem, it now takes a little under an hour of their day, the time Americans spend on a government errand in the national time-use survey, instead of a quarter of an hour, so people with little free time choose it a little less often.
