title: Townspeople now lean toward what is popular across the country, while candidates keep their own platforms
---

Each person's view on a question now starts from how popular it is across the country, so a town that mostly wants term limits or more school spending says so in polls, at the door and at the ballot, where before most questions split it down the middle. Candidates still run on their own beliefs, party and record, so rivals in a race stay on opposite sides of most questions instead of all moving to the popular side together.
