title: The Journal's "time since" matches the time that passed
---

When a year was let run on, the next line said how long it had been in words a
person would use, but the steps were too coarse. A Caribou life read "The
better part of two years later, and you're 6 now" after 13 months, and a
Winnemucca life read "A year on" after nine. The phrases now follow the real
gap: most of a year, a year, a year and a half, nearly two years, then whole
years.
