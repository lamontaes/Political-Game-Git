title: When your senior year ends without a diploma, someone at home talks it through with you.
---

A senior whose year ended without a diploma used to hear nothing at all. A graduate was congratulated at home the next day, but a student who didn't finish got no scene. Now someone you live with sits you down the day after ("I heard about school. It isn't the end of anything."). You can tell them what happened, promise to finish, apologize or say you'd rather not talk. Both the graduation scene and this one now open with a sentence that sets them up.
