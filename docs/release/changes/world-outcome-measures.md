section: Changed
title: The town's household earnings, home prices, rent burden and evictions are kept month by month
impact: patch
---

Each month the town's record now keeps what its typical household earns, what its typical home is worth, how much of a year's earnings the typical rent takes, and how many eviction orders were issued the month before. The wider effects of a place's conditions read these figures as they change.
