title: Later game years run faster and memory stays smaller
---

Each game year no longer runs slower than the one before. In a small town the third year takes under three minutes instead of nearly eight, and memory after three years is under half what it was. People offer their cause to one acquaintance at a time, forget the bookkeeping keys of old news and old events once nothing reads them, and the world plays out exactly as before. A new run plays every state and territory for a set number of years and reports each year's time, any crash, and whether money balances, ended jobs stay ended, empty seats are filled and every even year's contests reach the ballot.
