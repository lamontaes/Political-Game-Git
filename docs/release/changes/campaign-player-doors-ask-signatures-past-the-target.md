section: Changed
title: Doors ask for votes once your petition has enough signatures
---

Knocking on doors used to collect signatures for as long as your petition was open, even after it held far more than the clerk needs. Once your petition holds the signatures required plus a small cushion for names the clerk strikes, the same outing becomes a vote-asking visit: the doors strip reads who you reached, not "signed", and the people you meet are asked for their vote. If the target is reached in the middle of an outing, the doors after it ask for votes.
