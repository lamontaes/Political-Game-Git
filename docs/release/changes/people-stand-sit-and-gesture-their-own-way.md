title: People stand, sit and gesture like real people, men and women each their own way
---

People in scenes now take real poses instead of standing stiffly with their arms at their sides: explaining with their hands, arms folded, speaking at a podium, and sitting leaning in, in every body type and every outfit. Men and women have their own body language. A woman explains with open palms, rests a hand on her hip and crosses her legs when she sits. A man explains with a low, firm hand, stands with his hands in his pockets and rests an ankle on his knee. Every pose is also painted turned three quarters toward the viewer, ready for when faces and hair are turned too.
