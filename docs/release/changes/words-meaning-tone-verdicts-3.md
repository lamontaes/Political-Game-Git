title: When you don't graduate, the talk at home says what is at stake and holds you to your word.
---

When a parent talks with you about not graduating, they say what the diploma means for work. Once you promise to finish, they ask which way you'll do it, or when. Your own promises name what you'll do. A few more lines heard often now say plainly what they mean.
