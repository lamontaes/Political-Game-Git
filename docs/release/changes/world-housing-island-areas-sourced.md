title: American Samoa, Guam, the Northern Marianas and the Virgin Islands start with their own housing figures.
---

Homes in these four island areas now start from their own 2020 Census owner share, rent and home value instead of the nation's.

Owners' opening home values in every place also preserve household variation while centering the median on that place's own housing value, moved by its price index to the opening date.
