title: Every line written before Nov 15 is gone; scenes come back as their lines are rebuilt.

Every word the game said that was written before Nov 15 is deleted, on the owner's ruling of Nov 17 ("I'd rather it start from nothing and build it up correctly"). A scene whose lines went with it does not play until its lines are written again, and no empty talk reaches you. The tests that checked only the deleted words went with them.
