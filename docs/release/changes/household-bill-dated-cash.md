id: household-bill-dated-cash
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Overdue household bills preserve same-day cash shortfalls
---

An overdue household bill now uses the shared recorded-cash assessment. Spending
followed by replenishment on the same day cannot conceal a shortfall and fund an
earlier bill. Existing provider and outside-seller payments keep their saved
recipients, and each new payment credits exactly what the household pays.
Earlier receipts and repeated settlement after reloading remain unchanged.

A household returning to an earlier place opens a new dated bill while reusing
that place's existing outside-seller account. Its previous bill remains ended,
and existing receipts and cash balances are preserved.
