title: Hiring follows recorded applications and offers
---

Quarterly town reviews no longer hire a fixed share of residents ranked by how
recently they stopped working. The existing weekly review of people's goals,
applications to listed openings, offer decisions and accepted job starts remains
unchanged. Separately, a town employer with recorded books uses a saved staffing
decision before the existing town hire writer.
