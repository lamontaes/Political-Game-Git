title: The officeholder who advises you after your party asks you to run is now your mentor
---

When a party asks someone to run, one of its officeholders in town who has served a year or more now takes them under their wing: the one who knows them best, or else the longest serving. That person is the one who comes by the next day with advice, and they stay among your contacts afterwards. Before, the adviser was any officeholder of the party, picked at the moment of the scene, and nothing tied the two of you.
