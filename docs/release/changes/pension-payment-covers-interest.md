---
id: pension-payment-covers-interest
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: A pension paid in full now pays down what it owes
---

A government that paid its whole required pension contribution still saw its
unfunded pension debt grow, because the payment covered a thirtieth of that
debt but not the interest on it. The required payment now pays the debt off
over 30 years at the plans' assumed 7% return, so a government that pays in
full owes less each year.
