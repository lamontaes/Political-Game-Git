title: Office hours, rooms, offices and addresses read right
---

Office hours no longer draws a town hall's microphone, heckler or "Next question." Narration follows the room a scene is set in (the desk, the chamber, the phone, the hall after a meeting). Offices read in sentence case, and the oath names the office with its article. A scene's talk no longer asks "What do you think?" about news it never told. A mayor's and a governor's address name the town and the state instead of "this town".
