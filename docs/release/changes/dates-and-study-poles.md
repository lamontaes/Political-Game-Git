title: Dates say what the record says, and study partners give their own reasons
---

A date the game could not actually hold no longer arrives as a different real
one. February 30 was reaching you as "March 2", a year before 0100 was shown as
a year in the 1900s, and a year that was simply not on record came through as
the first four letters of the words saying so. All three now come back exactly
as they were written, so an unusual date reads as unusual instead of quietly
becoming a plausible one.

When someone you have agreed to study with says they would rather settle the
outline first, or read everything before deciding, that is now the reason given
by a person who thinks things through. It used to belong to the ones who act on
impulse, which is the opposite of what the words say.
