title: Personality changes at the pace of a life
---

How hard somebody is to change now depends on how strongly the trait is theirs,
which is the owner's answer to what the pacing should turn on. A faint lean
shifts sooner than a defining one, read from the strength already on the record
rather than from anything new about the person.

Two defects go with it. Repeating the same argument no longer counts as
repeated evidence: what accumulates is separate experiences spread over time,
capped by the declaring pack, so persistence alone cannot wear anybody down. And
having already changed no longer makes somebody permanently harder to reach — a
character who has lived through things stays reachable, which is what "every
character can change" has to mean.

For a player, an NPC who keeps being turned down still becomes a person who
reaches out less. It now takes years of it rather than a fortnight.
