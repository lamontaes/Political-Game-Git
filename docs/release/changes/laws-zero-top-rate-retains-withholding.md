title: A state income tax set to zero stops coming out of paychecks
---

When a legislature repealed a state income tax by setting its rate to zero but the old rate table stayed on the books, paychecks kept paying tax at the old rates. A zero rate now withholds nothing, for named workers and for the town's other workers alike.
