title: Later years of a long world run faster
---

A world still got slower each year it ran, even after paydays were fixed:
year 10 took about seven times as long as year 1. Paydays copied the whole
tax list once per paycheck, and the election turnover step, the scheduler and
Congress's votes each read records from the whole of the world's history to
find the few they needed.

They now read only what they need. On the same world, year 10 takes about
four times as long as year 1, and less than two-thirds of the time it took
before. Nothing that happens in the world has changed: the world after each
of ten years is exactly the same as before.
