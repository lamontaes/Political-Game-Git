title: A House seat stays empty until its special election
---

When a member of Congress from another state dies, the seat is filled by that state's law: a House seat waits empty for its special election, and a Senate seat takes an appointee until one is held. Before, the House or Senate quietly seated someone new the next time it sat, so a vacant seat still voted.
