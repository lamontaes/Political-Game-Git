section: Changed
title: Glossary card, plates, rail names and scene menus follow the menu look
---

The glossary card is a soft dark surface with the term over its meaning and a lit Mark as learned button. Plates in menus lose their brass corners and hard edge. A rail icon shows its name after a quarter second of hover or focus, and the Journal icon gets a dot when a new line has been written. While a talk or scene is on screen, the rail and the player card are gone.
