title: Corruption acts leave a record of who was involved
---

When a corruption act is recorded, the game now keeps the people directly
involved and the records the act leaves. Campaign money used personally and a
payment for an official's promised action use the same record, so later
discovery can follow what people actually knew. There is no automatic chance
of being caught.
