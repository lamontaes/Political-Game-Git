title: Senators weigh a Supreme Court nominee's views against their own
---

Before the Senate votes on a Supreme Court nominee, each senator now
compares the principles the nominee holds with their own. A senator of the
other party can vote for a nominee who shares their views, and a senator of
the President's party can hesitate over one who does not. The game does not
yet record court rulings, so a nominee's principles stand for their record.
