title: Numbered district identity, with whole-place home membership where sourced
---

Legislative filings can name a published district identity. Residence in that
district is proved only from World-established intervals. Choosing a district,
a map point, a city, a county, or living in the state is not that proof. When
Census relationship files show a home place wholly inside one state-legislative
district, that membership is recorded with its vintage and chamber. Split
places and older saves stay unknown. This is not a complete geographic system.
