title: Numbered district identity, separate from unproved home membership
---

Legislative filings can name a published district identity. Residence in that
district is proved only from World-established intervals, not from choosing
the district, a map point, a city, or living in the state. Older saves are
not rewritten to invent that history. A complete home-to-district membership
journey is still missing.
