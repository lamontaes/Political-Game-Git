title: Vacant public-body job offers read a sourced occupation median
---

Public-body vacancies read the recorded employer's pay for the same occupation when available. Otherwise the approved sourced occupation/workplace median supplies an explicitly estimated offer. The fixed national clerk rate is removed; existing openings retain their saved terms.

County workplaces retain their sourced five-digit Census geographic binding when the shared wage reader selects the occupational area and state median.
