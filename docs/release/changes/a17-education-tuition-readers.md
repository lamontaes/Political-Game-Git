title: School tuition readers live with the existing education price records
---

School tuition coverage, operative-price quotes and saved-term installment readers now live in the existing education tuition module. Billing and the price-cost engine consume the same readers from that module. Their calculations, prior terms, family payments and recorded public backfill stay unchanged; the old one-law budget module is retired.
