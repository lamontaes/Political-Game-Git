title: Issues lists every law in force where you live
---

Politics → Issues now lists every law in force where you live, not only taxes, wages and benefits. Each policy question your state or the nation has a law on, from abortion to rent stabilization, reads Yes or No, with the government whose law it is and the statute it rests on where the research names one. When your city, county or state passes a law, its card shows the new law from the day it takes effect. Dates on Issues are written out in words.
