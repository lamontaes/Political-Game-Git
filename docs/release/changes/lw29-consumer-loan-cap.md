title: Federal interest caps reduce covered borrowers' loan charges
---

An enacted federal consumer-loan cap now sets the rate on covered new loans
and lowers existing loans when their next monthly payment is serviced. The
bill's recorded rate, coverage and effective date govern the change in every
place. Monthly interest savings reach the named borrower through the shared
law-exposure record.
