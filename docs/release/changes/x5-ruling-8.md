title: Universal pre-K's effect on math now arrives when its first children reach eighth grade
---

Universal pre-K's small effect on eighth-grade math now shows up about nine and a half
years after a program starts, when its first four-year-olds take the eighth-grade test,
instead of five years on. Four effects once set to exactly zero are now marked as
unmeasured instead: housing vouchers on graduation and on arrests, a congressional
stock-trading ban on members' returns, and local book removals on reading. They still
move nothing.
