title: A law's effect on a named defendant stays in the court record
---

When a recorded cash-bail rule changes a defendant's pretrial outcome, or a
saved minimum-custody term applies at sentencing, the person's law history
records which law reached them and which court record shows it. A refundable
bail deposit is not treated as a loss.
