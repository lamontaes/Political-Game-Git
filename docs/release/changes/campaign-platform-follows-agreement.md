section: Changed
title: Candidates weigh how many of the town share a view before they run on it.
---

Candidates still run on the views they hold most strongly, but now they also weigh how many voters in town are on their side of each question. When two views are held about as strongly, a candidate talks about the one more of the town shares. A strong view on the losing side of town opinion can be left off for one that more voters agree with. Their stated positions never change; only which questions they run on. For now, most voters' sides follow their left-right lean, so most platforms look the way they did before.
