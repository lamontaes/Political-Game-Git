id: campaign-platform-follows-agreement

section: Changed
title: Candidates run on the questions where more of the town agrees with them.
---

Candidates now run on the questions where more of the town agrees with them, keeping their own positions. How strongly a candidate holds a view still counts, but a strong view on the losing side of town opinion can be left off for one that more voters share, and their ads, mailers, doors, events and donor meetings lead with the question they chose first. Their stated positions never change; only which questions they run on and lead with. When a candidate answers a reporter about a vote the town opposes, they now weigh the same reading of where the town stands as their platform does.
