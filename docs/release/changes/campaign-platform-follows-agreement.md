title: Candidates now run on the questions where their town agrees with them.
---

Candidates now choose what to campaign on the way real candidates do. They keep their own views, but of the questions their seat decides they talk about the ones they care about where most of the town is on their side, and they stay quiet on views most of the town opposes. A candidate who cares deeply about something unpopular may leave it off their platform for a question that wins more agreement. Their stated positions never change; only which questions they run on.
