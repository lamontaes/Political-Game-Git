title: A governor's resignation now reaches national papers, and a statewide officer's or member of Congress's gets more attention.
---

A resignation used to be weighed like any other named event about an office,
so a governor standing down could stay in the state's own papers. Now the
record of a resignation says how big the news is, by the office given up. A
governor's is national news. A statewide officer's or a member of Congress's is
notable. These weights are placeholders until the research question on how much
coverage a resignation gets is answered.
