title: People can face away from you, and the art for many more poses is in
---

When a spot in a room faces away from you, the person standing there now shows their back and the back of their hair instead of being turned to face you, as an audience faces a podium. The people art also now holds many more poses: hands clasped, a handshake, a wave, pointing, hands on hips, checking a phone, holding a cup or a folder, a shrug, walking, and more, plus turns to three quarters. Which pose a person gets comes from what they are doing and where they are, and the same person in the same place always gets the same one. Each new pose appears on clothed people as its outfits are painted; until then they are drawn in the nearest pose that is painted in their clothes.
