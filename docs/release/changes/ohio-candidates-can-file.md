title: Ohio characters can run for state office
---

Every Ohio state office asks for a candidate who is a qualified elector, and
the game never decided that requirement, so it turned away every Ohio
candidate for the legislature, governor, lieutenant governor, attorney general
and secretary of state. It is now read from the character's own record: at
least 18, a citizen by birth, and living in Ohio at least 30 days, as Ohio law
requires of a voter. A character who runs registers first, as real candidates
do, and the filing checks Ohio's registration rule. Missouri's requirement that a governor have been a
citizen for fifteen years is read the same way.
