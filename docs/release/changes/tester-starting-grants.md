title: Tennessee counties and cities may set transit fares and form police review committees
---

Where a state statute names a power for its counties and cities, they may now draft bills on it even though the state grants no general home rule. In Tennessee a county or city can propose fare-free transit, a county can shift its highway aid toward transit, and either can form a police advisory and review committee, each from the statute. Two bars the research could not support, a minimum reserve balance and a bottle deposit, no longer stop a Tennessee or Kansas place.
