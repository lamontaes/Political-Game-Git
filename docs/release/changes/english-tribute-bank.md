title: Officials can honor and remember people the way Congress does
---

A bank of 40 shapes from the Congressional Record's Extensions of Remarks, such
as "I rise today to recognize", "is survived by" and "I ask my colleagues to
join me in", is now in the game's data for the dialogue engine to word
tributes, congratulations and remembrances.
