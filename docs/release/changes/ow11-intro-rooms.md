title: The opening walks through the rooms of government instead of one building front after another
---

Before, the opening showed the outside of the Capitol twice, a landscape for
your state, and the outside of city hall and a main street for your town and
your life.

Now each step stands inside a room: the Senate and House floors for the year
and Congress, the governor's office for your state, the council chamber or the
county commission room for your town (whichever your place's government
records), and your own home for your life.
