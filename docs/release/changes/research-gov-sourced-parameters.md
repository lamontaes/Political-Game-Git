section: Changed
title: Bribery sentences follow each state's own law, and more officeholders open in their real party.
---

A court sentencing someone for bribing an official, or for taking a bribe, now
works within the range of that state's own bribery law instead of borrowing
the range for assault. Territory governors and legislators, offices elected
without party labels, and legislative seats that were vacant on the roster now
open the game with the party their real holders have, or with no party where
the ballot carries none, instead of the party that carried the state in 2024.
