title: Towns with many evictions keep running at full speed
---

Households in the wider population who lose their homes now stay part of the town's counts instead of each becoming a fully simulated person. A college town where many students fall behind on rent plays its first year in about a third of the time. Its homeless count includes those households, and they can find a place to rent again.
