title: Paying to keep farmland from being developed changes how much a state loses
---

Paying to keep farmland from being developed changes how much farmland a
state loses. Every state, D.C. and territory starts with its real answer: 30
states and Puerto Rico run a program that buys farmland easements, and
Tennessee's opened on 9/1/2026. The 48 contiguous states start at the farmland
they lost to development each year from 2001 to 2016. A state that starts such
a program loses 40% to 55% less farmland five years later, and one that ends
its program loses that much more.
