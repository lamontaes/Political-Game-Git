title: Households in town move for their own reasons
---

A household in town used to buy, move or sell on a roll each season. Now it
reconsiders its home when something in its life changed: its pay grew enough
to carry the payments on a house, a child joined a family in an apartment,
its rent reached half of what it earns, or an older owner was left alone in
the house. Each move says why. Rent stabilization keeps renters in place
because it holds their rent down, not by a fixed amount.

A tenant with a lawyer who is more than four months behind now keeps the
home only on a payment plan their pay can carry, so a debt no longer grows
without end.
