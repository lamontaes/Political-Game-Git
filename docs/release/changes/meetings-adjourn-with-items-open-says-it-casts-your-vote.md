title: Adjourn now abstains on open items you took no side on, and the meeting says so
---

Pressing Adjourn at a meeting you sit on with items still open used to vote each one for you the way Decide for me does, even on an item where you had said nothing. Now Adjourn votes the side you spoke for on an item (or yes on your own ordinance) and abstains on every other open item. The meeting panel says this above the Adjourn button before you press it. Decide for me still votes your own position.
