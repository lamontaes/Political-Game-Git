section: Changed
title: A town hall now plays as a resident's question and the answer they got
---

When a local official holds a town hall, the resident who stood up with a question now has a scene about that question: the issue they asked about, and whether the official's answer agreed with their own view, opposed it, or was not given. Before, the same meeting played as office hours, with a resident who had lost a job or faced eviction and said nothing about the meeting.
