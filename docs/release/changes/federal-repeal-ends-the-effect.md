title: When Congress repeals a law, what it changed ends that day
---

When Congress repeals a federal law, the change that law made to people's
lives ends on the day the repeal takes effect. Before, an effect that builds
slowly, such as the poverty a higher retirement age causes over five years,
kept going until the same number of years had passed since the repeal. Now a
law can be undone as well as passed, and the effect goes with it.
