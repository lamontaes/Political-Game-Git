title: Rent, food and bills cost what they cost where you live
---

Every life paid the same $1,500 a month for rent, food and bills, in every
state and town. The rent inside that month now follows how expensive housing
is in the home state, so a life in California pays more than one in
Mississippi, and one adult's share of rent never comes to more than a whole
two-bedroom rents for across the state. Food and bills are unchanged. An older
save moves to its place's figure from the next month it settles.
