title: A long-lost friend coming back and a relative's letter now each ask you for something.
---

When someone you have not been in touch with for years finds you again, they say how long it has been and ask for something. They may want to start over, catch up over lunch, find a lead on work, or have you visit. They tell you what has changed for them, like a new child or a new job. A letter from a relative who lives elsewhere asks for a visit or a letter back, and may mention their news. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
