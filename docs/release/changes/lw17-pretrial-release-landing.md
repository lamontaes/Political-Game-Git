section: Added
title: Ending cash bail now reaches the defendant it releases or holds.
---

Where the end-cash-bail law is in force, the saved decision about a named
defendant (released without bail, or held in jail by a judge) now writes a
recorded exposure to the law in their history, with Journal and news wording
for the new court-rule channel. The judge's decision still decides release or
detention; the landing only records what it meant for the person. Where money
bail stands, nothing is written. The other three LW-17 laws are not landed yet.
