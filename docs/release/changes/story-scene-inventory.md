title: Scenes that only talked, or never reached you, are gone.
---

Twenty-four kinds of scene left the game. Some never reached the player; in the rest, nothing in the world read what you answered: a second call after a concession, a word in the hallway that bound no one, a messenger bringing news the paper already printed. The scenes whose answers change something stay as they were.
