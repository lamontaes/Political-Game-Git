Internal groundwork. Something that happens in an ordinary life can now change
a person: somebody who reaches out and keeps getting turned down, or never
answered, becomes a person who reaches out less. One such evening does nothing
— it takes repetition, and every attempt is recorded so it can be pointed at.
Nothing about this is visible yet beyond the behaviour it produces, and the
played character is never changed this way.
