title: A seat no one won is filled for the rest of its term
---

When nobody runs for a county or town office and its holder stays on, the seat is now on the next regular ballot for what is left of the term, and then comes up again in its usual year. Before, the winner got a full new term, which moved the seat off its normal schedule.
