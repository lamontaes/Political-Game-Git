title: A session on the doors meets the neighbors who are home
impact: patch
---

A candidate's session on the doors used to record that people were talked to without naming anyone. It now walks the candidate's own neighborhood door to door, at the pace the campaign research gives for a volunteer, and meets by name every adult who is home at the minute of the knock; somebody at work or out is not met. Each person met knows of the visit, the meeting stays in both people's history, and the next session starts at the next door.
