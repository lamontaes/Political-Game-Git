People out of work find their next job at different speeds: a household days from the rent searches up to twice as hard as one with a year of money, so a town's out-of-work rate no longer climbs through the first year at the larger and middle-sized towns (Lake Arrowhead, Maine now ends its first year near 6 percent instead of 10). A search that reached its mark with no regional job to take it keeps its progress.
