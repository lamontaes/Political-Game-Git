title: The talks at home and at work are back, in new words that say what happened.
---

A layoff names the employer, the job and how long you held it. A friend calls about the job they lost. Your first day at a new job, the day after school ends, a partner asking to move in, a proposal, a breakup, a visit to a sick relative, a funeral and the talk after a rescue each say what happened, from the records behind them.
