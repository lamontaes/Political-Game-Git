title: When a crime happens to you, whether to call the police is your choice
---

When you are assaulted or robbed, or your home is broken into or vandalized,
and nobody else it happened to has called the police, the game now asks you.
You can report it, and it goes on the town's police log that day, where the
paper, the police and the state's mood can see it, or keep it to yourself.
Nothing decides it for you, and until you report it, it stays off the log.

For everybody else, knowing the person who did it no longer makes a victim
keep quiet: people hurt by family report about as often as people hurt by
strangers, as they do in the national crime survey. A victim it has happened
to before is now more likely to call.
