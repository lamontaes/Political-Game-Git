People can wear earrings, a necklace, a watch, a ring and a lapel pin. Women
wear earrings and necklaces at the shares counted from official portraits of
sitting members of Congress, men wear neither, and a lapel pin goes on someone
who holds an office and is dressed for it. Watches and rings use placeholder
shares, because a portrait shows no wrist or hand. Each person's jewelry is
remembered on the saved appearance and chosen in the creator. Nothing changes
on screen until the accessory paintings are in the pack.
