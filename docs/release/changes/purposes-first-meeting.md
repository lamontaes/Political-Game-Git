title: A new coworker's first meeting and a new friend's ask now say what they are about.
---

When someone new starts at your workplace, they come over, introduce themselves and ask you something real: how things run, the same job you do, where you went to school, the party you both belong to, or where to eat in a town they drive in to. When a tie with someone has grown into a friendship, they ask you to come along somewhere or to do them a favor. What you agree to or promise is kept, and putting them off after they press you strains the tie.
