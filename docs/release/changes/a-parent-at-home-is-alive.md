title: A parent who lives at home at the start is alive
---

A grown-up start could seat a parent in their home in town even though the
family history said that parent had died years before. A relative who lives
in a home at the start is now alive, and only relatives with no home today
can have died before it. Birthday invitations from a living parent come back
with it.
