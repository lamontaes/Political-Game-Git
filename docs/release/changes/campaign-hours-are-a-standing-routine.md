title: Campaign hours are a standing routine
---

A candidate can now set standing campaign hours on the campaign screen: which
days and hours go to knocking on doors and which to fundraising calls. The
hours repeat every week until the candidate changes or stops them. As the day
passes, the game books and does each session when its time comes, the same way
it keeps a job's hours, and each session is written up like one the candidate
chose by hand.

The hours are a limited resource, not something saved up. When a job, a class,
a scene or another promise already holds the time, that session is lost and the
next one goes ahead. No sessions are set for election day or after it.
