section: Changed
title: Every new law takes effect by one rule, and D.C. bills go through committee
impact: patch
---

Every new law now gets its effective date from one place: a state act follows its state's recorded rule, a Congress act takes effect when enacted, a District act takes effect after congressional review, and a town or county ordinance takes effect 30 days after adoption unless the council's own read rule says otherwise. A levy filed with an act still states its own delay.

D.C. Council bills now move through the same bill steps as every other legislature: referral to a standing committee, a hearing, the committee's vote, then two readings and the Mayor. Town council members whose rules allow floor amendments can now offer one before a reading, as D.C. Council members could.
