impact: patch
section: Changed
title: People stop saying lines that answer nothing
---

People no longer answer you with a bare "Oh, my.", "No kidding.", "Wow." or "Huh.", no longer open with "How are you?" and its stock answers, and no longer raise "Did you hear about..." with no reason to. Journal days no longer read "Nothing happened." or "It was hot.", newscasts drop "Welcome back." and "Stay with us.", and a stance answer is no longer a bare "Absolutely." The headline "reopens to the Public" now reads "to the public". Lines the owner graded good are kept.
