title: Leaving a job, a layoff and a laid-off friend's call no longer stop your day for a talk.
---

Jobs happen in the background. When you quit through Leave work, when you or someone you know is laid off, or when a friend loses a job, the job ends, the pay stops and the Journal and the person card tell it, but no talk at work, no farewell from coworkers and no phone call stops your day. A last day at school still has its goodbye.
