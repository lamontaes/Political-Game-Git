id: staff-dismissal-after-attendance
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Playing an aide, the game no longer stops on the day your official lets you go.
---

When the official you work for reviews your attendance and lets you go after you have already put in that day's shift, the job now ends the next morning before any more work, and the post is filled again. Before, the game stopped that evening and could not go on.
