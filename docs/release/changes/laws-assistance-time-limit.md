section: Improved
title: An assistance work requirement now stops benefits after three months
---

When a state requires work for assistance, adults who work fewer than 20 hours a week stop receiving the welfare benefit a raised budget line pays after three months, and the money goes to the households still drawing. Repealing the requirement pays them again. Two benefit changes on the same day no longer clash.
