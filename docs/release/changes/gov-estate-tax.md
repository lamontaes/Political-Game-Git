title: Estates pay the estate and inheritance taxes in force, and heirs learn which law took their share
---

When someone dies, their estate now pays the federal estate tax and their state's estate tax above the exemption in force, then each heir's share pays the state's inheritance tax by their relation, all to the named treasuries. The twelve states and D.C. with an estate tax and the five with an inheritance tax carry their real exemptions and rates. A legislature with the power can change an estate tax's rate or exemption by bill, and the next estate pays the new law. Heirs read the estate's accounting and hold a view on the law that took from their share.
