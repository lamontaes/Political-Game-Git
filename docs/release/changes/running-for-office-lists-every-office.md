title: Running for office lists every office where you live, each with its own age
---

A character too young for every office nearby used to see one unlabeled
sentence under "Running for office" ("This office asks for a candidate to be
at least 24.") instead of the offices themselves. In Rapid City, South Dakota,
an eighteen-year-old now sees the state House and Senate seats, the city
council and the mayor, each with its own requirement beside its name, and
choosing one shows why it cannot be filed for yet.

Every minimum age now reads the same way: "You must be at least 21 to stand
for this office." The governor's section no longer explains that the game had
not read the state's rule.
