title: The news of a recall petition's count says how many signed
---

The news of a recall petition's count used to say only that it had enough signatures for a vote, or that it fell short. It now says how many signed and how many were needed: "It had 519 signatures, and 230 were needed." Where the official heard the petitioners out and fewer signed for it, the story says so: "After Yasmin Cook heard the petitioners out, the count came in 79 lower."
