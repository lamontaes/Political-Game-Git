title: Starting health conditions follow recorded household income
impact: patch
---

Starting chronic-condition counts still follow the 2026 health pack's age and sex prevalence. Within each place's age and sex group, conditions are now spread deterministically through people ranked by recorded household income, with unknown incomes kept distinct from zero. Condition-specific income gradients remain explicitly unsourced in the placeholder ledger; no gradient is invented.
