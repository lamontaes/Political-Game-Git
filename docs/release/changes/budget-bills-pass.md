section: Fixed
title: A legislature's budget bill can pass again
---

An appropriation answers no policy question, so a legislator with no view of
its programs had no reason to vote, and the Alaska budget stopped reaching the
governor. A budget bill is now decided by each member's principles on public
spending, the party that carries it, and how near the fiscal year is when the
government's offices close without one. On the town government screen, a
colleague who abstains is now listed as "Answered present", so the player's
own "Present, not voting" choice is the only line with that text and the
prose-anchor check can bind it again.
