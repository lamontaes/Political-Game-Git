impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: On a small board, you are held at the meeting where your seat could decide the vote, and nothing is filed or voted in your name without you.
---

Playing a member of a county board or town council, you could press Day straight through your own board's meeting and come back to find a budget cut passed, filed in your name, with your yes vote on it. Now, when a body of nine seats or fewer has a bill on its agenda, Day stops at the meeting's hour with the meeting open, so you vote yourself. Other members still file what they want, but never in your name, and if you adjourn without voting your roll-call row says you abstained.
