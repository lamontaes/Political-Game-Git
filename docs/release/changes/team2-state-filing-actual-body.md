State amendment filing reviews now validate the actual saved chambers before
forming legislators' principles. A missing or closed institution cannot borrow
a congressional delegation. A governor term-limit review saves its existing
cause-backed proposal before actual chamber votes; a rejection keeps its named
rollcall, including after Continue or a repeated review. General policy filing
still retains its older scaled preflight verdict pending a separate ruling.
