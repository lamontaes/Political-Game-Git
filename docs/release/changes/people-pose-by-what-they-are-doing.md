title: People in a room pose by what they are doing
---

When you talk with someone, the person answering you now gestures as they
explain, and the others in the room listen with their arms folded or a hand
on the hip, turned toward whoever is speaking. Someone at a lectern stands at
the podium, and someone at a desk or a table leans in or sits with their legs
crossed. Each person keeps the same habits every time you see them, and a
reserved person folds their arms more often. The new poses show as their
paintings arrive. Until then, everyone stands or sits as before.
