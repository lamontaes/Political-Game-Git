title: A running mate who would say no can name a price, your word on the question they care about most
---

Someone you ask to run on your ticket who would turn you down may tell you what would change their answer: your word that you will back the proposal they care about most, named plainly. You can promise or refuse. If you promise, they join the ticket, and your later votes and signatures on that question keep the promise or break it. Breaking it strains things between you.
