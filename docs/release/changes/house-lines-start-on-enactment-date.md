title: Each redrawn state's 2026 U.S. House lines start on the day that state's plan took effect.
---

In Texas, North Carolina, Ohio, California, Utah, Florida, Tennessee, Louisiana and Alabama, a home town now answers from the 2026 lines on the day that state's plan became law, not on one shared January 1, 2026. Texas, North Carolina, Ohio, California and Utah are in force before any 2026 game date. A life on a date in early 2026 still reads the older lines in Florida until May 4, Tennessee until May 7, Louisiana until May 29 and Alabama until June 2. The dates are in `data/research/district-lines-2026/enactment-dates.json` with the event and a cited source for each state; California's row says its date is election day for Proposition 50 and Alabama's is the day the U.S. Supreme Court lifted the injunction.
