title: Twenty-one new places to stand in, and a new desk in the Oval Office
---

Twenty-one new places now have pictures for morning, midday, night and rain:
the mayor's office, a teenager's bedroom, a dorm room, a home kitchen, a church
inside and out, a coffee shop, a gas station store, a farm yard, the school
outside, in the hallway and in the cafeteria, a hospital lobby, a newspaper
office, a lodge hall, the election office, the clerk's back office, a committee
hearing room, the governor's residence from the lawn, and the city hall hallway
and steps. Each has marked places where people can stand, sit or speak, so the
people actually there are drawn in the room.

The Oval Office has a new, wider desk in all four pictures. Its spots were
re-measured: the president's seat sits behind the desk's far edge so the lap is
hidden, and visitors stand on the rug or beside the desk, not inside it.
