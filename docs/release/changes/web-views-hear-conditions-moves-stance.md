title: Reading that the town's rent, jobs or crime changed firms up people's views
---

When the local paper reports that rent rose, unemployment rose or crime rose, the people who read it lean a little harder toward the side they already took: on rent stabilization for rent, on spending for the jobless for unemployment, and on police spending for crime. Only readers move, each by how far they trust the reporter, and each remembers which story moved them, so they can bring it up later.
