title: Money reaches accounts in the played world
---

Every employer with staff now pays its workers, including post offices, police and fire departments, union locals and nonprofits, which used to open with no money. Every home in the town and the county around it has a value or a rent, and rents and mortgages leave households each month. The bank lends to a new business only what its founder's income and own money can carry, and a business opens at the size that pays for. Owners take money out of businesses that hold more than they need, and new businesses pay their suppliers. Workers whose employer closes look for work instead of disappearing from the town's counts.
