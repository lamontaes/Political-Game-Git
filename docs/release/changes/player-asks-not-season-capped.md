title: Your own asks are never held back by a season count
---

When you ask a colleague to sign on to your bill, to vote for it, or to serve, you now always get the conversation. Before, after several asks in a few months, a later ask became only a line in your Journal. What limits these asks now is what is on the record: you see the same person once a day, and someone who already said no to the same idea remembers it and tells you so.
