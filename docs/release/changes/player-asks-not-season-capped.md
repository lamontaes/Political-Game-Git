title: Your own asks are never held back by a season count, and asking again is not the same talk twice
---

When you ask a colleague to sign on to your bill, to vote for it, or to serve, you now always get the conversation. Before, after several asks in a few months, a later ask became only a line in your Journal. What limits these asks now is what is on the record. You see the same person once a day. When you ask someone again who said no before, the talk says so from the start, their no says you asked before, and a second no ends with a plain goodbye instead of another push.
