title: Territory workers are no longer taxed twice after their legislature changes the income tax
---

Workers in a U.S. territory pay the territory's own income tax in place of the federal one. When the territory's legislature changed its income tax, for one a flat tax in place of the graduated one, paychecks there started withholding the federal income tax as well. Now the territory's tax still stands in place of the federal one, whatever rate its legislature sets.
