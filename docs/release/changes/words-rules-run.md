id: words-rules-run
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Lines the owner has said he does not want no longer play.
---

His notes on the words are now rules, and every rule runs over every line in the game. A line that breaks one of them stops playing until it is rewritten, including the lines he rejected by voice on November 8. The chance tweaks that made a disliked kind of line only a little rarer are gone: a line plays or it does not.
