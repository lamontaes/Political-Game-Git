section: Changed
title: The owner's notes on the words are now rules that run over every line.
---

His notes on the words are now rules, and every rule runs over every line in the game. A line that breaks one of them stops playing until it is rewritten, and a new line that breaks one is refused by name. The chance tweaks that made a disliked kind of line only a little rarer are gone: a line plays or it does not.
