title: The note about a decision only you can make goes away when it is over
---

The note saying something came up that only you can decide now goes away once
that decision is made or its window closes. Before, a governor who let a
federal disaster request lapse kept seeing the note, with its "Go to it"
button, for months after there was nothing left to decide.
