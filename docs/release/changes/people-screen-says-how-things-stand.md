title: The People screen says how things stand with somebody
---

The People screen only ever said when the player was last in touch with
somebody. A mother who had publicly distanced herself read exactly like one
who had not, and a housemate read "Last in touch" with a date months old while
the two of them still lived together.

Now somebody the player lives with reads "You live together." and never "It
has been a long while". Each person also carries the same sentence the person
card uses for how things stand between them: that the player would not rely on
what they say, that something between them was never settled, or that they
were close once.
