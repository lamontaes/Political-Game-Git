title: Dates, proposals, weddings and breakups play out as scenes
---

When you or someone close to you starts dating, gets engaged, marries or splits up, you now get a scene: a first date with a few real questions, a proposal you can accept, put off or turn down, a wedding day, and a breakup where you can ask why, take it, apologize or push back.
