title: Corporate, customs, excise and estate taxes are paid by named payers on their due dates
---

The corporate income, customs, excise, estate and gift, selective sales and vehicle taxes that governments collect from outside your town are now paid by named groups of payers on each tax's due dates, such as the four quarterly corporate installments, instead of an even twelfth each month from no one.
