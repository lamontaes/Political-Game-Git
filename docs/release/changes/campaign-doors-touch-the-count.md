title: Knocking on doors no longer fills the town with full people.
---

An evening of knocking on doors now fills in only the one or two residents a porch scene could show. Everyone else who opens stays part of the counted town, still hears you out and still gives you an answer the vote reads. A full campaign season runs much lighter.
