title: Your council or board now asks for your vote on the budget at its meeting instead of casting it for you
---

The town's and county's budget now comes to a vote at the body's meeting on the first day of the fiscal year, on the meeting panel with the other items. If no regular meeting falls that day, the body meets at its usual hour to vote it. Your vote is the one you press; "Decide for me" casts your own position, and if you stay silent you abstain. The public hearing a month earlier no longer records a vote for you. If the budget fails, last year's budget carries, as before.
