section: Fixed
title: Town elections no longer draw a resident who has died
---

A town election could draw a candidate from a household member who had
already died, and the game stopped with "Election contest candidate is
deceased at scheduling date." The draw now passes over anyone no longer
living, so the next living resident files instead. Found in a watched game in
Evergreen, Missouri, where a village board candidate who died in June 2028
was drawn again for the 2029 race.
