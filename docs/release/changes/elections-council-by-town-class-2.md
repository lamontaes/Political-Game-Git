title: More kinds of town have the council their state's law gives them.
---

More towns whose own charter the game has not read now get the council their state's law sets for their kind of town, instead of the same five members everywhere. An Iowa city has a Mayor and five council members, all elected every two years. An Ohio village has a Mayor and six council members on four-year terms. Nebraska and Missouri villages have five trustees, with a chair the board picks. An Alabama town has a Mayor and five council members elected together every four years. A Colorado town has a Mayor and six trustees on two-year terms. A Michigan village has a Village President and six trustees. Louisiana villages have three aldermen and towns five. A Washington town has a Mayor and five council members on four-year terms.
