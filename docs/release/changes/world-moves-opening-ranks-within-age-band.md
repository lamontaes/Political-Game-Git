title: A town's first departures follow each age band's rate, not only who their records push hardest
---

In a new town the first months of moves were mostly the oldest adults, whose records push them out, so age did not shape who left. Adults are now ordered within their own age band, so how many of each age leave follows that age's rate and their records decide who goes first within it.
