id: friends-from-shared-rooms
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The people around a life make friends where they already meet, not by chance
---

The parent, classmate or teacher written around a played life used to be given
a couple of neighbors and a friend or two picked at random from their town.
Now nothing is picked by chance. A friend is someone they already share a
congregation, club, council, child's class or job with, closest in age first.
A sociable person keeps two friends and anyone else one. A neighbor is someone
their household already knows as a neighbor.

When the record places them in no group and no job, their friend is the adult
in town closest to their age, because friends are most often of an age.
