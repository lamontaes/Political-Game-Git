title: Passing time during state legislative filing season is faster
---

Passing two months of a new life during the spring filing season took about a
quarter less time in a measured Palm Beach, Florida life: 69 seconds instead
of 94. A year of a watched world took 498 seconds instead of 564. The same
people file, decide and win as before; only the reading of where candidates
live and how each state's chambers are drawn got cheaper.
