title: What someone thinks of an official now reaches their relatives
---

When a resident forms a view of an official from what happened to them, such as
a lost job the official answers for, they now tell the people they talk
politics with. Each of them learns it as told by that person, and a relative or
housemate weighs it, through their own temperament and party, with the view
they already held. People further out know what was said and decide nothing from
it alone. What a relative was told is not passed on again.
