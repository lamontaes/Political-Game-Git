title: SSI, child care help and home energy help are now worked out on the day the treasury pays them.
---

Who gets Supplemental Security Income, child care help and home energy help, and how much, is now worked out from the laws in force on the day the government pays each month. This is how food aid and the other benefits already worked. Before, these three were rechecked on a monthly date of their own.
