title: The Journal reads as one story from birth
---

The Journal is now one story told from where your family came from, a paragraph to each turn of your life, with no heading over any part of it. Each job before the start names only the employer on its own record, so work you did somewhere else before moving to town no longer borrows the name of your job today, and a family from elsewhere in the state is named by the state.
