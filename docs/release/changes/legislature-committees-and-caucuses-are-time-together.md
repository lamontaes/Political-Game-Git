id: legislature-committees-and-caucuses-are-time-together
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Legislators come to know the colleagues they sit with on committees and in their party's caucus.
---

A state chamber's committees and party caucuses now count as time members spend together, for every member, on the days the place's calendar has them meet. Before, only the floor counted for most members, and the player alone was given separate meeting time, so the player's ties grew in a way no colleague's did. Now the player is read the same way as everyone else, and a committee or caucus the player stays away from is a day they did not sit with those colleagues. Colleagues the player sits with also stay in the player's circle, so they keep choosing what to do each day while the player sees them, as they did when the meetings were the player's alone.
