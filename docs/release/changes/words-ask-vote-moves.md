section: Changed
title: A colleague asking for your vote names the bill by what it does, the vote they want and when the roll is.
---

When a colleague asks for your vote, or you ask one for theirs, the ask now says what the records hold: the bill by what it does, the vote wanted, whether the one asking brought the bill, whether the roll is tomorrow, and the section just added where an amendment caused the ask. The answers name the same bill. Stock lines that said the same thing about every bill are gone.
