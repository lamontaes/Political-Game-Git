section: Fixed
title: A candidate's stands no longer read as a column of budget categories
---

A local race's page shows only the few stands the race turns on, the ones its candidates disagree over most. A stand on a spending line is no longer printed as the line's name with For or Against, which read like being against the police. It will read as a sentence about more or less money for that line once those words are written, and until then it is left out. The small caption under each candidate's name is gone.
