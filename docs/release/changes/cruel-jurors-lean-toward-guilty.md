title: A cruel juror counts wanting the defendant to pay toward a guilty vote
---

A juror with a cruel streak, one who sometimes uses humiliation or suffering as
a means or an end, now counts wanting the defendant made to pay as one reason to
vote guilty. It weighs beside the evidence in the case, the burden of proof and
what the rest of the room says, so on a thin case the same juror without that
streak still votes not guilty.
