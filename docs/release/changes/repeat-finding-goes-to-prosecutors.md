title: Taking campaign money can lead to prosecution, and jail takes people out of office and off the campaign trail
---

A finding that somebody took campaign money for themselves can now go to
prosecutors, and that gets more likely with every finding against the person.
Prosecutors may charge or decline, depending on how strong the evidence is. A
charge can be dismissed, end in acquittal at trial, end in a guilty plea, or
end in a conviction. A plea or conviction brings probation or jail, and jail
is the less common of the two. The papers cover the charge, the outcome and
the sentence.

Someone sentenced to jail loses every office they hold. A jailed candidate
stays on the ballot but can't campaign. The screen says so, and a jailed
opponent stops campaigning too. Every chance, length and effect is a
placeholder until the real rules come back from research.
