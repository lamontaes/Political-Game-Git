title: A second finding for taking campaign money goes to prosecutors, and can end in jail
---

A first finding that a candidate took campaign money for themselves stays a
civil matter. A second one is now referred to prosecutors. Two months later
the candidate is charged in public, and four months after that they plead
guilty and are sentenced to jail. The papers cover both the charge and the
sentence. Every step and length here is a placeholder until the real rules
come back from research. Serving the sentence does not yet change anything:
a person sentenced to jail still campaigns and holds office.
