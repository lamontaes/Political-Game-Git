title: Governors declare emergencies when disaster strikes
---

When a federally declared disaster hits the state, the sitting governor now declares a state of emergency the day it is recorded, where the state's law gives them that power. The state's budget takes on its share of the recovery cost, and the declaration makes the news. If you are the governor, the game does not declare for you.
