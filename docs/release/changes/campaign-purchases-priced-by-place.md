title: Campaign purchases are priced by the size of the place
---

Yard signs, palm cards, postage and print ads now have a fixed unit price, and
the number you need grows with the households in your town or district, so a
big-city race costs more than a small-town one because it has more doors and
mailboxes, not because anyone set a bigger budget. Prices that have not been
researched yet are marked as estimates.
