title: Neighbors bring up who won an election, and say how it sits with them
---

After a count, a person who read the result in the paper brings it up when you talk: who won, and, if they clearly lean toward or away from the winner's party, how they feel about it ("It wasn't my pick." or "I voted for her, so I'm not complaining."). A line about their own vote is said only when their ballot shows it, "our side" only when you lean the same way, and "won it fair" only when the race was not a landslide. Someone in the middle just says who won. Nobody tells a candidate how their own race came out.
