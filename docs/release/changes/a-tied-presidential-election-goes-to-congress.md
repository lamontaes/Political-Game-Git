title: A tied presidential election goes to the House and Senate
---

When neither ticket wins a majority of the electoral votes, a 269-269 tie,
the presidency no longer stays empty. After the count, the House chooses the
President with one vote per state delegation, and the Senate chooses the Vice
President, as the Twelfth Amendment requires. The news says how many
delegations or senators each nominee carried, and the winners take the oath
at noon on January 20.

For now each member of Congress votes for their own party's nominee, and an
evenly divided delegation casts no vote. If the House or Senate cannot reach
a majority, that office stays unfilled.
