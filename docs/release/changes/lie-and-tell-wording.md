title: The English engine can word a lie and the answer to it
---

The English engine can now word a lie told in conversation, a flat denial such as "That never happened.", and what the other person says back. Each line comes from a new lie-and-tell bank mined from what people said in public-domain federal oral histories and testimony. Whether the listener believes the lie is never a roll: a listener whose records hold no knowledge of what happened takes it, an unsure one asks "Is that true?", and a sure one may say "I don't believe that." A denial in the first person is used only when the records show the speaker's own part, and a line the owner grades down is not chosen. No screen shows these yet; the dialogue box's Lie button will use them.
