A bill in Congress on the top income tax rate, foreign aid, a cap on consumer loan interest, the Social Security retirement age, farm subsidies, defense spending, passenger rail or the federal tax terms was listed by its number alone, with nothing to read. Now it has a title and a section, like every other bill.
