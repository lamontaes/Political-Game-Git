title: Cabinet members and national reporters no longer quit and "move away" to the place they already live.
---

National and state cabinet members and national press people were being treated like townspeople deciding whether to move. Every few weeks one "moved away" to the same state they were in and lost their job. They now stay in their posts. And someone leaving town with no relative or usual destination to go to now moves elsewhere in their state, not back into the town they left.
