title: Running for office opens the races as a menu
---

Where a filing office has many races, they now open as a menu beside the conversation, one row each with the office, the seat, the election day and who holds it, instead of a long numbered list in the clerk's talk box. The "(2025, 1)" codes are gone from the races. Your player card also steps out of the way of a conversation.
