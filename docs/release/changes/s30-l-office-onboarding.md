title: Record how your office handles votes and casework.
---

A seated member can set revisable voting and constituent-work preferences for
the current office and read a briefing from actual staff. Preferences save
only when Record is used. Opening the office does not cast a vote. Staff
show known items on file, not a recommended package. A saved instruction
applies only to the bill as it then read; a changed measure waits for a new
decision.
