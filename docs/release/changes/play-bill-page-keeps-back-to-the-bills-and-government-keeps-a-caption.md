title: No Back button on a bill's page, and no small print under a council's name
---

A bill opened from Bills, Government or a law's page closes like every other menu, with the tabs and the close control; there is no Back button. On Government, a council's or office's name sits on its picture with no line of small text under it; its seats are in Seats, and its next meeting and election are in Coming up.
