title: Relatives ring and new people turn up far more often
---

A new adult life saw about three new people and one or two phone calls in 56
days. Family who live elsewhere had no recorded contact on day one, so none of
them ever decided to get in touch, and everyone met new people on one
fourteen-day pace. Relatives now decide for themselves whether to ring, from
their own temperament, and how soon somebody meets a new person follows their
sociability instead of one number for everybody.
