id: lw16-substance-use-record
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: People now carry a private substance use health record at the survey's shares
---

Before: nothing on a person's health record said whether they live with a substance use disorder, so no law about harm reduction or treatment had anyone to reach.

Now: when the model first exposes a person aged 12 or older, their private health record may hold a substance use disorder, at the 2023 national survey's shares by age (8.5 percent of teens, 27.1 percent of 18 to 25, 16.6 percent of older adults, sliding between bands). It changes no one's mortality yet, and nobody starts one later in life until onset from recorded causes is built.

A resident who holds the record now asks the funded harm reduction service for a
visit; full-time work holds a person back, and people without the record have no
reason to ask. The shared funded-service test world keeps its own wage questions,
so the service tests run again.
