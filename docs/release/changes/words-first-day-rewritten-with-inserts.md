title: The first day at a new job now talks about the place you actually work.
---

The coworker who meets you on your first day names your employer and your job, tells you how long the day is, mentions the owner where the business has one, and says who had the job before you and how they left. The old lines about a generic workplace, with its inventory and its coffee mug, are gone.
