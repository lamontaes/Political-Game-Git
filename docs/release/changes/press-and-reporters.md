title: The press has people, owners and a point of view
---

Each town outlet has a named owner, editor and reporters. National channels, a magazine, an online show and a national paper sit beside the town's outlets. Reporters choose each week's stories from what their readers care about. A story's framing follows the outlet's lean and its owner's regard for the people it names. Readers' views move from what they read, and interviews read the outlet, the race and the candidate's record.
