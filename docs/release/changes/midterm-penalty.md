title: A midterm turns voters against the President's party in every seat
---

A congressional or state legislative count now carries the national mood.
In a midterm year, every seat's vote shifts 3.6 points of the two-party
vote against the President's party: the average loss over the 19 midterms
from 1950 to 2022 (Brookings Vital Statistics on Congress, Table 2-2, and
the Clerk of the House). No draw decides it: the same shift reaches every
seat, and a change of President changes which party pays it. A
presidential year or an odd-year state election carries no mood.
