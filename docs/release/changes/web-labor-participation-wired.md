title: A law that raises the full retirement age now keeps more people from 62 to 66 at work.
---

Raising the age for full Social Security benefits used to change nothing about who works. Now people from 62 to 66 weigh it when they decide whether to retire or look for work, and a two-year rise keeps about a tenth more of them on the job, as the research finds. Nothing changes while no such law is in force.
