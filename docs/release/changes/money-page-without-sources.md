title: The Money page stops naming its sources, and a house costs what prices say
---

In Bend, Oregon in 2039 the Money page listed "BEA regional 33 observations",
said a figure was "Available to the simulation on 2026-09-03", and still
offered a $250,000 house next to rent up by half. The page now shows the
figures and their graphs with plain place names and dates such as "July 2026",
without the products behind them, observation counts or the day a figure
reached the game. Graph titles read "Income per person" and "Two-bedroom rent".

The house price, down payment and mortgage payment are still one national
placeholder, but they now move with the world's price level, the same measure
the rent line uses, instead of standing still. The Jobs and study screen gives
a tuition deadline as a date in words.
