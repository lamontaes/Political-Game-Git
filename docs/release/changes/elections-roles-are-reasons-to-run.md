title: Party workers, business owners and civic leaders are likelier to run for seats near them
---

People who are active in a party, own an employer in the county, or hold a civic role now have a stronger pull toward running for the county's and town's seats. In Warrior, Alabama one county seat that drew a single candidate now draws three.
