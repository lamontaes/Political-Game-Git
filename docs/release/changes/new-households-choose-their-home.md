id: new-households-choose-their-home
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A household that arrives or is evicted finds a home by its own pay
---

A household with no home in town no longer gets its kind of home from a draw.
It buys a house when it has work and its pay carries the payments, and rents
otherwise: an apartment for one or two people, a rowhouse for more. A
household evicted in the last season records that it was evicted from its
last home. The town's opening homes still come from its housing mix.
