title: Relationships fade with time apart, without a number
---

Before, a friend the player had not seen in thirty years read exactly like one
they saw yesterday, and turning up again after a quarrel was enough to settle
it.

Now time apart changes how current a relationship is, never what happened in
it. The pace belongs to each pair: a busy month away from a lifelong friend is
nothing, while a single meeting a few years back has gone dormant. Warmth goes
quiet rather than sour and comes back quickly when the two of them meet again.
Trust keeps its record but stops being current until they have dealings again,
so one evening together does not prove reliability. What is owed does not fade.
A quarrel left alone goes quiet without ever being settled, and seeing each
other again is not reconciliation. The person who asked to meet and never heard
back feels the gap before the other does. People who share a home are never
apart, and opening someone's card is not contact.

The person card says all of this in words, never as a meter.
