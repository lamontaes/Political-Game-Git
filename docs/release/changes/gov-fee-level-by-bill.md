section: Added
title: A council or legislature bill can set what a park, sewer, toll, parking, hospital or tuition fee costs
---

Under "Fees and charges" on the bill paper, a body can now raise or lower a fee as a percent of today's. Once the bill's law takes effect, the people who use that service pay the new level, and what the government collects for charges follows it.
