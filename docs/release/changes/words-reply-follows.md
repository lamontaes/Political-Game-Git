---
id: words-reply-follows
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A reply is no longer offered after a line it does not follow.
---

Every pair of a line and the reply that can come after it is now judged once, and the game reads the result: a reply that does not follow a line is never offered after that line. The first judging covered the pairs that come up most. Sixty-seven of them did not follow, and those no longer play together.
