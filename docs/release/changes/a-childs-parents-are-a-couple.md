title: A new character's two parents are a couple
---

A new character's two parents had nothing to do with each other on the
record, and each was drawn a man or a woman on their own, so about half of all
parent pairs were two men or two women. Now two parents raising the character
together are a couple, and a parent who died before the game began was the
other's partner until then. A parent who lives elsewhere is not claimed to be
anything to the other. Most parent couples are a man and a woman.

This applies to new games. A game already in progress keeps the family it has.
