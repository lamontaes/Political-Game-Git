id: housing-arrears-on-grace-day
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Late rent is weighed on the day each state's grace period ends, not on the 6th everywhere.
---

Late rent is now weighed on each state's own day. Rent falls due on the 1st, and a household that hasn't paid is weighed the day after its state's grace period ends: the 2nd in a state with none, the 7th after a 5-day grace, 31 days on in Massachusetts. Before, every household was weighed on the 6th, which ignored any grace longer than five days and gave five extra days where the law gives none. In a year at West Dennis, Massachusetts, eviction notices fell from 23 to 17 as its 30-day grace was honored.
