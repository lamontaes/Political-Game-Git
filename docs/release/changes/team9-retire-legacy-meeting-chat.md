section: Changed
title: Public meeting panels keep agenda and attendance without legacy chat
---

Public meeting panels no longer show placeholder roster speech or a separate comment-choice interface. Attendance, agenda, recorded roll call, brief visits and leaving remain available through their existing actions. Meeting buttons omit duration suffixes. Existing comments and history records are retained; conversation through the dialogue window is separate future work.
