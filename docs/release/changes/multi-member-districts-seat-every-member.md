title: A legislative district seats every member it elects, not one.
---

North Dakota, Arizona, Idaho, New Jersey, South Dakota and Washington House districts now seat two Representatives each, Maryland's seat three (with its subdistrict splits), and Vermont's Windsor Senate district seats its three senators. Before, every district seated one member, so a North Dakota home saw one Representative in a two-member district and Maryland's House had no district bound to any seat.

The counts sit in a data file with a source for each chamber, and a chamber that lists none still elects one per district.
