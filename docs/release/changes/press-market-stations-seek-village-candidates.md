title: A market's TV stations no longer chase a village's council race; the town's own outlet still covers its government
---

Before, every paper, radio and TV station that covered a town acted as if the town were its whole audience, so three stations from a market of hundreds of thousands interviewed a village board candidate on three days running. Now the town's outlet of record (the one that runs its legal notices) keeps the town's government as its full beat, and every other outlet weighs a race and a story by the share of its own audience they reach, filling the rest of its week with the county's, the state's and the nation's news.
