title: A market's TV stations no longer chase a village's council race; small towns' news comes from the county, state and nation
---

Before, every paper, radio and TV station that covered a town acted as if the town were its whole audience, so three stations from a market of hundreds of thousands interviewed a village board candidate on three days running. Now each outlet weighs a race and a story by the share of its own audience they reach, and fills the rest of its week with the county's, the state's and the nation's news.
