title: People's figures fit what they're doing, and nobody has a twin

A figure carrying a skateboard or a basketball appears only outdoors, and one with a laptop, clipboard or tablet only at work. No two people in a scene share a look, and people are drawn nearer their real age. The player keeps one look family as they grow up and is drawn as an adult from their 18th birthday.
