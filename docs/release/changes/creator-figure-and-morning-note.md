On the "How you look" step, your whole figure now stands above the Reset
appearance, Undo and Begin buttons at any window height, and each choice
(Feminine, Average, a hair color) sits on a dark backing so it reads clearly
over the room. When a new life begins, the morning note no longer opens over
the opening tour; it appears once the tour is over and play has begun.
