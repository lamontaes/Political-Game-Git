title: Choosing a hometown shows what housing costs and pays there
---

When you pick where your life starts, the game now tells you how that state's
housing costs compare with the national average, what a two-bedroom apartment
rents for, what a typical family earns, and the latest unemployment rate.
Housing in Rhode Island costs about 5% more than the national average; in
Mississippi, about 44% less. A figure a place does not publish is left out.

Each of the fifty-six places also has a record of the issues that dominate its
politics, waiting on research. None is filled in yet.
