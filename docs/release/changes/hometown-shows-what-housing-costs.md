title: Choosing a hometown shows its state's housing and income benchmarks
---

When you pick where your life starts, the game now shows published figures
about that state: the federal fair market rent for a two-bedroom unit, area
median family income, how housing prices compare with the nation, and the
unemployment rate. Each line names its measure, area and year, and only
figures public by the day your life begins appear. For a January 2026 start
that is the fiscal year 2025 rent benchmark.

Each of the fifty-six places also has a record of the issues that dominate its
politics, waiting on research. None is filled in yet.
