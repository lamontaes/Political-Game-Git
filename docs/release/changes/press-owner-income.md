title: A press outlet's owner has an income behind the household they are given
---

An outlet's owner was given a household with a living cost and a lump of savings and no income, so their cash ran down and four of nine owners at a drawn place were out of money within half a year. The owner income in the press parameters is now paid in each month from the outside account, as an outside-living staff member's pay is, so the cash of owners who live in the town and of national outlets' owners grows instead of running out; an owner of a local outlet in a larger media market can still run dry for another reason.
