title: A job now states the hours it actually works and the pay for them
---

A full-time job used to state a 40-hour week while its schedule ran up to 48 hours, so its stated pay was up to 20 percent under what payroll paid. The job now states the week it works and the daily pay for that week at the same hourly rate, and a hire states the week it copies.
