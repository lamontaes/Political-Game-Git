title: Puerto Rico's municipios now pay their police, schools and offices from a treasury
---

In Puerto Rico a town's municipio employed its police, fire, health and school staff but had no treasury in the budgets, so none of them was paid from public money. The municipio is now the town's county-level government with a treasury and a budget.
