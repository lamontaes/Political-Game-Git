title: Automatic voter registration, home rule and council term limits now change the world
---

A state that adopts automatic voter registration now sees turnout rise by about one point a year later, and a state that repeals it loses that point. Whether a state gives its towns home rule now decides which local questions a town council may answer: under Dillon's rule a town may not pass ordinances on policing, zoning, rent, transit and the other powers the state has not granted, and adopting home rule opens them. A town that limits council terms bars a member with two consecutive full terms behind them from running again, and the seat opens.
