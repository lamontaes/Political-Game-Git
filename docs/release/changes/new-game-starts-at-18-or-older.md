title: A new game starts you at 18 or older
---

A new life now begins at 18 or older. The New game creator offers ages 18 to 70. A life started without the creator begins as one of the town's own adults, at any age the town has. A life begun at the age you pick is someone who lived that age's life, so your partner, children, school and job fit the age you chose. Life before 18 is planned for after early access.
