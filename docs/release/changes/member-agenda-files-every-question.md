title: Members and councils file bills on questions with no effect writer yet
---

A state legislator who opposes a question whose "no" answer has no registered
effect writer now files a repeal bill on it when the law in force says yes,
instead of filing nothing. A city or county council member who leans hard on a
question the town may answer, but no local effect writer covers, now files a
plain position bill that goes on the council's agenda like any other
ordinance. Mapping coverage no longer narrows what members may file. An
enacted bill of this kind is law in force and changes nothing else in the
world yet; that gap is marked PLACEHOLDER in the code.
