title: Cash bail moves money and returns it when the case closes
---

Full cash bail reaches the saved court government's account before a defendant is released. An explicit resident-household payment can also end a hold. The saved case closure returns the deposit to its original payer once; missing accounts and insufficient funds do not claim payment. Commercial premiums and unsourced court deductions are not treated as court deposits.
