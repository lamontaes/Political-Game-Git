title: Glossary help covers offices and works from the keyboard
---

Office names such as mayor, alderman, trustee, selectman, governor and lieutenant governor are now Guide terms, underlined in any text with their plurals. The card opens after the pointer rests about a quarter second, or at once when you tab to a term, and Escape closes it. Mark as learned removes the underline everywhere, and the Guide has a button to underline learned terms again.
