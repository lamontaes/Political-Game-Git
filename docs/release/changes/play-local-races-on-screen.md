title: Campaigns lists every local race on your town's next ballot, with who filed.

Politics → Campaigns now shows each city, township and county seat on the town's next local ballot. Every candidate who filed is listed by name, and a seat is marked unopposed or "No one has filed" when that is what the filings show. While filing is still open, it says when it closes.
