title: Before a hearing and after a verdict, someone close asks you what you will do.
---

When you are charged, someone in your household asks whether you have a lawyer, how you will plead, whether the family should come, and who tells the children and your employer. When a household member is charged, they ask you for the same. After a verdict, the conversation turns to what now: asking a lawyer what can still be done, who to call, what to tell the children and the job. You can agree, promise, ask, or put it off, and what you agree to is kept.
