When a body sends a bill it could not settle to a study committee, the paper tells who chairs it, what it is to study and when it reports, and tells what it found when it does. If you sit on the committee or chair it, the chair (or a fellow member) speaks to you when it is formed and again when it reports: you can agree to serve, call it a delay, ask what it is charged with or what carried the finding, or say nothing, and a line names whether the chair's speech moved your own view of the bill.
