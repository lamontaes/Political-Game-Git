title: Your speeches can start from what is going wrong in your town.
---

When you build a speech, one of the choices at its core is now the area of your town that has moved most since the term began: rents, jobs, schools, health care, the air and the water. If it has gone badly you can say so and take the remedy as your stand; if it has improved you can say that too.
