title: Campaign money a candidate took is still caught after you stop playing them
---

If a candidate paid themselves from campaign money and you then retired them
from play, their committee's reports, a rival's complaint and the state
regulator's review of the reports all stopped, because each one only ever
looked at the person being played. They now look at anybody with such a
payment on record, so the case can still open and reach a finding. Nobody is
accused of anything that did not happen: only a recorded payment starts it.
