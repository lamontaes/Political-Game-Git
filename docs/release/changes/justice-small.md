title: Bribes can be reported, prosecuted and punished
---

An official who refuses a bribe may report it, and the local prosecutor decides whether to investigate, following the money to everyone who took a payment. A case ends in no charge, a plea or a trial. A conviction brings a fine, probation or prison, and costs an officeholder their seat. If your character is charged, the hearing and the verdict play as scenes, and a prison term passes with the world carrying on until you are released with the conviction on your record.
