title: A budget bill sets the line to the amount you typed
---

When you wrote a bill on a budget line from Your office, the amount you typed was added to the line instead of replacing it: typing $71,390 for a $64,900 Highways line made a bill that set it to $136,000. The bill now sets the line to what you typed, and its page says the same amount everywhere.
