title: The paper names the council or legislature a bill goes back to, and when a seat fell empty
---

A story about a veto, a failed override or a committee vote now names the body that acts next ("The Powderly City Council can still override the veto with enough votes."), instead of the same sentence in every town. A new officeholder's story says the day the seat fell empty; a seat left open says since when; a vote won or lost by one says "just one vote"; and a business that closed within two years of opening says the month it opened.
