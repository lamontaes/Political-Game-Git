After a party meeting at the community room, Walk home answered "Nothing
changed." A phone shift from home had been booked for the minute the meeting
ended, and the walk home ran into it.

## What changed

1. Party work you ask for now leaves time to get home. A phone shift is no
   longer booked for the minutes you would still be walking back from the
   community room.
2. When something already on your calendar starts before you could get home,
   Walk home says what it is and when it starts, and you can give that
   commitment up.
3. A phone shift can be worked from Places when you are home. It used to be
   refused everywhere, home included.
4. An invitation to the community room that arrived while the clock was
   jumping ahead now lapses with its trip once the time to leave has gone.
   These used to pile up under Places, each refusing because its time had
   begun.
5. A player under eighteen is no longer offered party work that the game then
   refused.
