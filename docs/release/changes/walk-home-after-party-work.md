title: You can walk home after party work again
---

After a party meeting at the community room, Walk home answered "Nothing
changed." A phone shift from home had been booked for the minute the meeting
ended, and the walk ran into it.

1. Party work you ask for now leaves time to walk home first.
2. When something starts before you could get home, Walk home names it.
3. A phone shift can be worked from Places when you are home.
4. Missed community-room trips no longer pile up under Places.
5. Nobody under eighteen is offered party work.
