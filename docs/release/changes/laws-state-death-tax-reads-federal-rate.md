title: A state with no estate tax no longer reads the federal rate as its own
---

A state with no estate or inheritance tax read the federal 40 percent estate tax as its own, so a state bill creating a 16 percent estate tax was weighed by lawmakers as a cut. A state's estate and inheritance taxes are now its own: a state with none has none, and a bill that creates one is weighed as a raise.
