title: A party body decides something only when something is before it
---

Every three months each party body took up the next question on its list,
whether or not anything had happened. A line it had already settled was
decided again on a timer, and a member who lost the vote once lost it
again on schedule. That counted as a dispute that kept coming back, and it
could end with the member leaving to form a new party.

Now a body takes up a question only when there is a reason for it. It
settles a question it has never decided, or, after one of its own members
loses an election where it sits, it reconsiders how it chooses candidates.
Otherwise the meeting is quiet, and the body meets again the next quarter.

How members lean on each question is still a stand-in, stable for each
person. Which of a person's qualities should lean them which way is filed
as a research question.
