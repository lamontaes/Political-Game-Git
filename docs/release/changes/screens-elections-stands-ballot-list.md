title: The Elections ballot is one tidy list, and a candidate's stands read in plain words.
---

The Elections list has no headings over it, every race shows its line under its name, and the last race shown is never cut off. A candidate's stands read as what they are about, like "a bigger police budget", instead of a budget category's name.
