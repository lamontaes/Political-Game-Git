title: Your bill opens as a paper document you can read and click.
---

A filed bill now opens as one paper document: its number and title in
plain words, its sponsor, each section, the day the floor takes it up
and the whip count, and the vote and signature once they exist.
Clicking a section shows beside it what the bill would do: the law it
amends, the budget line and its amount, and what it costs and who it
reaches. The old bill rows are gone from your seat.
