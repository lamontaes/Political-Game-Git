id: p4-5-opening-record-values
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The opening's screens show recorded values, not written sentences
---

The opening's screens now show what the records hold, each value under its
label, instead of sentences the screens wrote. The year screen lists the
President, each chamber of Congress by party, unemployment and the change in
prices over a year. The White House lists each officeholder under their title.
The state legislature lists each chamber by party. Your family lists each
parent's work. The state screen gives the share of citizen adults who voted
with its margin, or the national share marked as an estimate. Helper lines,
picture captions, a roster note and a paragraph that repeated Congress's own
chart are gone.
