title: Issues names each tax by the government that sets it.
---

Politics → Issues no longer calls a town's combined federal and state income tax a local income tax. The income and sales tax cards are titled by the tax alone, and each rate under them names the government that sets it, such as Federal · United States or State · Texas, including the rates scheduled for later. A state's average local sales tax says it is that average and an estimate.
