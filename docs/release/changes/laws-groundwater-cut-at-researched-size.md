title: A groundwater limit cuts a farm's pumping by the researched amount
---

A state law that limits large groundwater withdrawals now cuts each town farm's pumping by about 31%, the cut Kansas irrigators made under their first capped allocation, instead of an estimated 20%. The farm sells that much less outside the town from the day the law takes effect. The outcome web no longer keeps a second copy of the same effect that nothing read.
