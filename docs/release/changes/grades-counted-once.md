title: Each of the owner's grades on a line now counts once.
---

The grades from batches 2 and 3 had been counted four times each, so a line graded once looked as if it had been graded four times. The ledger now counts every graded row once, however many times the grades are folded. This also brings in the batch 2 and 3 grades and the lines rewritten from them.
