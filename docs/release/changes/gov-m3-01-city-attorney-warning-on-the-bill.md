section: Added
title: A bill on a power the state has not settled carries the lawyer's warning
---

When you draft a bill on something your council or board may not have the power to do, its paper now says so: your town's city attorney warns that the council may not have the power to do this (before a county or township board, the board's lawyer warns instead). A bill on a power the state plainly gives your town carries no warning.
