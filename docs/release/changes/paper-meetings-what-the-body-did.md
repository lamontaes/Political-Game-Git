title: The paper reports what the council did, not that it met
---

The local paper no longer runs a story every time a council or county board meets. A meeting with business is told by its votes, and the week's meetings that took up nothing are one short dated brief. Another state's swearings-in stay out of a town paper, a small town's jobless rate is said in words instead of "0%", and the most-printed lines read more than one way.
