section: Changed
title: Every line in a bargaining conversation comes from the bill itself
---

When you bargain with another legislator, everything they say is now put
together from what the bill and the room actually hold: its number, the
section in play, who that section is written for, its cost and the next vote.
Lines that used to claim things nothing recorded are gone. One member used to
say "a dollar over that" and they would vote the way they always had, and
another would demand a program "standing inside a year". A member who wants
you to write a section in says so only after you have held out on them, and a
member reminds you of what they said earlier only if they said it.
