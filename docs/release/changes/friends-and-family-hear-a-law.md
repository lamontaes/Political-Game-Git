section: Changed
title: What a law did to a friend or partner weighs by the tie
---

When someone heard what a law did to a friend, it used to move their view of
the officials behind it by a fixed quarter, whoever the friend was. Now it
moves them as much as they care about the one who told them: half from someone
they are close to, a quarter from someone they like, and an eighth from anyone
else. A law that hit a partner's paycheck used to count at a fixed half; now it
counts in full against the couple's pooled pay, so it lands harder in a
household that lives on one paycheck than in one with two.
