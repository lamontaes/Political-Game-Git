id: a31-native-retail-receipt-tax
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Assess cannabis tax from paid retail receipts
---

Recorded retail receipts now supply the seller, payment amount and sales period
to an operative adopted cannabis levy. Its saved rate and allowance determine
the assessment; the existing scheduled tax writer collects from the seller's
actual cash. Replay does not create another sales receipt or tax payment.

The cannabis portion uses the cited Washington fiscal-year taxable-retail share
as an explicitly estimated allocation of the saved retail receipt, under the
selected October 2 reuse option, pending CTO review. The source denominator and exemption
limits remain visible in the data and recorded base note. No business revenue
level, annual-to-quarter conversion, new cash producer or default tax rate is
introduced. Starting authorization without a numeric operative levy remains
unsupported, rather than creating a fictional tax policy.
