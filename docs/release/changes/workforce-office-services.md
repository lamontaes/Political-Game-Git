title: Residents can explain what they need from a workforce referral.
---

At office hours, a resident can ask for interview practice, help with a résumé, training, or guidance on unemployment. A resident with a recorded layoff can also ask how to describe their last job or where that experience would count.

Once the resident explains what they need, the official can make that specific referral, explain a refusal, or ask a serving aide to help. A question about unemployment paperwork stays with that request through the handoff.
