title: A balanced-budget law no longer cuts Social Security checks, and Congress closes the gap over five years
---

A federal balanced-budget law now cuts the way federal across-the-board cuts are written in law: Social Security, veterans' benefits, the low-income programs and interest are untouched, and Medicare is cut by at most 4 percent. Congress closes the gap in equal steps over five fiscal years, as Gramm-Rudman-Hollings set its deficit targets, instead of all at once. A state still balances each year's budget, across every line but its debt payments.
