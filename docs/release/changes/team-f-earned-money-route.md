Before: The candidate own-money route had no proof that wages earned during ordinary play could pay for a campaign expense.

After: A focused test starts with no personal money, works eight shop shifts, waits for recorded pay, files for an offered office, contributes $500 to that campaign, and pays for one existing advertising buy. It checks both accounts, insufficient-funds refusal, the election and committee scope, and save/replay persistence. The test does not claim that advertising reach is modeled.
