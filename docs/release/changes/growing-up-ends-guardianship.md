title: Growing up ends a guardianship, and buying a home moves you out
---

A character who started a new game as a child stayed under their guardian's
or parent's authority for life. A twenty-seven-year-old who owned her own
house still had her guardian listed at home as "your guardian".

Now that authority ends on the character's eighteenth birthday. The person
who raised them is still named for it: a parent as a parent, and a guardian
as the guardian who raised you. A game already in progress is caught up the
next time days pass, dated to the birthday.

A grown character who still lives with a parent or guardian and buys a home
now moves into it, with a partner and their own children if those live with
them. The parent or guardian stays where they were, and the Household list
shows only the people in the new home.

Eighteen everywhere is a placeholder for now, filed as a research question.
Some states use nineteen or twenty-one.
