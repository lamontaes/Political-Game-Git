id: growing-up-ends-guardianship
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A grown character's guardian is no longer "your guardian", and buying a home moves you out
---

A character who started a new game as a child kept their guardian's or
parent's authority for life, and a twenty-seven-year-old who owned her own
house still had her guardian listed at home as "your guardian".

Now a grown character is not described as anybody's dependent. The person who
raised them is still named for it: a parent as a parent, and a guardian as the
guardian who raised you.

A grown character who still lives with a parent or guardian and buys a home
now moves into it, with a partner and their own children if those live with
them. The parent or guardian stays where they were, and the Household list
shows only the people in the new home.

When a guardianship legally ends differs by state, and the game does not know
it yet, so the record of it is left open and filed as a research question.
Eighteen, used for the wording and for moving out, is a placeholder.
