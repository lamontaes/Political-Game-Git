title: A cap on loan rates closes storefront lenders
---

A state law capping consumer loan rates now lowers the number of payday,
title and installment lenders in that state by about a third, a year after it
takes effect, and repealing a cap brings them back. An Act of Congress capping
consumer loan interest does the same in every state that did not already cap
rates. Each state starts with its real count of consumer lenders per 100,000
residents; Alaska, Vermont and the territories are unknown until their counts
are read.
