title: When your bill loses its vote, one of your aides brings you the news.
---

A bill held in committee or voted down on the floor is now on its sponsor's record. When the sponsor has staff, one of their aides comes into the office with the bill and the count, and the sponsor can ask how it happened, blame the count, or plan to bring it back.
