title: Townspeople decide to run for office, and every resident's vote counts in local races
---

A year or so before a local seat's filing deadline, every adult in town weighs running, and the record keeps each person's reasons. Those who decide to run file with the place's filing office once filing opens, on a platform from their own views. On Election Day the town's counted residents vote in local races from what they know (the incumbent's name, the party, how well known each candidate is), and the result shows their votes by age group. A named person's ballot now follows their own recorded views, with their party tie as one influence.
