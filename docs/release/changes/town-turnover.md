section: Changed
title: Town workers are let go at the national turnover rate, so out-of-work counts rise and fall
---

People who work in town now lose jobs at the pace working people do everywhere (about one in eighty each month, moved by their state's unemployment and their age), so a town's out-of-work count moves up and down instead of sinking to nothing once residents can find regional work.
