title: Past races and national polls differ in every world
---

Each new world opens with its own count of votes cast in the races before the start, and its own national polling on every policy question, drawn around the published figures. Voters deciding a ballot measure or a recall, officials weighing a vote, and the news's sense of a divisive question all read the world's polls.
