title: Parents can request funded pre-K and after-school spots for their children.
---

Recorded parents can request a funded service for an eligible child in their household. The child enrolls with the paid provider, and attended hours reach the existing delivered-service record. Service forms supply ages, enrollment exclusions, and session hours for both pre-K and after-school programs.
