title: Price and sales tax laws now reach households
---

Laws that change what things cost now show exactly who they reached. Fare-free transit, a carbon price, a bottle deposit, a clean electricity standard, a mileage fee, public broadband and legal cannabis sales each record the households, the wider population and the sellers or treasuries that paid or received the change, and the record ends when the law is repealed. An exemption of groceries from the sales tax now spares part of what households spend at retail stores, and a sales tax rate change shows up under the new law for every seller that collects it.
