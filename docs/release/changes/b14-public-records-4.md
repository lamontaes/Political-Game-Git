id: b14-public-records-4
impact: minor
section: Added
title: Public misconduct records are readable as filed
---

Contract awards, public payroll postings, disclosure filings, public budget
ledgers, and recorded legislative votes are now marked public evidence when
they are produced by a recorded misconduct act. Private correspondence and
other nonpublic artifacts retain their supplied access level, so readers can
use the existing evidence and institutional support paths without treating a
private event as a public record.
