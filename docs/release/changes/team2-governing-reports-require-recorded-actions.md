title: Governing follow-ups report recorded actions and delivery
---

Governing work counts as completed only when its completion cites the matter's
saved decision. Cancelled or lapsed work does not qualify. Budget and agency
follow-ups now require a linked enacted appropriation or saved delivery outturn;
party, staff skill, a spending decision, and a payment alone do not create results.
