section: Changed
title: The art queue can now ask for newspapers, charts and interface pieces
---

Nothing changes in play yet. Behind the scenes, the art request queue could previously only describe a room background, a title background or a piece of reference, so the newspaper, the charts and the interface pieces the game draws by hand had no way to be asked for. The queue now names those, along with the character parts it had been recording without them being listed, and five requests are filed: a nameplate for each of the four papers, the paper sheet the news is read on, the ground behind every economic chart, the card the Guide opens on a civics term, and the strip along the top of the Politics hub. Each one is checked against the art already owned before it is asked for, and none of them is approved art yet.
