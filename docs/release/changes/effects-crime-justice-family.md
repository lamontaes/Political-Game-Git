title: Vandalism happens to households, and ending cash bail changes how many defendants are convicted
---

Households can now be victims of vandalism, at the rate the FBI's incident counts give for homes, and more of it where unemployment rises, as research on unemployment and vandalism measures. Where a law ends cash bail, fewer defendants are held before trial, and the court convicts fewer of them by the size research on pretrial detention measured; each sentence keeps its reasons. Nothing changes where no such law passes.
