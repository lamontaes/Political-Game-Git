title: The icons over your card are a column of round buttons, with News
---

The menu icons above the player card are now a straight column of seven round icons: Journal, Calendar, Money, Politics, News, People and Travel. News has its own icon, and a dot shows on it while a story is waiting to be read. With a screen open, the Menu list on the left is the way between screens and the column steps aside.
