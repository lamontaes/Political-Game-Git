title: Your office says who has your bill next
---

The office already named the bill you are working on. It now also says who has
it next and what they decide — the committee, the chamber's leadership,
whoever the rules of that legislature actually put in the way — along with the
bar a vote has to clear when there is one.

That answer was already in the game, but only inside the bill itself, two steps
away. Standing in your own office is where you should be able to see that
something is waiting on you, or that nothing is.
