title: A person's card names only what you have learned about them
---

A card no longer lists the whole temperament of someone you barely know. It
names a trait only once you have seen it decide something they told you, such
as a neighbor agreeing to come to a meeting because they like company. The
small card names up to three. The full card says, for each, when you saw it and
why they answered as they did.
