section: Changed
title: Union dues come out of each member's own pay
---

A union member is now recorded on the job they hold, and each member pays dues as a share of their own pay instead of a share of the place's median wage. A full person pays through a standing monthly bill from their own cash, and counted members still pay from their place's pooled cash. The local's income follows those payments.
