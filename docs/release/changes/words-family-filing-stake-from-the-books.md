title: The relative at home speaks up about your filing only when the race costs your household something
---

After you file, someone at home confronts you only when the race costs the house something real: your own money in the campaign leaves the savings short of the race, or you work and a young child is at home. Their question, your four replies and their answers now name the office, the race's length, the money you put in or the job you left, or the child by name. With nothing at stake, the filing is simply news at home in the evening talk.
