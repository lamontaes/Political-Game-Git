title: Official reflections retain recorded vote knowledge
---

When a person reflects on a law, their view of a legislator now retains the
recorded vote knowledge it used. A public roll call can be learned through
news or an acquaintance; a private vote requires existing event knowledge.
Missing or future vote records supply no knowledge. The player continues to
decide their own view.
