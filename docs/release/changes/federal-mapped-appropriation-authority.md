section: Fixed
title: Federal appropriation authority follows its saved bill target
---

An enacted federal NPC appropriation now records spending authority only when
its saved proposition, game-profile authority, positive money parameter and
operative section match an eligible bank row. The program key follows that
authority while preserving the existing passenger-rail identity. A filed bill
or an authorization ceiling does not create cash, a payment or delivered work.

The bank still needs additional approved federal profiles and typed effects
before Congress can file across more policy families. This change does not
claim that federal breadth or the seeded ordinary-life route is complete.
