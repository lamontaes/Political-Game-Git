title: Towns now protest, rally and walk out depending on how the term is going.
---

When a town's schools, rents, jobs, health or air slide over a term, the people they touch begin to organize: neighbors who know each other pull each other in, and the longer it goes the bigger it grows. A term that goes well draws rallies instead. People in a protest press the town's officials on the issue, and the officials' views move by who is asking.
