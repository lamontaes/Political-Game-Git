section: Fixed
title: A person's card and every scene now show the same outfit for the day.
---

Before, a person could wear one outfit on their card and another in the scene
you were standing in. Now the card and every scene show the same outfit for the
whole day.

Before, a person's card could show different clothes from the ones the same
person wore in the room on the same day.

Now each person has one outfit a day, chosen from where they work, and the
card and every scene show that outfit.
