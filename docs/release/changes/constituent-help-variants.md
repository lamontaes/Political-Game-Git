title: A constituent at office hours comes in four ways: a lost job, a rent notice, pay under the floor or sickness
---

A resident who comes to your office hours no longer tells the same story every time. One who lost a job names the employer and how long ago it was. One whose household has a rent notice names what the household owes and the day it runs out. One paid under the minimum wage puts the pay stub on the table, with the rate and the law's rate. One who is sick, or caring for someone at home who is, says so, and names the relative. Which story you hear is read from the resident's own records: the layoff on the books, the open notice on their household, the pay in their case, the care their health asks of them or of someone close. A resident with none of these plays the plain arc as before, and every arc ends the same ways it did.
