section: Added
title: States now track their source-backed electricity generation mix
---

Each state and Washington, D.C. now starts with separate measures for the 2024
shares of electricity generated from coal, natural gas, nuclear, wind, solar,
and conventional hydro, plus a distinct remainder for untracked categories
and source-total rounding. The measures retain the published shares without
normalizing or reallocating the remainder. Places without a source row remain
unknown. This records starting values; it does not change fuel shares in
response to clean-electricity laws.
