title: The House elects its Speaker and the Senate its President pro tempore by vote
---

When the line of presidential succession reaches Congress, each chamber now
votes for its presiding officer. Each party caucus first chooses a nominee
from its longest-serving members, then the whole chamber votes among the
nominees. Every member decides from their party, how they get along with each
candidate and how close the candidate's principles are to their own, and
every ballot is recorded with its reason. Before, the majority's
longest-serving member was placed in the chair without a vote.
