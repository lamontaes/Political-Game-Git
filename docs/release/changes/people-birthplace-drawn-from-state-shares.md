title: Townspeople are no longer all born in the town
---

Everyone in a new town used to be recorded as born there. Each person's birthplace is now drawn from their state's census shares of where its people were born, so about three in ten Wisconsin residents, for example, were born in another state or abroad. Their card and life story say where, and someone born in another state is told as having moved to town from there.
