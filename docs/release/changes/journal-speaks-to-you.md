---
id: journal-speaks-to-you
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: The Journal reads as your own record
---

The Journal pasted a conversation in as a script ("Selena McGuire: Say hello.
Dakota Austin: Hi, Selena."). It now says what you did: "You said hello to
Dakota Austin." An entry that named you in the third person ("Selena McGuire
met Alice May") now reads "You met Alice May". A posted meeting agenda,
coursework still due and a choice still open no longer appear in your life
story; they stay with your open items.

When a crime happened to you, the Journal used to show the police log's line
("Police in Fairbanks took a report of an assault"), which read like somebody
else's news. It now says what happened to you: "You were assaulted."

A new test also reads every choice the Journal records and fails if one is
written in the present tense.
