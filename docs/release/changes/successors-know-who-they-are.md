id: successors-know-who-they-are
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Retiring from play no longer offers your own father as a stranger
---

Before: a 34-year-old in Reno who retired from play was offered her own
father and the person she lived with as "no connection on record", and the
middle-school teacher who had mentored her as "someone they taught". Every
stranger on the list read "Rachel Roth's no connection on record".

After: a recorded parent is offered as a parent, anyone under the same roof as
someone they lived with, and a mentor as someone who taught them. People with
no tie say so in a sentence: "No connection to Rachel Roth on record."

How: the successor list reads parents and the current household, and reads
which side of a mentorship the finished life was on. Who is told about an
estate is unchanged.
