title: People are named from your side, the same way on every screen
---

Before: a 34-year-old in Reno who retired from play was offered her own
father and the person she lived with as "no connection on record", and the
middle-school teacher who had mentored her as "Rachel Roth's someone they
taught, age 64". Elsewhere, people were introduced with clauses such as "Dean
Campos, who you live with".

After: people are named the way you would name them. On the successor list,
in the room, on the People screen and in scenes you see "Dean Campos, your
housemate", "Your former teacher, 64", "your grandfather" and "your coworker".
Someone with no tie on record reads "Not someone close to you, 41."

How: the successor list reads parents and the current household, and which
side of a mentorship the finished life was on. The relationship reader every
screen shares now returns a noun phrase from your side and knows mentors and
grandparents. Who is told about an estate is unchanged.
