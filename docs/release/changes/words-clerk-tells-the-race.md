title: The clerk tells you about the race before the paperwork
---

At the filing office, once you name the office, the clerk tells you about it: that you're running for it, the primary and general election dates (or that there is no primary), the length of the term, and what the office does, before the deadline and any rule that stops you. The signatures a petition needs come next, once the elections engine keeps the petition record.
