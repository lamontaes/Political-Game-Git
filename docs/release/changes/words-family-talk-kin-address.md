title: A brother or sister who lives apart asks after your parents as "Dad" and "Mom".
---

Relatives who share a parent or grandparent now ask after them the way family does, as Dad, Mom, Grandma or Grandpa, never by first name or as "your father". A brother or sister you share is asked after by first name. Someone who lives with that relative doesn't ask after them.
