section: Changed
title: Workers can explain what they will do while a wage referral is pending.
---

When a local official gives the office's deadline, a worker can respond to the concern they brought in, including how to document hours or who might see their name.

Job seekers can also identify the workforce office they want to ask about training, interviews, applications, benefits or a résumé.
