title: A new factory or inn no longer loses every outside customer before it can hire
---

A business founded in town opened with nobody on staff and, a month later, lost every customer from outside town because it had no workers to serve them. A one-person factory then never posted its first job and sold nothing for good. A new business now posts its opening jobs at once, as a real startup hires before it opens, and keeps the outside customers it won while those openings stand. Once it hires, or if its openings lapse unfilled, its staff decides how many customers it serves, as for every other business. In three towns played for three years, founded factories and inns now sell outside from their first month, and some still close.
