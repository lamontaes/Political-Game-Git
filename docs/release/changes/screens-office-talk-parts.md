Your office, the bill on paper, Campaigns, the filing counter, election day and night and the person card now draw every line, choice and button from the UI kit. They read as before; a stored kind with no worded name (a filing status, a venue) now shows nothing instead of its code, and a bill's yes and no counts come from the legislature's view instead of being counted on screen.
