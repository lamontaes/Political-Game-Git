Adds a drives module and a minimal health producer to the isolated new-core prototype. A recorded illness or death is weighed by each witness's role, closeness and traits, and the person chooses through the shared chooser between carrying on and forming a cause, grudge, family resolve, ambition or calling. Drives fade by half-life, strengthen when acted on and add pull to telling others, joining or founding groups, donating, volunteering, confronting and seeking standing. Each resident carries a personal resilience line that decides the day a chronic condition becomes a serious illness, with yearly deaths kept on the SSA 2023 life table. Adds 12 open stopgaps. Not wired into shipped play.
