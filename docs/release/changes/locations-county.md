title: County government has its own buildings.
---

The county courthouse now looks like its state's courthouses: Romanesque in the Midwest, a town-green courthouse in New England, adobe in the Southwest, a plain one on the Plains and in the Mountain West, a modern one on the Pacific, and a classical one elsewhere. Sheriffs, prosecutors, treasurers, clerks and assessors work in their own offices, and the jail, the health department and the county administration building have their own rooms. Filing to run for office happens at the elections office when elections take the filing, and at a clerk's counter with no painted map otherwise.
