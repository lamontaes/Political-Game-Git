section: Changed
title: Menus sit on framed plates, tabs on a rail, and a person's card tells their life as a timeline
---

Every menu's sections now sit on framed brass plates instead of the bare black panel, and every row of tabs sits in a recessed rail with the chosen tab raised and lit. A person's card was redrawn: their figure stands in a framed plate, their work, seat and home are small plates, and their history runs down a gold timeline with a year and an icon for each event, turning pages by years instead of scrolling. Their public record and the officials they know sit under a Record tab. Government's member cards no longer carry a colored mark for how someone feels about you.
