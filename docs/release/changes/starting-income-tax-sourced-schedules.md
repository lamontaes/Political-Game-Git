title: Use recorded opening income-tax schedules
---

Opening personal income taxes now use recorded 2026 single-filer schedules in Delaware, Missouri, North Dakota, Oklahoma, Massachusetts, New Jersey, and West Virginia. Massachusetts and New Jersey use their recorded base taxpayer exemptions to reduce taxable income. West Virginia uses its lower 2026 rates, legally retroactive to January 1, and one recorded $2,000 taxpayer exemption. Additional eligibility-based deductions and credits remain outside these base schedules.
