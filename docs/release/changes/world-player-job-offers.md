---
id: world-player-job-offers
section: Fixed
title: Your character is no longer hired without your say; employers offer you the job and you decide
impact: patch
---

When an employer picks your character for an opening, the game now stops and offers you the job, holding the position for a week while you decide. You can accept or turn it down; an offer you leave unanswered lapses. The game no longer decides for your character whether to look for work, quit or retire. Everyone else takes, leaves and quits jobs as before.
