title: A complaint to the prosecutor about you now plays four different confrontations.
---

When someone reports you to the prosecutor over a bribe, the face-to-face that follows depends on what they know. An official you offered money to says so plainly, someone who was only told of it asks who talked, a case where the money was taken names more than one person, and a friend asks you to tell them the truth. The old lines about a firm on a bid list no longer play for a complaint.
