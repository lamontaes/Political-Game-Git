title: A governor who dies is succeeded, and a vacant Chief Justice seat is filled
---

When a governor dies in office, the next officer in line takes over and serves
the rest of the term, instead of the office sitting empty until the next
election. When the Chief Justice dies, the President nominates a successor and
the Senate confirms them. A nominee who sat in Congress leaves the seat.

Candidates for the Maine House now need to be 21, the age the state
constitution sets, instead of the 23 the game had drawn.
