section: Changed
title: A group weighs a candidate by what its members want, and its endorsement reaches the townspeople who share its cause
---

A group used to weigh a candidate only on its own cause, so a party chapter ignored a candidate's plans for the town's budget. Now, on every question a candidate runs on that the group has no cause of its own, it weighs what its members want. And when a group backs a candidate, the townspeople who share its cause but are not counted one by one hear it too, by how firmly they hold that cause, and it moves their vote the same way it moves the members'.
