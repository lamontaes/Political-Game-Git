title: A state that adopts an income tax collects it
---

A state that began with no income tax and passed one started withholding
from paychecks, but its budget still counted no income tax from anyone else.
From the tax year the law governs, the budget now collects the median state
income tax per resident, estimated from the states that tax wages, and a
state's budget notes show that figure before any law passes.
