section: Changed
title: Filing for office no longer charges a fee
---

Filing for office charged the fee the place's law sets, and a new adult with no money was turned away at the counter in the places that charge one. The fee is out of the game for now: the clerk names no fee, nobody is refused for one, and no one in the world is kept from running by it. Petitions, signatures and the clerk's check are as before.
