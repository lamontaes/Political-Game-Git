title: A county board or town council is named the way people write it.
---

A county board used to read "COUNTY OF PIKE County Commission" and a town council "VILLAGE OF EOLIA Council", for instance in the opening line of a bill. They now read "Pike County Commission" and "Village of Eolia Council", and a board whose name already says "County" no longer says it twice.
