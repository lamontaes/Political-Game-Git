title: Office salary laws use the common payroll authority path
---

An operative office salary law now changes recorded weekly pay terms through
common payroll, preserving increases, decreases and limits on existing terms.
The saved terms name the actual law and retain its provision and enactment
records through canonical attribution.

Recorded NPC office holders now receive salary agreements and use the same
payday settlement as the controlled office holder. Operative office-pay laws
reach every matching recorded official; saved public accounts remain the
payers where their ownership is recorded.
