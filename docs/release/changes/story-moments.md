title: The world now notices which moments of a life matter
---

Every change in a person's life that the world records, from starting school to a death in the family, is scored once by how much it matters to that person, using published life-change weights. Most days score nothing. These scored moments are what the story director will build threads, scenes and journal chapters from. An introduction now also puts the person back in touch with whoever introduced them.
