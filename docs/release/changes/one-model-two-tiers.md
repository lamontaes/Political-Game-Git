section: Changed
title: The townspeople are counted by group, and move for reasons
---

The town's wider population is now one record per group (place, district, household kind, income band, housing, age band and work), read by the same tax, benefit, rent and mortgage rules as the people the player knows, with each group's money on its own account. A law's reach counts both tiers. People move between the town and every other state from causes (after-tax income, prices, jobs, the law in force), and a filled-in household returns to the counts after a long while out of the player's life.
