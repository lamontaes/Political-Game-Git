id: gov-ordinance-form
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A town or county measure reads as an ordinance, closing on when it takes effect
---

A council's or county board's measure now carries the form of an ordinance: numbered by the year, ordained by that body, its sections, and a closing section on when it takes effect (a set number of days after adoption, or the date it names). A state act still takes effect by its state's rule and Congress's on enactment, now worked out in one place for the bill's text and its enactment.
