title: Your grades of which reply follows which line now shape everyday talk.
---

Grades of whether one move reads naturally after another (an answer after a question, a reaction after news) only shaped speeches before. Now they also decide which four replies a talk offers you first: a kind of reply graded unnatural after a line is offered after the ones that read right.
