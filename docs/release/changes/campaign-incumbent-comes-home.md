title: A rival who learns you are gaining on them now campaigns harder.
---

Other candidates now react when the race turns against them. When their own poll shows a challenger ahead, or someone who knows both of you tells them about your campaign, including your evenings knocking on doors, they put in more hours and knock more doors themselves. A sitting official who lives out of town comes home to campaign, more of the time the further behind they think they are. The more you gain, the harder they push back.
