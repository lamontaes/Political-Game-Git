title: News is a front page with the stories' text
---

News opens as a newspaper's front page: the lead story under a large headline with its full text, the next stories with theirs in the column beside it, and short items as briefs. The "Public record" rows, which printed record kinds, series names and jurisdiction labels ("Series Local sales tax average · Jurisdiction Colorado"), are gone.
