title: In a conversation the speaker now sits on the talk card, with everyone else small above it.
---

Whoever is speaking now shows as one bust on the talk card itself. The other people in the room sit small and close together at the card's top, in a band only as wide as they are, so one person no longer gets a band across the whole card.
