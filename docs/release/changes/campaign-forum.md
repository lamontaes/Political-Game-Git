title: Candidates can host a forum.
---

On the Campaigns screen, a candidate can now host a forum: the campaign rents the evening's hall, and the residents who feel most strongly about a question come and ask it, whether or not the campaign had met them before. The candidate answers, the room makes up its own mind, and the people who came are now within the campaign's reach for its calls and events.
