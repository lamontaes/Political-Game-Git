title: A laid-off worker can have it out with the owner who let them go, and a governor fills an empty cabinet post
---

A worker who blames the business owner for a layoff can now confront that owner even when the town has not met them yet; the owner is filled in as anyone met is, and the falling-out costs the tie. A governor's cabinet is chosen by weighing loyalty against merit, as town department heads are, and a post left empty by a death or a move is filled at the governor's next review.
