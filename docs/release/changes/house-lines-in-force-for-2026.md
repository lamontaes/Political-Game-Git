title: A home town in nine states answers from the U.S. House lines in use for 2026.
---

In Texas, California, North Carolina, Ohio, Utah, Florida, Tennessee, Louisiana and Alabama, a life started in 2026 or later now finds its U.S. House district, the House seats it can run for, and the "possible districts" for a split town from the lines those states use for the 2026 elections, not the older 119th Congress lines. Across those nine states, 2,472 towns have a different answer than before. Missouri stays on its 2022 lines, and every other state is unchanged. The map's district outlines for those nine states now draw the 2026 lines too, on every date of the history slider, since play starts in 2026 or later.
