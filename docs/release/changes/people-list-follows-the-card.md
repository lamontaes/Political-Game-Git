title: The People list follows the person you are looking at
---

Clicking a name on someone's card in People shows that person. The list used to keep the first person's row lit, and clicking that row did nothing until you chose someone else. Now the lit row is the person shown, no row is lit for someone who is not on your list, and one click on a row brings its person back.
