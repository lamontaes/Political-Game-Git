title: A job offer is a phone call from the person at the employer
---

When an employer picks you, the person there calls: they say who they are, offer the job by its title and its pay by the hour, and you can ask about it, take it, turn it down or ask for time. Taking it hires you; turning it down frees the position; asking for time leaves it open until the offer's deadline, which they tell you.
