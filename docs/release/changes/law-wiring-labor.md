title: Pay and payroll laws reach workers
---

When a minimum wage, a public pay floor, a teacher salary floor, a union law or a payroll tax changes, the game now records which workers, worker groups and employers it reached and how much money moved. The federal minimum wage question now has a starting answer in every place, and unemployment benefits are paid from the state treasury that employers' unemployment tax fills.
