title: Pay and payroll laws reach workers
---

When a minimum wage, a union law or a payroll tax changes, the game now names the workers, worker groups and employers it reached and how much money moved. A minimum wage raise names each worker it raised and the act that set the wage, and the raise-the-minimum-wage question raises pay. A town's low-wage job count now follows its minimum wage. A household's income now counts the unemployment benefit it is owed, so the poverty rate falls while a claim pays.
