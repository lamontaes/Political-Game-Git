title: Laws that raise prices through the wider economy, like tariffs, now show which businesses charged more and by how much
---

When a law raises what businesses charge through the wider economy (tariffs, a bottle deposit), the law's page and dev mode's bill card now name the town's businesses whose prices rose and show what buyers paid for it. Prices themselves are unchanged.
