section: Changed
title: Members sometimes miss a vote they care little about
---

A member of a council, a legislature or Congress now decides whether to attend a vote the way people decide whether to go to work: a vote they care about, they never miss; on one they stand near even on, their own day and temperament decide, and a town member whose day leaves no free evening misses the meeting. Roll calls record who was absent.
