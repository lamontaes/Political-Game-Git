id: session110-protest-records
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A held protest names who came, and the local reporter covers it
---

A protest that is planned and held now records which named residents were
invited, who chose to come (people on shift or with a view against it do not),
and a single turnout count for the public event. The local-government reporter
picks the held protest up as a news lead and can file it, so a town hears about
its own protest. Scheduling the protest from a played action, what officials
decide about it, and its effect on statewide pressure are not built yet.
