title: Volunteers join without stopping you, unless they matter
---

Someone who offers to help your campaign is on it right away, and the Campaigns tab counts them in one place: the header and the Volunteers button now show the same number. A friend's offer in a small local race or your first campaign still plays as a scene where you can say yes or no; a stranger's offer in a big race no longer stops your day.
