section: Fixed
title: Worlds no longer stop when a clemency petition comes due
---

A saved clemency petition schedules its next step on the world's clock, but the clock had no handler for it, so the first petition to come due stopped time. Watched worlds in Louisiana and Indiana ended about 18 months in. The world clock now carries the clemency petition handler.
