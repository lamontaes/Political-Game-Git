title: Midterm standing follows recorded views of the president
---

The national component now follows the change in favorable views of the
sitting president since entry into office. Each recorded adult counts once.
Dated records and their sequence preserve the inauguration baseline. When
no views change, the shift is zero; missing view updates remain a producer
gap rather than a fixed historical penalty.
