title: Minnesota and Missouri starting parks laws retain their revenue shares
---

Minnesota's starting parks law records 14.25% of receipts from its added Legacy
sales and use tax. Missouri's records 50% of receipts from its additional
parks, soil and water sales and use taxes. Both terms use the existing ratio
reader and preserve their source revenue denominators and saved dates.
