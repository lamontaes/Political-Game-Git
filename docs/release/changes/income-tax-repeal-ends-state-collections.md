title: A state that repeals its income tax stops collecting it
---

A state that repealed its income tax kept booking income tax revenue for
years while every paycheck withheld nothing. The budget now collects none
from the tax year the repeal takes effect in, and the next budget expects
none. A state's income tax law now moves the budget from January 1 of the tax
year, the same day paychecks start withholding under it, so a law enacted in
spring changes both on the same day.
