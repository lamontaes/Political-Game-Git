title: The Campaigns screen says where the race stands in plain words
---

After you campaign, the Campaigns screen now says how many voters you reached, how many warmed to you or cooled, who you are running against and what a poll of fifty voters would cost, instead of the line of doors, plus and minus signs and "Retold". The last poll's answers are named by candidate.
