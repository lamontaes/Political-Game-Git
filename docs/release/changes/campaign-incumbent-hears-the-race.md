title: A seated official now hears when you are gaining on them and campaigns harder
---

In a council or other local race, the person you run against used to step up their campaign only when a poll or the warmth of their own friends said so, which in practice never happened. They now read the race from what people have actually told them, the yeses held by people in touch with them since and what residents said at their own doors, and put in more hours and doors when you pull ahead.
