title: Laws and budgets reach the economy
---

A federal or state budget moves jobs, rent help pays landlords, escrow pays property tax on each place's real due dates, and legal cannabis sells at each state's real rate. Towns keep a normal share of unemployed through the year, businesses pay property tax on their premises, and the town bank holds the wider population's savings.
