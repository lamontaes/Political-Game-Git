title: A crime nobody reported now reads as what happened to you
---

When you are assaulted or robbed, or your home is broken into or vandalized,
and nobody reports it, your Journal now says so by name. It
used to read "An assault in Juneau went unreported." That looked like a crime
you could not have heard of. Only the people it happened to see it.
