title: The Journal tells your life in a few paragraphs, each fact once, instead of a line per record.
---

The Journal tells the years before the game in a few short paragraphs: where you were born and who raised you, the family you made, your working life, and the household now. Each move, job and person comes up once. A long run of jobs is told as the kind of work you did, how it went and where it ended, never as a list. Relatives are named by relation and first name once, then by first name, and no paragraph opens on "Eight years on". What you do in play (a filing, a race, a vote, a town hall) follows, one paragraph per event.
