title: Ask somebody out, and become a couple
---

Before, nobody in the game could become a couple. Having a child with a
partner and the scenes about a partner's plans each looked for a partner and
never found one.

Now the People screen lets the player ask an adult they know, who is not
family, out on a date. The other person answers from their own side: whether
they are already with somebody, whether they want people in their life, and
how the two of them already get on. After two dates the player can ask them to
be a couple and hears the answer on the spot. A couple is recorded as a
partnership, so the person card says "your partner" and every system that
asks after a partner now finds one. Either of them can end it.

How many dates come before asking, and what weighs on the answer, are marked
placeholders until ChatGPT answers the two questions filed on how couples
form. Moving in together, marriage and one-night stands come next.
