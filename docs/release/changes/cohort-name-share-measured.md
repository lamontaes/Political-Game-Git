title: Names follow the years people were born
---

New towns name their people more like real life: how many carry one of the most common names for their birth decade now follows the real share for that decade and sex, about 70 percent of men born in the 1950s and about 40 percent of those born in the 2010s. Names in a new game differ from earlier builds.
