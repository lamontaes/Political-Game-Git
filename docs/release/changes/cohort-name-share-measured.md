People in newly generated towns are named from their birth year more like real life: the share who carry a top-100 name for their decade now follows the measured share (about 70 percent of men born in the 1950s, about 40 percent of those born in the 2010s) instead of a flat 60 percent. Names in a new game differ from earlier builds.
