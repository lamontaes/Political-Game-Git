title: The first minutes of a new life say where you are and what your choices do
---

The opening moments at home now say plainly what is going on: fifteen free
minutes are called fifteen free minutes, and somebody you live with is named
with who they are to you. A new moment lets an adult decide what to make time
for — learning something, the people they know, or themselves — and reading
or resting afterwards can keep that plan.

A child's bedtime-shadow and dinner-plate moments now only come up in the
evening, instead of at ten past nine in the morning. Moments you have already
seen keep the words they were shown with.
