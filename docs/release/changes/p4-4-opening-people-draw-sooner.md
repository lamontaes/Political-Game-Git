section: Improved
title: The opening's chambers fill with their people in seconds
---

The people in a picture are drawn sooner, and the screen the player has just
reached is drawn first. On the opening's Congress screen, 51 of the 54 people placed
in the House chamber now appear within 20 seconds; before, 1 did, because
the figures still waiting from the screens before it went first. Each figure takes
about 36% less time to draw, with the same picture as before. The list of
people the picture has no room for is now labeled "Also here", like every
other scene, instead of "More illustrated people".
