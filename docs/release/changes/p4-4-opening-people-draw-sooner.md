id: p4-4-opening-people-draw-sooner
impact: minor
section: Improved
title: The opening's chambers show their people far sooner
---

The people in a picture are drawn sooner, and the people on the screen the
player is looking at are drawn first, before anyone from a screen already
left. On the opening's Congress screen, 51 of the 54 people placed in the House
chamber now appear within 20 seconds; before, at most 1 did, because the
figures still waiting from the screens before it went first. Each figure takes
about 36% less time to compose, with the same picture as before. The list of
people a picture has no room for is now labeled "Also here", like every other
scene, instead of "More illustrated people".
