title: Call donors no longer asks the person running against you for money
---

When you ran for a seat and chose Call donors, the list of people the campaign phoned could include the other candidate on your ballot, and they answered "would rather keep the money for now." Someone running against you is now left off that list, the same way rivals were already left off volunteer, endorsement and donor-meeting asks. The same holds in reverse: a rival's campaign no longer phones you.
