title: Show recorded statements about the player in People
---

The People workspace reads dated word-of-mouth statements about the player
from their own knowledge records. Each entry shows the recorded speaker,
position, and date without exposing private beliefs or an opinion total.
An official who is among a speaker's actual hearers can now hear that statement.
The situation reader supplies present people's saved views as private scene
input. Scene dialogue about those statements still needs English-engine wiring.
