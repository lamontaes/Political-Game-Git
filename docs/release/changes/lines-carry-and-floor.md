title: The ask to carry a bill and the floor debate are back, in new words.
---

A member asks you to carry their bill and says what it does, and you can agree, bargain, wait or say no. On the floor a member speaks for or against a bill, and you can agree, disagree, ask them to yield for a question, or sit down.
