title: Council passage records the executive route
---

Town and compiled council ordinances now share the passage handler. A profile
that requires no presentment records that outcome without inventing a mayor's
signature. A council that requires executive action retains its presentment,
decision and deadline route. An unread presentment rule leaves the ordinance
pending. The recorded no-presentment outcome survives Continue without duplication.
