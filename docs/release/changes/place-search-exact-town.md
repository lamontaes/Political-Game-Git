title: The town you typed is the town you get
---

Searching for a town offered a longer name containing it ahead of the name
you actually typed, so asking for Columbus, Ohio and taking the first result
started your life in Columbus Grove — a village of a couple of thousand
instead of the state capital — and everything that follows from where you
live, your city, your representatives and the offices you could stand for,
quietly belonged to the wrong place for the rest of that life. The town whose
name you typed now comes first.
