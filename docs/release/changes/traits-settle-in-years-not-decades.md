title: A changed trait settles in two to five years
---

When something that happens to a person changes one of their five ordinary traits, the change now becomes established in two to five years rather than eight to fifteen. How hard a trait is to change still depends on how strongly the person holds it. This is a labeled trial of ChatGPT's second settling profile, and the first profile is kept for comparison.
