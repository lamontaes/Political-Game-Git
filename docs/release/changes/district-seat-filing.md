title: Filing for a seat that is filled by district
---

In states where a legislative seat belongs to a numbered district, the list of
offices would say you could run and then refuse to take your name, because
nothing on the screen let you say which district you were running in. Choosing
one of those offices now opens the district seat beside it, showing the
district your character is on record as living in and since when, and your
name goes in for that seat. Where the records do not say which district your
town sits in, the screen says so instead of choosing one for you.
