title: A town's hard times land on women by age (counted women only for now)
---

When jobs dry up in a town, fewer babies are born among the wider population, and the drop now falls hardest on women in their early twenties and not at all on women over forty, as the research finds. Women the game follows closely do not yet get the age part: their choice still rests on their own household's money. Every researched link in the town's web of causes can now land differently on people by income, age, household, tenure, work, sex or region, and build up over months rather than all at once.
