title: A town's hard times land on people by who they are
---

When jobs dry up in a town, fewer babies are born among the townspeople the game counts rather than names, and now the drop falls hardest on women in their early twenties and not at all on women over forty, as the research finds. Named couples still decide from their own jobs and income. Every researched link in the town's web of causes can now land differently on people by income, age, household, tenure, work, sex or region, and build up over months rather than all at once.
