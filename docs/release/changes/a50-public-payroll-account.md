section: Changed
title: Public payroll uses the recorded government's account
---

Town payroll resolves a public employer's saved government identity to the
existing canonical public account. Recorded account cash pays the worker;
the workplace remains the employer and keeps its pay schedule. Private
employers still pay from their own accounts. Public employers without a
recorded government identity cannot silently use an unrelated account.
