title: People sound like themselves, and scenes open with a few sentences
---

A warm person and a cold one now choose different words in the same moment. A scene opens by saying where you are, who is there and what led here. A relative asked about answers in a way that keeps the talk going. A lie about where you live names another place. Nobody says a date out loud. The journal names a newborn ("My daughter Jane was born."). The grading bench (`npm run words:bench`) composes these scenes from one cached world for each personality, and the owner's grades tune the weights the engine reads.
