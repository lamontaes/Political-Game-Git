title: People sound like themselves, and scenes open with a few sentences
---

A warm person and a cold one now choose different words in the same moment. A scene opens by saying where you are, who is there and what led here. A relative asked about answers in a way that keeps the talk going. A lie about where you live names another place. Nobody says a date out loud. The journal names a newborn ("My daughter Jane was born."). The grading bench (`npm run words:bench`) composes these scenes from one cached world for each personality, and the owner's grades tune the weights the engine reads. Scenes with a purpose (a vote asked, a donor's ask, a reporter at the door) are built and shown on the review page; their words are held until the owner grades them, so they do not play yet. The same holds for this change's other new hand-written lines (answers about family, scene openings, journal look-backs): until they are graded, those moments read with mined lines only, and a birth is told with the child's name from a mined sentence.
