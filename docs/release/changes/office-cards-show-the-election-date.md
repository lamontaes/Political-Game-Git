id: office-cards-show-the-election-date
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Every office on the campaign screen shows its election date before you file
---

A state house or senate seat said "Upcoming election timing is not established
in this save" until you filed for it, so a Maine Senate seat hid a date it
shares with the House. Each seat now shows the state's next regular election
before you file, and a town seat says its election is four weeks after filing.
