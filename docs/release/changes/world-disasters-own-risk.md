title: A world's disasters come from its own exposures, not FEMA's real days
---

The country's disasters used to happen on the days FEMA recorded real ones, so every world had the same list. Each state's exposure to flood, storm and wildfire now starts from the world's own seed and grows with its climate, and a disaster happens when an exposure holds one more whole one. Two worlds with the same laws now differ.
