title: A law that raises or lowers the juvenile court age now moves the state's corrections spending
---

Before, a state could raise the age at which teenagers go to juvenile court,
or lower it again, and its budget spent exactly what it had planned.

Now the state's corrections spending changes from the month the law takes
effect and stays changed while the law is in force. Raising the age costs
about $12.58 a resident each year, from New York's Raise the Age
appropriation; lowering it saves the same. The figure is marked as
estimated from New York's.
