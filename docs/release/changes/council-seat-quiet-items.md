title: A council member's voting workflow now covers the quiet items on the agenda
---

A seated council member's own ballot used to be ignored on the council floor,
so a player on a town council or county board was always recorded absent. The
council vote now reads the ballot the member saved. A council seat can also
hold the same voting workflow every other office has. With prior instructions,
a standing instruction that still matches an ordinance's text casts the vote
on any item nothing in the member's life makes matter, before the roll call.
With a review batch the quiet items wait in one list for the member to decide,
and handling each vote individually leaves every item to them. An item the
member never decides is still recorded absent, and the item that matters to
them is never cast for them.
