title: Favors from deals get called in at election time
---

A favor you trade for a vote is remembered. When a colleague who took your favor runs for office, their campaign asks you to endorse them, and a colleague who owes you weighs that debt when your campaign asks for theirs. An endorsement given for the favor repays it. When a colleague asks you to repay, the game waits for your answer instead of giving one for you; that ask doesn't reach you on screen yet.
