title: When someone asks you to carry a bill, you decide
---

When a mayor or other executive asked the member most inclined to carry a bill and that member was you, the game used to decide for you and file the bill before you saw the request. The ask now waits for your answer: agreeing files the bill under your name, and declining files nothing.
