section: Fixed
title: Turning on dev mode's full tracing no longer makes everyone in the world choose every day.
---

With the debug observer on, every full person used to choose what to do every day instead of on their own schedule, so a traced world played differently from the one the player is in (four times as many visits in two months at one place). Now tracing keeps more of the record and leaves each person's schedule as it is. A smaller difference through the relationship record remains and stays open on the item.
