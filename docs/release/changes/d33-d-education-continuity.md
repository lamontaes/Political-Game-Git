---
id: d33-d-education-continuity
impact: minor
section: Improved
title: Keep accepted study terms and arrange tuition without stopping ordinary life
---

Accepted study keeps its price, duration, credential and cash-funding terms even when later offers change. Review an editable, game-authored tuition grace before enrolling. If a period cannot be funded, its disclosed deadline pauses only study; ordinary work and pay continue. Pay from available personal funds to resume, without an automatic loan or a second charge. Personal shows accepted weekly demands and reversible interruption choices. Saved noncredit offers support parallel applications without written assignments.
