title: The home state's legislature is elected again at every regular election
---

The members seated in the home state's legislature when a life began used to
serve until they died, and nobody replaced them. Now every seat is decided at
the state's regular legislative election. Most sitting members win again.
Members who retire or lose are replaced by new members who take their seats
when the new term begins, and a seat left empty by a death is filled at the
next election. The news reports how many members returned and how many seats
changed hands.

For now, who runs again and who wins follows the same game profile Congress
uses. Primaries, campaigns and vote counts for these seats are not modeled
yet.
