title: A player's chosen surname now reaches their parents, children and relatives who live elsewhere.
---

Choosing a name at the start renamed only the household and the brothers and sisters who carried the drawn name; parents and children living elsewhere kept the old one, so a family showed several surnames. Everyone in the player's family facts who carried the drawn family name, or their parents' name, now takes the chosen one.
