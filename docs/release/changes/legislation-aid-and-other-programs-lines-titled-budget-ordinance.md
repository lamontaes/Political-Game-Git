title: Bills that change the aid or other-programs line are named for it
---

A member's bill to raise or cut the local aid line or the other-programs line is now titled for it ("Aid to Other Governments Reduction Ordinance", "Other Programs Funding Increase Ordinance") instead of "Budget Ordinance".
