id: tuition-faith
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Which families send their children to the private school now depends on their faith as well as their money
---

In a town with a private school, a family's faith now counts alongside its money when it picks private school: Catholic families are more drawn to it and families with no faith tie are less drawn, so a well-off family with no tie may stay with the public school while a less well-off Catholic family enrolls. The town's overall private share is unchanged.
