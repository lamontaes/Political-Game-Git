title: A vote you traded in committee comes due on the floor, and the sponsor comes to make sure of it.
---

When a holdout traded their vote in committee for their own section in a bill, the bill's sponsor comes to their office on the day of the floor vote to make sure the vote still holds. The member can confirm it, ask that their section stay in, put the sponsor off, or go back on their word.
