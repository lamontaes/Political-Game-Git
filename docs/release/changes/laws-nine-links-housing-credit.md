title: Zoning and housing laws now name the households and builders they reach
---

When a council lets apartments be built by right, the law now records the apartment buildings it brought and the households who move into them, with the rent they pay for homes the law added. Raising the housing budget pays rent help to the town's lowest-income renters, and its effect on evictions is carried by the rent help itself. Repealing a loan rate cap stops the law from counting interest on loans already written.
