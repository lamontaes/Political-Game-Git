title: Rent, zoning and housing-budget laws now reach tenants through the whole first year
---

Leases that already exist when a game begins now come up for renewal through the first year, not all in the same month, so a rent stabilization law reaches renters from its first months. When a council lets apartments be built by right, the law records the buildings it brought and the households who move into them. A housing budget a council passes keeps paying rent help to the town's lowest-income renters in later years. Repealing a loan rate cap stops the law from counting interest on loans already written.
