title: Businesses pay unemployment and profit taxes, and their books can be read as a record
impact: patch
---

Employers now pay federal and state unemployment insurance tax on each paycheck, each to its own government, and a business owned by another firm pays tax on each quarter's profit at the federal corporate rate. Taxes an employer can't cover are owed and paid first on later paydays. A business's books (cash, sales, costs, wages, profit, taxes paid and owed, its credit line) are kept as a record a screen can show once the owner decides who may see them.
