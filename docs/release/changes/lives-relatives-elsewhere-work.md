title: Relatives who live elsewhere now have working lives, and you hear when one loses a job, finds one or retires.
---

Your relatives in other towns used to have no work at all. Now each one of working age starts out working, looking or out of work in the same shares as real Americans their age, then gets let go, finds work, moves to a better job or retires at real-life rates, sooner or later by their own temperament and their state's job market. When one of them loses a job, starts one or retires, the family in your town hears about it.
