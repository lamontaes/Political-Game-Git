title: A state law on official pay changes the paychecks of its governor, legislators and judges
---

When a state's legislature passes a law that sets its governor's, a
legislator's or a trial court judge's salary, the office holder is paid the new
salary from the first week that begins on or after the law's effective date. A
law can say it reaches only terms that begin after its date, and then a holder
already in office keeps the old pay. Congress is not covered yet. California
legislators are now paid $128,215, the salary in effect since December 4, 2023.
A legislator in a state that pays by the day or the week (Utah, Vermont,
Virginia, New Mexico) is paid an estimate from what the other states pay, not
the flat $60,000 it was before.
