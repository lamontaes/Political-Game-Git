title: Council and meeting screens speak plainly
---

The public-meeting card called each session "Game-authored" and ended with a
reference code and a date the player had no use for. It now names the body and
the kind of meeting, says where it sits (or that the room has not been
announced), and says whether the public may attend and speak. The council panel
no longer talks about a missing "producer" or labels the other councilors'
ballots as game-authored; it lists how they will vote. Tax, transit and
amendment screens lost their "authored" and "not modeled" footnotes, trips say
simply that no fare is charged, and the attendance history gives its dates in
words. The vote records keep their full notes for developers.
