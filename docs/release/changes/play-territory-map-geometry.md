title: Maps for Puerto Rico, Guam, the Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the Marianas
---

A player who starts in Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the Northern Mariana Islands now sees their territory's map, with its municipalities or districts, its places and its legislative districts where the Census publishes them.
