title: Every scene opens by naming the day, the place and the person
---

Every scene now starts with one line that says when, where and who: "First thing on June 7th, you head to the clerk's office and speak to Maria Patton." A call opens with the phone ringing and who is on it, a letter with who it came from, a visit with who came by. The day is said the way people say it, never as a date code.
