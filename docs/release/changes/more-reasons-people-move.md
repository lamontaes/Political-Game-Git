title: People move for more reasons than floods and taxes
---

People in your town now leave for more reasons. Someone who lost a job this
year and hasn't found another is three times as likely to move away. When the
town's unemployment runs above the nation's, more people leave. An unusually
bad quarter of assaults and robberies pushes people out. Someone leaving
leans toward a state where a relative lives, and the move says so.

Moves between states also read a state's unemployment against the nation's
when the economy records that state separately.

Schools, housing costs, climate, politics, age and military service don't move
anyone yet. Every strength is a placeholder until research comes back.
