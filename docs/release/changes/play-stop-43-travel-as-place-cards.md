title: Travel shows pictured place cards that say who is there and why each is listed.
---

Travel is a grid of pictured places. Each card names the place, who is there now and why it is on your list (you work or study there, someone you know works there, you can do something there, or you file there for a named race). Town departments are no longer listed just for being town offices, and filing offices are named without the record's parenthetical.
