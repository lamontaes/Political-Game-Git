title: Who may sign a petition now follows the place's own terms
---

Petition circulation and the clerk's count used one national voting-age check
for every place. A signer must now be an eligible registered voter under the
place's own terms: old enough, alive and living in the area the petition
covers. Maryland lets a 17-year-old sign who will be 18 by the next general
election, so a petition circulated there now reaches them and one anywhere else
does not. When the clerk throws out a signature, the reason names what failed:
too young, no longer living, or not a resident.
