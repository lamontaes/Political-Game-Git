title: More states' counties elect only the officers their law names.
---

An Arizona or West Virginia county no longer elects a coroner; Arizona's law lists none and West Virginia's county commission appoints one. An Alaska borough no longer elects a clerk or treasurer; its chief administrator appoints them. A West Virginia county elects its Clerk of the county commission for six years, a New Hampshire county elects its sheriff, county attorney and treasurer for two, a Massachusetts county elects its sheriff for six, and a Delaware county elects its Clerk of the Peace and sheriff for four. Washington, Oregon, Arizona, West Virginia, Idaho, New Mexico, Nevada, North Dakota, South Dakota, South Carolina, Maryland, Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Wyoming, Massachusetts, Delaware and Alaska county officers now rest on their own statutes or constitutions.
