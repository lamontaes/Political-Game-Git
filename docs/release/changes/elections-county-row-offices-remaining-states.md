title: More states' counties elect only the officers their law names.
---

An Arizona or West Virginia county no longer elects a coroner; Arizona's law lists none and West Virginia's county commission appoints one. A West Virginia county elects its Clerk of the county commission for six years, and a New Hampshire county elects its sheriff, county attorney and treasurer for two. Washington, Oregon, Arizona, West Virginia, Idaho, New Mexico, Nevada, North Dakota, South Dakota, South Carolina, Maryland, Maine and New Hampshire county officers now rest on their own statutes or constitutions.
