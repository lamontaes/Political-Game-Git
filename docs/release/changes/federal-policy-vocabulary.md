title: The federal government has its own policy questions
---

A new world now knows what the federal government is about: twenty fields
and sixty questions, from appropriations and the debt to Medicare, trade,
immigration and federal courts, each with a matching subject a person can
come to know. They sit beside the state and local questions rather than
replacing them, so a federal education grant and a local school budget stay
two different questions. Registering them gives no one knowledge of them,
and no federal positions are authored yet.
