section: Changed
title: A governor dying in office is now tested through a real saved game.
---

A new check starts an ordinary life, lets the sitting governor die, lets the
next race open on the empty seat and then saves and reloads the game. The race
has two challengers, the dead governor is not on the ballot, and reloading
changes nobody's office. The governor election calendar used for most states
has also been sent for research.
