section: Fixed
title: A mayor's or governor's phase-in now reaches the bill the member files
---

A mayor, county executive or governor who drafts a law amount in dated steps or as a percent a year and asks a member to carry it now gets a bill with one section per step and its date, where before the member was asked to carry the amount set to zero.
