title: Background congressional seats follow their saved district views
---

When no played or separately recorded contest decides a congressional seat, its party outcome now follows the world's saved seat lean with a disclosed incumbency game assumption. The former random incumbent-return and party-flip rolls no longer decide those seats. Candidate slates, primaries, money, and person-level campaign quality still need their own records.
