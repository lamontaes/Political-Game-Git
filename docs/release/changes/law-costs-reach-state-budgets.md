title: A privacy law or social-media age check now costs the state to enforce
---

A state that passed a consumer data privacy law or required social media sites
to check ages used to spend nothing on it. Now the law puts enforcement staff
on the state's budget from the day it takes effect, and a repeal takes them
off. The cost comes from enacted bills: Colorado's and Virginia's privacy laws
each cost about $330,000 a year, and Utah's age-check law $220,500, which the
game spreads per resident, about 5 cents a person for privacy and 6 cents for
age checks. A budget with a balanced-budget law makes room by trimming its
other programs.
