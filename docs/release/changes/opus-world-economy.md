title: A living economy around the town
---

The nation's economy and central bank, state and metro economies, jobs that open and close, a town bank that lends, housing with rent, mortgages, home buying and evictions that follow each place's law, and businesses that are founded, grow into chains and pass to heirs now run in the new core. Laws and budgets reach it: a federal or state budget moves jobs, rent help pays landlords, escrow pays property tax on each place's real due dates, and legal cannabis sells at each state's real rate.
