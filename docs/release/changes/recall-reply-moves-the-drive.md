title: What you say to the people petitioning to recall you changes how many sign
---

When residents start a petition to recall you, its organizer now confronts you, and the day stops for the scene. Hearing them out or offering to meet turns some people who would have signed away, and dismissing them turns some toward signing. The count on the petition's due day changes by the people who heard what you said, from the organizer or the people the organizer knows.
