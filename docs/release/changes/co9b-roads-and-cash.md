id: co9b-roads-and-cash
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Every town keeps a public works department, and a county pays for services only with cash in hand
---

Before, a small town could end up with no public works department, so the county's voted roads line had nobody to pay. A county whose account was short of cash still committed its money, and every payment then failed. Now every seated town has a public works department, and a county manager commits a service line only once the county's account holds the cash for it; otherwise the money comes back to the office the next month.
