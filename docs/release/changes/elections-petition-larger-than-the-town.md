title: Small towns no longer ask more signatures than they have voters
---

A town council petition now fits the town. Where the state's law offers a petition only as a way around the filing fee, as in Minnesota's towns, the fee files you and no petition is required. Elsewhere a local petition never asks for more than a tenth of the town's voters, the most any local petition law read for the game asks, so a town of a few dozen adults no longer needs thirty names. Before this, 620 of 31,787 towns asked for more signatures than they had citizen adults.
