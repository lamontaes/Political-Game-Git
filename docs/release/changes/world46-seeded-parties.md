title: Each new world starts differently, and parties can change
---

A new game now starts from its own political and economic conditions, drawn around the real 2024 election results: most worlds look familiar, some differ modestly, and a few differ a lot. The President and Chief Justice now live in Washington instead of your hometown. Parties are no longer fixed at two: local party chapters have standing committees, and people who keep losing the same argument can found a new party or split away. Parties can also combine, rename or dissolve, and older records keep the party labels they had at the time. Replay links made before this update rebuild exactly the world they described.
