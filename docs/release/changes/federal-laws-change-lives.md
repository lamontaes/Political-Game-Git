title: Some laws Congress passes now change life in every state
---

When Congress raises the Social Security retirement age, more older people
in every state fall into poverty over the next five years, as benefits
shrink for each new group of retirees. Other federal laws now have a known
effect ready for the day the game tracks what they touch: negotiated
Medicare drug prices lower what retirees pay at the pharmacy, a cap on loan
interest cuts payday borrowing where a state has no cap of its own, housing
vouchers for every family that qualifies cut homelessness, higher tariffs
raise prices, and a later retirement age keeps more people in their sixties
at work.

Some federal laws that people expect to matter change nothing here, because
careful studies found no effect: admitting more immigrants does not raise
crime, shorter federal drug sentences do not raise crime, housing vouchers
do not change whether children finish high school or get arrested, and
neither a higher top income tax rate nor forgiving student loans changes the
poverty rate.
