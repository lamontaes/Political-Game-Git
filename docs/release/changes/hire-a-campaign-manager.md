title: Hire a campaign manager with real experience
---

When your campaign can afford a salary through election day, people you know,
and people the party chapter organizer knows, who have worked on campaigns
before can be offered as your manager. Once hired, the manager draws up your
weekly plan from their own experience and temperament, and the salary comes
out of the campaign treasury.
