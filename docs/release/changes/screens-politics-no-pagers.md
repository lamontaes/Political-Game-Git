title: The Politics menus show everything at once
---

Government, Parties and Budgets have no Back or Continue inside them: every body and every member sits on the screen at once, side by side, on each level. A county's one-seat offices share one section named for the county, each row naming the office; a legislature of dozens of seats is a grid of headshots, each opening the member's card.
