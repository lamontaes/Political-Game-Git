title: Elections in skipped years have candidates and winners
---

Skipping years with dev mode's Skip control no longer leaves town, county and legislative races with nobody on the ballot: people weigh running and file on the days they would in play, and each race is counted from the voters' views with no campaign days played, so every seat has a winner and a term when play resumes.
