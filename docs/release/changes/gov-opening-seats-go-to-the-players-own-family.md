title: A new life no longer opens with your own family holding the town's elected seats.
---

The town's council and county board seats at the start of a new life went mostly to your parents, brothers and sisters, because the game only looked at the few people it tracks one by one. Now the seats go to the best suited adults over the whole town, and someone from the wider population who wins a seat becomes a full person with their real job and household.
