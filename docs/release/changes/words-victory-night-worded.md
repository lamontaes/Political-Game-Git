title: The night you win plays.
---

After you win a race, the talk on victory night now plays: someone from your campaign asks what you'll say about the runner-up ("Then tell me what you're going to say about Sharon Cruz. Ninety seconds, tops."), or, unopposed, what you want to say first. Before, it could never be worded and was skipped.
