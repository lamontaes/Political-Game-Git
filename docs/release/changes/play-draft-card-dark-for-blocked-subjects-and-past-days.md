title: Draft stays dark for a subject your body cannot write on and until every day picked is after today
---

On the draft card, Draft no longer lights up for a subject the body may not write on after the percent-a-year panel closes, and a first increase or dated step on a past day keeps Draft dark with a line saying to pick a later day, where before pressing it did nothing.
