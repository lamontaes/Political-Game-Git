title: News about people close to you now actually reaches you
---

When a friend moves away, a neighbor has a baby, or somebody in your household
loses a job, the people tied to them by a household, family or close friendship
now hear of it, and it shows under "Around you this past year". People with no
tie to them hear nothing.
