id: conversation-choices-name-something-real
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Conversation choices name something real, and leaving is the screen's own control
---

Talking with someone no longer offers "Talk about what is happening here", "Ask if you can tell them something", "Say it can wait", "Let them know you heard" or "Say goodbye". What the player could tell is offered directly, each choice naming what it is, and the other person's own records decide whether they listen. While someone's invitation is open, the only choices are to agree or decline. Someone who turns down an invitation because they want privacy, asked why, says they are not ready to talk about it, even after that need has passed. The player leaves with the conversation box's own control.
