title: Storms and floods happen on their own, and starting politics fit their own evidence
---

Floods, flash floods and severe thunderstorms now occur by themselves where you live, drawn from twenty-five years of recorded storm reports for your state, and they run through the existing damage, request, declaration and repair chain. A place with nothing to damage gets nothing. When someone dies, each relative learns of it once, and a private cause stays private unless it was actually shared with that person. Starting politics now use each office's own evidence: a seat with no real two-party contest keeps the party its record shows instead of borrowing the state's presidential result, and governors keep theirs.
