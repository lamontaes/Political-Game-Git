title: Colleagues can tell you what you want to hear.
---

When you ask a colleague for their vote the night before a floor vote, someone whose answer is really no may tell you yes instead, if the no would cost them with you and their temperament lets them. Nothing on screen says it was a lie. You find out the way you would in life: the roll call shows how they really voted, you learn it from the record, and your tie with them takes the hit.
