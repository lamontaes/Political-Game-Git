id: routine-activity-leaves-scene-stream
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Routine activities no longer interrupt the life story
---

Reading, drawing and resting no longer arrive as daily life-story scenes.
The story can still continue a scene that was already open in an older save.
No separate leisure button is added to the opening panel.
The life story no longer offers separate time-pass buttons. Day, Week and
Calendar remain the routes for moving time forward.
