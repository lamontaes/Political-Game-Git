---
id: unanswered-decisions
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Choices can remain unanswered when reasons do not select an option
---

When constraints leave exactly one available option, that option is selected.
With two or more available options, a person with no formed answer or equally
supported alternatives keeps the choice pending unless their last recorded
answer still applies. Decision
results retain their saved reasons without random boosts or alphabetical
selection. Saved unanswered choices remain unanswered after reload.
