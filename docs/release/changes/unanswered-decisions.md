id: unanswered-decisions
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Choices can remain unanswered when reasons do not select an option
---

When constraints leave exactly one available option, that option is selected.
With two or more available options, a person with no formed answer or equally
supported alternatives first uses their last eligible recorded answer. If that
does not separate the choice, exact means from comparable people in the current
game may separate it. Estimates save their actual donor traces, dates, cutoffs,
counts and spread. Equal estimates keep the choice unanswered. Decision results
retain saved reasons without random boosts, rounded weights or alphabetical
selection, including after reload.
