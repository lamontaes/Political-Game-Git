title: A donor who stops you names what they gave, the bill they want and why, from their own work
---

When someone who gave to your campaign comes to ask for something, they say how much they gave, which bill they want and which way, and why it matters where they work. Your replies each name something different: the bill, the question, the donor, their workplace or the town. The same ask reads differently for every donor and every town.
