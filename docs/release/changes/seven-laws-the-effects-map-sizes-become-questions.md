title: Seven more laws can be passed, from stand your ground to a clean-air plan
---

Seven laws that research had already sized, and that nobody could pass because no question asked about them, are now real questions in every state: stand your ground, responsibility for unsecured guns near children, a handgun buying age of 21, same-day voter registration, ballots mailed to every voter, local police partnering with federal immigration enforcement (287(g)), and a clean-air plan for counties that fail the federal soot and dust standards. Each state, D.C. and territory starts with its real 2026 law on each, from Giffords Law Center, NCSL, ICE, news reports of 2026 state laws and the EPA's list of failing counties. A state that adopts stand your ground now sees firearm homicide rise about 8%, a state that mails every voter a ballot sees turnout rise about 3.5%, and a state that adopts a clean-air plan sees its air pollution fall about 2% a year later. The other four are asked and recorded, and their effects switch on as soon as the game measures what they change: young people's voting, youth gun deaths, school enrollment and immigrant employment.
