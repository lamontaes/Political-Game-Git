title: A place with no government of its own is served by its town, consolidated government or state
---

A census-designated place whose county area has no county government now
keeps the budget of the government that really serves it. In Connecticut,
Massachusetts and Rhode Island that is the New England town holding most of
its residents; around Honolulu, Baton Rouge, Lafayette, Houma, Louisville,
Nashville and Athens it is the consolidated city-county government; in
Alaska's unorganized borough it is the state. Before, 490 such places kept no
local budget at all, and their police were funded by no one.
