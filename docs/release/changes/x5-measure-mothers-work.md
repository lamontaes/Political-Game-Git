title: Each state now tracks how many mothers of young children work, and pre-K and paid leave move it
---

Every state, D.C. and Puerto Rico now keeps the share of mothers of children under 6
who are working or looking for work, starting from the 2024 Census survey. Universal
pre-K raises it about 15% a year after the program starts. Paid family leave raises it
about 2%, and repealing a leave program a state began with lowers it by the same.
