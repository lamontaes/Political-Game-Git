title: A paycheck owes the taxes that apply where you live
---

Take a shift at the shop in Ely, Nevada, and $5.50 of the $72.00 is withheld:
$4.46 for Social Security and $1.04 for Medicare. Nevada has no income tax, so
the game records a lawful $0 for it. The employer owes its own matching share.
Each tax records what was owed, withheld and still unpaid, and anything
unpaid stays owed in the save.

Where the research cannot price a tax, the game marks it unknown rather than
guessing: federal income tax withholding, state income tax
in the 40 states and D.C. that have one, and city and county wage taxes.
