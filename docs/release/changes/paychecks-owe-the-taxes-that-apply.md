title: A paycheck owes the taxes that apply where you live
---

Take a shift at the shop in Ely, Nevada, and $5.50 of the $72.00 is withheld:
$4.46 for Social Security and $1.04 for Medicare. Nevada takes nothing,
because it has no income tax, and the game records that as a lawful $0. The
employer owes its own matching share.

Each tax on a paycheck is its own record: what was owed, what was withheld,
and what is still unpaid. An unpaid amount stays owed in the save.

## What stays unknown

Where the research cannot price a tax, the game marks it unknown rather than
guessing or charging nothing. Federal income tax withholding is unknown for
now. So are state income taxes in the 40 states and D.C. that have one, and
city and county wage taxes. Federal payroll taxes in the territories, for
government employers and for household employers are unknown as well.
