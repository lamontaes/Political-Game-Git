title: You can buy a news outlet whose owner is selling
---

The press desk now shows, for each outlet whose owner will sell, what it would
cost. If your savings cover the price, you can buy it: the money goes to the
seller and the outlet is yours. If you cannot pay, the desk tells you why.

An outlet you own is not for sale to anyone else. Prices are set by outlet size
for now and are not based on the outlet's finances.
