title: A vice president named to a vacancy is always eligible to be president
---

When a president's nominee filled a vacant vice presidency, the nominee's birthplace was drawn like any official's and could come out foreign-born, which would have barred them from the presidency. Nominees now come from natural-born citizens, as the law requires.
