title: An officeholder can repeal a law from its own page, and the page names who can where they cannot
---

Every law's page in Issues now shows a Repeal action to an officeholder whose body can change that law: pressing it drafts the repeal bill through the seat and opens its paper. Where the player's bodies lack the power, the page names the bodies that hold it instead, and a resident with no office sees no action.
