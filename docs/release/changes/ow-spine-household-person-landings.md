title: Household outcome estimates reach named residents
---

Household outcome estimates now reach residents represented by the recorded household, SNAP, wage, and school-completion records, with retirement-age estimates routed to older adults. Each personal landing retains the measure's source and can feed an official-view reflection; place estimates remain estimates rather than personal poverty or benefit findings.
