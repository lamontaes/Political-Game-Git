title: A new life opens with what put you where you are
---

When the world introduction closes, a new life no longer drops you into a room with nothing said. The first moment opens over the room and says, from your records, how old you are, where you live and with whom, where you work, and what has you where you are now, such as your scheduled shift. Return to the room closes it.
