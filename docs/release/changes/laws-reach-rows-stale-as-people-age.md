title: A law aimed at an age group now reaches people when they turn that age and stops when they age out.
---

A law that covers, say, teens from 13 to 17 used to cover only the people who were that age the day it took effect. Now a person gains it on the birthday they turn 13 and loses it on the birthday they turn 18.
