section: Improved
title: Today and Work are now two clear places instead of one crowded pile.
---

Today now answers four things and nothing else: what is happening, what is
next on your calendar, what is waiting on you, and what you can do with the
time. It links to Work, the calendar and places instead of carrying their
panels. Work is one page for every kind of life — your office if you hold one,
running for office, jobs and study, and hiring — and starts by saying what you
do now, with a short list to jump to each part. The menu is grouped, and the
duplicate "Life scenes" entry is gone: the scene is the room, and closing any
page takes you back to it.
