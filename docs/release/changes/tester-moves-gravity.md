## Fixed

### People move to where the survey finds newcomers

Where people move to between states now follows the American Community Survey's arrivals and departures for each state, through the same pulls as before. A small, dense place such as Washington, D.C. no longer loses a few percent of its residents a year (it fell 7.1 percent; it now changes by under 1 percent), and a small territory such as American Samoa no longer drains away.
