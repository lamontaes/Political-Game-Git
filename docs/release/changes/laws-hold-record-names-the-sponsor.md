title: A bill held in committee names its sponsor, reaches the paper, and can be heard after the chair agrees
---

When a committee chair holds a bill without a hearing, the record now names the bill's sponsor, so the sponsor meets the hold as a moment, and the paper can say the bill died without a hearing. If the chair agrees in the scene where the sponsor asks, the bill goes back before the committee and is heard.
