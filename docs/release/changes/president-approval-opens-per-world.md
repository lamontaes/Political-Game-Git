title: The President's approval opens at its own level in each world
---

The President's approval no longer opens at 41% in every world. Each world opens at its own level inside the estimate's range of 36 to 45%, fixed by the world's seed, and dev mode's "How this world opened" shows the level, the published 41% and where it stands now.
