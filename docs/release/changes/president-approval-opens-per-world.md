title: The President's approval starts at the share of the vote that elected them
---

The President's approval no longer opens at a fixed 41% in every world. On day one, which is Inauguration Day, it is the share of the vote the 2024 race recorded for the new President, and each month after moves it by the economy and what happens. Dev mode's "How this world opened" shows the race it came from.
