title: Gun laws change how many people die of firearm homicide, and violent crime no longer feeds on itself
---

When a state adopts stand-your-ground or requires a permit to carry a concealed gun, the town's firearm homicides follow. The people you meet and the wider population both die of firearm homicide more or less often, at every age, as the research on those laws finds, and the cause recorded for each death follows.

A town's robberies and assaults no longer climb month after month on their own. They used to be scaled by an index made of themselves, so any rise counted twice and grew without end; burglary and vandalism still rise with the town's violent crime.
