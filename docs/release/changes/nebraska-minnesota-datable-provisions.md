title: Nebraskans and Minnesotans can stand for their own seats on an ordinary start
---

A life beginning in Nebraska or Minnesota on the ordinary January 2026 start
could not stand for its own legislature. The provisions behind those seats had
been read from the states' own publishers in September 2026, and a rule read
then was treated as unproven for any earlier date — so an ordinary life, which
begins before the reading, fell into the gap and was refused.

Each Nebraska section's page carries its amendment history, and Minnesota's
carries the date its constitution was last generally revised. Those lines are
in the pages the game already keeps, so the current words are now dated to when
they took effect — all well before 2026 — instead of only to the day they were
read. The seats open. A life reaching a seat before those words took effect is
still told the rule is unknown rather than having a later rule applied to it.
