section: Changed
title: Speeches no longer use British phrasing such as "look after", "sorted out" or "quarrel".
---

Thirty-eight speech lines now use the phrases an American says ("take care of", "fixed", "fight", "thanks to", "I'm counting on Congress"), and a governor's or president's greeting thanks the people named instead of greeting them.
