title: A housemate who is home when your life begins is in the room with you
---

Before, a new life that opened at home put only you in the room, so nobody at
home was there to talk to even when a housemate was home.

Now the household members the world has at home at that moment are in the room
too. A housemate on shift or at an activity is not, and a child in class on a
school day is not. If everyone else is out, the room stays empty, which is the
truth at that hour.
