section: Changed
title: A seat no longer makes every counted adult it could draw into a full person
---

When a seat weighs who runs, it now reads the counted adults it draws one at a time, strongest first, and makes a person full only while the field still has room for them, instead of making all of them full on the spot. The world keeps the same people running, with far fewer people made full, so a year runs faster and uses less memory.
