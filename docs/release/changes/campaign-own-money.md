section: Added
title: Candidates can put their own money into their campaign
---

The campaign screen now offers to put $500, $1,000 or $5,000 of your own
money into your committee, up to what you actually have. The committee
receives it the same day, and the contribution is on the public record. If
the game is not tracking your money yet, the screen says so instead of
showing $0. There is no limit beyond what you have for now; the real rules
on a candidate's own money, loans, party money and public financing are
waiting on research, and those other sources are not offered yet.
