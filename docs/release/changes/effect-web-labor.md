title: Pay and who is at work follow the town's policies
---

A town's pay and who is at work now follow the policies the research links to them. A corporate tax cut or rise passes into workers' pay, hitting women and the young hardest. Mothers of young children, married women and people nearing retirement are more or less likely to stay in or return to work as childcare, broadband and retirement-age laws change. How far these shifts move who is at work is not yet fitted to the research, so they are smaller than the studies find.
