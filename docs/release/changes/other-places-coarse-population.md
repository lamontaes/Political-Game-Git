section: Changed
title: People you meet on a trip to another town now have an age, job and wage that town's workforce shows
---

A person a president or member of Congress meets on a trip to another town is now drawn from that town's own workforce: an age from its county's employed by age, a job from its industry mix, and the wage that job pays there.
