title: Virginia, Tennessee and Texas county officers carry the titles and terms their law gives.
---

A Virginia county now elects its Attorney for the Commonwealth, Clerk of the circuit court (for eight years), treasurer and Commissioner of the revenue, as its constitution says, instead of the national guess. Tennessee and Texas county officers keep the offices they had and now carry the four-year terms read from their constitutions.
