title: An eighth grader who moved to town after school started does a little worse in math
---

A student whose family moved into town after the school year began is a little less likely to pass the eighth grade math test, by the size research measured for a school switch (about 0.03 standard deviations). The student's own result record names the move it stands on. Nothing changes for students who did not move.
