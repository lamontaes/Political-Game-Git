title: People on your campaign talk to you like people who know you
---

Someone who has been working your campaign is no longer met as a stranger: no "You've been running into them around town" scene, and nobody on your campaign asks what you do or where you are from. Replies in a conversation are each a different answer; a second wording of the same answer is no longer offered as a fourth choice.
