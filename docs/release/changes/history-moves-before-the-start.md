title: When people came to town is now part of their history
---

Someone whose family lives in another part of the state now has a record of
the day they came to town, set by how often people their age move, and held
back while they had children at home. Their Journal tells it ("I moved to
Burket, Indiana."), the schools their children went to before the move were
where the family lived then, and the jobs they held before it were there too.
