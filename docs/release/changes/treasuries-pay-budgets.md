title: Government treasuries pay out their adopted budgets
---

Each month every federal, state, county and town treasury pays each line of its adopted budget: its staff, benefits a law pays, aid to the governments below it and the town's businesses that do its work first, the rest to the world outside the town. A cut to the federal Social Security line lowers the next check of a retiree who claimed during play. Counted workers now have taxes withheld, a staffer is hired only when the budget line has room, and poll costs are filed as polls.
