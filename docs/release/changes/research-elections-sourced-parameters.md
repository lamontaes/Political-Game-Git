title: More places now follow their own election law for filing and ballot order.
---

Town races in Guam and Puerto Rico now close filing on the date each place's election law sets instead of an estimate, and New York's party date is read from its law (independents' later window is not yet wired, so New York stays an estimate). Candidates for the U.S. House no longer have to be registered voters to file, as the Constitution asks only age, citizenship and residence. In the Virgin Islands and the Northern Mariana Islands, candidates' places on the ballot are now drawn by lot, as their election offices do. Vermont's legislature takes candidates from age 18.
