title: Grown children decide for themselves when to leave home
---

A grown child living with a parent now decides whether to move into a place
of their own, weighing their age, their own pay against the town's rent for
one, a partner of their own and their taste for risk. One who moves out
starts a new household in town, which then finds a home like any other.

A household someone formed in the last year is now also a reason they might
leave town: moving out of a parent's home, moving in with a partner or moving
out after a breakup. It is a small push that tips only somebody already close
to leaving.
