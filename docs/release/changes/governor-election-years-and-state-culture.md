title: Governors are elected in their state's own election years
---

Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, New Jersey and Virginia now elect their
governors in odd-numbered years, and Delaware, Indiana, Missouri, Montana,
North Carolina, North Dakota, Utah and West Virginia elect in presidential
years. Before this, every one of them held its governor's race in 2026. The
years come from a nationwide calendar with each state's constitutional
provision. The dates a term begins stay the game's own until they are
verified.

Not modelled, and marked so in the code: Louisiana's October primary. Its
governor is elected on the November general election day of its odd years.

A governor is now term-limited only where the state's law says so. Before
this, a state whose law the game had not read got a limit drawn from the
states it had read; now it has none until its law is read.

Every state, the District and the five inhabited territories now has a
place for its political culture, read when a person forms a view on a
question. None carries a value yet; each waits on research, and until it
arrives a place pulls nobody's views any way.
