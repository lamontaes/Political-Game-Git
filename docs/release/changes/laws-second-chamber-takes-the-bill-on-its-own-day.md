title: A bill that passes one chamber now waits for the other chamber's own day
---

In a legislature with two chambers, a bill used to go through both chambers, and often the governor's desk, on the same day. Now a bill that passes the first chamber is sent to the other chamber and waits for that chamber's next sitting, usually a week later. The other chamber's committee meets on it first, then its floor votes on its own day, and only then does the bill go to the governor or the President. The bill card shows the chamber it is waiting in. A bill still waiting in the other chamber when the session ends dies there.
