title: Someone who offers you money for a town contract now says which contract they want.
---

When a business owner offers you money to win a town contract, they now name it ("the road contract", "the school contract") before they talk about money, and they bring it up again if they press you after you say no. The sum is kept in plain words ("about five hundred dollars") for when they say how much.
