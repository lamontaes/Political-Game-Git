---
id: household-money-simple
section: Changed
title: A household's money is pay in, rent or mortgage out
impact: patch
---

A household's money is now simple. Pay comes in, the rent or mortgage is set aside first, and one monthly amount for everyday living goes to the town's stores. Sales tax is still collected on that amount at the town's rates. A household that can't make rent still falls behind and can be evicted under its state's law.
