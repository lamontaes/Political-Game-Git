---
id: world-homelessness
section: Added
title: Renters and owners can lose their homes, and where they go next depends on their family, their money and the town's shelter beds
impact: minor
---

Every renter now owes rent, including households with no income, and can be evicted under the state's law. An owner who stops paying the mortgage goes through the state's foreclosure steps, and the bank carries any loss from the sale. A household that loses its home moves in with a relative who has room. If no relative can take it in, it gets a shelter bed while the town has one, and if not, it ends up on the street. It rents again once its pay and savings allow. Every person has a housing status (housed, doubled up, sheltered or unsheltered) that other parts of the game can read.
