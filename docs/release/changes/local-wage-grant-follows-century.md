title: Whether your town may set its own minimum wage now follows the law your world's history left.
---

A state's power to let its towns set their own minimum wage now comes from the answer its legislature left since 1925, the same one the law screen shows. Before, a council's wage bill and a city's own wage still followed today's real law even where your world's state never granted that power, and a state bill taking the power back changed nothing.
