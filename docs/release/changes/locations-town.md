title: Each business and public office in town has its own place.
---

Shops, restaurants, clinics, nursing homes, utilities, banks, post offices, state offices and farms now each get a fitting picture of their own, chosen per business so two shops in one town look different: a general store, a hardware store, a gas station or a grocery; a diner, a lunch counter, a coffee shop or a tavern; a doctor's exam room or a clinic; a power plant, a water plant, a solar or wind farm; the workforce center, the unemployment office or the benefits office.
