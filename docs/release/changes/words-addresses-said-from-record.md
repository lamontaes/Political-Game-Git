section: Changed
title: An address says the same words wherever you meet it, and dev mode shows why each line was said
---

The President's, a governor's and a town's addresses are now kept as what was said, line by line, and the address on the screen, the line people quote back to you and the paper's story all come from that one record, so the words never change after the speech is given. A "Because I believe ..." line keeps its comma. In dev mode, the Calendar's "Why each line of an address" shows every sentence of the recent addresses with the bank pieces it was built from, what each piece rests on (the speaker's view, the place, the law in force or a record), the facts it said and the records behind them.
