title: Things you have not got round to now say how long they have sat
---

The household errands and a posted meeting you have not decided about used to
read word for word the same on your first day and three months later. If you let
a season go by without doing them, the game still described your life as though
it were the first morning of it.

They now say how long they have been standing, once they have stood a week or
more. Nothing is added in the first few days, because a thing written on Monday
and still there on Wednesday is not a fact about your life. Nothing expires and
nothing is done on your behalf; whether an errand nobody ever does should
eventually lapse is a separate question.
