title: A bill drafted in a state legislature or Congress after its session opened now reaches the floor
---

A legislature's weekly floor took up only the bills filed on the session's first day, so a bill drafted later, including the player's, sat drafted until adjournment. The floor now takes up every bill in its chambers after the leader's filings, oldest first, and each one gets a floor date and a whip count the day before its vote.
