title: Bills in six researched states can reach the floor
---

In Minnesota, Illinois, Maryland, Missouri, Nevada and Ohio, no bill ever
became law. The game has read these legislatures' constitutions but not their
committee lists, and a bill that cannot be sent to a committee never moves.
Until those lists are read, each chamber in these states has one standing
committee, which hears every bill and reports it on a majority vote. This is
the game's own stand-in, not the state's rules, and the real committees will
replace it once they are read.
