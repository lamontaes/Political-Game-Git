A new life now opens with the year: who is President, how Congress divides,
how many people looking for work cannot find it, how fast prices are rising,
and the day's headlines. The White House follows, set in the Oval Office.

After your state comes its legislature, with each chamber by party and the
two members who represent your home. Your town's screen names its mayor where
one is in office, and the proposal people there are weighing. Before your life
so far, a new screen names the parents or guardians who raised you and what
they do for work.

On the home screen, a saved character now stands posed for their role, and a
judge wears the robe.
