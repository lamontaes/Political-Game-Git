title: A district map drawn under the commission law now reaches the members it pairs.
---

When a town's independent district commission law is in force and the map it
draws puts two sitting council members in one district, each of those members
now carries a recorded exposure to the law in their history. It records that
their eligibility to seek another term changed; it does not decide who runs or
wins. The relationship-record cost with people left outside the new lines is
not built yet.
