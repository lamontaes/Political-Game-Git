title: State amendments can now take up policy and town recall
---

A state's constitution can now be amended on more than the governor's term
limit. An amendment can write a policy into the constitution or take it out,
the way Prohibition was adopted and later repealed. It can use any policy
that states decide, such as legalizing cannabis sales or restricting
abortion. An amendment or an ordinary law can also change whether a state's
towns may recall their officials. If recall is taken away, a town's Recall
section says which law did it.

In a world you watch, states now and then propose one of these on their own.
Each goes to the legislature and then to the voters. The world record marks
it "No recorded cause", because the game does not yet know why such
amendments come up. A policy written into a constitution is recorded but
does not yet change anything else in the game. What it should do is part of
the research this depends on.
