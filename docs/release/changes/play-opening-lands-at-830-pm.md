title: The opening lands you at home with a line that names the day
---

After the President's address, whether you watch it to the end or skip it, the narrator lands you at home with one line naming the day, such as "It's the evening of January 20. You stay in." You get home when your household's last shift that day ends, and never before the President's address airs: nine o'clock Eastern, so eight o'clock in Texas, nine in West Virginia and seven in Montana.
