title: The opening lands you at home with a line that names the day
---

After the President's address, whether you watch it to the end or skip it, the narrator lands you at home with one line naming the day, such as "Toward evening on January 20th, the house is quiet." You get home when your household's last shift that day ends, and at the evening news hour if no one in your household works that day.
