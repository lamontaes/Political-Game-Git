title: A governor can pay themselves from public money, and staff may find it
---

On the office desk, a funded appropriation now has a choice, "Use this
money for something else". It says plainly that paying yourself from it for
something it does not cover is spending public money outside its purpose.
The money really leaves the state's account and lands in yours, and the
desk lists each payment afterward.

A month later, somebody who works in your office reviews the account. They
decide for themselves whether to say nothing or ask you about it. Someone who
already asked once and then sees it happen again may file a complaint. The
review that follows issues a public report, and the press can pick it up. If
nobody works for you, nobody finds it.

Who really investigates this in each state has not been researched yet, so
for now a governor's case goes to a simulated review that can only issue a
report. The research question has been filed. A legislator's case goes to
their state's ethics body where the game has one. When a real ruling finds
against somebody, the public money is ordered repaid to the state, and the
case may go to prosecutors, as with campaign money.
