title: Police, fire, health and other budget lines now buy from the town's own businesses
---

A town's or county's police, fire, health, corrections and natural resources spending now goes in part to the town's repair shops, stores, clinics, care homes, kitchens and builders, through the same contract awards highways and parks already used. Pool item: budgets-lines-buy-from-town-firms.
