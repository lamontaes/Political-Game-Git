title: Lines you liked are back
---

Seven lines you graded good, deleted when their old banks were rewritten, are back where the game can say them. A new child in the journal can read "My daughter was born." again. A hearing can open with the chair's "All right." or "Good afternoon." and the speaker can close with "Thank you, Chair." or "Thank you all." A speech can open with "Thank you all for being here." and the State of the Union with "Thank you very much." The rest of your good-graded lines with no scene yet to say them are listed, with the reason for each, so a rewrite can no longer delete one without a test failing.
