title: Lines you liked are back
---

Every line you graded good that a rewrite had deleted is back in the game's words. After a loss, the talk at home can have you say "We didn't win.", "This is not the end.", "We will be back." or "Thank you for everything." On election night, "I'm honored." is one way to answer what you will say first. On the floor, a member can rise "to speak briefly in gratitude today" or reply "Chair, reserving the right to object." A hearing can open with "All right." or "Good afternoon." and a speaker can close with "Thank you, Chair." or "Thank you all." A speech can open with "Thank you all for being here.", the State of the Union with "Thank you very much.", and a new child in the journal reads "My daughter was born." again. The lines no scene says yet (the chair's "The motion carries.", bill titles, meeting minutes, the election notice and a witness's "Yes, thank you.") are kept in banks of their own, each with a task for the scene that will say it.
