title: Cannabis tax budgets use recorded receipts
---

Legalization alone no longer creates tax revenue or a revenue-loss charge from population. A saved retail purchase records the buyer's payment and the seller's cash receipt through the existing financial writers. Its actual paid amount can supply the saved typed levy's taxable base; an asking price or unpaid flow cannot. The government's budget reads only the tax actually collected. Paid receipts retain their sale, law and assessment references through Save/Continue.
