impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Taking a job now introduces you to your coworkers, including the ones the town only counted before
---

When you take a new job, you now come to know the people you work beside on your first day, as many as a workplace that size lets one know. Before, you met only coworkers who were already full people in the game, so a small shop could leave your People list empty. Now the coworkers the business's books counted are filled in as the same workers they were, with the same job and pay, and show on People as people you know from working together.
