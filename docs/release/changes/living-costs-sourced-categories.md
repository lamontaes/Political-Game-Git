The separate nonhousing charge uses representative 2024 BLS national spending
on food, transportation, health care, apparel and miscellaneous expenses.
The selected annual consumer-unit basket is divided by the table-derived adult
average and twelve months, with one final cent rounding. Saved provenance
marks this as an average estimate, not an observed person's bill.

Actual rent and mortgage writers remain separate. Opening creates no payment
and does not reprice an existing flow. Settlement changes old estimates from
the current date; earlier saved terms and payments retain their amounts.
Medical and vehicle-purchase overlap needs an explicit category-binding review
before this numeric mechanism is admitted.
