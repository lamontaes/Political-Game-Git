The story director's pacing and tie numbers (the line where a moment takes over the screen, the weight of one's own death, the standing family ties, the anniversary fade, how long a person keeps every moment in full, how long a recorded event stays something to talk about, and the days between scenes from circumstance) were listed as open stand-ins. They are kept as tuned settings in the director's parameter table, each with its range, and the stand-in rows are closed. Nothing changes on screen.
