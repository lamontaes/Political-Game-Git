title: A filed candidate's season brings people to the door and the phone
---

Once you file, the race starts coming to you. Within days someone at home tells you who filed against you. Friends who hear you're running come by to offer a hand, one at a time as each of them learns of it, and a yes puts them on your campaign. When an opponent's mail or ad reaches someone you know, they pass it on. In the last weeks, the town's papers and stations call for an interview, once a week for all who asked, and the debate's host calls about the race's debate. Say yes and you sit for it, or take the stage, on the positions you filed; say no and they go on without you. Scene narration no longer opens by telling you the day or that you're at home, since the screen already shows both.
