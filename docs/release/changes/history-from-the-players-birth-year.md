title: Your town's history now reaches back to the year you were born
---

A new life's history before the start now runs from the year you were born,
not from a fixed year: the economy, laws, businesses, offices and the working
lives of the people around you are run through every year of your lifetime, so
a 35-year-old's first job after school is on the record, not only their recent
years.
