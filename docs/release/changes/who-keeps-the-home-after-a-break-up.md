title: After a break-up, who keeps the home follows the family, not a coin toss
---

When a couple in town breaks up while living together, the partner who parents
more of the children living there keeps the home, and the other moves out.
With no children to tell them apart, the one who lived there first stays,
and after that the older of the two. When a couple moves in together from
homes of the same size, the one who has lived in their home longer keeps it.
Both choices used to be a coin toss.
