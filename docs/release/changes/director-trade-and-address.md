title: A refused trade answers the trade, and job calls name the caller
---

A colleague who will not trade votes now says so ("I don't trade my vote") instead of "That's not mine to give." The President's address to the Vice President no longer promises a reason it never gives. A job-offer call names the caller the whole way through instead of "Someone is calling".
