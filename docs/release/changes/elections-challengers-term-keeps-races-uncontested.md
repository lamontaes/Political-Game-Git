title: More local races have a challenger
---

Town and county seats are contested more often, closer to real local elections: people in the wider population now weigh running by the same standing and the same reaction to the incumbent's votes as the people you know, and an incumbent who has voted against what a neighbor thinks draws a challenge more readily.
