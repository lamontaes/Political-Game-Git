title: Win a governor's race in any state and actually govern, while Congress and statehouses keep turning over.
---

Running for governor now follows the office's own election calendar in every state, and a win leads to qualifying, taking office on the term's start date, and a staff briefing: choose a chief of staff, set a first priority, direct the agencies and see what they report back. Governors you don't play hold their own elections and make their own decisions, which show up in the news. Congress holds its regular elections, so seats no longer sit empty after January 2027. Where a state's real rules aren't in the game yet, the office says so and follows the game's own calendar.
