id: news-reader-headlines
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: News stories have their own headlines
---

A story about a local proposal or an international development now has a short
headline above its report, such as "City of Lincoln seeks comment on road
repair schedule plan" above "City of Lincoln posted a proposal about the repair
schedule for several local roads and opened a public comment period." Before,
the same sentence was printed twice. Saved stories are unchanged.
