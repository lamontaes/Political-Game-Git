section: Changed
title: Other states' budgets follow their own economies and tax laws
---

Every other state's budget after the first year now comes from what that state's governments actually collected and adopted in the simulated economy. A state that raises or cuts its income or sales tax shows the change in its next budget, and its spending follows the budget it adopted. Lines the economy does not track yet, such as property taxes and fees, stay at their published values and are marked as estimates.
