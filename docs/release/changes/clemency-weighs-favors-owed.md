id: clemency-weighs-favors-owed
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A governor weighs a favor the person asking once did them
---

When someone asks for a pardon or a shorter sentence, the governor or board
member who decides now weighs any help that person once gave them and that
still feels owed, such as running their campaign office. It counts toward
granting the request, alongside the decider's views on mercy, how well they
know the person, the coming election and the board's advice. A decider who
owes the person nothing weighs nothing of the kind.
