Supported illustrated people can use matching listening and seated poses in private previews. Room placement uses the available floor and seat positions, and opening conversation keeps the people and camera in place. An outfit without a matching pose keeps its supported standing view.

Review a supported appearance before beginning a life, keep matching portraits across People views, and import compatible art through the reusable private asset kit.
