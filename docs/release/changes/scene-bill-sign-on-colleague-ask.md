title: Ask your colleagues to sign on to your bill
---

As a state legislator, when you draft a bill you go to the colleagues you are close to and ask them to put their names on it. They answer from where they really stand: some say yes, some aren't there yet and can be talked into it, and some say no and tell you why (they don't believe in it, the people back home don't want it, their party's leaders are against it, or they voted against the same idea before). Ask again later and they remember. A bill with names on it has a better chance of getting a hearing, because the committee chair weighs who signed. Plead for a name and you owe that person one.
