title: The first night's jobs story says "outside Kentucky's cities", not "Kentucky outside the Louisville and Lexington areas"
---

In a town outside its state's big cities, the first night's jobs story used to read "Unemployment in Kentucky outside the Louisville and Lexington areas at 4.4", which put the state's name where you look for your town. It now reads "Jobless rate outside Kentucky's cities is 4.4%". A town in a metro still reads "the Louisville area", and a territory with no metro area keeps its own name.
