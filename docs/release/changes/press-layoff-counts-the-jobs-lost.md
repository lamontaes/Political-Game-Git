A layoff now weighs in the town paper by the people it reaches, the jobs cut plus the staff still working there, instead of only the owner it names, so layoffs that cut jobs make the paper in a busy week.

The layoff story itself now says what the business is in plain words ("a doctor's office", "one of the town's wholesalers") instead of a trade-statistics label, says the day the cut was made, follows the count in its body, and no longer repeats the employer's name three sentences running.
