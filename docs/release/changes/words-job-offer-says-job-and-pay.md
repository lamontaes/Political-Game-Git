title: Every job offer says the job and what it pays
---

When someone calls to offer you a job, the offer now always says the position and the hourly pay from the employer's record, for example "The opening at Cunningham's Kitchen is yours if you want it: a server, $18.84 an hour." Before, three of the five ways an offer could be said left out the pay, and two left out the job.
