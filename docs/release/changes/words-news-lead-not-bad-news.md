title: Ordinary news at home no longer opens with someone asking you to sit down first.
---

When someone at home brings you ordinary news, like what they thought of the President's speech, the scene no longer opens as if bad news were coming ("Your partner asks you to sit down first."). It opens with "{name} has something to tell you as soon as you're in the door."
