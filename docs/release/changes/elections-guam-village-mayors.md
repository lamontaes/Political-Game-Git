title: Guam's villages elect their mayors.
---

A life in a Guam village used to open with nothing local to run for, because no village had a government. Each of Guam's 19 villages now has its Mayor, held by someone on day one and elected every fourth November. The next race is in 2028, and it is listed on the Elections page. A mayor must be 21 or older, so a player who is too young for this race is no longer shown a filing date that falls after the race.
