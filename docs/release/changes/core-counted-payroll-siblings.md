title: Workers on a twice-monthly payroll get the whole raise, hire and ending
---

For the wider population on a payroll paid twice a month, a raise or a new minimum wage reached only half their pay, a new hire was paid only half, and someone laid off kept drawing half their old pay. A change to a counted worker's job now reaches both paydays.
