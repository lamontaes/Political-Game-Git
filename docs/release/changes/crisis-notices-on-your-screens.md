title: Illness, emergencies and the decisions only you can make now appear on your own screens.
---

Who you are now holds your own health: an illness or injury the record shows,
who has been told about it, and the choice to tell someone you know, the people
responsible, or everyone. Nothing is guessed — with no record it simply says
there is none — and somebody else's private illness reaches you only when they
or the people around them actually told you.

The same screen lists the public emergencies any resident can read, in the
words of the public record.

When the office you actually hold is the one being asked, your office gains the
decision itself: whether to ask for a federal disaster declaration, whether to
declare one, and what to do about an international dispute, each shown with
what the record says and what your advisers said. A resident reads the
emergency and is never handed a decision that is not theirs. If one of these
comes up while you are passing time, the game says so and takes you to it.
