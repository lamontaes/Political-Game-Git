id: bills-on-day-one
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: Bills already filed in a sitting session are on the record when you start.
---

A state whose session had already convened before the game's opening, such as Rhode Island's on January 7, used to show no bills on the first day, and filed them over the following weeks. The session now takes up its work at the opening: the bills filed in its first weeks are there on day one, each dated the day it was filed.
