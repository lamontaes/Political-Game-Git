title: Rights, privacy, environment, farm, park and road laws now reach people and money
---

When a council or legislature passes a clean air plan, a groundwater limit, an age check for social media, a facial recognition limit, dedicated park money or a shift of highway money to transit, the town's books now show it. Factories pay for the clean air plan, farms sell less under a groundwater cap, online employers pay to check ages, the police and parks budgets rise, and the transit line gets the moved highway money. A ban on discrimination keeps small employers from marking down older applicants, and a restriction on abortion raises the births the town counts. Repeal each and the effects stop.
