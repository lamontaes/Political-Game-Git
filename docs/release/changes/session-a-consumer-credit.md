title: Consumer rate caps constrain personal loan offers
---

New personal-loan offers read the consumer rate cap before the borrower and bank decide. Applications and loans retain the market rate and governing law. Full and counted households share the borrowing comparison, and loans remain with the same borrower through fill-in. Product-specific rules and counted attendance history remain registered gaps.
