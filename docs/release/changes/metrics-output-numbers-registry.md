impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Metrics shows your town's rent and home value as they move, with last year beside them once a year has passed.
---

The Metrics tab now reads every figure from one list of numbers, each kept by the part of the game that owns it. Your town's typical rent and home value follow the town month by month; before, they stayed at the first day's figures for the whole game. After your first December, the town's rent, home value, evictions and unemployment claims show last year beside this year. Until a year has been kept, a figure stands alone, with no made-up comparison. People looking for work shows the people behind the count ("13 of 86"). The town's books on Metrics and on the Budgets tab are always your own town's government.
