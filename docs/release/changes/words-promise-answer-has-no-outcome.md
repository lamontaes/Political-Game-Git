title: The one owed answers what you say about a promise
---

When someone brings up a promise you kept or broke, they now answer whatever you say back in their own words. They accept an owned break, doubt an explanation, cool at a denial, or hold you to a fresh promise. After a broken word, the goodbye drops the cheerful farewells.
