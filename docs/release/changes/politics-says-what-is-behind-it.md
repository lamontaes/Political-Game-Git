title: The Politics menu entry no longer describes itself as jobs and study
---

On a new character the menu read "Politics — Jobs and study", one press away from the actual "Jobs and study" entry, while Politics is the only way to reach parties, government, campaigns, local records, running for office and the public budget. The line under it was assembled from your office, your campaign and your job, and a new character has none of the first two, so only the last one was left. It now says what is behind the entry whatever you have reached so far, and still names your office and your campaign once you have them.
