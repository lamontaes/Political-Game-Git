title: A census-designated town now starts at its Census survey count instead of 1,000 people.
---

About two in five of the towns a life can start in are census-designated
places, such as Kittery, Maine, Urban Honolulu or East Los Angeles. The
Census Bureau's annual estimates do not cover them, so each one was seated as
a town of 1,000 people. Each now starts at the Census Bureau's American
Community Survey 2020-2024 count: 5,110 people in Kittery, 345,482 in Urban
Honolulu, 842 in Lime Ridge, Pennsylvania. The town's households, homes and
workforce are sized from that count.
