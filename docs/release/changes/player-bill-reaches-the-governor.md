id: player-bill-reaches-the-governor
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: A bill the player carries through the legislature now goes to the state's governor, who decides it.
---

When a player's bill passed both chambers, the governor never decided it. In
Kentucky, Nebraska and Alaska, the bill replayed the answer written
into the developer scenario, which vetoed eight of its nine bills. In any
other state the step was refused, so the bill could never be signed or
vetoed. Now the bill lands on the seated governor's desk like every other
bill the legislature passes. The player waits while the governor decides, and
a veto goes back to the legislature for an override vote. A world with no
seated governor, such as an older save, keeps its written answer.
