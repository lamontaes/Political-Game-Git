title: Medical bills and medical debt run in the background
---

Households in your town now pay their own doctor and hospital bills each month. A family with someone seriously ill pays more, and people with a health plan, Medicare or Medicaid pay a smaller share than people with no coverage. A bill a family cannot pay waits four months, then goes to collections as medical debt on its books, and after six more months the bank sees it when the family asks for a loan. Expanding Medicaid now shrinks that debt: the adults it covers get smaller bills. You take no step; it all happens in the background and shows in how families' money holds up.
