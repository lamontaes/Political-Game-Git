title: Townspeople you haven't met can take up a cause after a death in the family
---

When someone in town dies, the family they lived with can now come away with a cause, such as fighting the illness that took them, even if you have never met them. Whether they do follows their own temperament, the same way it does for the people you know. If you meet one of them later, they already carry that cause.
