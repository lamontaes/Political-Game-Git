id: fairness-law-player-pay
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The player is hired under the same fairness-law pay rule as the town
---

A fairness law set the pay of townspeople but never the player's. Now the
player is hired under the same rule: a man partnered with a man who takes a
job where no fairness law covers him is paid about 2.6% below the job's pay,
never below the minimum wage, and the full pay where a law covers him. It
applies to a job taken through the job listings, the job a grown-up new life
starts with, and shift work. In one game in Charleston, South Carolina, the
player's four-hour shop shift paid $70.11 instead of $72.00; under a fairness
law, or single, it paid $72.00.
