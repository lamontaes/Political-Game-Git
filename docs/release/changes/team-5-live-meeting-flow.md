title: Starting a public meeting opens the room before it ends
---

Starting a posted public meeting from Today, the calendar, or its trip now opens
the meeting room. Read the agenda, see who is present, speak, and choose when to
finish. Existing recorded votes appear where available. Meeting duration no
longer reads as travel time, Until needed stops before a same-day meeting, and
completed meetings leave the waiting list.
