section: Improved
title: Lines you liked come up more often
---

A line you graded good in a voice note or in play now comes up more often itself, and a line you rejected less. Before, your grade lifted the other lines of the same kind but not the line you graded. "The party has a long memory." and the other threats you liked now carry 13.6 percent of the weight among a scene's threats, up from 12.5.
