section: Fixed
title: A new world's workers and officials get their whole first paycheck
---

A world opens partway through everyone's pay period. The town's workers who are counted in groups, and every official, now get their first paycheck for the whole period, the days before the world began included, so a town's renters no longer start the year a month behind on rent. The player's own household still gets a cut first paycheck until the next fix lands.
