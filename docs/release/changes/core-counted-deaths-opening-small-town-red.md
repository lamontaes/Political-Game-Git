A heavy counted record now dies in part: where the deaths a month owes are fewer than the households a record stands for, that many of its people die, their estate passes and is taxed for those households, the people who lived with them hear of it, their jobs stand for fewer heads at their employers, and the record stands for fewer households. Small towns no longer go whole months with no counted death.
