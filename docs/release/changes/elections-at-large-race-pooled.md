impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A board's at-large seats up on one day are one race, and the top finishers take the seats.
---

Where a council, selectboard or county board elects several members at large on the same day, every candidate now runs against the whole field. Each voter votes for up to as many candidates as there are seats, and the top finishers are seated. Running for one of three seats means finishing in the top three, not beating one seat's holder head on.
