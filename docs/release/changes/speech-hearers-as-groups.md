---
id: speech-hearers-as-groups
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: Campaign speeches in a busy election year take less time to simulate.
---

When a candidate the player is not following gives a speech, the people in the crowd who are strangers to the player now take it in together as groups of like-minded listeners instead of one at a time. The player, their household, the people they know and officeholders still hear every speech for themselves.
