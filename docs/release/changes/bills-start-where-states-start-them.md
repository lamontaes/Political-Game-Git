title: Bills start where each state starts them, and Columbus has its real council
---

Where a state's own rules say where its bill numbers begin, a session's first
bill now carries that number: Washington's Senate opens at SB 5000, Arizona's
House at HB 2001, Connecticut's House at HB 5001. Kansas, Michigan, South
Carolina and Washington keep counting through both years of the legislature.
Every other chamber still starts at 1.

Columbus, Ohio, now seats the council its charter describes: nine members,
each living in one of nine districts and elected citywide, on four-year terms.
