title: People dress for where they are
---

Every place now has a dress code, and people dress for it. At home and around
the neighborhood they wear everyday clothes. In offices and campaign
storefronts they wear work clothes, like a polo and khakis, a blouse and skirt
or a suit. In chambers, courtrooms, hearings and the offices of governors and
mayors they wear formal wear. Outdoors from November through March they wear
their coats. Each person keeps their own clothes for each kind of place.
Uniforms are saved for the jobs that wear them.
