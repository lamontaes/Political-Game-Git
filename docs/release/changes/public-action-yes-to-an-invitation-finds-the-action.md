title: Saying yes to a friend's invitation to a protest or rally puts you in it, whoever asked
---

A friend whose name the town knows by a longer record, a newspaper's owner for one, could ask you to a protest or rally and your yes joined nothing. The yes now finds the protest or rally it was for, and you are in it.
