title: States begin with their real voting, term-limit, district and rent laws
---

Every state, D.C. and territory now begins the game with the law it really
had at the start of 2026 on five more questions: whether voters show a photo
ID at the polls, whether people are registered to vote automatically, whether
legislators have term limits, whether an independent commission draws the
districts, and whether the state limits rent increases or bars its cities from
doing so. Where the law in a place couldn't be confirmed, the game treats it
as unknown rather than guessing.
