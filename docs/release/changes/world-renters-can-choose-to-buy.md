title: Renters who can afford a home and have reason to want one now buy.
---

A renter household's monthly choice to buy weighs what really pulls people toward owning: children at home, the head's age, years in the lease and savings beyond the down payment, against the cost and the down payment. Households that can afford a home and have reason to want one now buy it, where before every household chose to keep renting.
