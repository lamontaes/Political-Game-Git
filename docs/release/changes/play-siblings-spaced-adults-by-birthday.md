title: Brothers and sisters are born months apart, and no 17-year-old runs a city department
---

A new life could open with a sister born a week after the player, and a 17-year-old could be named the city's Public Works Director. Brothers and sisters at home are now born at least nine months apart, and a birthday chosen at the start moves a sibling born too close to it. Only residents who have turned 18 by their birthday are weighed for a department. Two relatives no longer ask each other after a relative they share by first name or as "your father".
