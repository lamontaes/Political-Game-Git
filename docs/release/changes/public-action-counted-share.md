title: A town's strikes, protests and rallies now count the wider town's people
---

A protest over rent, jobs, health or pay used to be only the people you could name. The renters, the jobless and the residents you have not met are now asked whether they join too, by the same view of the problem a named neighbor has, so a protest in a town of thousands is as large as the town's feeling makes it, and a town can have a protest nobody you know is in.
