title: The world report ends with the place's vital statistics, at the start and at the end
---

A watched-world report now closes with the same list of figures for every
place, so two worlds can be set side by side. The list covers people and
households, pay and unemployment, poverty, health insurance, graduation and
college completion, crime, turnout, births and deaths, businesses opened and
closed, and the governor's, legislature's and mayor's parties. Each figure is
read from what the world recorded, at the opening and at the end, and says
whether it belongs to the place or to its state. A figure the world does not
record yet, such as household income or rent, says so and why instead of
showing zero. This is a development report; nothing in play changes.
