title: Governors in seven states take office on the day their constitution names
---

Alaska and Hawaii now seat a newly elected governor on the first Monday of
December, a month after the vote, instead of in January. Delaware, Florida,
Louisiana, Maine and Virginia seat theirs on the January day their own
constitution names, such as the Saturday after the second Wednesday in
Virginia. Every other state keeps the game's first Monday of January until its
clause is read.

A new check walks every governorship from the start of a new game to 2046.
Each term begins the day the last one ends, so no state goes without a
governor, and each election falls in the state's real years. North Carolina and
Missouri now elect in 2028 and keep their governor from 2025 to 2029.

Legislatures whose session rules have not been researched still meet every
year and drop unfinished bills at adjournment. That is now labeled as the
game's standing rule rather than the state's law.
