title: Legislators deciding a tied election vote from their own views
---

When a state's law has the legislature settle a tied statewide race, each member now votes from their own recorded views, and a member the world had not yet filled in as a person is filled in the moment they decide. A member with no record at all abstains instead of voting their party's side.
