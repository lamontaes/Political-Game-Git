title: A president gets a cabinet; the Senate no longer rejects every nominee of a president whose party it doesn't hold.
---

A president gets a cabinet. Before, the Senate's vote read each senator only by their party, so a Senate the president's party did not hold rejected every nominee, again and again (1,020 rejections and no confirmation in four years at one world). Now each senator weighs the nominee from their own lean, a president nominates the first of their choices the sitting Senate would confirm (a player's own pick stays theirs), and a failed vote is recorded as it was cast. Played for a year at three worlds, every one of 15 nominees was confirmed each time, by 51-49, 58-42 and 67-33.
