title: A town hears about its own changing conditions
---

Each month the town's record now notes which of its conditions moved, such as rents, unemployment and the crime rates. The local press picks them up by how big the move was, and townspeople hear of them on different days, by how closely they follow the news and how far they trust it, or from a neighbor. The town's voters now count a change in jobs or crime only after a story about it has reached someone who lives there.
