section: Changed
title: Cities over 100,000 people now hold a mayor's race
---

Every town now knows its size, from the Census Bureau's 2025 estimates. Where
the game has not read a city's own rules, a city of more than 100,000 people
now elects its mayor, so Philadelphia, Dallas, Atlanta, Las Vegas, Saint Paul
and 64 other large cities offer a mayor's race they did not before. A city
whose own rules the game has read keeps them, whatever its size. This is an
interim rule until the rules by town size have been researched.
