---
id: gov-press-outlet-cost-scheduled-hours
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A town's radio station or paper no longer runs out of money when its reporter works every scheduled day.
---

An outlet's cost, which its wider audience helps pay, was counted at a 40-hour week for each of its people, so a reporter scheduled longer cost more than it took in and a station could close. Its cost now follows its own staff's scheduled hours.
