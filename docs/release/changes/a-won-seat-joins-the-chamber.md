title: A seat you win in your state legislature puts you in the chamber
---

When you won a state legislative seat in a chamber the game had already
filled, the member who held your district kept the seat after your term
began, and the chamber's roll never listed you, so you had no floor votes.
Now, on the day your term begins, that member leaves the seat and you take
it. You appear on the chamber's roll and vote on the bills put to it. If you
are re-elected, you keep the same seat. A reminder to decide a vote that
passed while you were busy elsewhere, such as out campaigning, no longer
stays on your calendar after the vote.
