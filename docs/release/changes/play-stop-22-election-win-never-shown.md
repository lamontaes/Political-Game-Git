title: Election night tells you whether you won
---

After an election you ran in, election night opens on your own race with every candidate's votes and whether you won. Campaigns marks your filing won or lost, News carries the result, the journal tells your race even against a rival you never met, and Government shows who was elected to each seat and when they take office.
