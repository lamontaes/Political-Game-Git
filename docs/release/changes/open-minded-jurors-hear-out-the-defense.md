title: An open-minded juror hears out the defense before voting to convict
---

A juror who gives unfamiliar explanations a hearing now counts that habit as
one reason to vote not guilty. It weighs beside the evidence in the case, the
burden of proof and what the rest of the room says, so the same juror on the
same testimony without that streak still votes to convict.
