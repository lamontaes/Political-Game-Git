title: Your own record has a Legal tab
---

Open your own record and choose Legal to see where you stand with the law:
every charge filed against you and where it stands, the hearing date, each
sentence and its term, and every request for clemency with the answers so
far. While a charge is waiting for your plea you can plead guilty or not
guilty there, and while a sentence is running you can ask for clemency where
the law of your place allows it. Other people's records have no Legal tab.
