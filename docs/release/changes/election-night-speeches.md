title: Election night has victory and concession speeches
---

When your race is decided, the other candidates give their speeches at once: a
victory speech from the winner, a concession to the winner from everyone else.
Yours is your choice. The result screen offers "Give your victory speech" or
"Concede to" the person who beat you, and what you said stays on the record.
