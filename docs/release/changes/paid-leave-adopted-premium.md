id: paid-leave-adopted-premium
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: Paid leave uses the adopted employee premium and wage cap
---

An operative paid-leave bill's final employee-premium rate (basis points of
covered wages) and annual covered-wage cap (dollars per year) now reach the
existing paycheck assessment and withholding writer. Missing or invalid terms
retain the existing sourced or labeled fallback; filed or later unadopted text
cannot change an assessment. Existing collection dates and completed payments
remain intact. Save/Continue accepts adopted rates above the old typical-program
5% surrogate, with withholding bounded by the actual covered wages.
