title: People stand on the floor of the room, not through it
---

In some rooms a person was drawn standing below the floor — sunk into it by
anything from a few inches to most of their legs, depending on the room. They
were being pushed down by however much of them did not fit above the floor
line.

Everyone now stands exactly on the floor where they are. Somebody too tall for
the space the room leaves above them is framed from the head down, the way a
photograph taken in a small room is, rather than sinking to make themselves
fit.
