title: Generosity is proven to change what two people choose
impact: patch
---

Two people in a generated game, alike in every other recorded temperament reason, make the same choice about a donation, a clemency petition and a request for campaign support. The one with a high philanthropic temperament donates, grants clemency and grants the request, and the other does not, with the reason traced to the trait.
