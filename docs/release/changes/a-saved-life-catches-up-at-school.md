title: A life saved before school stages moves on to the right school
---

A life saved before children moved on through school stayed at the first
school it started in, so a teenager, or a grown adult, was still at
elementary school. The first time such a save moves forward, it catches up.
A child still at the right school stays there and moves on at the end of the
year. A child who should be further along moves to the middle or high school
for their age that day, with their class. Someone past the end of high school
leaves school. Nothing earlier is filled in.
