id: framed-paper-theme
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Menus and screens are framed paper with brass trim instead of dark blue
---

The title menu, the new-game steps and every screen in play now sit on warm
paper panels with a thin brass frame and dark ink type, in place of the dark
blue glass. Headings use the serif face. The bottom bar's tiles are paper and
turn oxblood when pressed. Buttons, fields, check boxes and lists share the
same paper and brass. Party colors on the opening maps were darkened so each
one still reads on the lighter ground.
