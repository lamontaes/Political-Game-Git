section: Changed
title: When someone asks after your relative, you can tell them what happened.
---

Asked "How's your mother?", you can now answer with what the records say happened to her lately: that she's in the hospital, that she moved ("Mom moved to Tulsa."), that she started a new job, or that there's a new baby. Before, every answer was "She's doing well.", whatever had happened. You can still just thank them for asking.
