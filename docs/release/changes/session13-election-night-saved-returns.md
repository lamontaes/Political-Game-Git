title: Watch council returns without losing your place
---

At home on council election night, watch the saved precinct returns in order or skip to the final unofficial result. Saving and continuing preserves your place in the returns. The room TV shows the same running count. Only people actually present appear with you, and an available conversation uses their existing words. The speech and return choices use the existing campaign and result history. Paid venues, new reactions and ordinary two-place scene proof remain unfinished.
