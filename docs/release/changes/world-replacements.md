id: world-replacements
section: Fixed
title: When a worker dies or moves away, their employer hires someone into the same job
impact: patch
---

A worker who dies, moves away, quits or retires now leaves an opening for the same role, and the employer fills it the way it fills any other job. A worker who dies stops drawing pay the next day. An official whose aide dies or leaves the post hires another, and the new aide's record names who they replaced.
