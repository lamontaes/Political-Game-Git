title: The country elects a President and Vice President every four years
---

Before this, nothing ever held a presidential election. The President and
Vice President seated at the start served until January 20, 2029, and then
both offices stayed empty for good.

Now a presidential election is held every fourth November from 2028, whether
or not you take part. Each party names a ticket 60 days before election day.
A sitting President who is eligible may run again; the Twenty-Second
Amendment bars a third elected term. Every state and the District reports its
vote on election day, the states certify, and the electors vote on their
statutory day in December. Congress counts the votes on January 6, and the
winners take the oath at noon on January 20. A saved game picks up the
calendar where it left off.

The popular vote in each state is the game's own until research answers how
much a presidential result moves between elections: it starts from the
state's 2024 result and moves by a swing drawn each cycle. How nominees are
chosen is also the game's own for now.

Not modeled yet, and marked so in the code: running for President yourself,
primaries and campaigns, faithless electors, and a tie in the Electoral
College. A tie leaves the offices empty until the contingent election in
Congress is built.

The legislature of a state the game has not read now says plainly that its
yearly session and the rule that pending bills die at adjournment are the
game's own, not that state's law.
