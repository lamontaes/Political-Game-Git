title: People who bring up a change in town no longer add "I have no idea" when they don't know who caused it
---

When someone tells you a business closed or a law changed and has nobody to name for it, they now say only what happened, instead of adding "I don't know who did it." or "I have no idea." And a blame they never said aloud no longer passes to you.
