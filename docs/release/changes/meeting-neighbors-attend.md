title: The people at the posted public meeting are your neighbors
---

The chair and the two residents who speak at the posted public meeting are now
people who already live in your town. Before, where the town's council was not
seated, the game made up a chair and two residents for the evening, with drawn
names and ages. Now the neighbors who belong to the most groups, such as a
party, a congregation, a club or a board, are the ones who come, and the one who
belongs to the most chairs. Where the council is seated, a council member
still chairs.
