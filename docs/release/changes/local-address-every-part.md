title: A governor's and a mayor's address say every part again.
---

A governor's or mayor's yearly address could turn to a subject ("let me turn to collective bargaining for public workers") and then say nothing about it, because most of its parts had no words. It now greets the room, says how things stand, gives its stand on each question with a reason, says what the year's figures mean, and closes by holding the speaker to the side they asked for.
