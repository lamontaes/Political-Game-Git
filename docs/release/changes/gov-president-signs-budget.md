title: Budgets now go to the president, governor or mayor to sign or veto, and the news prints it.
---

A budget both chambers pass now goes to the executive's desk like any other bill. The president, a governor or a mayor with a veto signs or vetoes it from their own position, and a veto the legislature cannot override leaves last year's budget in force. National and state outlets now print an executive's signature or veto, because a story that names a public figure counts for more when a reporter picks what to cover.
