title: The clerk's talk after you file names your race, your signatures and the day that counts
---

After you file, the clerk's talk about the petition now says what your own filing holds: how many signatures, the day they are due, the office, your town and the day of the race's next vote. Lines that named nothing ("The counter closes at five sharp.") are gone. When others filed before you, the clerk names up to three of them and then says how many more, without a party before every name.
