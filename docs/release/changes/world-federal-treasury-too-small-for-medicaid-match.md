title: The federal share of Medicaid reaches the people it covers
---

Where Medicaid expansion is law, the federal government's share of each covered adult's care is now paid every month alongside the state's. The federal treasury borrows for the program payments falling due in the month, not only for its budget lines, so it no longer runs dry before they come due.
