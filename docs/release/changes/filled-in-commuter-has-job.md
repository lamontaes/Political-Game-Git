title: A neighbor who works outside town now has a real job with a boss and a taxed paycheck once you meet them.
---

Before, a stranger who commuted to a job outside town was handed a slice of one town-wide pay stream, with no employer and no tax taken out. Now they hold a job at their employer outside town, paid on a regular payday with income and payroll tax withheld, and the town and state treasuries receive the tax.
