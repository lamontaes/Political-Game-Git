title: Townspeople hold organizing meetings only at schools that open their rooms to the public
---

People who start organizing around a cause now meet at a high school only when that school's district lets outside groups use its buildings, which about six in ten districts do. A church or union hall is a place to meet for its own members, and colleges are not meeting rooms at all. Before, every school, college and congregation in town counted as a place anyone could hold a meeting.
