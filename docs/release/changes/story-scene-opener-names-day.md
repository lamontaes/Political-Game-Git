title: The filing counter opens by naming the day
---

When you go to file, the narrator now sets the scene first and names the day: "First thing on June 7th, you head to the clerk's office and ask for Maria Patton." It plays every time you walk in, including when you pick a race straight from the list, and the clerk no longer greets you with just the office name.
