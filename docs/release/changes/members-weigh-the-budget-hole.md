title: Lawmakers weigh what ending a tax would cost their own budget.
---

Congress could vote away the federal income tax in the first spring, and paychecks stopped withholding it. Every lawmaker now weighs the hole a bill would leave in the budget their own body must pass. A tax that carries half the budget is not repealed on a whim; a small one still can be.
