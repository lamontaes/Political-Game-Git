title: College dorms, prisons and barracks now turn over, and no program counts them as poor households
---

A college town's students used to age in place and its dorms emptied over a long game, and prisons, juvenile facilities and bases never released anyone. Each kind of institution now discharges its residents on its own calendar (a college graduates its class in May and admits the next in August; the others turn their beds over once in a stay) and refills the room with a new resident of the same kind. A nursing home still refills when a resident dies. A means-tested program (food aid, work requirements, welfare) no longer reads a cell, a dorm or a nursing-home room as a poor household; the Medicaid expansion still reaches nursing-home rooms.
