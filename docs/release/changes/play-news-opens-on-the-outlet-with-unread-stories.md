title: News opens on the paper or station that has something you have not read
---

News used to open on your town's paper even when it had printed nothing, while a station or network you follow had stories waiting. It now opens on the tab you chose last if it has something new, otherwise on the outlet you follow with the newest story you have not read, otherwise your town's paper; and it stays on the page you are reading while the day moves on.
