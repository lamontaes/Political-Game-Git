title: The county courtroom shows the day the court sat on your case
---

On a day a court tries or sentences your case, the screen now shows the county
courtroom. The prosecutor who reviewed the referral stands at the counsel spot,
the jurors who cast ballots sit in the jury box, and the judge who chose the
sentence takes the bench. Nobody is added who the saved records do not name:
defense counsel are not recorded yet, so no second counsel spot is filled, and a
day with no sentencing carries no judge. The next day the room clears.
