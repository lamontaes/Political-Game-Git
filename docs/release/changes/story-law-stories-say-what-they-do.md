title: Laws in the News now say what they change.
---

A law passed in the last year used to read the same way whatever it did: its name, "became law" and a date. News now tells most of them by what they change and where: a benefit or a tax rate that went up or down, from one figure to another, the limit or policy the law sets, who it covers and the day it starts, in different wordings. A law whose record says too little still shows its name and its date.
