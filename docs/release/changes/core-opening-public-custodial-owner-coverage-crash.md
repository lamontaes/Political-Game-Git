title: A town inside a township no longer stops the game at its first public settlement
---

A town that lies inside a township, such as Robertsdale in Wood Township, used to stop the game at its first monthly settlement, on February 1, 2025, with a missing owner coverage record. The township's custodial contract is now built only where its coverage of the town is recorded, and the year goes on.
