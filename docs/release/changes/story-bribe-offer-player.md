title: A bribe offered to you is yours to answer.
---

When someone offers an official money for a vote or a contract and the official is you, the offer now arrives as a scene: you take it, hand it back, stall or say you will report it. Nothing is decided by your traits behind your back, and the money moves only if you take it.
