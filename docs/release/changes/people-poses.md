title: People lean on the lectern, write at the desk and stand at the counter
---

At the debate podiums, the council's public lectern, the clerk's counter, the mayor's desk and visitor chair, the governor's visitor chair and the Resolute desk, people now stand and sit the way they would there: gesturing to the crowd, leaning in to make a point, writing at the desk, hands on the counter, seen from behind. Each keeps their own skin, clothes colors, face and hair.
