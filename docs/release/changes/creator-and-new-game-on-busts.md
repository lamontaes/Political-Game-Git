New game now shows a bust over Start a life and three over Advanced. The creator shows your character as a bust the moment it opens, with rows for skin, hair and facial hair; the two full-length figures and the color rows are gone. The look you make is the one on the title's Continue and on every screen in play. New game, the creator and Advanced now sit in the middle of the screen.
