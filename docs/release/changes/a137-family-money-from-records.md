title: How well off a child's family is now comes from what the household actually earns
---

A child's family money now comes from the household's recorded pay, compared
with the poverty line for a family of that size in that state: at or under the
line is hard scarcity, up to twice the line is strained, and above that is
secure. When the game has no pay on record for that part of someone's
childhood, such as an adult who grew up before the story begins, they take the
median American child's level, which is secure, instead of a random one.
People already in a saved game keep the temperaments they have.
