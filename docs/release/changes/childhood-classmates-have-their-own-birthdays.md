title: The classmate from your childhood is no longer born on your birthday
---

A grown character's summarized childhood gives them a parent, a classmate and a
teacher. The classmate was born on the player's exact birthday in every save,
the parent exactly 28 years earlier to the day and the teacher exactly 30, so
every new life had a stranger sharing the player's birthday.

New games now give each of them a birthday of their own. The classmate is born
within half a year either side of the player, the parent 22 to 39 years
earlier, and the teacher 24 to 56. These ranges are chosen for the game, not
measured. Saved games keep the dates they were created with.
