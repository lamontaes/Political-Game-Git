section: Changed
title: People you have drifted from get in touch on their own days
---

Before, everybody you had drifted from got in touch on the first day it was
possible, always for an evening nine days away, so every life heard from
somebody on the same schedule. Now each pair of people has its own days: how
far ahead they ask, how long they wait before asking again, and which day
they pick up the phone. The same two people keep the same days after a save
and reload. The ranges are calibration, not research, and are filed as
questions for the relationship research.
