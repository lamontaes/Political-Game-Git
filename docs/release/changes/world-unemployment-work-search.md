title: Unemployment benefits follow the state's weekly job-search rule
---

People collecting unemployment now have to look for work the way their state's law asks. Each week the benefit pays the share of its amount that their employer contacts earn against the state's rule, so someone who sits out an active job market is paid less or nothing, and where nobody is hiring the full benefit is paid. Nothing is added for the player to fill in.
