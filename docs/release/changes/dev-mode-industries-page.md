title: Dev mode lists the town's industries, their businesses and each business's months.
---

In dev mode, the Calendar has an Industries panel. It lists every industry in the town with its open and closed businesses, staff, sales, payroll, profit and cash. Pick an industry to see its businesses by name, then pick a business to see each month's sales, payroll, costs, law duties and the people hired, let go or gone. Every record opens in Trace.
