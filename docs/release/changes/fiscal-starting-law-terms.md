section: Fixed
title: Starting fiscal terms reach pension budgets and flat income-tax withholding.
---

Starting pension laws carry their cited full-actuarial contribution share into
the existing budget calculation. Fifteen states with sourced flat income taxes
carry their 2026 rate and taxable-income threshold into the existing paycheck
assessment and collection path. Existing payments remain unchanged on replay.
Graduated schedules and unrelated starting-law questions keep their existing
behavior; this does not supply missing category or bracket contracts.
