title: Effect sizes use their recorded central estimates
---

Outcome links use their recorded place-specific central size, or the existing
link-wide central size. Seeds no longer draw a different coefficient within a
research range. Research bounds remain available as evidence checks. Existing
teacher-floor and tuition-growth callers use the same shared reader; their
legal terms and bases remain with their owning pay and budget consumers.
