section: Changed
title: Your Journal starts with your birth.
---

The Journal's first line now tells where you were born, from the record of your birth ("I was born in Crescent, Louisiana."). The old line that said where your family came from told the same record again and is gone. A district or an island territory is named with its article: "the District of Columbia", "the U.S. Virgin Islands".
