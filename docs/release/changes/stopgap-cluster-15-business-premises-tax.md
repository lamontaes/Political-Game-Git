section: Changed
title: Businesses pay property tax on their premises
---

Every business now pays property tax each quarter on its premises, at the rate in force where it stands, from its own cash to the place's tax collector. Its premises are valued at an estimated share of a year's sales. A business that cannot pay owes the rest and pays it first next quarter.
