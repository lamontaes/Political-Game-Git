title: The Stops checklist shows your own election day
---

The Stops checklist beside Day and Week, and the Calendar's Interruptions tab,
now list your own election day. Passing time always stops the morning after an
election you are running in and says how it came out, so the row is shown
checked and cannot be switched off.
