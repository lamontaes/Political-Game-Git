title: People treat you the same way in talk, scenes and the Journal
---

Whether someone knows you, counts you as family, or is a friend or a rival used to be worked out three ways: the talk box, the scenes and the Journal each had their own copy and their own cutoff for a close tie. They now read one record of how two people stand and one closeness cutoff, so a person who greets you as a close friend on the phone also stands as one in a scene and in the Journal.
