id: gov-first-race-no-local-office
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A newer small town the Census place-to-county list missed now gets its county's council or board for your first race.
---

A home the Census place-to-county relation misses (a newer place in Virginia, California, Indiana, Florida, Nevada and others) is now served by the county the place map holds for it, so your first race there is that county's board, not your state's legislature. Homes with no government at all (Alaska places outside every borough, the island-area villages) still start with the state's or territory's own lawmaking body.
