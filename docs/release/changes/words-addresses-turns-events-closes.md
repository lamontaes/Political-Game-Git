title: Governors' and local leaders' addresses now name what happened in your place this year
---

A governor's or a mayor's address now says the one thing that happened in your state or town this past year (a law that took effect, a disaster, a business that closed, or the vote that put them there), moves from one point to the next by naming both, and closes on whether their party holds the council or legislature. Only an address given in the evening says good night. The President's address never names your town.
