Exposes a read-only candidate list from saved people with professional
qualification records and their linked career facts. It shows a current court
seat and leaves unsupported judicial philosophy unresolved. A professional
record is evidence for review, not a federal eligibility requirement.

Once a complete recorded Senate vote confirms a sitting judge for another
seat, the old tenure ends with an appointment-to-another-seat cause before
the new tenure begins. The same person cannot hold two ordinary judicial
seats. The source seat becomes vacant unless an enacted smaller court size
retires it. The confirmation player screen and NPC vote formation remain
separate work.
