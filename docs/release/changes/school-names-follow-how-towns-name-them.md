title: A small town's high school is usually named for the town
---

Generated schools named one high school in five after a national figure,
wherever it was, so a new life in Rugby, North Dakota, a town of about 2,500,
went to "Booker T. Washington High School". New games now draw the kind of
name from how schools are actually named. The measurement comes from the
school directory the game already ships, and no real school's name is used.
Where a town has only one school at a level, about two in three carry the
town's name, and a national figure names about one high school in five
hundred. In a city with many schools, most are named for a person, a
neighborhood or the landscape. A national figure is picked by how often schools
in that part of the country carry the name, so Cesar Chavez turns up mostly in
the West. Saved games keep their schools, and so does a person someone
continues as.
