title: Every death now has a cause, and your laws can change which
---

When someone in town dies, the record now says why: an overdose, a gun, a crash or something else, in the mix the national numbers show for that age. Harm reduction and cannabis sales change the overdose share, a child access law and a minimum handgun age change the youth gun shares, and a higher speed limit changes the crash share, each at the ages it applies to. The same cause is recorded for people you know and for the town's wider population.
