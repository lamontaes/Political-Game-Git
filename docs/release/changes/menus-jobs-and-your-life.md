title: Your life tells your own history as a timeline; Jobs and study puts your job on a plate
---

Who you are has a new Your life tab that tells your own history from birth down a gold timeline with a year and an icon for each event, the same way a person's card tells theirs. Jobs and study shows your job on a framed plate with its title in large type, your pay and hours, and your work and schooling as a timeline beside it.
