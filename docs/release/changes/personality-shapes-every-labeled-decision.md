title: Personality now shapes every labeled decision through one table
---

A person's recorded traits now bear on every choice whose options are labeled with the kind of act they are, through one shared table instead of a separate rule for each decision. Someone hot-headed leans toward answering a reporter back, someone self-conscious puts off the invitation, someone who values keeping what they know stays put. A trait the game has no record of still adds nothing, and nothing is rolled: the traits put reasons on the table and the same scorer picks.
