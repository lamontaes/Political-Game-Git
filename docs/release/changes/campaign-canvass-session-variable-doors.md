section: Changed
title: A day of knocking on doors can bring no conversations, one, or several.
---

A day of canvassing now stops for every door whose resident has a reason to talk, in the order you reached them, and a day with no such door brings in its signatures without a conversation. Each door knows how far into the day it came.
