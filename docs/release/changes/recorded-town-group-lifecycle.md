---
id: recorded-town-group-lifecycle
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Local groups follow recorded membership and founding choices
---

Empty congregations and clubs close when they have no active members, and
their staff jobs end through the existing closure writer. Former members
can decide to rebuild their recorded community using their existing goals.
Only people who select founding join the new group. Annual rolls and fixed
membership and group-count limits no longer decide the lifecycle.
