impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A new life opens several times faster.
---

Beginning a life checked the entire world after every record it wrote: each legislator seated, each reporter hired, each party committee formed. It now checks the finished world once. Opening a life in Concord, New Hampshire, whose House has 400 seats, went from 3.3 seconds to 0.9 seconds in Node, and the opened world is byte-identical.
