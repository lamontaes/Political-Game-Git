title: A close real race no longer opens on the same share as every other close race
---

A seat whose last race is in the research now opens with its winner's share inside what the record allows: from a one-vote win up to the real share moved by the world's drift. It is no longer clamped to 50.5%, so the President's day-one approval and close House and Senate seats differ from world to world.
