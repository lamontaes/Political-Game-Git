title: After you lose a race, the talk at home comes from the person who worked it or paid for it
---

When you lose a race, someone at home talks to you only if they put something into it: they worked the campaign, or you spent your own money on it. They open on how close the count was and on their part, and you answer four ways. If you say you will run again, their answer is kept, and the next time you weigh a run it pulls you toward running if they are behind you, or away if they doubt it. A loss nobody at home had a part in no longer gets a scene.
