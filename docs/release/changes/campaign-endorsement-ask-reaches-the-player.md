title: People close to a candidate can now ask you for your endorsement
---

A candidate's campaign now asks you, when you are one of the people closest to them, whether you will endorse them, and calls in a favor you owe them from a deal in office. You answer in a scene: a yes is public and your circle hears it, a no is kept and the person remembers it, and a put-off comes back after a few weeks until the race is decided. Before, the campaign skipped you and nothing was asked.
