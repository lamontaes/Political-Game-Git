section: Improved
title: Clearer People, campaign and character creation screens
---

In People, a face is now the whole thing you click. Choosing someone lights up
their connections and says, in plain words, how you know them, and the
categories no longer call everyone you have met a friend. The campaign screen
has one button for each thing you can do right now, keeps rule sources and
dates tucked under a detail link, and no longer runs labels together. Character
creation offers month names for your birthday, a button to pick a random name,
a town list that says how many places there are, and it tells you straight away
when an age is out of range.
