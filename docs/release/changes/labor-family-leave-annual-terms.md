section: Changed
title: Family leave monetary terms retain their sourced calendar year
---

The existing starting-law row now carries the official New Jersey 2026
employee Family Leave Insurance contribution, covered-wage cap and wage
replacement share. The earlier categorical program answer stays intact;
annual monetary terms are not backdated or extended into an unread year.
No leave-duration conversion, disability contribution, automatic bargaining
raise or new payment mechanism is introduced.
