---
id: labor-family-leave-annual-terms
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Family leave monetary terms retain their sourced calendar year
---

Existing family-leave starting rows now carry supported employee contribution,
covered-wage cap and replacement fields from their official program pages.
Optional employee maxima, tiered replacement and unlimited wages are not
turned into universal scalar amounts. Earlier categorical answers stay intact;
annual monetary terms are not backdated or extended into an unread year.
No leave-duration conversion, separate disability deduction, automatic bargaining
raise or new payment mechanism is introduced.
