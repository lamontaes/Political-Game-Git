title: Real disasters reach the town, and Social Security follows the law in force
---

A town now lives through the disasters FEMA actually declared for its county or state, on their real dates. Each one is public news when it begins. When it's declared, federal aid is paid to the county's government, the town's government and the town's households, in the amounts FEMA recorded. People who reach retirement age during the game claim Social Security under whatever ages the law sets at the time, so an act of Congress that raises the full retirement age moves their claims. Families with children in private school now split their tuition among the town's private schools instead of each family being assigned to one, and families who move to town get new family names.
