title: The loser calls the winner after the count
---

When you win a race, the candidate who finished behind you now phones after the count. They congratulate you on the margin and ask for something or say what comes next, and you answer in one of four ways: take the call warmly, offer to meet, ask them to say it in public tonight, or wait for the county to finish counting. How they answer depends on who they are, and what you say is kept on the record between the two of you.
