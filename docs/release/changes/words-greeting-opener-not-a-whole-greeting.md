title: People no longer greet you twice when a talk opens
---

A talk could open with "Hi there." and then "Twice in two days." or "Good to see
you." and then "Funny running into you." Only a short hello ("Hello.", "Hi.",
"Hey.") now comes first and the rest follows, so a person greets you once.
