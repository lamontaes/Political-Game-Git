title: A bill's Compare strikes only the law in force
---

A bill's Compare view struck out a sentence that repealed rules that were not in force, and then underlined the same sentence as added, so you could not tell what the law said today. Compare now strikes only a rule that is in force, worded as that rule, and shows a new rule as added text alone.
