title: People give to causes from what they can spare, and groups raise from the whole town
---

What a person gives to a cause group now depends on how much cash they have beyond what they keep on hand, and how strongly they hold the cause, rather than a day's living costs every time. Givers average about what American households give to causes like these each year. Groups also raise money from the rest of the town, the people the game counts rather than follows, in proportion to how many of the town's followed people belong. A group whose month of spending falls short of the smallest ad an outlet sells saves toward it instead.
