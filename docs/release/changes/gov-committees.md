title: Legislatures send bills to the committee for their subject
---

A state legislature or Congress now sends each bill to the standing committee for what it does: finance, judiciary, education, health, labor and commerce, transportation, environment, housing or government. Leaders seat members on the committees their own interests run to, so different members hear different bills, under different chairs, instead of one "general" committee hearing everything.
