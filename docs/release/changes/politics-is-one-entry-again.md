title: Politics opens in one press again, and the budget names your town
---

A Map entry had been added beside Politics in the menu, which quietly turned
Politics into a folder you had to open first. The map was already a tab inside
Politics, so the extra entry is gone and Politics opens directly again.

The Issues and budget tab said it was showing public finances for "Here: Home"
when your character was at home. It now names the place, such as "Here: Alamo,
Nevada".
