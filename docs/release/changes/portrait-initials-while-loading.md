title: Portraits show initials while a face loads, and the time previews are readable
---

A portrait drawn by the people engine was a blank circle until its face finished loading, so the People web opened as rows of empty rings. It now shows the person's initials until the face appears. The previews beside Day, Week and Until needed (where each jump stops) were small brown text straight on the room picture; they now sit on the same paper as the buttons beside them.
