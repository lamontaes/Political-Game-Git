title: Every household in your town has a home, and people move
---

Before, nobody in town had a home on record, so every home in the game
looked like the same suburban house, and nobody ever bought, sold or moved
within town unless you did.

Now every household in your town, yours included, lives in one of six kinds
of home: a small apartment, a rowhouse, a suburban house, a large house, a
mobile home or a farmhouse. Each home is rented or owned. A city has more
apartments and rowhouses, and a small farm town has more farmhouses and
mobile homes. Young people living alone more often rent an apartment, and
families with children more often live in a house. Your own home now shows
the picture of its kind.

Every few months some neighbors buy a house, a family with children moves out
of an apartment, an older couple sells a house that has grown too big, and
renters move. Newcomers and people who move out after a breakup find a home,
often one somebody else left. A household that leaves town gives up its home
on the way out.
