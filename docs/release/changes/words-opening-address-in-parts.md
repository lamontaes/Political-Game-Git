title: The President's address plays in parts, one per Continue
---

The opening address now plays a paragraph at a time: the opening, the country's story, then the close, each on its own Continue. The President says what they believe in their own party's terms and, at an inauguration, that they serve those who voted against them too. After the country's numbers, they say what they mean to do about them: a new President never claims the last year's gains, and a rise in unemployment or prices is what they start with. Numbers are said the way people say them ("is at 4.4 percent").
