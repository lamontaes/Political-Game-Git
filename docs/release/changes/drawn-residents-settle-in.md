title: Council members and candidates drawn from town have homes and jobs
---

Before, a resident picked to sit on the town council, or to run in a town
election, sometimes came from a household that had no home and whose adults
had no jobs, unlike everyone else in town.

Now that household gets what every other household in town has: its
children in school, its working adults in jobs, and a home.
