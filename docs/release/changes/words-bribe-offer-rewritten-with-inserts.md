title: A bribe offer talks about the bill or contract it asks for, and you answer from where you stand
---

When someone offers you an envelope for a vote or a contract, they now talk about the bill or the contract itself and say how big the envelope is in words, and your replies are about the favor and your own side of it, instead of the same stock lines in every town.
