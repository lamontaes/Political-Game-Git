title: Filing for your own district counts your whole life there
---

A lifelong resident could be told on the office list that they could stand for
their state legislative seat, then be refused when they filed for their own
district with "lived here 1 month". The list dated how long they had lived in
the district from their household record; the filing dated it from the day the
world was written. The filing now dates the district the world recorded for
their home the same way the list does, so the two agree. Found in Waterbury,
Nebraska, and in Massachusetts, Minnesota and Alaska lives.
