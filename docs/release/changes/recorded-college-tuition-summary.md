title: College tuition summaries retain the recorded annual remainder
---

College progression summaries use the same saved school-price reader as tuition billing. The next installment includes the final term's minor-unit remainder, and the quoted program total sums all installments rather than multiplying the rounded regular amount.

Recorded tuition caps still apply through the existing reader. Accepted terms, enrollment, payments, and legacy paths without recorded school billing remain unchanged.
