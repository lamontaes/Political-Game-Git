title: Choose what stops passing time from beside the Day and Week buttons
---

A Stops button now sits beside Day and Week. It opens a checklist of what
interrupts passing time: work shifts and invitations you have not answered can
be switched on or off, and the stops that always apply are shown as checked.
It is the same list as the Calendar's Interruptions tab, so a change in one
shows in the other.
