title: A law's page says what it did to the state, and Conditions shows how the state is doing
---

The world tracks how each state is doing: the share without health insurance,
poverty, test scores, turnout, homelessness, violent crime and more. It also
records every law that moves them. Until now only the developer's world report
read those records, so a law that lowered homelessness changed nothing a
player could see.

A law's own page now says what it is doing to each condition it moves, for
example: "People homeless per 10,000 people in Derby Line, Vermont: 57 per
10,000 people when the law took effect, 38.1 per 10,000 people now. This law
lowers it by about 37.8%." A law that another law has replaced says so, and a
law whose effect comes later names the month it starts.

Politics, then Issues and budget, now has a Conditions page. It shows each
condition in the player's state as it stands now and where it began. It
names what is moving each one, and puts the ones a law is moving first.
Reading it passes no time.
