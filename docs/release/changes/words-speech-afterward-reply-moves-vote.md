title: Telling a candidate "you've got my vote" after their speech now counts as your word in the race.
---

When a candidate comes over after a speech you heard in person and you say yes, that is now kept as your word to them, as a yes at your door is, and the ballot reads it. Before, the reply was only a conversation.
