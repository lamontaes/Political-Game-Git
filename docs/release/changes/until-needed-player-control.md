title: Time controls include Until needed beside Day and Week
---

The play screen now offers Until needed with Day and Week at the bottom right. Before a click, it shows the next known stopping date from the existing quiet-stretch command. The same command runs the player's ordinary routine and returns at a protected commitment or new need; the result says where time actually stopped. A work offer reply or accepted work start already due leaves Until needed unavailable with a Work-directed explanation, while Day and Week remain deliberate choices.
