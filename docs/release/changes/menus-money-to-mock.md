title: Money shows your cash in large type, each month as a column, and where last month went as a ring
---

The Money screen was one line of cash on an empty panel and lists of amounts. It now shows your cash in large type, your household's cash at each monthly review as a column, the household's last month as a ring with each reason's share, your home with its worth or rent, and what you own and owe as small plates, all on framed plates, with nothing scrolling. Amounts are rounded to whole dollars for reading, and figures that are the household's say so.
