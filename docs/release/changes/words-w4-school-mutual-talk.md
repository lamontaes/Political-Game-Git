impact: minor
section: Changed
title: People you both know come up with what happened to them, and an old school by name and years.
---

When someone brings up a person you both know, they say why: what happened to that person and when, from the records ("Did you hear about Alex? Moved over to Hawaii County.", "Have you run into Sonia Reese lately? Out in Lenawee County now, I hear."). You hear each piece of news once. Talk about a school you both went to names the school or how many years it has been ("It's been 22 years since Lincoln High School."). The old fixed lines ("That was a long time ago.", "Those were the days.", "Why do you ask?") and the answers that jumped from a friend to work or home are gone.
