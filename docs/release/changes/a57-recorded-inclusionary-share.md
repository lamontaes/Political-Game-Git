title: Inclusionary housing uses the enacted affordable share
---

New affordable-home allocations now use the governing law's recorded share
instead of a universal fifteen percent. Adopted amendments determine the
allocation, and the affordable rent record retains their source chain.
Missing or mistyped shares do not invent an affordable-unit requirement.
Existing affordable leases remain intact.

Starting D.C. and New Jersey laws now carry cited share estimates. Existing
opening stock does not count as new construction in the set-aside allocation.
