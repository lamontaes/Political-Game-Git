title: The national economy now swings on its own, with recessions and recoveries
---

The national economy no longer settles flat. Spending carries its own momentum, hits capacity limits and answers last year's interest rate, so every world has booms and downturns of its own length and depth, about four recessions in 37 years like the real country. Towns feel it through their customers' demand.

Prices now swing too: energy's price answers demand and capacity that takes years to build, so inflation moves in booms and busts instead of sitting near two percent.
