title: Amendment ballots are decided by the voters' own views
---

When a state's legislature refers a policy amendment to the voters, the result
now comes from what the state's eligible residents actually think about that
policy: each supporter or opponent counts once, and an even split fails. When
nobody on record holds a view, no result is invented and the amendment waits.
Term-limit amendments wait for now, since no one can hold a recorded view on
them yet.
