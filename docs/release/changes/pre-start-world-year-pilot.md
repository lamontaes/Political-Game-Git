title: New Game can let the world live a year before your character begins
---

An optional New Game choice now lets a supported ordinary-life start advance a prior-date World through one canonical year before the chosen player exists in it. At Begin, the chosen person and their earlier history join the saved world at the requested age. The choice starts off, and unsupported office starts and boundary ages show the source reason before Begin.
