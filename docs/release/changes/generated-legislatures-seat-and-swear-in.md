title: Winning a legislative seat in Maine, Georgia and other generated states now seats you, and swearing-in is an event
---

A player who won a state House or Senate seat in a state whose legislature the
game generates, rather than one of the nine it has written out, was never
seated. This was true in Maine and Georgia, among others. They saw no
"Before you take office" section, the seat never began, and Your office read
"You hold no office in this life yet" beside a House role. Now these winners
wait for the term like everyone else and are seated on its first day. Their
office opens onto the legislature's work.

Swearing-in is now something the player does. From the first day of the term,
a legislator or governor sees a Swearing-in section that describes the
ceremony. Taking the oath is recorded as a public event. The ceremony follows
a general description until each state's own is researched. Not taking the
oath does not yet withhold the office.
