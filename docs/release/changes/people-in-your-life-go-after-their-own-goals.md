title: People in your life go after their own goals while time passes
---

The people around a life now act on their own private goals each week, whether
or not the player looks at them. Somebody in town whose job ends starts looking
for work: they apply to openings the town's employers actually listed, hear back
on the day the employer answers, and take the job and start it, or keep
looking. Family, housemates and the people the player works with are given the
same ordinary goals every generated person already had, and act on them: a
person keeping up with people calls somebody they actually know, and the person
called decides for themselves whether to talk. A step is never taken twice, and
opening a screen changes nothing.

When a step cannot be taken, the reason is recorded once, as a fact: no opening
is listed, or nobody they know teaches. A learning goal with no route at all is
set aside after a long stretch, and the person turns to the people around them
instead. The earlier goal and its history stay.

The player learns about this the way anybody would. A housemate or relative who
applies somewhere mentions it, and the Journal records what they said. Nothing
shows another person's private goal on a card.

Somebody keeping time for themselves is also a little more likely to say no to
an optional plan, including one the player asks them to.
