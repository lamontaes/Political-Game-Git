title: The Jobs screen says the lowest legal pay where you live and which law set it
---

The Jobs screen now opens with the lowest pay the law allows where you live. It
says whether federal or state law sets the rate. After a raise is enacted, it
names the new rate, the rate it replaced and the day it took effect. It reads the
same rate paychecks use, so the line and the pay agree. Where a state's rate is
not known, the line is left out instead of showing zero.
