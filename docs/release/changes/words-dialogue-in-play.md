title: All the dialogue is in the game, and only the lines you rejected stay out
---

Scenes, conversations, the journal and the news now use every line the
English engine has, not only the few that were graded. People with a reason to
talk to you say it in ordinary play, and the campaign speech screen offers its
four choices at every stage again. A line you graded bad is left out
everywhere at once, and each new round of grades reaches the whole game.
