title: An officeholder's card no longer says they work at a treasury
---

A President's, senator's, governor's or legislator's card told the seat's own pay record as a job ("Works as president at US.", "Works as legislature-upper at Rhode Island treasury."). The card now tells only the office and since when, and a U.S. office reads "U.S. senator" in a sentence.
