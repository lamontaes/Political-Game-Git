title: An official can be left an offer, with five ways to answer it
---

When someone who wants a vote or a contract leaves something for an official, the official now has a scene to play: five different answers (take it, hand it back, say it will be reported, put it off, or play along), the offerer pressing, pleading or threatening in reply, and an ending for each. The scene waits for the corruption engine to record offers as events, so it does not appear in play yet.
