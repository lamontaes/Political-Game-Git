title: When the body you sit on votes on your ordinance, the paper prints it.
---

A local body's vote on a bill, the council's or the county board's, carried or failed, is now weighed by the paper as news for the whole town, as a law taking effect is. A paper covers a meeting whole: a reporter who writes up one of the body's votes writes up the others it took that day, so your ordinance isn't crowded out by the bigger votes beside it. When you sponsor an ordinance, the local paper prints how the vote went within days, and hundreds of people in town read it.
