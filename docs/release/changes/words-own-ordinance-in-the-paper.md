title: When your council votes on your ordinance, the paper prints it.
---

A town body's vote on a bill, carried or failed, is now weighed by the paper as news for the whole town, as a law taking effect is. When you sponsor an ordinance, the local paper prints how the vote went within days, and hundreds of people in town read it.
