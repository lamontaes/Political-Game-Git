title: Turning eighteen ends a parent's authority wherever the character lives
impact: patch
---

A character who came of age kept both parents' authority over them forever, because no place had an age of majority on record. All 56 places now carry one from the Defense Department's age-of-majority table: nineteen in Alabama and Nebraska, twenty-one in Mississippi and Puerto Rico, and eighteen elsewhere, with American Samoa and the Northern Mariana Islands estimated at the common age. The authority ends on that birthday, and a guardian is then "the guardian who raised you".
