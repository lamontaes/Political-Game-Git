title: A scene says it is evening only when it plays in the evening.
---

Scenes stop your day when their cause is recorded, usually in the morning, and some of their lines said "tonight" anyway ("Thanks for your time tonight."). A line that says the present is the evening now plays only in the evening, a morning greeting only before noon, and a line that says the afternoon is still ahead only before the evening. Promises for later tonight ("I'll read it tonight.") play at any hour.
