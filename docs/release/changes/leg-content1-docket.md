title: Carry a real docket of bills, and keep working them week to week.
---

A legislator's desk now holds more than one measure at a time. Bills are drawn from composable families, so a docket can hold several unrelated ones in a single life and each still knows what it is. Filing checks the authority the life actually holds and refuses plainly when it does not hold it, and the work carries forward instead of resetting.
