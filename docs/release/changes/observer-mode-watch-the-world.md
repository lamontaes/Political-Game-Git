title: Watch the world with nobody played
---

The title screen has a new button, Watch the world. One press opens a real
town in a state picked at random, with nobody played in it, and the world goes
on by itself. Its people work, die and hold elections, its parties meet, and
its papers print the news.

While you watch, you can let a day, a week, a month or a year pass, or press
Run and let it go on week after week until you press Pause. The World record
lists every bill and what became of it, every constitutional amendment, every
election and who won it, who holds office, and every living person, with the
news and what has happened lately. Choosing a person shows their whole record:
their work, their party and everything that has happened to them, including
things nobody else in the world knows.

You can save a watched world, and it comes back as a watched world. The save
list shows it as "Watching the world", not under the name of the resident the
town was opened around.

What a watched world does not have yet: nobody in it introduces bills,
ordinances or amendments without a player, so the laws section stays empty for
now. That work is under way elsewhere, and the record will fill in as it
lands.
