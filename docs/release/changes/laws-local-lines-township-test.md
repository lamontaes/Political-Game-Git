A New England town's select board (a township body) now amends its government's budget lines: its bills and the budget they change were looked up by the township's law jurisdiction, which named no government, so they never found it. One lookup (`governmentOfJurisdiction`) finds the government by id or by its government key for the budget amendment, the line filing and the bill-values reader.
