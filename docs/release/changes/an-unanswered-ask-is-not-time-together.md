title: An ask nobody answered no longer reads as time spent together
---

In a San Antonio life, James last saw Stella Shaw in January 2039, yet for two
years his Journal kept saying "You and Stella Shaw were in and out of each
other's business more than once." Stella had only asked to
meet, and the day passed without an answer. The game also writes a note that
it offered the scene, and that note was being counted as something the two of
them did together. It no longer counts. The line now reads "Stella Shaw tried
to reach you," and it says "more than once" only when she asked more than once.
