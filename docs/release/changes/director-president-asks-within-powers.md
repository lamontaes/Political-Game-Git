title: The President asks Congress only for what Congress can do
---

The opening address no longer asks Congress to change a state's law, or to keep a law your state already has. Each ask now goes to the level that can answer it: Congress for a federal question federal law does not yet answer, and the governors for a state law your state answers the other way from the President.
