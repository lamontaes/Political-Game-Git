title: A resident's visit to your office hours is a scene
---

When a resident brings you a problem at office hours, out of work with their
savings running thin or paid under the minimum wage, they come to your office
and say it in their own words. You can promise the referral the office really
makes (the workforce office or the labor standards office), ask more, or turn
them away. The visit is on the office's record, and the scene stands on it.
