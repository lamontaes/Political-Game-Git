title: Every state legislature has members from the first day
---

A new game now seats a full legislature in every state, not only your own, and
a council for your city and county. Each member is a person with a district
where one can be named, a party, and principles of their own, so their votes
follow what they believe. The News lists the nation's officeholders and your
own state's, not every state's governor.

Starting a new life takes a few seconds longer while the legislatures are
prepared, and the loading screen shows the progress.
