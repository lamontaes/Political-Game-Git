title: Election day stops at the polls, where you mark your ballot
---

On the eve of an election you can vote in, your day now stops at the polling place, with the members of your household who can vote. You mark each race on the ballot, or leave it blank, and the count on election day records what you marked. Election day is in Options among the scenes that stop your day; take it out and your character votes on their own.
