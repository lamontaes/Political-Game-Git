id: another-day-reaches-the-asker
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: When somebody offers another day, you see it and can answer
---

Before, when somebody answered an invitation or a date with "not that day,
but another," the player saw nothing: no reply, and no day offered back.
Asking again got the same silence. Now the other day shows on the People
screen as their question to answer, and a yes to a date says so.
