id: state-judicial-seating
impact: none
---

Adds a saved-state join from a completed state judicial selection to the
canonical seat tenure. It carries recorded selection provenance, computes a
known fixed term, closes an expired or deceased predecessor, and refuses an
incomplete or repeated selection. It creates no candidate, election, or
appointment decision.

This remains a draft boundary. A state executive stage checks the saved
governor; other stages do not yet prove commission or confirming-body
membership. No ordinary-player state-selection caller or state election
outcome adapter exists yet. These gaps must close before a playable
appointment route.
