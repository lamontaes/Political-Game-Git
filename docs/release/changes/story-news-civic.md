title: The paper now covers protests, rallies, walkouts and your governor's and mayor's addresses.

A protest or walkout in your town is a story with a headline, the number of people and what it was over, and so is a rally. The governor's and the mayor's yearly addresses are covered too, with how the speaker framed the year and a line they said.
