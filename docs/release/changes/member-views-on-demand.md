---
id: member-views-on-demand
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: State legislatures weigh floor amendments faster again
---

Before offering a floor amendment, a legislator asks how two members would
vote. Since town councils joined the same vote engine, answering that worked
out every member's own view first, so each question cost a whole chamber's
thought. A member's view is now worked out only when that member votes, or
when a colleague who trusts them asks how they voted. In South Fork,
Pennsylvania, game years one to five take 0.9, 3.8, 4.7, 6.0 and 3.7 seconds
less. Every vote comes out the same: the world after each of five years is
exactly the same as before.
