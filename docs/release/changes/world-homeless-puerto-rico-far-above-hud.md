title: Rents in a town the county tables leave out come from its own state
---

A town whose county the Census tables do not place, as some towns in Puerto Rico are, now starts its rents, home values and owner share from its own state's or territory's counties, not from the nation's. In one Puerto Rico town that halved the rent and ended a year in which one renter in twenty was put out on the street.
