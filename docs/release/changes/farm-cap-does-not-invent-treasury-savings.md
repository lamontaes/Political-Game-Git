title: Farm subsidy caps no longer invent savings in the federal forecast
---

A farm subsidy cap no longer subtracts a historical percentage from the federal agriculture forecast. Saved farm-authorized commitments now pay the recorded recipient through the existing cash writer, limited by the adopted annual dollar cap after that recipient's actual same-year payments. Budget outturn uses transferred cash, and payment records retain the law and term sources across Save/Continue. Ordinary farm eligibility and recipient-commitment production remains unsupported; the game does not invent recipients from aggregate research.
