title: Homes look like the homes people live in.
---

Every household now has its own home: houses, rowhouses, farmhouses, mobile homes and apartments each have several living rooms, from a rundown walk-up to a nice apartment and from a plain suburban living room to a large house, chosen by what the home is worth or rents for in its town. People are in the room that fits the hour: the kitchen at breakfast and supper, the living room in the day and evening, their own bedroom at night, and a teenager's bedroom for a teenager.
