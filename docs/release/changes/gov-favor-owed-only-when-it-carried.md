title: A colleague who did you a favor no longer does it twice
---

A colleague who said yes after you pleaded now counts as someone you owe, and the same plea no longer works on them again until you have paid it back. A colleague who said yes on their own is owed nothing. Dev mode's Trace shows who owes whom and what the chair's lean on a held bill added up from.
