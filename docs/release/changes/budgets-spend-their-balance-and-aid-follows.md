section: Changed
title: Governments spend their unreserved balance, and a city's state aid follows its state
---

Each new budget now carries the balance a government holds above its reserve
target into the year and spends it across the year's programs, once, instead
of letting it pile up untouched. A county's or city's aid from its state now
rises and falls with what the state actually spends on aid to local
governments, so a state's mid-year cut reaches its cities the same month and
a state's growing budget raises their aid.
