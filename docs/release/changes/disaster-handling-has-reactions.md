title: How a governor handles a disaster now wins or loses voters, and family and party react
---

A governor who asks for federal help when a disaster calls for it gains
support in a race under way and starts later races slightly ahead. One who
declines, or lets the request window close, loses support and starts behind.
The played governor's family and party organizers read what happened and
criticize, defend or praise it, and their relationship changes to match. The
sizes are placeholders until they are researched.
