title: Party chairs and committee regulars weigh their role when deciding to run
---

A person who chairs a party in town, or has sat in on its committee meetings in the last year, now counts that role as a reason to run for office, as people who already serve are the usual pipeline to local office. Before, no one in the world held a civic role at all. In three test towns a few more races drew a second or third candidate, and none was left empty.
