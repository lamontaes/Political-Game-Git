title: Scenes go only to someone who could be in them.
---

A parent or sibling who lives with you no longer "comes to the house" or knocks at your door with a request: someone who comes, calls or asks lives apart from you, and a scene that would need it goes to your journal instead. A scene set at a legislative seat or in a chamber now reaches you only when you hold an office.
