id: see-it-journal
section: Changed
title: The Journal shows your own court case and the laws the place began with
impact: patch
---

Your Journal now carries your own charge, plea, verdict, sentence, pretrial hold or release, and the suspension of your vote, in the court's recorded words. It also names a law the place began with when that law reached you, using the policy question's own name. Referrals, declined charges and the prosecutor's review stay out, because the court does not tell the defendant about them. A view a friend told you about an official no longer appears in the Journal as a raw record string.
