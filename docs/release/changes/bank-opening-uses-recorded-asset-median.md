title: A town bank's opening profile comes from recorded assets
---

New town bank books use the state's observed median-asset small bank instead of
a drawn profile. Its cushion, other assets and uninsured deposits stay paired
with that bank's report. Existing bank books keep their original profile.
Places with fewer than five observations wait for sufficient evidence.
