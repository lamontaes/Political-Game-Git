---
id: tuition-freeze-holds-state-tuition
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A state tuition freeze lowers what the state's colleges collect
---

When a state passes a law freezing in-state tuition at public colleges, each
school year that begins with the freeze in force holds tuition where the year
before left it instead of letting it rise 3.1%. The state budget's charges and
fees come in lower by the share of them that is college tuition in that state,
and the next budget is set on the lower revenue. A later law ending the freeze
lets tuition rise again from the next school year.
