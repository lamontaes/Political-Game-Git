title: Talk with the party organizer about running
---

Going to a planned talk about running for office now opens the community room
with its organizer present. You can ask about qualifications or filing, stay
through the conversation, or leave and return home. The visit and the words
you heard remain in your history. A time advance that stops at a calendar
commitment now names that commitment.
