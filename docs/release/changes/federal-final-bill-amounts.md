---
id: federal-final-bill-amounts
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Federal spending readers use the amounts in adopted bill text
---

Federal spending readers now use the final annual amounts written in a bill, including recorded starting law terms. They no longer substitute the size of an old debt-limit deal, a historical defense growth rate, or an old rail ridership forecast. Missing amounts, recipients, or delivery records remain unsupported. A defense appropriation is compared with a complete recorded year of federal defense spending.

The preserved federal defense reader now records the bill’s annual target against its complete saved spending base, with its law attribution. Debt offsets use adopted dollars over recorded total spending. Federal categories retain their existing order and exports through one shared list.

Foreign aid now uses its adopted annual amount over the recorded International Affairs base. Debt-limit offsets use the adopted amount over recorded eligible categories, including valid zero-spending lines. Their annual difference restores the deficit-to-borrowing link using the existing sourced GDP denominator. Final annual rail terms reach the existing appropriation writer and payment ledger, with the same federal account and rail program key. Authority is recorded separately from actual cash and completed service.
