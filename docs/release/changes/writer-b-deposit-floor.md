The floor scene can identify whether a single-question bill would start a policy or end a law the debating government has in force. Members can refer to people the committee heard on the bill and the side recorded for each witness. The draft includes floor questions about how customers learn the last return date and how a dealer collects the final reimbursement. Members can voice a reservation and hear what the sponsor would check before a vote. The draft links sponsor statements to questions, agreements and objections about the same concern, from the space behind a grocery counter to the price a school club can get for its cans. A member can also leave the floor to the sponsor while naming the part of the argument they want to hear out. The live debate closes when the bill fails or the recorded chamber has voted.

The review draft follows the store’s final claim and a school club’s trip budget through the member’s decision to hear the sponsor out.
