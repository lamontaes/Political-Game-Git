title: Every state has its own laws from the first day, not from the second year.
---

Outside the state you play in, most states ran the game's first year with no unemployment insurance, no corporate income tax and, for many, no minimum wage of their own, then got them all on January 1, 2026. Every state now opens under its own law, so the country no longer lurches at the start of the second year.
