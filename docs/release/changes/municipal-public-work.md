title: Public meetings and private preparation for municipal members.
---

Residents of supported municipalities can inspect government records and attend
public sessions created in their saved world. Members can prepare private meeting
notes through scheduled work. Attendance and completed notes survive saving and
reloading. The records distinguish researched government facts from sessions
written for the game, and show when the sources do not establish a power.
