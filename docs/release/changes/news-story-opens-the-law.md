title: A news story about a bill opens the bill's page
---

A story on the News front page that reports on a bill now names the bill under
the story, and the name opens the bill's own page, where what the bill did once
it became law is written. A law's page that another law has replaced now names
the law that decides the question instead of saying "another law".
