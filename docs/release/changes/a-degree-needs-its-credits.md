title: A degree needs its credits, not only the years paid for
---

Paying for every term no longer hands you the degree by itself. Each term you
take a share of the credits your program requires, and how many you earn
depends on the hours you already owe to work and care that week. If you are
short at the end of the planned terms, you keep studying, and paying, one term
at a time until you have the credits, up to half again the program's length.
Past that, your studies end without the degree.
