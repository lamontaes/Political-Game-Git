title: Every story the press prints has words, and interviews name what they were about
---

Some stories in News had no words at all: a reporter's story that an official took a bribe, a seat in another state left empty, and interviews about a budget line. They now read, for example, "Records show Jonathan Moseley took a bribe while in office." and "A U.S. Senator from Minnesota has died in office, and the seat is empty." Interviews now say what was asked about in plain words: "We asked Shane Randall about a higher minimum age to buy a handgun." instead of "about raise handgun purchase age".
