The English engine's core arrives on main without a caller in play. It holds a
fact packet (facts with the records they came from, what the speaker and the
reader know, and their traits), reviewed banks of line variants with slots, the
facts, knowledge and traits each variant needs, a stable choice for the same
moment after a reload, and a refusal when a needed fact is missing. It also
builds the packet for a saved work start, which the Journal will read.

Nothing changes for a player yet. The callers that put these lines on screen
(the My life Journal, spoken conversation words, the political bargaining lines,
news reading and first family introductions) edit files other lanes own, and
they land once that ownership moves to the English engine.
