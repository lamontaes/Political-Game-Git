title: Answer known meeting questions from recorded facts
---

A recorded meeting chair can speak even when no council agenda is recorded.
When a speaker agrees to answer a factual question, the existing English engine
uses the speaker's supported knowledge or participation to answer that question.
An unsupported agenda does not become invented business, and saved conversations
retain their original words.
