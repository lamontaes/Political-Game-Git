title: A party's chair now speaks when they ask you to run, and the party's leader speaks when you join.
---

When a party's chair comes to ask you to run for a seat nobody on their side has filed for, the talk now has lines: the chair names the seat and the party, answers what running takes, and takes a yes, a no or a "let me think" and says what comes next. When you join a party, its leader welcomes you at the party's office, asks whether they can count on you for an evening or two, tells you what the party holds or runs in your town if you ask, and takes a yes, a put-off or a goodbye. Before, both talks had no lines to say.
