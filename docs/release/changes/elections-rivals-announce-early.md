section: Changed
title: Other candidates announce before filing opens.
---

The town's other candidates no longer wait for the filing counter to say they are running. Once someone decides to run, they weigh saying so early from their own life: how much they want it, how many people know them, whether the seat is open, the party and civic roles they hold, and how close filing is. Those who announce show on the race page under who's running, marked Announced, and count on your campaign board, then file when the window opens.
