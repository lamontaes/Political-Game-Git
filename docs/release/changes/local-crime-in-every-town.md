title: Towns now have ordinary crime
---

Towns now have crime. Assaults, robberies, home burglaries and vandalism
happen in the background each month. A reported crime goes into the town's
police log, where the local paper can pick it up, and the victim is never
named. An unreported one stays private to the people it happened to. The month
after a report, the police either make an arrest or they don't.

Every rate is a placeholder and is the same in every town, so a village is as
busy as a city for now. Crime does not yet change anyone's vote, and nobody is
charged or tried yet.
