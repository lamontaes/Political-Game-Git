title: People think over a bill they meet, and form a view from the principles they hold
---

A member who files a bill, or somebody you tell about a bill in the news, has now met the questions it puts, and thinks them over, sometimes the next day, sometimes within a week, and sometimes not until something later brings the question back: the bill being signed, vetoed or dying, or a serious illness at home bringing back questions about health care. A view forms only from the principles they hold that the question engages, and is held no more firmly than those principles are. Each adult also cares about a few issues, and a view on one of them matters more to them. Nobody holds a principle yet in an ordinary game, so for now people consider what they meet and form no view; where principles come from and which issues people care about are open research questions, and the handful of issues is a marked placeholder until they are answered.
