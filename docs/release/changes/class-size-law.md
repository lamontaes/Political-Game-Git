title: A state law can cap class sizes, and smaller classes lift how many pupils read and count at grade level
---

A state can now pass a law setting the most pupils allowed in a class. Each fall, your town's school district hires teachers from people in town who want work until its classes fit the cap. The yearly count names the law behind every new teacher and lists any teaching jobs it couldn't fill. Families whose children's school gained a teacher hear about the law.

Each count also reports how many pupils are at grade level. Smaller classes raise that share, and larger ones lower it.
