title: A new life in a territory no longer reads "No reviewed state identity"
---

A new life in Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the Northern
Mariana Islands opened on a territory card that ended with "No reviewed state
identity matches this selection." under its voting section. A territory's card
now leaves the voting section out, Puerto Rico's included, since the numbers it
shows are for the fifty states and the District of Columbia.
