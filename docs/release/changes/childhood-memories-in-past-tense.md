title: Childhood moments are remembered in the past tense
---

The choices you make as a child were saved in the present tense ("You ask Riley
how they know the story is true"), so the Journal switched tense in the middle of
a paragraph. They now read as memories: "You asked Riley how they knew the story
was true." People also "call" each other now instead of "ringing" each other.
