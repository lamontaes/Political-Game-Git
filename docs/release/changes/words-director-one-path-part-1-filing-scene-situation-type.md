title: The clerk at the filing counter talks with you about the race, and a rival who filed comes up
---

At the filing counter, once you name the race, the clerk tells you about it in one breath (who else is in, the term, the primary and the general, what the office does, the day your papers are due), and you can file, ask who else has filed, raise a rival who already filed, or leave to think it over. Filing ends the talk with the clerk taking your papers; the next day the clerk finds your filing on the desk and goes over the petition, without taking the papers a second time.
