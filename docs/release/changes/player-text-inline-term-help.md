title: Read what a term means without leaving the screen
---

Institutional words like committee referral, appropriation and quorum now carry
their explanation where you meet them. Open one and a short definition appears
beside the word, with a way through to its full entry in the Guide. It works
from the keyboard and with a screen reader, and it never covers the sentence you
were reading. Every definition is the Guide's own, so a term reads the same
everywhere it appears.
