title: People talk about a charge, a conviction or an acquittal
---

When someone in your circle is charged with a crime, convicted or acquitted, they can bring it up with you, and you can ask them about it, in your own words: comfort them, promise to stand by them, ask what happened, or tell them they brought it on themselves. If it is you who was charged, a friend asks and you can own up, deflect, refuse or apologize.
