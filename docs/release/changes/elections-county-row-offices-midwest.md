title: Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan and Ohio county officers follow their state's law.
---

County officers in Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan and Ohio are now the ones each state's constitution or code has the voters elect, with their four-year terms. An Indiana county elects its Clerk of the circuit court and an Ohio county its Clerk of the court of common pleas under those names, and Michigan and Ohio counties elect a Prosecuting attorney.
