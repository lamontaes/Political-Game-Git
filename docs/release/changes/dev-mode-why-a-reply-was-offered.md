title: Dev mode shows why a scene offered each line
---

In dev mode, a scene with an exchange now shows, under the talk, what the scene knows with the records each fact comes from, and for each line said and each reply offered, the facts that placed it, what it follows and the other wordings that fit as closely. Every record opens in the Trace box. A reason a member gives for a vote also cites that member's own vote, so its parts and their sum are one click from the reply.
