title: A new game in a small place with no trade beyond its edge no longer stops while setting up the government's money.
---

The opening treasury's money comes from outside the place. Where no household or business had paid anything outside the town yet (Putnamville, Indiana), the game stopped before day one. The treasury now opens the town's one outside account, the same one a campaign's first outside purchase opens.
