title: Journal chapters open only on your own turns or a relative's, never on someone else's oath or a stranger's birth.
---

The Journal used to start a new chapter, titled "Commissioner" or with a stranger's name, when someone you knew took office or a neighbor had a baby. Those moments are now told inside the chapter you are living. A relative's birth or death still opens a chapter, and a death's chapter names them by your tie ("My father").
