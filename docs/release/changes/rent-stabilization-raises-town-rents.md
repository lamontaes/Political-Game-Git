section: Changed
title: Rent stabilization raises the town's market rents a year after it comes in
---

A town or state that brings in rent stabilization now sees its landlords
answer it: from a year after the law takes effect, every market rent in town
is written 5.1% higher, the San Francisco measure, while a covered tenant's
renewal is still held to the cap. Repealing a rent stabilization law the
place began with lowers market rents the same way. The watched-world
report's list of state laws now also names the code that acts on each law.
