Adds the map that tells the game what each part of a bill should change, and
which level of government may change what. Nothing changes for a player yet:
these are the tables the next steps build from, once they are approved.

The lever table sorts all 61 kinds of bill part found in 220 real enacted
laws into six levers (money, rate, who qualifies, rule, structure and
process), with the record each one changes and how its fiscal note is
figured. Today 7 of the 61 have a partial writer and 54 have none. The powers
catalog gives 66 dials across the 18 subject areas at eight levels, each
marked sourced, a labeled game profile, or unknown with the question that
would settle it. The placeholder ledger lists all 258 placeholder values in
the code, 155 of them on the paths that move money or make law.
