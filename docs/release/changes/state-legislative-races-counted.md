title: Every state legislative race records its nominees and a vote count
---

A state legislature's regular seats were decided from each seat's lean plus
a seeded swing, with no ballot and no count anyone could read. Now each seat
whose nominees come from different parties gets a ballot 45 days before
election day, counted on election day from the seat's own voters: its
starting lean, the sitting member's usual edge, and a share of the state's
ballots divided evenly among the chamber's seats. The count's winner takes
the seat, and the seeded swing is gone. The world report sums each
legislature's round in one line, names every sitting member who lost, and
draws a random place from the seed when none is named.
