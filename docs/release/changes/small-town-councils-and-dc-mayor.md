title: Small towns no longer get big-city councils, and D.C.'s mayor shows as its mayor
---

A town of fewer than 2,500 people no longer draws a council of eight or more seats; its council size comes from the smaller councils real towns have. In a watched world's vital statistics, D.C.'s executive row now reads "Mayor of the District of Columbia's party," and the mayor row names the same person instead of saying no mayor is recorded.
