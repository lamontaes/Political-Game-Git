title: After public comment on a bill, a resident who spoke comes to you, as a member, about the bill and the side they took.
---

When you sit on a council or board and residents speak at public comment before a vote, one of them may catch you afterward ("You heard what I said about Ordinance 2025-3. I'm asking you to vote for it."). What you answer is kept: you can stand by your view, give ground, offer to talk later, or brush them off.
