title: Who is in a scene is decided once, and a home's rooms match
---

Everyone standing in a scene is there for a reason the story director recorded when it cast the scene (they live there, they work there, the moment is about them, they were asked); the screen draws those people and decides no one of its own. Every room of one home is now shown in the same condition, so a run-down living room no longer sits beside a spotless kitchen.
