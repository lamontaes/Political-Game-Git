title: Teacher salary floors read the saved law's annual amount
---

A teacher salary floor now comes from the law's recorded annual amount,
including a starting law, rather than a drawn share of a wage survey.
Missing amounts do not become an estimated legal floor. The starting-law
records include the $50,000 amounts already cited for Arkansas and Iowa.
