---
id: a39-adopted-wage-terms
impact: patch
---

Federal, state, and authorized city minimum-wage changes now use the hourly
amount in the adopted law. A yes answer without a numeric amount supplies no
new wage floor. The invented federal amount, average state raise, and city
premium are removed. Existing recorded hourly rules retain their effective
dates; no yearly increment is inferred from an unanswered bill amount.
