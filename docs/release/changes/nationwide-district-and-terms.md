title: The District of Columbia has its own mayor, and short-term states keep short terms
---

A life in the District of Columbia now opens under the District's own chief
executive — a mayor, under the District's own Code, not a fifty-first governor.
The District is one government, so everything district-wide and everything
citywide names the same jurisdiction: one office, one public account, no
second mayor beside the first.

Every state's and the District's ordinary term length now comes from research
that was actually checked for that jurisdiction, so New Hampshire and Vermont
run two-year terms and elect every two years instead of inheriting a four-year
default nobody had read. The rule the game applies is still the game's own
disclosed rule, and the office now says which part of it was read and which was
not.
