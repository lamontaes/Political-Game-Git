title: A charged player enters their own plea
---

Once prosecutors charge the player, the player can enter a plea before the
hearing. A guilty plea is taken at the hearing and ends the case there, and
the judge's sentence follows. A plea of not guilty, or no plea at all, sends
the case to a jury. The player's case appears once they are charged, with the
date of the hearing, any plea entered and how the case ended; a case that
prosecutors are still weighing stays out of sight.
