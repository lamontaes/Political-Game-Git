title: A long life's memories no longer go blank after the parents die
---

An older life whose parents died and who had no brothers or sisters used to
show no family memories for decades before the start. Those years now fall to
the life's own partner and child. The fixed template family is gone.

A save made before this still opens. A world rebuilt from an older setup now
draws its family the new way.
