title: Who you are describes a middling trait in words, not "Neither, much"
---

A trait with no strong lean used to read "Neither, much" on Who you are, the same two words for every such trait. Each now says what you tend to do: "Usually keeps a promise", "At ease alone or in a crowd", "Picks which fights to have", "Takes a chance when it seems worth it" or "Weighs the big choices, not the small ones".
