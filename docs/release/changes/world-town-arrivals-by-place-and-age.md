title: Your town no longer loses people to moving year after year
---

A played town could lose 2 to 5 percent of its residents in a year, then gain as many the next, because newcomers to the area were sent to one place at a time by the luck of a draw and a large neighboring place blocked the rest. Newcomers now arrive in each place by its share of the people their age, the same way leavers go, so a town's arrivals match what is due to it within a household each year. Families with children now arrive in proportion to the children due, so a town keeps its school-age residents.
