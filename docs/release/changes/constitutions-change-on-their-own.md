title: State constitutions can change without the player
---

Every state can now amend its constitution through its own legislature and
voters, not only California. Once a year each governorship in the world is
reviewed, and when there is a reason on record (a governor barred from
another term, or one who has served three or more), the legislature may
propose changing the governor's term limit. It happens rarely. A proposal
goes through each chamber and then to the voters at a November general
election. If it is ratified, the next governor election follows the new
limit.

Governor elections now follow whichever term limit is actually in force,
enacted or default, instead of a fixed two terms.

Law now ranks by level: the federal Constitution, federal law, a state's
constitution, state law, then local charters and ordinances. A higher level
governs a lower one on the same rule. What the game does not model yet (home
rule, compacts, how often amendments really happen and why) is marked as a
default and has gone to research.
