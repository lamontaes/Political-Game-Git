title: Campaign money no longer leans on press people who had no bills to pay.
---

Reporters and news directors who live outside your town now have the living costs and savings an official at their pay has, and they spend them each week. Before, they had no bills at all, so their whole balance counted as spare and they gave nearly half of a campaign's money, $1,200 at the median. Supporters' gifts were refit to match what local campaigns raise: an evening of calls brings in about $120 to $157 an hour, with a typical gift of $92 to $107.
