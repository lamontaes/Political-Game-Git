title: A player who wins a state legislative seat holds it alone
---

When the player won a seat in the home state's legislature, the game also
chose its own winner for the same district at the same election, so the
chamber listed two members for one seat. The state election now leaves the
player's district to the player's race. The member who held the seat stops
serving when the winner's term begins, and the chamber lists the winner in
that district's seat. A member who wins through a campaign serves out their
term. If they don't run again, the seat opens at the next election.

A vacant seat on the State screen used to say that state legislative
elections weren't held yet. It now gives the date of the election that fills
the seat, or, once that election is over, the day the new member takes it.
