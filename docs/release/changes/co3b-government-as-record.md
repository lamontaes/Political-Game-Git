---
id: co3b-government-as-record
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The opening card shows your local government as a labeled value, not a news line
---

Before, card 1 of a new game listed "Mineral County is governed by County board." under In the news, and the government's own records described it as a disclosed game profile. Now the card shows "Local government" with the name from the government's record, and the news section carries only real headlines.
