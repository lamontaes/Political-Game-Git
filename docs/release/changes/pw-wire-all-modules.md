title: Every built engine runs in the world you play
---

The world you play now runs ten engines that were built but never switched on: other governments pass their own laws, state and federal races elsewhere are decided, outcome readings (crime, schooling, coverage) are kept, and the town has interest groups, ballot measures, recalls, debates, local outlets, constituent cases and officials' staff. A new check fails when an engine is written and not switched on.
