title: A town's proposals can now be adopted, not only withdrawn
---

A town government posts small proposals for public comment, such as the
rules for reserving a park shelter or a road repair schedule. Nobody could
adopt one. Unless a resident commented, every proposal was eventually
withdrawn: 111 of 111 in two long runs where nobody was played. A proposal can
now end adopted, and the news says so.

Which ending comes is an even chance among the ones still open. That is a
marked placeholder until the real shares of adopted, amended, tabled and
withdrawn proposals have been researched.
