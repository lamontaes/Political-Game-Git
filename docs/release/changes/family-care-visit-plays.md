title: When someone you live with falls seriously ill, you now sit with them in a scene
---

When someone at home fell seriously ill, the visit was only ever staged for them, never for you, and it could not be worded. Now you are the one who comes, and they ask you to tell a relative of theirs. A relative who lives in another town is still told to you as news.
