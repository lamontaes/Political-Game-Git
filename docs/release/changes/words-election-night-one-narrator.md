title: Election day narration no longer repeats the town's name

On election morning and election night the narrator no longer names the town the screen already shows: "The polls opened this morning." in place of "The polls opened this morning in Lake Lindsey." A candidate who has moved away hears that they can't vote in this race without the old town named. The narrator's election day and night lines now come from the same word maker as every other scene.
