---
id: a50-recorded-government-employer-identity
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Recorded local governments carry their own employer identity
---

Local-government organizations created from an active, dated government-unit
record now retain that government's exact identity. This prepares their
payroll records to resolve the canonical public account without treating a
workplace name, location or funding source as proof of legal ownership.

This change opens no cash account and invents no balance. Separate generated
schools and offices still need their own sourced legal-employer relationship;
they are not assigned to the nearest government by this change.
