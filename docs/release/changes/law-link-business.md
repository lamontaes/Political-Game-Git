title: Cannabis sales and police facial recognition follow the law
---

Where cannabis sales are legal, households buy cannabis each month, and a licensed cannabis store that opens takes those customers; repeal ends the purchases. Police budgets pay a facial recognition vendor monthly, and a restriction on government facial recognition stops those payments.

The share of household spending and the cost per officer are provisional national estimates.
