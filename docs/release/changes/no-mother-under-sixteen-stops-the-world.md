title: A long game no longer stops when a 15-year-old in town draws a birth
---

Town families drew births from every woman aged 15 to 49, but the birth record
refused any parent under 16, so a 15-year-old's draw stopped the whole world
with "would be under 16 at the birth." It stopped watched worlds in Seattle
and Philadelphia. Now the birth chances and the birth record read one rule for
the youngest parent, taken from the national birth-rate table, and a birth the
record ever refuses is noted privately while the world goes on.
