title: A long game no longer stops when a 15-year-old in town draws a birth
---

Town families drew births from every woman aged 15 to 49, but the game
records no birth to a parent under 16, so a 15-year-old's draw stopped the
whole world with "would be under 16 at the birth." It stopped watched worlds
in Seattle and Philadelphia. Now nobody under 16 becomes a mother. The town's
births for ages 15 to 19 still match the real rate: the older women in that
age band carry the share the 15-year-olds would have had.
