section: Improved
title: Browse local government from home, not a nationwide list
---

Local government now opens on where you live and your actual standing there,
using the saved place scope so a state home reads as Kentucky rather than
Kentucky, Kentucky. You can search other supported governments by name or state,
see known people and public meetings, review pending work and attendance
history, and keep a government pinned without it trapping the dropdown when you
inspect somewhere else.

Publisher references and research material stay available under Source review
rather than on the normal screen. Inspecting another government still does not
move you, change your residence, or grant any role there.
