title: People stand in the room, and the only list is the one you make
---

The strip of faces above the room is gone. It filled itself with whoever
happened to be present and was the only way to choose anybody, which made it a
list you never asked for sitting where the room should be.

You now click the person themselves, standing where they are. The same things
follow: a quick look, their full record, talking to them, keeping them to hand.
Keyboard alone reaches them too — they take focus in turn, with a visible ring,
and Enter opens the same menu the mouse does.

One rail is left and it is yours. It holds only what you chose to keep there,
it can mix people with the governments you follow, and nothing puts anything in
it on your behalf. What you kept is still there after you save and come back.

Browsing everyone you know, rather than everyone in the room, is under People,
and that is where you can keep somebody who is not standing in front of you.

When an action is refused, the reason is now written beside it rather than left
to a greyed-out button. If you cannot talk to somebody yet, the room says why.
