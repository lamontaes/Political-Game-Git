title: Temperament shapes health and stress, and changes over a life
---

People who are careful and dutiful live a little longer and are less likely to fall into drug use or lung disease, while the town's totals stay what the life tables say. A layoff or an eviction now weighs on a person's stress, much more on someone who dwells on setbacks than on someone steady. People also change slowly: they grow more reliable and less reckless through their twenties and thirties, and a hard blow such as losing a job or a home can leave someone a little less calm.
