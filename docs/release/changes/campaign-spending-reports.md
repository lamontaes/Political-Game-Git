title: Campaigns file monthly spending reports you can read on the campaign screen
---

Every campaign committee, yours and your opponents', now files a public
spending report once a month listing each payment it made: the date, who was
paid, what it was for, and the amount. The campaign screen shows them under
"Spending reports". A payment a candidate made to themselves appears as
"Paid to the candidate", so a careful reader can spot it. The monthly schedule
and the categories are placeholders until the real filing rules are
researched.
