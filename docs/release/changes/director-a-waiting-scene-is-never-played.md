title: Scenes the story director set aside for you now play
---

Some scenes the story director staged for you were set aside to wait and then never opened: a reporter's call after your vote, a hearing, a verdict, a gathering you missed. Now one waiting scene opens each morning, the most important first; one that waits past its time passes without stopping you.
