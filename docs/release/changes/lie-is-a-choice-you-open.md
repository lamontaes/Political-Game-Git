title: A conversation keeps its lies behind a Lie button
---

When a conversation offers something you know to be false, its replies now
start with the truthful ones. A Lie button beside them shows the false replies
in place of the truthful answers they contradict, each still marked as a lie.
The button is unavailable when nothing you could say is false. Replies that
come with recorded words now show those words on the button.
