---
id: whole-life-past
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Each person's past now reaches back to their own childhood.
---

When you open a person's card, or meet someone for the first time, their schooling, their jobs and the year they moved to town now go back to when they were born, a year at a time, instead of stopping the year your character was born. The people you start out knowing have it from the first day. The town's laws, elections and businesses still begin in your birth year.
