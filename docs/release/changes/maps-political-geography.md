Politics now has a map of all fifty states and Washington, D.C., with Alaska and Hawaii drawn as insets. Color it by House district or Senate delegation, or pick a state to see its state senate and state house districts, counties, and cities. Colors follow your world's actual parties, including any that form later. Vacant seats, ended terms, and offices your world doesn't track each look different, so a missing record never passes for a real result. Click a district to see who holds it, their party and term, and any elections there. Names open the person's card, and looking never moves you or changes the day. A history slider shows the map as your world recorded it on an earlier date, and later party switches don't change how the past looks. Your home and where you are now are marked. If your city is split between districts, the map lists every district your home might be in instead of guessing. Every district can also be reached from a searchable list.
