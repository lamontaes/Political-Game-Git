title: Losing a primary is a scene.
---

Where a top-two or top-four primary sends only its top finishers on, a candidate it puts out now hears it the night of the primary: someone at home meets them, or, for one who lives alone, the race's own moment. Before, the loss went by with nothing, and only the November count had a scene.
