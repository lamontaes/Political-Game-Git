title: People in your town keep working hours
---

Before, a neighbor with a job had a title and an employer, but no working
days or hours, so nobody in town was ever at work at any particular time.

Now every job has its own working week. Clerks and city staff keep office
hours on weekdays. Nurses work 12-hour day or night shifts, police officers
rotate through 10-hour shifts, and firefighters are on for 24 hours and off
for 48. Diner and store staff work morning or evening shifts through the
week, and coworkers take turns so the place stays open. Some new hires,
mostly at diners, stores and recreation centers, work part-time.

Each workplace also knows which picture it is: the city clerk works at the
clerk counter, a nurse in the hospital hallway, a union representative at
the union hall. On a weekday morning the clerk is at the counter; at 9 p.m.
the counter is empty and the clerk is at home.
