title: A grown-up start holds a job
---

A grown-up New Game now opens with the job the person already holds, at a
business in their own town, instead of between jobs. The employer is chosen
from their own situation: where a relative or someone in their household
works, else the kind of work they have done before, else the best-paying work
they are fit for. The job pays each week and can be left like any other.
