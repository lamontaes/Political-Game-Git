title: A town's wholesaler keeps selling beyond the town, so it no longer closes on its own.
---

A wholesaler sold only what the town's own businesses bought from it, so in a town where it was the main employer it lost money every month, laid off its staff and closed within months with no cause anyone could find. It now sells to buyers beyond the town for whatever the town's businesses don't buy, as every other business that sells outside its town already did, and keeps its staff.
