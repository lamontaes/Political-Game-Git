title: A meeting somebody else asked for can be attended
---

When somebody asked the player to meet and the player said yes, the meeting
was held as the other person's, and Attend never offered it. A meeting the
played person agrees to is now theirs to attend, whoever asked.

The People screen also says what became of the last request between two
people while it still matters: that they said yes to a day, could not make it,
or never answered. Before, an answered request simply vanished and the Ask
button came back, which read as though it had been lost.

Where two people meet is still not chosen. It is filed as a research question.
