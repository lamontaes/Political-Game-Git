Your sister's card used to say she had served as commissioner since 2018, while your journal said she was a cook, because each screen read a different record. Now the card, the journal and Travel all read what she does from the same records. A relative who holds a part-time office beside her job is told with both, the office and where she works, on her card and in your journal.
