title: Candidacy uses each office's recorded minimum age
---

Candidacy checks the selected office's recorded minimum age instead of applying
a general age requirement. An unresolved age rule remains unresolved. Dated
qualification assessments and laws enacted in the world keep precedence.

Kentucky legislative candidacy now reads the office's constitutional age.
Other researched missing ages retain their own citations and supported dates;
an earlier date without evidence still leaves the requirement unresolved.

World-aware office projections now read an operative age law recorded in that
world through the existing enacted-rule reader. The enacted-law fixture seats
the actual governor and uses the existing executive desk before recording law.
