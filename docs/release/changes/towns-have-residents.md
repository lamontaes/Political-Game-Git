section: Added
title: Your hometown now has people in it
---

A new game's town is now sized from its real population. Every home in town
exists in outline, and the game writes out the people you are likely to meet:
the neighbors on your street, the staff at the market and the schools, and
four congregations with their members. Their children go to the town's
schools. Everyone else is written out only when you meet them. Household
shapes, the number of congregations and the starting drift from the Census
figure are placeholders until they have been researched. Existing saves are
unchanged; new games open with a different world than before.
