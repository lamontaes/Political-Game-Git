title: People have their long-term illnesses from the first day, and new ones begin as they age
---

A new life used to leave everyone's health record blank until the start of the next quarter, when every long-term illness appeared at once. Now the illnesses people already live with are on record from the first day. Diabetes, cancer, heart disease, stroke and COPD also begin during play at the pace the game's own figures show them becoming more common with age.
