title: Labor outcomes reach recorded people
---

Six labor outcome estimates now use the shared person outcome, lived outcome,
and official-view path. The married-women estimate requires an active recorded
legal marriage and female gender identity. Childcare and paid-leave estimates
use active parental-authority records and sourced child-age cohorts; they remain
estimates from maternal participation studies, not findings about an individual
care or leave enrollment. Retirement-age changes use a sourced 62-to-67 cohort.
Public bargaining and defense-contract estimates reach residents represented by
the place-level earnings measure because individual historical schooling and
contract employment are not recorded. The common path covers all 56 places.
