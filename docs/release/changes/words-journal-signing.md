id: words-journal-signing
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Your journal tells the bills you signed or vetoed, and the sponsor's tells what you did to theirs.
---

When you sign or veto a bill as mayor, your journal says so ("I signed Ordinance 2025-1, the Free Fares for Riders Ordinance."; "I vetoed Ordinance 2025-2, the Transit First Funding Ordinance, but it was overridden."). The member who sponsored it reads it in their own journal ("My bill, Ordinance 2025-1, the Free Fares for Riders Ordinance, was signed into law."). Three lines about a failed bill no longer drop the comma after the bill's title.
