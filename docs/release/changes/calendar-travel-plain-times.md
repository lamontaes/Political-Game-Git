section: Changed
title: Calendar and travel times read in hours and days
---

Times on the Calendar, Places and meeting screens read as people say them: "1
day 9 hours" or "1 hour 5 minutes" rather than a count of minutes. A travel
button no longer runs into its time ("Travel · until Tuesday, January 6, 2026,
6:30 PM"). A Calendar entry of yours says "You're going.", and its "On the
record" heading is now "How it was arranged".
