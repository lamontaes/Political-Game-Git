section: Fixed
title: "A bill that changes a law's amount keeps the law's terms, so a SNAP raise pays more instead of stopping"
---

A bill that raised or cut a law's amount wrote a new law with none of the old one's terms: a SNAP raise stopped every benefit, and a payroll tax raise lost its wage base. An amendment now keeps every term of the law it changes.
