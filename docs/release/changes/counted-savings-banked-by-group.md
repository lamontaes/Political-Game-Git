---
id: counted-savings-banked-by-group
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: Families in the wider population keep their bank savings in step with their cash.
---

The bank put each family's part of its group's money in savings by headcount, while its cash was shared by how much each family saves, so savings evened out who had what and one family's deposit came out of everyone's cash. Now a group's savings are banked together and each family's share of them is the same as its share of the cash, so what a family holds in cash and savings together is its own.
