title: A public program's note to the books no longer prints as news
---

"In the news" showed a public program's own bookkeeping sentence, program key
and all, such as "$750,000 may be committed for a program-key from one date
through another. Authority to spend is not cash." The press desk no longer
publishes those notes, and saves that already printed one no longer show it in
the news, the opening year or the recap. The program's record keeps its
fields; no news line is made from them yet.
