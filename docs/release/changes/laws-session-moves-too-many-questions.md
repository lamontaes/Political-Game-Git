title: A state legislature takes up a handful of laws a session, not a third of them
---

Touches pool item: laws-session-moves-too-many-questions

A legislator now carries the changes they care about most, as far as their own conviction reaches, instead of filing on every question where the law differs from their view, and a state legislator's bill goes through the session's floor weeks instead of being given a vote the day the leader agrees to hear it. A state session files about ten changes, not thirty-odd, and the bills the leader leaves off the agenda stay off by a recorded answer.
