title: Charlottesville councilors can carry an ordinance from introduction to law
---

A seated Charlottesville councilor can now introduce an ordinance, put it on the
council agenda and bring it to a vote once the city code's required three days
have passed. The recorded result takes effect the day it passes. The rules come
from the city's own code and charter. Other councilors' ballots are clearly
labeled as game-authored until the game models how they decide. Nevada
legislative candidates are also no longer turned away on the game's opening date
by a qualification law that has been in force since October 1, 2025.
