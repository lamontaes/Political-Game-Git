id: job-loss-news-rule
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: An employer's group layoff is a public record, and one worker let go by turnover is not
---

A layoff that is an employer's decision about a group (a staff cut or a closure) is now a public record the wire can carry, and the briefs column lists the kind. A single worker let go by the turnover pace stays private. The press prints nothing of it until it has words for the kind.
