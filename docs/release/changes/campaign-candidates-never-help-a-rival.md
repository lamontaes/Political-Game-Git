id: campaign-candidates-never-help-a-rival
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: People running against you no longer work for your campaign.
---

Someone who has filed for the same seat as you now campaigns only for themselves. They no longer knock doors as your volunteer, offer to run your campaign, host or come to your donor meetings, or say yes when you ask them for an endorsement, even when they are your relative, your friend or a member of a group that backs you, and a group whose spokesperson is running gives no new endorsement to anyone else in that race. An endorsement given before its giver filed still stands. Someone already working on your campaign who then files for the same seat leaves it that day and stops coming to your events, though the time you spent working together is still part of your history. The same holds for every other candidate in town.
