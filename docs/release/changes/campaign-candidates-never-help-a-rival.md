section: Fixed
title: People running against you no longer work for your campaign.
---

Someone who has filed for the same seat as you now campaigns only for themselves. They no longer knock doors as your volunteer, offer to run your campaign, host or come to your donor meetings, or endorse you, even when they are your relative, your friend or a member of a group that backs you. The same holds for every other candidate in town: nobody running in a race helps a rival in it.
