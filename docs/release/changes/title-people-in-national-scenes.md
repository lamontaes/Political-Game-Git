title: The title screen shows national scenes with people in them
---

The title screen now rotates through places the whole country knows: the
Oval Office, the U.S. Capitol, the Senate and House floors, the Supreme
Court, a rally, a national convention, a debate stage, an election night and
a television studio, each by day and again at night. State capitols and town
or county rooms no longer appear there. Each scene has several generated
people standing, sitting and speaking at its marked spots: a speaker at the
lectern with the crowd listening, members at their desks, justices in robes
on the bench. A returning player stands on the scene's main spot in their
own look, and a saved game opens on the player's own federal room when the
role has one, otherwise on the U.S. Capitol.
