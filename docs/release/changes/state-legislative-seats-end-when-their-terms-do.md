title: A state legislative seat ends when its term does, and a re-elected member keeps one seat
---

A state legislative seat won in an older save outside Kentucky had no end
date, so the member held it indefinitely. One Nevada Assembly seat won in 2032
was still held in 2039. When you continue a save like that, the seat now ends
when its term does. If the term's end is still ahead, the seat ends on that
day. If the date has already passed, the seat ends the next time a day
passes, and your work line no longer lists it.

A member who runs again for the seat they hold and wins now keeps one seat.
Before, the old seat stayed open next to the new one. The result now says
"won and keeps the seat. The new term begins January 1, 2028." It no longer
says the office is not theirs until then.

The list of offices you could run for now shows every office's next election
as a date, for example "The next election is November 3, 2026." Before, most
offices said the timing was not established. An office with a race already on
record shows that race's date.
