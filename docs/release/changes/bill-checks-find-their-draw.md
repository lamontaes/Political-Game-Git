id: bill-checks-find-their-draw
impact: none
---

The bill browser checks (bill-stage, bill-floor, bill-desk, bill-override, bill-state) find a qualifying bill, seat or day from the world's records instead of assuming the draw: no cosponsors line is expected where none signed on, a hearing day is let pass on the way to the floor, the mayor is found by the pick list's real label, and a bill is opened by its place in the list, not by a number two chambers share.
