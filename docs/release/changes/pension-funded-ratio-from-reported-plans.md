---
id: pension-funded-ratio-from-reported-plans
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Each government's pension opens as funded as its own plans report
---

A government's pension now opens holding the share of its liability that its
own plans reported in the Public Plans Database, each plan's latest year:
Illinois' state plans 54.8%, Chicago's 26.2%, Cook County's 65.9%. A
government the database does not list opens at the median of every plan,
76.7%, marked as estimated from the average. Before, every pension opened at
the same hand-set 75%, so Chicago's plans opened as healthy as any other's
and asked for a far smaller yearly contribution than their real gap does.

A budget's record of a rainy-day deposit or a carried balance now names the
reserve size its own law sets, such as Missouri's 7.5%.
Before, both records still said every reserve was a hand-set 5%.
