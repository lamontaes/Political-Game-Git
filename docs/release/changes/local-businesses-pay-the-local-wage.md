title: Local businesses pay what the job pays where you live
---

The staff of a town's shops and offices are now paid the published wage for
their job in your area (Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2025) instead of $2,800
a month everywhere. A job that started at one of these businesses before your
life began pays the same.
