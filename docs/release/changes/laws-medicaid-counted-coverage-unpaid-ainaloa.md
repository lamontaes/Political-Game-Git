title: Federal benefits are paid in small towns
---

A federal benefit set by law, such as the federal share of Medicaid, was sometimes paid short in a small town because the national treasury had spent its cash on other payments that day. The treasury now borrows a month of these standing payments ahead, so they arrive in full.
