title: State and county treasuries stop piling up billions in surplus
---

A state's treasury used to keep the whole surplus of the year its budget came from, about ten billion dollars more every year, while the outside world's books ran the same amount negative. Now a state or county keeps about a month and a fifth of its yearly spending in reserve, as real states do, and invests the rest elsewhere.
