impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Fewer births in hard times follows the research
---

Before, a year of high unemployment cut the number of babies born in town by
a rule the game made up: at twice the normal jobless rate, about 30% fewer.

Now, the town's births follow the research: about 1.4% fewer for each point
of unemployment, felt nine months later. At 8% unemployment, that is about
6% fewer births than at 4%.
