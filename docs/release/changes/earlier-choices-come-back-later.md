title: Some childhood choices come back days or weeks later
---

A few early moments now have a later answer that depends on what you actually
chose, with the same person, once enough days have passed.

Keep the blue crayon, and on another drawing day the classmate has it and you
want it. Hand it over, and next time they hold it out to you first. Say
nothing about the broken mug, and a few days on you are asked again. Put the
broken truck aside, and its owner asks whether you know how the wheel came
off; show them instead, and they come back with it taped and ask you to play.
Tell a classmate not to push during tag, and the next game they tag your arm;
shrug it off, and they knock you over again. An adult who asked a housemate
for quiet is asked, later, whether they want quiet again or company.

Nothing forces these. Each is one more thing that can happen, it plays once,
and a child who chose differently meets a different later moment, or none.
