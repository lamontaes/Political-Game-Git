title: A bill the legislature passed still becomes law after the session ends
---

A bill that passed both chambers of a state legislature used to freeze when
the session ended, even after the governor had signed it, so it never became
law. Now the clerks still send it on, the governor still signs or vetoes it,
and a signed bill becomes law after the legislature has gone home, as it does
in real life. A bill still waiting in a committee or on a floor when the
session ends still stops there.
