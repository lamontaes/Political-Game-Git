title: Department heads are chosen for the work and schooling on their record, not their age.
---

A mayor or manager filling a department now looks at what each resident has done: years in the department's own line of work, other work, and schooling. Someone with none of those heads a department only in a town where nobody has any, so an 18-year-old fresh out of school no longer runs Public Works.
