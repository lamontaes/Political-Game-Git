title: The talk at home after filing has its lead-up, and how a scene ends is kept
---

After you file, the narrator first sets the money worry from the household's own books (the bank statement on the table, how long the savings cover what the paychecks don't, how long the race runs); Continue, then your partner speaks. A household whose pay covers its costs has no money worry to raise. How any scene ends is now written on its record, and an ending that strains a tie costs closeness with that person.
