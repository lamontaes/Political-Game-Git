title: A town's air, roads and clinics change who dies
---

Dirtier air and new abortion bans raise the chance a baby dies in the first year, a higher speed limit raises road deaths at the ages where crashes take the most lives, and Medicaid expansion lowers deaths among low-income people aged 55 to 64 and does nothing for the well off. The same factors act on the people you meet and on the town's wider population, and an unemployment rise is set to cut men's coverage more than women's and fall hardest on men in their fifties.
