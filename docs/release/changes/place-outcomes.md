title: Every state now has a real uninsured rate and poverty rate that laws and the economy move.
---

Each state, D.C. and Puerto Rico starts at its real 2024 share of people
without health insurance and share of people in poverty, and both move month
by month. Expanding Medicaid cuts a state's uninsured about 15%. The federal
Medicaid work requirement, starting in 2027, raises the uninsured in states
that cover low-income adults by about a third. Hard times raise both the
uninsured and poverty, and a higher state minimum wage lowers poverty over
the following years. Medicaid's work requirement is now its own policy
question, separate from work rules for food and income assistance.
