title: Your town's council and mayor are your neighbors, and they are elected and vote
---

A life now begins with the town council full and a mayor in office where the
town elects one, all people who live in town. The town holds its own
elections: members retire, neighbors file, a field of more than two meets in a
primary, and a sitting member can lose. A member may resign, and the council
appoints someone until the next election.

The public meeting on your calendar is a council meeting and ends with a vote
on its agenda item. After that the council meets every two weeks and votes on
ordinances. If you win a seat, you replace a sitting member, and the
government screen lists the council's members.
