title: A town's Executive page lists only the town's own officers.
---

The Executive page has a tab for each government at the level, so a town's page shows its mayor and officers and the county's sheriff, clerk and assessor sit on the county's tab. Each office's title is said once, a county board shows its own government's chief beside it instead of the town's mayor, and Coming up is left out when nothing is coming.
