title: Saved election results and meeting choices reach play
---

The watched World's record now displays saved congressional and state
legislative result summaries in pages, with named winners available on demand.
Attending a posted public meeting opens its recorded room at the start; the
player can read the agenda, choose exact words for one comment, stay, make a
brief visit, or leave. The new result labels and speech remain marked for
overnight copy review.
