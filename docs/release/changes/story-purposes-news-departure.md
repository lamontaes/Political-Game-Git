title: A story read at home, a goodbye and a rent notice now say what they are about.
---

When someone at home or at work reads a story and brings it up, they name it, and you can agree, disagree, ask what it said or put the talk off. When someone you know moves away and comes to say goodbye, they tell you where they are going, who goes along and why when it is on record, and you can comfort them, ask, promise to keep in touch or thank them. The rent notice now says about how much is owed and how long the rent has gone unpaid, and a few dollars short no longer reads like months behind.
