title: A vertical icon rail replaces the menu, and your portrait opens You
---
The long menu is gone. A vertical row of icons sits above your player card (Journal, Calendar, Money, Politics, News, People, Travel) with names on hover. Clicking your portrait opens You, with Work and study as its second tab. Options and the guide open from the Esc key. The office screen no longer carries interrupt toggles, and Politics shows its tabs in the new order.
