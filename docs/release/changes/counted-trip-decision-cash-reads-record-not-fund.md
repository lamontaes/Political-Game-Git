title: A household that can't pay for the trip no longer ends a pregnancy for free
---

Where a restriction lengthens the trip to a provider, households in the wider population now weigh the trip against the money they can actually spend. Each one that makes the trip pays for all of it, and that payment shows in the law's reach on the bill card. A household that can't pay for the trip doesn't make it.
