title: Addresses now sound like the year the country had.
---

The President's address picks a kind from how the country is doing and who is speaking: a candid reckoning in a bad year, a railing against whoever let it slide, a hopeful lift in a fair one, a victory lap after a good one, or words for the dead after a disaster. It tells the bad news as well as the good, including schools, crime, health, rent, roads and the environment, and it gives an address to Congress each year, not only the first.
