title: People you met choose every day only while you keep in touch
---

Everyone you met used to be run in full every day for the rest of the game, so a town slowed down as your circle grew. Now someone outside your family and household chooses daily only while the two of you have been in touch in the last 30 days, and at their own pace otherwise, until you meet again.
