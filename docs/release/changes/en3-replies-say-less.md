title: Replies no longer quote a news headline back, echo "Me too" or add stock advice
---

When you ask someone what they remember about a matter you talked over, they no
longer read the news headline back to you in quotation marks. A friend who has
the same plan no longer opens with a bare "Me too", and a friend with no worry
about your run for office says only that, without the stock advice to go in
with your eyes open.
