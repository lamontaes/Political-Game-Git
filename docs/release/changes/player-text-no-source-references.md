id: player-text-no-source-references
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Screens state what they know, without citing where it came from
---

The docket, character creation, national results, candidacy, the constitutional
and municipal screens, the press and public-information explanations and the
patch notes no longer print citations, data-series labels, observation dates or
research links beside the facts they show. Each screen simply tells you the
thing. Where a sentence only made sense alongside its citation, it has been
rewritten to say plainly what the game actually knows.
