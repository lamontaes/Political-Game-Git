section: Changed
title: Screens state what they know, without citing where it came from
---

The docket, character creation, national results, candidacy, the campaign
office list, the education options, the constitutional and municipal screens,
the press and public-information explanations and the patch notes no longer
print citations, data-series labels, observation dates or research links beside
the facts they show. Each screen simply tells you the thing.

Refusals were the larger part of it. When the game will not let a character
stand for an office, it now says which state, what is in the way and whether a
different life could reach it, instead of quoting the provision it was reading.
Several requirements blocked for the same reason used to repeat that reason
once per requirement; you now read it once.
