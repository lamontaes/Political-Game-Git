title: Local party chapters are named the way people say the county
---

The Parties tab named a home chapter from the county's filing name in the
Census listing, so a player in Catonsville, Maryland saw "County of Baltimore
Democrats". Every county in the country read that way, and every parish and
borough too. Chapters are now named for the place as its residents say it,
such as "Baltimore County Democrats" or "Caddo Parish Republicans". A town
with no county on record is named without its state attached. Chapters in
saved games keep the names they were created with.
