title: Local party chapters are named the way people say the county
---

The Parties tab named a home chapter from the county's filing name in the
Census listing, so a player in Catonsville, Maryland saw "County of Baltimore
Democrats". Every county in the country read that way, and every parish and
borough too. Chapters are now named for the place as its residents say it,
such as "Baltimore County Democrats" or "Caddo Parish Republicans".

A town whose county area has no separate government is named for that area
too, because parties organize by county, parish and borough either way: a life
in Houma, Louisiana now meets the Terrebonne Parish Democrats rather than the
Houma Democrats, and Nashville's chapter is Davidson County's. Where the city is
itself the county (Denver, Juneau, Anchorage, Richmond), in a New England town,
in an Alaska Census Area and in a city spread over several counties, the
chapter is named for the town, without its state attached. Chapters in saved
games keep the names they were created with.
