title: No line shows a blank in braces
---

A family member at home could say "Haven't seen much of you since {last-together}." with the blank showing. Every blank in a line is now filled from the records, and a line whose blank cannot be filled is never said. The line itself now comes up only when you have been apart a week or more, never "since yesterday".
