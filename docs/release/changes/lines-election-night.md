title: Election night is back, in new words.
---

When you win, someone close reaches you once the race is called and you speak to the room. When you lose, your aide asks you to call the winner, and you can ask how far apart it is, wait on the count or refuse. At home, your family talks over what the race cost and whether you'll run again.
