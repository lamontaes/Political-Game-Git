title: Every state now has a turnout rate that drifts with the times and rises with its graduates.
---

Each state starts at its real share of eligible voters who voted in 2024, and
that share now changes over the years, partly with the country and partly on
its own. A state that graduates more of its students sees more of them vote
once they come of age. Strict voter ID laws, as the research finds, change
turnout about not at all.
