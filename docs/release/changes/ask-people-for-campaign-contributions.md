title: Ask people you know for campaign contributions
---

You can now ask the people you know, and active members of your party chapter,
for campaign money one at a time. Each person answers from what they think of
you, how they are related to you, what they can afford and how much more the
law lets them give. Gifts go straight to the campaign committee, and the
campaign screen lists who gave and why they said yes or no.
