title: You can answer what the President said four ways, including asking what it means for them or letting it go.
---

When someone at home brings up the President's address, you can agree, disagree, ask what it means for them (offered when their stand comes from their own life, and answered from it), or let it go. Letting it go ends the talk without you taking a side, and they bring the point up again on another evening. Someone who watched the address with you and keeps talk short may say it in one line, praise or scorn, without asking you anything.
