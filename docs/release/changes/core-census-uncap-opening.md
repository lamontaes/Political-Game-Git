section: Changed
title: Big towns open at their real Census size.
---

A town of more than 10,000 people now opens at its real Census size: the people you meet are drawn from a 10,000-person sample, and every count in town (jobs, homes, voters, money) is scaled to the real population. A game year in the largest towns takes about as long as before: measured on one machine, Washington, D.C. about 17 percent less and New York about 11 percent more, and the two-minute year for the largest town is still open.
