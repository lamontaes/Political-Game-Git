title: A town's power company keeps selling beyond the town, so it no longer closes on its own.
---

A county's electric and water company sold only to the households of the town it stands in, so a plant town's biggest employer lost money every month, laid off its staff and closed within months with no cause anyone could find. It now sells to the businesses and the grid beyond the town, as every other business that sells outside its town already did, and keeps its staff.
