title: Legislative rule packs declare which saved seats they read
---

Congress now declares its saved seat roll and institution-wide party lookup
in its rule pack. The shared chamber reader preserves the members, vacancies,
parties, and supplied ballots already recorded in the world. State packs
without this declaration keep reading their existing candidacy openings.
Malformed declarations are refused rather than supplying invented seats.

The common bill agenda and executive desk select their federal or state route
from the bill's actual jurisdiction. This changes how existing rules are
selected, without changing term lengths, session limits, or vote requirements.
