id: paychecks-withhold-income-tax
impact: minor
section: Added
title: Paychecks now withhold federal and state income tax
---

Before, a paycheck took out Social Security and Medicare and nothing else,
so nobody in the game paid income tax.

Now each paycheck also withholds federal income tax at the 2026 rates, for a
single filer or a married couple filing jointly. It also withholds state
income tax for single filers in the 32 states whose 2026 rates are known,
and that money goes to the state rather than to Washington. States with no
wage tax withhold nothing. Montana is now counted among the states that tax
wages.

Where a rate isn't known yet, the tax is left unknown instead of zero. That
covers head-of-household filers, married filers' state tax, the 10 states
that use personal exemptions, the territories and local wage taxes. A
worker's filing status comes from their own family: married, a single
parent living with their child, or single.
