title: People miss a colleague only for the time since they last sat together.
---

When someone looks for company, a colleague they sat with in a meeting yesterday no longer counts as someone they have not seen since their last talk. Before, a colleague with an older conversation on record drew company as if unseen for weeks, so the player drew colleagues' company ahead of the people they actually sit with.
