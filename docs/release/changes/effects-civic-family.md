title: A town that loses its local newspaper turns out a little less for local races
---

When a town's own newspaper closes, its voters are a little less likely to turn out on a day whose ballot carries a city or county race, or a recall of a town seat, by the size research measured. The relief-spending, newspaper borrowing-cost and media-trust links stay unread, each naming the engine that would read it and the record it lacks.
