title: A town's newspaper follows the town's size
---

A town among the 1,000 largest places in the country now has a daily
newspaper, and every other town has a weekly, the most common kind of local
paper today. Before, which kind of newsroom a town had was picked at random
when the game began, so a town of 4,000 people could have a daily and a
large city a newsletter.
