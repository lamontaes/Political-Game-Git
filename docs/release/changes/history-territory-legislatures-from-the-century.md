title: Guam's, the Virgin Islands' and the Northern Marianas' legislatures have parties.
---

The legislatures of Guam, the Virgin Islands and the Northern Marianas' Senate now open with members from the parties your world's history elected, instead of members with no party. American Samoa's Fono and the Northern Marianas' House stay as they are, since most of their members run without a national party.
