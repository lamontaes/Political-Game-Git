title: A teenager's first job pays, and job offers no longer wait forever or lapse unseen
---

A teenager's first job now pays every week from the day it is taken, at a
placeholder hourly rate, and ends when an adult job begins. An unanswered
offer lapses after its reply window, and an employer follows up once or
withdraws after a missed start. A job whose shift overlaps one already held is
refused. Skipping days stops on the last day to answer an offer, the day
overview names that date, and the life record keeps the offer and how it
ended. A plain day skip now also brings the employer's answer to a job
application.
