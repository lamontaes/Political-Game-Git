title: In other states, the lieutenant governor now wins with the governor, the way the law elects them.
---

In the states where the law elects the lieutenant governor on one ticket with the governor, the two now win together. Before, the count could seat a lieutenant governor of a different party from the governor elected the same night. Other states' tickets follow the same rule in the news and the state numbers.
