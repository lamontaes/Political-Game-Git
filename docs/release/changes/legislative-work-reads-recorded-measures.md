title: Legislative work opens a recorded bill with a seated sponsor
---

Opening legislative work reads a pending bill already recorded in the save and
checks its sponsor's current seat. It no longer draws an opening bill or creates
a member. When no eligible bill is recorded, the office explains how to continue.
Older saved office assignments keep their bill identity and procedure.
