title: State legislative seats follow their own regular election cycles
---

State legislative elections now follow each chamber's recorded regular cycle.
Where senators are elected in alternating district groups, a seat that is not
due stays occupied while the other group votes. A candidate filing for a
recorded district sees the next regular election for that district. New
openings also seat the correct number of members in states with more than one
legislator per district.

Some term-start dates and West Virginia's specific Senate seat classes remain
declared game assumptions until their legal timing is established. Existing
saved openings keep their recorded seat plan and election history.
