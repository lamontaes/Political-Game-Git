title: The first evening's talk about the President's address goes on past one exchange.
---

On the first evening a relative raised a point of the President's address, you answered, they answered back, and the talk ended. It now goes on: the relative says where they themselves stand on that point; where they don't, they say what the point would do to your household, from its own records (someone's job, or money being tight); and where the address made another point, the talk can turn to it.
