title: Older workers who lose a job are less likely to set out to retrain or start over.
---

After a layoff or a closure, a worker's urge to retrain or start a business now fades with age, as the research on displaced workers shows, so a town where many middle-aged and older people lose their jobs no longer has a third of them setting out to prove themselves.
