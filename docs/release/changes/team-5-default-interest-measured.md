---
id: team-5-default-interest-measured
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A government with no recorded debt borrows at the measured national rate
---

A government whose Census figures show no debt or no interest no longer
opens at a hand-set 4% interest rate. It opens at the measured national
rate for its level: about 3.6% for a state and 3.9% for a local
government. Its opening notes say whether the rate is its own or an
estimate from the average.
