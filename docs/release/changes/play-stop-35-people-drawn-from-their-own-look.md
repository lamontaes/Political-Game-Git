title: The people in a room look like different people, and look the same each time you meet them
---

Everyone in a scene is now drawn from their own record: their body, build, outfit, skin tone and clothing color come from who they are, not from what is left over once the others are placed. Two neighbors differ, and a person is drawn the same wherever you see them.
