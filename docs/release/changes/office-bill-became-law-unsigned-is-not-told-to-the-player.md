title: A bill you leave on your desk past its deadline is on the record as law without your signature
---

When a bill sits on your desk as mayor or governor until its deadline passes, it becomes law without your signature, and that is now on the public record like a signature or a veto. The paper words it as a law that passed without your signature, never as a signing, and the reporter can ask you about it.
