The glossary entries for the minority leader and cosponsors no longer lean
on the phrase "on the record". The phrase now appears only in the press term
of that name. The Legal tab's sentences about where the player stands, and the
Conditions page's empty-state line, are now written by the presentation
layer instead of typed into the screens. Several charges now read "Three
charges against you are waiting for your plea."
