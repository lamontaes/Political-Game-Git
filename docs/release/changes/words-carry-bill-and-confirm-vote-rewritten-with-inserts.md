title: Being asked to carry a bill, and a sponsor checking on a vote you promised, now say what is on record
---

When someone asks you to carry a bill, your yes or no now gives your stand on what it is about, and they remember an earlier ask of yours. A sponsor who comes about a vote you traded for names the section you got, and one who comes back after a veto says who vetoed it; a thank no longer follows a no.
