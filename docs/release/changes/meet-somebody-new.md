title: New people come into a life, and the player can go and meet them
---

Before, a new character knew the people they started with and nobody else. A
24-year-old in Houma or Reno knew the same two people after six months as on
their first day.

Now new people come into a life as time passes, and the People screen has a
"Meet somebody new" choice: at work, on the same program, through a group the
player belongs to, or through a friend. Only settings with somebody real in
them are offered. Nobody is invented to be met, and living in the same town is
not a way of meeting somebody. Meeting somebody makes the two of you
acquainted; asking them to meet and then spending the evening together is what
makes a friend. Played through those routes, a new character in Houma or Reno
who joins the walking group has a friend they did not start with inside their
first month.

How often people meet on their own, and how much one evening together counts
for, are labeled calibrations: ChatGPT found no rate to use for either.

Also fixed: asking two people for the same evening could crash passing time
when both said yes. The second person now offers a different day. A meeting
the player asked for is also named after the other person on their calendar,
not after the player.
