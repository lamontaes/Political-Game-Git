title: Metering and limiting groundwater changes how much farms pump
---

Metering and limiting groundwater withdrawals changes how much groundwater a
state's farms pump. Every state, D.C. and territory starts with its real rule:
31 states, Puerto Rico and Guam require a permit that sets how much a large
well may pump, and the rest only register wells or limit pumping in some areas.
The 50 states and Puerto Rico start at the groundwater they pumped for
irrigation in 2015. A state that starts limiting withdrawals pumps 21% to 40%
less for irrigation a year later, and one that stops pumps that much more.
