title: The debate no longer asks why when no answer can give a reason, and a pass is taken as a pass
---

When you hold a stand and the moderator names the rival's other stand, the question now asks "Is that still right?" and your four answers all answer it. If you pass, a moderator no longer calls you undecided when your record says where you stand: they say "Then your record will have to speak for you."
