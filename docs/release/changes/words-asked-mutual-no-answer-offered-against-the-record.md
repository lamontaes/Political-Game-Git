title: Asked about someone you saw lately, you are no longer offered a plain no
---

When someone asks whether you have seen a person you saw in the last few weeks, you are no longer offered "Nope. Not in a while." You can say when you saw them, or say it and ask how they are or why they ask, and they answer each.
