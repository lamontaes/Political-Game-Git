title: People in several looks, and a new Oval Office
---

People now come in several looks each: suits with and without ties, shirtsleeves, blazers, a dress and different hairstyles. The character creator chooses everyday clothes and work clothes separately and shows both side by side; officials and people at their own workplace wear their work clothes, everyone else their everyday clothes. The Oval Office is a new wide room where the President sits at the Resolute desk or stands behind it, staff stand around the room, and visitors sit on the couches. The address is a new close shot with the President's forearms on the desk. Everyone stands in the character creator and in their portrait. Phones, papers and pens appear only where they belong (a rally, a hearing, a desk), never at home. Picture frames in the Oval Office show plain glass for now.
