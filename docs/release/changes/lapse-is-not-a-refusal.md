---
id: lapse-is-not-a-refusal
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Letting a day go by no longer counts as turning something down
---

Optional things on the calendar that the clock simply ran past were being
recorded as though you had declined them, with your name on the decision. That
included holds you were never shown and had no control for, so the game could
credit you with refusing something you never saw.

Three things downstream read those records as refusals. An organizer could
follow up to say there was no problem about a meeting you had supposedly turned
down. A chapter invitation could show as declined when nobody had ever put the
question to you. A campaign activity could be counted as refused for the same
reason.

Time passing now records what actually happened — that the time came and went
without an answer — and only an answer you actually give is recorded as one.
Records written before the two were told apart are left exactly as they are
and now read as neither, because the game does not get to decide today what
you did then.
