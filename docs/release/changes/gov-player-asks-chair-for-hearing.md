title: Ask the committee chair to hear your held bill
---

When a committee chair holds your bill without a hearing, you can now go to the chair and ask. The chair answers from the committee's own reasons: some agree, some can be pleaded with and owe you the hearing as a favor you then owe back, and a no tells you why. A chair who holds a bill meets the sponsor's ask as a scene.
