id: own-money-estimate
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The campaign own-money line shows an estimate when no money is on record
---

A candidate the game holds no money record for now sees about how much they would have, an estimate from the typical balance for a family in the middle of the income range, in place of "Own money: not on record". Putting some of it into the campaign opens their account at that estimate and moves the money like any other gift.
