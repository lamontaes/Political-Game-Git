title: People give their own reason for where they stand on what the President said.
---

When someone brings up the President's address, their stand can now come from their own life beyond their job: a rent that takes most of what the house brings in, a relative at home on Medicare, in school, retired or in college, or a story they read in the paper. They say it in their own words ("With my mother on Medicare, I hear that part."), and when you ask them why, they answer from it.
