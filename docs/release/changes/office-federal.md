title: A day in Congress and in the White House
---

A player seated in the U.S. House or as president now spends each day in the office. A representative's week follows the House's real calendar, in Washington when it sits and at home otherwise, unless the player chooses. The player picks what stops them, and what they let pass their character does by the same rules as any member. A president nominates a cabinet the Senate votes on weeks later, sends Congress a budget request on its legal day, reads the daily brief and flies to towns to meet people.
