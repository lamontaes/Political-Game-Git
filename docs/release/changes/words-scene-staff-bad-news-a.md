title: When the press runs a story against an officeholder or their bill, one of their aides brings it in
---

When a paper or station runs a story that comes out against an officeholder, or against a bill they sponsored, one of their own aides comes in with it: which outlet ran it, what it's about, and who wrote it. They can ask about it, push back, take it, or put it off, and their day stops for it. A story in their favor brings no one in, and an officeholder without an aide hears it the usual way. You can't hire an aide yet, so for now this plays for the town's other officeholders.
