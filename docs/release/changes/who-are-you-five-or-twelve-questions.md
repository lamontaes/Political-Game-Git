section: Changed
title: "Who are you?" asks five questions, or ten to twelve, and can open differently
---

The questions at the start of a new life are shorter now. The quick route asks
five. The longer route asks about ten to twelve, and the setup screen says so,
instead of aiming for thirty to fifty.

The first question is no longer the same three scenes in the same order for
every new life. It is chosen from the strongest questions for the character's
age, so two starts can begin differently, and the same start always begins
the same way.

One new question asks you, the player, to imagine being mayor of a fictional
American city. The council has paid for a license-plate-reader pilot, and it
goes ahead only if you sign. You choose 90-day storage, 14-day storage, or not
signing. If you choose 14 days, one follow-up asks what mattered most to you.
Nothing in the question becomes part of your character's history.

Questions about adult jobs no longer appear in a young child's route unless
they are clearly framed as imagined situations. Lives started before this
change keep the questions they were asked.
