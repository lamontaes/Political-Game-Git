title: The World overview no longer shows where a fact came from
---

Opening the details under a public officeholder on the World overview listed links reading "Institutional source 1", "Institutional source 2" and so on, pointing out of the game. That is reference material about where the office record came from, and it was showing to every player in every life. It is gone from ordinary play, along with the same kind of block on the national election results screen. Nothing is lost: the records themselves are unchanged, and a developer opening the game as a developer surface still sees them.
