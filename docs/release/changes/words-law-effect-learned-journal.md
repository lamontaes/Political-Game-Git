title: A resident's journal says when they learn a law changed what they pay
---

When someone hears a story about a law and ties it to a change in what they pay, their journal now says so, naming the law by the bill that enacted it: "I found out the Free Fares for Riders Act changed what I pay." Before, the journal said nothing about it.
