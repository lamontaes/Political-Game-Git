id: player-first-local-race
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Your first local race is your place's own council, whatever it is called.
---

The player's first local race is now the lawmaking body of the place they live, under whatever name it has. A city or village runs for its council, trustees or aldermen; where a town or township is the local government, the player runs for the township board; where no town government exists and the county governs the place directly, the player runs for the county board as their council and for the county executive as their mayor where the county elects one. A city that has its own council never offers the county board or a township board instead. State and federal races stand as before.
