title: Every Supreme Court seat is filled by the President's nominee and a Senate vote
---

When a justice dies, the seat now opens and the President names a sitting
federal appeals judge or state supreme court justice. About two months later
every senator in the world votes, each for their own reasons: whether the
nominee is the choice of their own party's President, whether the nominee
already serves as a judge, and whether the nominee is from their state. A
majority confirms; a tie goes to the Vice President. A nominee the Senate
rejects sends the choice back to the President.

The Chief Justice is now chosen the same way, from the sitting associate
justices and the other judges, instead of from any adult in the world, and the
Senate no longer confirms without a vote. A justice promoted to Chief Justice
leaves an associate seat that is filled in turn, and a judge who joins the
Court leaves the court they sat on.
