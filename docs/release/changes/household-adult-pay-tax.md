id: household-adult-pay-tax
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A working parent's paycheck pays its applicable tax
---

When a child moves time forward, an adult in the household with an existing
recorded job receives weekly pay into the adult's own money. Applicable payroll
tax is now assessed on each new paycheck. What is withheld leaves the adult's
balance and reaches the public account; any unpaid amount remains a separate
debt. The child does not gain knowledge of the adult's pay from this transfer.
