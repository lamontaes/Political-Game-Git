title: Who leaves after a disaster, where they go and who moves in follow their lives
---

A family whose home a flood or storm wrecks no longer leaves town on a
chance. They weigh the wreck against what holds them: a renter living alone
may go to a parent in another town, while an owner with children in school
stays to rebuild. Anyone who leaves goes where their reason or their family
is, never to a random state. Newcomers no longer arrive at a fixed rate. One
comes when a job in town is left open by a worker who moved away, died or
retired, nobody in town is looking for work, and the pay covers the rent.
