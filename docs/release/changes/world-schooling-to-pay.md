title: Schooling now affects the pay you are hired at
---

Adults with more schooling are now hired at somewhat higher pay than adults with less for the same job, and a job offer shows the pay that person would actually get. A teenager who finishes high school is hired at a diploma's pay, and one who does not is paid as a worker without one, so a town that raises its graduation rate sees it in its households' pay.
