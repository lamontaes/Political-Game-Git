---
id: rival-campaigns-without-dice
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A rival campaign acts from its own records, not dice
---

A rival candidate's way of campaigning now comes from their own temperament:
a sociable, steady candidate works the doors, one who would rather not meet
strangers or who weighs every word puts out paid messages, and one who avoids
conflict works through the party and donors. A rival with no strong leaning
simply answers the race in front of them. A rival's paid message now costs a
set amount when the committee can afford it, and everything left when it
cannot. Its messages and events now move voters exactly as the player's own
do, with no luck added, and its weekly choices are no longer nudged at random.
