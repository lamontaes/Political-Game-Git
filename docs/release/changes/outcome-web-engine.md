title: Crime now responds to hard times the way the research says it does.
---

When work dries up in a town, burglaries climb about 3% for each point of
unemployment, while robberies and assaults rise much less, as studies of real
American places find. This is the first part of a larger change: how schools,
health, housing, safety, the environment, the economy and elections affect
one another now comes from one shared, researched table, and more of it
switches on as the world starts tracking each measure.
