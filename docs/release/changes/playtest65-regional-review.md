The home state now follows the White House, with Government, Population and Voting cards over a suitable regional illustration when one is available. Population figures identify their historical year and source. The voting card shows the November 2024 Census survey with reported counts, group rates, rounding and uncertainty; it does not present survey estimates as current voter rolls. D.C. keeps its distinct government and Congress sequence. Compatible alternate regional views can be browsed without changing the information card or saved game. Unsupported places and seasons keep the information cards without an illustration.

The childhood questionnaire introduces its imagined caregiver and sister before asking about them. Existing answers and saved lives retain their identities.
