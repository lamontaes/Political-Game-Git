title: Parties shows each party's officeholders as faces, one party at a time
---

Politics → Parties now shows the district map and its key on a plate, your party (or the parties you can join, in a tray) beside it, and each party's officeholders as face cards, one party at a time on a rail of party tabs, paged with arrows. Joining a party keeps you on Parties so you see your new membership.
