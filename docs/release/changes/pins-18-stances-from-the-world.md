title: Lawmakers and the public now read the world they live in.
---

A legislator's sense of where the country stands now comes from the laws the world's own states have passed, so when other states enact a policy, lawmakers everywhere drift toward it, and a state feels its neighbors' new laws. What people think of a question is no longer copied from a national poll: it comes from the people in your world, their leanings, their own lives and what they have heard, so two worlds can disagree on the same question.
