title: Your state's capitol at dawn, at night, in the rain and in winter
---

Each state's own capitol building now changes with the day and the season: a sunrise morning, lit windows at night, a gray rainy day with wet pavement, and a winter look with bare trees and snow where the state really gets it. Warm places like Hawaii, Guam and American Samoa keep their green in winter. The building, its grounds and the view stay exactly as in the midday painting, so it is always the same place.
