id: player-shifts-follow-the-minimum-wage
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: When a law lifts the minimum wage above what your shift pays, your shift pays the new wage.
---

A completed shift at a job you took through the career paths, such as shop
assistant, is now paid at least the minimum wage in force where you live on the
day you worked it, when a law passed in play set that wage and it is above what
the shift paid. The rise is recorded as a change to your pay terms that names
the law, so your Journal shows it and later shifts pay the same. A law that
lowers or repeals the wage cuts nobody's pay, and a wage that is already below
what your shift pays changes nothing.
