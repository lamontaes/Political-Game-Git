The story director now decides which moments in a life become scenes. A moment that reaches the person being played becomes a scene when a situation type it opens can be filled from the records and it ranks within the life's pace: about one scene a year in early childhood, two in middle childhood, three in adolescence and four as an adult. Each scene records where it happens, who is there, each person's role and bearing, and the days it stays open. A life's earlier years stay in the journal. Moments the library cannot stage are logged with the reason, and `npm run story:coverage` totals them. Players see the scenes once the English engine can word them.
