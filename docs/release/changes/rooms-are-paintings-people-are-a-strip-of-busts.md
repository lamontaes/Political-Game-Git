title: A room is its painting, and the people in it stand as busts on a strip
---

Nobody is painted into a room any more. The people who are there stand as busts on a strip of soft glass along the foot of the room, with their names; a crowd names the people you know and says how many more. Click someone with something to say to talk. In a talk the strip joins the top of the talk box, and whoever speaks is lit and a little larger. The small round face in the talk box is gone.
