title: A federal law to expand passenger rail brings more Amtrak riders
---

When Congress votes to pay for expanding passenger rail, Amtrak riders grow in
every place it serves. The first added trains run two and a half years after
the law, and riders keep growing for 15 years toward Amtrak's own plan of 62.5%
more. Rail plans rarely reach their forecasts, so each world takes between a
fifth and three quarters of that rise. A later law that ends the expansion
takes the added riders away.
