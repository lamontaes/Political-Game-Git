title: A state law raising the minimum wage raises your neighbors' pay
---

Before, a state's minimum wage was fixed at its 2026 rate for the whole
game, whatever its legislature passed.

Now, a state law can set a new minimum wage from the day it takes effect.
Everyone in town paid less than the new rate gets a raise, starting with
their first pay period that begins on or after that day, and the paycheck
names the law that raised it. In a test game in Omaha, a Nebraska law
raising the minimum wage to $18.00 an hour raised 14 of 79 town jobs, and
52 paychecks were paid at the new rate within 100 days.
