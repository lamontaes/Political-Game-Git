section: Changed
title: One menu on the left: the icon rail
---

The Menu panel that repeated the icon rail is gone. The rail stays beside any open screen, your portrait under it opens Who you are with Jobs and study as its second tab, and the Guide and Dev mode sit in Options on the Escape key. Screens open wider, since the panel no longer takes the left third of the window.
