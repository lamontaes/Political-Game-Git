id: state-oversight-one-rule
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Every state's campaign-money regulator follows the same federal calendar
---

A complaint about a candidate's campaign money now moves on the same
calendar in every state: the respondent is notified within 5 days and has
15 days to answer, as federal law sets for the Federal Election
Commission. Before, each state was given a made-up calendar and fine of its
own. The fine for each misused payment is now about $6,789, the Federal
Election Commission's average penalty per closed case in fiscal 2025, and a
regulator's own review can flag a filed payment after 30 days. A state's own
rules replace these once they are researched.
