title: The economy now changes month by month
---

A new game's economy now keeps moving after you begin. At the start of each month, News carries the country's unemployment rate for the month before. Once a year has passed it also reports how much prices have risen, and each quarter how fast the economy grew. A shipping disruption abroad now shows up in growth and prices while it lasts, then eases gradually, and a flood or severe storm where you live slows the local economy until the repairs are finished. These figures belong to your world, not real government data, and games started before this update keep the history they already had.
