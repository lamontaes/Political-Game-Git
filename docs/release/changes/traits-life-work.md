title: People miss work when they are ill, not because of their temperament
---

Missing a shift now follows a person's health: someone who is ill stays home, and about 1.7 in 100 scheduled shifts are missed, as in the national survey of work absences. Temperament still tips a close call, but only a little, as the research on absence finds. Before, almost every missed shift came from a person's temperament alone.
