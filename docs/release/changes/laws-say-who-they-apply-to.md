title: A passed law's page says who it applies to
---

The section of a law that says who qualifies, or who must follow it, now
shows on the law's page in the words the law was passed with. Where the town
has bodies of that kind on record, the page counts them. Where the answer
turns on something the world does not track, such as a household's income or
a bridge's inspection rating, the page says who meets it is not known yet,
rather than showing zero. A section that only states why the law was written
is no longer listed as a part nothing acts on.
