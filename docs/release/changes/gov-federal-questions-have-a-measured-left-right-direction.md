title: The President and Congress now take sides on national questions
---

The President and every member of Congress now hold a view on questions only federal law answers, such as tariffs, the federal minimum wage, immigration and student loans. Each side follows how their party really voted, so the President's address speaks on national points and Congress writes bills on them.
