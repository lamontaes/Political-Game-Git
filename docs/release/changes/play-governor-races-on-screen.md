title: Election night shows every governor race around the country on its own page, two columns that fit the window.
---

After your race is called, election night turns to a page of the other states' governor races: each state, the winning party and the margin in whole points, in two columns that fit a 1024 by 768 window with nothing cut off. Before, the rows were added under your race's count and the ones past the panel's foot could not be seen.
