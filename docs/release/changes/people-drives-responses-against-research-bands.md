title: People answer a death in the family, and a layoff, the way research says people do
---

About two in three people carry on after a death in the family, as grief research finds; the rest take up a cause, resolve to protect their family or answer a calling. A worker who is laid off can now hold a grudge against the person who let them go, and may confront them, which costs the two of them their closeness. Whether a person carries on or holds on still follows their own temperament: agreeable people let it go, and people who hold on to a quarrel do not.
