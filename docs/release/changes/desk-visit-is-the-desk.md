---
id: desk-visit-is-the-desk
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Telling a visitor you'll sign or veto a bill now signs or vetoes it.
---

When a bill reaches your desk as mayor or governor and its sponsor, or a group against it, comes to see you, the visit now stops your day. If your last words to them are that you'll sign it ("Bring the pen.") the bill is signed, and if they are that it goes back with a veto, it is vetoed. A plain no, or saying you'll decide by the deadline, leaves the bill on your desk as before. With the desk stop switched off, the visit does not stop your day and nothing is decided for you. The replies that offered to sign once the sponsor brought a fix, gave their word on a follow-up, or carried a second cleanup bill are gone until the game can bring the fix back and hold them to it.
