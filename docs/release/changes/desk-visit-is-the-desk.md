id: desk-visit-is-the-desk
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Saying yes or no to someone who comes to your office about a bill now signs or vetoes it.
---

When a bill reaches your desk as mayor or governor and its sponsor, or a group against it, comes to see you, the visit now stops your day. If you tell them you'll sign it, the bill is signed; if you tell them no, it goes back with a veto. Asking for a fix or saying you'll decide by the deadline leaves it on your desk as before. The reply that offered to sign if the sponsor carried a second cleanup bill is gone until the game can hold them to that promise.
