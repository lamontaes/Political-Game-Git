---
id: desk-visit-is-the-desk
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Telling a visitor you'll sign or veto a bill now signs or vetoes it.
---

When a bill reaches your desk as mayor or governor and its sponsor, or a group against it, comes to see you, the visit now stops your day. If your last words to them are that you'll sign it ("Bring the pen.") the bill is signed, and if they are that it goes back with a veto, it is vetoed. A plain no, asking for a fix, or saying you'll decide by the deadline leaves the bill on your desk as before. With the desk stop switched off, the visit does not stop your day and nothing is decided for you. The reply that offered to sign if the sponsor carried a second cleanup bill is gone until the game can hold them to that promise.
