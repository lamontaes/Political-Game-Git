title: Petition signatures follow recorded requests and answers
---

Petitions close on named, eligible signed records from recorded requests and signer decisions. Circulators reach people through the existing campaign field model. A saved human answer follows the canonical life action and its player-choice appraisal; an ignored or generated answer stays unsigned. Duplicate, eligibility and clerk review records explain invalid signatures. Proposition petitions share the signature counter. Required geographic certification and ballot referral remain explicit next steps.
