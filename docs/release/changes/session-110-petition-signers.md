title: Petition signatures follow recorded requests and answers
---

Recall petitions close on named, eligible signed records from recorded requests and signer decisions. Circulators reach people through the existing campaign field model. A saved human answer follows the canonical life action; an ignored request stays unsigned. Duplicate, eligibility and clerk review records explain invalid signatures. Shared proposition petition integration is still pending.
