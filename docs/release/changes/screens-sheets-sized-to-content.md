title: Screens end where their content ends, and Who you are says what you're like
---

Journal, Jobs and study, Money and the other reading screens are now as tall and as wide as what they hold, instead of a few lines in the corner of a full-window panel. Who you are says what you're like in a few plain sentences ("You act on impulse. You keep to yourself.") in place of the Traits table, and lays its sections out side by side. A lone chapter title over the journal is gone.
