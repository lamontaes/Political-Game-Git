title: Six Guam villages elect a Vice Mayor.
---

Hågat, Barrigada, Mangilao, Tamuning, Dededo and Yigo each elect a Vice Mayor alongside the Mayor, and both races are on the same November ballot. When a Mayor leaves office, the Vice Mayor takes the seat for the rest of the term, and the Vice Mayor's seat stays empty until the term ends. In a village with no Vice Mayor, or one whose Vice Mayor's seat is empty, the village's planning council fills it.
