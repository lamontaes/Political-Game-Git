section: Improved
title: Paydays and the daily record check stay quick in later years
---

After each payday, the game looks for pay a law changed so the worker can
notice it. That look listed every payment arrangement and pay term in the
world first, even when no law had changed anyone's pay; it now looks up only
the records a law names. The check that each day's new records can be saved
also stops remembering every value inside every record, which had made its
memory, and the work of clearing it, grow every year. In South Fork,
Pennsylvania, each of the first ten game years runs 0.4 to 5.5 seconds
faster, and the world after each year is exactly the same as before.
