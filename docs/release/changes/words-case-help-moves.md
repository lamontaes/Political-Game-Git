title: Office hours says what the constituent's own case holds
---

When a resident brings a case to your office hours, they now talk about their own case: how long since a layoff, how their savings stand, what they are paid against the minimum wage. What you say decides the case: promising a call or handing it to an aide takes it up, and a no turns it away.
