title: At office hours, people bring the trouble their own records hold, and your answer decides their case.
---

A resident who comes to your office hours now says the problem their own case holds: the job they lost and how long ago, how their savings stand, or the pay under the legal floor. Your replies are what your office can really do: take it up, which sends it to the workforce office or the labor standards office; hand it to an aide on your staff; or turn it away for the cases already on your desk. What you choose decides the case: taking it up refers it, and a no turns it away. Stock lines that said the same thing in every town are gone.
