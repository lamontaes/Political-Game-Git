title: People name a policy question by its subject when they talk about it
---

In talk, a policy question is named by its subject ("drug prices", "the retirement age", "Medicaid") where the line asks about it or says where someone stands on it, instead of by the bill's own words. The bill's words stay on the paper and the screens.
