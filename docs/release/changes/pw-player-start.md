title: The player turns 18 in play, and an idle young adult meets people and looks for work
---

A new game opens on the resident whose 18th birthday is coming soonest, so the first skip reaches it. A young adult with free time can now look for work, meet someone new through a friend, at work or around town, and find out where the town's offices, meeting places and groups are. Each choice carries the reasons behind it.
