title: A group's endorsement in the News says what the group works for
---

When a group backs a candidate, the story is no longer a one-line brief. It has a headline, a lede with the vote's day, and a second sentence saying what the group works for, taken from the stance its endorsement stood on ("Its cause is free fares on public transit.").
