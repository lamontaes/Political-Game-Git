title: Build a speech part by part on the campaigns sheet
---

A candidate now builds a speech on the campaigns sheet: pick one of four opening lines, then two parts at the core of the speech, then one of four closing lines, each worded from your own record. Then choose where to give it and see the people it reached. This replaces the single fixed Speech button.
