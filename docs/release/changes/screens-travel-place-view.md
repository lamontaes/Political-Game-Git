title: A place in Travel opens its own view
---

A Travel card says only what is yours about a place (you work here, what you can do there, where you serve, where you file), never what someone you know does there, and counts nothing as "and N more". Clicking a card opens the place in its own view: a large picture, what you do there (at the filing office, every race it takes, each a way in), who works there, and Go to travel.
