title: Members of Congress weigh what a budget line sends their state
---

When Congress votes a budget, each member now weighs what each line sends their own state per resident against the national average: a state that lives on farm payments or veterans' care pulls its members toward more for that line, one that barely sees a line toward less. Their vote records it as its own reason beside their views, their district, their party and the press.
