title: Find saved general election summaries in the World record
---

The World record now has a General election summaries section for saved
Congress and state legislative general results. Select a year to reach older
results, then Show more to continue through that year's entries. Each entry
shows the date, kind of election, jurisdiction when recorded, and the summary
the world saved at the time. Individual contest results remain in their own
list.
