title: Homeowners without a mortgage get their property tax bill on their state's due dates instead of a little each month.
---

Homeowners with a mortgage still pay a twelfth of the tax each month through escrow; owners without one are billed a share on their place's due dates, so a bill can land as a lump and be missed.
