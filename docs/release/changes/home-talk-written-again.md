title: The people you live with talk again about your day, the rent and the news.

After the line cut of November 17, a housemate with something new had nothing to say about it. Now the household asks how school, a shift or filing for office went, frets about the rent coming due, weighs in on what the council or the government decided, and tells you when they've lost a job.
