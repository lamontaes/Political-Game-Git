title: Keep the ordinary day focused on choices that need the player
---

The room calls its situation “What’s happening.” The Calendar shows dated
activities without separate Day and Week skip boxes or commute rows; the time
buttons move time. Date format is set in Options. A routine work session runs
through the clock without a separate calendar stop, and its pay remains in the
saved record. The leisure-plan scene and grocery route are removed, and
ordinary conversation no longer offers household errands. Candidate guidance
keeps unknown filing rules out of the organizer's spoken answer.
