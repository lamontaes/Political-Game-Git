title: Connect a recorded finding-subject resignation to office continuity
---

A bounded press adapter offers office responses only for a finding's actual respondent and held office. The existing player office-answer caller now records the chosen words and the subject's own selected decision before using the existing resignation writer. Missing, blocked or undecided inputs do not create a resignation. Autonomous caller integration remains separate, and browser verification is pending.
