impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Fewer residents leave town for a job offer
---

Residents now look for work out of town less often, and weigh an offer that
pays no more than they earn now more lightly. About 2 in every 100 adults a
year move away for a job offer, in line with how often Americans move for a
new job. A resident whose pay is not on record is no longer treated as
earning nothing, and someone working short hours is no longer counted twice
as both short of hours and short of pay.
