id: unrest-comes-from-lasting-anger
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Unrest, threats and attacks on public figures now follow from how long anger lasts
---

Unrest in a state used to break out on a roll each quarter while its anger
ran high, and a threat or an attack on a public figure came on further rolls.
Now unrest starts whenever a state's anger is over the line and lasts until
anger falls back. Once it has lasted, the state's most prominent political
figure is threatened: its governor, then its U.S. senators, then its
U.S. representatives, then its party organizers. An attack comes only when the anger that follows a threat
has added up past a set line, and a threat lapses when the unrest calms. Each step is recorded with what it rested on.
