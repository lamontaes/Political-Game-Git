title: Towns can be recalled only where their state's recall law reaches them.
---

South Dakota's recall needs one of the causes its law lists, so a council vote alone no longer starts one there. Wyoming's recall covers only commission-form towns, and Mississippi's covers only county officials, so ordinary towns in those states have none. Where a state's recall depends on a town adopting a form or charter, a town that hasn't adopted one follows the state's default instead of a national estimate.
