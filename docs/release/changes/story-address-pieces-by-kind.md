title: Every kind of address now opens in its own voice.
---

A speech no longer opens the same way whatever kind it is. A President or governor giving a hard-year address now greets the room, says why they are there, swears the oath, says what they believe and speaks to the people who voted against them in a grave, candid voice. A hopeful address does it warmly, a combative one hard-edged, a victory lap proudly, a mourning address quietly and briefly, and a policy push in plain working terms. What they believe is still said in their party's terms.
