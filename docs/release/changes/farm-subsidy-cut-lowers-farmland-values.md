title: A federal law that cuts farm subsidies lowers farmland values.
---

When Congress answers yes to "should federal payments to farmers be cut?",
every state's farm payments fall by about a fifth, the usual size of a cut in
the years payments have fallen since 1996. Payments are built into what farmland
sells for, so a year later the value of farmland drops in each state, most
where payments are large next to what the land is worth. A later law that
answers no restores the payments, and land values recover as land trades. Farmland
value per acre is now tracked for all 56 places, starting from the 2025 land
values survey.
