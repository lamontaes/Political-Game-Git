title: News opens on your own paper every time
---

News no longer opens on whichever outlet printed the newest story. It opens on the outlet you follow, or else your town's paper of record, which has its tab from the first day. The tabs keep the same order while you play, and the tab you last chose stays in front when you close and reopen News.
