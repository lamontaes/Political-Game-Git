title: Vote on the bills put to your own chamber
---

A player who holds a legislative seat was recorded absent on every vote the
chamber took, with no way to cast a ballot. Now each question you sit on,
whether a committee report, a floor vote, the other chamber's changes or a
veto override, goes on your calendar the day before it is put, and time
stops there so you can decide. The Work screen lists the votes coming up
with Yes, No and Present, not voting. Your ballot is what the roll call
records. If you have not decided by the day of the vote, you are recorded
absent; nobody votes for you.
