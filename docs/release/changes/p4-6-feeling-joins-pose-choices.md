---
id: p4-6-feeling-joins-pose-choices
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A person's feeling joins their pose choices instead of a roll deciding it
---

A standing person whose face shows a feeling, such as anger, sadness or
surprise, no longer has a roll against a fixed share decide whether they
stand the way it reads. The poses a feeling reads in now join the activity's
own poses as weighted choices. An open person carries the feeling in their
stance more often and a guarded one less, read from their recorded traits.
