title: Town council races fall on the November election day where state law puts them
---

A race for a town's own council used to be decided four weeks after filing,
wherever the town was. In the states whose municipal election law holds town
elections on the November general election day, the race is now held on that
day, in the even or odd years the state sets. Ely, Minnesota, for example,
votes on November 3, 2026. Where state law leaves the choice to each town, the
town's choice is drawn from the options the law allows.

Where the law read so far does not fix a day, as with town meeting day or a
spring election, the race stays four weeks out. That remains a marked
placeholder until the town's calendar has been researched.
