title: You see people at their jobs
---

The places people work are no longer empty. At the clerk's counter, the diner,
the hospital hallway, the union hall, the church supper hall, the community
room, the county party office, the campaign storefront, the store, the office
and the classroom, the townspeople whose shift it is stand there, dressed for
work, in their uniform if their job has one. The clerk stands behind the
counter. Come back at night and the counter is empty. Click someone to see who
they are.
