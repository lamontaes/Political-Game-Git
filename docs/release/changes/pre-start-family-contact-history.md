title: Family contacts remember their recorded time together
---

New lives now link their dated, generated family-time events to the same relatives' contact history. A family member who spent time with the character before play no longer appears as though they have never spoken. The interaction keeps the event's date and two recorded participants; it does not add another visit or a new relative.
