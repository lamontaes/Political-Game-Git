title: An at-large council or board is one race on Elections
---

A council or board whose seats are elected at large now shows on Politics, Elections as one race, such as "Council member", with everyone running for any of its seats and how many seats it fills. Races are no longer named after whoever holds a seat now, and File for it files for that one race. The list beside a race's page also keeps all its races in view when the page is long.
