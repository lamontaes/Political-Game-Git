title: Endorsements weigh temperament at the size research finds
---

Whether a resident publicly backs a candidate now turns on their temperament at the size a study of political persuasion finds, rather than at a general size that made temperament decide most endorsements. Wills and trusts now read their own temperament weight, unchanged for now.
