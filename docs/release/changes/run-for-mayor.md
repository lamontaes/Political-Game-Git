title: A life can run for mayor where the town's voters elect one
---

Towns whose voters elect their mayor now offer a mayor's race beside the
council race. It falls on the same election day and is won the same way. The
winner takes office as mayor, and the sitting mayor's term ends that day. The
office screen and the day's summary then read "Mayor", with the length of the
term.

Where the game has read a city's own government, that decides whether the
mayor is elected and for how long. Duluth and Boise elect theirs; in Iowa City,
Bangor and Charlottesville the council chooses, so there is no race. Every
other town draws from national survey shares: about three towns in four elect
their mayor directly, and a four-year term is the most common. That draw is
labeled as typical on the screen. It is a marked placeholder until the rules
by town size, and those of the larger cities the game has not read, have been
researched.
