title: A failed veto override remembers who could still be moved
---

When a vote to override a veto is taken, its public record now says which chamber decided it and which members there voted no or stayed out, the most persuadable first. If the sponsor talks with one of them afterward and the member agrees, or the talk goes badly, that member's next vote on the sponsor's bills weighs it, and the vote's reasons name the conversation.
