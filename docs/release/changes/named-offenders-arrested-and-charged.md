title: Police arrest a named resident for a reported crime, and prosecutors take the case
---

A reported crime in town is now laid at the door of the resident whose own
circumstances point to it: being young, being out of work (more so for a
crime that takes money or goods), a past record, knowing the victim, and a
taste for risk. Nobody is picked by chance, and when nobody the world names
fits, the case stays open. Police arrest that person when the victim knows
them, when they have a record, or when their circumstances point to them
plainly. The arrest is public and names them, and it goes to prosecutors,
where the case is charged, pleaded or tried, and sentenced. A resident
already answering for a recent case is not blamed for a new one. Every
weight is a placeholder until it is researched.
