title: Mayors and governors know the lawmakers they work with
---

A mayor and the council used to meet as strangers, and so did a governor and the legislators whose bills cross the governor's desk. A mayor now sits with the council at its meetings, and a county executive does where that state's executive presides, so they know each other from day one. A governor knows the legislature's leaders from their weekly meetings while it sits, and knows a lawmaker once the governor has signed that lawmaker's bill. None of these people greets the other as a stranger.
