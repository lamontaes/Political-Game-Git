title: Each state's reserve law fills its rainy-day fund to its own size
---

Under a minimum-reserve law, each state now fills its rainy-day fund to the
size its own law sets, and moves in at most the yearly share its law sets, as
NASBO's 2021 survey of state budget laws reports them: Rhode Island 5% of
revenue at 3% a year, Nevada 20%, Missouri 7.5%. A state whose law sets no
size, and every county and city, takes the median of the 36 states that set
one, 10%, marked as estimated from the average. Before, every government used
the same hand-set 5% floor and 1% a year.
