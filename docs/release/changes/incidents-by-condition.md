---
id: incidents-by-condition
impact: none
---

The incident engine no longer draws. Its drawn occurrence mode and the
probability evaluator are gone, and the engine's three test incidents (a local
storm or flood, an economic slowdown and an outbreak) now occur only when the
world has recorded their cause: a hazard, the start of a recession, or a
reported outbreak. Nothing changes for a player: the production boundary already
keeps those three definitions out of every player's world, and the incidents a
player can meet (unrest and political threat) were conditions already. Saves
written while the drawn mode existed still open.
