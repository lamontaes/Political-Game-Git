title: Attend a public sitting and appoint a compiled city manager
---

In Charlottesville, a resident can attend an authored public council sitting
without gaining a seat. A seated council member can appoint the City Manager
under the compiled charter appointment, and that office and history survive
save and reload. A visitor, a second appointment, and a repeated write after
reload are refused. Ordinance introduction and votes stay closed until the
compiled introduction, readings, and post-adoption gaps are filled.
