title: Attend a public sitting and appoint a compiled city manager
---

In Charlottesville, a resident can attend an authored public council sitting
without gaining a seat. A seated council member can advance a City Manager
election only when the same government's recorded vote meets the compiled
Charter § 12 quorum of three councilors; one member's standing is not the
appointment. That office and history survive save and reload. A visitor, a
lone-member or no-quorum roll, a vote of another government, a second
appointment, and a repeated write after reload are refused. Ordinance
introduction and votes stay closed until the compiled introduction, readings,
and post-adoption gaps are filled. Inspection does not create a sitting.
The 60/90-minute public-session helper remains authored review convenience,
not ordinary meeting discovery.
