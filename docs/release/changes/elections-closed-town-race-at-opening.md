title: A town race already filed when the game opens still happens.
---

A world that opened after a town's filing had closed used to skip that town's November race and move to the next one. That put a four-year office two years off its real cycle, so a Mayor elected in 2022 appeared as elected in 2024, with the next race in 2028. The race now happens on its real day. The people who filed are on the ballot, and the Elections page says its filing has closed.
