title: Turning down someone who helped you gets around
---

When you say no to someone who once helped you, they may tell the people they
confide in: their family and household, the people they are warm toward (an
outgoing person tells more of them, a reserved one only the closest), and
anyone who saw the favor done. Whether they tell is up to them, from how much the favor meant
and who they are. People who heard about it are a little less willing to meet
you, or to back your campaign, when you ask.

A person caught up in a news story is now reacted to by the same circle of
confidants, instead of the first six relatives and housemates.
