title: People in the same scene now say it in more than one way
---

Scenes that used to have one way to say a line now have three or more: a bribe offered or pressed, a hallway word, a reporter's question, a donor's ask, a town hall question, a party chair's push, and the talks at home about running, winning and working late. The wording varies by tone and by what is on the record, and where a reply drew on how the owner talks, it keeps that voice.
