id: world48-compliance-sentences-drop-citations
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Campaign-finance answers say what the rule is instead of quoting the statute at you
---

When a campaign is told it cannot take a contribution yet, or that it may, the sentence now says what the rule is in ordinary words. It no longer opens with the statute's chapter and section number. Where the answer came from is still recorded against the ruling, so anyone who wants to check it can, but nothing on a screen reads like a citation any more.
