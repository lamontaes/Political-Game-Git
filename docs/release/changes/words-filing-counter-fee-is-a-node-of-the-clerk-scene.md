title: At the filing counter the fee and the refusal are part of the clerk's one talk
---

The clerk now tells the fee with the race, in the same answer, and a candidate who cannot pay it is refused as the clerk's reply to "I'll file", with "I'll come back with it" as the next thing they can say, instead of fee lines said in separate steps beside the talk.
