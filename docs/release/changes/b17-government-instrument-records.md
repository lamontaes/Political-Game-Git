title: Executive orders and regulations enter the shared law record
---

Executive orders and regulations can be recorded as government measures with
filing, publication, effective and expiry dates. The shared law reader ranks
them below their government's statutes and stops applying them after expiry.
Orders are limited to branch management or terms a current statute delegates;
out-of-range and independent-policy clauses receive a plain refusal reason.
