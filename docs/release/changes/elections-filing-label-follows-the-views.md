title: A resident who files for a partisan seat before the party meeting has met them files under the party their views name
---

A town resident the monthly party meeting had not yet met used to file for a House or state seat with no party label, so the ballot could read as missing a major party. They now take their party by the meeting's own rule first and file under it.
