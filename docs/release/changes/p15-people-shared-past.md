Gives the separate core prototype's residents a shared past from their own records. Each child now attends one of their place's real public schools, chosen among the schools teaching their grade and weighted by students per grade, instead of every child in a town sharing one school; children who live together share a campus. Residents of the same employer now know a workgroup of coworkers by name. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build. School assignment, coworker groups and their tunable size are registered stopgaps that still block release.

Pairs who shared a class or a workgroup may also have fallen out in that shared past, more often between people quick to conflict and less reliable, and more the longer they shared. Each falling-out is a dated fact both people keep, so an old classmate or coworker can return with something at stake. About two thirds of adults have at least one, close to the 68% of U.S. adults who report having ended a friendship.
