title: The President's opening address is different in every world
---

The address that opens the game now speaks in the President's own voice. A
combative President leans on urgent, fighting lines and a warm one on hopeful
and consoling ones, drawn from their recorded personality. The address also
asks Congress and the states for what that President actually wants, from
their own views and signature issues, so two worlds open with two different
speeches.
