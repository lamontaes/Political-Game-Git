Seated people now show what they are doing: reading or writing at a desk, on
the phone or with legs crossed while they wait, leaning in or with hands folded
while they listen, hands folded or leaning in at a meeting, and plainly or
leaning back when they just sit. Men also stand with their hands in their
pockets and sit with an ankle on the knee. Six outfits for each presentation
are painted in the new seated poses; anyone in another outfit sits plainly.
