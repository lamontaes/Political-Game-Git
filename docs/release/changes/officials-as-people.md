title: Congress, governors and legislators are people with their own minds
---

Every member of Congress, every governor, your state's other statewide officials and legislators, and the leaders of every other state's legislature are now people when a new game starts. Each has a party, a career, colleagues, personality traits and views of their own: some sit in their party's liberal or conservative wing, some near the middle, and each cares about a few issues more than most. When Congress or your legislature votes, each member weighs their own views, their district, their party's leaders, their wing, their ambitions and the next election, and how closely the press is watching, so votes no longer split strictly along party lines. Independents who caucus with a party usually vote with it; an independent who caucuses with no one decides from the reasons they left the parties.
