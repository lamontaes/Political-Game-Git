title: Nine of the lines you hear most now name what the talk was about.
---

When you put off a job offer, the caller names the deadline, the pay or the job instead of a generic goodbye. Someone who brought up a news story names it, or the paper that ran it, as they wrap up. A colleague after the governor's speech no longer sounds like an old flame.
