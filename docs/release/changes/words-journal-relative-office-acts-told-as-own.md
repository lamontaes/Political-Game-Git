title: Your journal tells a relative's bill and vote as theirs, not yours
---

When someone in your household holds office, your journal no longer says "I introduced" their bill or "I voted for" it. It says "My mother put forward the Free Fares for Riders Ordinance." and "My mother voted for the Free Fares for Riders Ordinance." A moment that belongs to someone else is told only with a line that names them.
