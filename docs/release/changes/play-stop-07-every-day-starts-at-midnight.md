title: Days start in the morning and an outing spends its hours
---

Pressing Day from an evening now lands on the next morning instead of skipping to a midnight, Week lands on the same morning seven days on, and a calendar jump lands on that day's morning. A campaign outing that takes three hours moves the clock three hours.
