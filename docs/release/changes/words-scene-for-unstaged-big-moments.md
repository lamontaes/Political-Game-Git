title: A candidate whose speech moved you comes over to ask for your vote
---

When a candidate's speech at a town hall or a campaign event changes your mind on something, the candidate now comes over afterward and asks for your vote, and you can ask what they'd do, say yes, say not yet, or keep your vote to yourself. Before, that moment stopped your day with nobody there to answer. A neighbor who invites you to join a cause now names the cause.
