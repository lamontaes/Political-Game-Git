title: Civic attendance follows the town’s recorded council calendar.
---

Quarterly civic reviews bind attendance to the latest council meeting held in that town during the quarter and date it on the meeting. A still-scheduled meeting is eligible only on the review date. Canceled, future and past unheld meetings produce no attendance. Contacts keep their existing route, and saved attendance retains its meeting identity.
