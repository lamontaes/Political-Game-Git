id: life-changing-favors-last
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Help that changed someone's life is still owed years later
---

A favor's felt debt used to fade to nothing within about three years, however
much the help had meant. Now a life-changing favor never fades below a marked
debt, and a great one never below a slight one, until it is returned. Someone
who lets things slip holds half as much. In a new life in Billings, Montana,
eight neighbors the player had helped through a hard year three years earlier
agreed to meet on all 80 asks, against 50 of 80 without that help. A slight
favor from three years earlier changed nothing.
