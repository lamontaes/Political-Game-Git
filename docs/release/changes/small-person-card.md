title: The person card is a small card
---

Selecting somebody opens a small card: their picture, their name, how you know
them ("Your housemate", or where you work together) and up to three things you
have seen of their temperament ("Confrontational, cautious and self-conscious,
from what you've seen."), with when you last spoke. Only the ways of reaching
them that work right now are drawn. Everything else, from the facts and
connected people to why an action is unavailable, is under More details.
