title: People answer in a scene the way they decide everywhere else
---

In a scene with a purpose, the person asking now answers each of your replies by the same temperament read every other decision in the game uses, so someone who presses in a negotiation presses in talk too. The lines your character leads with follow the same read, a lie included.
