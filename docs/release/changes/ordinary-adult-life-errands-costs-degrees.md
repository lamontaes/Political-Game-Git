"Get things done" now clears the week's errands, and new requests keep arriving
while one is waiting. On the first of each month you pay $1,500 for rent, food
and bills. Money and property offers a home for $250,000 with $50,000 down and
$1,200 a month in place of rent. An officeholder you play is paid $60,000 a
year, weekly. Associate's, bachelor's and master's degrees can be applied for
and finished. Every dollar figure here is a placeholder until
research on real costs, pay, home prices and admissions comes back.
