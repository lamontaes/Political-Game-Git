Choosing "Get things done" now does the week's errands, and a new list comes a
week later. Each week the person you play pays a share of the household's
rent, food and bills. Associate's, bachelor's and master's degrees can be
applied for and finished.

## What changed

The errands take two and a half hours on the calendar and clear the list.
Before, the same list came back every week.

A life with something already waiting can be offered one new thing at a time.
Before, one unanswered request stopped anything new from arriving.

The weekly cost is one placeholder figure for every state and town until the
research on real costs comes back. The first week you cannot cover becomes a
moment of its own.

A finished bachelor's counts for law school and graduate study. Until the
admissions research comes back, every applicant is admitted, and length and
tuition match the game's existing degree paths.
