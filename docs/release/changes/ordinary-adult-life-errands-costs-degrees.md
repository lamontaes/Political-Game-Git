impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Errands get done, living costs are charged, and degrees can be applied for
---

Choosing "Get things done" now does the week's errands. It takes two and a
half hours on the calendar and clears the list, and a new list comes a week
later. Before, the choice changed nothing and the same list came back every
week.

A life with something already waiting can now be offered one new thing at a
time. Before, one unanswered request stopped anything new from arriving.

Each week the person you play pays a share of the household's rent, food and
bills out of their own money. The amount is one placeholder figure for every
state and town until the research on real costs comes back. The first week
you cannot cover becomes a moment of its own.

Associate's, bachelor's and master's degrees at colleges that offer them can
now be applied for, accepted and finished. A finished bachelor's counts for
law school and graduate study. Until the research on admissions and tuition
comes back, every applicant is admitted and the length and tuition match the
game's existing degree paths.
