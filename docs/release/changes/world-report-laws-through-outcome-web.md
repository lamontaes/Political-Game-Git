title: The world report credits a law with the outcomes it moved through the outcome web
---

A watched-world report used to say a law "changed nothing outside its own passage" even when the outcome web moved homelessness or insurance through it. Each law now lists the outcomes it moved and in how many places, and the check on whether any law changed anything counts them.
