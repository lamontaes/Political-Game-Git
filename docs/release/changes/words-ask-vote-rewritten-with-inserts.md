title: A colleague's ask for your vote says where you stand and how short the count is, and a thank never follows a no
---

When a colleague asks for your vote the evening before the roll, your yes or no now gives your reason from where you stand on the bill's question, the colleague says whether you are the one vote they need or one on a list, and they no longer thank you after you said no.
