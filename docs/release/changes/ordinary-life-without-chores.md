section: Changed
title: Ordinary life no longer asks you to do chores, favors or shop shifts
---

Days no longer stop for household chores, grocery runs, favors for people you
know, household evenings or shifts you had to start by hand. A job still pays
on its own schedule as time passes, without clicking to work. Your own
temperament is now written from the choices you make growing up, such as
speaking up at school or putting money away, and the people you meet read it.
