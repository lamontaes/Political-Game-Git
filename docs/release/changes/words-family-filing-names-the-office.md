title: Your answer at home after filing names the office you filed for
---

After you file, when someone at home asks about the race, one of your answers used to be "The council's been asleep for ten years. Somebody had to." whatever office you filed for. It now names the office from your filing: "Somebody has to run for County assessor. Why not me?"
