title: Clothes in colors, and a Shirt, a Tie and a Jacket to choose
---

People's portraits now wear clothes painted as layers in colors. When you make your character you choose the color of your shirt, a tie or none, and a jacket or none, and the portrait changes as you choose. Everyone in the game is dressed from the same pieces: everyday tops at home, a shirt at work, and a shirt and jacket for anyone who holds office, with a tie for the men who wear one.
