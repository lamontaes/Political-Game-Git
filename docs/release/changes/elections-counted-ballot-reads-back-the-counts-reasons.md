title: A counted voter's reasons match how their vote was counted
---

When you looked at why one of the town's counted voters voted as they did, the reasons shown were rebuilt without two things the count had weighed: the world's own view of each question and the household's circumstances. The count now keeps both with the ballot, so the reasons read back are the ones that were counted.
