title: People at the door say what the race's own office can do for them, in how it touches their life
---

When you ask someone at the door what matters to them, they now raise a question the office you are running for actually decides, and say it as what it does to them (their work, their bills, their rent), never as a bill's name or a figure. You can no longer promise them something above that office, even after they turn you down. A stranger never calls you by your first name. After you press someone, they get one more turn to answer.
