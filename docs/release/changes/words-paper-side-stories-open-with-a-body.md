title: The spelling bee in the paper opens into a short story, with a way back to the front page
---

The town's spelling bee story now has a body: the school, the winner's grade and family when the records hold them, and the regional bee that comes next. A brief with a body opens from its headline, and "Back to the front page" returns.

New businesses and retirements in the paper's Briefs strip open the same way: who started the business, what it does and how many it took on; the years the retiree held the job and where they live.
