title: Your state's legislative chamber in the opening is filled with its lawmakers, seated at their desks
---

Before, the legislature card showed two people at the front desks and an
empty chamber, and the members' desks that face away from you stayed empty.

Now the chamber holds your state's lawmakers from its roster, with your own
members first. They take the members' desks, seated and facing you until a
back view is drawn, and the open floor beside them.
