---
id: deaths-from-recorded-health
impact: patch
title: Natural deaths follow a serious illness that a person's own records bring on
section: Changed
---

Nobody's natural death is drawn anymore. Each person's health strain builds
from their age, their recorded conditions and their coverage. When it crosses
one level, the same for everyone, they fall seriously ill and their family is
told. The illness runs for a number of days that shrinks with age, with the
weight of their conditions and without coverage. They die on its last day,
and the death names the illness.

Two people with the same records fall ill on the same day. A recorded
condition brings the illness sooner. Deaths that earlier saves wrote as
sudden or as accidents still read the way they were written.
