title: The Journal now says when a committee chair holds your bill
---

A bill the committee chair held without a hearing left no mark in its sponsor's Journal, so the bill seemed to vanish after its introduction. The sponsor's Journal now says the chair held it, and the chair and the other committee members do not tell it as their own.
