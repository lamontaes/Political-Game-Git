title: A hard-up official can be bought, and the tie a bribe makes can sour
---

Officials at the same pay no longer hold the same savings, so a statehouse member with a thin cushion sometimes takes a bribe, and the money moves. Passing a bribe ties the payer to the official; when a contract award the bribe was meant to buy goes to another firm, that tie sours, which is how a bribe can come to light.
