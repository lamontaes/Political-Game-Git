title: A donor who gives big to your campaign comes back wanting something
---

When someone gives a large share of what your campaign has raised, it is on
the record, and a little later the donor wants a word. If a bill they care
about is pending before the body you are running for, they ask you about it
by name; otherwise they want to know they will have your ear. You answer as
you choose: agree, refuse, stall or bargain.
