title: The person who got you help when you fell ill now asks you for something.
---

When a serious illness hits you at home and someone in your household calls for help, they come to you afterward with a request: a ride to the doctor, a visit to one, to stay close for a few days, or a promise to tell them the moment you feel sick again. If a child was in the room, they may ask you to talk to the child. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
