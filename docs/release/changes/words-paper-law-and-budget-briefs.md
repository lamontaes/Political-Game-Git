section: Changed
title: The paper's law and budget items read as news
---

A law in the paper now says which government made it, what it changed and whom it reaches: "Idaho will raise its maximum weekly unemployment benefit from $590 to $624 on January 1, 2026. It matters most to workers who lose their jobs." The bare lines such as "State unemployment tax rate took effect on January 1, 2025." are gone, and so are stories about a tax that stayed the same. A law or a budget appears in the paper only while it is still news, so a January paper no longer reports an October budget as if it just passed. Large sums read in trillions: "$7.1 trillion", never "$7145 billion".
