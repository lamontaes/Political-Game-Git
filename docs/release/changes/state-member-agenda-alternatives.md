title: State lawmakers can bring forward another eligible priority
---

When another member has already proposed their first-choice question, state lawmakers can propose another eligible question from their recorded principles and the full policy catalog. Existing filing limits, session rules, pending bills, and cooldowns still apply. Each intake admits at most one bill per member and question; the player's member remains under player control.
