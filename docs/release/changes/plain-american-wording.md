section: Changed
title: Plain words for trips, and no British English
---

The button for a trip now says where you are going, such as "Go to the public meeting", instead of "Make the journey", and other activities say "Start now". Trips on the calendar read "Trip to the public meeting". The game's wording check now catches British spellings and words (favour, neighbour, whilst, fortnight, queue, programme and others) and stiff phrases (make the journey, proceed to, commence), so new ones can't come back.
