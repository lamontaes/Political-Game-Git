title: A bill held in committee shows why
---

When a committee's chair holds a bill without a hearing, the committee record now keeps the chair's reasons: where they stand on the bill and what their temperament adds to holding a hearing. A person who learns from a news story that a law changed a price their household pays now has the bill that enacted it on that record, and the "My life" journal has a line for it, held back until the owner grades its wording.
