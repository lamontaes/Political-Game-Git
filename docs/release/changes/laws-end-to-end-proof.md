title: A bill held in committee shows why
---

When a committee's chair holds a bill without a hearing, the committee record now keeps the chair's reasons: where they stand on the bill and what their temperament adds to holding a hearing. A person whose household paid more or less under a law now ties it to that law the day they hear a story about it; before, they could only do so on the monthly billing day. The record of what they learned names the bill that enacted the law, and the "My life" journal has a line for it, held back until the owner grades its wording.
