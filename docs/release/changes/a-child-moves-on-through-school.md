title: A child moves on through school as the years pass
---

A character who started a new game in school stayed at the school they began
at. A five-year-old was still at the elementary school at sixteen, and a
grown character still had it on their record at twenty-four.

Now a child finishes elementary school in late spring and starts middle school
that fall, then moves on to high school, and graduates at the end of their last
year. The classmates they started with move on with them. Nothing already on
the record is rewritten: the elementary school stays where it was, marked
finished.

This applies to new games. A game already in progress keeps the school it
has.

When school years begin and end is a placeholder for now, filed as a research
question.
