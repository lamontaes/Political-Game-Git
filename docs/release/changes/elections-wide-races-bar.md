title: Statewide and congressional races draw bigger fields
---

Statewide and congressional seats now weigh running against a bar of their own, set from Ballotpedia's count of House primary candidates, instead of the one fit on town seats. In three drawn places, state legislative races drew 3 to 7 filers (up from 2 to 4) and statewide offices 2 to 6, before primaries thinned them.
