title: A town's newspaper now serves its whole county, and a paper that loses money can close
---

A town's newspaper used to be kept alive by a weekly top-up that paid whatever its town readers did not. Now each paper is paid by its county's readers beyond the town, at what a reader really brings a paper, and it carries a cost sized from its own newsroom jobs and circulation. A paper in a large market lands near the national margin; a paper in a small market, with several papers splitting few readers, can run at a loss, and its owner may close it. Television and radio stations are unchanged for now.
