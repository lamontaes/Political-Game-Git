section: Changed
title: A new business's cash, a new group's founders and a fallback chair are decided, not drawn
---

A new business now opens with the cash its industry typically keeps: 16 days
of spending for a restaurant, 19 for a shop and 47 for a real estate office.
Before, a random draw set it. A new congregation or club is started by people
who belonged to one before, then by the eldest. When a President names nobody
to the central bank, the longest-serving governor becomes chair, and an empty
governor's seat stays open until the President knows someone who may serve.
