id: laws-assistance-work-requirement
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The assistance work requirement now ends benefits for households it covers
---

Once a state's work requirement for assistance is law, a household whose every
member it covers (adults 18 to 64 under the income line, working under 20
hours a week, with no child under 14) loses the welfare benefit after three
months. A household with a working adult, a child or an older member keeps it.
