title: Officeholder principles form from recorded life
---

New officeholder principles now form from recorded life instead of a seeded
stance and conviction draw. Existing saved principles and the player's choices
remain intact. A life with no supported pull receives no invented view.

A public party label now supplies only a slight pull, below the threshold for
forming a principle on its own, including for sitting officeholders.
