title: Asking somebody out says what happens next, and a meeting can be called off
---

Before, asking somebody out or asking to meet showed nothing, so it looked
like the button did nothing. A meeting both people had agreed to could not be
called off, and time stopped at it until it was attended. A child could also
be offered party work, see a party organizer among their contacts, or ask an
unrelated adult to meet.

Now the People screen says who will answer and by what day. Decline on the
Calendar calls off an agreed meeting, and the other person is told. Party
work and party organizers are for adults, and a child meets an adult outside
the family through a parent or guardian.
