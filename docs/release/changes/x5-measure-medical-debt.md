title: Each state now tracks medical debt in collections, and expanding Medicaid lowers it
---

Every state and D.C. now keeps the share of adults with medical debt in collections on
their credit reports, starting from the Urban Institute's August 2025 credit data. Seven
states whose own laws keep medical debt off credit reports start at zero. A state that
expands Medicaid sees that share fall about a third six months after the expansion takes
effect, and a state that ends its expansion sees it rise.
