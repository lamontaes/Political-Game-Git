title: In a race for several seats at once, you are either elected or you lost, never both, and only someone who missed the cut calls to concede.
---

In a town council or town board race that fills several seats from one field ("vote for three"), the Journal could say you were elected and that you lost, and another winner could phone you with a concession. Now each candidate gets one result: elected if they finished inside the seats, lost if not. The concession call comes from the first candidate who missed the cut, and none comes if everyone who ran was seated. Your race also reads "won" whichever of the race's seats the count gave you.
