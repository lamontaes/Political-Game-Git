title: A city budget reaches the legislature that governs it
---

A city whose council is recorded as its state-level legislature now sends its budget to that body when it has no separate city council. The mayor's proposed administration change can be voted and adopted instead of being carried forward unvoted. The proof follows a cut or raise through staffing and later paydays.
