title: Every place opens with a seventeen-year-old player who turns eighteen in play
impact: patch
---

In about one place in fourteen, the census sample for the town showed no family with children, so the town opened with nobody under eighteen and the player started the game already eighteen. Every town now opens with at least one seventeen-year-old: where none turns up, the town gets one more family, its parents and children aged from the place's own records, and the player is that family's seventeen-year-old, who turns eighteen during the first year of play.
