title: A town or county race can be run on that government's own budget
---

When you file for a town, township or county seat, the positions you can take now include each line of that government's own budget, such as police protection, parks or highways, named as the Budgets screen names them. In states that let their towns decide none of the policy questions, a town candidate had no position to take and could not hold a campaign event; now they campaign on the budget, and voters weigh those stances like any other.
