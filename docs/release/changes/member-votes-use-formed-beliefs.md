title: A legislator's formed view can shape a vote
---

When a bill says whether it supports or opposes a policy question, a
legislator who has formed a view on that question can vote accordingly. The
decision cites the legislator's recorded belief. A bill that only names the
question gives no such voting reason, and an uncertain view does not become a
yes or no vote by assumption. The importance assigned to a view is a marked
game assumption pending calibration.
