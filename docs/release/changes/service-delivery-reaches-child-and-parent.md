---
id: service-delivery-reaches-child-and-parent
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A child's funded pre-K, after-school or private-school day names the child and parent in the law's record
---

When a child completes a day of a service a law pays for (pre-K, an
after-school program or a private-school program), the law now shows up as
something that reached that child and each of the child's recorded parents. The
parent's partner hears of it at home. Officials behind the law can be seen in a
better or worse light because of it, and a repeal stops the record from
growing.
