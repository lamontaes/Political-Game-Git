title: When you are seriously ill, a relative who visits now offers you something or asks whether to tell someone.
---

A relative who comes to see you while you are seriously ill offers dinner, to take a child, a ride to an appointment or a night's stay, or asks whether to tell your employer or someone in the family. You can accept, say you will ask when you need it, ask what would be easiest for them, or turn it down, and what you agree to is kept.
