title: A legislative seat in a lettered district no longer reads "the NaNth District"
---

A seat in a district named by a letter or a name, such as District A, 5B or Addison-1, was said as "the state Senate in the NaNth District" on the campaign board and at the filing counter. It now reads by its office and its district, as in "state senator in District 5B".
