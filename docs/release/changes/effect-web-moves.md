title: Rent rules and crime change who moves away
---

People and households now leave a town faster or slower when the town's web of causes says so. A rent rule slows renters' moves, most among older renters, and a rise in violent crime speeds families' moving away more than other households'. The same factors act on the people you can click and on the counted households around them.
