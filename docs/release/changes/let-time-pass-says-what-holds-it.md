title: "Let time pass" says what is holding the clock
---

You ask a friend to meet, and the meeting goes on your calendar. When its
hour comes, time will not move past it until you keep it or call it off.

Pressing "Let time pass" on the moment used to do nothing you could see: the
date stayed the same, and nothing on the screen said why, so the button read
as broken. It now says what stopped it, naming the meeting, right under the
button.
