section: Changed
title: Election day and night are scenes, and voting is your choice
---

Election day no longer drops you at the polls. On election morning the narrator sets the day, and if you're on the ballot, a volunteer can take you canvassing before the polls close. Then the day asks whether you go vote: Go vote opens the ballot, Stay home casts nothing. That night the returns for your race come in place by place until the race is called, with the other states' governor races that night, in a pop-up of its own. The Election Night results sheet is gone, and it no longer opens again on every load.
