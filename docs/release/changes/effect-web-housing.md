title: Rents, apartments and homelessness follow the town's laws
---

When a town raises its property tax, renters' next lease renewals come in higher, for families in the counted population as well as the people you meet. Laws that open land to apartments or put limits on rents change how many apartment buildings get started, and housing programs like vouchers and Housing First make it easier for a household without a home to sign a lease again.
