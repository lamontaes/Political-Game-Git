title: The sponsor of a bill, or the group against it, comes to the executive's office before the decision
---

When a bill is presented to the mayor or governor you play, its sponsor now comes in and asks for a signature, or, if the sponsor cannot, the lobbyist of a group that argued against it comes and asks for a veto. You answer one of four ways, and every ending takes you to the desk to sign or veto.
