id: gov-bill-form
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Bills take their body's real form, and a town's council takes up first what its people live with
---

A bill now carries its own body's number and enacting clause: "LB 12" in Nebraska's legislature, "H.R. 25" or "S. 3" in Congress, a year-numbered ordinance at the town council, opening with the words the state's constitution prescribes. The vote on it records the motion a body really makes: a council adopts an ordinance, a chamber passes a bill.

A council's agenda no longer opens with the same question in every town. The presiding member weighs each change by how many members back it and by how many of the town's adults live with what it touches (work, children at home, age), so a town's own people shape what comes up first.
