id: home-quotes-from-sourced-financing
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Home quotes use sourced down payments and recorded mortgage rates
---

Opening down-payment estimates use the 2025 NAR median separately for
first-time and repeat buyers. Completed purchases recorded in the game then
supply the average for that buyer group; old placeholder purchases do not.
The quoted monthly payment uses the recorded local or national game macro
rate and the approved 30-year fixed term through the shared financing reader.

When no rate is recorded, the quote stays unavailable. The purchase panel
shows that gap, and households do not qualify for a mortgage using a guessed
payment. Existing mortgage servicing remains a separate implementation.
