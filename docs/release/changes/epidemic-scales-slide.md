title: The illness going around weighs age and season by the day
section: Changed
---

How readily somebody catches the illness going around, how serious their case
is, and how strongly the season brings it into town now slide by the day
instead of jumping at a birthday or on the first of a month. A child the day
before and the day after turning five, or somebody the day before and after
turning eighty, gets nearly the same case. The levels in the middle of each
old age band, and at the middle of each month, are what they were.
