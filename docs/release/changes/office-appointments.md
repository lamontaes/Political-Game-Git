---
id: office-appointments
section: Added
title: A mayor or council can name a department head
impact: minor
---

An official who appoints a town department head now has "Name a department head" among their acts of office whenever a post stands open because its head died or moved away. The official's own reasons pick the candidate, the council confirms where the town's form of government asks it, and the new head goes on the town's payroll.
