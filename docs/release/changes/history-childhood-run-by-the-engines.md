title: Your childhood is now lived through, not made up
---

The years from your birth to the start are now run by the game's own school
and household rules: each school you went to is the public school district of
the town your family lived in that year, and the day you and your partner set
up home comes from when each of you was grown and working. Your Journal tells
your first day of school and where your family came from from those records,
and the school names you see are your town's own district, not a real campus.
