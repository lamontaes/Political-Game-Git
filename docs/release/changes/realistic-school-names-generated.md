title: The schools in your past have this world's own names
---

Every new world names the schools its people went to itself, in the shapes American schools take (the town, a compass point and the town, a public figure, a family name, a piece of local landscape), and never with the name of a real school near you. The Journal's "I started at" line names the school your world gave you. How many students each school had is drawn per world too.
