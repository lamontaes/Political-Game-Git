title: A person you live with no longer reads as someone you haven't spoken to
---

Before, the card for a sister in the same home said "You haven't spoken,"
and a father's said "You last spoke" more than a year back. Now the card
for anyone you live with says you live together, and adds that you spoke
today when you did.
