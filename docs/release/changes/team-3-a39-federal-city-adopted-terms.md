title: Federal and city wage readers use adopted dollar terms

Federal wage schedules and city ordinances read their adopted hourly amounts and
operative dates through the existing final-law reader. A yes answer no longer
substitutes $15 federally or an average city premium. The initial federal setting
uses the same canonical starting-law terms. Missing numeric authority remains
explicit; state reader and historical cutoff handling are preserved.
