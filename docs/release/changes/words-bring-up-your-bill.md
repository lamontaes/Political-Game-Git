id: words-bring-up-your-bill
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: People who read about your bill say it was yours and how the vote went.
---

Someone who read the paper's story on the vote on your ordinance or bill now tells you it was yours and how it came out ("I read in the Chronicle that your ordinance on transportation failed.", "KWTP-TV says your ordinance passed. Good for you."), from the vote's own record. Before, they said only that the paper had a story on the topic.
