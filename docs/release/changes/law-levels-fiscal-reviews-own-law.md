title: Treasury reviews keep each government's fiscal repeal in force
---

A treasury review no longer revives a repealed fiscal rule because another government still has that rule. Each government's current law now supplies its own budget, pension-funding and reserve review.

A state bar on a local fiscal rule now stops its budget effect; withdrawing the bar restores the existing local act.
