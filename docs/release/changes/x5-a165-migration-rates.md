title: Young adults leave town more often than their parents, at the pace people really move in your state
---

How often a town's residents pack up and leave now depends on their age and their state. Across the
nation a twenty-two-year-old weighs moving away about six times as often as someone past seventy,
and each state keeps its own pace. Newcomers arrive at the state's real pace too, and the island
territories follow the national pattern.
