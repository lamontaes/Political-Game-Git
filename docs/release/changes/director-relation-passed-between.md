title: Someone who already came to you does not introduce themselves again
---

A resident who had already come to you in an earlier scene, or who already knows you, no longer opens with "You are Angela, aren't you?" as if you had never met. The scenes now read what passed between you before: that they came to you, and whether you helped or turned them away. At office hours, someone you helped or turned away says so in their greet.
