title: Raising federal transportation, education or health spending now raises each state's and town's roads, schools and hospitals.
---

Before, the extra money reached a state or town only as general aid, so its roads, schools and hospital budgets did not move. Now each one's matching budget line rises by its share the day the bill passes, and falls back if the federal line is put back.
