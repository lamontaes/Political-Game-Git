title: Budgeted revenue now moves from year to year
---

The county, the state and the federal government used to publish the same revenue figure every year, whatever the economy and the law in force actually brought in. Each now re-budgets its revenue every fiscal year from what its treasury collected over the year before, so Budgets and Metrics show revenue that rises and falls with the world.
