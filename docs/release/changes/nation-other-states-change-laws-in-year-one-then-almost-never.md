title: Other states keep changing their laws year after year, each for a reason.
---

Other states' legislatures used to change hundreds of laws in the first year and then almost none. Now each state takes up what moved there since its last session: people out of work, prices, pay, a budget that came in short or over, or members newly elected. A few dozen to a hundred or more laws change across the country every year, and each change records what moved in that state before it.
