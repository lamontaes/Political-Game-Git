title: Election filing deadlines follow the selected office
---

Governor and state legislative filing calendars now use the shared nomination
reader. The 2026 legislature deadlines cover all 50 states, including four
states without a regular legislative election that year. Governor deadlines
cover the 36 states with regular 2026 elections. Massachusetts and New Mexico
retain their separate legislative and federal dates, and Nebraska retains its
earlier incumbent deadline. A filing deadline remains open through its date.
