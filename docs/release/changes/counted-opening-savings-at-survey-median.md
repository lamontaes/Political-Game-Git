title: Families in the wider population open with the savings a family like theirs really has.
---

Each family's share of its group's money was spread so that the typical family opened with well under half of what a family like it really holds, and many went looking for work on the second day for want of money they would have had. Now each family opens at the typical amount for a family like it, spread by how much it saves, so the typical family holds the typical amount.
