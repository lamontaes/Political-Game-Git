---
id: upbringing-from-household-records
section: Changed
title: People's temperaments now differ by how they were raised
impact: patch
---

People whose earlier life is summarized had no recorded schooling or care, so everyone started with the same sociability, conflict and risk and the same opening goal. Their upbringing now reads the household records (siblings, congregation, household pay, caregivers per child, school-year moves and a parent's death), so a lonelier or more strained childhood leans differently from a crowded, secure one. When childhood sibling counts or pay are not recorded, labeled estimates use national Census distributions with stable per-person variation.
