title: The Journal is written in your character's own voice
---

The Journal now reads as the character's own account, in the first person and
the past tense: "I was born in Lincoln, Nebraska, on January 1, 1992." and "In
January, I began working as a store assistant at Neighborhood Market." Quoted
words, names and places stay exactly as recorded, and dates read as dates.
