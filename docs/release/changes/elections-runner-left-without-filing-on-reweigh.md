title: Someone who decides to run for office stays in the race until filing day
---

A resident who decided early in the year to run for a local seat could drift back into the town's counts before the filing day came, and then never filed: in one Puerto Rico town, ten people who meant to run never reached the ballot. Someone who has decided to run now stays a full person until their race is decided, and anyone who still cannot file on the day is recorded as refused, with the rules they no longer meet.
