title: The President's address is given in Congress's chamber, and a speech heard on television shows its speaker on a set.

The opening address now happens where it is given, in the House chamber, instead of the Oval Office, with the President shown as a large bust. A speech you hear on a television station shows the speaker on a small set with Live over it and the station under their name, so you can tell who is talking and where it is coming from.
