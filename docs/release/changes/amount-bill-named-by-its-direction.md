title: A bill that changes an amount, like the minimum wage, has its real name.
---

A bill drafted as a new amount (a minimum wage, a tax rate) now records whether it raises or lowers the law in force and takes its real name, such as the Wage Floor Increase Ordinance. Before, such a bill had no name, and the member whose bill you vetoed never brought the veto up.
