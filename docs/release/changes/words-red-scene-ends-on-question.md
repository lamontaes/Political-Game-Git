title: A scene no longer ends on a question nobody answers.
---

When someone offered to run your campaign and you put them off, they could end the talk with "Then we start tonight. Where do you keep your contacts?" and nothing answered it. They now say "Then we start tonight. Bring me your contact list." A reporter's "Fair. Who wrote it?" is now "Fair. I'll ask who wrote it." for the same reason.
