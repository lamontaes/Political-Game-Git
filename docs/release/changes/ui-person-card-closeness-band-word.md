title: A person's card no longer says how close you are
---

A person's card no longer opens with a word for how close you are ("You barely know each other", "You are very close"), and the line about when you were last in touch no longer says you grew closer or drifted apart. It says when you were last in touch and why, and nothing about how it moved.
