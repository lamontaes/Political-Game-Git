title: Recruits weigh the cost of a campaign
---

A person a party asks to run for the state legislature now weighs what a
campaign takes from their life, a year and their money, next to the odds. The
recorded reasons for declining include that cost, so a recruit's age and
circumstances can tip the answer instead of every district drawing the same
number of candidates.
