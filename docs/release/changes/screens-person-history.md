id: screens-person-history
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Clicking a person on the People map opens their card, which now tells their history
---

Clicking a face on the People map opens that person's card at once. The card has a History section, oldest first, read from the person's own records: where they were born, the schools they started, when they moved to the county, meeting their partner, their children's births ("You were born in 2007." when it is you), the job they started, a parent's death, an election won, and when they grew closer to you or apart. The selection bar under the map and its View person and Talk buttons are gone; you talk to someone by clicking them in the room.
