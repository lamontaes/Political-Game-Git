title: Growing up no longer invents a sick relative
---

A child who shared a home could be handed a scene about illness in the house,
or a teenager a scene about caring for someone, even though nobody in the
household had ever been recorded as sick or in need of care. The scene simply
assumed it because other people lived there.

Those two situations now wait for a real cause: a named person in the home and
a recorded illness or care need. Until the life has one, the growing-up years
offer other moments instead.
