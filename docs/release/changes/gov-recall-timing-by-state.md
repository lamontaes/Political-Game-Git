title: A recall's earliest start and its wait after a failed one follow each state's own timing
---

A recall petition can start only when the state's law says: six calendar months from the first day of a term, a fraction of the term at hand, a stretch before the seat's next election, or a set wait after an earlier recall failed (six months in Alaska, twelve in Nebraska, two years in Montana, a year in the District of Columbia, the rest of the term elsewhere).
