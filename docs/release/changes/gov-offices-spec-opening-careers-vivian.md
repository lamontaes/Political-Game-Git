title: No official at the opening was first seated under the minimum age
---

An official holding a seat when the game opens no longer has a term that began before they were old enough to serve. A county board member born in 2001, for one, was shown as elected in 2020 to a six-year term; a holder too young to have begun the current term that far back now took the seat when the game opens.
