title: People tell you where they stand, and the paper does too
---

The people you are close to, and who follow politics, will sometimes tell you where they stand, in their own voice: a guarded one says it plainly, a glad one says it proudly. Once they have, their card shows it. A letter to the editor does the same for its writer, whether you read it yourself or someone tells you about it. Until one of those happens, a person's card still says you do not know where they stand.
