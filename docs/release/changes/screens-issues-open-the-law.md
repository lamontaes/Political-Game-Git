section: Changed
title: Issues are cards by subject that open the law itself
---

The Issues tab shows the laws in force here as cards by subject (taxes and money, work and pay, health and welfare, rights and safety, and the rest), each with its value and who sets it; clicking one opens the law's own screen: what is in force at each level and since when, the statute it rests on, the measure of this world that enacted it (readable as its paper), what is coming, and who voted. The folded rows that repeated the state and a record date are gone.
