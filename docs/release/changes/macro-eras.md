title: The economy now lives through eras, recessions, depressions and inflation scares.
---

The economy no longer settles around one growth rate. Its long-run pace
wanders and can shift into a new era; recessions arrive every few years with
their own depth and length, and now and then a depression; long slumps leave
unemployment higher for years; and a price shock can pull inflation up until
it is brought back down. A long game can see booming decades, lost decades
and a 1970s-style inflation, and no two games follow the same path.
