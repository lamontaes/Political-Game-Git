title: The President's opening address is built from the world's own records
---

The address that opens the game now reads like a speech. It opens with the occasion, tells the story of the country's year, names the few things this President cares most about, says what the law is on each in your state or in the nation, draws the one line the President will not cross, and speaks to the governors about a state law that differs from their view. Which things the President raises come from their own views on every policy question in the game, so a different President, a different state, or different laws give a different speech. Every figure is read from that year's records. The President speaks plainly or formally, depending on who they are.
