title: Couples can have an unplanned pregnancy, and some of them end it
---

A couple can conceive without having planned to, and the pregnancy is carried or ended according to the law in force where they live and what a trip would cost them. A pregnancy that is carried is due after the usual gestation. One that is ended is recorded, and the couple pays for the trip out of their cash, to the outside market of the place, the household member with the most cash paying first. No screen or scene shows it yet.
