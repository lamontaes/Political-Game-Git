title: Some pregnancies in town are unplanned, and each woman decides from her own life and the law where she lives
---

People in town now sometimes have an unplanned pregnancy. Whether she carries it or ends it comes from her own health, money, household and views and from the law in force where she lives; when she ends it, her household pays the trip, and a carried one becomes a birth in the family.
