---
id: pension-share-from-reported-payments
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Each government pays the pension share its own plans reported
---

Each state, county and city now pays the share of its required pension
contribution that its own plans reported paying in the Public Plans Database,
instead of a random draw that drifted each year. A government with no plan
listed pays the median of all plans, marked as estimated from the average, and
the share holds until budgets pass as bills.
