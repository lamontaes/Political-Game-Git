title: Your journal tells a move, a child's birth, a partnership, a death in the family and a school's end as small stories.

Where the journal said "I moved to Springfield," it now says where you came from and which of your children came with you. A child's birth says how old you were and when it was your first. A partnership says how long you have been together. A death says how old the person was and whether they had been ill. A school's end says how long you had been there.
