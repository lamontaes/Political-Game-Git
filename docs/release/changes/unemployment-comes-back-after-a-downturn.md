title: Unemployment comes back after a downturn
---

Unemployment used to climb in every world and never come down. A save that
began at 5.0% sat between 7% and 11% a dozen years later, with some towns
above 16%, even while the economy kept growing. Now a slump raises
unemployment and it comes back when the slump ends.

## Why it climbed

Unemployment moved only by changes: last month's rate plus the effect of
slow growth, a random draw and any shocks. Nothing pulled it back. Shipping
delays are the only nationwide disruption the game records, so every world
got slowdowns and no booms, and each one left a permanent step up.

## What changed

Unemployment still rises when growth slows or a disaster hits a town. Its
distance from a normal rate of 4.6% now fades each month, and a town's gap
from the nation fades the same way. After a slump it takes about four years
to recover half the ground. A slowdown lasting decades keeps unemployment
about a point and a third higher instead of climbing without limit.

The recovery speed and the normal rate are placeholders, filed with the
research queue as unemployment-return-to-normal. Months already recorded in
a save stay as they were; later months are written under the new rule and
carry its name.
