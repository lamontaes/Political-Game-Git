title: Unemployment comes back after a downturn
---

Unemployment used to climb in every world and never come down. Six test
saves that began at 5.0% ended between 6.8% and 10.8% after 13 to 16 years.
Now a slump or a town's disaster raises it, and it drifts back toward a normal
rate of 4.6% once the cause ends, about halfway in four years. A town's gap
from the national rate fades the same way. Recorded months are unchanged; the
recovery speed and the 4.6% are placeholders filed with the research queue.
