title: Unemployment comes back after a downturn
---

Unemployment used to climb in every world and never come down. Six test
saves that began at 5.0% ended between 6.8% and 10.8% after 13 to 16 years
of play, and three of their home towns were above 16%. Now a slump raises
unemployment above its normal rate of 4.6%, and it comes back when the slump
ends.

## Why it climbed

Each month, unemployment was last month's rate plus the effect of slow
growth, a random draw and any shocks. Nothing pulled it back. Shipping
delays are the only nationwide disruption the game records, so worlds got
slowdowns and no booms. Each slowdown left a permanent step up.

## What changed

Unemployment still rises when growth slows or a disaster hits a town. Its
distance from 4.6% now shrinks each month, and a town's gap from the
national rate shrinks the same way. After a slump, about half the gap is
gone in four years. A slowdown that lasts decades holds unemployment near
5.9%, and it no longer climbs without limit.

The recovery speed and the 4.6% rate are placeholders, filed with the
research queue as unemployment-return-to-normal. Months already recorded in
a save stay as they were. Later months use the new rule and record its name.
