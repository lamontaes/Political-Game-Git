title: When your bill fails, someone who voted for it comes to you.
---

An officeholder's aide used to bring the news of a lost vote, so a council member, who keeps no aide, heard nothing when their own ordinance failed. Now a fellow member who voted for it finds you the same day ("We lost the vote on Ordinance 2025-1."), and you can talk over what comes next.
