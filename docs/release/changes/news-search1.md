title: Search the news you have already read
---

The published news you have collected can now be searched. Type in the search
box at the top of the newspaper and only the stories that mention what you
typed stay on screen — the headline, the story itself, the place it happened,
anybody named in it, and any correction printed since. Clearing the box brings
everything back.

It searches the news this life has actually published. Nothing is hidden from
you that you could see before, and nothing new is revealed: a story you have
not been shown does not appear because you searched for it.
