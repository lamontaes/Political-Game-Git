impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A resident tells only the candidate they would vote for that they have their vote.
---

When your campaign asks for a vote at the door, a resident now says yes only if you are the one they would vote for, and a resident who already gave a rival their word mostly keeps it. Before, anyone who enjoyed the visit said yes, so the same residents promised their vote to every candidate who knocked, and your rivals' doors counted as much as yours.
