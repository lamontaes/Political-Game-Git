title: Campaigns shows the ballot as race cards; each race opens its own page
---

Politics → Campaigns now shows the coming ballot as race cards on a framed plate, paged with arrows, each naming the office, who is running, election day and when filing closes. Click a card to open the race: who is running as face cards with where each stands, the race's dates beside them, and File for it in its tray.
