title: A state law on cannabis sales now moves its overdose deaths
---

Legalizing or ending adult cannabis sales used to change nothing a player could
see. Now a state's overdose death rate moves about 11 months after the law takes
effect, which is how long stores have taken to open in six states that
legalized. The research disagrees on the direction, so each world draws its own
size for each state, from a fall of 20% to a rise of 10%. The effect ends the
day a repeal takes effect. In one world, Alabama legalized sales in 2026, and
its overdose deaths ran 1.5% above the same world without the law until the
repeal in 2029.

Four more questions now say, with the research behind it, why their effect on
everything the world keeps is about zero: a consumer data privacy law, age
checks for social media, local control of library materials, and a city
nondiscrimination ordinance.
