title: The townspeople the game counts in groups feel the crimes that happen to them
---

When a burglary, theft, vandalism, robbery or assault happens to a household the game counts as part of a group, that group's view on crime now moves the way a named victim's does, by the share of its people the crime reached. The local paper's sense of what the town worries about reads it.
