title: Congress and the White House on the office-day engine
---

A U.S. representative's and the president's day now run on the same office-day engine as local office. The player takes the sitting member or president, and that day's floor vote, a vacant cabinet post, the budget request and the joint address hold them the morning they fall. What the player leaves, the character does.
