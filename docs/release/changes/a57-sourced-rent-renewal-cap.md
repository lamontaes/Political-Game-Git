title: Private rent renewals use sourced caps and recorded construction coverage
---

Private lease renewals now apply the recorded governing cap through the shared
price-cost dispatcher. Starting-law formula components come from the cited
California, D.C., Oregon and Washington records and use recorded game inflation.
Later enacted caps use the adopted scalar and explicit tenancy coverage.

Opening stock is pre-existing under the approved game model. In-play dwellings
use their saved creation date and the sourced construction exemption window:
15 years in California and Oregon, 12 in Washington. D.C. has no rolling expiry.
No construction date is invented. Owner-occupied exemptions remain a follow-up.
Existing payments and saved rent terms are preserved.
