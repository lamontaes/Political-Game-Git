title: An employer you turned down stops calling with the same job, and a relative stops writing the same letter.
---

After you turned down or let lapse a job offer, the same employer used to phone again and again with the same job at the same pay. Now it calls back only with another job or a real raise. A relative who lives elsewhere writes again only when something has happened since their last letter: news of their life, a birthday they missed or their own birthday coming up. Otherwise the letter is a line in your journal.
