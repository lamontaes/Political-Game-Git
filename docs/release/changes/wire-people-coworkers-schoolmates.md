title: Coworkers and schoolmates come from the town's own records
---

The people you know from work are now the people your employer actually employs: at the start, everyone knows the coworkers in their part of the staff, and a coworker who talks to you about the old job names the place you both worked. A coworker or schoolmate is never greeted as a stranger. Schoolmates come from the school you attend in play; nobody starts the game knowing an old classmate yet.
