After a recorded federal judicial nomination, the existing Congress calendar now schedules Senate referral. At that due date, a still-pending nomination is recorded as received by the Senate and referred to its Judiciary Committee. A later committee consideration due item records the specific missing authority when no committee convener and actual attendance have been established. The judiciary panel shows the saved referral and the pending status. This does not hold a hearing, make a committee report, grant hearing knowledge, cast a ballot, confirm a nominee, or fill the seat.
