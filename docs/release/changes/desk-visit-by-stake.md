---
id: desk-visit-by-stake
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The person who comes to your desk about a bill is the one with the most at stake who knows you.
---

When a bill reaches your desk as mayor or governor, one person comes to see you about it. If someone who knows you has money riding on it, they come first: a group's lobbyist who argued against it and says what it would cost the people they speak for, or the sponsor whose bill adds to the budget, whichever has more at stake. If nobody who knows you has a stake, the sponsor comes, and if the sponsor cannot, the lobbyist whose group it costs most.
