title: A law that lets more homes be built lowers the town's home prices and rents
---

A state or town that allows apartments in single-family zones, permits homes
by right, or overrides local limits on housing now sees its home prices, and
the rents that follow them, rise more slowly from a year after the law takes
effect, ending about 11% lower five years on, as in Minneapolis. Repealing
such a law the place began with moves prices the other way. The
watched-world report now counts the laws that act through their own area's
records, and says how many of the questions the law in force answers act in
the world.
