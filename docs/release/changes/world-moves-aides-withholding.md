title: People move for their own reasons, aides come from nearby, and pay is taxed where the worker lives
impact: patch
---

Who leaves town is no longer seeded by chance at the start: each adult's pull to move comes from what their home costs against what their household earns, a job in another town, how close they are to family living elsewhere, a death at home, and whether the region's jobs are getting worse than the nation's. Officials now hire aides who live within reach of the office and are of working age, a relative living out of town is no longer taken for someone out of work, and a family tie is kept as one of the hire's reasons. Public pay to someone living outside the town has tax withheld by the law where they live, a local income tax uses the base its law names, and a closed school's voucher eligibility ends the day it closes.
