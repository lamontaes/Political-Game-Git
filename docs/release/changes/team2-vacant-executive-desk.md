title: Bills stay pending when no governor is seated
---

A passed bill stays pending when the world has no seated governor for its
jurisdiction. An authored scenario ending cannot supply a signature or veto.
Existing seated governors still receive the bill through their bound matter.
The player-session wait uses the same executive desk instead of recording an
authored signature or veto.
