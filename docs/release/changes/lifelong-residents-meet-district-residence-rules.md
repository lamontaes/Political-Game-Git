title: A seat that cannot be filed for says why beside the button
---

When a seat you have named on the Campaigns screen still cannot be filed for,
the reason is shown beside the button and the button is off, instead of a
refusal appearing at the bottom of the page after a click. The district card
also no longer shows notes about map data.
