title: A lifelong resident can run for their own district
---

Choosing your own legislative district on the Campaigns screen used to date
your residence there from the day the game started, so a forty-year-old who had
lived in Kotzebue all her life failed a one-year residence rule. The district
is now dated from when your life's records show you came to live there, the
same way the office list already read it. When a seat you have named still
cannot be filed for, the reason is shown beside the button and the button is
off, instead of a refusal appearing at the bottom of the page after a click.
The district card also no longer shows notes about map data.
