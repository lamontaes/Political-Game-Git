title: The people strip fits the people in the room, and only the speaker is big.
---

The strip of people along the bottom of a scene is now only as wide as the people on it, so one person no longer sits on a wide empty band. In a talk, only the person speaking is shown large; everyone else stands small. Every named person in a scene opens their card when you click them, even when they have nothing to say.
