title: A signature from a counted resident counts for the people like them
---

In a city where most residents are counted rather than followed one by one, every candidate's petition fell short of the signatures the law asks, so races like Washington, D.C.'s council at-large seats ended with nobody on the ballot. A counted resident who signs now counts for the people like them, the same rule the count uses.
