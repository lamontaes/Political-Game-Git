section: Changed
title: The home screen shows the places of government, and your character in theirs
---

The home screen no longer opens on an apartment. It moves through every
painted place of government in the game: the Oval Office, the U.S. Capitol,
the House and Senate floors, each state's own capitol, legislative chambers,
council and county chambers, courtrooms, city hall and campaign offices, with
a new picture every 15 seconds.

With a saved game, Continue now shows your character's role beside their name,
and the first picture is where that role is done: your chamber, your
courtroom, your campaign office, or your state's capitol. Your character
stands in front of it, dressed for the place. Someone who holds no role starts
at their town's city hall, never at home.

Saved games, Options and Patch notes now open beside the menu, and the
pictures keep changing behind them. The menu stays in place and never fades
while a picture changes.
