title: Taking campaign money after a finding, and lying to a reporter, now catch up with you
---

Campaign money a candidate takes for themselves after an ethics finding is now
reviewed and charged in a second case, repaid, and fined more heavily than the
first. Money taken in a campaign's last weeks still reaches a public report
after the campaign ends. A candidate's hometown paper now covers an ethics case
against them. A reporter who catches a candidate lying now takes the
contradicting source's on-the-record account to their desk as a story and asks
the candidate to respond before it runs. How much harder a repeat finding hits
is a placeholder until it is researched.
