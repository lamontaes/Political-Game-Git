title: Bottle deposits and carbon prices follow what people return and emit
---

In a state with a bottle deposit, how much of the deposit a household loses depends on how many containers the state's people bring back, and that share climbs once a new deposit has been law for about half a year. A carbon price is owed on the tons a household emits, so it eases a little as the state's emissions fall. The families you meet and the wider population pay by the same rule.
