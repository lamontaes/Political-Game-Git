title: Crime and births now rise and fall over the decades in each state.
---

A state's crime and birth rates are no longer tied to fixed national figures.
Each drifts over the years, partly with the country and partly on its own,
with now and then a nationwide wave, the way crime roughly halved from the
1990s to the 2010s and births rose and fell across generations. Hard times
and laws still push them on top of that.
