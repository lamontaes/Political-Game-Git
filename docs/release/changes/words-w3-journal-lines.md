section: Improved
title: Your Journal says how many came to your town hall and who you gave an interview to
---

A town hall in your Journal says how many people came and how many questions they asked, and an interview names the outlet. A lost job no longer says the loss twice ("I lost my job. I had no job after Lopez's Market."). Fixed sentences that said the same thing in every life, and lines no part of the Journal ever used, are gone.
