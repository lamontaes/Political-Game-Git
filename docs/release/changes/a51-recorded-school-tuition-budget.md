impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Keep tuition changes on recorded school bills
---

Tuition freezes cap the affected public college's unpaid bill using its recorded ownership, applicable state law and dated tuition price. They no longer reduce every government fee by a state tuition share. Paid school bills and government appropriations keep their existing cash recipients; no extra government revenue or automatic backfill is created.
