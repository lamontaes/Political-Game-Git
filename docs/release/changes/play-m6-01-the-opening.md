title: The President's address plays as a montage, starting at the desk in the Oval Office

The address no longer plays over the home backdrop alone: it opens on the President at the Resolute desk, cuts to the town's own people along a route, and ends at home. A longer address also cuts to the colonnade and to the Vice President, the chambers' leaders and the player's state's key officials named by office.
