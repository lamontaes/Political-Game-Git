title: The game can write the President's speech that opens a new life
---

The game can now write the speech the President gives as a new life opens: the State of the Union, or the inaugural address when the President took office that year. It greets the Vice President and Congress, gives the country's unemployment, inflation and growth from the world's own figures, and speaks in turn to Congress, the governors, the mayors and the families watching before it closes. Each stretch of the speech names the stop of the opening the camera cuts to while it plays. Nothing shows it yet; the cinematic opening that plays it comes next.
