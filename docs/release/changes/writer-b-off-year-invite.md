Proposed dialogue for a local party chair inviting a member to a committee meeting. Returning members can ask who could change the bargaining-fee rule they heard the chair discuss. A member who heard the chair can also accept an introduction, ask for a private conversation, or explain why they would rather speak in the room.

When the member passed on the last meeting, the chair can offer a seat together, an introduction or an evening to listen. Each offer has its own acceptance and refusal.

A returning invitee can agree to check the canvass map, ask how a precinct appointment is recorded, or decline the meeting while still offering to help find a candidate.

When the chair offers introductions, a member can ask to sit with the longest-serving regular, offer to stay late and help, or suggest meeting someone over coffee instead.
