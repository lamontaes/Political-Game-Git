id: studs-money-delete-sentences
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The finances screen shows figures, not hand-written sentences
---

Before, the Money and property screen explained itself in fixed sentences:
who may spend each balance, that the household has no shared money, that no
new law has reached anyone's money, how many other people work at a business,
and a paragraph on what a house costs and what you pay down and per month.

Now those sentences are gone. Each balance shows its name and amount, the
house shows its price, down payment and monthly mortgage payment, and an
owned home shows the mortgage left. The graph captions and the loading line
on the economic context are removed too. The buy-a-home button still turns
off when you cannot afford the home.
