title: A data privacy law now binds only large businesses, as the real statutes do
---

A state or national consumer data privacy law no longer charges every small business in town; it reaches only businesses large enough for the statute (yearly sales over about $25 million). Pool item: laws-data-privacy-binds-by-size.
