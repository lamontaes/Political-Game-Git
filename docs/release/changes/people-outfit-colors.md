Each person's clothes now have their own colors: everyday tops in 14 colors
and trousers in 8, suits in 6, shirts in 5 and ties in 5, chosen once per
person so neighbors no longer dress alike. In the creator and in Appearance
and wardrobe, arrows choose the color of each piece.
