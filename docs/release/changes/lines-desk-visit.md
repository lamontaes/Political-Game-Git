title: The visit to your desk about a passed bill is back, in new words.
---

As a mayor or governor, the sponsor of a passed bill comes to your desk to ask you to sign it, or a lobbyist comes to ask for a veto. You can ask what the bill does, what it costs and who voted against it, and the reply that signs or vetoes says so plainly.
