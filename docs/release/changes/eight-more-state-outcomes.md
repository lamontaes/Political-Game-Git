title: Every state now tracks hunger, reading, college degrees, broadband, children's asthma, overdose deaths, earnings and transit.
---

Each state starts at its real recent share of households that go hungry,
eighth graders who read at grade level, adults with a college degree, homes
with broadband and children with asthma, and at its real overdose death rate,
typical earnings and amount of bus and train service. Each drifts over the
years, sometimes with the whole country, and is never pulled back to where it
started. Places with no published figure record nothing rather than a guess.
