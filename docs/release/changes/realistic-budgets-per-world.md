title: Each new world opens with its own federal debt, public budget mixes and federal stakes
---

Each new game opens with its own federal debt and its own mix of who holds it, its own split of where public budget lines send their money, its own mix of the fees governments charge, and its own pull of federal spending on each state, instead of the same published figures in every world.
