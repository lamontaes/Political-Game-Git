title: Town workers' pay and old replays work again
---

A day could stop with an error when a grown-up in your household worked for
the town or another public employer that pays every two weeks, twice a month
or once a month. Weekly pay now leaves those paychecks to the town's own
payday, so the day goes on.

A game replayed from an old saved setup again records finishing high school
the way it did when that setup was made, instead of a slightly different
record.

When your own race is decided on a day full of candidate filings across the
state, the news around town now leads with your result instead of dropping it
below eight statewide filings.

Kentucky's legislative terms again start on January 1 after the election for
every screen that reads the rule.

Days that seed the people around you, seat a town's businesses or put a state
constitutional amendment to a vote run faster: each is checked once when it
finishes instead of after every record it writes.
