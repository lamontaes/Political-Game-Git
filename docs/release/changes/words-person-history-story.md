A person's card tells their life as a short story: the first sentence names them, the rest use their pronouns, and your own card speaks to you. A run of terms in one office is one sentence ("won election as state representative, and won every race for the seat after that"). Two or three lines under the name say who they are now and where they come from. The seat's "since" year, the public record and those lines all date the seat from the first term the story tells.
