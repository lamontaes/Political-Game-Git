title: Everyone with a job in your town is paid
---

Before, only you and officeholders were ever paid. Your neighbors' jobs had
titles and hours but no pay.

Now, everyone with a job in town is paid by their employer on the employer's
own payday: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month or monthly. Governments
pay every two weeks. Each worker's pay comes from what their occupation
really pays in the town's area, higher the longer they have held the job,
and never below the state's minimum wage. Social Security and Medicare are
taken out of every paycheck, just as they are from yours. In the first 200
days in Columbus, Ohio, 84 neighbors were paid $2.38 million.
