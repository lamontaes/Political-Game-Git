title: Taxes keep coming out of pay, passed budgets change spending, and laws pay people
---

Income tax, Social Security, state income tax, SNAP amounts, the Medicaid match and local minimum wages no longer lapse after their first year; each stays in force until a law changes it. A budget that a council, county board, legislature or Congress passes is the budget in force from the first day of its fiscal year, and town payroll is booked against its lines. SNAP pays eligible households each month, unemployment benefits follow the state's own law, and the sales tax in force is collected on the town's sales. Every law in force shows who it reached, or the fact about the town that leaves it with nothing to do here.
