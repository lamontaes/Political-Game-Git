impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A count before a vote says "tomorrow" only the day before, and nobody names a vote shortfall the count does not hold.
---

The scene where you or a colleague goes looking for votes says the vote is tomorrow only on the day before it, and today on the day itself; before, it said "tomorrow" on the morning of the vote. Lines that named how many votes were missing ("Three votes. That's all I'm short.") now say only that the votes are short, because no count of the shortfall is on record.
