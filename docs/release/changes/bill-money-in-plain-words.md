section: Improved
title: Bill money is written in plain words.
---

A bill's money lines round like the Budgets screen, the arrow between today's amount and the new one is gone, the engine's fiscal note ("no pay effect") no longer prints on the paper, and budget lines read in plain words ("Corrections", "Aid to other governments") instead of Census category names.
