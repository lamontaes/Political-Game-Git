section: Changed
title: News tells every story the press prints in plain words
---

Every story your town's papers and stations print during play now shows in News as a headline and a first sentence, under the outlet that printed it: a law taking effect, a bill signed or vetoed, a council vote and its count, a charge or a sentence, a business closing, the month's unemployment, a filing, a debate. None shows as a record name or a row of labeled fields any more. Papers also stop printing the record kept when someone new enters the world.
