title: A visit to meet a new baby now only comes to the baby's parents, from a relative who is not the other parent.
---

When you have a baby, a relative now comes by to meet the child and asks how you are really doing, naming the baby and your partner. Before, other people's babies could cast you as the parent, and the baby's own father could be the one who came to visit.
