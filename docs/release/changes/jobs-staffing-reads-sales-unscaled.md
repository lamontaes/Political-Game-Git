title: A town's employers no longer shrink their staff for sales they are making
---

An employer's sales-supported staffing was divided by how many jobs each opening record stood for, though its receipts already match the demand its books expect. At a small town the staffing target read about a sixth of what its sales supported, and at a large city about a thousandth, so every employer let staff go over the first year. At a drawn town a played year now holds employment (2,042 to 2,087 people, not 2,042 to 1,623) with hires and layoffs near the JOLTS rates.
