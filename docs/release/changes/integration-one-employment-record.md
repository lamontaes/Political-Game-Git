impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Job offers come from a person, not a pop-up
---

The panel that offered a job with no one behind it is gone. An employer's offer now comes as a call from someone who works there, who tells you the job and the pay, and you answer them in the scene. Until the call's lines are written the offer is not shown and lapses.
