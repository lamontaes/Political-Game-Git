title: The Study tab shows the programs you can enroll in
---

The Study tab under Education and work showed only the directory of real
colleges, where nothing can be applied for. The programs a person can actually
enroll in, including the certificates that open better-paid jobs, sat only
inside the collapsed "Other paths and invitations" section, so a Casper life
reached 21 without finding them. The Study tab now lists those programs first,
each with its cost, length, credential and an Enroll button, above the college
directory. Work, volunteering and invitations stay where they were.
