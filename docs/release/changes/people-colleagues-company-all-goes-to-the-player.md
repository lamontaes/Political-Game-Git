title: Your colleagues keep company with each other, not only with you.
---

Members of a council, board or legislature now spend their free time with the colleagues they have come to know from sitting together, each with their own nearest colleagues. Before, a legislator's colleagues all sought out the player, because the player was the only one whose meetings were recorded, so the player was close to the whole chamber and never had to ask anyone for a vote.
