title: With no Vice President, the Speaker acts as President
---

When the President dies while the vice presidency is empty, the office no
longer stays vacant. The Speaker of the House resigns as Speaker and as a
Representative and acts as President for the rest of the term, as federal
law provides; failing a Speaker, the President pro tempore of the Senate
does. The Speaker's House seat is then filled like any other vacancy.
