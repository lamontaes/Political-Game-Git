title: Groups in town now endorse candidates and send them volunteers.
---

The organizations in your town now take sides in its races. A residents' group, a cause group someone founded, or a party's local chapter weighs everyone running, you included, whose platform takes up what the group stands for, by how well it fits and how well its leader knows them, and backs the one it favors most, or no one when nobody agrees with it or stands out: a group's leader being your friend never wins you its backing on its own. When a group endorses, its members hear about it, and the ones who care most about its cause and have the free time join that campaign as volunteers. Your Campaigns screen counts your endorsements, and everyone you ask to endorse you now gives an answer that sticks, along with the reason behind it.
