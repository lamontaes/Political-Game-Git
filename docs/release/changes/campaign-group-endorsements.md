title: Groups in town now endorse candidates and send them volunteers.
---

The organizations in your town now take sides in its races. A residents' group, a cause group someone founded, or a party's local chapter weighs everyone running, you included, by how well each one's platform fits what the group stands for and how well its leader knows them, and backs the one it favors most, or no one when nobody stands out. When a group endorses, its members hear about it, and the ones who care most about its cause and have the free time join that campaign as volunteers. Your Campaigns screen counts your endorsements, and everyone you ask to endorse you now gives an answer that sticks, along with the reason behind it.
