title: Local recalls now follow twelve more places' own filing dates, three of them closely estimated.
---

In twelve more places, a petition to recall a council member or mayor now waits for the dates that place's own law sets, instead of one national rule that no recall may start in a term's first six months. Most of these dates are read straight from the law. Three are close estimates: Florida, where the law waits for a quarter of the term and the game waits a few months too long when a term is three years, and North Dakota and Washington, where the law counts from an election. Nebraska's bar in the months before the incumbent filing deadline is not applied yet.
