title: Local recalls now follow twelve more places' own filing dates, seven of them closely estimated.
---

In twelve more places, a petition to recall a council member or mayor now waits for the dates that place's own law sets, instead of one national rule that no recall may start in a term's first six months. Five of these dates are read straight from the law. Seven are close estimates. In Florida the law waits for a quarter of the term, and the game waits a few months too long when a term is three years. In North Dakota and Washington the law counts from an election. In Louisiana, Montana, Nebraska and Nevada the law counts in months, and the game counts a fixed number of days, which can be off by a day or two. Nebraska's bar in the months before the incumbent filing deadline is not applied yet.
