title: The story reads how close two people are from the same record everyone else does
---

How far apart two people have drifted is now the same in the story as everywhere else in the game. Before, the story director faded a friendship by its own clock, about fifteen times faster over a year than the people themselves did, so it treated friends as drifting apart when they had not.
