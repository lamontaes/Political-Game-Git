title: A business that closes now records which costs and laws made its losing months
---

A closing recorded its reason only as losses kept, so nothing could say which cost or law was behind it. Each business now keeps what its losing months paid, cost by cost with the laws behind each, and which of those months a law's charges tipped: months that would not have lost money without those charges. The closing record and the closing event carry it, and dev mode's Why for a business shows it.
