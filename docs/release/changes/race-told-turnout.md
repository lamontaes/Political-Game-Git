---
id: race-told-turnout
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Asked who turned out for a winner, a neighbor now says how much of the vote and the turnout the count shows.
---

When someone tells you who won a race and you ask who turned out, they now say what the count shows ("Karen Ali drew 57 percent of the ballots cast," or the share of the people who could vote who did, or the place that gave the winner the most) instead of "that's all I know for sure."
