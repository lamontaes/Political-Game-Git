title: A life can start on Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the Northern Mariana Islands
---

The new-game place list now offers all fifty-six places: the fifty states,
D.C., Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa and the
Northern Mariana Islands. Before, only Puerto Rico of the five territories
could be chosen, because the Census list the game's towns come from covers
the states, D.C. and Puerto Rico and nothing else.

Each of the four new territories offers a short list of its well-known
villages and towns for now, such as Dededo on Guam, Charlotte Amalie on
St. Thomas, Pago Pago on Tutuila and Garapan on Saipan. The full lists are
filed as research, and the placeholder towns will be replaced when the answer
arrives.

Each territory is its own government. None of them is given a legislature
drawn from the states, and the introduction to a new life now says what a
territory is: not a state, with its own Governor and legislature, a Delegate
(or, for Puerto Rico, a Resident Commissioner) in the U.S. House, and no seat
in the Senate. It used to call Puerto Rico "Your state government" and say
no governor was recorded there.
