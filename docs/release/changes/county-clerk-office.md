title: Towns in a county have the county clerk's office
---

Before, a town had a city clerk but nobody from the county clerk's office,
the office that keeps a county's records and takes filings.

Now a town inside a county with a county government, such as Belzoni in
Humphreys County, has a county clerk who works the clerk counter on
weekdays. A town whose county has no separate government, such as
Lexington, keeps its city clerk and has no county clerk.
