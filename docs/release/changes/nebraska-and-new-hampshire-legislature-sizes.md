title: Nebraska and New Hampshire legislatures have their real sizes
---

Nebraska's one-house Legislature had 128 members and New Hampshire's House
had 93. Nebraska now has its 49 members, and New Hampshire has its 400
representatives and 24 senators, each citing the state constitution. Every
other state the game has not read still draws its chamber sizes from the
range of the states it has read, and those sizes are with research.
