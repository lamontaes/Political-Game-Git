---
id: local-seats-file-at-the-clerks-counter
section: Changed
title: Local seats are filed at the clerk's counter, not from the Campaigns list
impact: minor
---

A seat that a clerk's counter in the world takes, a town or city council, a mayor, a county executive or a county row office, is no longer offered on the Campaigns screen's list of offices: the player books a visit through Places, goes to the counter, and files there. A council filed by numbered seat, such as the District of Columbia's, now names the seat at the counter. The counter's buttons can be pressed again; the corner notes around them had been swallowing every press. The Campaigns screen no longer opens on the "Role:" sentence. State, statewide and federal seats keep their filing on the Campaigns screen until their own counters exist.
