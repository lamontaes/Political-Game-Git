title: Each county's politics in a new world comes from its own past
---

In a world whose past runs from 1925, every county now votes in each presidential election at its own lean and carries its own results forward, so a county on day one stands where its own history left it instead of where 2024's map put it. A state stands where its counties' vote adds up. A world's counties keep their order against each other but drift on their own.
