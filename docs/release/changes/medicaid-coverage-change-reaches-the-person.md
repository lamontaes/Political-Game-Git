section: Changed
title: Gaining or losing Medicaid coverage names the person and the law that did it
---

When a state's Medicaid expansion or work requirement puts someone on coverage
or takes it away, the change now shows up as something that law did to that
person, and their partner hears of it at home. The record is a gain or a loss
with no dollar amount, because the coverage record shows enrollment, not what
it was worth to them. Officials behind the law can be seen in a better or worse
light because of it, and a repeal that ends coverage records its own loss.
