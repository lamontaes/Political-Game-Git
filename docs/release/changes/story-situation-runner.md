title: A friend's request to meet no longer claims you have been apart without a record of it
---

When someone you know asks to meet, their first line says "It's been a long time" only when the record shows the two of you have been apart. Behind it, the story director's scenes can now run on the conversation engine: the records cast every role, the other person answers through their own decision, and what each says changes how the two stand. Those scenes appear once the English engine has lines for them.
