title: Officials' aides in the capital now turn up for work
---

An aide hired where an official's office sits (a state capital or Washington) used to have no household costs, so nothing pulled them to a paycheck and about a quarter of them never came to work or got paid. They now have living costs and savings like the officials they serve, and nearly all of them work.
