title: The paper says each thing once, the way a paper does
---

An endorsement brief no longer repeats its headline as its body. It says when the vote is instead, and a group like "Friends of policing" keeps its capitals and takes "back", not "backs". A bill that failed "fell short by" the votes it lacked for a majority, not by its margin. A law is told by whose it is and what it does, not by a catalog label. An empty station page says "Nothing here yet." instead of talking about print.
