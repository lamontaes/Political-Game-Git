title: People at the door talk about their own lives when you ask for a signature or a vote
---

A door you knock on now plays the scene its resident's own life gives it: someone laid off this year, a household with a rent notice on the door, someone sick or caring for someone sick inside, a friend or relative who knows you, a member of your party. Each has its own lines in many wordings, and a second knock on the same door raises the next thing down instead of the same talk. Backing off from someone unwell is never held against you; pressing them strains the tie.
