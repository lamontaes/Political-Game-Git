title: Your character turns 18 in play, and a young adult without work looks for it
impact: patch
---

Your character used to start already 18 and turn 19 within days. You now start as the 17-year-old in town whose 18th birthday comes soonest, so the birthday happens while you play. A young adult who has never had a job now looks for work when the household's money runs short, even when a parent pays their living costs. They apply to every opening worth more to them than waiting, and they can take a job that was last held by someone in the wider town, on that job's usual hours. On the first shift a new hire meets the coworkers already there.
