title: Each rate under Politics → Issues names the government that sets it.

Issues no longer calls the federal income tax a local one. Each law is named for what it is ("Income tax rate"), and each rate shows the government that sets it ("United States 37% top rate", "Texas 6.25% · City of Anson 1.951%"). A government that levies none is left out.
