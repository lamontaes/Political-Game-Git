section: Changed
title: Thirty of Connecticut's largest towns now have their own governing bodies
---

Connecticut towns such as Wallingford (a nine-member Town Council and an elected mayor), Wethersfield, Windsor and Glastonbury (council-manager towns with a Town Council) now open with the body their charter names, not a three-member Board of Selectmen. Towns with a Representative Town Meeting and the ones still unread keep the old default.
