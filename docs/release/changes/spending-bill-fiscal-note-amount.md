title: A spending bill's fiscal note states what it provides
---

When a spending bill came up for negotiation, its fiscal note said the bill
appropriated nothing, even when it provided millions. The note now states the
amount the bill provides. A bill that charges a fee states the charge for each
payment and says its total is unknown until the number of payments is known.
