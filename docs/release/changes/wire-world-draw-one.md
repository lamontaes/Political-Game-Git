title: One draw sets how far each new world opens from the published figures
---

Every starting figure a new game draws around its published value (the national and state economy, budget lines, the federal debt, housing medians, outcome measures and the rest) now takes its offset from the one world draw, with one floor for how far off it must land; the four copies of that rule and their four floor settings are gone.
