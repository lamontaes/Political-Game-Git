title: Desk, floor-vote, nomination and meeting scenes hide the menu and card, and hold pop-ups
---

While you sit at the desk (signing or vetoing, a floor vote, nominations, orders), in a meeting, on election day or at the ballot, the menu buttons and your card step aside, and news pop-ups wait until it ends, as they already did in talks.
