---
id: item-veto-saved-view
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A governor's item veto now follows a view the governor actually holds
---

A governor who strikes a floor-added section with the item veto first forms a
view on that question from the principles they hold, and the view is kept in
their history. Anything that reads their views, the veto included, reads that same
view. A governor whose principles say nothing about the question leaves the
section in place.
