title: Conversation choices can be worded as things people really say
---

The English engine can now word an everyday conversation choice as the sentence the player would say, such as "Good morning." for saying hello or "Why is that?" for asking why, in place of a fixed label. Each sentence comes from a new talk-choice bank mined from what people said to each other in public-domain federal oral histories and testimony. A greeting that names a time of day is used only at that hour, a person is called by name only when the player knows them, and a sentence the owner grades down is not chosen. The screen does not show these yet; they go to the owner for grading first.
