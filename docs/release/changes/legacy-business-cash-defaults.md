---
id: legacy-business-cash-defaults
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Old business payments require recorded cash
---

Legacy business payments use dated recorded payer money instead of completing
automatically. Untracked money blocks payment; insufficient money records a
partial or missed payment. Unsupported opening locations no longer receive a
fixed business revenue table. Existing saved contracts and receipts remain intact.

Every registered locality can receive an explicitly labeled business estimate
from existing CBP and Economic Census rates. Missing local population uses
recorded 2020 county-part totals, then a calculated regional or national
populated-place mean with its source and denominator stated. New owner draws
remain unestablished until operating costs and distributable earnings are
recorded; existing saved draw contracts continue to settle from recorded cash.
