title: Firms in a big town stop closing for want of cash to pay their workers
---

In a town opened at its full Census size, shops, offices and local TV stations used to run out of cash and close, taking their workers' jobs with them. Their sales now grow with the town as their wages do, and a business owner no longer draws out the cash the next payroll needs. A business short of cash on a payday now draws the savings it holds at the bank before the payroll goes unpaid, and puts aside enough there to cover its workers' full payroll. A shop staffed by a big town's workers can now serve as many customers as those workers stand for.
