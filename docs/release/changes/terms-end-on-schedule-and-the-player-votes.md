title: Terms end on schedule, seats open when an official moves away, and your character votes while you skip
impact: patch
---

Local terms are now dated from the place's own election calendar. A county or town that votes only in odd or even years holds the election for each seat before its term runs out, the winner takes office on the day the new term starts instead of on Election Day, and the seat comes up again a full term later instead of at the next election on the calendar. An official who moves out of the town or county they serve leaves the seat, and it is filled the way the state's law says, by appointment or a special election; someone who has moved away can't be appointed and doesn't stand for re-election. When you skip past Election Day without marking a ballot, your character goes to vote or stays home for their own reasons, the same as everyone else, and a ballot you mark yourself still counts.
