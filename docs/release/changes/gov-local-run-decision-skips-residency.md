title: A county seat's race no longer reaches its ballot day with no one on the ballot
---

A county board seat's special election now takes county residents as candidates, leaves the person at the filing counter out of the race, and asks a district seat's petition of its district's voters. The clerk's residency check and the people who weigh running read one rule, so nobody decides to run and is then refused for where they live. A campaign's circulators spend the money set aside for them on the last turn before the petition is due.
