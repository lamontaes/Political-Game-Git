title: Recover the next day's available time and enter supported legislative terms.
---

Keep the same character and home when a busy day gives way to the next morning.
Kentucky House and Senate results now plan the recorded winner's office work
for the supported term boundary, including a rival. Office entry, changed work,
expiry and saved reopening use the existing clock and work records. A result
alone grants no current office authority.
