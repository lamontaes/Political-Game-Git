title: Officials weigh each constituent's problem on its own merits
---

When a resident brings a problem to an official, the official's answer now turns on how hard the problem presses, how well they know the resident and how many cases their office already has, and a staffer's follow-through reads the same load. A resident who knows no official goes to the office with the fewest open cases. Temperament tips these calls at the sizes studies give.
