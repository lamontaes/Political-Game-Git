title: A celebration and a birthday missing a relative now each ask you for something.
---

At a wedding or a new-job celebration in your home, a relative asks you for something: a few words for everyone, a picture, a talk about an old quarrel, or help finding work. When a birthday at home goes by with a relative missing, someone in your household asks you to call, write or visit them, and may mention their news, like a new baby or an illness. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
