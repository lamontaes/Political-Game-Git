title: Your working life before the game starts is lived, and the Journal tells it
---

The years before your first day are no longer blank. Everyone in your town who is old enough lived a working life from the day they left school: the jobs they took, the times they were let go when work dried up, the months out of work before the next job, the moves to a better-paying employer, and the year they stopped to raise a child or retired. Your Journal tells yours, employer by employer, and a different world gives a different life. People you talk to know how long they have held their job from the same record, and a mayor weighing an appointee counts all the years they worked.
