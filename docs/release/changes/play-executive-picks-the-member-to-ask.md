title: A mayor, county executive or governor chooses which member of the body to ask to carry a bill.
---

When a mayor, county executive or governor sends a drafted bill through a member, a list of the body's members opens with the one most likely to say yes marked. Asking a member who is not the likely yes can bring a no, kept with its reason and date like any other.
