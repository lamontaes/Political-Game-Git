title: Long games no longer stop when the paper's staff list is refreshed
---

Someone who had faded back into the town's wider population and then moved away could still be hired for a job in town. When that job was at a newspaper, the paper's weekly staff refresh tried to bring the absent worker into the story and the game stopped (Montpelier, Vermont, in year 19). Job seekers who have moved away or died are now taken off the list before anyone is hired.

When a household that had faded back into the wider population moves away, the people in it now move away too, as anyone else who leaves town does: their relatives in town hear of it, and they stop looking for work here.
