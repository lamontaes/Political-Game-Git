section: Improved
title: State and federal officials have a full personality, like the people of your town.
---

Every governor, legislator and member of Congress now has a level on every personality trait, drawn the way a resident's is, and still leans more outgoing, dutiful and driven than the average person. Their personality now weighs in their choices as fully as a neighbor's does in theirs, where before most of it was missing.
