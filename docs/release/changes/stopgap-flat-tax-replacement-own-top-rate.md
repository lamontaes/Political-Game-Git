section: Changed
title: A repealed graduated income tax becomes a flat tax tied to the place's own old rate
---

When a vote ends a graduated state or local income tax, the flat tax that replaces it is now set from that place's own old top rate, at the share real states chose when they flattened theirs, instead of one national rate for every place.
