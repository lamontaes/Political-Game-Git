title: A sponsor writes the flat rate that replaces a graduated income tax
---

When a vote ends a graduated state or local income tax, the flat tax that replaces it is now the place's own old top rate times a share its sponsor chooses: a sponsor who holds the question hard writes the deepest cut real states chose, one who barely holds it the shallowest.
