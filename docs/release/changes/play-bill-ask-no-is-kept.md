title: A member's no to an executive's bill is kept with its reason and date, and the same member can be asked again.
---

When a member declines to carry a mayor's, county executive's or governor's bill, Your office now says why, in plain words (or in the member's own words for a named question). The no is kept with its date, and asking the same member again later says when they said no before.
