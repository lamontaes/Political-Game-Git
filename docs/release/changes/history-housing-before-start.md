title: Housing has a past before your first day, so Metrics shows last year's rent and home value from the start
---

The years before your story begins now run for housing as they already did for people, jobs, budgets and laws. Every lease in town was signed on the day its household moved in, at that month's rent, and has been renewed each year since; every home was bought in a year its owners' lives agree with, and has moved with the state's home prices since. On your first day, Metrics shows the town's typical rent and typical home value beside last year's figures, and a lease comes up for renewal on the month it was first signed.
