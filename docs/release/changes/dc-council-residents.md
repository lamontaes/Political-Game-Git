---
id: dc-council-residents
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The D.C. Council's members are District residents
---

A life that starts in Washington, D.C. now opens with a Council whose thirteen
members are residents of the District, drawn from the same households as the
rest of the city, and never from the player's own household. Before, each
member was made up for the seat with an age drawn between 25 and 80. The
Mayor's part in the District's acts is now read from a data file that cites
the Home Rule Act, not from a rule written for one place.
