title: At the filing counter, the clerk talks about your race, and other scenes can join their lines from pieces
---

At the filing office the clerk greets you by the town, names the office you came for, tells you how many others have filed and who, and your replies each name the race. Scene lines can now be joined from pieces (an opener, a core that names something from your world, a reason, a closer), so the same moment reads differently in every town.
