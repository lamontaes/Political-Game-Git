title: A town's city clerk now shows up on the town office's staff, so you can find who to ask there.
---

Towns record their government under the municipal-government classification, which the public-office list did not recognize, so a town's clerk was left off the office staff while a county's clerk was listed. The town office is now built from the same recorded government facts as the county office, with its clerk on the staff.
