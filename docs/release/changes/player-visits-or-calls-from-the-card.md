title: Visit or call a person from their card
---

A person's card now has Reach out, with Visit and Call. Visit takes you to where they are now, their home or their workplace while they are on a shift, spends the trip and a visit's hours on your clock, and opens a talk with them and whoever is there. Call opens a talk with no trip. Both count as time together, so the two of you stay closer. When an act cannot be done the card says why: they are in the room with you, they live too far away to visit, or they have nothing to say right now.
