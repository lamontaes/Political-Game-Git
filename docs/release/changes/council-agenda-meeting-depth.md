section: Added
title: A council member sees the agenda with what matters marked and chooses how much to sit through
---

Before a council meeting a seated member can now read the agenda: each item
with the saved reasons it matters to them, whether they take it themselves,
and what their voting workflow does with it if they do not. A seat can carry a
default depth, only what matters or everything, and a member can choose to sit
through one whole meeting. When they do, or when everything is their default,
or when they handle each vote individually, no standing instruction casts
those items for them. They stay the member's own, and any item they leave
undecided is still recorded absent.
