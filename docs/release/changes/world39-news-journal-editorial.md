title: News speaks about the place; the Journal tells the life you lived
---

News now opens with the public situation where you live: who governs the
place, what serves it, who holds office and since when, and what has happened
in public lately, told plainly rather than as a list of records. The Journal
tells your life year by year in your own second person, with what you privately
think and what you said in public attributed to you; your private notes and the
exact record remain underneath.
