id: tax-laws-reach-state-budgets
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Adopting an income tax, exempting groceries and legalizing cannabis sales now change a state's budget
---

A state with no income tax that passed one withheld it from paychecks, but its
budget collected nothing. It now collects the new tax from the next tax year,
at the average the states with a wage tax collect per resident, moved by how
much the state's workers earn. A later repeal ends it.

A law exempting groceries from the sales tax, or taxing them again, now moves
the state's sales tax from the day it takes effect, by the share Oklahoma's
2024 grocery tax repeal was estimated to cost.

A law legalizing adult cannabis sales now adds cannabis taxes to the state's
budget once the first stores open, 11 months after the law takes effect, at
what the states with established stores collect per resident. A law ending
legal sales removes that revenue the day it takes effect.
