title: A tuition freeze now shows up in the Journal of the student it saved money
---

When a state's tuition freeze holds a student's college charge below the price
the school billed, the student now gets that law and the amount it saved in
their own Journal, and their partner hears of it at home. A charge the freeze
left unchanged writes nothing.
