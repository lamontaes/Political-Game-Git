title: Census-designated towns now have the businesses their size supports
---

A census-designated town such as Kualapuu, Hawaii or Lime Ridge, Pennsylvania
used to get one of every kind of business, with made-up staff and sales,
because the game had no population estimate for it. It now uses the American
Community Survey count for the town, and its grocery stores, diners, garages,
law offices and other businesses follow the county's published establishment
counts like any other town.
