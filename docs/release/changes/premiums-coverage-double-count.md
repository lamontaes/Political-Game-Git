---
id: premiums-coverage-double-count
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Workers who keep their jobs keep their health plans when others are laid off.
---

A worker who kept their job and paid their premium could lose their employer health plan just because the town's jobless rate rose. Losing a job already ends a plan, so the rise was counted twice. Now only the jobs lost cost plans. Dev mode's Watch tab on the Calendar shows the town's employer plans today: workers with a job, the share who hold their own plan, the plans held, and those left out for a missed premium.
