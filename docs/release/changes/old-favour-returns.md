title: A favour you actually did can come back, larger
---

If you agree to help somebody with something and then actually carry it out,
that person may come back to you later asking for something considerably bigger.
The scene says how many times you have helped them before, because the whole
question it puts to you is what your earlier help is now being read as meaning.
You can help again, help and say plainly that this is not the same favour, or
say that the first one did not buy this one.

It only happens where you really did the thing. Agreeing and never getting round
to it does not count, and refusing certainly does not — the game will not have
somebody turn up on the strength of help you did not give. It happens once, and
it waits for a quiet stretch rather than arriving on top of everything else.
