id: office-pay-period-role-eligibility
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Overdue office salary uses the role held when its period began
---

An unpaid office salary period remains eligible after the same recorded work
changes to a nonoffice role. Eligibility uses the period's starting date,
matching the shared payroll's payer, law and amount readers. A period that
begins in a nonoffice role is skipped without preventing later office periods.
Existing weekly dates, cash settlement, taxes and replay identities are
preserved.
