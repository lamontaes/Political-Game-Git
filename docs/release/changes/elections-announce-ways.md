section: Changed
title: An announcement keeps how it was made and who heard it.
---

When someone says they are running, the game now keeps how they said it (a quiet word to someone close, at their party's office, at a town hall or gathering they are part of, or by asking someone for their vote) and who heard it that day. The town's other candidates choose their way from their own life: how many people it reaches and the party roles they hold. Your Announce button, for now, tells the person closest to you.
