title: Party work that cannot happen is shown as unavailable
---

Asking a party organizer for something like a phone shift could offer a button
that only answered that you and the organizer had no free evening in common in
the next two weeks, or that nobody was available to host it. Saying yes to an
offer could fail the same way. These choices are now shown as unavailable,
with the reason, before you press them. A party chapter's meeting invitation
also stops offering Accept once the meeting has begun.
