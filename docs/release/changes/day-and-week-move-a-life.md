title: Day, Week and Calendar move a life forward
---

A life now moves forward through the Day, Week and Calendar controls, from
childhood on. The quiet reading, resting and drawing choices and the separate
buttons for letting time pass are gone. A meeting or invitation due today can
still be played from the story screen, and a birthday invitation shows its
reply and time. A notice about somewhere else waits until the player is there.
