title: Raising the top federal income tax rate now changes top earners' paychecks
---

A federal law raising the top income tax rate now taxes pay in the top
bracket at 39.6% instead of 37%, the top rate before 2018, from the first tax
year that begins with the law in force. A later law lowering it puts 37% back
the same way. Pay below the top bracket is withheld as before.
