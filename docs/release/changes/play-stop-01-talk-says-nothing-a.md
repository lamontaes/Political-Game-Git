title: Talking to your family at home has something to say on the first evening
---

On the first evening, your mother, father or sibling asks whether you saw the President's speech, the household having watched it together, and you have four replies. Whatever you say, they tell you what they made of it, for or against, and you have four more replies. Before, Talk at home gave one goodbye line and nothing to say.
