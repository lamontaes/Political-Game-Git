title: The paper words a study committee's forming and its report, and an empty News page says why it is empty
---

When a body sends a bill that narrowly failed to a study committee, the paper now prints who chairs it, how many sit on it and when it reports; when it reports, the paper says which way its members lean on the bill, says they turned only when their lean changed since the committee formed, and whether the chair gave the findings to the body. Before, those stories were listed with no words at all. An empty News page now says either that you follow no paper or station, or that a paper or station you follow has nothing yet.
