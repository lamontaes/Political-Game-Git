title: The paper words a study committee's forming and its report, and an empty News page says why it is empty
---

When a body sends a bill that narrowly failed to a study committee, the paper now prints who chairs it, how many sit on it and when it reports; when it reports, the paper says whether its members now favor or oppose the bill and whether the chair gave the findings to the body. Before, those stories were listed with no words at all. An empty News page now says either that you follow no paper or station, or that the outlet open has printed nothing yet.
