title: A resident who read about a law can talk about it, and the replies fit
---

A neighbor who learned what a law did to them now has the scene, where it was skipped every time before. A law not yet in force or one from another state is said as what it is ("That's a law in Delaware. It doesn't apply in New Jersey."), and the replies fit it ("That's Delaware's law. It isn't the law in New Jersey."), where only a promise to call was offered. A law that ended is named: "The minimum wage ended on July 24, 2007."
