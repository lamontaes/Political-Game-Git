title: A resident who read about a law can talk about it, and the replies fit
---

A neighbor who learned what a law did to them now has the scene, where it was skipped every time before. A law not yet in force or one from another state is said as what it is ("That's a law in Delaware. It doesn't apply in New Jersey."), and the replies fit it ("That's Delaware's law. It isn't the law in New Jersey."), where only a promise to call was offered. A law is said by its spoken name ("Letting customers pick their electricity supplier took effect, and I'm the one living with it."), and a rule that ended during play is named as the old rule ("The old rule on the minimum wage ended on ...").
