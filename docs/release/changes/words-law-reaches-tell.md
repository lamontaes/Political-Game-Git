title: Someone who read about a law that does not reach them can still talk about it
---

A neighbor who read about a law not yet in force, or about another state's law, had no words, so the scene was skipped. They now say it as it is: "The Social Security payroll tax rate doesn't start until January 1, 2026." / "That's a law in Delaware. It doesn't apply in American Samoa."
