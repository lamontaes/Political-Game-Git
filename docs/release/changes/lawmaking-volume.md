id: lawmaking-volume
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Legislatures take up every change their members want, as time allows
---

Your state legislature, Congress, your town council, your county board and your township board no longer stop at a fixed few bills. Each member files the changes their own views and their voters favor wherever the law differs, and the floor takes up as many as its session time allows. A state legislature now passes about two dozen policy laws in a session instead of three. Councils and boards meet twice a month and pass their own ordinances, and a town governed by a township board now legislates too. The number of different laws a government can pass is still limited by the policies the game models.
