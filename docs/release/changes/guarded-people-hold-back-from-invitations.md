title: A guarded person holds back before agreeing to meet
---

A person who protects their intentions and vulnerabilities until trust is earned
now counts that as one reason to hold back when somebody asks them to meet. It
weighs beside what is between the two of them, the day and everything else they
carry, so the same person asked the same thing without that streak still says
yes.
