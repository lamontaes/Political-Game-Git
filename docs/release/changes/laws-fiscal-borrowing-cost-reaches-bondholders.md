title: A state's fiscal laws change what it pays its bondholders
---

A balanced operating budget law, funding pensions on schedule or keeping a minimum reserve lowers what a state pays to borrow, as the research found, and repealing one raises it. The state's monthly interest payments now follow that cost, a year or more after the change, so a state that drops its balanced budget rule pays its bondholders more.
