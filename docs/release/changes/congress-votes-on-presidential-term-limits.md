title: Congress and the states decide a presidential term-limit amendment member by member
---

When a President is barred from another term, or has served three, members of
Congress now decide for themselves whether to amend the Constitution's term
limit. Each weighs the President's party against their own, how they get
along with the President, and the higher bar of changing the Constitution.
Congress proposes only when two-thirds of each house would vote yes, and each
state legislature ratifies when most of its members would. Before, a draw
decided whether Congress proposed it, how each house divided, and whether each
state ratified.
