title: Starting juvenile laws retain their recorded age limits
---

Starting-law rows now carry the numeric juvenile age ceilings already stated
in their cited source notes. Existing dates and source qualifications remain.
American Samoa and the Virgin Islands receive no invented numeric limit.
The shared dated reader and juvenile consumer remain required dependencies;
this data does not authorize a transfer to adult court.
