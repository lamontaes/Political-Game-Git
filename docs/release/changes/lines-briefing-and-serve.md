title: The emergency briefing and the ask to serve are back, in new words.
---

As a mayor or governor, after a disaster your aide asks you to declare an emergency before the deadline, and you can ask what it changes or hold off. As a candidate, you can ask someone to take a post or run with you, and they answer with their own reason.
