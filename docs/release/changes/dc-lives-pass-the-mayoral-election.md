title: A life in Washington, D.C. goes on past the first mayoral election
---

Every life in the District of Columbia stopped on November 2, 2026, the day
before the first mayoral election: each press of a time button failed with
"The contest was not run in the jurisdiction this office governs." The game
checked the Mayor's election against a district-wide stand-in rather than the
District's one government, which is where the election is actually held. The
Mayor's term is now dated against the District's own government, and time
passes through the election and into the new term.
