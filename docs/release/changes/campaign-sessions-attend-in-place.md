title: Campaign sessions can be attended from the calendar
---

A campaign's booked sessions can now be carried out from the calendar. A field
shift, a fundraising call session or an advertising sign-off booked by a
committed week (or on its own) no longer refuses with "You have no way to get
from … to The campaign's call desk" and no longer offers "Give up on this".
Attend in the Calendar, Places, or Today's "Use your time" does the session in
place, the same way the Campaigns tab's "Do it now" does: the money raised,
the doors knocked and the buy placed are recorded, no journey is invented, and
time moves on past it. A session that cannot be done says why — an earlier
session in the week comes first, the committee can no longer pay for the buy,
or its time has already passed ("Let it go from the campaign's week") — and
nothing is written. Measured running for mayor of Eufaula, Alabama; it affected
every campaign.
