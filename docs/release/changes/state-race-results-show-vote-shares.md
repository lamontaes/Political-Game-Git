title: Race results say the vote shares
---

A race result used to say only who won: "Fatima Erickson won. The seat is
theirs." It now gives the margin, "won, 52.3% to 47.7%", on the campaign screen,
in the governor's status on the Candidacy screen, and in the public record of
the result. The papers write their story from that record, so a statehouse
paper's report of a governor's race now carries the numbers too.
