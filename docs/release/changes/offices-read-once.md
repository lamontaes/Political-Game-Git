title: Appointments and emergencies look up office holders once
---

Choosing someone for an appointment, such as a new Chief Justice, weighs the
public offices each candidate holds. Each candidate's offices were found by
reading the whole of Congress and every state's executive offices again, so
an appointment cost more with every year a world ran. The offices a world
seats are now read once for each moment of the world and kept by holder.
Every choice comes out the same.
