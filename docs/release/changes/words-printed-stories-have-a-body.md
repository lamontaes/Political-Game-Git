title: Printed news stories have a body, not only a headline and one line
---

A story about a new law now says what the law is about and the bill it came from: "The Highway Funding Restoration Ordinance took effect in Milbank on February 24, 2025. It deals with returning highway money from public transit to highways. It came into law as Ordinance 2025-2." An election story names who the winner beat. A sentence that only repeats the opening line is left out.
