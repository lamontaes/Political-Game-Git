title: A candidate asks you for your endorsement once, not every few days
---

A candidate who asked you for your endorsement and heard nothing used to ask again at every campaign turn, so one council season could bring dozens of asks. Now an ask waits until you answer. Only an ask you put off comes back, once, after a few days, and nothing asks after the vote.
