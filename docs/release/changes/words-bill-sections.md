title: A bill you draft now reads like a real bill, with its sections and effective date.
---

When you draft a bill from your seat, you see it on paper: a title, a numbered section with your body's own enacting words, what the law said before and what it will say after, and the day it takes effect. A town's ordinance reads as an ordinance, not as a federal act.
