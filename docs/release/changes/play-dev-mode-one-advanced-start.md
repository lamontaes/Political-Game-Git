title: New game offers Start a life and Advanced; Advanced builds a world and lets you play as any officeholder in it.

New game now has Start a life and one Advanced entry. Advanced holds the world's seed and settings, builds the world with no questionnaire, and lists everyone who holds an office in it to step in as. The Custom world button and the dead Watch the world button are gone.
