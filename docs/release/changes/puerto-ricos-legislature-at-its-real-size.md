title: Puerto Rico's legislature has its real 51 Representatives and 27 Senators
---

A life in Puerto Rico used to start with a legislature whose size was drawn
from the states' range. It now starts with the Legislative Assembly the
Constitution of Puerto Rico sets: 51 Representatives (one from each of 40
districts and 11 at large) and 27 Senators (two from each of 8 districts and
11 at large). Represented by, under Government, names the home's district
members along with the 11 elected at large in each chamber.
