title: Washington, D.C. and Rhode Island open at their own published unemployment rates.
---

A place whose state is made up only of metro areas used to open at the whole metro area's unemployment rate, which takes in neighboring states. Washington, D.C. opened at 2.9 percent instead of its own 5.6, and Rhode Island at 4.2 instead of 4.5. Both now open at their published rates.
