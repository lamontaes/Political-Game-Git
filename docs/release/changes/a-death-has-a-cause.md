title: A death has a cause, and a long wait stops on the day your character dies
---

When somebody dies of ordinary causes, the death now has a cause: an illness
that ran its course, a sudden illness, or an accident. The screen that tells
you your character has died says which, for example "died after a serious
illness" or "died in an accident". A death recorded in an older save still
reads plainly as "died"; no cause is made up for it afterward.

A death from an illness no longer comes out of nowhere. Some weeks or months
beforehand the person falls seriously ill, and their household and close
family are told. You can see a relative's illness under Health and
emergencies. If the illness is your own character's, you see it there too and
decide whom to tell.

Who dies, and on what day, is unchanged.

Letting the weeks run on now stops on the day your character dies. Before,
the ending screen could appear with the date already weeks past the death.
