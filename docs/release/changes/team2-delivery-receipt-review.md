title: Delivered public work reports its saved result
---

A saved public-service delivery now reports its actual result to the completed
governing matter linked to that appropriation or enacted measure. Immediate and
delayed deliveries use the same receipt reader. The report keeps the original
service and payment identities through saving and continuing; repeating a
receipt does not create another report. Earlier blocked due records stay intact.
