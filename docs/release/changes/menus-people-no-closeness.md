id: menus-people-no-closeness
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: People no longer marks how close anyone is; its list is face cards
---

People no longer marks anyone "Closer this year" or says whether your five closest changed, and a person's history no longer says they grew closer or apart: how someone feels about you shows in what they are to you and what happened between you. The list view is now face cards, paged, with the one you chose marked.
