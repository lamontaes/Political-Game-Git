section: Fixed
title: Filing opens weeks before the deadline, not on the game's first day
---

Every race now opens for filing on a set day: the day the place's calendar gives, or about two months before the deadline where the calendar gives none. Before, most races stood open from the first day of the game, so a race in November 2026 could be filed for in January 2025, and a campaign stretched over almost two years with nothing in it.
