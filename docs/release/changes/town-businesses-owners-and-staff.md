title: Towns have businesses with owners, staff and monthly revenue
---

Every town now opens with eight businesses, such as a market, a diner, a law
office and a construction firm. Each is named for its owner and has staff who
work there. Money and property lists them. Each business takes in revenue and
pays its owner and staff on the first of every month. Nobody can start a
business yet. The kinds, staff counts and dollar figures are placeholders until
research on real towns' businesses comes back. A child is no longer offered a house, and the offer only appears once
the game holds your money.
