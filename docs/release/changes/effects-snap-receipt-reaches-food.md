impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Losing SNAP to the work requirement shows up in what a household buys, counted once
---

When the work requirement for assistance stops a household head's SNAP benefit, the household now buys less of its everyday plan, and that is the one place the change shows. The town's separate food-insecurity figure no longer moves on its own for the same loss of benefit, so the effect is not counted twice.
