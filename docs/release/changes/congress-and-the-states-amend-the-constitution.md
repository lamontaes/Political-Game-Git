title: Congress and the states can amend the U.S. Constitution
---

The U.S. Constitution can now be amended without the player, through
Article V. Once a year Congress is reviewed. When there is a reason on record
(a President barred from another term, or one who has served three or more),
Congress may propose changing the presidential term limit. It happens
rarely. Two-thirds of each house must vote for it, then each state
legislature acts on it within seven years. It becomes part of the
Constitution on the day the thirty-eighth state ratifies. The District and
the territories do not ratify, and the President has no part in it.

The presidential election now reads the term limit the Constitution holds in
that world, so a ratified amendment decides who may run.

The reasons, how often Congress acts, and how the votes divide are the
game's own until research answers them. Only the presidential term limit can
be amended so far. Not modeled yet, and marked so in the code: other
subjects, a convention called by the states, ratification by state
conventions, and a state withdrawing its ratification.
