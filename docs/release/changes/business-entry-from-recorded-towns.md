id: business-entry-from-recorded-towns
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Businesses open from recorded demand in comparable towns
---

Business openings compare recorded residents per business with the game's
other towns that have an open business in the same line. A missing comparison
no longer supplies an opening from the authored job mix. Existing founder
and staffing rules still write the new employer.

Quarterly business closures come from the existing books. An empty staff
list no longer closes an employer by itself, and the player's workplace
receives the same books review. Congregations and clubs keep their separate
lifecycle rules.
