title: The news starts with what happens in your life, newest first
---

A new life used to open with four local reports already in the archive, about
two public matters that had been posted and closed before the game began. They
came from a fixed template placed at set dates, not from anything that had
happened in that town.

Those fixed reports are gone. The news now fills with what happens after the
life starts. The front page also orders saved reports by date, newest first.
Before, every national story came ahead of every local one, so a report from
your own town could sit below national news from weeks earlier.
