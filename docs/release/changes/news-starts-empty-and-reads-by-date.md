title: The news starts with what happens in your life, newest first
---

A new life used to open with four local reports already in the archive, from
a fixed template placed at set dates rather than anything that had happened in
that town. New lives now start without them, and the news fills with what
happens after the life starts. The front page also orders saved reports by
date, newest first; before, every national story came ahead of every local one.

A replay link made before this change still rebuilds the same world, archived
reports included: new games record a new opening-data version.
