title: The opening night's unemployment rates say where they apply.
---

On the opening night the President now gives the national unemployment rate as the nation's, and the evening news gives the region's rate under the area it covers, such as the Phoenix area or Texas outside the Dallas and Houston areas. News's economy panel shows that area's rate under its name instead of raw field names.
