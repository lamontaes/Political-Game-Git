title: Someone you meet keeps the job they were hired into this year
---

A household that isn't yet known to you now earns what its jobs pay after hires, raises, layoffs and closures. Before, it kept its opening pay forever, so a laid-off family still counted as well off, and a worker hired during the year lost that job the moment you met them. Now they keep it, on their employer's payroll.
