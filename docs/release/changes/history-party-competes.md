title: A party founded in a world's history now stands in its presidential elections

In a world whose past runs from 1925, a party founded there now stands a ticket in each later presidential election. Its voters are those whose lean is near where it stands, so it takes votes from both national parties and moves their shares. The dev opening view lists the races it stood in and what it took.
