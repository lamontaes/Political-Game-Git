title: The corridor scene says what got broken, and who broke it
---

A childhood scene used to open "Something got broken in the corridor at your
school and your name is the one that came up", and then offer "Say who did it"
with nobody named. There was no object, no accused, and no way to tell what you
had seen from what you had been told.

It now names the thing — the trophy case, the fire-alarm cover by the stairs,
the projector cart outside the science room — and names the classmate who broke
it, taken from the school you actually attend. The option that names them says
their name. That person and that object hold through the choice you make, the
memory it leaves, a reload, and the later continuation. The branch that says a
year has passed now waits a real year before saying so. Where a school holds
nobody else, the scene is not offered rather than written around somebody who
does not exist.

At home, the scene about somebody coming in late names the adult you could tell
instead of "a grown-up at home" — and two people in one house no longer answer
to the same name, which is what had made that choice unreadable.
