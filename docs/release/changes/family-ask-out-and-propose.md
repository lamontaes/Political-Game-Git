title: You can ask someone out, and later propose, from their card
---

A person's card now offers Ask out for an adult in town who is not family, and Propose for the person you are with. They answer right away in a short scene, from their own life: whether they are seeing someone, how well they know you, how far apart your ages are, and for a proposal, how long the two of you have been together. A yes starts the two of you dating, or makes you engaged. A no is remembered, and you can ask again once what stood in the way has changed. Waving off a no with "Forget I asked" cools things a little.
