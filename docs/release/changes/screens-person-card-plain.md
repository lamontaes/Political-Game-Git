title: Click a person to talk; their name opens their card
---

Clicking someone in the room starts a conversation, and their name at the top of the talk box opens their card, which no longer has a Talk button. Under the name the talk box says who they are in a sentence, such as "Your partner. Works at Quinn Supply." or "Your sister. Goes to Klamath Union High School.", instead of "Warehouse worker · Quinn Supply Company · partner".
