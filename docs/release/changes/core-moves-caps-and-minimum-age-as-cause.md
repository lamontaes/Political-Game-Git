title: Whether people move away now follows their place's age of majority, their whole family and their savings
---

Young people decide for themselves whether to leave town at the age the law where they live sets, not at 18 everywhere. A big family in town holds someone a little more with each relative, instead of stopping at four. A household that has no income but has savings feels what its home costs less than one with nothing put by.
