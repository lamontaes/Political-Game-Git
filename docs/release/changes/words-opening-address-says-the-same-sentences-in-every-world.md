title: The President's address that opens a new life now speaks of your world, in many versions.
---

Every sentence of the President's address is now put together by the English engine from many versions, and names things from your world: the Vice President and the Speaker of the House, the President's home state, what the President asks Congress for first, and how far prices, pay and jobs moved this year, in words. Two new lives no longer hear the same speech.
