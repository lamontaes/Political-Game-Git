title: The President's address has its fuller parts again.
---

The President's address can again speak to the occasion, the oath, the people and the country's history, ask Congress and the states to act, pose a question the country faces and make a call to the nation, each in new words from the world's records. The opening evening's inaugural stays short; the fuller parts play in the addresses that call for them.
