title: A lawmaker meets a colleague in the hallway after a committee hearing again.

A colleague who sat on a committee hearing, or a reporter after a bill dies in committee, now finds a lawmaker in the hallway on the first free day within two weeks. Before, that day's floor debate or another recent scene always crowded the hallway out, so it never came.
