Adds the core of the people appearance engine, not yet connected to the
game: it measures where each painted body's head, neck and feet are,
repaints skin in seven shades from one painting while keeping its shading,
assembles a person in one fixed layer order with the head above the
clothes, and checks for the two causes of the face seam (garments that carry
painted skin, and a head's neck that doesn't meet the body's). Includes a
skin-tone proof script and a garment skin scan.
