title: Governor races follow the state's voters
---

A governor's race in a state nobody campaigns in is no longer settled by
drawing a few thousand votes for each candidate. The state's own voters decide
it: the race starts from how the state split between the two major parties in
the 2024 presidential election, moved by this world's own political swing, and
counts as many ballots as the state cast that year. The candidates run as the
Democratic and Republican nominees, and the sitting governor, when running
again, carries their own party's line.

A state whose governor was elected shortly before the game opens, like
Virginia's in November 2025, now has that governor-elect ready to take office
when the outgoing term ends. Before, the office sat empty until the next
regular election.
