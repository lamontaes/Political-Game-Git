title: Government rules record their effects on residents
---

State laws can now record whether a photo-identification requirement applies
to voting, whether same-day registration is available, and whether a former
public official is subject to a lobbying restriction. Each permission keeps
the law, date, person and saved records that support it. The current law data
does not specify a lobbying restriction's duration, so no duration is
invented.
