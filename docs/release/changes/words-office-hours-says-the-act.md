id: words-office-hours-says-the-act
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: At office hours you say what your office will do, not which made-up office gets the case
---

When a resident brings their case to your office hours, your answers say what you will do: put their name forward to employers, or press their employer to pay the minimum wage. They no longer send the case to an office the town does not have, and you can no longer claim you already called about it. Turning a case away says why. A resident whose relative is in custody no longer hears the lines meant for someone out of work.
