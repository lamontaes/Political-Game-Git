title: Other states' legislators and governors can die in office again, and their seats are filled by each state's own law.
---

After other states stopped building their officials as people nobody had met, those officials never fell ill or died, so no seat elsewhere ever fell vacant in the middle of a term. Each one now lives by the same health rules as everyone else. When one dies, the term ends and the seat is filled by appointment or special election under that state's own law, and nobody is built for it. Opening one of these officials builds the same person their seat names. A death in the month before the game starts leaves its seat vacant on day one again, and a special election that would come after the seat's new term has already begun is no longer held.
