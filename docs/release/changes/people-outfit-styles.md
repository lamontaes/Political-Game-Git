title: People wear many kinds of clothes
---

People now dress in many different styles, not just one everyday outfit and
one suit. Women wear cardigans and jeans, hoodies, sweaters with slacks,
winter coats with scarves, blouses with skirts, dresses with blazers and skirt
suits. Men wear polos with khakis, hoodies, sweaters over collared shirts, work
jackets, overcoats and double-breasted suits. Each person keeps their own
everyday clothes and their own formal wear, and every piece comes in its own
color. In the creator and in Appearance and wardrobe, the Outfit arrows step
through every style, with a color choice for each piece.
