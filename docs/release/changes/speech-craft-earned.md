title: A President's turns of phrase come from the speeches they have given
---

The opening address now uses a crafted turn of phrase, such as a contrast or a list of three, only when the President's record shows enough speeches to have earned it. Each campaign stop on their record counts as a speech, and every speech the game records adds to it. A line with a crafted turn cites the speeches that earned it.
