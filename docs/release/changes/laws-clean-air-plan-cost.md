impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Polluted counties' factories pay for clean air only under a state clean air plan law
---

A factory or quarry in a county that misses an air quality standard now pays a
monthly compliance cost only while the state's clean air plan law is in force.
Pass the law and the cost starts within the month. Repeal it and the cost
stops.
