title: News is the paper itself
---

News opens as the newspaper: the page in the paper's stock and type under its masthead and dateline, the lead story with its text and photo, the next stories beside it and every other story across the foot, with nothing to page through. The nation, region and jobs rows above it are gone. A story opens under the same masthead and the masthead takes you back; there is no "Back to front page" button, and a story is never its own headline printed twice.
