title: A scene's narrator no longer says its opening line twice
---

A scene with something to settle, such as a job offer on the phone or a classmate's goodbye, opened with two narrators, the situation's line and the scene's own, so a job offer read "The phone rings." twice. It now opens with the scene's own narrator alone, the same voice that speaks between the lines. A moment with no exchange keeps its situation line.
