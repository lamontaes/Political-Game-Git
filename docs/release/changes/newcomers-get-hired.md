title: People who move to a town can get hired there
---

A person who moved into town with no job on the town's record was treated as
never having worked, so every local laid off after them was hired first and
the newcomer never was. Employers now count a newcomer as out of work since
moving in, and a young local with no job since turning 18, so they take their
place in line with everyone else looking for work.
