title: Neighbors now use the parks, libraries and transit your laws pay for.
---

When a parks, public land, library or transit law is funded and its operator is paid, people who live there decide for themselves whether to use it. A parent with a child at home or someone who wants time to themselves signs up for a park program. A worker whose job is in town asks for a ride, while one who works elsewhere does not. Someone whose reasons for and against weigh the same stays undecided. Whoever goes is counted as served once the visit is over, and only then.
