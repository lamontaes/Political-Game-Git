title: Student financing respects saved age, marriage and study progress
---

Supplied undergraduate financing is constrained by the student's recorded age,
active marriage and completed study periods. Published representative annual
and aggregate principal limits cap new borrowing through the existing tuition
shortfall writer. Interest charges do not consume the aggregate principal cap.
This does not create aid awards or infer military service or financial dependents.
