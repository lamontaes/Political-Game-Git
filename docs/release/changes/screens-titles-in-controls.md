title: Screen names come from the controls table; a public figure's start in office reads as a date
---

The names of the screens on the rail and at the top of each sheet now come from the controls table, as every other label does. On a public figure's card the record reads "In office since January 20, 2025" instead of a bare "since" and a stored date, with "Party", "Votes on record", "Laws sponsored" and "Laws signed" beside it.
