title: A reporter lied to about campaign money now finds out when the finding is published
---

Choosing "Deny it (this is a lie)" when a reporter asked about campaign money
used to be checked once, before any finding existed, and then forgotten. Now
every reporter who was given that denial reads the finding when a watchdog
publishes it, learns the denial was false, and takes a follow-up story to
their desk, which asks the candidate to respond before it runs.
