section: Added
title: Each territory has its own Governor, and a life there can run for the office
---

A life in Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the
Northern Mariana Islands now opens with that territory's own Governor in
office, and the player can put their name in for Governor through the same
campaign route a state resident uses. Before, the Campaigns screen told every
territory life that it was "not set in one of the fifty states, so there is
no state executive office to stand for," and no territory had a Governor.

None of the territories' election laws has been read yet, so each office runs
on the game's own national range for a term and a calendar, exactly as an
unread state does. That will be replaced by each territory's real rules when
the territory research comes back.

The government screen now treats a territory as a territory. The scope button
and heading say "Territory" instead of "State." The Represented by list shows
the territory's Delegate to the U.S. House (for Puerto Rico, the Resident
Commissioner) in place of a congressional district, and it no longer shows a
U.S. Senate row the territory does not have. Empty branches read "No record
of Guam's legislature is kept in this save." instead of "The game has not
established Guam's legislature."

Still missing: each territory's legislature, its local governments, and the
person in the Delegate's seat. The government map also does not show the
territories yet.
