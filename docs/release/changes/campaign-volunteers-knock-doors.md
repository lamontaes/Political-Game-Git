title: Campaign volunteers now go out and knock on doors.
---

People who volunteer for a campaign, yours or a rival's, now walk its doors in the evening when their job and the rest of their day leave them time. Someone who cares more about the candidate goes out more often; someone whose job keeps them at work that evening stays home. The neighbors they reach warm to the candidate and give an answer about their vote that counts on election day, so a campaign with willing friends and family can reach far more of the town than its candidate could alone.
