title: Someone who testified on your bill catches you after the hearing
---

As a state legislator, when a committee hears your bill, one of the people who testified may stop you in the hallway afterward to tell you what the bill would do to their own life: their job, their rent, their kids' school, their doctor's bills, or just what they believe. You can thank them, ask what it would really mean for them, tell them you see it differently, or brush them off. They remember how you treated them. Witnesses who oppose a bill now speak against it at the hearing, where before they spoke for it.
