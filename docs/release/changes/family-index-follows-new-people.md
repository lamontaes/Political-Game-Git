---
id: family-index-follows-new-people
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: A game day takes about a third less time while the world fills in its candidates
---

Before, every batch of new people the world added in a day made the game read
every household in the country again to learn about families, about 445 times
in ten days. A day cost about 2.2 seconds on an ordinary life, and a month
cost 131 seconds.

Now, when a day only adds new people in households of their own, the family
reading carries forward and adds those households. Anything that touches an
existing person or household still reads everything again. A month on the same
life costs 84 seconds, and the world it ends in is identical.
