title: The President's opening address cites the country's numbers and takes its President's shape
---

The opening address now cites unemployment and three or four more of the country's numbers, from inflation, mortgage rates, wage growth and the economy's growth. Which numbers, and in what order, follow the President's own goals. A President whose first goal is about the economy opens with the numbers and cites one more for each such goal; any other opens with the occasion. Before, every address cited only unemployment and kept one shape.
