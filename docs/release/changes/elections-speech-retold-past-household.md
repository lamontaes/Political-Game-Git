title: A speech you give is retold to friends and family outside the house, not only at home
---

When you give a speech, the people who heard it used to retell it only to the family they live with. Now a listener who retells it also tells the few people they are closest to outside the house, such as relatives elsewhere, friends and coworkers. Each one decides for themselves whether to take it in, by how close they are to the person telling it, so a speech now travels past the front door.
