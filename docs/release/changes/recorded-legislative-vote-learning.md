id: recorded-legislative-vote-learning
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Recorded roll calls can inform a member's later view
---

When a named legislator takes part in a recorded vote, the save can now record
that they encountered the bill's explicit policy question and learned the
roll-call outcome. A later reflection can use their saved principles; a belief
formed that way may inform a later vote when the bill states how it answers
that question. Absent members and people merely seated in the chamber do not
learn from the roll call, and the vote already cast keeps its original reason.
