title: People have many more hairstyles and hair colors
---

People now wear 11 hairstyles for women and 15 for men instead of one each,
among them long straight hair, waves, curls, afros, box braids, locs, buns,
ponytails, pixie cuts, bobs, fades, buzz cuts, side parts and man buns. Each
comes in seven hair colors, from black to blonde, with gray and white growing
more common with age. Hair is chosen for each person on its own, separate
from skin and face. In the creator and in Appearance and wardrobe, new
arrows choose your hair and hair color.
