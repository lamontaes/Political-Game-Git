title: Groups that residents form over a stance get a name that says what they want
---

A group residents form over one stance is named from the cause its side would put on a sign ("Committee for Public Safety backs Nina Jacobs for the Laurel City Council"), so a name never says the other side's cause and two groups in one town never share a name. The story's second sentence says what the group wants, and the race is said by its body.
