title: Stiff replies to a school question, a neighborhood mention, a game invitation and a reporter are gone
---

Four fixed lines that read like a form are out of the conversation: a parent no
longer adds "What still needs doing?" after answering about unfinished school
work, a neighbor no longer asks "What do you think about going?" about a
meeting, a person turning down a quiet evening no longer says "I'd rather try
something new," and a reporter asks "What happened?" about the news instead of
"What is established, and what is still open?"
