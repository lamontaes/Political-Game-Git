section: Added
title: Every county now has an election calendar from its state's law
---

Before, only two counties had an election date. Every other county showed
no scheduled election, so nobody could plan a run for a county seat.

Now every county with a governing body takes its election date, term length
and term start from its state's statute, with DeSoto Parish and Loudon County
keeping their own published dates. A state with no county government shows
none.
