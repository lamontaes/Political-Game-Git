---
id: judicial-hearing-ballot-boundary
impact: none
---

Records a federal judicial nominee's new public hearing statement from their
own prior answer. Only Senators recorded as attending learn that statement
directly. A yes or no Senate ballot requires recorded knowledge of the
hearing. One ballot cannot fill a seat, and a roll call with no yes or no
votes remains unresolved. A confirmed nominee who held a Congress seat
leaves it through the existing vacancy route.

This is a simulation boundary for later play. The hearing interface,
individual Senator reasons, complete roll call and player-visible
confirmation remain pending.
