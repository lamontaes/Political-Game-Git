title: A lost right or eligibility now counts with the people who lose it
---

When a law takes away a right or an eligibility without costing money, the
people it reaches now feel it. They weigh it like losing a tenth of a month's
pay. That size is a placeholder until research measures it. They hold it against the officials who voted for the
law, and six neighbors who lose it can band together against the law.
