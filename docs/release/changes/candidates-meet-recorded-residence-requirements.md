title: Fictional legislative candidates meet the recorded residence requirement
---

Fictional legislative candidates now receive the residence duration required
by the chamber's sourced rule instead of a random number of years. Calendar
months preserve month-based requirements and leap-year dates. When no duration
has been read, the background records that requirement as unknown without
inventing prior years. Existing candidates and saved residence history remain
on record.
