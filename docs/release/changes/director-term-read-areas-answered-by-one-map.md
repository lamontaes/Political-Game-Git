title: A governor's address after a term that hurt pay or prices can open on it, and asks on a question about it.
---

A governor's State of the State after a term where pay or prices slid furthest now opens on that, and the paragraph that states it asks about a policy that bears on it, such as the minimum wage or the sales tax on groceries. Before, only trouble in rent, crime, schools, health, roads or the environment could open a hurt address.
