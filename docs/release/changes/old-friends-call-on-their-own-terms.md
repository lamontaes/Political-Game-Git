title: Whom you meet next, and when an old friend calls, come from the people, not chance
---

An old friend who gets back in touch no longer rings on a day picked by
chance. They ring on a day they are not at work. How long they leave it
follows how warm they are toward you, so a close friend calls sooner. How far
ahead they suggest meeting follows how much of a planner they are.

Whether they call at all is still their own decision, weighed from their
temperament and their own goals.

Meeting somebody new is no longer drawn either. The next person you meet is
the one who turns up in the most of the places you share, such as the same
job and the same congregation, and then the one nearest your age.
