title: A Politics hub, and a person card that opens beside the person
---

Politics now opens as one hub with Your office, Campaigns, Government, Parties
and Issues and budget. Government shows who governs where you are right now,
at the local, state and federal level, split into legislative, executive and
judicial branches, and says plainly when the game has no record of a branch.
You can switch to your home when you are elsewhere, and looking costs no time.
Clicking someone in the room now opens their card right beside them, kept
inside the window and clear of the controls at the bottom; a card opened from a
list keeps its usual place. Your own card no longer talks about when you last
spoke to yourself.
