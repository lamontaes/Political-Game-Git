section: Changed
title: A governor's, a legislature's and the President's races and addresses now read how their state's and the country's jobs and prices went.
---

Before, only the town's own record was read, so a state or federal race never leaned on how its term went. Now a state's jobs and prices are read from its own monthly records, and the country's from its real months, history included.
