title: A colleague who asks for your vote at the capitol is shown at the capitol, not in your kitchen
---

Scenes set at a chamber or an office used to show your home. A vote asked in the chamber is now pictured in your body's own chamber. A talk at the office is pictured where your post works, such as a governor's office or a legislator's capitol hallway, or in your body's chamber when the post has no office of its own.
