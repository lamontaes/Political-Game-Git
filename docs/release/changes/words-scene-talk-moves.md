title: A reporter's questions and your answers are about the bill itself, your vote and why.
---

A reporter's call after a vote is said as moves about the bill's records instead of stock lines: your answers name the bill, what it is about, the side your vote took and the reason that weighed most on it (your views, the town, a section you won, your party or your last vote); putting off an answer says what comes next for the bill; the reporter's follow-up and close name the bill and the paper. A member who voted no is never offered "I'm voting for it". The words vary by reporter and by your bearing.
