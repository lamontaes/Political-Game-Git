title: Your family reacts when you file for office, and when the votes are counted
---

The day you file to run for office, someone at home is waiting for you: a
partner who asks "So you're really doing this?", a parent who wants to know
why you didn't tell them. You answer from a set of replies, and each one takes
the talk somewhere different. The night your race is counted, your household
meets you with the win or the loss. These scenes come from the filing and the
count themselves, so they happen to anyone who runs.
