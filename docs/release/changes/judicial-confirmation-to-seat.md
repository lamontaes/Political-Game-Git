title: Carry a federal judicial nomination from Senate vote to the seat
---

After a reported nomination reaches the Executive Calendar, a Senate floor sitting records each Senator's own disposition. A confirmed nominee takes the vacant court seat only after a separately recorded Presidential commission, and the court becomes their active workplace. Rejection leaves the seat vacant. Government shows the saved stages, and News carries the public nomination, hearing, committee decision, floor result, and commission. NPC same-day commission timing is a Wave 2 game placeholder. Pay for the new confirmed judicial role is not yet settled.
