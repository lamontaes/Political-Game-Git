title: Carry a federal judicial nomination from Senate vote to the seat
---

After a reported nomination reaches the Executive Calendar, a Senate floor sitting records each Senator's own disposition. A confirmed nominee takes the vacant court seat only after a separately recorded Presidential commission, and the court becomes their active workplace. Rejection leaves the seat vacant. Government shows the saved stages, and News carries the public nomination, hearing, committee decision, floor result, and commission. NPC same-day commission timing is a Wave 2 game placeholder. Pay for the new confirmed judicial role is not yet settled.

If a recorded Senate floor sitting cannot resolve the nomination, the game schedules a later Congress sitting and records fresh attendance and ballots there. Earlier ballots remain in history but do not count toward the later sitting. The later date is a PLACEHOLDER(wave2) game calendar choice.

When the ordinary mortality path records a federal district or appellate judge's death, it ends that judge's court tenure and schedules an NPC President's consideration of the real vacancy. Any nomination still requires the candidate's own saved response. The next-day review and bounded seven-day retry are PLACEHOLDER(wave2) game timing; a vacant Presidency, unresolved candidate answers, and other vacancy causes can still leave the seat open.
