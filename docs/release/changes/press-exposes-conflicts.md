title: The local press now reports an official who votes a contract to their own family's company
---

When a council or board awards a public contract to a firm that one of its members owns, or that their relative or housemate owns, and that member's own vote helped it win, the award now keeps a record of the conflict. At the next weekly news meeting the town's reporters can read it: each decides whether the paper trail is worth digging into, and the paper's owner and publisher decide whether it runs. A story that runs puts the official's name in print, lowers readers' opinion of them, and gives the official a scandal call from the reporter. Reporters never cover their own relatives or the firm's owner.
