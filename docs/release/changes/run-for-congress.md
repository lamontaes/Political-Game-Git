title: Run for the U.S. House or Senate
---

Campaigns now offers a seat in Congress from the state a character lives in:
every House district in the state, since a Representative need only live in
the state, and whichever Senate seat is elected next. Filing opens a campaign
in the seat's own November election. The winner sees the transition before
January 3, takes the seat that day, and appears as its member in the Congress
overview. Their office names the seat and the term.

The background model that fills Congress no longer decides a seat the player
is running for, and a player who holds a seat keeps it only by filing again
and winning. Winning creates no separate legislature job. Floor votes,
committees and a member's staff are not playable yet, and the office says so.
