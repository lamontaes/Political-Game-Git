section: Fixed
title: Preserve stated birthdays and opening family choices
---

Custom childhood starts now let you choose one parent, two parents, or a
guardian. Unspecified opening families use the game's recorded family spread,
with the estimate and its source retained in the save. Continuing the creator
preserves your birthday fields: choose both month and day, or leave both blank
to begin from an age without drawing missing birthday fields.
