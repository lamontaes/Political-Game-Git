In the separate core prototype, every recorded relative of a resident, including those living in the same home, now names that relative's own record rather than only their name, so each family fact a person shows can be followed to the person behind it. A new check confirms, in randomly drawn places, that an 18-year-old's parents and siblings follow the family rules, that their schools are real schools from the place's records, and that every fact about them has a record behind it. Players see no change: the prototype is not in the playable build.
