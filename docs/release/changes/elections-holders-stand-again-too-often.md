title: Local officeholders who hold a job weigh the time it takes when deciding to run again, so more seats open up.
---

A council member or county officer with a full-time job now weighs the hours it takes before standing for another term, as anyone thinking of running does. About three in four holders run again, near the real share, so more seats come open for newcomers.
