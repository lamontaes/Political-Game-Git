id: dossier-laws-they-wrote
impact: minor
section: Added
title: A lawmaker's record lists the laws they wrote and what each is doing
---

A person's full record now has a "Laws they wrote" section for anyone who
sponsored a bill that became law. It lists their five most recent laws, newest
first, with the date each became law. Under each law it says what the law is
doing to the conditions the world keeps: how far it moved a condition, the
month a delayed effect starts, or that a later law replaced it. Each title
opens the law's own page.
