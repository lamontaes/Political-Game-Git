id: budget-lines-round-once-exactly
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Budget lines carried into a new year are no longer a cent off.
---

Each spending line a government carries into a new year is last year's amount times how far its own costs moved, and it is now rounded once, to the nearest cent of the exact result. Before, the steps were rounded along the way, so a federal line of a hundred trillion cents could land a cent away from what the rule gives.
