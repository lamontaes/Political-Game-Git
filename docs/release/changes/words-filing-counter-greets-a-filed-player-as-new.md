title: Back at the filing counter, the clerk no longer greets you as new or offers the race you already filed in
---

When you come back to the filing counter after filing, the race you are already running in is not offered again. If all that is left is your petition, the clerk tells you how many of the signatures you need are in and when they are due, and you can only leave. If nothing is left to do there, the counter has nothing to play. The filing office is called what people call it, the same at the counter and in Travel: "County board of voter registration and elections", never "… of the candidate's home county".
