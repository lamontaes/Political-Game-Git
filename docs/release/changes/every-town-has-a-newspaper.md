title: Every town has its own local press, every state has a newsroom from the start, and Puerto Rico's press is its own.
---

A town used to get a local outlet only if the game had its city government on
record, so San Juan, Ponce, Louisville and many small places had none at all,
and Puerto Rico had no island newsroom either. Now every town a character lives
in has local journalism, and their state's or territory's politics is covered
from the first day, not only once they hold office there. Taking a legislative
seat or an office in another state brings that state's newsroom too.

Newsrooms now differ. A town might have a weekly paper, a small digital
outlet, a community radio station or, less often, a daily; a state might have a
public-radio capitol desk, a big daily's bureau or a lean politics newsletter.
The kind of outlet decides its staff, how often it publishes and how many
stories it can chase at once. Puerto Rico's outlets carry Spanish mastheads,
and the District of Columbia's newsroom is named for the District.
