title: A new world opens with a year behind it
---

Laws in force carry when and by whom they were passed, every officeholder won a prior election and has a voting record that voters weigh, the news holds the year before day one, everyone knows what the public knows about the president, vice president, governor, members of Congress and mayor, and each household has its own history: how the parents met, where the family came from and moments its members remember.
