id: every-door-conversation-counts
section: Changed
title: Every conversation on the doors counts toward support
impact: minor
---

Every neighbor a candidate talks with on the doors now moves support by the measured effect of a candidate's own visit. What was said moves that a little: a neighbor who took to the candidate carries a little more of it, one who took against them a little less, and one who only heard the candidate out carries it as measured. The two shares are the range the same field experiment found across its two kinds of message. A cool neighbor no longer takes support away.
