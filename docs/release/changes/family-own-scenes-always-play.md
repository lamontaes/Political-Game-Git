title: Your own first date, a proposal to you and the birth of your child now always play as scenes
---

Your first date with someone, the moment your partner proposes, and the birth of your own child used to slip into the Journal when bigger news filled your year. They now stop the day and play as scenes, so you can answer a proposal yes or no.
