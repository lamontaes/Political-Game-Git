title: A town business's customers from out of town now rise and fall with its own industry in its state and the nation
---

Most of what a small town's businesses sell goes to buyers from outside the town. Before, all of those sales moved together by one number for the whole town, so a builder and a factory next door always gained or lost the same share. Now each business's outside customers follow its own industry: the part that sells close to home follows that industry's sales in the town's state, and the part sold across state lines follows the industry nationwide. A building boom in your state lifts the contractors without lifting the diner, and dev mode's Trace on a business's outside sales names the industry and the state it followed.
