title: Two research questions ask for the sizes behind D.C. statehood funds and parks funding.
---

The game cannot yet move federal money when D.C. becomes a state, or a parks budget when a place dedicates revenue to parks, because no sized source exists for either. Two questions in the research queue now ask for them: the dollar changes to D.C.'s federal funds and budget, and which places dedicate revenue to parks, how much it yields and what it does to park access.
