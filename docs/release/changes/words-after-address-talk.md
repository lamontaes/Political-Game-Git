title: After the address, the people you live with talk about it
---

After the President's address, the people you live with say what they made of it in their own voice, with eleven new openers for those who liked it and those who didn't. Every answer you give is met by a line written for that answer: a disagreement gets "Fair enough. Ask me again in a year.", not "Suit yourself." Your replies name the President instead of saying "they", and "what it means here" is now "Washington's a long way from here, though."
