title: People around town watched the President's address too, and can bring it up.
---

On the evening of the President's address, the households in town that follow the news most closely and were home at the hour watch it, about one resident in eleven, as many as watch an inauguration across the country. People who watched it tell the people they spend time with, at work, at home and out in town, so by the end of the week many in town have heard of it. A neighbor or coworker who watched it or heard of it can bring it up with the player, and someone who watched it still says they watched it once the paper runs the story.
