title: People around town watched the President's address too, and can bring it up.
---

On the evening of the President's address, the households in town that follow the news most closely and were home at the hour watch it, about one resident in eleven, as many as watch an inauguration across the country. A neighbor or coworker who watched it can bring it up with the player the next day, and a person met later in the week who watched it remembers it too.
