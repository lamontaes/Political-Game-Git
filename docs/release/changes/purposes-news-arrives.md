title: When someone in your household brings you news of a death or a relative's new or ended relationship, they ask you for something.
---

A death, a relative's new partner or a break-up now reaches you from a member of your household who asks you for something it brings: to call the family, to come over, to meet the new partner, or to sit with the one who is left. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
