title: Teenagers see the town's part-time jobs from sixteen
---

Employers already took applicants from sixteen, but the town's job listings
never reached a life still in its growing-up years, so a sixteen- or
seventeen-year-old saw no jobs until eighteen. From sixteen the listings now
arrive as time passes, limited to ordinary part-time hourly work. Full-time and
salaried jobs still wait for eighteen, and a fifteen-year-old sees none.
