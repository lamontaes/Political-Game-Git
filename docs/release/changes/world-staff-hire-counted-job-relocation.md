title: A household met again after returning to the counts no longer stops the year
---

A household the player has lost touch with returns to the town's counts. If one of its members had a serious illness due and died after the household returned, meeting the household again (to hire a staffer from it, or when it inherited) stopped the year. Now a person back in the counts is counted: the counts decide their illness and death, their record follows, and meeting the household again brings back only its living members.
