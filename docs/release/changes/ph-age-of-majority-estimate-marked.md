section: Changed
title: The grown-up presentation age is marked as an estimate from most states' majority age
---

Before, the age from which a person is no longer described as somebody's
dependent was labeled a placeholder. Now it is marked an estimate of 18, the age
of majority in most states; nothing about when anyone legally becomes an adult
changes.
