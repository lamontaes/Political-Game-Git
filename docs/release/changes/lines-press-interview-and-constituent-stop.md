title: A reporter's questions and a resident's stop are back, in new words.
---

In office, a reporter asks about the bill or the act you were part of, and a resident stops you about a law or a story that reached them. Their words are new and name what happened: the bill, the story and the paper that ran it, the law and whether it is in force yet.
