section: Changed
title: Executive desks show recorded veto and override results
---

Executive desks distinguish expected override support from recorded roll calls and outcomes. Player item-veto selections use the existing signature and enacted-law writers, reject stale or ineligible choices before recording a decision, and retain the selected items in the saved decision. Council desks require their own recorded item-veto grant. Additive bill-scene data is available for the central scene consumer.
