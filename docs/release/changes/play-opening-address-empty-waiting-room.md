title: The opening's cutaways show the town's own people, not empty rooms
---

When the President names the jobs or prices, the cutaway to a factory, grocery or workforce office now shows people from your town: the workers at an organization of the town whose own picture it is, or adults in town with no job. A cutaway with nobody to show stays on the address instead.
