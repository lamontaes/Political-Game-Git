title: Where cannabis sales are legal, slightly fewer teens use it
---

Each teenager now has a monthly record of whether they used cannabis, starting from the national share of 12- to 17-year-olds and shifted by how dependable they are. Where the state allows legal sales, the share is a little lower, by the size national surveys of teens measured. This runs in the background; nothing new is asked of the player.
