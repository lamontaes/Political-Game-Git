title: Your state's legislature is full of real people from the first day
---

A new life now opens with every seat in its home state's legislature held
by a person: a name, an age, a district where the state's map names one, a
party and the years they have served. Before, only Congress had members; a
state chamber was empty until somebody won a campaign. A chamber takes its
size from the state's rules where the game has them and otherwise one
member per legislative district. Party balance follows the state's own
politics, so Illinois opens mostly Democratic and Missouri mostly
Republican. A state whose legislature the game cannot yet describe seats no
one rather than borrowing another state's.
