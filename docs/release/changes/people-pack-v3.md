title: New people, drawn only where a room has a place for them
---

Everyone in the game is now one of the new painted people, each with their own face, hair and body. Everyday clothes come in the colors each person can wear, and suits, pantsuits and blazers are painted whole. People are drawn only in rooms painted with a place for each of them (the living room at night, the Oval Office, the White House colonnade and the train depot so far), seated or standing in the room's own pose. Rooms without those places show the room alone for now. Faces in the talk box, the person card, the People and government lists, and on the title's Continue are the person's own painted head. In the character creator you choose one of the six people, everyday clothes or one of their suits, and the color of each piece of everyday clothing.
