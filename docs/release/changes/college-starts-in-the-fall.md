title: College applications get an answer after a wait, and classes start in the fall
---

Applying to a college used to offer a place on the spot, and accepting it started classes that same day, even for a high-school senior in March, who then read that they were enrolled in two programs. Now a college answers an application after a wait, and an accepted place starts with the fall term once high school is over. Until then the place is not counted as a program you are enrolled in and no tuition is due. A senior can apply from the start of their last year of high school. The college screen no longer says where its listings came from.
