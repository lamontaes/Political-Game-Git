After a federal judicial referral, Senators can organize Judiciary through recorded attendance and votes. An adopted resolution appoints members and chair for that Congress. The chair can give three days' notice of business consideration; the judiciary panel shows the saved result.

A fictional game profile proposes the slate and chair. Each NPC decides whether to attend; saved conflicts prevent participation, and only recorded attendees ballot. A controlled Senator's uncast choice, failed quorum, rejected resolution, or missing authority cannot produce a chair. The later consideration notice creates no hearing, report, floor ballot, confirmation, or filled seat.
