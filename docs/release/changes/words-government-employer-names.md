title: A government job is said as people say it
---

A card read "Works as planning director at COUNTY OF MCDOWELL treasury." It now reads "Works as a planning director at McDowell County." The same goes for a city ("at the City of Woonsocket"), every job takes its article ("a teacher"), and work out of town is never said as an employer or a place.
