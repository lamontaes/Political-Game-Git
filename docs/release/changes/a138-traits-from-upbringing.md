---
id: a138-traits-from-upbringing
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: People's temperaments and everyday preferences now come from how they were raised
---

The notable qualities a person starts with, their five core leanings and their
everyday preferences (how they talk with people, how they spend free time, what
they want for themselves) now come from their upbringing: the family's money,
the care they had, school and a first job. Two people raised the same way start
out the same. Where an upbringing leans neither way, the person starts in the
middle instead of at a random end. People already in a saved game keep what
they have.
