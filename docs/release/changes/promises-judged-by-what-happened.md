title: A promise someone brings up again is no longer counted as kept
---

When a neighbor brought up something you had promised, the game counted the
promise as kept, whatever you had done. It now stays open until something on
record answers it. Saying you will go to the posted public meeting is kept when
you are there and broken when the meeting ends without you, and a canceled
meeting leaves nothing to keep. A promise to vote is judged by the roll call.
The neighbor who heard you weighs a broken promise when deciding whether to
raise it with you, and has less reason to raise one you kept.

Help between people is now recorded as a favor: who helped whom, what was
done, and how much it mattered. How much the person helped still feels they
owe fades with time, faster for small favors and for people who let things
slip, while a giver who wanted something back expects more, and a favor
returned settles it. No list of who owes whom is shown anywhere.
