id: time-apart-for-a-reason-is-not-neglect
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Time apart for a known reason no longer reads as drifting apart
---

A friend who was in jail, who moved to another state, who was grieving a death
in the family, or who had just started a new school or job is no longer read
as someone you neglected. Those stretches do not count toward the two of you
drifting apart, and a friendship now fades a little more with each unexplained
week rather than all at once.
