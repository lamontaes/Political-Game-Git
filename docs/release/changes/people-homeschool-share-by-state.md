section: Changed
title: Private-school quotas account for state homeschool shares
---

A town's private-school enrollment quota now uses its state's private-school share times one minus the Census Household Pulse homeschool share. States with more homeschooling therefore open with a smaller private-tuition quota. The existing cash-per-child ranking selects the families that pay.
