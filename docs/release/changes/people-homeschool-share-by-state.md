section: Changed
title: Homeschooling families no longer pay private tuition in the opening
---

A town's families who homeschool are now counted out before the private school's share is figured. The share comes from the Census Bureau's Household Pulse survey for the town's state, so a state where more families homeschool opens with a little less private tuition.
