section: Changed
title: Saved clemency petitions carry their own review dates
---

Saved clemency petitions schedule review through the existing world clock using existing board waits, advisory deadlines and sentence-end dates. A request whose sentence has ended can close even when no executive is seated. Undecided executives receive no new retry timer. The weekly caller remains until its replacement is integrated and proven.
