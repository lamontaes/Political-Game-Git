title: A business owner no longer loses Medicaid for working no hours
---

The Medicaid work requirement read a person's hours only from their job records, so someone who owns and runs a business, such as a town newspaper's owner, counted as working no hours and lost coverage. An owner's hours at their own business now count, at the average for the self-employed.
