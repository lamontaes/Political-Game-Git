title: The campaign trail's tiles show the place each act happens
---

On the campaign trail, each thing you can do today shows a picture of where it happens: a street of doors, the hall, a newsroom, the campaign office, a supporter's living room. The tiles were black boxes. An act you cannot do today says why under "Not today".
