---
id: local-budgets-must-balance
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Counties and cities balance their budgets instead of borrowing every deficit
---

Before, no county or city read a rule on whether its budget must balance,
so every one of them borrowed whatever it fell short.

Now a county or city whose own ordinances don't answer follows the most
common real rule: it must pass a balanced budget. When collections fall
short it cuts programs, draws its reserve, and cuts the next budget to fit,
as a state under a balanced-budget law does. The rule is marked as estimated
from what most local governments must do, until each state's rule for its
local governments is read.
