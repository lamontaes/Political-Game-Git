---
id: mr-9b-calendar-entry-values
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The calendar's entries show labeled values, not sentences
---

A calendar entry lists its kind, who is in charge, who it came through and where it is. A planned activity shows Starts and Length. The written lines are gone: "You might go.", "Starts Tuesday ... and takes 1 hour, 15 minutes.", the summary under each entry, and the empty-calendar notes.
