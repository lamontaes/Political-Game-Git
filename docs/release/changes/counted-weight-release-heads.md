title: In a big city, a business no longer swells past its customers when you meet its neighbors.
---

When you meet someone in a large city, the households their record stood for are spread over the rest of the town without carrying jobs to employers that never hired. A business's staff now changes only when it hires, lays off or someone leaves, so city businesses stop closing under a payroll they never chose.
