title: Who you are now says how you grew up and what happened around you
---

The Who you are screen now has a short "How you grew up" section: what money
was like at home, whether home stayed put, how the adults who raised you were,
and what school was like, with whatever your temperament was left with. Below
it, "Around you this past year" lists births, layoffs, moves and deaths in your
town and household that the world has recorded.
