The room rests quietly with a small corner control for who, when and where,
plus Day and Week. The menu opens on click into Calendar, People, Politics,
News, Journal, Personal, Travel, Options, Save and Quit, and Quit asks before
leaving an unsaved life. Anybody you click — in the room, on the People web,
in a list or a pin — opens one card on the right with their face, what they
are to you, what you actually know, who they are connected to, and the real
actions. Conversation sits at the bottom centre with the faces along the top,
the speaker lit, narration in italics and one line at a time; browsing
elsewhere keeps the exchange and offers a way back. The Calendar opens on
today and what is next, keeps History separate, and carries the interruption
checklist that decides where a day or week skip stops.
