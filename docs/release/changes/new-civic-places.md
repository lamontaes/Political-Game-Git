title: Twelve new places to be in the world
---

The world has twelve new painted places: a polling place set up in a school gym, a school board meeting room, the motor vehicle office, a local newsroom, a courthouse hallway, a hotel ballroom set for a fundraiser, the public library, a police station lobby, a fire station bay, a county party office, a small-town diner and a neighborhood barbershop. Most come in morning, midday, night and rain light. They appear as the game gives people reasons to go there.
