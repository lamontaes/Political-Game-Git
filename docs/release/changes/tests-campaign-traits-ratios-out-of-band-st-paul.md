title: Personality moves who answers a pollster and who retells a campaign story by the researched amount
---

A voter's personality moved their answering a pollster and their retelling a campaign story about four to twenty times too far in every town, because the table of how acts pull on traits had been rebuilt since the weights were set. Both weights are set again from the current table, and a retelling's second-hand share is set from the new average reach.
