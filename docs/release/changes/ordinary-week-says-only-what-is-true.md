---
id: ordinary-week-says-only-what-is-true
impact: patch
title: A new life's first week no longer hands out errands that do not exist
section: Fixed
---

Every new life opened with the same three errands: groceries, a clothing
fitting and a visit to fix a kitchen cupboard hinge, followed by "Neither
appointment has a time yet", which counts two of three. The fitting and the
hinge were never anything in the world. The item now asks only for the week's
groceries, which is a trip the player can actually make.

The public-meeting item said "The agenda is posted" without saying which
meeting. It now says what is true of it in any life: a public meeting is on the
local calendar at 6:30 in the evening, its agenda is posted, anyone may go and
nobody has asked you to.

The journeys to that meeting and to a party chapter's community room described
themselves as "game-authored" and explained that no fare is charged. They now
just say it takes about twenty minutes to get there.

Lives already saved keep the wording they were written with.
