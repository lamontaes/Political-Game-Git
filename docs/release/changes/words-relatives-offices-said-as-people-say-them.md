title: A relative who holds an office is told the way people say it
---

In the first Journal note, a relative on a council or board "sits on the Woonsocket City Council", another officeholder "serves as county clerk for the County of Nuckolls", and a job they hold beside it is told in the same sentence. A job title takes "a" or "an" by its sound: "a union representative".
