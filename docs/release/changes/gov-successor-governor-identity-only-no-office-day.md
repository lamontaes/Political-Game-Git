title: A governor who takes office mid-year starts the job the next office day
---

When a governor left office during the year, the successor could sit in the seat as a name only until some other part of the game happened to look at the seat: weeks, or in Guam five months, with no clemency petitions taken up. Now the successor becomes a full person on the governor's next office day and takes up the weekly petitions from then on.
