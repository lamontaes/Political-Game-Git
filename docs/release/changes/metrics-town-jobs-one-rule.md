title: The town's jobless, its rate and the Metrics tab now agree
---

The Metrics tab could say more people were looking for work than the unemployment rate counted, because people who already had a job and were looking for a better one were counted as jobless on one screen and not on the other. The number of residents at work, the number out of work and the rate now come from one count, so every screen and the paper show the same figures.
