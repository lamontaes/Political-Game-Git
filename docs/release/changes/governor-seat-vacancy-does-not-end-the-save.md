title: A governor's race opening on an empty seat no longer ends the game.
---

When a sitting governor died or left office and the next race opened with
nobody in the seat, the game stopped with an error and the life could not
continue. The race now opens normally against the state whose office it is,
and the record says the seat was vacant.
