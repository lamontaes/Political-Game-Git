title: Money reads as dollars on the campaign screen
---

The campaign screen used to say "The committee has USD 0.00" and then, a line
later, "Your committee has $0.00." Every campaign line now writes money the
second way, with a dollar sign and cents, and so does the summary after a
routine week ("Received $72.00"). That summary also gives the day your shift
pay is due as a date you would say out loud, not a code like 2026-05-09.
