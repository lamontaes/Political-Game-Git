id: town-births-by-age
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Babies are born in your town about as often as in real life
---

Before, a town saw about one birth in five years, and only to a couple
living together.

Now any woman in town from 15 to 49 can have a baby, at the rate real
women of her age do. Married women are the likeliest; a single mother is
possible. A mother with a newborn, or with three children already, is less
likely to have another soon, and nobody has two babies within about 15
months. Over five years, Columbus, Ohio, now sees several births instead of
one.
