title: A House campaign shows its own race and its own district
---

The campaign screen's list of what the other campaigns have done in public
now shows only the rivals in the race you are running. Someone who ran for
city council and then for the U.S. House saw the council opponent's old
advertising filling the list while the House opponent was missing. An earlier
race's rivals stay with that race; if nothing has been heard yet about the
current rivals, the list says so.

A U.S. House campaign is now placed in its congressional district, such as
Washington's 7th Congressional District, rather than across the whole state.
The weekly plan offers the district, and field shifts and call sessions are
held there. Statewide races, including the U.S. Senate, still campaign across
the state.
