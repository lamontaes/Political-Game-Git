title: Senate nominations use the shared member vote decision
---

Chief and associate justice confirmations carry their actual saved nomination
into the shared chamber vote decision. Senators keep their existing reasons;
the recorded roll preserves present without voting and explicit absence.
A roll with no yes or no votes keeps the nomination pending and leaves the
nominee's current office intact. Bills retain their existing question identity.
