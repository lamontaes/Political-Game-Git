title: A township head's address says "Green Township" and "the Green Township Board"
---

A township head's address used to say the place as its legal listing wrote it, "Charter Township of Allendale", and greet a board called "Charter Township of Allendale Township Board". The address now says the place the way people name it, "Allendale Township", and its board as "the Allendale Township Board". One rule says every government this way: a county, parish, borough or township after its place, a legal form such as "Charter" dropped, and a town, city or village by its name alone.
