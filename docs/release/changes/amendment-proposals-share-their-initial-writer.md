title: Automatic amendment proposals share their initial writer
---

State amendments, federal reform proposals, and Article V proposals now share
their initial proposal setup. Each route keeps its supplied wording,
designation, constitutional change, deadline, and convention identity. An
explicit text version or operative date remains part of the saved proposal.
The existing producer still refuses invalid dates, sponsors, and ordinary
bill links. Chamber votes, ratification, and replay guards keep their existing
rules.
