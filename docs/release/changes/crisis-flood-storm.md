title: Floods and severe storms now follow a real recovery path.
---

When a flood or severe storm strikes, it damages the homes and workplaces the
world actually knows about, and the people living there can be hurt. Local
responders act first. The governor then decides whether to ask the President
for a federal disaster declaration, and the President can approve or deny it.
Rebuilding takes weeks and goes faster with federal help, but a declaration
never repairs anything on the spot, and a denied request does not make the
disaster disappear. If you hold one of those offices, the decision is yours.
