title: The Journal says which month things happened in
---

Each year in the Journal now reads in order with its months: "In March, you
started at the plant. In July, you joined the county party." A sentence that
already says its own date is left alone, and a long year breaks into
paragraphs where the month changes. Nothing is added that the record does not
hold; the storytelling voice itself is waiting on a style ruling.
