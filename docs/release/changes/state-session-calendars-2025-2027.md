section: Changed
title: State legislatures now meet in 2025, 2026 and 2027
---

Every state legislature, the D.C. Council and the five territories' legislatures
follow one calendar of regular sessions for 2025 through 2027. State
legislatures now meet in 2025, and Montana, Nevada, North Dakota and Texas, which
meet in odd years, hold their sessions in 2025 and 2027 and skip 2026.
