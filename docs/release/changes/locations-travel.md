id: locations-travel
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Travel lists only the places you have a reason to go.
---

Travel no longer lists every business in town. It lists where you, your household and your family work, your school while someone at home is of school age, places where people you know work, places where you can do something right now, and your town's and county's offices once you are 18. While you hold an office, the state's offices join the list.
