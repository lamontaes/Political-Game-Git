id: opening-savings-from-scf-by-income
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A new adult's opening savings follow what families at their pay really keep
---

A working adult's opening bank balance now comes from the Federal Reserve's
Survey of Consumer Finances 2022: the median that families at their income keep
in checking, savings and money market accounts. A person earning $33,600 a year
opens with about $1,800; one earning $115,000 with about $15,600. Before, each
adult drew a savings habit between 3% and 13% of pay by chance and multiplied
it by their years of work.
