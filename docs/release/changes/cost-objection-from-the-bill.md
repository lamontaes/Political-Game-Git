id: cost-objection-from-the-bill
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A legislator objecting to cost talks about the bill in front of them
---

Before, a legislator who balked at what a bill cost could mention things that
were never true in your game, such as earlier votes against "smaller" bills or
money that would be "baked in" by a third year.

Now an objection to cost speaks only from the bill itself: its number, what it
commits as it now reads, the person being spoken to and, for a member who
answers to a district, the place they represent. A careful spender asks where
the offset is; a district member asks how much of the money reaches home.
