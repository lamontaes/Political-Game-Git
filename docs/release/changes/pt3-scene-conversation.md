section: Improved
title: Talking to somebody now happens in one conversation box in the room.
---

Choosing a person — in the scene, from their record, or from People — opens
the same conversation box over the room. It shows who you are talking to and
who they are to you, their face when the game has one, what the conversation
is about, what you just said and what they said back, and what you can say
next, all without a scrollbar. Earlier exchanges page back inside the same
box, and Back returns you to the scene. Turning to somebody else keeps the
conversation going and says whether they heard what was just said, instead of
starting over with a greeting. Listen is offered only while somebody still has
something to add. Conversations take the same time they always did.
