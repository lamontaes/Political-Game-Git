title: A city or county council can pass a property tax, and the bills land on named households
---

A city or county council can now take up a property, sales, payroll or
corporate income tax, if the state lets that level of government levy it. A
passed property tax takes effect on its own date, then assesses every
household's home or rent once a year and sends each bill to a named person.
Where a household's rent is not recorded, the bill starts from the average
rent for a home its size and says so.
