title: A child who moves now starts school in the new place
---

When a family moves, each child who was in grade school starts at a school
in the new place, in the same grade. They start right away during the school
year, or on the first day of the next one over the summer. If the game knows
of no school there for that grade, the child's childhood record says so,
and the child is not placed at a made-up school. Families with children in
school are also slower to move in the middle of the year. The further a move
would land from a school break, the more the school year weighs against it.
