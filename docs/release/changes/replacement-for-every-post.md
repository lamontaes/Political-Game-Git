title: The presidency and party chairs are filled the day they fall vacant
---

When a president dies or leaves office, the vice president succeeds that day; with no vice president able, the Speaker of the House, then the president pro tempore of the Senate, then the cabinet in the order the law sets, acts as president. The president names a new vice president, whom both houses confirm, and names a cabinet the Senate confirms. When a party's chair dies, moves away or leaves the party, its members choose a new chair the same day. Each change is reported as news.
