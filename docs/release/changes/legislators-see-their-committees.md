title: Members of Congress see the committees they sit on
---

A member of Congress's office card now names the committees they sit on, for
example "You sit on the Committee on Agriculture and the Committee on Armed
Services." These are the same committees whose votes count the member when a
bill comes out of committee.

A state legislator's office record says why they have no committee seat. Either
the chamber has no committees in the game yet, or its seats went to the members
who were already serving. It no longer says "not appointed" when the chamber
has no committees at all.
