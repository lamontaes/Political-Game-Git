id: team8-a154-recorded-reporting-capacity
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: Reporting assignments reserve their estimated effort against actual staff hours
---

Newsrooms use their reporters' recorded job hours and open work reservations
instead of a fixed small, standard, or major story limit. Assignments save an
estimated reporting task, and publication waits for its actual work progress.
Effort and experience factors are labeled CTO-admitted estimates, not measured
durations for an individual story.
