title: A state's abortion restriction names the same law everywhere it reaches someone.
---

When a state restricts abortion, the women it reaches are now recorded under the state law that governs, the same law the births engine cites for what the restriction cost a household, instead of a town or federal row that does not govern the question. A law's record, who it reached and its journal entry now name one law.
