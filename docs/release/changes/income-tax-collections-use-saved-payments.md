title: New income taxes collect from recorded pay
---

Adopting an income tax no longer creates a revenue level from other states'
collections per resident. The existing adopted-term paycheck writer assesses
actual recorded wages and withholds payment; the government's existing cash
reader carries completed receipts into its monthly budget. Missing accounts or
payments do not become forecast cash, and Save/Continue keeps the same receipts.
