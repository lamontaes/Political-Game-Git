section: Changed
title: A governor is not asked about a disaster that damaged nothing
---

A played governor is no longer asked whether to request federal disaster help
after a minor or moderate disaster that damaged no home or building and hurt no
one. The governor's office records that no request was made, and it does not
count for or against the governor. A major or catastrophic disaster still asks.
This is a placeholder until research on when real governors ask comes back.
