title: Savings in the wider population spread the way real savings do, not into a few very rich households.
---

How much each family in the wider population saves was drawn by a method that gave a handful of families far more than real savings spread would, so most families in a group held little and went looking for work on the second day. Families' savings now spread as the survey's do, and fewer people are out of work at the start for want of money.
