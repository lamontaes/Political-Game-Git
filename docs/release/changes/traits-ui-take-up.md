section: Changed
title: About two in three laid-off workers file for unemployment
---

A worker laid off in town now files for unemployment depending on how much of their pay the benefit would replace, how hard the layoff hit them and their temperament, so about two in three file, as in the country, instead of nearly everyone. A worker whose benefit is capped at the state's weekly maximum files less often.
