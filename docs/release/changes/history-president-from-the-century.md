title: Every world now has its own history since 1925, and its President is the one that history elected.
---

A new world runs every presidential election from 1928 to 2024 before you arrive. The President you meet on the first day won the last of them, so a world may open with either party in the White House, after a long run of one party or after many changes. A new world can still start from today's real record instead.
