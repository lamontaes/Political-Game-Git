section: Changed
title: An independent ward commission law takes a town's ward map from its council
---

A town, or a state over its towns, that adopts an independent ward commission now has its council wards redrawn by the commission at the town's next yearly review, at equal population and without regard to where members live, so two sitting members can land in one ward. A repeal hands the next census-year redraw back to the council.
