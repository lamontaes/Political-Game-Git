title: Crime, courts and gun laws now reach the town
---

The town now has ordinary crime from start to finish. Arrests follow the town's crime rates and the police budget, then each case runs under the laws in force: the juvenile court age, whether cash bail exists, self-defense rules, and jail or prison time paid for by the county or state. Gun laws reach the households that keep a gun: lockboxes bought under child access laws, permit fees and classes, and refused handgun sales to people under 21. A civilian oversight board costs the city money, an immigration partnership holds arrested noncitizens for agents, and cutting the police, fire or corrections budget gives the town less service until it is restored.
