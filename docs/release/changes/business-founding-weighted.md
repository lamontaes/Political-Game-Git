section: Fixed
title: A town opened at its Census size no longer founds a business in every market every month
---

A business record in a Census-size town stands for many firms, and a founder weighed one person's pay against the whole market's profit, so someone founded in every market every month: a year opened 40 businesses from a stock of 32 and closed 21 of them as they split the town's demand. A founder now weighs one firm's prospect and cost, and a founding in a weighted market is made when a pull of one over the weight a month reaches one whole. At the same place a year now opens 13 and closes 4. A town at the sample's size founds as before.
