---
id: county-boards-seat-their-members
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Your county board votes its levy with every member seated and in session
---

A county board now votes under a rule pack that seats the same number of
members the county's law sets, so a board of seven is no longer refused a vote
for having more members than a fixed five. A county board is also no longer
shut for the year on the day its state's legislature adjourns, so a levy heard
in April is voted in April in every state.
