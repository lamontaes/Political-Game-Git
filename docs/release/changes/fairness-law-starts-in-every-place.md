id: fairness-law-starts-in-every-place
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Every place now starts with its real fairness law, and two states bar city ordinances
---

The game did not know which places already bar discrimination by sexual
orientation and gender identity, so a bill to add those grounds had nothing to
compare against. Now all 56 places start with their 2026 law: 26 cover both
grounds in both jobs and housing, and 30 leave at least one out. Tennessee and
Arkansas also forbid their cities from adding grounds of their own, so a city
council there cannot pass a fairness ordinance until the state changes its law.
In the other places a city may still go further than its state.
