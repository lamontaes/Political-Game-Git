---
id: premiums-from-pay
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Health premiums come out of each paycheck.
---

A worker's share of their employer health plan is now taken from each paycheck, the way a payroll deduction is, instead of from the household's cash on the 1st of the month. A household that had spent down its cash by the 1st used to lose its plan for a month while still working and earning; now a plan lapses only when the paychecks themselves can't cover it. Dev mode's employer health plans table on the Calendar also shows a month's premiums taken from pay and the policyholders whose last paycheck fell short.
