title: A yes to an interview request is to one outlet, and requests come one a day
---

A candidate's week of interview requests no longer arrives as one call that one yes turned into six sittings in a single day. Each outlet now calls on its own, one a day, the one whose reporter knows the candidate best first, and a yes sits that outlet's reporter and no one else. A national outlet asks only candidates in federal and state races, so a county or town candidate is no longer asked by a national cable network about federal questions.
