title: A county's ballot lists only the offices its state's voters elect.
---

A New Jersey county's ballot no longer lists a County treasurer or County assessor. The county commissioners appoint the treasurer, the Governor appoints the county tax board, and each town appoints its own assessor. The county clerk and sheriff stay on the ballot, now with their own terms from the state constitution: five years for the clerk and three for the sheriff.
