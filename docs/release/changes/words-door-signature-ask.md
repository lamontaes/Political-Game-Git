title: While your petition circulates, a door you knock asks for a signature
---

In the weeks your petition still needs signatures, a door on your canvass now plays as a signature ask instead of a vote ask. You ask the resident to sign, and they answer from the petition record: whether they signed when you knocked. Signing is not a promise to vote for you, so someone who would not back you can still put their name down. You can press a resident who said no, but the answer stays the same.
