title: Scenes at home and work know what happened.
---

A layoff, a first day at a job, the day after school ends, a proposal or breakup, a funeral and a quarrel now carry what happened from the records behind them: the employer and how many years the job was held, the school and whether the senior passed, how long two were together and the child at home, who died, what a quarrel was over, the illness and how long it has run. A fact the records do not hold is left out, never guessed.
