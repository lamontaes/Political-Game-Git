title: A neighbor who works outside town now has taxes taken out of their paycheck once you meet them.
---

Before, a stranger who commuted to a job outside town was paid in full the moment you met them, while the same worker among the unmet crowd paid income, Social Security and Medicare tax. Their pay now comes with the same taxes taken out, and the town and state treasuries receive them.
