title: A place with no government of its own is served by its county's budget
---

A census-designated place, such as Bear, Delaware, or Palmas, Puerto Rico, has
no government of its own, yet it was given its own budget. Now its residents
are served by the county that holds most of them, or in Puerto Rico by the
municipio, and that government keeps the budget. Where the county area has no
county government (New England towns, Alaska's unorganized borough and
consolidated city-counties), the place is listed with that reason until the
government that serves it is linked.
