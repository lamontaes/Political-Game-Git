section: Changed
title: Couples in a new town are mostly a man and a woman
---

Before, a new game chose each partner in a town couple without regard to the
other, so about half of the couples were two women or two men, and few couples
could have a baby. Now most couples are a man and a woman, and about one in
seventy is two women or two men.
