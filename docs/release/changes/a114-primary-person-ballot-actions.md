title: Primary ballot requests follow the elector's recorded decision
---

On primary day, non-player electors with recorded candidate views can request a legally admitted party ballot through the existing decision evaluator. The nomination caller saves the decision and ballot choice before counting votes. Missing views, tied choices and unsupported admission produce no automatic choice. The controlled person's ballot request remains explicit.

Party affiliation does not enroll anyone. Closed and qualified access still require actual registration or party authorization records; this change supplies no enrollment deadline or invitation policy.

CTO sendback follow-through: at opening, eligible electors with an existing recorded affiliation receive a separate party-registration receipt where the primary category requires enrollment. This is the explicit opening initialization contract; later public affiliation changes do not silently rewrite registration. Party members can choose their own ballot in qualified categories, while unsupported crossover or unaffiliated invitations remain ungranted.

A primary preserves each resolved group and marks only an unresolved group pending. The other party's unopposed filing no longer disappears with a failed contested count. Clock steps crossing the primary date write an NPC's actual dated decision and ballot receipt; a controlled player cannot make a late retroactive ballot request.

Primary electors can also decide from actual recorded conduct with a candidate. The same relationship reader supplies trust, respect or conflict to ballot choice and counting, using only interactions dated by primary day. No candidate belief or relationship is manufactured, and a party label or an empty relationship log supplies no reason.
