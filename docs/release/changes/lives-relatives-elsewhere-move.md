title: Relatives who live elsewhere now move house over the years, and you hear about it.
---

Your relatives who live outside your town used to stay exactly where the game started them. Now each one weighs moving every month the way people in town do: by their age, their state's moving rates and what ties them where they live. When they move, to a relative's town, to where people from their county usually go, or to a new home in their state, the family in your town hears about it.
