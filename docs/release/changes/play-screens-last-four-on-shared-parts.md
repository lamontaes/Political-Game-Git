title: The person card, the opening box, the debate and the speech use the game's shared controls.
---

The opening address, a person's card, a debate invitation and a speech you are building now look and behave like the rest of the game: the same speaker head and lines as a conversation, the same panel head and side figure as the character screen, a choice of tabs for each debate question, and numbered replies for the parts of a speech.
