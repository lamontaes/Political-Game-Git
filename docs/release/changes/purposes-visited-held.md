title: When you are held, a relative who visits now brings news from home, money, a lawyer, or asks whether to come again.
---

A relative who comes to see you while you are serving a sentence or held until your case is heard now offers to put money on your account, call a lawyer, see to the house or the children, or call your employer, or asks whether you want them to come again. You can accept, promise to say what you need, ask what it would cost them, or turn it down, and what you agree to is kept.
