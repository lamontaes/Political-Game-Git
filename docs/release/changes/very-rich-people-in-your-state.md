title: Very rich people live in your state and may offer your campaign money
---

A few very rich people now live in your state, each with a fortune and a company of their own. Once you run for office, they may offer your campaign money, sometimes asking you to take their side on a policy question in public, and a deal you take and never honor can be made public.
