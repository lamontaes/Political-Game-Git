title: Your neighbors fall in love, split up and have children
---

Before, the families around you never changed. Nobody in town started seeing
anybody, married, divorced or had a baby unless you were one of the two
people involved.

Now the town's families change with time. Every few months single neighbors
may start dating somebody in town close to their age, and a couple who has
been dating for a while may move in together and later marry. Couples also
break up, more often early on, after a layoff or when money is tight, and
less often when they have a small child at home or many years together. When
a couple living together splits, one of them moves to a new home in town.
Couples who live together have children, most often in their late twenties
and early thirties, and less often when they already have several children
or when nobody at home is working. Your own relationships and children stay
yours to decide.
