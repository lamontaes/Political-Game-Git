title: File for a local seat in person, at the clerk's counter
impact: minor
---

Filing for a town, township or county seat used to be a button on the Campaigns screen. Places now lists the office that takes those filings, named for its government with its clerk's title, and a visit goes on the calendar during office hours. At the counter the clerk answers three questions from the records: what each seat asks, when its election and filing deadline are and what the fee is, and who has already filed. The player files there, and the answers stay in what they know. Filings go to the government that runs the place's elections under its state's rule, and a clerk is seated where none sits yet.
