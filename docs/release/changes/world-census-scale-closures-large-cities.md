title: Firms in a big city stop overpaying, overhiring and closing
---

In a city opened at its full Census size, such as Washington, D.C., a few firms used to close within the year. One raised the pay of an unfilled opening far past what it could afford, and another kept hiring for sales its books could not carry. A person who joined the story from the wider population kept bank savings that belonged to many households, and an owner took home the profit of many businesses. Now a raise fits what the month left over, hiring follows the firm's own books, and savings and profit go to the households they belong to. Washington, D.C. at its Census size now closes no more firms in a year than at its sample size.
