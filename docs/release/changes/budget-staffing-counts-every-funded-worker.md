title: Budgets count every police officer and teacher in town
---

A town's budget counted only the officers and teachers its job market had
hired, so those written with the town's residents at the start were never
laid off or counted. A Duluth, Minnesota world kept 16 public school
teachers against a funded count of 4. The budget now counts everyone living
in town who holds the funded role, and it never ends the player's own job,
which the player's career decides.
