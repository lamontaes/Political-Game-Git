title: The sales tax no longer reaches medical care, tuition, insurance or the services your state does not tax
---

A clinic, a nursing home, a private school, an insurance agency and most service businesses no longer pay sales tax on what they sell, except where the state taxes services (Hawaii, New Mexico and South Dakota). Pool item: laws-sales-tax-base-by-seller-kind.
