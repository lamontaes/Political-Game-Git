Each person now has a childhood record, read from what the world already
keeps: how many times they moved between homes before 18, and, as other
parts of the game start recording them, their years with health coverage,
in poverty, on food aid, in a better neighborhood and in school. Years
before the world opened are marked as not watched, never as years without
anything happening. The outcome web can now apply its lifelong links, such
as childhood coverage to graduation, to one person from that record. Those
links carry no approved size yet, so nothing changes in play.
