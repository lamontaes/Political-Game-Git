title: Every child now has a childhood record built from what happened to them
---

Each person now keeps a childhood record. It starts with where and when they
were born, and it notes any move that pulls them out of school in the middle
of a school year, along with the grade they were in. Families with children
in grade school can now move away. Before, a child's school held the whole
household in place. The child leaves their school as a transfer, and the
school's record says whether they left mid-year or over the summer. The
record can also tell how long a child was eligible for public health
coverage, though today's coverage rules reach only adults. Saved games load
with an empty record.
