title: A state income tax law changes the state's income tax revenue
---

When a state moves between a flat and a graduated income tax, its budget's
income tax revenue now changes from the month the law takes effect, and each
later budget expects the change once. The sizes come from real fiscal notes:
Illinois' 2020 graduated-rate estimate raises it 15.0%, and Iowa's 2024 move
to a flat rate lowers it 12.2%, each as a share of the state's own 2022
collections. Before, a state that flattened its income tax kept booking the
same rising revenue.
