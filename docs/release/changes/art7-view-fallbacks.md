Person recipes accept back and side views and fall back to a complete three-quarter view, then front. A facing direction mirrors the resolved painting and its anchors together. Existing pose, wardrobe availability, and recipe keys remain compatible.

Back and side paintings are still awaiting delivery; this change adds view resolution and facing support without importing or approving artwork.
