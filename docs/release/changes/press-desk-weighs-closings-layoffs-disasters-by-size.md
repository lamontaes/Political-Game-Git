title: The paper now covers a business closing, a layoff and a national disaster by how big it is
---

A town's biggest employer could close and the paper printed nothing, a layoff never made the page, and a disaster that struck a million people ranked the same as one that struck a few hundred. The paper now weighs a closing or a layoff by the share of the town's jobs it ended, and a disaster by the share of the country's people who live in the counties its declaration names. A closing prints once, with how many people worked there, why it closed and, where it is so, that it was the town's largest employer. In dev mode, a closing, a layoff or a disaster now shows how each paper weighed it, term by term, and why each ran it or dropped it.
