title: A state's rent help reaches a town only in proportion to its people
---

When a state raised its housing line, the played town used to receive rent help for the whole state's extra, about a hundred times its share in a small town. The town now gets the part its own residents stand for, and a tenant is helped in part when a full voucher would overshoot that share.
