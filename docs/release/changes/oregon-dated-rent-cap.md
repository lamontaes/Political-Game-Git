title: Oregon rent renewals read the announced annual cap
---

Oregon's starting rent law now reads the official 9.5% annual cap for 2026
instead of the statutory 10% ceiling. Its next dated phase reads the officially
announced 10% cap for 2027. Both retain the sourced 15-year construction
exemption. Coverage still depends on the existing recorded tenancy facts;
this correction does not establish missing affordable-housing exemption facts.
