title: Your family looks like the person you made
---

When the face you choose in the creator has a skin tone your drawn family does not have, your blood family (parents, brothers and sisters, children, grandparents, grandchildren, aunts, uncles, nieces and nephews) now takes the family origin that matches it. A partner, in-laws and housemates keep the origin they were drawn with, as in life.
