title: Elected officials can hire office staff
---

A new state legislator now has an office to staff. "Look for staff" brings
three applicants each for a Legislative Aide and a Constituent Caseworker, with
their working lives on record, and a Hire button for each. Your staff briefing
then reads the people you hired.

A governor's Legislative Director and Caseworker posts, which nothing ever
filled, open for hiring on the governor's desk the same way.

A legislator's two positions are a marked placeholder until research says how
many staff each legislature really gives its members.
