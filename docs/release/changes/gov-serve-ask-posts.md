title: As mayor or governor, you ask someone to run a department, and they can say no
---

Picking someone for an open department or cabinet post now asks them in a sit-down, as you ask a running mate. They answer from their own record: what the post pays against their pay now, the job they would leave, how well they know you, where you stand on the question they care most about, and what they have heard about you. A yes names them, and the council or the state senate confirms them as before. A no keeps the post open, and you can ask them again later.
