Engine only, with no player-facing entry point yet. A legislative measure can
now name the policy questions it is about, directly rather than through a
quantitative alternative, and a world refuses to hold a measure pointing at a
question its catalogue does not carry. Nothing in play passes the new field
yet, because a new world's policy catalogue is still empty, so no screen and no
save written so far reads differently.
