title: A territory life saved before territories had a Governor gets one
---

A life in Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa or the
Northern Mariana Islands saved before territories had their own Governor went
on with nobody in the office. The first time such a save moves forward, the
territory's Governor is seated for the term in progress that day, the same way
a new game seats one. Nothing earlier is filled in: no past Governors and no
past election. The government screen's "Territory" label, Delegate row and
missing Senate row already applied to old saves, because they are read fresh.
