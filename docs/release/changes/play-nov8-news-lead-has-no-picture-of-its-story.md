title: Every lead story has a picture of what it is about
---

The lead story on every paper now shows a picture of the place it is about: the council chamber or county commission room for a local vote, the state's own capitol for a state law, the Capitol, the White House or the Oval Office for federal news, a committee room for a bill in committee, the business for a business story, and the town's street for a change in the town. A paper with only short items leads with them instead of leaving its first column empty.
