The owner's rules about a line and its reply now judge each answering line together with the line it answers, so a reply is no longer judged with its ask stripped off. A "no" after one line keeps the reply from being offered after that line only. Three such rules (a quick, agreeable wrap-up; a lie about where you are from; words put in the player's mouth) have their first verdicts and are samples: no line is pulled or barred until the Words lead reads them.
