Three of the owner's rules about what a line names now judge an answering line together with the line it answers, so a promise or a plea that leans on the ask before it is no longer judged with that ask stripped off. A "no" after one line keeps the line from being offered after that line only. No line is pulled or barred yet: both judged rules stay samples until the Words lead reads their "no" pairs.
