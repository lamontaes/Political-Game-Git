id: gov-staffing-sized-against-prior-line
impact: patch
title: A budget bill's raise or cut reaches the staff a line funds
section: Fixed
---

A department's funded staff is now sized against the budget line before this year's bill, so a raise or cut the council adopts changes how many people it hires or lays off. Before, the bill was counted in the line it was measured against and changed nothing.
