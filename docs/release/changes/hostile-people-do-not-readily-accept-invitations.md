title: A hostile person no longer readily says yes when asked to meet
---

A person who often begins an exchange looking for a fight now counts that as one
reason to turn down an invitation to meet. It weighs beside what is between the
two of them, the day and everything else they carry, so the same person asked
the same thing without that streak still says yes.
