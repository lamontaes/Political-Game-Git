section: Added
title: Council seats are chosen by part of town where the place's records say so
---

A town's council now has the seats its charter says are chosen by district or ward (or an estimate from the national survey, the larger towns choosing some by district), and the count of such a seat takes only the voters of that part of town. At-large seats are counted across the whole town as before.
