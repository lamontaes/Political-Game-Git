title: When Congress raises Social Security, retirees' incomes rise and fewer are counted poor.
---

A change to federal Social Security now reaches every retiree's income where poverty is counted, including retirees the town counts as a group and people who claim during play. Before, only some retirees' checks moved and the poverty count did not follow.
