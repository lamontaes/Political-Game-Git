The story director now picks the one thing each of the opening's six stops says about a new life: the federal law that reached the player or who leads the country, the members of Congress and state legislators who represent their home, who runs their town or the county that serves it, the family member who matters most to them, and why day one starts at work or at home. Each pick names its records. The opening's new layout and its lines come next, from other lanes.

A household that was never enrolled in food assistance no longer has the work requirement for assistance recorded as costing it benefits. Before, every such household carried that loss from the first day.
