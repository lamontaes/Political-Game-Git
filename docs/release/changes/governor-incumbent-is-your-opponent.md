title: A sitting governor who runs again is your opponent
---

Filing for governor used to put you against one newly made-up rival, and the
sitting governor quietly left the race. When the governor stands for another
term, they are now the person you run against, and if they win they stay in
office. An open seat still draws a new challenger.
