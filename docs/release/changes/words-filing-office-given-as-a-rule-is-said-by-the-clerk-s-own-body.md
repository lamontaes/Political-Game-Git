title: A filing office given as a rule is the named body it points to, with its clerk
---

A filing office the record gives as a rule, such as the board of elections of the most populous county in the district, is now the body the rule points to for your town where the game can know it, and the clerk at that counter works there and says so; where it cannot, the clerk is named and the counter is the plain board of elections, with no county guessed.
