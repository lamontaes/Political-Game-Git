id: town-school-district
impact: patch
section: Added
title: Your town has a school district that counts its pupils and teachers
---

Before, your town's public school hired teachers and taught children, but
nobody kept count of how many children it taught or how many teachers it had.

Now your town has its own school district. When the game opens, and every
fall after that, the district counts the children enrolled in the town's
public schools and the teachers working there, and how many pupils there are
for each teacher. Each year's count says what changed from the year before:
more or fewer pupils, or more or fewer teachers.
