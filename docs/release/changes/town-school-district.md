title: Your town has a school district that counts its pupils and teachers
---

Before, your town's public schools hired teachers and taught children, but
nobody kept count of how many children they taught or how many teachers they
had. Each school also started with several teachers, however few children it
had, and children who turned five, moved to town or outgrew their school
never started or changed school.

Now your town has its own school district. Every fall a new school year
starts: children who have reached school age start school, pupils who have
outgrown their school move up to the next one, and pupils past high school
age finish. Then the district counts the children in its public schools, the
teachers working there, and how many pupils there are for each teacher. Each
year's count says what changed from the year before: more or fewer pupils,
or more or fewer teachers. Schools hire teachers for the pupils they have,
about one for every 15, and every school has at least one.
