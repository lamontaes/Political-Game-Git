title: A caucus or committee meeting no longer makes you close to everyone in the room.
---

A state legislator at a caucus or committee meeting now gets to know the colleagues there the way they do on the floor: the few whose service began nearest their own, a little at a time. Before, every meeting counted as hours spent with each person in the room, so by spring every colleague felt like a close friend and nobody had to be asked for a vote on your bill.
