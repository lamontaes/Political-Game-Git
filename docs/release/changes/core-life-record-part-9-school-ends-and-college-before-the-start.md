title: A person's past now holds the end of high school and college years
---

People's pasts now say when they finished high school and, for those who went on, when they started college and when it ended, with a bachelor's or without one, on the same school calendar every child shares. A first job before the game began never starts before school ended. The card tells them once their lines are written.
