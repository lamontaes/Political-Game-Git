title: A grown character starts with their schooling and working life on the record again
---

Starting a new life at thirty gave you somebody with no school behind them and
no job they had ever held. The reasoning was sound — the game should not invent
a biography you never chose — but a blank past is not the absence of a claim
about your life, it is a claim, and a stranger one.

It cost more than a line on a page. The people you had worked with were part of
that history, so they did not exist either, and they are the people most likely
to get in touch with you. An ordinary forty-year-old was about half as likely to
have anybody reach out to them at all.

A new character again begins with a school, a working life and the people who
came with it. The past it writes is a placeholder, not research, and it will be
replaced by histories built from real schools — but a generated past is nearer
to your life than none.

Saves made under the previous behaviour are untouched and still rebuild exactly
as they were.
