title: The President issues and reverses orders from their own views
---

A President who is not played now weighs the federal power-plant carbon rule each month from their own views and issues or revokes it, the same way a governor does for the orders they can give. A Democratic-leaning President can put the rule in force, and a later Republican-leaning President revokes it.
