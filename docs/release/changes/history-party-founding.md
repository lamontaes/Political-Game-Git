title: A world's history can found a new party when its voters are left unreached
---

In a world whose past runs from 1925, voters whose lean does not reach either national party now build pressure for a new party each presidential year. When that pressure outweighs the cost, a party is founded at the middle of those voters, and the dev opening view lists it with its year, position and cause. Worlds differ: some found one, some never do.
