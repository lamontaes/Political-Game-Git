impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The national and state economies are now made by each state's households and employers.
---

Every state is kept as households, employers and a state ledger, and steps once a month under its own law and the central bank's rate. The nation is the sum of the states. A tax change, a rate change or a disaster now moves jobs, pay, prices and budgets across the country. The old national formula, with its built-in cycle, and the pull of each state toward the nation are gone.
