title: Public college tuition uses recorded annual prices and saved school terms
---

College applications save sourced annual tuition and the institution's recorded term count. Billing splits the annual amount across those saved terms, with the minor-unit remainder in the last term. Recorded state-controlled colleges carry their state identity. Tuition freezes cap revised unpaid charges at the operative recorded school price through append-only terms, preserving prior terms and payments. Extra periods needed to earn credits remain charged. The aggregate state effect is retained while the separate accounting consequence remains unfinished; no appropriation or school-loss ledger is fabricated.
