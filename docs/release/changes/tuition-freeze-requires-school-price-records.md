title: Public college tuition uses recorded annual prices and saved school terms
---

College applications save sourced annual tuition and the institution's recorded term count. Billing splits the annual amount across those saved terms, with the minor-unit remainder in the last term. Recorded state-controlled colleges carry their state identity. The existing price-cost engine caps revised unpaid charges at the operative recorded school price, preserving prior terms and payments. Extra periods needed to earn credits remain charged. The aggregate state forecast is removed. An actual adopted tuition-backfill appropriation pays the recorded reduction through the existing public-payment writer; without that authority, the school receives only the capped tuition.
