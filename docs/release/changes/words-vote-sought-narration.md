title: Asking a colleague for their vote opens from your side
---

When you ask a colleague for their vote the evening before the roll, the scene used to open as if they had come to you ("Pat Rivera catches you outside the chamber"). It now opens from your side: "It's the evening before the vote on Ordinance 2025-4. You catch Deborah on the way out." An ask the evening before the roll that falls in the chamber is now a scene too; before, only one at the office or by phone was, and one in the chamber went to the journal unseen.
