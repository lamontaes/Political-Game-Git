section: Changed
title: Everyone is drawn by the new people engine
---

Every adult in the game is now drawn by the new people engine: in rooms, in
the conversation box, on person cards and in the character creator. Each
person has one of six bodies (feminine or masculine, lean, average or
heavier), one of seven skin tones, a whole face with no seams, and hair, and
wears everyday clothes or, in government rooms, a formal suit. A person looks
the same on every screen and after every load. In the creator and in
Appearance and wardrobe you choose your body, build, skin tone and outfit
with arrows, or press Randomize. Seated people and children still use the
older pictures until the engine has seated and child bodies. More faces,
hairstyles and clothes arrive as they are painted.
