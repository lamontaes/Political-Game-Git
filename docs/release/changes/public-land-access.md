title: Public-land access pays for management and brings visitors to town
---

New public-land access laws add the proposal's management costs to the state budget. Nearby visitor spending reaches existing lodging, restaurant, retail and recreation businesses and their cash, with demand removed when the law is repealed.

Opening estimate follow-up: the all-state NASPD visitation report replaces the North Carolina-only visitation proxy, saves per-place opening values, and preserves exact filed prices. Unresolved source coverage remains explicit in the Team 1 handback.
