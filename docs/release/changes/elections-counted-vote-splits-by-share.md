section: Improved
title: Close local races come out close
---

In races without party labels, and in a party's primary, the town's wider population now divides its votes between the candidates by how much it prefers each, so a small edge wins a few points instead of every group in town. An incumbent who is only slightly better known no longer sweeps the whole electorate, and a close race for council or mayor stays close on election night. General elections between parties count as before.
