title: Fare bill sponsors answer the executive's follow-up questions.
---

When an executive asks what a fare bill does, its sponsor can explain what changes at boarding. When asked what changes for local people, the sponsor can describe a family trip, a connection or a commuter's pass. When the executive is leaning toward signing, the visitor can report that the bill is still pending.
