title: Loading a new game plays your town's story: a year counter through your life, your journal writing itself, headlines and the map.
---

While a new game's first scene is drawn, a panel plays the world that was just built: the years of your life count up, each year writes its lines of your story and prints the headlines you heard, and your state's map fills in county by county, your districts last. A click ends it early.
