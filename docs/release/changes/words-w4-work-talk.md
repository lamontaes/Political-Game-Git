title: When you meet someone, talk about work names where they work and what they do there.
---

Meeting someone new, the talk about work is put together fresh from that person's records: where they work, what their job is, and the town it is in when it is out of town ("These days I'm at Singh Building Services. They've got me on as a janitor."). Your own answer can name your employer or your job. Your replies are different moves, and no two of them repeat the same name: tell them where you work, ask how long they have been there (only when that is on record), ask what they do there, or just take it in. The old lines that asked how busy a place was, or whether someone likes it there, are gone.
