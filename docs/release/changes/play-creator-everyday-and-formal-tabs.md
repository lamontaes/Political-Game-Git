title: Everyday and Formal tabs in How I look
---

New game's How I look has two tabs, Everyday and Formal. Everyday lets you pick the top you wear away from work and its color; Formal holds the shirt, tie and jacket. The portrait beside the form changes clothes as you switch tabs, and the top you pick is the one you wear in everyday scenes.
