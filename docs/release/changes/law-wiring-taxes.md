title: Tax laws reach the people who pay them
---

When a government changes an income tax, a property tax or a corporate tax, the new rate is now what comes out of paychecks, home tax bills and company profits, for named residents and for the wider population alike, and each payer's record names the law that took the money. A graduated income tax follows a changed top rate, businesses owned by a corporation now pay the state's tax as well as the federal one, an estate settles under the death taxes in force and names who paid them, and the budget acts for balanced budgets, pensions and reserves show the treasury they moved.
