title: Executive desks use declared legal deadlines
---

Bills on an executive desk use the action window declared in their rule pack.
An unsupported window leaves the bill pending without an invented ten-day
deadline. The briefing shows that no deadline is established. Saving and
continuing preserves the matter, and a supported lapse still records the
pack's consequence without a signature.
