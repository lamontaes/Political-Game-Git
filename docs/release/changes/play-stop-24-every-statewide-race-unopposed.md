title: Statewide races draw a Democrat and a Republican, and each result shows one vote total

On the first general election night, races for governor, U.S. senator and the other statewide offices were often won unopposed, because a major party with no sitting officeholder never put up a nominee. Each major party now fields one in every statewide partisan race. Each result shows its candidates' votes and the ballots cast in the race, instead of a turnout figure for a handful of named neighbors and a second total under every county.
