title: A friend and a rival say different things on the call after the count.
---

The calls after an election now depend on how the two people stand. A friend who won takes your concession warmly ("Hey. You did everything right, you know. Some years it just goes the other way."). Someone you last quarreled with doesn't ("I didn't think you'd call. I'll take it."). The same holds when a runner-up calls you after you win. The tie comes from your record together: a friend is someone you're closer to than not, and a rival is someone your last contact was a quarrel with.
