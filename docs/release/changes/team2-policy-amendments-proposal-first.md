title: Rejected policy amendments keep their actual chamber votes
---

A state policy amendment supported by legislators' recorded principles is saved before its actual chambers vote. A defeated proposal retains its named members and reasons instead of disappearing behind a preliminary forecast. Policy and Article V proposal ballots share the existing chamber decision path. Existing terms, thresholds and consideration weights stay unchanged.
