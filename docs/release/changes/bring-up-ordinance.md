id: bring-up-ordinance
impact: minor
section: Fixed
title: A neighbor no longer tells you "your ordinance didn't pass" about a vote on someone else's bill.
---

When a neighbor read about a vote you only sat in on, they said it was your ordinance and that it failed, even while your own ordinance had passed. They now say "your ordinance" only about a bill you sponsored, with its own result, and they bring up your own bill's vote before a vote you merely cast.
