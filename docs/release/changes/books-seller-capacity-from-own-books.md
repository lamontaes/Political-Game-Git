id: books-seller-capacity-from-own-books
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A seller's capacity comes from its own books, not an industry utilization rate
---

A seller serves what its staff and shop are projected to sell, or the most it sold in a recent month if that was more. Spending beyond that buys the same thing outside the town. The 79.6% industry utilization rate that set it before is gone.
