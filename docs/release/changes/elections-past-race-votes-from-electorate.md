title: Last year's legislature races show the votes the district really cast, and name the office.
---

On the opening's front page, a territory's legislature races printed about fifty votes each, the number of people the game had drawn in town, and every story said "the race for Legislator". Now each race counts the ballots its territory reported, the whole territory for an at-large seat and a district's share for the rest. Each office is named the way its chamber names its members: Senator in Guam and the Virgin Islands, State Senator in Nebraska, Assemblymember, Delegate or Councilmember where those are the titles. A one-chamber legislature is called by its own name, such as the Legislature of the Virgin Islands.
