section: Changed
title: Legislators reflect on questions they actually meet
---

When a non-player sponsor encounters a policy question in a filed measure,
their saved principles can now prompt a dated private reflection. The resulting
view cites the exposure and those principles, survives Save and Continue, and
can be reconsidered after another encounter. No view is assigned without a
recorded bearing, and a player-controlled sponsor keeps authority over their
own opinions.
