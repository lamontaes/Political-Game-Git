title: One kind of scene can play several different ways, chosen by what the records say
---

A bribe offered to you no longer plays the same way every time. The offer can be a small gift, a large sum, a favor from a friend, a second ask from someone you already turned down, or a plea from someone with nothing left, and each of those has its own opening, its own replies and its own endings. The same goes for the person who comes back to press you after you handed a bribe back: a veiled hint from a friend, an open threat from a stranger, a begging, or a third visit. Which one you meet depends on the size of the offer against your household's year, how close the two of you are, how hard pressed they are for money, and whether they have asked or been turned down before, and on their own temperament. Two offers from the same person about two different matters are now two separate scenes that say different things.
