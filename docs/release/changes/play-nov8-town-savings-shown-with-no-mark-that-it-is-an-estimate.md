title: Savings say when they are the game's starting estimate
---

On Politics, Budgets, a government's Savings now reads "(estimate)" while it is still the amount the game started that government with. The mark goes away once play replaces that amount: at a month's close, when the treasury's extra cash is invested, or once the government has paid out more than it started with.
