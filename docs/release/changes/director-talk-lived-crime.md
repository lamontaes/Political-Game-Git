title: A break-in, theft, robbery or assault is now something that happened to a person in the people's memory.
---

When a household is burglarized or someone is robbed or attacked, the people it happened to now remember it, and someone who knows them may ask after them. A victim can tell a friend what happened, but those lines wait for grading, so for now the talk does not raise it.
