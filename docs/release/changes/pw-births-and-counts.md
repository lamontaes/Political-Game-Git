id: pw-births-and-counts
impact: minor
section: Improved
title: Towns gain births, deaths and moves every month
---

A town's counted residents now change each month. Babies are born at the state's recorded birth rates, people die by the national life tables, and households move away and arrive at the state's recorded moving rates, so a town of ten thousand moves by tens or hundreds of people in a year. Couples the player knows now decide about a child from their own lives (age, how long they have been together, pay and work, room at home, children, health), and a couple with no income is no longer ruled out at once.
