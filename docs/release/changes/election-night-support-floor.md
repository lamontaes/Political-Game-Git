title: Election night keeps the floor the campaign kept
---

Every candidate holds at least one percent of support for the whole of a
campaign, but the result on election night could still print a share below
that — a runaway race finished 99.99 to 0.01, which is one vote in ten
thousand. The count now respects the same floor the campaign did.
