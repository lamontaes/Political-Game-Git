The executive engine family is two: `offices` (who holds every seat: terms, vacancies, succession, other places' officials, appointments, pay and the local government rules) and `governing` (what an officeholder does with their day: the office day at every level, the executive's desk, the president's acts, staff and constituent cases). The six seats files governing reads (appointments, officials' identity, official living, outside governments, official pay and local governments) are entries of offices, so the split adds no reach-ins: the guard's count falls by 26 files' worth and nothing rises. No game code changes.
