title: A disaster's repairs and aid now stay in the town it damaged, wherever you live.
---

When you move away from the town you started in and a disaster hits it, the aid and the repair bills now go to that town's own households and builders. Before, the town you had moved to paid for repairs to homes in the old one, and the old town's builders got nothing.
