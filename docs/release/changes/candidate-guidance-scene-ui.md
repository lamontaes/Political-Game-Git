title: Candidate guidance opens its conversation in the room
---

Going to a scheduled conversation about running for office now opens the community room with the recorded host. You can ask the available questions, stay for the talk, or leave and return home. Asked questions and answers remain visible during the visit. The party work list no longer says something blocked your trip when you reached the room.
