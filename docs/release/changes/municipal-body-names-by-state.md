section: Improved
title: Town councils go by the name their state gives them.
---

A town's governing body now carries the usual name for its kind of town in its state, such as a village's Board of Trustees, a Wisconsin city's Common Council or a Tennessee town's Board of Mayor and Aldermen, instead of a plain "Council". The Government screen shows that name under each local seat, and the town's ordinances are enacted in it.
