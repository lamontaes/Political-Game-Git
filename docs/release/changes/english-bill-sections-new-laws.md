Bills that change a governor's term limit, the federal SSI benefit rate or the home energy assistance income limit, and the unemployment insurance program, now read in the bill's own words.

The law-reach parameter table for benefits and payments now gives each of its rows a role (a rule the law sets, a rate or weight, or a level the opening world starts from).
