title: A legislator holding out on your bill gives reasons from the bill
---

Before, a legislator who would not commit yet, doubted how a section would be
run, or liked your goal but not your method could back it up with things that
were never true in your game: three agencies each blaming the others, a
department that missed a deadline with twice the staff, rulemaking stretching
past March, or grants going to the places that need them least.

Now those remarks speak only from the bill and the room: its number, the
section and its heading, its next step, what it commits as it now reads, and
the analyst who would write the fiscal note. Where the record says nothing,
such as how many agencies are involved or how many staff a program would
need, the member asks rather than claims.
