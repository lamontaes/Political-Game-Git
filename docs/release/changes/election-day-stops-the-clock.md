title: Your own election day stops the clock and tells you the result
---

Passing a day or a week, or letting the weeks run on, used to run straight
through your own election, so a win could arrive with nothing said. Time now
stops the morning after your election, the report says whether you won and by
how much, and the result goes into your journal.
