title: A charge or an exposure can start a recall of a local official.
---

When a serving city, county or township official is charged with a crime or exposed by the press, the residents who heard about it may organize a recall petition, sign it and vote, each by their own grievance, their ties to the official and their temperament. This holds in states that allow a recall only for cause, where a vote on an ordinance is no ground for one.
