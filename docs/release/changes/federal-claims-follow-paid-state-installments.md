---
id: federal-claims-follow-paid-state-installments
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Recorded state provider payments feed federal matching claims at settlement
---

Successful state program installments bind their completed paid outcome to
an existing federal appropriation's admitted provider-class claim rule. Providers
come from the state's recorded organizations and current profiles; a missing or
ineligible provider creates no substitute. The saved expenditure period, due date,
and exact adopted matching term remain authoritative. The existing federal
commitment and installment writer posts cash and protects replay. This adds no
ordinary Medicaid rates, awards, eligible expenditure assumptions, or cash.
