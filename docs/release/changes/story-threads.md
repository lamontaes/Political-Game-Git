title: The people in a life now rise and fade
---

Each person now holds a thread to everyone who matters to them: family, the people they live with, and anyone a scored moment ties them to. A thread's importance grows with what happens between them and fades when they fall out of touch at their own usual rhythm, and a meeting after years apart renews it. Threads stay hidden in play; the observer's person file shows them, with each person's recent moments. An illness that keeps someone from getting about now explains time apart, and losing a job counts for more when it was most of the household's pay.
