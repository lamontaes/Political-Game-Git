title: A child's School screen names their school and grade
---

A child opening School read only "You are a student," and over the summer not even that. It now names the school they go to and the grade they are in, or, over the summer, the school and grade they start in the fall. The Study tab shows a child still in school their school instead of a list of colleges to apply to, and the college list no longer prints where its listings came from.
