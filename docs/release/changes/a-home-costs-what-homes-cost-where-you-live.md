title: A home costs what homes cost in your county
---

Buying a home no longer costs $250,000 everywhere. The price now starts from
the median home value in your county, from the Census Bureau's 2020-2024
American Community Survey, and moves with the world's prices from there. The
down payment and the monthly payment scale with the price.
