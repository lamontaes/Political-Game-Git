title: A dead person's account no longer pays the household's bills while the estate waits
---

When a household's main payer dies, the household's living costs, property tax and price changes are now paid by its oldest living adult, and the dead person's wages stop. The estate that passes to the heirs is the estate that was left, not one drained by bills during probate.
