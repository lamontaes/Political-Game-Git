title: Races name their district in words, such as District 5, instead of a code like (05), and say it once.
---

At the clerk's counter, on Campaigns, in Government and on election night, a race's title names its district as "District 5" rather than the records' "(05)", and the counter no longer repeats it as "Seat 05". A seat elected at large shows no district.
