title: "As governor, you can fill a cabinet post with people from across the state"
---

When you are governor and a cabinet post stands open, the people offered include the state's own people from other towns, by name, and not only your town's neighbors. Only the one you appoint is brought into the game, living where they lived, on the state payroll.
