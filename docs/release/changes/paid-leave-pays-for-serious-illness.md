title: A state paid leave program pays part of the wages a serious illness takes
---

Where a state's paid family and medical leave program is in force, a part-time worker home with a serious illness, or with a seriously ill child, now receives a benefit from the program for the unpaid days. Days of the worker's own illness count after a 7-day waiting period, and days caring for a child count from the first day. The benefit replaces the program's lower-wage share of the pay lost, up to its weekly maximum, and it is paid out of the state account the premiums went into. Where no program is in force, or a law ends one, nothing is paid.
