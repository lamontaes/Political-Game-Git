title: An officeholder can answer for a complaint, or resign, from the press desk
---

When a complaint or question about you is public and you hold an office, the
press desk now offers five answers under it: explain it yourself, stand behind
your account, cooperate with the inquiry, decline to cooperate, or resign the
office. Resigning asks you to confirm first. Whatever you choose is recorded
in public, and the desk says what it did to the office, including the date the
office becomes vacant if you resign.
