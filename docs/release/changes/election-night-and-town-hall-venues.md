title: Election night is held at the venue, and a school's town hall is in the gym
---

When you give your victory speech or concede, the screen for that day shows the
election night venue, with the count on the wall screens. The next day it goes
back to where you were.

A town hall that a school or a civic group hosts is held in the school gym,
with its own spot to stand in. A town hall hosted by a party chapter or a
campaign stays in the community room. The four other unused pictures
(convention hall, debate stage, rally stage and radio booth) stay unused until
something in the game holds those events.
