title: A person's past can hold an earlier partner and a night in hospital
---

People now have more behind them: some had an earlier marriage or long partnership that ended before the game began, by parting or by the partner's death, and many spent a night in hospital for an illness or injury, each with its year, at the rates real towns show. The card tells them once their lines are written.
