title: Blank and over votes are no longer listed as parties
---

In states whose ballots print count lines beside the candidates, such as Vermont, Hawaii, Wyoming and D.C., the Politics screen listed "Blank Votes", "Over Votes" and "Under Votes" as parties people could join. Those lines, and others like "Void", "Scattering" and "None of These Candidates", are no longer made into parties.
