title: The town's business list names every business people work at
---

Before, the list of businesses in town showed only the two or three seated with
an owner, so a whole town seemed to have three employers.

Now it also lists every other private employer the town's people work at,
such as the trucking firm, the builders and the family restaurant, each under
its own name.
