title: Housing and lending laws reach households
---

Housing and lending laws now reach real households and move real money. Rent stabilization, the inclusionary requirement, the multifamily, all-electric and farmland rules, right to counsel in eviction, the consumer loan rate cap and a bigger housing budget line each record the households, builders and governments they touched, the dollars involved, and stop when the law is repealed. A housing budget increase now pays rent assistance to the town's lowest-income renters, and a tenant with a lawyer in an eviction case can settle on a payment plan the city has paid to support.
