section: Changed
title: Committees are the ones leadership seats
---

A bill now goes to the committee the chamber's leadership appointed, with its chair, and goes straight to the floor where no committee is seated; the old rule that handed committee seats to the longest-serving members is gone. The opening no longer guesses who was already pregnant before day one, so every pregnancy starts in play. A budget nobody was seated to vote continues the prior year's, and says so.
