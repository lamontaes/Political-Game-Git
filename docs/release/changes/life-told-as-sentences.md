title: A life is told as sentences, and people now remember what happens to them.
---

Your life and every person's card tell a life as a few sentences grouped by what mattered. The timeline is gone. Each person now keeps a short list of the things that happened to them while you play, such as a job lost, a move, a death in the family or an election won, and the card and the You screen read that list. Your own life is told as "I".
