title: An employer on the older work list calls back for a reason
---

When someone accepts a job from the older work list and does not come in on
the start date, the employer no longer tosses a coin over whether to call.
It calls once with a new start date when somebody the person knows works
there, or when nobody else is waiting to start whom it could call instead;
otherwise it withdraws the offer. This is the same rule the job market's own
employers follow. The new start date comes a few days later, or a week later
when the person has a job to leave first, and an unanswered offer lapses
after the short reply window the job market gives work paid by the shift.
