title: Personality shapes civic life and news
---

Who turns out to vote, who stands up at a meeting, who joins a party or a group, who passes a story on and who believes the story they are told now follow each person's own temperament, sized from the research. A neighbor who doubts a story you pass on no longer remembers it, and a listener who does not trust a retelling at home is not moved by it. Open-minded, trusting people are moved further by an argument than closed, wary ones.
