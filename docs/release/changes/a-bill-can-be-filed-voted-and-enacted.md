title: A bill can be filed, voted on and enacted
---

A seated state legislator can now file a bill, see it voted on and enacted, and find it still there after saving and reloading. The route works for numbered state House and Senate districts, and members of the U.S. House and of a city council can take part in the votes of their own chamber.
