title: Campaign money ordered repaid now goes to the government, not back to the campaign
---

When an ethics or elections body finds that a candidate took campaign money
for personal use, the repayment it orders is now paid to the state (or to the
United States Treasury for a federal case). Before, the money went back into
the campaign account, where the same candidate could take it again. Reactions
to how a disaster was handled now arrive with the weekly news, which also
fixes a loading-order fault that stopped part of the test suite from starting.
