title: The evening before a close vote, the sponsor asks for the votes the bill needs, and an answer counts at the roll call
---

When a bill in your chamber is short of votes the evening before its floor vote, its sponsor asks the members it needs, nearest to yes first, and a member asked can be you. Each ask is a scene. A member who agrees carries that promise into the next day's vote, and their recorded vote names the answer among its reasons; a refusal counts against the bill.
