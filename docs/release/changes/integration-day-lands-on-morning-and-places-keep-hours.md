title: Days start in the morning, and scenes wait for their hours
---

Pressing Day or Week now lands on the next morning at 7 AM, not midnight, and an outing on the campaign moves the clock by the hours it takes. Something that happened in the night is met the next morning: nobody turns up in your bedroom at midnight, and an office scene waits for office hours.
