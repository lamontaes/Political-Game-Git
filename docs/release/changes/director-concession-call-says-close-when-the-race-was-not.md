title: The concession call no longer tells you a lopsided loss was close
---

On election night after a loss, the campaign manager no longer answers "How close was it?" with "Closer than anybody expected" whatever the count was. When you lost by more than your own campaign's poll had it, they say so, and you can tell them there's nothing to wait for.
