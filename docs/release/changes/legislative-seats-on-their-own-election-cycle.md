section: Fixed
title: Legislative seats come up in the election for their term
---

Kansas Senate seats no longer appear in the 2026 regular election. They next
come up in 2028, while Kansas House seats still come up every two years.
Nebraska's even-numbered legislative districts come up in 2026 and its
odd-numbered districts in 2028. A member whose seat is not due keeps serving;
the game does not replace them or leave their seat vacant at the wrong term
boundary. A player filing for a recorded district sees that district's next
regular election. Special elections remain separate from these regular cycles.
