title: A reporter's lines no longer call the story "the piece", and evening lines no longer play in the daytime.
---

The reporter's interview lines now say "the story" and "the reporter is writing about". A donor's or reporter's "today" line plays only on the day it is true, and a "tonight" line plays only after the day is over.
