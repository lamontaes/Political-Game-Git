title: A child who starts in school attends a school with a real name
---

A new life that starts at school age put the child in "Ely, Nevada public
school", or the same phrase with their own town's name, which is not what
anybody calls a school. New games now give it a generated name for the level
the child is at: an elementary school below eleven, a middle school from
eleven, a high school from fourteen. The names are made the same way as the
schools in a grown character's summarized past.

A child past elementary school also has the schools they already finished on
their record. A seventeen-year-old started at 17 went to elementary school
from five, completed it at eleven, completed middle school at fourteen, and has
been at the high school since then, where before they had one school attended
since the age of five and no earlier life at all. Saved games keep the school
they started with.
