title: Mortgage rates since mid-2019 come from FHFA's own published series
---

The 30-year mortgage rate the economy opens with, from June 2019 on, now comes from the federal housing agency's published index instead of the excluded private survey. Rates differ by less than a third of a point, and a home bought with a mortgage pays that rate.
