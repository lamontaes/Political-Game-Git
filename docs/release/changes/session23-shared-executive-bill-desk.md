section: Changed
title: Council acts use the shared executive bill desk
---

Acts presented to a mayor now appear on the same governing desk as state and federal bills. Signing, returning, action deadlines and override forecasts use the shared executive functions, while council voting, publication and congressional review retain their existing procedure. Generated legislative profiles retain the day-count basis from their recorded veto-window table.
