title: At another official's town hall, a neighbor beside you asks whether you will speak.
---

When you go to an official's town hall, someone you know who is there too asks you something: whether to stand up and ask about the question the room took or the bill the hall was called for, whether the official's answer meant anything, whether you will speak, or whether you will come to the next one. You can agree, promise, ask what they make of it, or put it off, and what you agree to is kept.
