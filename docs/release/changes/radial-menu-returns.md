id: radial-menu-returns
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: The radial menu is back in place of the labeled bar along the bottom
---

The bottom bar of seven labeled sections is gone. Clicking your portrait in
the corner opens the radial menu again: Calendar, People, Politics, News,
Journal, Personal, Travel, Guide and options, Save and Return to title fan out
in rings above it, each a paper disc with a brass rim. A section with several
screens opens its own ring, with Back to return. On a narrow or short window
the same entries open as a framed list. Your portrait, name, the date and the
place stay in the corner beside the day controls.
