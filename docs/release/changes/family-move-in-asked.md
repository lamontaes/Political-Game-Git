title: When you have been dating someone a while, they ask to move in together, and your answer decides
---

Before, someone you were dating could never move in with you, and the two of you stayed dating forever. Now, when it is time, they ask you in a scene. Say yes and one of you moves in with the other; say no and you go on dating; ask for time and they will ask again later.
