title: You can be a couple with one person at a time
---

Before, a player already with somebody could ask a second person to be a
couple, hear yes, and end up with two partners, and was still offered more
dates. Now nobody in a couple is offered a date or a couple with anyone
else, nobody is offered one with a person who is with somebody, and the
existing couple stands until one of them ends it.
