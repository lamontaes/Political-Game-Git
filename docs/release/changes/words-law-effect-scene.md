section: Changed
title: When you work out what a law did to your household's bills, the scene says which law and how much.
---

The scene where you connect a change in what you pay to a law now names the law, by its bill's name or what it is about, and the amount: "The law on electricity and the grid cost your household $4.13." or "The law on transportation put $9.61 back in your household's hands."
