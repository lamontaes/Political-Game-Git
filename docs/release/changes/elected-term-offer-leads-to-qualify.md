impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A won governorship says to qualify, and takes you to the button
---

A player who won a governorship read "An offer of work as Governor is waiting
for your answer" on every screen, and the line opened Work, where there is no
way to accept it. The answer is "Qualify for the term" on Campaigns, and a
player who never found it lost the term on the day it began.

The line now says "You won the race for Governor. Qualify for the term under
Campaigns before it begins on …", and pressing it opens Campaigns with the
Qualify button on screen. Once qualified it stops asking and says when the term
begins. A term whose start date passed without a qualification is no longer
listed as waiting.
