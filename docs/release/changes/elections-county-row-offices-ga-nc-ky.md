title: Georgia, North Carolina and Kentucky counties elect the offices their law names.
---

A North Carolina county's ballot no longer lists a County treasurer or County assessor; the county commissioners appoint both. A Georgia county elects its Clerk of superior court under that name, and its sheriff, for four years. A Kentucky county elects its sheriff, county clerk, coroner and property valuation administrator for four years, as its constitution says.
