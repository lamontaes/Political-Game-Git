title: A protest in your town can put you on the spot.
---

When people in your town organize a protest or a strike over rent, wages, health or the air, an official who answers to the town is confronted by someone in it, and a friend in it can ask you to come along. Saying yes puts you in it, and your case goes to the town's officials.
