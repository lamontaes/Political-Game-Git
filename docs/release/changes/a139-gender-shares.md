title: The people the world invents now match the country's real mix of women, men and nonbinary people
---

People the world makes up are now about 50 percent women, 49 percent men and
1 percent nonbinary, following the national census count and a national
survey, instead of an even split with 4 percent nonbinary. People already in a
saved game keep who they are.
