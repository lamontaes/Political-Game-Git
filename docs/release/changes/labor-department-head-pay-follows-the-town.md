section: Changed
title: A department head's pay follows what the town's own college-educated workers earn.
---

An appointed department head was paid the same fixed hourly wage in every town for the whole game. The pay is now the median of what the town's private jobs held by college graduates pay that day, so it rises and falls with the town's labor market. A town with no such job keeps the estimate, which is registered as a stopgap.
