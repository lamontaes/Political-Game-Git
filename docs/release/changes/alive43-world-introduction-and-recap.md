title: Meet the world you start in, and catch up after time passes.
---

A new life now opens with a short look at who holds power: the White House,
Congress, your state's governor and your local government, drawn from the same
people you can later meet. When you skip ahead, a brief summary shows what
changed that you could know about, and people you talk to can bring up those
matters. Organizations and people you pin return you to them without replaying
the introduction.
