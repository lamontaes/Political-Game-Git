title: Price laws change each household's bill by who lives in it
---

A law on fares, mileage fees, public broadband or power now changes each household's monthly bill by its own use: how many of its people work and drive, how many adults it has, how many ride transit, how big its home is for its people, and whether it takes up broadband. Under a fare-free law, the riders it adds drive less, and their own miles and fuel spending fall.
