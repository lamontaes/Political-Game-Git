id: real-campaign-finance-regulators
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Campaign-money cases are heard by each state's real regulator
---

A case about a candidate's campaign money now goes before the body that
actually oversees campaign finance in that state, D.C. or Puerto Rico, such as
the Washington State Public Disclosure Commission or the Texas Ethics
Commission, instead of an invented name. How long each step takes and what it
fines are still simulated until researched.
