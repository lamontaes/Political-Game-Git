title: Members of Congress file and vote by their own principles
---

A member of Congress now files a bill on the federal question their own
principles press hardest, such as raising the minimum wage, forgiving student
loans or making the District of Columbia a state, and colleagues who lean the
same way sign on. Each House's roll call now divides: a bill that used to pass
432 to 0 now passes something like 341 to 91, or fails. Once a bill is law,
its answer is federal law, which outranks a state's. There are twenty federal
questions for now, one in each field of federal government.

A bill's steps in the news are now dated the day each one happened. Before,
a long stretch of time put a bill's House vote, its trip to the Senate and its
arrival on the President's desk on one date.
