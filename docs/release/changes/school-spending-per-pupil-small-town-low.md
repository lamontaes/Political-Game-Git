title: A town with no city government now has its whole local budget in its county
---

In an unincorporated town the county's budget held only the county's share of local spending, because the city's share belonged to a government that did not exist. Schools, police and the rest were about a tenth of what the place spends. The county now carries the whole local share where there is no city or township government.
