title: The clerk tells you how your petition came out
---

The morning after your petition is due, the clerk's office tells you whether the papers are in. When names came off, the clerk says why: people who moved away, died, were too young to sign or are barred from voting. If your petition fell short, you can say you will back someone else in the race, and that becomes your own endorsement, printed in the paper and heard by your circle.
