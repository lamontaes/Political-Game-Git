section: Changed
title: New standing people, and the creator dresses you in them
---

Everyone you meet is now drawn from a new set of standing figures, in everyday
clothes, work clothes or a blazer depending on who they are and where. The old
figures, some caught mid-gesture with their arms out, are gone. The character
creator now offers the same figures the game draws, chosen by build and
clothes, and the look you pick is the one you wear in every room.
