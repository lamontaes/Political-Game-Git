title: Cabinet members and officials drawn for a post open with savings and a household to keep.
---

A governor's cabinet members who live elsewhere, and officials drawn to staff a filing counter, opened with no money and no household costs until their first pay. They now open with the savings a family at their pay keeps and spend on their household each month. Places whose records say they hold no local filings, such as territories with no county or town governments, no longer get a filing clerk for races they do not hold.
