title: Crime falls where its causes point, not on a roll
---

Ordinary crime in a town no longer comes from a monthly roll. A town's offenses
fall on the people and homes that its residents' circumstances point at. That
means a neighbor who knows someone out of work with a past record, or a person
something has already happened to. Each offense comes on the day that pressure
builds to it, and the police log fills at a steady pace that the town's
conditions move up or down.
