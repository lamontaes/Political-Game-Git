title: County boards and Puerto Rico's municipal legislatures are seated
---

A life now opens with its county's board seated, as well as its town's
council. The board has the number of members its state's law sets:
California seats five supervisors, and Arizona and Nevada seat three, five or
seven by the county's population. Nine other states set a size of their own.
A state whose law has not been read yet seats seven members, the national
average. In Puerto Rico, each municipio is now a government, with a mayor and
a municipal legislature of 12, 14 or 16 members by population, 17 in San Juan
and 5 in Culebra, as the Municipal Code sets. A municipio also hires, as any
local government does.
