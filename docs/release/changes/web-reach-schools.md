title: A bigger schools budget raises graduation, class by class
---

When a mayor or council raises the schools line of the city or county budget, the town's graduation rate follows as classes pass through: each senior class gains in proportion to how many of its twelve school years had the higher spending, and children from low-income households gain most. A cut works the same way in the other direction.
