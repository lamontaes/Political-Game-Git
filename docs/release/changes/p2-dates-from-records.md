title: Rule dates follow their records and calendars
---

Governing sessions apply to the year recorded in their source calendar. Constitutional observations use their own source dates. State and local tax evidence keeps the date being assessed, and all callers supply that date. Federal withholding reads the paycheck year and marks uncompiled years as median estimates from the available IRS schedules.
