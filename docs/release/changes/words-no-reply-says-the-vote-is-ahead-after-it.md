title: After a vote, the official no longer tells the reporter the vote is still ahead.
---

On the reporter's call after a vote, the replies no longer include "I'm still reading it." or "They'll see when I vote."
After the vote, an official who would rather not talk yet says so plainly: "I'll explain it at the next meeting, in front of everybody."
