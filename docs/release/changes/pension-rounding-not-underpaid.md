---
id: pension-rounding-not-underpaid
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A small government paying its whole pension share is no longer recorded as underpaying
---

A town or small county that paid its whole pension share was recorded as
underpaying by a dollar or a few, because each month's payment rounds to a
whole dollar. The budget now counts a shortfall only when it is more than a
dollar a month.
