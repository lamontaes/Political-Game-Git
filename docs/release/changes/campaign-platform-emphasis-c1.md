title: Rival campaigns now talk about more than one issue
---

A rival's ads, mailers, nights at the doors, events and house parties used to be about one issue, the top of their platform, every time. Each now takes up the platform's questions in turn, by how much of the town stands behind each one, so a rival who runs on three issues is heard on all three, the one the town backs most the most often.
