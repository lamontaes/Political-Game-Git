section: Changed
title: Asked after a relative, people say what happened to them, by name and when.
---

When someone asks after one of your relatives, the answer comes from that relative's records: "Olivia took sick last week.", "Thomas passed away a few days ago.", "William moved over to Thayer County." or a new job by its employer. People now also mention relatives you have not met, by name, with where they live or work ("Have you ever met my partner, Jennifer? Works over at Wall's Market."). Once you have heard of them, the next talk asks after them by name. The old fixed lines ("He's in the hospital.", "My father passed away.", "I don't think so.") are gone.
