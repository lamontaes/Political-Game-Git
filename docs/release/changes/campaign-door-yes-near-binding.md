id: campaign-door-yes-near-binding
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A "yes" at the door now holds at the ballot.
---

When a resident tells a campaign at the door that they will vote for its candidate, the vote now holds them to it, unless something heavy they learned since changes their mind: a scandal story about the candidate, a rival they are closer to, or promises of the candidate's they heard and saw broken. Counted residents who answer a volunteer's or a rival's door are held the same way.
