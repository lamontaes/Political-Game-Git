title: Medicaid expansion covers named people, and a work requirement takes it away
---

Each month the World reads who holds Medicaid expansion coverage: adults aged 19 to 64 in a state whose law expands Medicaid, with household pay at or under 138% of the poverty line. Where a work requirement is in force, the state's own or the federal one from 1/1/2027, an adult who works fewer than 80 hours a month and is not exempt loses it. Covered people aged 55 to 64 face 9.4% lower death risk, from a year after the expansion took effect, and a repeal ends coverage at the next monthly pass.
