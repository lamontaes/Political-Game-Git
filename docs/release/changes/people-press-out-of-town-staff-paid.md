title: Reporters who live out of town get paid
---

A station's news director in the market city and a national outlet's reporters are now paid each month for the job they hold, with taxes taken out like any paycheck, from the outlet's audience and advertisers outside the town. Before, they paid rent and bills from their savings with no paycheck coming, and most ran out of money within the first year.
