title: The speech you build is planned like everyone else's
---

When you give a campaign speech you still pick one part at a time, but each part is now written the way a candidate's or a mayor's speech is: one subject, what you believe about it, and the ask for the vote. What you see on each choice is exactly what you say. The greeting and the close are also chosen from wordings written for your race. Parts about your job, home, children, school and your town's changes are being rewritten for the same planner and are not offered for now.
