section: Changed
title: A person's record shows whom they credit or blame, and who credited or blamed them
impact: patch
---

Opening somebody in People now lists the officials they told you they credit or blame, and, for an official, the people who told you they credit or blame them. Each row is a name, the position and the date it was told, and a name opens that person's record. Only what you were told appears, never what a person keeps to themselves.
