title: A bigger school budget no longer makes a senior who would have graduated fail
---

When a school year's graduation share rose, a senior who would have graduated could fail instead, because a heavier household ahead of them took the share first. Seniors now clear in order of standing and the walk stops at the first one the share does not cover, so more share never fails anyone.
