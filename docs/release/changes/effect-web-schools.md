title: Students' test scores and graduation follow the town they live in
---

Each spring every eighth grader in town gets a math and a reading result, and each June every senior graduates or does not. How many do comes from the place's real level moved by the laws and conditions that reach it, and who does comes from each student's own household income, attendance and study habits, so children from poorer households fall short more often. The town's graduation rate then feeds the things that depend on it, such as turnout and poverty.
