title: A town group against a law starts with the neighbor who founded it
---

When six people in a town lose the same right or the same eligibility to a
law, the group they form against it now starts with a member: the first of
them with the temper to stand up alongside the others. Until now such a group
could appear with nobody in it. A patient neighbor leaves the founding to
someone quicker to act, and the group's record says what the law took from
the person who founded it.
