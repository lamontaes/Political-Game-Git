title: The loading screen says the world ran two months on its own, and a lawmaker's vote can show the hole a bill leaves in their budget

The loading line now says the country, the state and the town ran two months on their own before your first evening. On a bill's page and in the Trace box, a member who votes against a tax cut can show "The hole it leaves in their budget" as the reason, and a sponsor asked for a vote hears it as "it would take money out of the budget they have to pass".
