title: Personality shapes jobs, borrowing, homes and businesses
---

How people handle money and work now bends to who they are, at sizes taken from research. Cautious and bold people, in the same circumstances, differ in whether they start looking for work, take an offer, borrow, buy a home, found a business or leave town, and the people not yet named in your town follow the same rules as the named ones. Households that can afford a down payment now weigh a home by the interest it costs over rent, not the whole mortgage payment.
