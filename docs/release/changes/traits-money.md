title: Personality shapes jobs, borrowing, homes and businesses
---

How people handle money and work now bends to who they are, at sizes taken from research. Cautious and bold people, in the same circumstances, differ in whether they start looking for work, take an offer, borrow, buy a home, found a business or leave town, and the people not yet named in your town follow the same rules as the named ones. Households that can afford a down payment now weigh a home by the interest it costs over rent, not the whole mortgage payment.

Whether to apply for a job, buy a home, take a draw from the business or start a business now rests on each person's own circumstances, so temperament can tip them: a seeker's push to apply eases the longer they have looked, a renter weighs the share of the down payment they are short, an owner weighs the business's cash against a month of everything it pays out, and starting a business sits within reach of a strong lean. A household behind on rent settles part of what it owes each month from the cash it holds beyond a month's living, as far as its temperament leans toward paying what is owed.
