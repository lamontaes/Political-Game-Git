title: Governors and legislators start their terms on the day their state's constitution or law sets.
---

Every state and territory now starts a new governor's term, and a new legislator's, on the day its own constitution or election law names: the first Monday in December for California's legislature, the fifth Tuesday after the vote for Kentucky's governor (Florida's, Hawaii's and Tennessee's legislatures start on election day itself, which the game still sets a day late). Before, every legislator and the governors of 24 places started on January 1. The party of 60 statewide officials at the start was checked against their offices' pages: Guam's attorney general is now elected without a party label, the Northern Mariana Islands' lieutenant governor at the start is an independent, and the Virgin Islands' boards of education and elections carry party labels.
