title: Going to a Saturday invitation you had not answered takes you there
---

In Lewiston, Jenna pressed to go to a Saturday afternoon at Connor Yoder's
home and was told "You have no way to get from Home to Connor Yoder's home
yet." Saying yes to the invitation books the short trip there, but going to it
straight from Places or the calendar skipped the yes, so there was no trip.
Going now counts as the yes: it books the trip, and the afternoon is spent at
the asker's home.
