title: When the paper reports a bribe, the friend who comes to ask you about it now speaks
---

When the paper printed a bribe and the person who came to ask about it was a close friend, the scene was set up and then never played, because the friend had no opening line for a printed story. The friend now asks, and so do the three other kinds of accuser (someone who was offered the money, someone who only heard about it, someone who knows it was taken), each in their own way.
