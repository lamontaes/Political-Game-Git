title: Talks keep to what happened, and family news is a scene
---

A conversation now opens on what happened lately to either person, keeps to that topic, and the other person closes it when it's done; nobody asks after someone absent unless something happened to them. When a parent loses their job, the family scene plays at home: they ask you to talk, tell you, and if you ask what happened, tell you why from the record (the place closed). A parent who quit for another job is not a lost job. When your household is evicted, the parent at home tells you. A member of a town body can try to win one colleague's vote before it is cast, and the vote reads the case they made.
