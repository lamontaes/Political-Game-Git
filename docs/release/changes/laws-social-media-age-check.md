title: A social media age check eases teenagers' stress a little
---

When a state or city puts a social media age check in force, a little fewer of the town's 13- to 17-year-olds are in a depressive episode a few months later, by an estimate from the nearest research that could also turn out to be nothing, and each teenager's resting stress eases by that much.
