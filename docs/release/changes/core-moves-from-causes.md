section: Changed
title: People move away on their own day, and officeholders stay for their seat
---

People no longer all decide to move away on the first of the month. Each person weighs leaving on the day their reasons add up to it, or the day after something happens to them: a lost job, an eviction, a death at home or a relative leaving. Holding office is the strongest tie to a town, and an officeholder leaves only when something like that happens. A town in Delaware no longer stops on its first visitor payment, and a paid-off mortgage no longer stops a town whose owner had moved away.
