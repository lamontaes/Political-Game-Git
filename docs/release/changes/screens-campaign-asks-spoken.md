impact: patch
section: Changed
title: People answer your volunteer and endorsement asks in their own words.
---

When you ask people you know to volunteer or to endorse you, each one now answers in a spoken line of their own, worded by their character and by how close they are to you, with their name shown as the speaker. The old narrated answers ("Wendy Ford signs on to help the campaign.") are gone.
