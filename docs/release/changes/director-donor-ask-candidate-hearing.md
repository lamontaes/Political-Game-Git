title: A donor's ask of a candidate is a hearing, not a promise, and being heard warms the tie
---

A candidate with no office is asked by a donor who gave to their campaign for a hearing. The donor no longer speaks of a scheduler, staff, a session, a vote, a weekday or the dollar figure. The candidate can hear the donor out, which warms the tie a little, or put the donor off, which strains it. The old lines that only an officeholder could say are out of the game until a played officeholder ask shows them.
