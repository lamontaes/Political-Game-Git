title: A donor's ask of a candidate is a hearing, not a promise, and being heard warms the tie
---

A candidate with no office is asked by a donor who gave to their campaign for a hearing. The donor says what they care about once the candidate agrees to hear them, and no longer speaks of a scheduler, staff, a session or a vote. The candidate can hear the donor out, which warms the tie a little, or put the donor off, which strains it. An officeholder's donor ask is unchanged.
