title: A resident who read that a law took effect now stops you to talk about it.
---

When someone read that a law took effect and stopped you in the street, the scene used to be skipped whenever the law had no local bill number, a state law for instance. The lines for a law in force could never be said. Now the scene knows the law is still in force, from the laws engine, and the resident says so ("Shift highway funds to transit took effect, and I'm the one living with it.").
