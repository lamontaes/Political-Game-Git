title: A new life's family leans toward the national two-parent share when the town has recorded few families
---

A new life still copies one of the families its own world recorded, but when the
world has recorded only a handful, two-parent and one-parent families are weighed
so the share moves toward the national figure (about 71 percent of children live
with two parents) instead of swinging from none to nearly all.
