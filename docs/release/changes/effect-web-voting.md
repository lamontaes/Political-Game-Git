title: Voting turnout follows the town's conditions
---

How many people turn out to vote now moves with what the research finds for the town: a right-to-work law, an independent redistricting commission and a rise in graduation each shift every voter's chance of voting, and the ballot keeps the record that moved it. Election laws such as photo ID, same-day registration and mail voting keep acting through the voting cost alone, so none of them counts twice.
