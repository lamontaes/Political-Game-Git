title: A phased minimum wage's cost note counts every step
---

A minimum-wage bill raised in dated steps now shows what each step adds to the pay of the jobs under it, and the full cost once the last step takes effect. Before, the bill's cost note counted only the first step, so the cost colleagues and employers weighed was a fraction of the real one. Employers' stake in the bill now counts every step too.
