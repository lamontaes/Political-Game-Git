title: The state's election chief and others made for a role have a past
---

People the game creates to fill a role outside the town, such as the state's election chief, now have a birth date, a birthplace and the earlier work that led to their post when you open their card, marked as estimates. Station and national news staff who live outside the town still have only their schooling.
