title: People remember the elections they lost, the jobs they took and left after the game began, and what happened to relatives living far away.
---

A person's card now says when they ran for an office and lost the race, when they took a new job, and when they quit, retired or lost the job because the business closed. A relative who lives in another state now has more on their card than the year they were born.
