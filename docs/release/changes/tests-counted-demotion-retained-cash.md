title: Retirees back in the wider population are paid as counted
---

A retiree whose household has gone back into the town's wider population after a long while out of the player's life no longer has their Social Security award paid to them as if they were still a full person. Their award, whether claimed before or after they went back, is paid into their neighborhood's pooled income, and it is paid to them again once the player meets them.
