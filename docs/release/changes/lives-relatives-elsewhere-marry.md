title: Relatives who live elsewhere now meet someone, marry and sometimes split up, and you hear about it.
---

Your single relatives in other towns used to stay single forever, and married ones stayed married. Now a single relative may meet someone and marry, sooner or later by their age, their temperament and how often people their age marry where they live. A couple may split up, at the rates real marriages end. The new partner moves in with them, and your family in town hears about the wedding or the breakup.
