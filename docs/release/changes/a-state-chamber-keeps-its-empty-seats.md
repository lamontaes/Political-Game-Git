title: A state chamber's roster keeps the seats of members who have died
---

The State screen listed only the living members of the home state's
legislature, so after years of play a chamber looked smaller than it is:
Maine's House showed 103 seats instead of 151. Every seat is now listed. A
seat whose member has died reads "Vacant since" the date of death. The game
does not hold state legislative elections yet, so no one has filled it.
