title: Every town can say how the place is doing
---

"The place you live" used to be blank almost everywhere. Outside one city, the
panel that reports how a town's area is doing was simply missing from Money and
property, with nothing said about why.

It now appears wherever you live. It finds the county areas your town sits in
and reports on those, and it is careful about the difference: a town inside a
county is described as sitting in it, not as being it. Living in a county
itself still reads as your own jurisdiction.

Where there is genuinely nothing to show, the screen says so and says why,
instead of leaving a gap. That includes the case you will see on an early date:
the figures for your area exist and are being held back until the game can show
that they had been published, and it now tells you how many are waiting rather
than claiming nothing has been published at all.
