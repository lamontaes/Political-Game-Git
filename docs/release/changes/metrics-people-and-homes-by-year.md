id: metrics-people-and-homes-by-year
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Metrics shows how many people and households live in your town, how many rentals stand empty and how many people have no home, each with last year beside it.
---

Your town's Metrics now lists the people in town (the people you can meet plus everyone else who lives there), the households, the rental homes, the rentals standing empty and the people without a home. Each comes from the part of the game that already counts it, is kept once a month, and shows last year's December figure beside it after your first year.
