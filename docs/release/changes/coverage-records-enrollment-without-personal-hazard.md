id: coverage-records-enrollment-without-personal-hazard
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: Coverage records enrollment without assigning a population mortality effect to each person
---

Gaining or losing Medicaid expansion coverage still records the person's eligibility and enrollment. It no longer applies a population-level mortality finding to that person's death strain or reschedules a fatal illness during the monthly coverage pass. Existing saves retain their coverage records.
