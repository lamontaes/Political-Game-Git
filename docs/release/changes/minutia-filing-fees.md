impact: patch
section: Changed
title: Candidates no longer pay filing fees
---

Running for office no longer costs a filing fee, for any seat at any level: town, county, state or federal. Whether someone can afford the fee no longer weighs on whether they run, nothing is charged when they file, and places that took petition signatures only in place of the fee no longer ask for them. Places where a petition is the only way onto the ballot still take one.
