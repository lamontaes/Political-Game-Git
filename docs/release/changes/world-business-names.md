section: Changed
title: Businesses carry names Americans would recognize
impact: patch
---

A town's shops and firms are named the way their real counterparts are: a local family's market, a county machine works, the town's diner. No business is called a general store anymore. A new business takes the first name of its kind that no other business in town has, and a business that opens another location keeps its name, so chains grow from local names.
