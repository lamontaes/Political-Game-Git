title: A research question asks which cities and counties set their own minimum wage.
---

The research queue now holds a question about the cities and counties that set
their own minimum wage: which ones, at what rate today, and on what schedule the
rate rises. Until it is answered, a job's pay floor is the higher of the federal
and state rate, and the game starts from the most common real rule, no local
minimum, and marks it as estimated from the average.
