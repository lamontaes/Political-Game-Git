title: A council petition asks the signatures your state's law asks of a town your size.
---

A small Iowa town now asks 10 signatures (2% of its voters, minimum 10), a large North Dakota city 300 (10%, maximum 300), and an Ohio village 10 where a city asks 50. Before, every town's petition was held to the same 10% of its voters.
