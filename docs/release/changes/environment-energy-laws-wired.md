title: Clean-electricity standards, carbon prices and bottle deposits now change the world
---

A state that adopts a clean-electricity standard now sees its average price of electricity rise about 11% seven years later, and a repeal takes the rise back. A state that prices carbon lowers its carbon dioxide from energy per person about 3% a year later. A container deposit cuts litter about 45% within a year and raises the state's consumer price level about 0.05% within three months, through the price of covered drinks. Each state's electricity price and emissions start at its real 2024 figures.
