title: A new life records its U.S. House district when its town lies in only one.
---

A new life in a town the Census Bureau lists with only one congressional district, such as Elko, Nevada or Lewiston, Maine, now starts with that district recorded as its home district, and the Government screen names its Representative. A city split between districts, such as Peoria or San Antonio, is left unresolved: the map and the Government screen name only the districts the city touches, and the opening no longer presents Texas's 1st district's member as a San Antonio life's own. Lives started before this change keep the history they were started with.
