title: A grade on part of a line teaches every line like it
---

The owner's grades now fall on parts of a line as well as whole lines, with a strength, and build a map of what reads right for each kind of speaker and each kind of scene. Every line in the game is scored against that map, so lines that share a graded part come up more or less often everywhere they play.
