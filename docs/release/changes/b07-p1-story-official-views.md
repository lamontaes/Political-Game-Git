title: Recorded story knowledge informs a reader's view of an official
---

Accurate knowledge of a published story uses its attributed official acts and
the reader's policy belief at the knowledge cutoff to form an official view
through the existing belief writer. Nonreaders and controlled people retain
their views. Stories without recorded policy attribution create no inferred
view.
