title: A bill's Compare view says what the law in force says, and a rate reads the same everywhere on the paper
---

The struck side of a drafted bill's Compare view now reads as the law in force ("the property tax rate is 0.28 percent") instead of "raised from 0.284 percent to 0.284 percent", and a rate in the bill's text reads to at most two decimals, as the card and the estimate already say it. Where two rates would read the same at two decimals, both show more.
