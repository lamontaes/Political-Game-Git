title: A couple at home can talk about having or adopting a child
---

Talking with your partner at home now offers "Talk about having a child together" and "Talk about adopting a child together". Your partner takes a couple of days to think it over and answers from their own temperament. A yes leads, on its own later day, to a birth or an adoption through the game's existing family records. Nothing forms a couple in play yet, so this appears only for a partnership that already exists.
