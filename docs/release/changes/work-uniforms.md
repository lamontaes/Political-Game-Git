id: work-uniforms
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Nurses and police officers wear their uniforms
---

People now wear their uniforms at work. Nurses, nursing assistants and medical
assistants wear scrubs, police officers wear their uniform, and construction
and quarry workers wear a safety vest. Their own portraits show the uniform
too, so you can tell what someone does. At home and around the neighborhood
they wear their own clothes, and a police officer on duty in a chamber or a
courtroom stays in uniform.
