title: The People web marks who came closer this year and who drifted away
---

The People web marks the people who joined your five closest in the last year and those who left it, and names why on selecting them: a visit, a move away, a death or no time together lately, each read from the tie's record. When your five closest are the same as a year ago, it says so.
