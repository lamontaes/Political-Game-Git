title: Paid public programs retain their law and payment records in local budgets.
---

State and local budget months retain the saved appropriation, commitment,
installment and actual payment behind a public-program cost. The existing
federal reader supplies the same validated chain for every government. Cash
amounts and payment schedules stay on their original records; an unpaid
appropriation creates no expense.
