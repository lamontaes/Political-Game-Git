title: A town that opens above or below the nation's unemployment rate keeps near it
---

A town that opened with a high share of people out of work saw it fall to the nation's level within months, and one that opened low drifted up, with nothing causing either. Layoffs now run at the rate that holds each place near the rate it opened at, and a place that opens at the nation's rate is unchanged.
