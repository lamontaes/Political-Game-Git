title: A 17-year-old's high school shows on Who you are and Jobs and study.
---

Teenagers now have the high school (and, for younger ones, the middle school) they started after 2020, so a 17-year-old sees their high school at the top of their schools. School names from the national directory are written out in full: "Anson H S" and "Anson El" now read "Anson High School" and "Anson Elementary School".
