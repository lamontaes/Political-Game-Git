title: New Game no longer starts you in Kentucky
---

A new life now asks you to choose a state, then a town in that state. Nothing is selected for you. After you pick a town, searching again or choosing Change lets you pick a different town in that state without dropping the first one until you choose the new one. Changing states still clears a town that does not belong there.
