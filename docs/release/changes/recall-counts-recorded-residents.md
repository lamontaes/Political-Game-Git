title: Recall counts use residents' recorded views
---

Existing qualified recalls count living adult residents' saved support and
opposition views without drawing or extrapolating turnout. Counts retain their
source records and exact zeros. An election with no recorded views waits for
evidence. Petition qualification waits for valid signature records and the
concrete electorate base required by its existing legal threshold; a private
opinion is not treated as a signature.
