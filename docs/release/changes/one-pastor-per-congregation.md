title: A congregation has one pastor
---

When a town's jobs were filled, a resident drawn to work at a church could be
made its pastor even though the church already had one, so Frankfort, Kentucky
opened with five pastors for four congregations. The pastor of each
congregation, like the principal of each school, is still hired first, one to
each, and the other church jobs no longer add a second.
