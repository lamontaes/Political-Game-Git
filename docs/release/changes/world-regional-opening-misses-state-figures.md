title: Every state opens at its own published average hourly pay.

A state's regions took their pay from the state's pay moved by each region's income, and those moves did not average out, because a metro's income takes in neighboring states. Virginia opened at $38.24 an hour against its published $35.35, and Hawaii at $36.63 against $37.29. Every state now opens at its published pay, and its regions still differ by their incomes.
