title: The evening news on opening night names the area whose unemployment rate it gives
---

The opening night's jobs story gave the metro or state-wide unemployment rate under the town's name ("Philipsburg unemployment at 2.8"). It now names the area the rate belongs to (for example "Montana outside the Billings and Missoula areas").
