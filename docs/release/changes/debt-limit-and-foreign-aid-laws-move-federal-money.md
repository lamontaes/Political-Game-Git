title: Laws on the debt limit and foreign aid change the federal deficit, what states pay to borrow and the aid in local budgets.
---

When Congress makes itself cut spending before it raises the debt limit, federal
spending falls by about 2% a year, the size of the last deal that did this. Every
state, county and city budget then gets that much less federal aid, and because
the federal deficit is smaller, every state pays less to borrow. When Congress
spends more on foreign aid, the deficit grows and what states pay to borrow
rises by a very small amount, because aid abroad has almost no measured effect on
places at home. A later law answering no ends either change the day it takes effect.
