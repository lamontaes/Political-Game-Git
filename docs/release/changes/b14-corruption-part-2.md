impact: minor
section: Added
title: Open corruption leads only from saved authority and acts
---

Corruption opening checks now require evidence for the authority behind an
appointment or public purchase. A personal appointment opens the patronage
lead only when its saved decision is personal and the saved post is classified
under civil-service rules. Contract leads remain closed until a canonical paid
purchase and award identify the business, official, and that official's
authority and personal interest. Existing program commitments do not stand in
for purchases. These checks prepare opening facts for the existing misconduct
record writer and do not create a second record flow.
