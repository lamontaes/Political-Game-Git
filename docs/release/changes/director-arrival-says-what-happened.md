id: director-arrival-says-what-happened
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: A scene's first line now says what it is about, instead of "what happened yesterday".
---

Scenes used to open with "you stay in, thinking about what happened on January 21st" or "talk over what happened yesterday" without ever saying what that was, often when nothing had happened the day before. Now a scene's first line names what it is about when its cause is a bill, a law or a charge ("you meet Kay Thompson downtown to talk over the Missing Middle Housing Ordinance"), and opens plainly when it is not. The date of the cause is no longer said in the first line, which already opens with today's date. Six openers that spoke for nobody (a call, a letter or a neighbor with no name, and the town all talking about "the news") were removed.
