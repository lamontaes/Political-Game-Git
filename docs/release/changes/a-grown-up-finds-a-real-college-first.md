title: The Study tab opens on real colleges, and a degree can be applied for once
---

The Study tab under Jobs and study now opens on the college finder, so an eighteen-year-old sees real colleges first instead of a long scroll of the game's own programs. The status line names the school a student attends. Study terms, tuition notices and the journal speak plainly, with dates written out and amounts like "$3,840", and the directory's "Certifiicate" misspelling is corrected. Applying again for a degree you are already studying, or already hold an offer for, is refused with the reason shown.
