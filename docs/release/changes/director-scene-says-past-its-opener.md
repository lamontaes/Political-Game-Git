title: A housemate no longer says "I need to talk to you about something" and then nothing.
---

A death, an illness, a lost job or an eviction opened a talk at home with "I need to talk to you about something." even when the person had nothing to tell, for instance about a constituent you met who died and whom they never knew. Now they say it only when they have that news to tell, and a scene that would say nothing past its first line goes to the Journal instead of stopping your day.
