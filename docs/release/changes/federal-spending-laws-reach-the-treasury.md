title: Federal laws on foreign aid, the debt limit, farm subsidies and defense now move the federal books
---

Four federal spending laws now change what the government spends each month.
More foreign aid raises International Affairs spending by about 6.7%. Cuts
that pay for a higher debt limit take $150 billion a year from every program
but defense and interest. A farm subsidy cut saves about $3.9 billion a year.
A defense build-up grows defense spending each year it stays in force, for at
most five years. The government borrows less, or more, to match.
