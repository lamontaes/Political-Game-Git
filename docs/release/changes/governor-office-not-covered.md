---
id: governor-office-not-covered
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A governor's decisions are no longer covered by an Office work card
---

Once a player took office as governor, an "Office work" card was drawn over
the decisions waiting in Your office, and the office's programs, staff and
casework floated over them too. Both now sit in the page below the decisions,
so every choice a governor is asked to make can be read and pressed.
