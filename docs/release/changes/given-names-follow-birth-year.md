id: given-names-follow-birth-year
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: People are named for the year they were born
---

Every generated person drew a name from one list made mostly of 1990s names,
so someone born in the 1950s could come out named Aiden. Names now
follow the year a person was born. Most people take a name from the Social
Security Administration's top 100 for their own sex in the decades around their
birth year, weighted by how many babies really got that name. A birth year
between two decades draws from both, so there is no sudden change at 1950. The
rest keep a name from the full list, so an uncommon name is still possible.

In a Columbus, Ohio test world, the share of people born from 1935 to 1964
whose name was in their own decade's top 100 went from 17% to 67%. How many
people take a decade name is set by the game, not measured: the real figure is
how many births each decade's top 100 accounts for, which is not compiled yet.
It applies to the people a new life starts with and to the members of Congress
in a new world. Saved games keep their names.
