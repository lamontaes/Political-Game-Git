title: Election laws reach voters and cost money
---

Mail voting, automatic registration, same-day registration and photo ID now reach every voter in town, named or counted, and the county or city that runs the election pays for ballots, registrations and IDs out of its administration budget. A raise in the administration line hires real city staff, and a cut lays them off. Term limits and the lobbying ban are applied when someone files or lobbies, home rule is counted when a council drafts a bill, and a commission draws the ward lines where independent redistricting is law.
