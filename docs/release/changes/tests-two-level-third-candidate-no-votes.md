title: A state race already past its primary holds that primary, so November has one candidate a party
---

A statewide race whose filing and primary fell before the game began now holds its primary on the first day, so the November ballot no longer carries two candidates of one party, and a party with a candidate of its own from the town is no longer given a second, stand-in nominee who then got no votes.
