section: Changed
title: Names on the ballot follow each place's ballot-order law
---

The order of names on the ballot now follows the law of the place: alphabetical, a drawn alphabet, a drawing for each race, rotation from precinct to precinct, by party, or the incumbent first. Voters who know nothing else about the candidates lean toward the name listed first on the ballot they hold, so where the law rotates names, those votes split across precincts.
