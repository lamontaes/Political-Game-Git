title: Newcomers now arrive in towns in step with the people who live there, and every part of a state's people can move away.
---

In most states the wider population around a town never moved away, while newcomers kept arriving in it, and a town made mostly of named people got too few newcomers. Newcomers now settle in proportion to everyone who lives in a place at their age, and every part of the state's people weighs the same moves.
