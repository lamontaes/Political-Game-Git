title: Your town no longer empties out: newcomers arrive in their share, as leavers go.
---

A played town lost 4 to 6 percent of its residents a year to moving, because newcomers to the area were sent to one place at a time by chance, and a large neighboring place blocked the rest. Newcomers now arrive in each place by its share of the people their age, the same way leavers go, so a town's arrivals match what is due to it within a household.
