impact: patch
section: Changed
title: The day screen says nothing when nothing is happening
---

The day screen no longer fills a quiet moment with a sentence such as "It's a quiet day. Nothing is happening right now." or "Next: ...". Its Now block appears only when a scene, a meeting or a finished activity is there to describe, and its Next block only when a commitment is on the calendar. The unused sentence that opened the ordinary day, such as "Monday in Hiko, Nevada. Nothing is waiting on you.", is deleted with the code that wrote it. This follows the owner's rule from grading English batch 2: the day screen is a picture of the calendar, and words appear only when something actually happened.
