title: Travel shows where politics happens, and says why each place is listed
---

Travel now lists your town's or county's office when you have business there: you hold a seat on its board, or its board meets this week. Every place in Travel says why it is listed: you work there, your family does, it is your household's school, people you know work there, or it is open to the public.
