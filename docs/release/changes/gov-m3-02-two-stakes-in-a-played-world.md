title: Your office remembers where colleagues stand on your bill
---

When you talk to a colleague about your bill, or your aide briefs you on it, the office now keeps where each colleague stands and how you learned it. Your bill's list of colleagues reads it back: for, against or undecided, from a talk or from the record.
