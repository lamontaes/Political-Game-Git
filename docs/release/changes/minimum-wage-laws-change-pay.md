title: A minimum-wage law now raises the pay of every job below the new floor, and a first job starts at the state minimum.
---

When a federal minimum-wage raise takes effect, every job in town paid below the
new floor is raised to it from the first pay period that begins on or after that
day, and each raise is written down as a change of pay terms that names the law.
The floor where a job is stands at the higher of the federal rate, the state's
own rate and any state law passed in play, so a Tennessee job below the federal
floor rises with the raise while a Washington job that already pays more does
not. A teenager's first job now starts at the state's minimum wage for that
date, where it used to start at $7.25 everywhere. Jobs found through the job
market are raised the same way, and listings never start below the floor. A law
that lowers the floor cuts no one's pay. City and county minimums are not on file
yet, so none is claimed.

Until the level of a passed federal raise is researched, it takes the rate the
outcome links are calibrated for, $15.00 an hour (marked PLACEHOLDER in the
code).
