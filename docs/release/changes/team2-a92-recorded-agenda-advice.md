title: A chief's agenda advice comes from recorded views
---

Chiefs of staff now advise an agenda priority from their recorded principles and
the policy answers the program bank actually declares. The officeholder weighs
that advice with their own views, and both the advice and the decision are saved.
An unsupported or tied choice stays open instead of being picked at random.

The agenda and budget show every subject in the existing program bank, so a
random shortlist cannot hide a supported priority. Player choices remain explicit.
