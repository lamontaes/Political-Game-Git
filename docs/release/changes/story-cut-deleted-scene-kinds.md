title: Sixty scenes whose lines were not written from your world are gone.
---

Scenes no longer play unless their lines come from what actually happened in your world. Gone are the scenes at home and in the family (the eviction notice, a damaged home, a move-in, a proposal, a breakup, a new baby, a funeral, a diploma, a letter, a homecoming, a hospital visit and the rest), the ones with friends and neighbors, and the officeholder's scenes at the desk, in office hours, at a town hall, on the floor, in a hearing or a study, the swearing-in, the recall and the concession call. Filing, the doors, the campaign's asks, election night and the scenes kept from your records still play; the steps that lost a scene are done from the menus.
