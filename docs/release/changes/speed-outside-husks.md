section: Changed
title: Other states' legislators are built when something opens them
---

A game no longer builds roughly 6,700 legislators from other states as full people in its first days. They are recorded by name and seat, vote and pass laws as before, and become a full person, the same one every time, when you meet or open them. A year in a small town takes far less time.
