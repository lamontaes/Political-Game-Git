impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A district campaign's ads and mailers reach a small town in proportion to its share of the race
---

A candidate for an office the town is only part of spreads their ad and mail money over the whole district, so the town's outlet sells them, and the town's mailboxes receive, the town's share. Races decided in the town itself are unchanged.


A paper in a small town no longer interviews every candidate for the U.S. Senate or House each week; it asks them in proportion to the town's share of that race's voters.
