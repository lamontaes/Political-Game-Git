## Changed

### A district campaign's ads and mailers reach a small town in proportion to its share of the race

A candidate for an office the town is only part of spreads their ad and mail money over the whole district, so the town's outlet sells them, and the town's mailboxes receive, the town's share. Races decided in the town itself are unchanged.
