title: A new character's family no longer shares their birthday
---

Every parent, brother, sister, housemate and classmate a new game wrote for the
character was born on the character's own birthday, a whole number of years
apart. Now each has a birthday of their own. Brothers and sisters are still two
to four years apart.

This applies to new games. A game already in progress keeps the family it has.
