title: A damaged home is talked over at home
---

When a disaster damages your home, the day its aid is paid someone who lives with you sits you down: what the repairs cost, what the aid came to and what you still owe, as people round a sum. You can promise to see the repairs through, ask how bad it is, put in your savings or put it off, and what you promise is kept.
