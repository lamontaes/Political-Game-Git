id: no-source-panels-on-player-screens

title: No player screen offers a panel of sources
section: Changed
---

Four player screens offered a panel of sources. They no longer do.

The career panel's "Occupation source context" showed an occupation code, a
record id and a publisher attribution; what a player wanted from it — what the
work pays nationally — stays, as a plain sentence. The constitutional
workspace's procedure notes keep every word about how an amendment works and
lose the three links to the authorities behind them. The nationwide candidacy
screen no longer attributes government names to a listing, and no longer prints
the statute citations behind a refusal; the refusal's own sentence is the whole
of what a player is told. The party-life panel no longer lists the citations
behind what a host said about running for office.

The provenance itself is untouched and stays on the records, where a reviewer
reads it.
