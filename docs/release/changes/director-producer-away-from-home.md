title: When an evening sitting keeps an officeholder from home, someone they live with brings it up
---

On the evening a council, board or other body the player sits on meets past the hour it meets at night, someone the player lives with can bring up the time the office takes, at home, and the player answers. The scene comes no more often than other scenes from circumstance, and the person is the one in the household closest to the player.
