title: A state law on the teacher salary floor raises teachers' paychecks
---

When a state passes a law setting a minimum salary for teachers, public school
teachers paid below it now get a raise from the first pay period of the next
school year, and each raise names the law. Teachers hired while the floor is in
force start at least at the floor. Private school teachers are not covered. A
later law that ends the floor stops it for pay set from then on and cuts nobody's
pay. In Appomattox, Virginia, a floor of $53,096 raised all three public school
teachers, from about $1,892 to $2,042 a paycheck.
