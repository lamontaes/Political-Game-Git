title: The People screen says who kept away from you, and a new count says how far your support moved
---

When somebody keeps their distance after a case against you becomes public,
the People list and their card now say so from your side: "Carol has kept
away from you since September 3, after the case against you became public."
Before, it showed only in the case record and, in other words, deep in their
card. Any other falling-out that is the latest thing between you shows as
"Things have been strained with ... since ...".

On the campaign screen, a new count now says how far it moved since the one
before, for example "Down 3.0 points since the count on May 2, 2027." Before,
a finding's cost to your support never showed anywhere, and in the Alaska
race the candidate still won 99 to 1.
