title: An official's own sense of duty decides what their staff does with a case
---

When a resident brings a problem to an official with staff, the official's own temperament now decides whether the office helps, and the staffer carries that out as far as their own work allows. Whether someone brings a problem at all, tips a reporter, reports a crime, signs a recall or votes in one now follows their personality at each decision's own weight.
