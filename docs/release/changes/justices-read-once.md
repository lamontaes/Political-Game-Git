id: justices-read-once
impact: patch
section: Improved
title: Official funerals stay quick in later game years
---

When an official dies, the funeral looks for the colleagues who served with
them, and asks every person in the world which offices they hold. Each of
those questions read every Supreme Court seat again. The justices are now read
once for each world, with the other office holders. In South Fork,
Pennsylvania, the tenth game year takes 13.3 seconds less. The world after
each of ten years is exactly the same as before.
