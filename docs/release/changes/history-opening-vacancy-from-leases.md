title: A town's empty rentals on day one come from its own renters' moves.
---

Every town used to open with the country's rental vacancy. Now a town's empty rentals on day one are the leases its renters signed in the year before, each leaving a home empty for a few months, so a town whose renters move often opens with more homes for rent. Renters move more often than owners before day one, as they do in life.
