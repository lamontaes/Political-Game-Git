id: vote-ask-second-round
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: When a colleague presses you for your vote, your second answer says something new.
---

After a colleague pleads, presses or offers a trade for your vote on a bill, your replies no longer repeat your first answer. You can say yes with a condition, give the reason for your no, ask for the next draft or a word from the sponsor, sit the vote out, or end the talk politely.
