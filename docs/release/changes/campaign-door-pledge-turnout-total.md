title: A yes at the door becomes a vote
---

People who tell a campaign "yes" at their door now turn out the way people who say they'll vote really do: about three in five of them vote in a town race, against about one in four of their neighbors. Before, a promise at the door barely moved whether someone showed up, so the doors you knocked won you fewer votes than they should have. It works the same for your rivals' doors.
