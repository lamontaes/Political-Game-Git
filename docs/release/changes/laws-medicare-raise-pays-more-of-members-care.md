title: A federal Medicare raise now pays more of members' care in town, and the bill shows who it reached
---

When Congress raises Medicare, Medicare members in town now pay a smaller share of their medical bills, the federal treasury pays their providers the difference, and the law's page and dev mode's bill card name them. Dev mode's bill card now shows any budget bill's lines and who they reached; before, it said such a bill made no law.
