id: employers-hire-by-the-record
impact: minor
section: Changed
title: Employers list work when it comes open and hire the best-placed applicant
---

A town employer no longer lists a job on a weekly chance, and no longer says
yes or no to an application by chance. It lists work when somebody doing it
has left, when the business is new, and otherwise as often as work that many
people hold comes open nationwide: about 3.3% of jobs a month. A town
government's staff is taken from the national average of about 20 for every
1,000 residents.

When more than one person applies, the employer offers the job to whoever has
spent longer in the same line of work, then to whoever somebody who works
there put forward, then to whoever applied first. The others are told the
employer chose another applicant, which is now always true. A lone applicant
is offered the job.

An offer pays what the employer pays for the work now, a little more when its
last listing went unfilled, for the hours the people doing it work. A
missed start gets a call back when somebody vouched for the applicant or
nobody else applied.
