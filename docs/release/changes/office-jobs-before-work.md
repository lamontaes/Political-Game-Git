---
id: office-jobs-before-work
section: Fixed
title: An office job starts and ends before the day's work, never after a shift was worked
impact: patch
---

A person who takes a local seat, or whose term ends, now changes jobs at the start of the next day instead of after that day's shift was already worked. A full-time seat that replaces a holder's own job no longer ends a job they worked that same day, and the one-day wait added for ending such a job is gone.
