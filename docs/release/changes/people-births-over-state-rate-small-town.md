title: Couples without savings or steady income have fewer babies
---

Couples weigh their money more when deciding whether to have a child: a couple with no savings or no job now leans against it, and living together no longer counts in favor of every couple. The savings and partnership terms now weigh the couple's circumstances rather than always adding a push toward having a child.
