id: ban-the-box-reaches-employers
impact: patch
section: Fixed
title: A law that bans asking public job applicants about a conviction now shows which public employers it covers the day it takes effect.
---

Before, the law showed no one reached until a hire was made under it. Now each public employer in the town that has staff is on its record the day the law takes effect, and the record ends when the law is repealed.
