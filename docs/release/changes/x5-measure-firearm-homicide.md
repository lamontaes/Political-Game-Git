title: Each state now tracks firearm homicides, and concealed-carry permit laws move them
---

Every state and D.C. now keeps a firearm homicide rate, estimated from its 2024
homicide rate and the national share of homicides committed with a gun. Requiring a
permit to carry a concealed gun lowers it about 8% once the law is in force, and
dropping a permit requirement a state began with raises it by about the same.
