title: More state chambers can consider federal amendments
---

Six more states can record their members' federal amendment votes under cited
chamber rules. Elected-member and voting-member majorities remain distinct, and
explicit vote floors remain required. States without an admitted rule or an
implemented legal condition continue to await that evidence.

Direct rules and cited bridges both require valid source quotes, HTTPS links,
calendar dates and vote fractions before a chamber can use them.
