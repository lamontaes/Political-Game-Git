title: Every Issues subject can be reached

Politics → Issues used to lay its fourteen subjects of law cards in columns that ran off the right of the panel, so most subjects could not be seen. The subjects now sit on a rail; pick one and its laws appear as cards on a plate, with arrows when there are more, and the search narrows both as you type. A law's own page now shows what is in force at each level on plates, with what is coming, what changed and how members voted beside it.
