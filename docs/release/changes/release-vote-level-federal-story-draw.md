title: A committee hearing reaches the news as the story of its bill
---

A committee hearing in your town's state or in Congress now reaches the news
as what it is: a story about the bill the committee took up, naming the
members on its roll call. Before, a hearing was filed as a bare
"committee-hearing" record about nothing in particular that named nobody, so
no newsroom's desk ever picked it over stories its readers actually appear in.
The national outlets' coverage of the nation's business is now checked from
the record to the printed story, in whatever place a game draws.
