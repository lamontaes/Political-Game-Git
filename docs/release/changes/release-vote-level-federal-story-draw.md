title: A committee hearing is filed as the story of its bill, naming its members
---

A committee hearing in your state's legislature or in Congress is now filed for
the newsroom as what it is: a story about what its bill changes, naming the
members on the committee's roll call. Before, a hearing was filed as a bare
"committee-hearing" record about nothing in particular that named nobody, so a
desk had nothing to weigh it by. Whether a hearing makes the paper is still the
desk's call against the week's other news.
