title: Prices, drug costs and older workers start from real levels
---

Each state now starts at its real price level against the national average
(California about 11 percent above, Arkansas about 13 percent below), its real
average yearly Medicare drug spending out of pocket (Alaska $233, Alabama
$378), and its real share of people 62 to 66 who are working (Alabama about 39
percent, New York about 46 percent). A tariff, a drug price law or a higher
retirement age moves these from where each place began, instead of from a flat
100 everywhere.
