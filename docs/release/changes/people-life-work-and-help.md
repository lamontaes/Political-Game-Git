title: A better-paid job gets mentioned to you, and a favour gets offered with something behind it
---

Two situations that were written but never reachable now happen. Somebody outside your workplace tells you about a better-paid job going where they are, and says nobody you work with has heard it from them — you can follow it up, take it to your current employer, or let it pass. And when the household list has been sitting there a week, somebody you already lean on offers to take care of it and asks for nothing, which is its own kind of problem. Both are written from the people and records your life actually has: a world with nobody outside your workplace in it, or nobody you depend on, simply does not raise them.
