title: When a bill fails, your aide may apologize, and you can tell them not to.
---

After a lost vote, the aide who brings the news may say sorry for the count. You can answer that it isn't theirs to apologize for: you asked people to vote for it, you owe the people it was for, and you'll bring it back next session.
