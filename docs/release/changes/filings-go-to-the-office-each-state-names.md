title: Filings go to the office each state names, with a dated deadline
impact: minor
---

A candidate for a local seat now files with the office the state's election law names: a county board of elections in New York, a town's board of canvassers in Rhode Island, a city auditor in North Dakota, and the town or county clerk elsewhere, read for 33 states. The counter's officer carries that title, and where nobody holds the office yet a resident is seated in it. The filing deadline now reads as a full date before the seat's own election, set by the lead time the state gave candidates before its 2026 primary and marked as an estimate. Rhode Island towns now seat a five-member town council elected in November of even years, so a Hopkinton race no longer falls in April.
