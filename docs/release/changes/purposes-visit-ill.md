title: Visiting someone seriously ill now has them asking you for something.
---

When you visit someone you know who is seriously ill, they ask you for something: to look in on their house, to call someone who has not been told, to bring their child, to tell them honestly how they look, or to come back. It may name how long they have been ill and who is at home. What you agree to is kept, and a no that is pressed strains the tie.
