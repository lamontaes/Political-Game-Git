title: Offering another day to a friend no longer reads "I can't do on January 15"
---

Before, turning down a far-off meeting day said "I can't do on January 15, 2026."
Now the line reads "I can't make it on January 15, 2026," and the yes answer and the
friend's own request read the same way for every distance.
