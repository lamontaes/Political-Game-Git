title: A reporter asking why you voted gets your real reason
---

When a reporter asks why you voted the way you did, your answer now gives the reason the vote record holds: that you believe in it, or that the bill is yours. With no such reason, the plain answer has two wordings after a no vote. Two lines that answered "why" with no reason are gone, and two reporter lines that pointed at nothing are deleted.
