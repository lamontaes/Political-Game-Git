---
id: recall-of-town-officials
impact: patch
section: Added
title: Residents can petition to recall a town official
---

Your town's government screen now has a Recall section. Where your state's
law lets towns recall their officials, you can start a petition against
anyone sitting on the council. It circulates for as long as that state's law
allows (thirty days in Nebraska, for example). When the window closes, it
either falls short of signatures or goes to a recall election about two and
a half months later. If the voters remove the official, the seat stays
empty until the town's next regular election. If the official leaves office
first, the recall lapses.

Where the law gives towns no recall, as in Indiana, or where it has not been
settled, the section says so and offers nothing to press. How many
signatures a petition gathers and how the vote goes are placeholders until
the research on recall returns: the game does not yet count a town's
voters or measure how they feel about an official. Recalling governors,
legislators and judges is not built yet.
