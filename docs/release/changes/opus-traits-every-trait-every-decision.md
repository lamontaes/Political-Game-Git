title: Every trait shapes every choice people make
---

Every person now has a whole personality, and every part of it, low as well as high, shapes their choices: whether to go to work, look for a new job, take an offer, buy a home, borrow, start a business, run for office, retell a speech, offer or take a bribe, blame an official, believe what they're told, have a child, and when to claim retirement.
