title: A new friend asks you to come with them to the next town council or county board meeting
---

When a tie with a townsperson grows into a friendship, they now ask you to come with them to the next meeting of your town council or county board, naming the body and the day ("tonight", "tomorrow night", "on Tuesday") and, once the agenda is posted, what is on it. Saying yes marks you to attend that meeting. If you go, they thank you the next time you talk; if you stay away, they say they looked for you, and the friendship cools. A friend with nothing to ask the game can settle no longer asks at all, and the request to come to a party meeting is gone.
