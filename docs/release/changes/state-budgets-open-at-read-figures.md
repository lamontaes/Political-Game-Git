id: state-budgets-open-at-read-figures
impact: patch
section: Changed
title: A state's opening budget is its real figures in every world
---

A state, and a territory the budget survey covers, now opens its budget at
exactly the figures read for it, in every world: Illinois' income tax opens at
its Census figure per resident in every new game. Only an estimate from an
average, a county's or city's share of its state's local finances or an
unsurveyed territory, still opens with a spread around the average, so two
worlds differ where the game has no figure of its own. Before, every line of
every budget, a state's included, was drawn by chance around its figure.
