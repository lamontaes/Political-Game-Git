# The bill on the desk: before and after

The mayor's desk with a bill open, before (left) and after (right), at 1440 by 900, shown at half size. Both are the District of Columbia, drawn by `src/bench/bill-desk.browser.mjs`.

Before, the bill's three columns were squeezed into the side panel and ran over each other. After, a bill read from the desk or from a meeting takes a menu's frame.
