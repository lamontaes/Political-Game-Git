# The paper turns pages

`menus-no-scroll.browser.mjs` (MENUS_PLACE="Delaware|Brookside", MENUS_SHOT_TABS=news), New Game through the creator in Brookside, Delaware. At 1024x768 the front page holds what fits, and the stories it can't hold go to the next page, turned by the shared arrows in the dateline ("Page 1 of 2"): news-1024.png is page 1, news-page2-1024.png and news-page3-1024.png the pages after. news-1440.png is the front page at 1440x900. Every page passes the no-scroll check.
