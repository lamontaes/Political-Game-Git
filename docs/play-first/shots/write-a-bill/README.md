# Writing a bill: before and after

Your office, writing a bill, before (left) and after (right), at 1440 by 900, shown at half size.

- Before: Indian Hills, drawn by `src/bench/bill-screens.browser.mjs` on main.
- After: Pittsfield, New Hampshire, seed `8d44354e-00d5-4462-a0f7-91dde4a7602d`, a county commissioner.

Before, a bill was one dropdown, one number box and a Draft button inside a small box. After, every subject the body may write a bill about is listed by law area with what is in force today, and the chosen subject has its own page.
