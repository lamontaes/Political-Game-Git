# The title's monuments

`title-monuments.browser.mjs` over a full round of visits to the title: six frames, the White House at midday, the Lincoln Memorial at night and the U.S. Capitol in the morning, at midday, at night and in rain. Each picture fills the window under the menu with nothing scrolling at 1440x900, 1280x800 and 1024x768. One 1440 picture per frame (title-<monument>__<light>-1440.png) and all three sizes for the Capitol in the morning. No person is on the title until the people system lands.
