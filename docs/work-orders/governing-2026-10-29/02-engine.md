# How governing works in the engine (researcher B, Oct 29 ~21:00 UTC; CTO spot-checked 4 claims)

One legislature engine, one law store. Levels = 8 rows in src/core2/data/offices/bodies.json (House, Senate, state lower/upper/one-house, county board, council, township board). Played year (bench world, all modules, start 2025-01-20), Middleport NY (3646998, seed b77677eec45693a3): council 9 policy bills, county 8, state 64, Congress 2. Tuolumne Co. CA place: no council. Umatac, Guam: no local body. Script in the CTO session scratchpad (year3.ts).

1 TAKING OFFICE WORKS (no oath). Winner calendared for term-start day (voting.ts:2543-2547, 2617-2629); takeOffice (voting.ts:3253) → seatWinner (offices.ts:1645) ends old term, writes term row, emits took-office; read by officials (officials.ts:116/1613), offices (dual-office rule, offices.ts:197), elections (elections.ts:212). termStartAfter from data (elections-calendar.ts:652). No oath step for local/state (only a Congress calendar note). Tests: seat-winner-day, succession (played); town-term-start, terms-and-vacancies (unit). 12-24 took-office/yr.

2 LEGISLATURE WORKS with gaps (modules/legislature.ts). draftBill (1924): number, form, cost, state permission check; player picks from bill-catalog (18 menus) via bench/legislature.ts:248. Any member proposes; an executive asks the member most inclined to carry it (proposalRight 3046, proposeAsExecutive 3068; mayor/county-executive rows estimates).
Member vote (positionOn 2720-2830) = sign of sum: own views on the bill's questions; constituents' lean (0.5); closeness to sponsor (0.5); deals accepted; lobbying (interest-groups.ts:540) and public-comment balance (meetings.ts:481); consistency with recent votes; a bribe; replies from earlier scenes; state/federal only: party, wing, ambition, press, state stake; personality term (taste for change). NOT inputs: campaign donations; party for town council. Attendance a choice (missesSitting 3340).
Bargaining: offerDeal (3114) amendment / back their item / favor; floor and committee amendments and poison pills (floorAmendments 3867). Favors write an obligations row (3162) but obligationsOf has NO caller: a favor is never collected (CTO verified).
Committees (committeeStage 3580) only in chambers > 9; seniority and party ratio; chair decides hearing; witnesses only for minimum-wage bills (3665). Vote (countVote 4204): yes > no; presiding officer tiebreak where law says; Senate 60% cloture. NO quorum rule (CTO verified grep). Levels differ by data (bodies.json, mayor-veto table, series-powers.json, question-powers.json, session calendars). Tests played: floor-every-bill, committee-routing-played, carry-ask-played, office-day-member-filings, session-moot-bills. Stopgaps: SG-GOV-committee-structure, SG-LAW-held-bill-chair-reasons-unread, SG-GOV-committee-names-national, SG-GOV-local-bill-numbering, SG-GOV-local-executive-proposal, SG-GOV-carry-ask-no-screen.

3 EXECUTIVE PARTIAL. Sign/veto WORKS all levels (decideAtDesk 4511: own position + temperament); override two thirds of whole membership (overrideVote 4636); mayor veto only where state data gives one; player's desk waits until signing deadline (executiveAction 4376). 16-36 executive actions, 7-32 overrides a year. Appointments WORK: department heads by form of government with council confirmation (executive.ts:476), governor cabinet (635), desk review every 30 days refills vacancies (deskReview 1194; Middleport 9). Appointees read only by filing clerk and scenes; nothing shows a department head changing performance (inferred). Executive orders: governor only (pendingGovernorOrders 1263, issueOrder 1011, same law store); mayor none; president none (SG-OFFICE-FED-presidential-orders, -pardons); other states' governors never act (SG-GOV-world-governor-executive). Emergency powers MISSING: declareEmergency (1091) has a test but no caller; 0 declarations in 3 played years. Staff: aides and briefs before votes (staff.ts:154).

4 BUDGETS WORK. adoptBudget (budgets.ts:1378) on fiscal-year start via councilBudget (legislature.ts:862). Proposal = player's priorities, else executive's lean line by line (executivePriorities 1107, 5% scale), else department requests. Public hearing; members vote position + "duty to adopt"; failing → leaders halve most-opposed line up to 3 rounds, then drop it; then executive desk (budgetDesk 4480); failed budget carries last year forward. Monthly each treasury pays a twelfth of each line (budgets.ts:810; closeTreasuries budgets-treasury.ts:965) to payroll, contracts, transfers; budget-line-changed drives law-reach staffing handlers. Tests: budget-adopted-by-vote, budget-desk (played), federal-dollar node test. 3-4 budgets, 25-71 line changes a year. GAP: budget roll call is a separate loop (~legislature.ts:893-930): skips attendance, never recordVote, so budget votes never reach a member's record.

5 LAW IN FORCE WORKS. enactLaw (laws.ts:600) writes law:<jurisdiction>:<level>:<series>:general:<date>:<bill>, schedules effective day; lawTookEffect (735) emits law-took-effect, read by: law-reach host (law-reach/host.ts:41: 45 of 59 policy questions have a handler, + 14 budget lines + income tax, Medicaid match, SNAP), budgets.ts:348, recalls.ts:119, press desk/topics, outcome web, bench step-9 check (reached.ts). 89 lawApplied call sites. Outcome web: 246 links, 127 wired, 119 waiting. Chain: council minimum wage → bills row, legislative-vote, record votes, executive-action → laws row → effective 30 days later (estimate) → raiseToFloor (wage-floor.ts:42) re-rates every full person's job and every counted group's wage, writes laws.raises and pay-raised-to-minimum-wage → next paycheck larger; recall engine weighs the vote; press can run it. Tests legislature.test.ts (unit), law-wiring-labor.test.ts (played, journal), step-9-is-a-read.test.ts.

6 BACKGROUND GOVERNMENT WORKS locally and state; Congress weak. world-legislating.ts on the same draftBill/holdVote. Council, county board, township board at each regular meeting, up to 5 items (150-minute agenda, 30 each); own state legislature and Congress through sessions (openWorldLegislating 182-192). Members file when own reasons positive and law in force differs (fileChanges 440, filingReason 397); else executive asks a member to carry; floor leader orders by backing; governor reviews orders every 30 days. Limits: background bills are yes/no policy questions only, never amounts (1052-1060); local bodies can only add a policy no level has and repeal their own (280, 1430); other states' legislatures and other towns' councils never sit (inferred). Middleport year: council 9 (3 enacted, 3 passed over veto, 1 vetoed, 2 failed); county 8 (2 enacted); state 64 (6 enacted, 3 vetoed, 49 failed); Congress 2 (none). New law rows 9 city, 3 county, 10 state. Congress passed no policy bill in any of 3 worlds (SG-GOV-federal-proposal-coverage).

7 NEXT ELECTION READS THE RECORD: WORKS for voters, PARTIAL for rivals. recordVote (record.ts:68) only by floor votes (legislature.ts:3486). recordAppeal (record.ts:160) read by voters who know the incumbent (voting.ts:763), the town's public record (voting.ts:908), counted voters (electorate-inputs.ts:404). Promises judged kept/broken vs later votes. Challengers run partly by how incumbent's votes oppose their views (candidacy.ts:242); candidates' answers follow votes (campaign-stances.ts:228); press reads votes (press.ts:1348, 2157). GAPS: attack ads carrying a rival's record exist (campaigns.ts:2685) but no caller ever passes attackFilingId (CTO verified), so they never run; budget, committee, override votes not on the record; voters judge votes by agreement not by what the law did to them (only outcome term: economy); vote:<number>:<person> id may collide across sessions (not checked). Doors/mail only raise recognition (voting.ts:929, 1041); a "you've got my vote" at the door is not a commitment the ballot reads (CTO verified the recognition path).
