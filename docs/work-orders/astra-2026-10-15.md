## How Astra works here

- One draft PR per order, on its own branch off `main`, plain pushes, never force. Nobody merges their own PR; the CTO merges.
- Checks run in your session on the files you changed only: typecheck, eslint, prettier, the tests for those files. Never the full suite. Write the result in one line at the top of the PR body. A new file nothing reads needs no run.
- Every push to `main` needs a release declaration in `docs/release/changes/<id>.md` (`id` equals the file name; `impact: patch`, `minor` or `none`; a none declaration carries no title or section).
- The owner does not read PR bodies. Anything for him is a full-window screenshot (1672×941) of the screen, and a plain sentence of what he can do now.
- No hand-written player text: words come from the English engine's banks (`data/english/parts/`) and `src/bench/controls.json`. No dice. American English. Replace, never layer: delete what you replace and count it.

The shape:

- A talk starts from the place and the occasion, then the biggest recorded event of either person or the relationship. One main topic with side remarks, chaining to a related one. News only as received. No cold "How is X?" about anyone not in the room unless something recorded happened to X. A talk ends when its topic is done and the other person closes it. The listener remembers the last talk. The old subject loop (every known person in id order) is deleted.
- A scene has a goal and consequential dialogue, told as narrative with the player's character, the settled narrator (second person in scenes, first-person "My life" journal, grounded in records) and the other people. Two first shapes: a family scene at home from the household's records (a lost job, a divorce), and an office scene with a goal against one person (convince one member to vote no on an amendment), whose outcome the engines carry through that person's traits and relationship, never a roll. A quiet day fires nothing.
- Prove it in a played world in a random place: a year of a 17-year-old's household with the scenes that fired, each with the record that caused it, in the PR body.

**Owner plays it:** talks to his mother at home and gets a talk about something that happened, not a list of relatives; a scene interrupts him when the records say so.
