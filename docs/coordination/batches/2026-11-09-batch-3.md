# Batch 3 to grade, Nov 9, 2026

150 lines in 50 moments, mixed the way you asked after batch 2: a piece of legislation, a speech, a reaction, a constituent, and never three of one kind in a row. Some moments are new lines from the scenes you liked in batch 2 (the envelope turned down, the appointment taken, the vote declined politely), and a few are batch 2 moments you hadn't reached yet. Your batch 2 lessons are already in: "Alright", no fragments, every line with its context.

In each moment the three lines differ on purpose: one the engine already rates highly from your grades, one it rates low, and one in between. Telling us which is right teaches the most.

Grade by voice note, as before: say the number ("five point two") or read the line, then what you think. Read back a part to grade just that part, or say "the first part" or "the ending". Say how you'd put it in your own words and that goes in as a line. A line with a word in braces, like {bill}, gets the real name in play.
