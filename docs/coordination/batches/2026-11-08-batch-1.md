# A small batch to grade, Nov 8, 2026

18 lines from tonight's new scenes, three to a moment, each moment described. Say good, fix (with what to change) or bad for any of them, as you did in the earlier batches. Your grade on a line now reaches every line like it in the whole game: the same move, in the same voice and register, wherever it plays. You do not have to grade them all; a few is enough.

Each line says what it is, so you can grade by voice. A line with {name} or {office} in braces gets the real name or office in play.

## 1. A member of the council has just been offered an envelope by a man who wants a contract. This is what the member says to turn it down.

1.1 A thank-you is a card. This is not a card.
1.2 Still no. I'd rather you hear it from me than from someone else.
1.3 If everybody does it, they can do it without me.

## 2. The man whose envelope you handed back comes to find you a week later. This is the first thing he says.

2.1 You made a mistake handing that back, {first}. People like me have long memories.
2.2 Nothing has to change between us, {first}. Unless you make it. Think it over.
2.3 I would hate for this to get complicated, {first}. We could still be friends.

## 3. A reporter asks you, on the courthouse steps, about a vote you have not decided how to explain. You duck the question.

3.1 The text is public. I'd start there.
3.2 I don't want to speculate on that.
3.3 I'm not going to negotiate this through the press.

## 4. A donor asked you for a favor and you said no. He answers.

4.1 Try what? I asked you a plain question.
4.2 I'm being straight with you.
4.3 No. I'm telling you what people will say.

## 5. You won. At home, someone who lives with you answers your good news with one more thing.

5.1 Promise you'll still come home for dinner.
5.2 We'll see how thankful you are when you're buried in meetings.
5.3 Remember who was here before the votes.

## 6. Someone who once offered you money comes back, short of cash, to ask a different favor.

6.1 Please, {first}. I've got nothing else to try, and everything I have is riding on you.
6.2 I was wrong to come at you before, {first}. Help me, and I'll never ask for anything else.
6.3 I didn't come to threaten you, {first}. I came to beg. I'll be ruined if this doesn't go my way.
