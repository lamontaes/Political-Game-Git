# Batch 2 to grade, Nov 9, 2026

210 lines from every part of the game, three to a moment: scenes on the floor, in the hallway, with the press, at town halls and at elections, plus the speeches, the paper, the evening news, your journal, your calendar and your menu. They were chosen so your grades reach as much of the game as possible: graded together, they touch 10,039 of the game's 10,042 lines, all but three.

Grade any line good, fix or bad, as before, or grade part of one: "I like the first half, but 'you know' is filler." You can give a strength too ("50%", "kind of"). A grade is a lesson: it moves every line in the game that shares the part you graded, in the same kind of scene or from the same kind of person, never just this one. You do not have to grade them all.

Grade by voice note, as before: say the number ("five point two", "moment twelve, the second one") or read the line out, then say what you think. Read part of a line back to grade just that part ("Make it smaller. That doesn't make sense."), or say "the first part" or "the ending". "I'm rating that one a three" works too. The voice-note reader (`scripts/dialogue-batch/voice-note-reader.ts`) turns the note into grades, and any rule you state in passing ("don't say gaps") goes into the writing rules.

A line with a word in braces, like {name} or {office}, gets the real name or office in play. The number after each line is how many lines in the game a grade on it reaches.
