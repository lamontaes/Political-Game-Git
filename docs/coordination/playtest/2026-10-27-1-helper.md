## Q17 (priority). Day/Week stop at midnight, and no scene for three weeks

Owner, verbatim: "Again, I'm seeing simulations with no screens happening, and it's staying at midnight for whatever reason. There's no scenes or director interjecting. It's literally just sitting in a room simulator."

### (a) Why Day and Week always land at 12:00 AM
- The Day and Week buttons call `/api/advance?mode=day|week` (`src/bench/play.mjs:1231-1236`), which calls `BenchPlayer.advance` (`src/bench/handler.ts:1226`, `src/bench/player.ts:169-215`).
- `advance` builds the target from `at = this.packet?.at ?? { date: core.date + 1 day, minuteOfDay: 0 }` and then `through = { date: at.date + 1 or 7 days, minuteOfDay: at.minuteOfDay }` (`player.ts:182-199`). It keeps the minute of the last stop and adds days.
- At the opening there is no stop yet, so `at` is **tomorrow at minute 0** (Tue Jan 21 12:00 AM) and the first Day adds a day: **Wed Jan 22 12:00 AM**, skipping Tuesday. That is exactly what you saw (Mon 8:30 PM to Wed 12:00 AM).
- After that every packet's `at` is minute 0, so every Day and Week keeps minute 0. `run()` (`player.ts:223-275`) then steps one civil day at a time with `advancePlayerV1(...through: next)` and the core rests at midnight. Only a stop (a director scene, a filing story, an appointment, a canvass) puts the clock at another minute. With nothing to stop it, the clock never leaves midnight and nothing in the room changes.
- Known and fixed in an open PR, not on main: **PR #6059** "Day lands on the next morning; an outing moves the clock" (open, not draft, `mergeable_state: dirty`, last update Oct 26 19:27 UTC; pool items `play-stop-07-every-day-starts-at-midnight-a` and `play-day-lands-at-midnight`, both open). Its own Merger 3 recheck says Day twice reaches consecutive mornings (Jan 21 and 22 at 7:00 AM), Week reaches 7:00 AM seven days later, and a 3-hour canvass advances 3 hours. Remaining failure named in the PR: in a held filing story the buttons "Mark gap" and "Say nothing" are inert (`play.mjs:1188-1195` returns when `talk` is unset). It has been sent back twice and is now conflicted with main (dirty). Nobody has landed it, so v0.56.2 and main both still land at midnight.
- The room's light and office hours ignoring the clock is the sibling item `play-stop-07-every-day-starts-at-midnight-b` (open, no PR). See Q21.

### (b) Why the director gives Carlos no scene
- A scene is staged only from a **director moment** in his ledger, ranked against his other moments (`data/situation-types.json` `scheduling`: adult pace 4 scenes a year over the trailing 365 days, repeat 30 days). Moments come from records the core writes that he learns of. A settled household with no event writes none.
- Types that fire at home for a household with no event (own records only): `householdScenes` = job ended, a death, a serious illness; `householdRecordScenes` = eviction; `ownRecordScenes` = filing, a race result, a press story, an interview/debate invite, a volunteer offer, a rival's word or filing, a party ask. For Carlos (no job to lose, no race yet, no birth, death, illness or notice) none of these has a record, so none fires.
- The library has 78 situation types. 25 have an `awaiting` list: the type has **no cause at all** because the records it needs are not produced yet (for example `gathering-missing` awaits `record:birthday-gathering` and `record:holiday-calendar`; `interview` awaits `record:job-application`; `warning` awaits `record:arrears`; `letter` awaits `record:contact-from-someone-away`; `argument` awaits `record:dispute-over-money`; `favor-asked`, `confession`, `proposal`, `reconciliation`). Of 98 causes in total, 28 name an `awaitingProducer` (a cause whose producer the core does not have: `departure`, `reach-out`, `first-meeting` for a new contact, `date`, `breakup`, `celebration`, `arrival` for a birth, `advice`, `family-absence`, `eviction-notice`). Only 70 causes can fire from a real record. The ones that need a tie turning (`argument`, `becoming-friends`, `reunion`) need a thread turn of `soured` or `grew` on a contact; none happens in three quiet weeks at home.
- For a jobless 35-year-old specifically: `layoff` and `first-day` need a job record started or ended (he has neither); `interview` needs a job application record (nothing applies for him); `new-baby`/`arrival` need a birth. So the list of types that could fire for him is: none at the start, and every one that might later (`news-arrives`, `law-takes-effect`, `filing`, race types) needs something he or the world does first.
- Quiet-day talk at home is **on request**, not a director scene: a household member has a `quiet` subject read from records (`src/director/household-quiet.test.ts` describes it: weekday from the calendar, time together from the relationship record). The player must click that person; nothing interrupts.
- So "sitting in a room simulator" is by design of the build: the director interjects only from records. The records for an ordinary jobless week are not written.
