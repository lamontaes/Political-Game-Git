## Q1. Journal: are the player's and family's pasts simulated and shown?

**Short answer: no, not simulated year by year, and in v0.56.2 only a thin slice of the drawn past is shown. Main has since changed what is shown (PR #6345), not how it is made.**

What the player SEES in v0.56.2 (on screen, in the Journal):
- The Journal tells three sources: the director's own moments (`book.moments`), office acts, and `householdMoments(...)` (`src/director/journal.ts:171-290`, merged at `journal.ts:~548-552`).
- `householdMoments` reads only these `pastFacts` kinds: `household:partners-met`, `household:parents-met`, `household:roots`, `household:moment` (parent's new job), and the family links `family:parent`, `family:other-parent`, `family:sibling` (`journal.ts:198-286`). So the opening Journal is: where the family came from, how the parents met, who raised you, who lived apart, whether a sibling is older or younger. That is exactly the owner's Oct 26 line ("Our family came from Albion... My brother Leo was born after me").
- Schools and work are drawn for the player but are told nowhere: the Journal rows mark `school:`, `work:opening` and `job-started` as `"entry": "setting"` (`src/director/data/journal-rows.json:~94-105`). `journal.ts:~597-610` says a setting "neither opens a chapter nor titles one". So the school years and the jobs a 35-year-old has had never appear as lines.
- Nothing in the Journal shows any year between birth and the opening day except those few household facts. No lines for the 2000s, 2010s or the player's own 20s.

What is actually "simulated" for the past at the build:
- The past of a person is generated once, as dated facts, by `buildDeepPast` (`src/core2/deep-past.ts`: birth `:442`, schools `:566`, work `:628`, "eras" of public context `:673`) plus family facts from `src/core2/past-family.ts`. Called at `src/bench/world.ts:337` (opening population). It is a generator of records, not a run of the world.
- The owner's Oct 12 ruling "history from 2000" is built as the pre-start pass (`preStartFromYear`, `preStartStepMonths`, `src/core2/data/parameters.json:5179-5190`) and run at `src/bench/world.ts:516-518`. For people, `peopleHistory` (`src/core2/modules/people.ts:226-259`) only copies the already-generated `pastFacts` into the history log. It simulates nothing. The modules that really step 2000 to the start are government and economy (laws `laws.ts:93`, budgets `budgets.ts:309`, offices `offices.ts:160`, business `business.ts:2410`, macro `macro.ts:582`), and the bench reads those for the person card (`src/bench/card.ts:82`) and not for the Journal. Pool item `gov-prestart-history` is that work.
- Registered stopgap that says so: **SG-P8-deep-past** (`src/core2/data/stopgaps.json:399`, status open): school cohorts reuse common grade stages and present-day names and the opening locality stands in for where a person went to school; "not sourced personal attendance". Open pool item `core-stopgap-deep-past-generated-history-proxy`; related `core-deep-past-school-place-from-birthplace`, `core-deep-past-kin-past-on-fill-in`.

New life at age 35 (creator):
- At the build the chosen birthday goes into the world build (`playerBirthDate`: `src/bench/world.ts:243,326`, `src/bench/handler.ts:287`, `src/bench/creator.ts:259-280`), so the drawn resident is about 35 and has schools, work and household for that age. This resolves **SG-PLAY-creator-age-keeps-past** (status resolved, `stopgaps.json:4620`). So a 35-year-old has about 17 years of drawn school and work facts, a partner-met fact, parents and siblings, but the Journal shows only the household facts above.

Did main change it? Yes, the display, after the build:
- **PR #6345** (commit `92a26df32`, "play-first-10: the Journal opens with a memoir of the family's past", merged Oct 27 05:46 UTC; supersedes #6230): adds a memoir opening in `src/director/journal.ts` (+215 lines), `src/director/data/journal-rows.json` (+27) and `data/english/parts/journal.json` (+381 parts). Follow-ups `e5e9c99fa` (high-school line no longer repeats "I went on to {school}.") and `db216e72a` (no numeric literal in the family-work entry id).
- Pool item `play-first-10-journal-from-day-one` is `done #6345`, "awaiting the owner's installed-build check". The installed v0.56.2 may predate it; check that the build includes `92a26df32` (`git merge-base --is-ancestor 92a26df32 <build commit>`).
- Still not changed on main: the past is still generated, not simulated (`peopleHistory` is unchanged on main: no commit after the build touches `people.ts`, `deep-past.ts`, `past-family.ts` or `pre-start.ts`). The owner's complaint "people's histories still aren't being simulated" is true of the engine. What #6345 fixes is that the generated past was mostly hidden. ## Q2. Realistic, not real: where a real published statistic is a live value

Owner, verbatim: "realistic, not real. That goes for the entire game." and "no real published statistics. Realistic."

**Short answer: a lot. The build runs on real published data in about 24 systems; I count 28 separate real-data inputs below. 610 parameters in the core's tables carry the tag SOURCED (444 in the main table `src/core2/data/parameters.json`, 166 in 21 system tables). I did NOT classify each of the 610 as "live" or "calibration only"; the list below is the ones I traced to code that reads them at play time. Nothing on main after the build changed any of it (no merged PR touches `macro-model.ts`, `modules/macro.ts` or `data/macro`; only `opening-story.ts` word changes `e8169dbea`, `6428cdb2c`). No pool item exists for "realistic, not real" yet: I searched `docs/pool/items` for realistic, real-stat and not-real, nothing. Recommend one.**

### Your starting example, corrected
- `src/director/opening-story.ts:233-256` (`macroMonths`) reads the macro engine's recorded months, and before the macro engine's first day reads `historyMonths(core.startedAt, p)` (`src/core2/macro-model.ts:106-157`). Those rows are the real BLS/BEA/Fed/FHFA series in `src/core2/data/macro/history.json` (453 monthly rows, 1989-01 to 2026-09).
- The start date is fixed: Jan 20, 2025 in every place (`src/core2/game-start.ts:1-24`, `parameters.json:5174`). The opening month is the month before the start, so **December 2024**, not January 2025: the row is `unemploymentPct 4.1`, price index 317.604, payrolls 158,316 thousand, hourly earnings $35.69, policy rate 4.48%, 10-year 4.39%, 30-year mortgage 6.59%. (The January 2025 row is 4.0.)
- The macro engine's own opening state is the same rows (`src/core2/modules/macro.ts:315-330`, `open`), unless a custom world sets its own figures (`CUSTOM_ECONOMY_KEY`, `macro.ts:~325`). So every world, in every one of the 56 places, opens with the real December 2024 national economy. Only a custom-economy world differs. After day one the engine steps forward itself (`stepMacroMonth`, `macro-model.ts:~160`), so play is not real after January 2025, but the opening and anything that reads history are.
- The player SEES it: the President's address and that night's news say the unemployment rate, inflation, growth, wage growth, mortgage rate and jobs added (`opening-story.ts` `NUMBER_FACT`, ~line 258).
