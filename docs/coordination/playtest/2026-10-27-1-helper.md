## Q1. Journal: are the player's and family's pasts simulated and shown?

**Short answer: no, not simulated year by year, and in v0.56.2 only a thin slice of the drawn past is shown. Main has since changed what is shown (PR #6345), not how it is made.**

What the player SEES in v0.56.2 (on screen, in the Journal):
- The Journal tells three sources: the director's own moments (`book.moments`), office acts, and `householdMoments(...)` (`src/director/journal.ts:171-290`, merged at `journal.ts:~548-552`).
- `householdMoments` reads only these `pastFacts` kinds: `household:partners-met`, `household:parents-met`, `household:roots`, `household:moment` (parent's new job), and the family links `family:parent`, `family:other-parent`, `family:sibling` (`journal.ts:198-286`). So the opening Journal is: where the family came from, how the parents met, who raised you, who lived apart, whether a sibling is older or younger. That is exactly the owner's Oct 26 line ("Our family came from Albion... My brother Leo was born after me").
- Schools and work are drawn for the player but are told nowhere: the Journal rows mark `school:`, `work:opening` and `job-started` as `"entry": "setting"` (`src/director/data/journal-rows.json:~94-105`). `journal.ts:~597-610` says a setting "neither opens a chapter nor titles one". So the school years and the jobs a 35-year-old has had never appear as lines.
- Nothing in the Journal shows any year between birth and the opening day except those few household facts. No lines for the 2000s, 2010s or the player's own 20s.

What is actually "simulated" for the past at the build:
- The past of a person is generated once, as dated facts, by `buildDeepPast` (`src/core2/deep-past.ts`: birth `:442`, schools `:566`, work `:628`, "eras" of public context `:673`) plus family facts from `src/core2/past-family.ts`. Called at `src/bench/world.ts:337` (opening population). It is a generator of records, not a run of the world.
- The owner's Oct 12 ruling "history from 2000" is built as the pre-start pass (`preStartFromYear`, `preStartStepMonths`, `src/core2/data/parameters.json:5179-5190`) and run at `src/bench/world.ts:516-518`. For people, `peopleHistory` (`src/core2/modules/people.ts:226-259`) only copies the already-generated `pastFacts` into the history log. It simulates nothing. The modules that really step 2000 to the start are government and economy (laws `laws.ts:93`, budgets `budgets.ts:309`, offices `offices.ts:160`, business `business.ts:2410`, macro `macro.ts:582`), and the bench reads those for the person card (`src/bench/card.ts:82`) and not for the Journal. Pool item `gov-prestart-history` is that work.
- Registered stopgap that says so: **SG-P8-deep-past** (`src/core2/data/stopgaps.json:399`, status open): school cohorts reuse common grade stages and present-day names and the opening locality stands in for where a person went to school; "not sourced personal attendance". Open pool item `core-stopgap-deep-past-generated-history-proxy`; related `core-deep-past-school-place-from-birthplace`, `core-deep-past-kin-past-on-fill-in`.

New life at age 35 (creator):
- At the build the chosen birthday goes into the world build (`playerBirthDate`: `src/bench/world.ts:243,326`, `src/bench/handler.ts:287`, `src/bench/creator.ts:259-280`), so the drawn resident is about 35 and has schools, work and household for that age. This resolves **SG-PLAY-creator-age-keeps-past** (status resolved, `stopgaps.json:4620`). So a 35-year-old has about 17 years of drawn school and work facts, a partner-met fact, parents and siblings, but the Journal shows only the household facts above.

Did main change it? Yes, the display, after the build:
- **PR #6345** (commit `92a26df32`, "play-first-10: the Journal opens with a memoir of the family's past", merged Oct 27 05:46 UTC; supersedes #6230): adds a memoir opening in `src/director/journal.ts` (+215 lines), `src/director/data/journal-rows.json` (+27) and `data/english/parts/journal.json` (+381 parts). Parents, brothers and sisters are told together, with the schools the player started and each earner's work, from the player's own records. Follow-ups `e5e9c99fa` (high-school line no longer repeats "I went on to {school}.") and `db216e72a` (no numeric literal in the family-work entry id).
- Pool item `play-first-10-journal-from-day-one` is `done #6345`, "awaiting the owner's installed-build check". The installed v0.56.2 may predate it; check that the build includes `92a26df32` (`git merge-base --is-ancestor 92a26df32 <build commit>`).
- Still not changed on main: the past is still generated, not simulated (`peopleHistory` is unchanged on main: no commit after the build touches `people.ts`, `deep-past.ts`, `past-family.ts` or `pre-start.ts`). The owner's complaint "people's histories still aren't being simulated" is true of the engine. What #6345 fixes is that the generated past was mostly hidden. Open lane items still on the page: `play-stop-19-turning-eighteen-and-journal-silent` (a/b).

Owner's quote kept verbatim from the pool item (Oct 26): "Again, there's no history. I keep saying history should be simulated. It shouldn't be blank when it pops up."

## Q2. Realistic, not real: where a real published statistic is a live value

Owner, verbatim: "realistic, not real. That goes for the entire game." and "no real published statistics. Realistic."

**Short answer: a lot. The build runs on real published data in about 24 systems; I count 28 separate real-data inputs below. 610 parameters in the core's tables carry the tag SOURCED (444 in the main table `src/core2/data/parameters.json`, 166 in 21 system tables). I did NOT classify each of the 610 as "live" or "calibration only"; the list below is the ones I traced to code that reads them at play time. Nothing on main after the build changed any of it (no merged PR touches `macro-model.ts`, `modules/macro.ts` or `data/macro`; only `opening-story.ts` word changes `e8169dbea`, `6428cdb2c`). The opening-month part already has an open pool item, `core-macro-opening-month-per-world` (lead m0-m2, filed Oct 27 from `docs/reports/opening-root-cause-2026-10-27.md`): each opening figure gets a per-world spread around the published value. I found no pool item for the other 27 inputs below (searched `docs/pool/items` for realistic, real-stat, calibr). Recommend one for the whole list.**

### Your starting example, corrected
- `src/director/opening-story.ts:233-256` (`macroMonths`) reads the macro engine's recorded months, and before the macro engine's first day reads `historyMonths(core.startedAt, p)` (`src/core2/macro-model.ts:106-157`). Those rows are the real BLS/BEA/Fed/FHFA series in `src/core2/data/macro/history.json` (453 monthly rows, 1989-01 to 2026-09).
- The start date is fixed: Jan 20, 2025 in every place (`src/core2/game-start.ts:1-24`, `parameters.json:5174`). The opening month is the month before the start, so **December 2024**, not January 2025: the row is `unemploymentPct 4.1`, price index 317.604, payrolls 158,316 thousand, hourly earnings $35.69, policy rate 4.48%, 10-year 4.39%, 30-year mortgage 6.59%. (The January 2025 row is 4.0.)
- The macro engine's own opening state is the same rows (`src/core2/modules/macro.ts:315-330`, `open`), unless a custom world sets its own figures (`CUSTOM_ECONOMY_KEY`, `macro.ts:~325`). So every world, in every one of the 56 places, opens with the real December 2024 national economy. Only a custom-economy world differs. After day one the engine steps forward itself (`stepMacroMonth`, `macro-model.ts:~160`), so play is not real after January 2025, but the opening and anything that reads history are.
- The player SEES it: the President's address and that night's news say the unemployment rate, inflation, growth, wage growth, mortgage rate and jobs added (`opening-story.ts` `NUMBER_FACT`, ~line 258).

## Q3. Opening address picture: who stands with the President, and why

Owner, verbatim: "There's random people with him. He's not in front, like it should be like a TV kind of thing."

**Short answer: the three people with him are the Vice President and the two federal chamber leaders (Speaker, Senate majority leader), by design. He is on the podium by design, but the podium is the far back of a wide room shot, so he is the smallest figure in the picture. Nothing on main changed this after the build; no open PR covers it.**

Who and why (all on screen):
- First frame is the "president" stage. Its picture is the House floor for an inaugural or State of the Union (`src/bench/data/opening-pictures.json` `stagesByKind`; the game starts on Inauguration Day, so inaugural). Note in that file: SG-PLAY-opening-venue-has-no-inauguration-record, the House rostrum stands in for the Capitol steps.
- The cast is built in `src/bench/opening.ts:184-191` (`stageCast`): the President (`story.president`) plus the stage's `people`. Those come from the director's sequence rule for stage "president": reasons `vice-president` and `chamber-leader` scope `federal` (`src/director/data/opening-story.json`, `sequence.stages[0]`). That is the owner's Oct 15 key-people ruling (OD:290 as cited in pool item `play-stop-36-opening-starts-with-the-address`): "the president and vice president, the state's legislators, the most powerful person in each chamber". So the others are generated officeholders, tagged by name and office as the camera passes (the VP's tag shows in the screenshot). They are "random" to the owner because the world draws them and he has not met them.
- Placement: `stagePeople(core, place, cast, story.president.id)` (`opening.ts:246-250`, `src/bench/scene.ts:586-740`). With a speaker, the lead spot is the podium; the speaker takes it if a figure fits (`scene.ts:~660-680`); the others fill the nearest free spots (`reach`, `scene.ts:~600`).
- The picture's spots (`art/backdrops/staging.json`, `us-house-floor`): the podium is spot 1 at x50, y45.4, on the rostrum. The nearest others are spot 3 and 4 (seated either side, y45.4) and spot 2 (seated behind, y38.3). The room's front spots are at y57 to 90.

What the screenshot shows (committed with PR #6284, `docs/play-first/screens/play-opening-pictures-from-records/after-1-president-at-the-podium.png`): President Katherine Knight small and centered at the podium, a man seated on the rostrum left, a woman seated beside her, and a man seated at right, the whole rostrum about a fifth of the picture's width. The President is the same size as the others and farther from the camera than most of the empty benches. Her speech box shows her as a head-and-shoulders bust. So the podium requirement of #6284 is met (the done-when only said "stands on the podium spot"), but nothing makes him the subject of the shot.

What would put him in front, TV-style (I did not build any of it):
1. A camera shot for the speech frame: crop and scale the backdrop to the podium (a medium shot, President about half the frame's height, the other three as smaller figures beside or behind him), with a slow push-in. The bench has no shot or zoom for scenes: `grep -i "zoom\|crop"` in `src/bench` finds only the title-screen drift (`play.css:129`) and the head-and-shoulders bust (`play.mjs:151`). So this is new rendering, not a data row.
2. Or stage only the President on the podium in the first sentence's frame and add the VP, Speaker and majority leader as a second "camera passes" beat, which the code already does for tags (`play.mjs:289`, "the camera passes the people on a stage"). That changes `opening.ts:184` (`stageCast` for stage "president") only.
3. Or a different backdrop with a podium nearer the camera. The House floor is the only national picture with a podium and seats behind it (the file's own note), so this needs new art.
