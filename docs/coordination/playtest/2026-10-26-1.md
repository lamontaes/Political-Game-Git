## New rulings (record in OWNER-DECISIONS.md; none is there yet unless #6166 added it)

1. **Shifts never stop the player.** "Shifts are for the simulation. Absolutely not... Remove every semblance from that in the game. It's only for the simulation, you know, scenes, stuff like that... The player should not be clicking through that." Also: "there's no reason for this to be a job simulator."
2. **Wildermyth pacing.** "a month might jump by... It's okay if it goes from January 14th all the way to March 11th, and then there's something on March 11th, 17th, 18th, and 21st, then nothing till July."
3. **Campaign seasons are full of scenes.** "getting ready for the election, having interviews, a debate, assembling... an election team, meeting people, having moments that... change your life, working with other candidates, working with current elected officials, holding rallies... participating in corruption if it comes up."
4. **Election day is about five minutes of scenes:** canvassing, "going to vote, this should be a scene, you know, maybe with a narrator", the returns, "conceding or... celebrating." Election night "needs to be its own pop-up menu kind of thing."
5. **One UI system and one scene system.** "There's a bunch of independent UI things or a bunch of independent campaign scenes or Rails or whatever... This all needs to be unified... a UI.js or something... it needs to reference that... too much duplication or individuality."
6. **Watch the World and custom world become one pseudo dev mode, where you can do essentially anything, from the start.** "the opening thing needs to be more like RimWorld or Crusader Kings... get rid of the opening questions... The questionnaire, that's useless." Playing as anyone, starting as a legislator or the President.
7. **The world lives without the player.** "Everything that I say I want my character to be able to do should be simulatable in the background. Remember, the world keeps living without my character. I am not the main character." Legislatures meet on their real calendars ("nor should any body only vote yearly"), with bargaining, deals, vetoes, killing bills and pork in the background.
8. **Bills on paper, every term clickable, an estimate beside it** (reconfirms Sept 25 and Oct 14): "the bill is not animated, but illustrated on screen with the UI, and you can click that specific part... then it shows like the simulated stuff on the right or how, you know, the CBO would estimate." Citations should open the game's own display of that law ("not to actual web pages").
9. **Menus are menus.** Remove the menu subheadings; "If there's this many options, it should open an actual menu. It shouldn't just be in the chat"; chat history set apart ("different color, separated"); a scene's name above Day and Week, and "Continue" becomes "Skip scene" or "Simulate scene" (his idea).
10. **Creator:** hair, skin and clothes colors; everyone currently "bald... gray."
