# Owner sitting, October 28, v0.61.2 (8dbfff2), live narration

The owner plays and narrates; the CTO logs each point here as said, then reads the code behind it. Nothing is built until the sitting ends and the owner sees the plan. Numbered in the order said.

## Title and new life

1. Patch notes is grayed out on the title menu. No reason anymore; get them in.
2. The double background jump still happens: it fades into a background and then fades again, as if two pictures load.
3. Continue with a field missing (the gender, for one) does nothing. It should flash red, or say what is missing at the bottom.
4. Add a Random button to picking where you live.
5. Back on "where are you from" after picking a state (Hawaii) goes back to name and birthday. It should go back to the states.
6. The places list still shows the Mac's native scroller.
7. A strange bar sits at the end of the loading bar.

## The opening

8. The intro goes way too fast.
9. The chamber holds random people. No President, nothing in the Senate; it is the player and one other person.
10. Continue on the speech is when the scene should change, not before.
11. The speech is too thin and reads the same every time. Each Continue can carry a paragraph; the first chunk can be long, then Continue, then the next. Word by word: "Well, here we are. Thank you for being here" sounds like a late-night host, not a president. "Today the work starts, and I mean to do it right" is unnatural. "Things are better than they were a year ago... I intend to keep it going" makes no sense from someone just taking office. "unemployment is 3.4 percent" should be "is at 3.4 percent", and then: so what is he going to do about it? Why is this president talking about unemployment and pay in particular, what in the world prompts it, what makes him think he can do it? Read previous inaugural addresses. The outro ("To any that aren't, I haven't forgotten you. Thank you. God bless you, and God bless America") is fine. The Words session of yesterday knows this ask.

## Politics menu (Government tab)

12. No initials in circles anywhere in the game. Pictures: headshots or body shots.
13. The senators and representatives under Your members cannot be clicked.
14. "Your members" is in the same font as everything else; the menu is not illustrated correctly and the Local, State, Federal tabs have no clear delineation once one is picked. Look at other games.
15. "Your state" in the typing box: unclear what it meant. It should be a separate box, like the main menu's, as every typing box should be, and it should read "Ohio", not "Your state".
16. "Governor, Ohio" below it is repetitive.
17. No measuring bar with a 1 for the Republican governor; there is one governor.
18. The Leaders list (President, Vice President, Speaker, Louise Velez, Ohio) is printed text out of order with some of it gold. The whole UI kit exists: real buttons, accents, usable UI, not printed lines.

## Politics menu, the other tabs

19. Campaigns shows "Trustee, No one has filed, Filing closes August 6, 2025" three times. Remove all of it. Campaigns should be the local races actually on the coming ballot (November, May, whatever the place has), the real races, clickable. Clicking a race shows who is running; clicking a candidate's rally lets you go there and learn their views. "File for it" on a race triggers the travel to the filing office: Travel is how you go places directly, and menus carry hot links that take you there (click President, see the three running, click Run for President, and you are at the filing office).
20. Parties makes no sense as printed (12 seats, 1 seat, names and offices). It needs to be illustrated. There was once a map with districts; bring it back. Parties illustrated in that same way, not Democracy 4's way but in that spirit.
21. Issues: what are these and why these four? Issues should open a real menu where you click an issue (minimum wage) and see the law itself and find the law. The expanded rows read "State, Ohio, January 1, 2026" when we already know we are in Ohio; repetitive and offering nothing useful. A good start, no more.
22. Remove every Continue and Back from every menu. They serve no purpose.
23. Guide does not belong as a tab in Politics. Terms get the carbon-copy underline and show the guide text on hover; the Guide itself is a separate entry in the menu.
