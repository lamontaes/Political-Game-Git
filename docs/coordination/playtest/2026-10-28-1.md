## The opening

8. The intro goes way too fast.
9. The chamber holds random people. No President, nothing in the Senate; it is the player and one other person.
10. Continue on the speech is when the scene should change, not before.
11. The speech is too thin and reads the same every time. Each Continue can carry a paragraph; the first chunk can be long, then Continue, then the next. Word by word: "Well, here we are. Thank you for being here" sounds like a late-night host, not a president. "Today the work starts, and I mean to do it right" is unnatural. "Things are better than they were a year ago... I intend to keep it going" makes no sense from someone just taking office. "unemployment is 3.4 percent" should be "is at 3.4 percent", and then: so what is he going to do about it? Why is this president talking about unemployment and pay in particular, what in the world prompts it, what makes him think he can do it? Read previous inaugural addresses. The outro ("To any that aren't, I haven't forgotten you. Thank you. God bless you, and God bless America") is fine. The Words session of yesterday knows this ask.
