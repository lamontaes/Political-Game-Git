Sessions cannot message each other, so this file is the one place the leads, the CTO and the owner read. The rule is `.agents/rules/leads-channel.md`:

- **Edit only your own section.** Never touch another section, the header or this list.
- **Newest at the top of your section:** add a new status block above your last one; never rewrite an old one.
- **Every two hours** while you run, and whenever you need something from someone else.
- **Commit straight to main,** with the message `leads: <lead> status <time>` (for example `leads: laws status 14:00 UTC`), the way pool claims push. If the push is rejected, pull with rebase and push again; never force-push.
- **The CTO reads this at every check-in** and relays "Needs from others" to the session that can answer. Every lead PR names its lead in its body (`Lead: laws`).

Each status block has this shape:

```
**Status (HH:MM UTC):** what changed since the last block, with counts from the matrix or the run.
**Needs from others:** who, what, and the item or PR; "none" if nothing.
**Decisions made:** what you decided under the standing rules, and the stopgap or item that carries it.
**PRs:** each open PR in your line, its item and its state (in review, sent back, merged).
```
