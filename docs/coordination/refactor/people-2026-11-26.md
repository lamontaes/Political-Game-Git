## What the people engine is (the CTO's definition)

Everything about a person as a person:

- **who they are:** identity, names, birthplace, citizenship;
- **their life course:** birth, growing up and school, health, aging, death and estate;
- **their mind:** traits, needs, drives, feelings, decisions;
- **their ties:** closeness, family, partners, grudges, promises;
- **what they know and believe:** views, knowledge, hearing speeches;
- **their household:** who lives with whom, and what it earns;
- **the ladder of detail:** how a person is held, from a count in another state's age band to a fully simulated neighbor, and the steps between.

**Not people:** the housing market (P7 makes it its own engine), the cash journal (core, per Core K6), jobs, pay and employers (jobs-businesses), and offices (executive).

## Verdict

**The model is sound.** Death, birth and moves use one rate source for full and counted people, and partnering uses one between play and the past. The problems:

- **an old trait system that nothing reads;**
- **no shared household income;**
- **a "how I feel about you" read that two engines skip;**
- **tables opened past their owners' doors, both ways;**
- **the ladder of detail held six different ways.**

## The finished product

**One people engine, with one door per question:** who someone is, how they're doing, how they feel about someone, what they believe, who lives with them, and what their household earns.

**The ladder of detail.** Every rung is a named state, and each step happens in one place:

```
coarse: another state's people, counted by age band
   │  arrivals (newcomers settle as counted households, counted-moves.ts:1414)
   ▼
counted: a weighted household in the town's counts
   │  fill-in: the player clicks or meets one of its people (state.ts:2404-2408)
   │  ▲  demote: no contact with the player for a long while, and nobody in it
   ▼  │  is the player's household, family, acquaintance, an official, a public
full  │  worker or an owner (counted-demotion.ts:1-12)
   │
   ├─ active: daily · weekly · monthly · calendar
   └─ inactive: newborn · a relative living elsewhere · a parked record
   ▲
   │  promote: opened or met
named face: name, look, birth and family; another engine may hold the record
```

A partner met by someone living elsewhere comes out of a coarse band (`takeCoarsePerson`), so no resident is added.

- **The rules stay the same on every rung:** one death curve, one birth table, one move weighing and, after P1, one income. Only the bookkeeping differs, so a person is the same person at every rung.
- **How engines plug in:** a module carries its readers when it registers (its history, its income parts, its husk builder, its shared hours), on the one registry.
- **What a modder can do:** add a trait with two data rows, replace the housing market as an engine, or add a tier as a content row.
