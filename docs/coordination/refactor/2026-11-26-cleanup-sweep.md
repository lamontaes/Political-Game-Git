## What stays and why

- **Payroll is live.** The work module dispatches paydays. The file labeled “Proposed recorded-payday cash owner” is called by that dispatch to settle the cash journal. The file labeled “Proposed internal payroll arithmetic” prepares the request and settlement that owner consumes. Recorded shift outcomes, payday settlement, and the finance observer have different jobs. There is no second “Proposed” payday path to delete. The same files also carry active Reach-ins changes.
- **Ending a job has similar residue cleanup but different commitment rules.** The job lifecycle path keeps an already-ended commitment and clamps a new end date to the first shift. The employer-closure path does neither. The narrow matching residue block spans the protected jobs and money code, so leave it to those owners.
- **The date, article, and office helpers differ.** One spoken date uses ordinal days without years and another shows a year when it differs from today. The screen formatter's options and invalid-date path differ. The Journal office phrase uses first letters and omits an absent title, while the English article helper uses sound exceptions. These are not interchangeable, and the Journal, Talk, and English owners are editing those engines.
- **The remaining age, month, and grade-article copies in printed words are in History's active file.** Other age copies are in protected or active engines, or use the validating February 29 rule rather than plain year and month-day text.
- **Other unused exports held by active branches.** The three election reads `candidateLean`, `campaignMentorOf`, and `placeLeanRow`, the press's `declineInterview`, and the executive's `CABINET_POSTS` and `decideDeskItems` are in active Reach-ins paths. The national-opinion writer `pushOpinion` is named by an open scandal producer item. Retain the invented-person age research label until its owner has checked the generator and its evidence.
- **Full-body screen remnants are a coordinated retirement.** The dated archive plan holds their code and art, current bust and room callers, and stopgap cleanup. The remaining placement and draw-ahead helpers are tied to the retirement’s current routes or stopgap markers. Move them with the owning changes.
- **The game-only pass is last.** Do not move tests, strip comments, or change shipping exclusions until the four crew queues are done or the CTO posts the go-ahead.
