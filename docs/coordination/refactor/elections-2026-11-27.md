## What the elections engine is (the CTO's definition)

Everything that decides who holds a seat by a vote:

- **the races:** the election calendar and contests, filing and eligibility, candidacy, primaries and recalls;
- **the parties:** party identity, interest groups and endorsements;
- **campaigning:** doors, ads, volunteers, money and the campaign day;
- **the electorate:** leans, turnout and registration;
- **the count:** voting and the count, the electoral college, ballot measures and national opinion;
- **the century's elections** run before day one.

**Not elections:**

- the seats and terms themselves: those are executive's offices module (modules/offices.ts), which elections seats winners into through `seatWinner`;
- what a law does to the vote: that's laws;
- the words of a campaign: that's Story.

## Verdict

**The vote is one model, done right.**

- Every voter in the town's own races, named or counted, decides on the same appeal function, and a personal tie goes through the one feeling rule.
- Races counted from afar use one count in play and in the century before day one.
- A winner is seated through the offices module's own door.

The problems:

- party identity lives in the wrong engine;
- elections and the offices table are tangled, and offices is read by everyone;
- the past engine writes office terms;
- two 8,000-line files;
- eight side registries;
- 59 reach-ins into elections' insides.

**Checked and fine:**

- **One appeal function for every voter.** "One named voter's appeal for each candidate, on the one appeal function every voter uses (`countedAppeal`)." A named voter adds only what they themselves know: warmth from what reached them, their party tie, their tie to a candidate, the incumbent's record and a scandal they know (voting.ts:1404-1500).
- **A voter's tie to a candidate they know** is the one feeling rule, `feelingBetween` (voting.ts:1378-1386). **This corrects the people map:** campaigns' warmth isn't a third "how I feel" rule. It's what a campaign contact left behind, weighed beside the personal tie.
- **A person's lean follows their live views.** The town's adults are ranked by the views engine's lean and placed against their area's real 2024 vote. "Later views are located against that opening distribution" (campaign-views.ts:177-186).
- **The century before day one counts races the way play counts the world's races:** `countParties` is "The one path for the world's races in play... and the century before day one" (electorate.ts:1192-1197). It also shares play's candidate reasons and turnout readiness. The town's own races in play use the per-voter appeal.
- **A winner is seated** through the offices module's `seatWinner` (called by voting.ts).
- **One election calendar** (elections-calendar.ts reads the county and town calendars).
- **39 registered stand-ins** in elections' files, for the Stand-ins session's register. These sit on the golden path: `SG-GOV-who-runs-stand-in`, `SG-GOV-filing-officer-never-runs`, `SG-GOV-counted-runner-announces-at-filing`, `SG-GOV-campaign-walk-order`, `SG-CAMPAIGN-volunteer-shift-no-scene`, `SG-GOV-rival-donor-meeting`, `SG-GOV-threat-from-told-yeses`, `SG-GOV-polling-place-kind`, `SG-GOV-ward-map`, `SG-GOV-recount-confirms-count` and `SG-SPEED-results-mode-no-campaign-effect`.

## The finished product

```
executive's offices module (seats, terms, vacancies) ◄─ seatWinner ─┐
   │ "who serves", "a person's terms"                      │
   ▼                                                       │
elections: calendar → filing (eligibility, races open to a person) → candidacy
           → campaigns (doors · ads · volunteers · money · the day)
           → electorate (lean from views · turnout · registration)
           → the count (one appeal function · ballots · the electoral college)
           → result → seat ─────────────────────────────────┘
   │ one door: a contest, its filings, its result, a person's races
   ▼
screens · director (Story) · news · bodies · laws  (read through the door)
```

- **One model for every voter, play and past alike.**
- **For a modder:** a new kind of race is a calendar row, an office and a contest. A new campaign act is one file in campaigns' folder behind its entry.
