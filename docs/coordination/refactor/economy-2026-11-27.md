## Verdict

**Sound where it counts:**

- One household cost, worked out in one place: household-cost.ts works out the amount, and cost-of-living.ts pays it day by day from the household's accounts.
- Public posts are paid "on paydays like any job's" (public-payroll.ts:1-5).
- Pay rates have distinct jobs:
  - town-pay.ts: wages from federal labor statistics;
  - town-labor-pay.ts: a post's pay against the town's own private pay;
  - question-wages.ts: the minimum wage law.
- No engine writes another's tables from these files (Core's baseline).

**The problems:**

- the money system is filed under government budgets;
- jobs' tables are opened by hand;
- 13 side registries;
- very long functions;
- three files that aren't game, or aren't wired in.
