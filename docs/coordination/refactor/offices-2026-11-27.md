## What it is (the CTO's definition), and the split

The executive family becomes **two engines:**

**1. `offices`, the seats.** Who holds every seat in the world:

- offices, seats, terms and vacancies;
- succession;
- other places' officials, kept as names;
- officials' pay;
- appointments (nominate and confirm);
- the local government rules, meaning how each place is governed.

33 files, 11,222 lines, read 197 times by other engines.

**2. `governing`, the officeholder's day.** What a holder does, at every level:

- the day's acts and stops;
- the legislator's day (session days, committee days, floor holds), built on the legislature engine;
- the executive's desk (appointments with confirmation, orders);
- the president's acts, staff and constituent cases;
- asking someone to serve (serve-asks.ts, from elections EL9), which moves only after its imports are measured: it reaches candidate-pool.ts, which isn't an elections entry.

15 files, 12,867 lines, read 51 times.

**Why split:** governing depends on seats 29 times, seats on governing 3. Ten engines need seats and few need governing. A modder can replace how officeholders spend their day without touching who holds the seats.

**Leaving the family** (counts in this map are from before Reach-ins' stack, #10701, which changes 12 of these files and adds board-appointer.ts; the Offices session re-measures at its head):

- central-bank-board.ts and central-bank-appointments.ts go to Economy (the board's job is the rate decision);
- gov-rules/invented-person-age.ts goes to People (a person-generation rule ported from the old engine);
- gov-rules/place-slots.ts goes to Core item 2 (only a tooling check and a test use it);
- county-election-calendar.ts, town-election-calendar.ts, municipal-ballot-rules.ts and municipal-election-rule-packs.ts go to Elections (EL7).

## Verdict

**Sound where it counts:**

- No function is copied between levels.
- The executive's desk is "on one engine for every level" (executive.ts:5-9).
- Executive appointments go through one rule, `appointerWeighing` and `nominateAndConfirm` (appointments.ts). executive.ts and the president's file use it, state posts go through executive's `appointStatePost`, and the central bank board calls the appointer that executive registers (central-bank-appointments.ts:74).
- The office day's levels plug in through a kit (`OfficeActKit`, office-day-registry.ts:34-52). The engine's imports of the level files are front-door re-exports, not reach-ins.

**The problems:**

- the seats are a phone book opened by hand everywhere, and some of its doors are missing;
- "who holds a seat" lives in two stores, with no door that reads both;
- six side registries and 61 reach-ins;
- two possible duplicates to measure.
