# Story split, Nov 26 to 27, 2026: where every file of #10390 went

Story PR #10390 (`story/first-evening`, head `e6169835`) changed 273 files since its merge base with main (`ea60e9eb`): 127 engine, 93 words and data, 31 tests and 22 other. Each file was sorted by git (the story branch's blob against the merge base and against the newest train), its own commits told apart from the History reader's copy, and ported by a three-way merge against the merge base, byte for byte for new files. Story's own banks fail the sameness gate at `story/first-evening`'s head for every address kind, the Journal's evening, the loading story and three of the four news kinds, so no words part counts yet; none has a pre-grade. The packet script (`scripts/words/first-evening-packet.ts`) ran at three seeds on the merged head.
- **#10745's red guard:** 12 of Story's new `scene-talk` lines name a policy by its catalog words (`policy-name-in-speech`, added to train after Story's merge base). They are Story writers' to reword; the split does not edit a bank line.
- **Merging them:** `tests/guards/english/line-register-baseline.json` conflicts between #10706, #10721, #10735 and #10745; take each bank's lower count. Merging train into `claude/talk-delta-full-arcs` undoes Talk engines' removal unit (train carries their revert of it): re-apply its 14 files from 790a1a9b11.
