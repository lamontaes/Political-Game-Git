## What conversation does that scene-talk does not

1. **Subjects from records.** Each talk is about something the speaker holds: an eviction, a lost job, a relative's news, the player's own record, the day's shift, news the speaker received, the address's point. A scene's subject is fixed by its arc and its facts come from one cause record. _Plan:_ the gatherers stay, and become the home talk's facts provider. Each `Subject` is a bundle of facts (`Facts`, each with record ids) plus a kind; the engine takes it the way `composeBinding` hands it a scene's facts. Nothing is computed twice.
2. **The lie control (the scales).** See the step-by-step section below. scene-talk has two tree nodes for a lie and writes nothing.
3. **News passing at contact.** Every exchange is recorded through the core's relationship writer (`contact`, `minutesPerExchange` / 60 hours, cause `conversation`) with the player and every hearer present, and the first talk is the meeting (`meetOverTalk` → `meetInTalk`). The core passes news as at any contact; the conversation adds no news logic of its own. `replyInScene` writes the same `contact` hours (cause `scene-reply`) plus the ending's strain. _Plan:_ one write-back function takes the hearers, the hours and the cause; both callers use it. No new rule.
4. **Mined lines.** Home talk speaks only from the mined bank and "a moment no mined sentence covers is left unsaid". scene-talk records a gap and can skip the whole scene. _Plan:_ see Decision 2. The bank is not touched; the engine reads it through the same English entry point.
5. **The topic rule.** One main topic, at most one related topic (`data.related`), the other person closes it; last talk's topic is not opened again (the listener's `talked` key, kept a week, a day's subject a few days). scene-talk has no memory across scenes. _Plan:_ stays in the topic planner and feeds the engine only the subject.
6. **Heard today.** `noteHeard` and `withHeard` keep every piece said to the player as heard (by speaker, by subject, by question), so the next housemate does not say the same sentence. scene-talk takes a `said` set per path and `usage` per page. _Plan:_ the engine's `said` input is filled from the heard record for home talks; the writes stay.
7. **Beliefs.** `beliefsModule` keeps what each hearer was told that is false, until someone who knows better sets them straight. No scene writes it. Stays as is.
8. **Records the answers write:** told facts, stance on a question (`learnView`, `learnViewQuestion`), the address's point (`pointKey`), a promise answered (`answerPromise`), a veto answered (`answerVeto`), the interview sitting (`saySitting`). Stay, called from the engine's answer step.
9. **Said once, by role and pronoun** (`laterMentions`: employer, President, a policy question named once, `nameOnce`). Applies to the finished lines, so it stays as a pass over the talk.
10. **Greeting, goodbye, phone.** Housemates do not greet or part; hard news ends on its last answer; a call is closed by the person called. These are the open and close steps' conditions; they become rows the engine reads, with the same conditions.

## The lie control, as it works now

1. **When it shows.** Only when the last line is the other person's question about the player's own record (`last.subject.own`, `act` is `ask`) and they do not already hold the true value (`knowsTruth`: the same fact known, a housemate or relative, a coworker at that employer, or a shared campaign in `sharedHistory`). Otherwise the scales are dimmed.
2. **What it offers** (`lieChoices`, `lieClaims`). A set that mirrors the true choices: each keeps its move (what it raises or turns back) and swaps the answer for a mined denial (`stage` "none") or a false alternative (`stage` "elsewhere": another employer in town, or a place a relative or acquaintance lives; the pick among equals is `stableHash` of the seed, the players and the subject). Where no mined lie exists, the plain answer is said with the claimed value in its slot. A lie that reads the same as another is left out. Every claim differs from the real record.
3. **What it does** (`sayNow`, lie branch). It records `conversation.lie` (topic is the claim key, fact `claim:<key>`), with the player and the listener as persons and everyone present as witnesses. Each hearer either doubts it (`doubtsClaim`, through `believesClaim`: the stranger's pull, closeness to the teller, every trait's lean) or believes it: the claim is observed as told, a belief row is put (`hearerId`, `subjectId`, `key`, `claimed`, `truth`, `lieEventId`) and the hearer joins the believers. A listener who doubts says so; the talk carries on.
4. **What comes of it.** Anyone who knows the truth sets a believer straight when they next talk; the director keeps the lie.

scene-talk's lie is a different thing: the `reply.move === "lie"` branch builds two nodes, "caught" (only when the facts hold `knows-truth`) and "believed", and writes nothing. **Plan:** keep step 1 to 4 exactly. The engine gets a lie provider with one job: given the last line and the choices, return the set and the claim. `knowsTruth`, `lieClaims`, `doubtsClaim`, the belief writes and `beliefsModule` stay as they are; only `lieChoices`'s own wording call moves behind the engine's word entry, with the same inputs and the same picks. The scene branch is left alone. The proof is a before and after dump of the scales set and the belief rows at the same seeds.

## Decisions the plan rests on

1. **Shape.** The engine becomes drivable one node at a time. Today the queue loop in `composeSceneTalk` makes, for a path, the reply menu and the asker's answer to a chosen reply. Slice 1 factors those two into functions the loop calls; `composeSceneTalk` keeps building the whole tree by calling them, and a home talk calls them for the turn it is on. A home talk is not built as a tree: its subjects come from live records and the player's own state, so a tree would be wrong the minute it was built.
2. **Words.** Home talk's words are in the mined bank and scene talk's are in the scene bank. Two banks stay. The engine takes a `bank` on the move and calls `wordConversationMove` or `wordSceneMove` through one English-engine entry point, with the inputs home talk gives today. The text does not change, so before and after read the same. No line, bank or arc text is edited. (Not decided by me: whether the two banks should one day be one; that is a words question.)
3. **Ranking.** The home talk ranks by reach and graded pairs; a scene by trait lean over act kinds. They are different rules for different needs. First migration keeps each as its own hook so nothing the player sees moves. **Question for the CTO:** whether a later change makes them one lean. It would change what the player sees, so it is not part of this plan.
4. **Leaving.** In 74 of 83 purposes and 8 of 9 generic arcs `leave` is an arc reply, which I may not edit. The engine offers a reply only if the arc lists it. A home talk's arc lists none, so leaving stays the screen's control there. Scenes keep `leave` as they have it. **Question for the CTO:** the brief says "leaving as the screen's control, never a choice"; I read that as the home talk's rule from the header of `conversation.ts` and the scenes' arcs as theirs. Tell me if scenes should lose `leave` too.
5. **Home arcs without editing arc text.** The engine needs an arc for a home subject (a state move, reply moves, follow moves). I will build them in code from the mined bank's own structure (`SubjectBank`: raise or ask is the state, answers are the replies), the way `variantArc` builds a variant's arc from `arc-variants.json`. Nothing is added to `purpose-arcs.json`. `src/director/arc-variant.ts` is in the history reader's file list, so I only import it. **Question for the CTO:** if you would rather have rows added to the arcs file (an addition, not an edit), say so.
6. **Surface.** `openConversation`, `say`, `sayNothing`, `meetOverTalk`, `noteHeard`, `noteSceneHeard`, `doubtsClaim`, `beliefsModule` and the `Conversation`, `ConversationChoice` and `Subject` types keep their names and shapes. `src/bench/handler.ts`, `world.ts` and `sitting.ts` do not change, nor the roughly 40 test files that import conversation. Only `scene-talk.test.ts`-style tests that name tree internals can need an edit, and then only to point at the same behavior through the new function, never to loosen it.
