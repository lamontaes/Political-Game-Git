## 3. What the code can do now

### 3.1 How a scene starts today (plain version)

1. The director watches what happens to the watched people: the player, their household and the officials who are full people. It weighs each event's impact on channels: feeling, ties, money, home, health and causes (`src/director/data/director.json`, `channels`, `eventKinds: ["*"]`).
2. A second producer reads each day's new public records. It casts someone with a reason to bring the record to the player, by the record, the tie and how sociable the person is (`src/director/data/circumstances.json`, 34 rows; `src/director/circumstance.ts`).
3. A situation type whose cause matches the moment is staged (`src/director/data/situation-types.json`). A type with a purpose plays as a back-and-forth (`purpose-arcs.json`, 72 purposes, with endings).
4. The English engine words each turn. Nothing is rolled.

So a scene can exist only when **(a)** some engine writes a record, **(b)** a director row turns that record into a moment for the player, and **(c)** a situation type matches it. Every gap below is a missing (a), (b) or (c).

### 3.5 The three questions the CTO asked

**Is there a record of how an official's term is going by area (education, health, crime, jobs)?**

**No.** The world measures most of those areas for every place every month (the outcome web). But nothing ties the measures to the official in office, adds them up over a term, or compares them with where the term started. Nothing hands them to the director.

- The only place results meet an officeholder is the vote. Voters read the town's jobs and crime and the national economy, and only once a story carried it to them (`electorate-inputs.ts` lines 2 to 9 and 315 to 321).
- The code says outright that no approval record exists (lines 295 to 298).
- Education and health never reach the paper either, because outcome news covers only jobs, four crimes and rent.

**Is there any strike, walkout, protest or union action?**

**No.**

- The word "strike" appears in code only for striking a bill section or a line item (`legislature.ts` lines 2466 and 5220; `office-day.ts` line 794). "Walkout" and "picket" appear nowhere in `src`. "Protest" appears only as a faith label in `views.ts`.
- Unions exist only as a membership rate and the dues under a right-to-work law (`src/core2/right-to-work-dues.ts`).
- The people a teachers' walkout would need do exist: public schools employ teachers (`src/core2/town-workplaces.ts` lines 392 to 398).
- The closest things to organized pushback are three. The drives engine's organizing meeting puts an aide in front of the officeholder with a "warning" scene, which never fired in the Oct 29 year. Residents confront the person they blame (`grievance.confronted`), with no scene. And recall petitions.

**Is there a path from a law's effects, or a place's outcomes, to a scene?**

- **From a law's effects: yes.** When a person works out that a price change came from a law (`law.effect-learned`, the budgets engine), three things can follow: law-takes-effect at home, constituent-stop in the street for an officeholder or candidate, or news-arrives through someone they know. Since #7211 the scene names the law and the dollars.
- **From your own votes: yes.** A resident who read the story of a vote you cast stops you (circumstance row `vote-read-street`, #6837 and #6899). Recalls start from ordinance grievances.
- **From a place's outcomes: only roundabout, and only for jobs, crime and rent.** The path runs outcome web, then outcome news, then the press deciding to print, then a reader, then someone at home, work or the street mentioning the story (`story-read-home`, `rent-news-street`, `national-news-street`). Nobody meets the player as the person responsible for a result. No scene reads a school, health or roads outcome.
- **No row reads `outcome-web.month` or any measure directly.** I searched `src/director` for "outcome" and every hit is about a scene's own ending.

### 3.6 How the English engine picks lines, and whether it varies by kind of scene

**How a line is picked.**

- The director names the purpose, the situation key, the speaker, the move each turn makes, the line it answers and the facts on record.
- `src/english/scene-talk-words.ts` (lines 2 to 17) picks a piece from `data/english/parts/scene-talk.json` (4,971 pieces; 4,954 marked `origin: authored`).
- It fits the piece by step, move, role, purpose, situation key, the move it answers, the ending and the facts it needs. The closest fit wins. Among equal fits, a stable pick by the speaker's voice decides. No dice.
- Narration comes from its own bank (`scene-words.ts`). Talk comes from its own bank (`conversation-words.ts`).
- "Each surface has its own reviewed bank and register: a Journal line is not interchangeable with dialogue, a menu action, legal text, or news copy" (`grounded-english.ts` lines 9 to 10).

**How the weights move.**

- `calibration.ts` pulls each piece by the speaker's traits (bearing and length), by the piece's running grade, and by whether a pair of moves read naturally.
- The owner's grades sit in `data/english/grades/` (two voice-note files). They are folded into `part-grades.json`. A piece graded bad or fix and never good is held back (`english-grades.ts`).
- One grade reaches every piece that shares its speech act, its voice (bearing) and its register class (`grade-reach.ts` lines 1 to 29; `grade-reach.json`).

**Does it vary by kind of scene? Only through the pieces written for each purpose.**

- Every scene talks in one register. `scene-talk-words.ts` line 256 passes `register: "scene-talk"` whether the scene is a floor whip, a reporter's question, a donor call or a kitchen talk.
- `calibration.json` puts all scene talk in one register class, "private" (line 667). So a grade on a press exchange reaches family talk with the same move and bearing.
- The only length target is for everyday conversation: 5 words, measured from a real corpus (`calibration.json` lines 286 to 288). The scene bank's cap is 70 words.
- **Register cards already exist** for the House floor, Senate floor, committee questioning, a state floor, council comment, a press briefing, a town hall, a rally, election night, a eulogy, small talk and family (`speech-registers.ts`). Each card has who is addressed, which devices are allowed and measured figures. But only public addresses use them (`composeAddress`, `english-composition.ts` lines 552 to 562), not scenes.
- An open question blocks the fold. Lane 3's talk fold "waits on the CTO's answer about one bank or one per register (`words-everyday-talk-one-engine`)" (`docs/lanes/state/lane-3.md`, line 5).
- What is per scene today:
  - the pieces written for each purpose;
  - a friend versus a rival (`scene-talk-rules.json`, `tieSides`, #7186);
  - a blunt asker who skips the greeting (`bluntGreeting`);
  - the time of day (`timeOfDay`);
  - endings that write back to closeness, promises, the owed talk and the paper (`endingStrain`, `owedTalk`, `concession`).
- I found no per-kind setting for how many turns a scene runs, how often the narrator speaks between lines, or how formal it gets. The owner's Oct 19 note that every scene opens "narrator, one sentence, and then two" may still hold. I didn't play it to check.

## 4. Gaps, and a proposed order for the Story session

### 4.1 Gaps

1. **No record of how a term is going, so the owner's strike example can't happen.** The pieces exist. The world measures education, health, crime and jobs for every place monthly. Teachers exist as employees. The drives engine lets people organize and confront whoever they blame. What's missing is the link: nobody reads an official's area results over their term, and nobody acts on them. _Inference:_ a term reading can be a read of records the outcome web already keeps, scoped to the office's own place, with no new mechanic. A strike or walkout is a new act by workers, though. Lane rules put new mechanics out of scope ("Out of scope: new mechanics", `docs/lanes/lane-3.md`), so it needs an owner engine (jobs, drives or interest groups) and probably a CTO ruling.
2. **Outcomes don't reach people, except jobs, crime and rent.** Outcome news covers 6 measures. Schools and health never make the paper, never come up in talk, and never reach a voter.
3. **Political scenes exist but rarely play.** In the Oct 29 year, most wired office types never fired: bill-signed, veto, appointment, recall, scandal, public-hearing, vote-sought, vote-confirmed, bill-carry, desk-bill, party-recruit, rally-opponent. Losses and floor votes took the year. That report is ten days old and predates the 14 new moments, so this needs re-measuring on main.
4. **Office-specific scenes are thin.** One legislating set serves every level. There are no scenes for these, even where the engine records exist:
   - executive orders;
   - emergencies and disasters (the governor declares on their own);
   - the budget a mayor or governor proposes;
   - department heads reporting results (an episode exists: `budget-cut-department-head.md`);
   - staff reviews and firings;
   - a member of Congress's week in D.C. (the owner's "spends a year in her bedroom", Oct 26);
   - the President in office after the opening.
5. **Twelve types can't fire, and two fire only in part** (section 3.2).
   - bribe-offer needs the corruption module to write an offer the player answers, instead of deciding it from the player's traits (pool item `corruption-bribe-offer-reaches-player-seat`).
   - polling-place waits on `election-day`, which Lane 3 says has a core event.
   - married, dating, breakup, school-started and mentorship need the lives engine. `separated` is built on a branch, but couples never part in town yet.
   - The seven types with no cause need a producer or deletion (the Oct 28 plan, section 1).
6. **Systems with records and no scene:** grievances confronted, cause groups founded, press snubs and attacks, disasters, emergencies, executive orders, staff reviews, school results.
7. **The words don't change by kind of scene.** One register and one grade class for every scene, a length target only for small talk, and the speech register cards unused by scenes. The owner wants a reporter, a floor whip, a donor and a mother to sound different (Oct 19, and tonight). The "one bank or one per register" question is still open with the CTO.
8. **Lines sit where lives don't go.** Press-comment has 422 pieces; the talk after a loss, which nearly every new life reaches, has about 62.

### 4.2 Proposed order (my inference from the record and the code; the owner's voice note decides)

1. **Walk lives first, then build.** This is his Nov 8 rule and his Oct 19 "start as the president... work backwards." Play years on random places, starting as a council member, a mayor, a state representative, a governor, a member of Congress and the President. Regenerate the scene web on main. List every dated record that names the player and has no moment: the producer gaps, the types that never fire, the systems in section 3.4. This replaces the Oct 29 report and gives the session its own list, not only the owner's.
2. **A term record, read from the outcome web.** For each office, its own place's area results since the term began: schools, health, crime, jobs, housing, roads. Then scenes that read it, using engines that already exist:
   - a department head or staffer bringing the slipping area;
   - a reporter asking about it;
   - a constituent stop about the schools;
   - a town-hall question;
   - the party chair or a donor reacting;
   - the organizing warning, now caused by the weak area.

   Widen outcome news past its 6 measures in the same pass, so people actually hear about schools and health. This is the owner's "good term except for education" without a new mechanic.

3. **The strike itself, as its own item, after a ruling.** Workers at a public employer (teachers at the district) walk out when the area's results and their own pay or conditions give them cause. This needs an engine that owns it (jobs, drives or interest groups) and the CTO's or owner's call on scope. _Inference:_ without it, step 2 gives warnings and pressure but never the walkout he named.
4. **Make the waiting types fire.**
   - bribe-offer, with the player's choice (its pool item has a finish line);
   - polling-place on `election-day`;
   - the life producers (married, dating, breakup, school-started, mentorship) once the lives engine writes them;
   - for the seven cause-less types, give each a cause or delete it.
