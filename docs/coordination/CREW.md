# The crew: who is running, what each owns, and who talks to whom

Current as of 2026-10-29, 18:30 UTC (CTO, the owner's second account). The CTO keeps this file current whenever a session starts or stops.

**Why (owner, Oct 29): "I say this to make sure that systems aren't duplicated or anything like that again. I want to try to get this correct now."** One engine per mechanic (`AGENTS.md`, first bullet). Before you build a record, a reader, a bank, a screen part, a builder or a helper function that touches another session's area, message that session and ask whether it exists or is being built. If it is theirs, they build it or point you to it; you never build a second one beside it. If you find a duplicate already on main, tell both owners and the CTO.

**How sessions talk (owner, Oct 29):** "be sure that the sessions communicate with each other... the news writing stories using the words... something else coordinating because words needs a section someplace." Where two sessions' work meets (the seams below), they message each other directly with the `send_message` tool, using the session ids in this table, and settle it between them. Questions for the owner, and anything that changes the plan, go to the CTO, who is the owner's one point of contact. A message carries the conversation; the outcome is still written down where it belongs (a PR body, a pool item's Notes, a channel block). Never call a scheduling tool and never edit anything under `.git`.

## The seams: who must talk to whom

- **Words and the lane: the newspaper.** Words writes each printed story's text (`words-printed-stories-have-a-body`, `words-printed-stories-no-body-battle-mountain`); the lane lays it out on the front page (`play-news-paper-prints-the-stories`). Agree first on what the paper reads (the story record and the composed headline, lede and body) so neither builds a second copy. The lane prints whatever body the record carries today; words tells the lane when more story kinds get bodies.
- **Words and the lane: person history and every label.** The lines on a person's card come from `data/english/parts/person-history.json` (`play-person-history-for-everyone-you-open`). The lane asks words for any new line or label rather than typing one; words reviews every lane PR marked `Review: words` the hour it opens.
- **Words and laws: laws people hear about.** When a law's effect reaches a person (a paycheck, a price, a service), the player meets it in the news, the journal or a conversation (golden path steps 9 and 10). Laws tells words each time it wires a record a player could hear about (the record kind and its fields), and words gives it a story or a line. Words tells laws when a story or scene needs a field the law's engine does not keep. The background records themselves (youth substance use, the age check, the college step, small-dollar lenders, medical debt) are never shown as a step; only what they change in someone's life can be told.
- **Words and laws: the school engine.** The laws lead's helper builds the college step (`school-college-step-record`) and the words session's helper builds school and sports achievements in the same engine. They agree files and record shapes before either writes, so there is one student-record writer.
- **Words and people v3: who is in the scene.** The director decides who is present and why (plan step 7, one cast and one dwelling); people v3 decides how each one is drawn at a mannequin spot. Agree on the hand-off: the scene's cast list with each person's pose and spot, read from the mannequin plate, so nobody is drawn without a reason and no spot is filled twice.
- **Words and the lane: the campaigns engine.** Words stages the debate and the campaign team through `src/core2/modules/campaigns.ts`; the lane finishes call donors (`play-stop-11-campaign-actions-do-nothing-visible-a`) in the same engine. Whoever edits that file first tells the other.
- **People v3 and the lane: `src/bench`.** Both edit the client. People owns figures, faces, the creator and scene staging (`scene.ts`, `ui/headshot.mjs`, the figure files); the lane owns menus, News and the card's history. Before touching a file the other is in (`play.mjs`, `card.ts`, `handler.ts`, `government.ts`), say so; merge `origin/main` often. `play-faces-show-the-moment-a-menu-opens` starts when people v3 is merged.
- **Red main and every owner.** A red in an engine a running session owns (laws in `src/core2/law-reach`, words in `src/director` or `src/english`, people in the figure code) is messaged to that session with the failing test, place and seed before the red-main fixer touches it; the owner of the engine fixes it within the hour or hands it back.
- **Mergers and authors.** A send-back is a PR comment and a message to the author session naming the PR and the one defect, so it is fixed the same hour.
