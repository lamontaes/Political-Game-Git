## What the owner can play now

- **Steps 1 and 2 in the core (not yet on screen).** A new life at 18 in any of the 56 places has parents and siblings who follow the kin rules, real schools from the place data (public, or a real private school when the family's means lead there), and a record behind every fact. Over 91 days that person meets new people on visits, on joining groups, at town halls and hearings, and (once WORLD sends the hire event) at a new job; each meeting is a dated fact naming the place, and some of those ties grow closer. - **In `/play` today (the bench installs every people engine):** young adults go to college at 18 (a college in the county, or they leave town for one) and graduate; people move away, have babies, fall in together, move in, marry, and sometimes split up or divorce (the one leaving goes to a relative or out of town); they join groups, change party, and carry health coverage from their own circumstances (losing it is a moment). In conversation, people tell news they saw ("Did you hear? Angela got married.") and their own ("I lost my job."), and the player can tell news too. The director turns the economy's and the elections' events into moments, and "My life" tells them ("I won the race for City Council, Seat 2.", "I married Daniel.", "Mann manufacturing closed."). Conversations and scenes draw on real mined speech beside the hand-written lines. Groups in `/play` still need OPUS-PLAY's one `peopleOpening` call (under Needs); education and faith work without it.

## Questions for the owner

- **Births to mothers with no partner at home (asked Oct 13).** About 40% of US births are to unmarried mothers (NVSS 2022). Most are to couples living together, and those are modeled: they plan a child together, as married couples do. Births to mothers with no partner at home, about 15% of births, and teen births are not, because every birth here is a plan both partners choose, and an unplanned pregnancy would need either a chance roll (ruled out) or a cause. The simplest version that follows the rules: a dating couple's plan (people courting, not yet living together) is weighed the same way, at a weaker pull, so it comes from their circumstances, and the birth moves the couple in together or leaves the mother raising the child alone by each one's choice. I'm building that simplest version next, per "no stopping"; say if you'd rather it work differently.

- Oct 13: talking politics moves views. At each real contact, a stance someone formed or renewed in the last 30 days passes to the person they talked with, once per original event, however many people it went through. The step scales with how close the two are and how closely the teller follows politics (`talkStanceStep` 0.1 per unit of each), so it's smaller than living through the event.
