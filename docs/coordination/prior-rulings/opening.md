## Conflicts

**A. White House motion: restore Ken Burns, or no motion?**

- Older side, September 16 and September 20: the brief asks for overscan "for subtle Ken Burns movement" (item 21). The September 20 Calais addition says "Lamontae reaffirms restoration of the White House Ken Burns movement" (item 16).
- Newer side, September 22: "You said there should be no motion here and the Pause motion button should go (finding 5)." The fix removed the slow drift and its button ("The slow camera drift and its button are removed; the scenes hold still"), and PR #444 merged that day (item 12).
- The newer owner words are the most recent direct ruling, and they are implemented. But "no motion here" may refer to the card transitions or the Pause control, not the slow drift itself. The record does not say which. Flag for the owner. Recommend asking him before the drift is restored.

**B. Does the President's card include the Vice President?**

- Older side, September 20 (item 17): both President and Vice President visibly composed at the White House.
- Newer CTO brief, September 27 (item 9): the President on the first card, then "Next shows the Vice President."
- The September 28 order ruling (item 5) does not mention the Vice President. The built order (item 2) does not say either. Flag. The owner's September 20 text is the more specific owner ruling; the September 27 brief is a CTO plan. Recommend keeping both people on the White House card unless the owner says otherwise.

**C. Order of the opening screens.**

- Older side, September 27 CTO brief (item 9): President, Vice President, governor, local officials, then "Your world," with no mention of Congress or family in that order.
- Newer side, September 28, 3:30 p.m. owner ruling (item 5): the year and the world, the White House, the state and then county and town, then the story (family, household, upbringing).
- The built order (item 2) follows the owner ruling: year, White House, state, legislature, Congress, town, parents, life so far. The September 27 brief is superseded for order. Congress placement is not in the September 28 ruling, so the built position of Congress (after the legislature, before the town) is not owner-ruled; it comes from the September 20 order (item 17). Flag as a gap.

**D. The palette: navy and brass, or no navy?**

- Older side, undated Register block (item 20): "instead of more navy/gold modal slabs"; September 27 image briefs (item 10) and the CTO brief (item 9), which say to keep "today's colors" and to use "deep navy panels (#141C2B)."
- Newer side, October 2, UI packet (item 1): "No navy in the game UI."
- The October 2 packet is the newest and the most specific. It is also the only one that says "No navy" outright. Flag: the source of the October 2 lock is not named in the doc. Recommend the owner confirm it, since the September 27 image prompts are still in use for art.

**E. Furnace question and "Who are you?" questions.**

- Standing decision (item 13): setup answers may suggest temperament, the questionnaire is a varied route of 10 to 12 questions, and the Register says "several possible opening situations rather than three mandatory fixed opening questions" (September 20 refinement, in the same OCD-LIFE-004 entry).
- Built state (item 14): every adult still opens on the same furnace scene. The research doc measures that no answer sets a trait. The September 28 hand-back (item 6) says the furnace question is still in the game although the owner rejected it.
- This is not a conflict between two rulings. It is a ruling that is not built, plus an owner decision that is still open (item 6). Flag for the owner: remove the furnace question now, or keep it until the replacement is built.

**F. Family draws: owner's "no dice," or a draw from Census shares?**

- Standing rule (from AGENTS.md, not in Drive): no seeded roll or fixed percentage decides what a person does. Seeded generation of who exists is allowed.
- Newer build report, September 29, 12:50 a.m. (item 2): the family draw was merged. Its own text calls it "a draw from measured shares at the start, before play, not a choice a person makes."
- September 29, 6:55 a.m. (item 3): the same hand-back asks the owner to rule on the two family draws, and says the rules they would otherwise come from "are themselves chance rolls, and they are on my zero-dice list."
- Flag. Either the owner accepts the start-up draw as seeded generation, or the family must be produced from the decision rules in item 3. The build status (12:50, "NEEDS LAMONTAE: Nothing") and the 6:55 proposal (which needs a yes or no) disagree on whether a ruling is still needed.

**G. Build 7 upbringing choices (Part B).**

- The September 28 handoff says Part B (growing-up choices) "is still with Lamontae... Do not build it until he approves." The September 29, 12:50 a.m. hand-back says "NEEDS LAMONTAE: Nothing" and lists only Part A as built. The September 28 research (item 7) asks two questions, one of which was answered (item 4). The second (how many choices) has no recorded answer.
