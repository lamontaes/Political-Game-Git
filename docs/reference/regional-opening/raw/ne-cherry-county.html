About Our County

County Seat: Valentine
Founded: 1883
Named After: Lt. Samuel A. Cherry
Region: Nebraska Sandhills
Time Zones: Central & Mountain

Quick Facts

  • Land Area: 5,960 sq mi (largest in Nebraska)
  • Population (2025): ~5,506
  • Population Density: <1 person/sq mi

Towns & Villages

  • Valentine – 2,633
  • Cody – 167
  • Merriman – 88
  • Crookston – 73
  • Kilgore – 69
  • Wood Lake – 48
  • Nenzel – 17
  • Brownlee – 9
  • Unincorporated: Sparks, Eli

Schools

Public School Districts:

  • Valentine City Schools
  • Cody-Kilgore Public Schools
  • Plus rural districts

Local Highlights

  • Valentine – Cherry County Tourism
  • Home to Nebraska’s tallest waterfall (Smith Falls)
  • Fort Niobrara National Wildlife Refuge
  • Famous for re-mailing Valentine’s Day cards with a special stamp
