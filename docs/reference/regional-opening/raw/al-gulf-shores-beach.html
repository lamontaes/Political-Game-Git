Beaches

Gulf State Park boasts over 3.5 miles of beautiful white sand beaches available in both our main park area in Gulf Shores at the Beach Pavilion and from our access points in Orange Beach, Alabama. Our family-friendly beaches are a perfect place to enjoy some much-needed time of fun and relaxation. Some beach access points offer food and drink on a seasonal basis. Please visit the Dining page to see what options you may have. 

 

At each Gulf State Park beach access point, you will see a flag indicating water conditions. Please review the flyers below to learn more about the beach flags and rip tide warnings.

Parking Access and Rates

Parking QR Codes are located at the Gulf State Park Fishing and Education Pier and the Beach Pavilion in Gulf Shores, as well as at the public beach accesses in Orange Beach, including Romar Beach, Cotton Bayou, Alabama Point, and Shell Beach.  Daily pass purchase is valid for all park beach accesses regardless of location of purchase.

  • QR Code: If you pay using the new QR Code, you will enter your license plate number into the secure app and follow the instructions. This will register your vehicle for the day into our new system. NO receipt is required in the vehicle.
  • To pre-purchase parking permit or pay for parking tickets go to Gulf State Park Patron Portal

 

Daily Parking Rates:

$15.00 – All day for personal vehicle

$35.00 – All day for vehicles with a trailer, buses, or large RVs (Parking for trailers, buses, or large RVs is permitted at the Beach Pavilion and Pier only - no overnight camping)

 

Annual Beach Access Parking Permit:

Annual Parking 

  • $200.00 per registered vehicle
  • Available for purchase at Park Headquarters (Monday thru Friday)
  • Available for purchase at the Visitor Center (Daily)
  • Valid for one calendar year 
  • One permit per vehicle

Orange Beach Resident Parking 

  • Free with a valid driver's license displaying the Orange Beach address
  • Available at Orange Beach City Hall under Orange Beach Policy
  • Valid for one calendar year
  • One decal per vehicle

United States Military Service Member Parking 

  • Free for active, former, and veteran service members as defined by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.
  • Proper proof of service and a valid ID are required.
  • Available at Gulf State Park Headquarters (Monday thru Friday)
  • Available at the Visitor Center (Daily)
  • Permit does not expire
  • One registered vehicle per service member

 

Leave Only Footprints

Alabama beaches are beautiful and pristine. The Leave Only Footprints program protects our beaches so that we may enjoy the coast for many years to come. You can do your part by following beach ordinances and disposing of trash. You can use our toy boxes found at every beach access to leave toys behind! Take a toy from these boxes as well and enjoy a fun day on the beach! Please help us preserve our beaches while having a relaxing, safe vacation by following the beach regulations.

 

Visit Leave Only Footprints to learn more about the important conservation initiatives in Gulf Shores and Orange Beach.

Share the Beach 

The Alabama Gulf Coast offers nesting beaches for sea turtles during the summer months. A volunteer program, Share the Beach, devotes time and passion to Gulf State Park and the surrounding beaches to protect the sea turtles. 

 

Visit the organization's website to learn more about this conservation program and what you can do to help!

Commercial Photographers

Commercial Photographers must purchase an annual permit for $100 to use any of the Gulf State Park beach access points listed above as a venue.

 

Complete the Commercial Photographers Permit Form and email it along with your business license to gulfevents.statepark@dcnr.alabama.gov or call 251-948-7275 ext. 7
