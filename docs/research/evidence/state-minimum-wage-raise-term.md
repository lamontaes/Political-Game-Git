# A state minimum-wage raise with no stated figure

A state bill that answers "raise the minimum wage?" with yes, and names no dollar figure, raises the state rate by $2.00 an hour, in steps of $0.90 a year, from the law's effective date.

Both numbers are the median of what states actually did from January 2013 to January 2024, measured from the Department of Labor's table of every state's January 1 rate. They are estimates from the average, not the terms of any real bill.

| Measure                                | Median | Middle half of states | Count        |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | --------------------- | ------------ |
| Total raise over one stretch of raises | $2.00  | $1.36 to $3.24        | 68 stretches |
| Single-year raise of $0.50 or more     | $0.90  | $0.75 to $1.00        | 161 raises   |

A stretch is consecutive years in which a state's rate rose $0.50 or more. Cost-of-living increases of $0.50 or more are counted with the statutory ones because the table does not separate them.

Source: U.S. Department of Labor, Wage and Hour Division, [Changes in Basic Minimum Wages in Non-Farm Employment Under State Law: Selected Years 1968 to 2024](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/state/minimum-wage/history), fetched September 29, 2026.

The figures live in `data/research/labor/state-minimum-wage-raise-term.json`.
