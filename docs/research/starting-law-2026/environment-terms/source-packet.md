## Farm payments

The [official FSA payment-limitations table](https://www.fsa.usda.gov/tools/informational/payment-eligibility/payment-limitations) publishes a 2026 ARC/PLC combined limit of $164,000 per person or legal entity. Peanuts and other commodities have separate limits for each program year. [7 U.S.C. §1308(b), (c), (i)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/7/1308) sets a $155,000 base adjusted annually with CPI-U; the 2025 amendment increased the previous base. Use FSA's published adjusted amount rather than an invented inflation calculation.

This is not a universal farm-payment cap and is not evidence that the policy question “cut farm subsidies” should start with a yes answer. Marketing-loan gains and loan-deficiency payments are excluded from these limits; conservation and disaster programs have different rules. The [September 2026 FSA ARC/PLC fact sheet](https://www.usda.gov/sites/default/files/guidance-documents/FSA.ARC-PLC-Fact%20Sheet-2026.pdf) describes enrolled program interests and crop/program-year coverage, not calendar-year cash totals.

Measured in source: the existing farm payment survivor reads a `dollars/year` cap, groups authorized `farm:*` commitments by recipient, and subtracts calendar-year paid transfers. It does not provide separate peanut/nonpeanut program-year buckets or direct/indirect attribution. Feeding $164,000 into that unchanged reader would apply the researched rule to the wrong scope. The missing starting question row remains untouched; no farm cap is installed from this evidence alone.

## Required consumer binding

Measured in source: starting rows accept numeric terms, but no categorical terms. The numeric unit type includes minor currency units and annual dollars, not the catalog's per-container or per-tonne units. The existing starting numeric reader requires an exact unit match. The categorical reader accepts enacted provisions and rejects starting origin. Team6 owns the shared required-terms guard; these inputs require its unit/coverage contract before an additive row patch.

The environmental outcome-web links currently consume a Boolean law answer, not numeric dose or container coverage. Those links are existing survivors, not proof that these monetary terms have been applied. No new deposit charge, refund writer, or modeled quantity is proposed in this packet.
