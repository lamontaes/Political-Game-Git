### What vote shares real American elections produce

**P0** · asked by running for office across America (lamontae, 2026-09-22 at 6:11 p.m. Eastern ('93% is super unrealistic'), filed by the research intake thread) · filed 2026-09-22 · `realistic-vote-shares-and-how-far-a-campaign-moves-them`

**The question.** lamontae watched a playtest candidate win on election day with 93% of the vote and called it super unrealistic. Answer with empirical distributions, nationwide, not a single state. (1) For each race type (nonpartisan municipal council and mayor; partisan county office; state house and state senate; statewide executive; U.S. House; U.S. Senate), what is the distribution of the winner's share and of the margin, split by: contested versus uncontested; two candidates versus three or more; incumbent running versus open seat; and district partisanship (safe, lean, competitive)? (3) How do opponents respond: how often do they campaign back, and how does a rival's own activity bound what one campaign can gain? **Why it matters.** The game counts the vote as each candidate's campaign support share plus a seeded swing of at most 3.5 points (src/simulation/campaigns.ts evaluateCampaignAwareOutcome, at main 2bf9ba97). Support rises with every outreach session as a transfer from the field, 1.5 basis points per volunteer-minute times a 0.6 to 1.41 draw, with no diminishing returns and no ceiling other than a 1% floor for everyone else (requestedGainBasisPoints and campaign-support.ts SUPPORT_FLOOR_BASIS_POINTS). ## For whoever is answering these

A question belongs here if no explicit research has been done on it. That is
the owner's instruction and it is wider than the test this queue used to
apply, which asked whether a question was bulky enough to be worth handing
over. So expect this document to grow, and expect more of it to be judgment
than citation: design questions belong here too, not only factual surveys.

Two kinds of answer are worth as much as a filled-in table. "It depends, and
here is what it depends on" is a real answer to a design question. So is "I
checked and could not establish this", with what was checked — which beats a
plausible guess, because a guess silently disables the refusal that would
otherwise have told somebody the fact was missing.

Replies go in **CHATGPT REPLIES TO CLAUDE — CURRENT**, or the Art Bench
exchange when the subject is art. A reply left as a comment on this document
never arrives: our Drive connector cannot read comments at all, on any
document, so the channel reads as silent while you are answering.

Superseded renders of this document are renamed and archived rather than
binned, so anything left on an older copy survives.
