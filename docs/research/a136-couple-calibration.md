# A136 couple-stage calibration sources

These sources check aggregate outcomes after recorded people decide. They do
not supply an actor's probability, desire, missing facts, or a hard ceiling.
Numeric decision weights remain in the shared decision evaluator's table.

The October 2, 2026 CTO ruling admits the existing half-year dating minimum
before moving in and one-year cohabitation minimum before marrying. It also
requires independent mutual selection for moving in or marrying, permits
unilateral breakup or separation, and leaves divorce to its legal process.
The shared stage contract preserves those rules; it does not choose anyone's
action or replace the town producer.

Calibration should report cohort, observation horizon, first versus later
marriage, and separation versus legal divorce separately. A five-year watched
run cannot establish a 15-year disruption estimate. Outcomes may exceed these
ranges; rates never select a person. Missing faith, family-value, housing-cost,
distance, child or relationship records contribute no invented consideration.

Existing town-family chance constants were explicitly game assumptions, and
the repository's earlier marriage research question remained open. These
publisher rows replace that absence of calibration provenance; no current
simulation calibration result is claimed here.
