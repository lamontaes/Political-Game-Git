## A06 — Party bodies act because something matters to them

**Question:** `what-makes-a-party-body-take-up-a-question`.

The agenda item names the issue, grounds for review, body and initiators. Participants evaluate actual alternatives using their own goals, concerns, relationships and information. The organization's adopted outcome does not rewrite every participant's belief.

**Acceptance:** an empty agenda remains quiet; unrelated public news does not force a party crisis; repeated clicks on the same complaint do not create more independent dissenters; actual changed terms can reopen a decision; later organization records preserve their real history and members.
