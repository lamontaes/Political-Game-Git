# Jobs should reflect the place and make a real offer

The owner has settled the Jobs screen's main direction. Ordinary work should vary with the place and remain optional to play in detail. A listing should describe a specific in-world offer, and getting hired should involve people and employers rather than an automatic button. The nationwide research can guide plausible industries and pay, but it does not identify actual openings. Engineering can connect these decisions to canonical organizations and work histories without turning every job into a played shift.

## The eight answers for the Jobs request

1. **Who employs people.** Generate private employers whose kinds and scale fit the place. Use a real public body's name when an official source verifies that body and its jurisdiction. A verified name does not create a vacancy. The mix and number of organizations should reflect a small town or large city rather than repeat one list everywhere. Preserve organizations as stable in-world identities so their work and people can continue changing.
2. **Which jobs appear.** Offer varied ordinary occupations, including jobs available without advanced education, real full-time possibilities, and roles that require a relevant credential. Use the place's industry evidence and simulated circumstances to shape the mix. Do not copy a county total into a town or treat a missing source cell as zero. Agriculture and government work need sources or explicit bounded game profiles because the main Census employer-business file excludes much of that work. Campaign and political-adjacent jobs can arise, but should not crowd out ordinary work.
3. **What a listing says.** Show the title, employer, actual work area, expected weekly hours or schedule, offered pay, qualifications, and start date if known. Keep a regional wage median behind optional labor-market detail, not beside the offer as though it were promised pay. Show only what the character could reasonably learn about the opening.
4. **How pay reads.** Quote hourly work by the hour and salaried work by annual salary. Use per-shift pay only if that employer actually offers it. The offered amount can vary by employer, role, and place within a plausible locally informed range. The in-world offer, not a published median, governs the character's compensation if hired. Applicable wage rules still constrain generated offers where established; unknown rules require an explicit game profile rather than a guessed legal claim.
5. **How a person gets hired.** A character may find and apply to an opening or receive an opportunity through a relationship. A contact can introduce or recommend them but does not guarantee the job. The employer may decline an application for a reason connected to the role and applicant. Keep a refused offer distinct from an unsuccessful application. A job search is an inspectable, optional screen, not a requirement to play every workday.
6. **When work starts.** An ordinary opening and a personal unanswered offer have finite, variable availability under the [current owner decision register](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bZWrzjUgDql2CIo_k1ElcQ2GrBZOzC_xJs8D-JswKpE/edit); the reply deadline must be visible when relevant and cannot reset on reread or save load. A valid acceptance ends the unanswered-offer deadline. It records an expected start date; active work begins only when the person starts. If that date passes without a start, the employer may follow up or withdraw the accepted offer according to the situation, as the owner answered today. A missed start is separate from an unanswered-offer expiry, and a listing is “filled” only after a represented hire. Routine waiting or choosing to simulate work is not itself a player penalty. Record meaningful consequences through the employer, the character's knowledge, and existing work history. Exact deadline lengths remain private calibration, not an owner-selected number.
7. **How work meets politics.** Ordinary employment remains the base. Public employment, campaign or office work, unions, and possible patronage connect to the relevant organizations, law, relationships, authority, and histories when those systems support them. A political contact can create an opportunity, not automatic appointment or office power. Specific union, civil-service, campaign-finance, and patronage rules remain jurisdiction or system research questions; do not hardcode one national rule.
8. **What the owner answered.** The owner selected place-based private employers plus sourced public bodies; offer facts with a median in optional detail; each job's actual pay terms; both applications and relationship offers; and context-aware follow-up or withdrawal for a missed start. These five answers resolve the choices asked in one batch after checking prior owner records. The remaining research and legal gaps below do not reopen those decisions.

## Two illustrative listings for wording review

These are fictional sample offers, not claims that these employers or openings exist. Pay, dates, and requirements below are example world facts for copy review only. A real listing must render the offer and the character's knowledge recorded in that save.

**Small town — Ely, Nevada, illustrative generated world**

> **Parts counter clerk**  
> Cedar Basin Auto Supply · Ely  
> $18.50 an hour · usually 36–40 hours a week  
> Help customers find parts and keep the counter stocked. No certificate required; some Saturday hours.  
> Earliest start: October 5.

**Large city — Long Beach, California, illustrative generated world**

> **Patient scheduler**  
> Harborline Clinic · Long Beach  
> $23.40 an hour · usually 38–40 hours a week, weekdays  
> Schedule visits and answer patient calls. Prior scheduling experience requested; no license required.  
> Earliest start: October 12.

The copy should omit any field the world does not know, rather than invent it. The labor-market median is a separate optional view. An actual public listing would use the verified body's name and a simulated, recorded opening.

## What happens next

Engineering can use the owner choices as the product brief. Its own source and save checks must decide the implementation route and protect existing work identities and old saves. Jobs should be tested in more than one place, including a territory when coverage is available, with a real search, application or introduction, possible rejection, accepted start date, missed start, and saved continuation. This handoff is research and owner direction; no game change or playtest is claimed here.
