## Decision and evidence boundaries

The owner chat export is direction, not source evidence about labor markets or law. It supports optional, varied work; accessible and credential-linked roles; full-time possibilities; location/pay inspection; naturally arising political-adjacent opportunities; and meaningful effects on the rest of life. Do not turn examples such as a Democratic organization, mayor's project, or college presidency into universal job availability.

The accepted decision log establishes canonical organizations, work relationships, effective-dated histories, expected versus active work, and recurring work-load representation. It explicitly leaves adult career content and player UI as dependency-bound future work. It does not answer the eight listing and hiring choices. [D-037](</Users/lamontae/Documents/PG-LAND/docs/decisions/DECISION-LOG.md:378>); [D-039](</Users/lamontae/Documents/PG-LAND/docs/decisions/DECISION-LOG.md:412>).

The nationwide jobs research notes that Census CBP, LODES, and BLS OEWS provide different labor-market baselines and coverage, not a complete place-level employer or vacancy list. Any use must retain geography, year, covered population, and suppressed/missing cells. Source data can inform a bounded proposal; it cannot decide whether the game should generate businesses or how an offer resolves.
