# Governor qualifications across 52 jurisdictions

Research status: September 22, 2026. The nationwide baseline below is Council of State Governments (CSG), *Book of the States* 2023, Table 4.2, “The Governors: Qualifications for Office,” based on a 2023 survey of governors’ offices. These broad fields are explicitly labeled **SECONDARY UNVERIFIED** unless a row says **PRIMARY VERIFIED**. CSG reports state-citizenship duration, U.S.-citizenship duration, state-residence duration, and qualified-voter duration; ✓ means a formal provision with no duration specified, ✕ means CSG reports no formal provision. Its footnotes warn that U.S. citizenship and state residence can arise indirectly from elector qualifications. This baseline is a research lead, not current-law certification. CSG marks starred rows as carried forward due to nonresponse. Candidate filing and continuing holding restrictions remain UNKNOWN unless directly verified below.

## Reading the evidence

- CSG’s table reports broad nationwide dimensions and values but does not supply exact legal citations row by row. It is a secondary survey, published in 2023, and must not be treated as current primary law. The symbols ✓ and ✕ are reproduced with CSG’s definitions; in particular, ✕ is not represented as a verified legal absence.
- CSG notes some values were carried forward because a state did not respond. An asterisk marks those rows. The table is useful for broad coverage while the official-source verification remains open.
- California is a material qualification: its constitutional text states five years of state residence, while the California Secretary of State says that residency clause is unconstitutional and unenforceable. A game rule should not silently treat the printed clause as an enforceable bar.
- “State citizenship” is included because CSG tracks it separately from U.S. citizenship; it is not assumed to mean state residence.
- Verified sample: California, Florida, New Hampshire, New York, Texas, Vermont, and Virginia, plus Alaska, D.C., and Puerto Rico. Exact timing is stated only when the controlling text says it. For all other rows, timing and other bars remain UNKNOWN.
