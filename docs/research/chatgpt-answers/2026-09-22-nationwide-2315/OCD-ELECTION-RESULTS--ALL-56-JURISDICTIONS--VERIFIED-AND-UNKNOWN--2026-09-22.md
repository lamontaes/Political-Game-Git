# Election results: complete 56-jurisdiction source-status matrix

**Research date:** 2026-09-22. **Coverage:** 50 states, the District of Columbia, and the five permanently inhabited U.S. territories. This is a **complete row inventory with partially verified legal detail**, matching the owner’s instruction to transmit nationwide coverage with marked unknowns. It does not claim that a state rule applies to every office, local election, special election, federal contest, or territory. `UNKNOWN` means the bounded official-source pass did not establish the exact rule; it does not mean no rule exists. Every filled legal claim has an inline official source. The source vintage is 2026-09-22 unless a row names an older document. No values from this matrix have been implemented or playtested.

Read this alongside the [election results, recounts and ties process handoff](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1W8Tv96azR9lczvfxfu5SaiHirCuj-kv1/view) for the distinction between unofficial returns, media calls, canvass/certification, recount, final tie resolution and lawful start of authority. The owner has chosen a few skippable election-night updates, separate projection and certification, a lawful option to seek recount or concede, and a visible decisive event for a final tie. These are presentation choices, not uniform legal rules.

## Reading the unknowns

The rows are complete for all 56 jurisdictions, but numerous authority, deadline, recount and tie cells remain UNKNOWN. In particular, territorial rules and office-specific exceptions need primary-text follow-up before they become simulation rules. Do not turn an UNKNOWN into zero days, no recount, a generic coin toss, or immediate authority. New Mexico and Ohio are deliberately downgraded where the source located was only a rulemaking/index pointer. The table is a legal research queue and coverage handoff, not a release declaration.

Primary research components: 25-state A–MO pass and 31-jurisdiction MT–territories pass, both QA-revised on 2026-09-22. Their detailed limits are preserved in the separate research files; row claims and links above are the authoritative handoff content for this snapshot.
