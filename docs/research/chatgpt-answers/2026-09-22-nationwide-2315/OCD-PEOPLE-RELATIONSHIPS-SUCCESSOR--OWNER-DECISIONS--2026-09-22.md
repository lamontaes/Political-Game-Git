# People, relationships and successor: owner decisions for existing lanes

**Date:** 2026-09-22. **Status:** direct owner intent, no coding or playtest claim. Send to People and life, Relationships, and Playtesting through existing ownership.

## Who

The current playable person, an eventual successor who already exists in the world, and their separately recorded contacts. A successor is not a blank extension of the prior character. Other people can initiate contact from their own lives.

## What the owner chose

- **Successor:** continue as an existing person with their own work, contacts and history. The handoff presents a shortlist of people connected to the current life and also a searchable route to **any eligible existing person in the world**. Show each person's own work and relationships before selection. They may know *of* or know the previous character, but do not inherit that person's friendships, trust, spouse, job, or social knowledge by default. The existing world and its history persist.
- **Greeting — corrected owner decision:** it is a dated contact and can preserve familiarity and some existing trust. Context can also increase trust a little when the person already admires the other person or the encounter is personally meaningful. A routine hello usually has little effect; there is no universal trust bonus. The earlier “familiarity only, never trust” statement is superseded.
- **NPC initiative:** a known person may initiate a call, invitation or request when their own schedule, relationship and reason support it. The player can answer, decline or leave it for later. Their initiative is an in-world action, not an unsolicited generic popup disconnected from their life.
- **Unanswered invitation:** the invitation expires at its event time. Whether the inviter notices or feels let down depends on the relationship, what was asked, and whether they reasonably expected an answer; ignoring every invitation must not produce one automatic trust penalty. If there is a meaningful consequence, surface it in the **next interaction with that person**, instead of issuing a standalone notification solely for this missed answer.
- **Writing:** name a relationship naturally in player text, e.g. “Dean Campos — your housemate,” without the mechanical category “who you live with.” Do not invent a spouse, job or picnic proofreading scene for a successor whose facts do not support it. The owner previously rejected those examples.

## When, where, why, how

When the current life ends or a permitted continuation point occurs, the handoff offers connected people first and a route to other existing people whose life and relationship records already exist. The exact browsing/filter UI remains open. Contact happens through an appropriate channel and at an available time. A greeting's trust effect depends on the character's existing regard and the meaning of the encounter; do not apply a uniform bonus or forbid all trust effects. The purpose is a world of people with independent continuity, while keeping ordinary player detail optional. Reuse the existing relationship work (PR #395 was the named base in the owner packet); verify its present merge/head status before implementing.

## Open boundaries

The owner has not chosen the eligibility rules for selecting another existing person, how long greeting familiarity lasts, numerical trust effects, or decay rates. The current report is not a claim those mechanics already run. A player-facing retest should hand off to a connected person and also inspect the searchable route to another eligible existing person; verify preexisting contacts/work, compare a routine hello with a meaningful admired-person encounter, leave an invitation unanswered past its event time, advance time to the next interaction with that person, and confirm outcomes follow recorded context after save/reopen.
