# What governors and equivalent executives can do in all 56 U.S. jurisdictions

For the game, these are the legal powers to check before a governor, D.C. mayor or territorial executive acts. All 50 states, D.C. and five inhabited territories appear below. The cited sources support the stated facts at the scope shown; some details still need direct current-law confirmation. **UNKNOWN** means the particular power or limit has not yet been established from a primary source. An unknown is never permission, a ban or a national default. This is a research intake, not a decision to add every listed action to the player menu.

## How to use this research

- Use a verified clause only at its cited scope and simulated date. A specific judicial vacancy or emergency transfer does not establish broad appointment or reorganization authority.
- In the game, veto presentment, timing, override and item-veto rules belong to the legislative rule pack under accepted decision D-083. The executive authority pack references that pack; it must not duplicate the veto rule.
- A state or territorial constitution may be changed during play. The game must resolve the rule effective at the simulated date, then record what action was possible and what actually happened.
- The outstanding UNKNOWN cells need later primary-source work before becoming enforcement rules. This document is intentionally complete by place while leaving those details visible.

- **Veto:** Five days excluding Sundays in session; if general adjournment prevents return, 20 days after adjournment excluding Sundays. Whole-bill veto and veto of full sections or appropriation items; cannot veto less than an entire section except appropriation items. Wash. Const. art. III, §12 ([official text](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/Constitution/State%20Constitution%20Articles%20and%20Amendments%20Combined/WA%20CONSTITUTION.htm)).
- **Appointments/removal:** UNKNOWN for general confirmation and removal rules.
- **Emergency/termination:** UNKNOWN for general disaster powers/termination; Constitution art. II, §42 concerns continuity during emergency periods.
- **Budget:** UNKNOWN for Governor's proposal, cuts and balance duties.
- **Orders/reorganization:** UNKNOWN.
- **Clemency:** Pardon power vested in Governor subject to statutory regulation/restriction. Wash. Const. art. III, §9 ([official text](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/Law/Constitution/Articles/Article%20%203/Article%20%203.09.htm)).
- **Special session:** Governor may proclaim session for up to 30 consecutive days; Legislature may also call by two-thirds resolution. Wash. Const. art. II, §12 ([official text](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/Constitution/Articles/Article%20%202/Article%20%202.12.htm)).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** Constitution lists Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, Treasurer, Auditor, Attorney General, Superintendent of Public Instruction and Commissioner of Public Lands as executive officers. Art. III, §§1–3 and succession provisions ([official Constitution index](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/Constitution/Table%20of%20Contents/TABLE%20OF%20CONTENTS.htm)).

### West Virginia (WV)

- **Veto:** Five days excluding Sundays while session; adjourned action period and return mechanics require article-level confirmation. For budget bills, five days excluding Sundays; if adjournment prevents return, file within five days after adjournment. W.Va. Const. art. VI, §51 ([official Constitution](https://www.wvlegislature.gov/wvcode/wv_con.cfm)).
- **Appointments/removal:** Governor appoints specified officers with Senate advice/consent and has removal power subject to constitutional/statutory grounds. Detailed scope UNKNOWN ([official Constitution](https://www.wvlegislature.gov/wvcode/wv_con.cfm)).
- **Emergency/termination:** UNKNOWN for general disaster declarations and termination. Legislature has continuity provisions for enemy attack ([official Constitution](https://www.wvlegislature.gov/wvcode/wv_con.cfm)).
- **Budget:** Governor submits budget and proposed appropriations; may revise agency estimates other than legislative/judicial. Veto, reduce or disapprove budget items. W.Va. Const. art. VI, §51 ([official Constitution](https://www.wvlegislature.gov/wvcode/wv_con.cfm)).
- **Orders/reorganization:** UNKNOWN.
- **Clemency:** Governor may remit fines, commute capital punishment, and grant reprieves, paroles and pardons after conviction except House prosecutions; must report each case and reasons to Legislature. W.Va. Const. art. VII, §11; implementing code §5-1-16 ([official statute](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/5-1-16/)).
- **Special session:** Governor may convene when public safety/welfare requires and must convene on written application of three-fifths of elected members of each house. Art. VI, §19 ([official Constitution](https://www.wvlegislature.gov/wvcode/wv_con.cfm)).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** Governor, Secretary of State, State Superintendent of Free Schools, Treasurer, Auditor, Attorney General and Commissioner of Agriculture are statewide elected. W.Va. Const. art. VII, §§1–2; W.Va. Code §6-5-1 ([official code](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/6-5-1/)).

### Wisconsin (WI)

- **Veto:** Whole-bill veto; six days excluding Sundays. If final adjournment prevents return, an unsigned bill does not become law. V, §10 ([official Constitution portal](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/constitution); [official state documents reproduction](https://www.wistatedocuments.org/digital/api/collection/p267601coll4/id/32552/download)). - **Appointments/removal:** UNKNOWN for appointment confirmation, recess appointments and removal.
- **Emergency/termination:** UNKNOWN.
- **Budget:** UNKNOWN.
- **Orders/reorganization:** UNKNOWN.
- **Clemency:** Governor may grant reprieves, commutations and pardons after conviction, except treason and impeachment, under conditions and restrictions the Governor sets, subject to statutory rules for pardon applications. Governor reports grants annually to the Legislature. Wis. Const. art. V, §6 ([official state documents reproduction](https://www.wistatedocuments.org/digital/api/collection/p267601coll4/id/32552/download)).
- **Special session:** Governor may convene the Legislature on extraordinary occasions; in invasion or danger from contagious disease at the seat of government, the Legislature may be convened elsewhere in Wisconsin. Wis. Const. art. V, §4 ([official Constitution portal](https://docs.legis.wisconsin.gov/constitution)).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, Treasurer, Attorney General and State Superintendent of Public Instruction are statewide elected officers. The Superintendent is nonpartisan; other listed offices are partisan. ([official state annual report](https://doa.wi.gov/DEBFCapitalFinance/2024/CompleteAnnualReport_2024_vF_2024.12.20.pdf)).

## D.C. and five inhabited territories

For D.C. and the territories, the table separates the local executive from congressional or federal approval and review. Federal limits are stated only where a source was identified; UNKNOWN remains an explicit research gap.

### District of Columbia (Mayor)

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Mayor approves or disapproves Council acts within 10 calendar days, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and holidays. Council may override within 30 calendar days by two-thirds of members present and voting. Acts are separately subject to congressional review, ordinarily 30 legislative days or 60 for specified criminal matters. [D.C. Code §1-204.04(e)](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/1-204.04); [§1-206.02(c)](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/1-206.02).
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known in part:** Mayor administers appointment/promotion/discipline/separation for covered executive personnel; agency heads generally subject to Council confirmation. [D.C. Code §§1-204.22, 1-523.01](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/index.full.html). Full removal matrix **unknown**.
- **Emergency and termination:** **Known in part:** Mayor may declare public emergency under D.C. Code ch. 7-23; declarations and extensions are time-limited and Council controls extension by resolution. Exact current maximum/renewal conditions **unknown** in this pass. [D.C. Code ch. 7-23](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/7/chapters/23/index.full.html).
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known:** Mayor submits proposed budget to Council; Congress retains final control through the federal appropriations process and Home Rule Act restrictions. Detailed executive reductions/balance rules **unknown**. [D.C. Code §1-204.42](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/index.full.html); [31 U.S.C. §1301 note / Home Rule Act framework](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/index.full.html).
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Known in part:** Mayor directs executive departments and executes District law; may reorganize functions only within legal/appropriation limits. General unilateral reorganization breadth **unknown**. [D.C. Code §1-204.22](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/sections/1-204.22).
- **Clemency and board:** **Known:** D.C. local mayor has no general criminal clemency power; federal clemency is presidential. The District has a statutory Clemency Board, but it concerns recommendations/policy rather than transferring the President’s constitutional clemency authority. [D.C. Code ch. 24-4A](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/24/chapters/4A/index.full.html).
- **Special session:** **Known:** Mayor cannot call Council special session unilaterally; Council is a continuing body with prescribed sessions. Specific call mechanisms beyond those rules **unknown** here. [D.C. Code ch. 1-2, subchapter IV](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/index.full.html).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Mayor and Attorney General are elected; no other citywide executive officer included here as separately elected. [D.C. Code §§1-204.21, 1-301.81](https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/1/chapters/2/index.full.html).

### Puerto Rico

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Governor may sign or veto bills; item/line veto applies to appropriation bills. Window and override mechanics require current Art. III §19 statutory text check; **unknown** here. Federal approval is not a general routine presentment step for Puerto Rico local laws. [Puerto Rico Constitution, arts. III–IV](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM).
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known:** Governor appoints officers as constitution or law provides; appointments requiring Senate/bicameral consent made during recess expire at end of next regular session if not confirmed. [P.R. Const. art. IV, §4](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM). Full removal rules **unknown**.
- **Emergency and termination:** **Known:** Governor may call militia, summon posse, and proclaim martial law for rebellion/invasion/imminent danger; Legislature must meet promptly to ratify or revoke. [P.R. Const. art. IV, §4](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM). General disaster declaration/termination statutes **unknown** here.
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known:** Governor presents proposed expenditures for ensuing fiscal year at start of regular session with Treasury report. Detailed budget amendment, executive cut and balance mechanics **unknown**. [P.R. Const. art. IV, §4](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM).
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Known in part:** Governor executes laws and has constitutional executive authority; specific general-order/reorganization limits **unknown**.
- **Clemency and board:** **Known:** Governor may suspend criminal sentences, grant pardons and commutations, and remit fines/forfeitures. Parole Board advises on clemency under statute; it does not displace constitutional gubernatorial power. [P.R. Const. art. IV, §4](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM); [P.R. Parole Board law](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/139/JLBP.HTM).
- **Special session:** **Known:** Governor may convene Legislature or Senate in extraordinary session; subject limited to call/special message; session max 20 calendar days. [P.R. Const. art. III, §10](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Governor is directly elected; Resident Commissioner is elected federally but is not a Puerto Rico territorial executive officer. Cabinet secretaries and other constitutionally specified officers have appointment/confirmation processes. [P.R. Const. art. IV](https://presupuestosanteriores.pr.gov/af99/BASLEGAL/038/038.HTM).

### Guam

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Full veto; no item veto identified in the Organic Act. Return within 10 days excluding Sundays; if adjournment prevents return, law only if governor signs within 30 days. Legislative override is two-thirds; certain measures are transmitted for federal review under Organic Act procedures. [48 U.S.C. §1423i](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A48+section%3A1423i+edition%3Aprelim%29).
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known in part:** Governor appoints executive officers; department heads require Legislature’s advice and consent under Organic Act; governor has substantial supervisory/removal power over executive officers, subject to law. Exact office-by-office exceptions **unknown**. [48 U.S.C. §1422](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A48+section%3A1422+edition%3Aprelim%29); [Guam Organic Act](https://www.guamcourts.org/CompilerofLaws/GCA/OrganicAct/Organic%20Act.PDF).
- **Emergency and termination:** **Unknown:** current statutory declaration powers, duration and termination process not resolved. Organic Act commander-in-chief provisions do not establish a general civil disaster duration.
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known in part:** Legislature appropriates; governor administers appropriated funds. Detailed submission, veto/cut and balance procedures **unknown** here. [48 U.S.C. §1423j](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A48+section%3A1423j+edition%3Aprelim%29).
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Known in part:** governor executes law and directs executive departments; no broad authority for executive order to make law. Court authority recognizes limits on standalone orders. General reorganization scope **unknown**. [Guam Organic Act §6](https://www.guamcourts.org/CompilerofLaws/GCA/OrganicAct/Organic%20Act.PDF).
- **Clemency and board:** **Unknown:** local clemency authority and any board need confirmation in current Organic Act/local code. Do not infer a power or its absence.
- **Special session:** **Known:** Governor may call Legislature into special session; agenda is restricted to call/message. [48 U.S.C. §1423a](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A48+section%3A1423a+edition%3Aprelim%29).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Governor and Lieutenant Governor elected together; Legislature elected. Whether any additional executive positions are separately elected **unknown**; attorney general was made elective by local law, but current citation not verified here.

### U.S. Virgin Islands

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Governor may veto legislation; override is by two-thirds of elected members. Bills not returned within 10 days become law unless adjournment prevents return, in which case the governor has 30 days after presentation to sign. No general federal executive approval step identified; Congress retains territorial authority. [48 U.S.C. §1573](https://usvieda.org/sites/default/files/RevOrganicAct_1954.pdf).
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known in part:** Governor appoints department heads and specified officers; terms generally run with appointing governor and until successor, unless sooner removed by governor. Some quasi-judicial boards may require Legislature confirmation by law. [Revised Organic Act §16](https://usvieda.org/sites/default/files/RevOrganicAct_1954.pdf). Broader appointment exceptions **unknown**.
- **Emergency and termination:** **Unknown:** current local emergency declaration, renewal and termination provisions not resolved.
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known in part:** Governor submits budget; Legislature enacts appropriations; separate federal approval is not a general bill-presentment step. Detailed executive cuts, balanced-budget constraint and fiscal controls **unknown**.
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Known:** Organic Act directed executive department organization and governor exercised initial reorganization by executive order; current general order/reorganization authority **unknown**. [Revised Organic Act §16](https://usvieda.org/sites/default/files/RevOrganicAct_1954.pdf).
- **Clemency and board:** **Unknown:** clemency power and board status need direct current Organic Act/VI Code verification.
- **Special session:** **Known:** Governor may call Legislature at any time when public interest requires; special-session agenda is limited to call or special message. [48 U.S.C. §1572(a)](https://www.congress.gov/105/crpt/srpt22/CRPT-105srpt22.pdf).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Governor and Lieutenant Governor elected together. Legislature is elected. Other independently elected executive offices **unknown** in this pass.

### American Samoa

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Governor may sign or veto whole bills and object to appropriation items or parts. A bill not returned within 10 days, Sundays excluded, becomes law unless adjournment prevents return; then the governor may sign it within 30 days after adjournment. Within 14 months, two-thirds of the entire membership of each Fono house may repass a vetoed bill. If the governor still withholds approval for 15 days, the bill goes to the U.S. Secretary of the Interior, whose approval within 90 days is needed for it to become law. The 2024 referendum to remove that Interior step failed. [Revised Constitution of American Samoa art. II, §9](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/); [U.N. 2025 working paper, ¶8](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4079673/files/A_AC.109_2025_1-EN.pdf).
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known in part:** Governor and Lieutenant Governor are locally elected. Governor appoints officials except elective, Interior-appointed or otherwise provided offices; department-head confirmation is provided by local statute. Current confirmation/removal matrix **unknown**. [Revised Constitution art. IV, §§1, 11–12](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/); [ASCA §4.0112 discussion](https://asbar.org/case-law/governor-of-american-samoa-tuika-v/).
- **Emergency and termination:** **Known in part:** Governor may summon a posse or militia to prevent or suppress violence, invasion, insurrection or rebellion. This clause does not itself establish martial-law or disaster-declaration authority. General disaster-emergency duration and termination **unknown**. [Revised Constitution art. IV, §5](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/).
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known in part:** Fono makes appropriations; governor proposes/executes under territorial law. Current budget submission and balance/cut rules **unknown**.
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Known in part:** Governor may issue executive regulations not conflicting with law. General reorganization scope **unknown**. [Revised Constitution art. IV, §6](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/).
- **Clemency and board:** **Known:** Governor has clemency power for local offenses; no board is identified in the cited constitutional provision. [Revised Constitution art. IV, §9](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/).
- **Special session:** **Known:** Governor may convene Fono in special session and sets its start and duration. Other statutory limits **unknown**. [Revised Constitution art. II, §8](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/).
- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Governor and Lieutenant Governor are elected together. [Revised Constitution art. IV, §1](https://asbar.org/revised-constitution-of-american-samoa/). Other separately elected territorial executive officers **unknown**.

### Northern Mariana Islands

- **Veto form and window; federal review:** **Known:** Full veto plus item/section/part veto in appropriation bills; 20 days for appropriations, 40 days for other bills. Two-thirds in each house overrides. [NMI Const. art. II, §7](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution). Federal review/approval is not a routine local bill condition identified in this provision.
- **Appointments, confirmation and removal:** **Known in part:** Governor appoints executive officers under constitution/statute; Attorney General elected at large. Exact confirmation/removal scope **unknown** in this pass. [NMI Const. art. III, §10 and art. III, §11](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution).
- **Emergency and termination:** **Known:** Governor may declare emergency for invasion, civil disturbance, natural disaster or other calamity “as provided by law” and mobilize resources. Duration, extensions and termination **unknown** absent statutory follow-through. [NMI Const. art. III, §10](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution).
- **Budget, cuts and balance:** **Known in part:** Governor reports annually and includes comprehensive financial report; proposal/cut/balance details **unknown**. [NMI Const. art. III, §9](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution).
- **Orders and reorganization:** **Unknown:** general executive-order/reorganization rule not resolved in this pass.
- **Clemency and board:** **Known:** Governor grants reprieves, commutations and pardons after conviction after consultation with a board of parole established by law; impeachment excepted. [NMI Const. art. III, §9(c)](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution).

- **Separately elected executive officers:** **Known:** Governor/Lieutenant Governor elected together; Attorney General separately elected. [NMI Const. arts. III, §1, §11](https://cnmilaw.gov/constitution). Other separately elected executive posts **unknown**.
