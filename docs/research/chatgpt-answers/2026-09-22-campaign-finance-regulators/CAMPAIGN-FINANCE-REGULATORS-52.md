# Campaign-finance regulators: nationwide research handoff

**Coverage:** all 50 states, the District of Columbia, and Puerto Rico (52 jurisdictions). **Research checkpoint:** September 22, 2026. **Status:** broad agency and process source map, with gaps marked UNKNOWN. This is research input for the game, not an approved gameplay profile, legal advice, implementation, or a closed exact-law question.

## How to use this

- Each agency is a regulator or filing/enforcement lead identified from the linked public source. Some jurisdictions split filing, complaint intake, civil enforcement, and criminal referral; read the detail before assigning authority in the game.
- A cited overview does not verify every legal detail in its row. `UNKNOWN` means no fact should be generated from that field. Local office rules, deadlines, remedy amounts, and effective dates need provision-level checks before a legal profile is approved.
- Keep the game’s actual fictional case history and government-unit IDs separate from this real-world source map. A jurisdiction-wide game fallback should be labeled a simulated profile, not verified law.
- The live question `state-campaign-finance-regulators` remains open for exact authority, own-initiative review, complaint stages and deadlines, personal-use rule, and civil-penalty proof in every jurisdiction.

### Alaska

- **Regulator / lead:** Alaska Public Offices Commission (APOC).
- **Scope:** Expressly covers state and municipal campaign-finance reports; local campaign-finance ethics bodies not researched.
- **Own-initiative review or audit:** AS 15.13.030(7) directs APOC to examine, investigate, and compare required reports, statements, and actions; no review deadline located.
- **Complaint process:** AS 15.13.380(b)-(h): filing limit 5 years; expedited request decided in 2 days, hearing within 2 days if granted, action within 1 day after hearing; regular notice within 7 days, answer within 15 days, hearing within 45 days after answer due, order within 10 days after hearing; complainant may go to superior court if APOC has not completed action within 90 days; appeal within 30 days.
- **Personal use:** AS 15.13.112(b)(1)-(2) prohibits personal benefit and conversion to personal income.
- **Civil penalty / remedy:** AS 15.13.390(a)(4): up to $50 per day for each day a chapter violation continues. No fixed one-time personal-use penalty found; application to a completed expenditure UNKNOWN.
- **Still unknown:** civil_penalty
