# First modular conversation family: a goal meets a real opportunity

The owner chose **goals meeting real opportunities** as the first reusable conversation family for Claude to prove across different lives. A person's aim should change which existing openings they notice, pursue, or discuss. It must not create a vacancy, invitation, role, or willing counterpart by itself. The simulation supplies the opportunity and each participant's circumstances; dialogue expresses what they can ask, offer, negotiate, decline, or leave open. Broader personality-shaped wording can deepen this later without delaying the basic family.

## Inputs the family must read

Use the saved person's current aim and relevant past actions; an actual opening, request, or plan; its initiator and terms; time and place; eligibility and authority; schedules, resources, and existing commitments; the relationship and each person's knowledge. The initiator has a separate aim and may decide not to offer, agree, hire, help, or attend. The controlled person is never auto-answered. A conversation can end without a new relationship effect.

These are already accepted directions in the [Decision Register, OCD-LIFE-006/007 and OCD-CONTENT-001/002](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bZWrzjUgDql2CIo_k1ElcQ2GrBZOzC_xJs8D-JswKpE/edit) and [ALIVE44, paragraphs 159 and 176](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wDpqx3b9a_O0iqLgxm3YREJNN469pC4LPyJ2eJWD_jA/edit). The owner's priority choice was made in this conversation on September 23, 2026.

## Contrasting generated cases for review

These are **illustrations of fact combinations**, not scenes to encode or lines to copy.

| Saved facts | Conversation that can become available | What must stay open |
|---|---|---|
| A person wants to enter public life; a real group they know has a dated, accessible event; an organizer knows them and has a reason to approach. | They can ask about the event, offer to help, propose a different role, or decline. | The organizer may refuse or have no role to offer; attendance and later standing require actual action and observation. |
| A person wants different work; a verified opening fits their skills, but their current care commitment conflicts with the schedule. | They can ask about hours, seek an adjustment, apply, or leave the opening alone. | An application is not an offer; the employer decides, and the care obligation does not disappear because the conversation went well. |
| A person has a strong aim but no eligible opening or informed counterpart. | No offer conversation appears solely to give the aim screen something to do. | The life may be quiet; the aim can still guide what the person notices and later pursues. |

The first two cases should be generated in more than one place with meaningfully different participants, terms, constraints, and possible outcomes. Changing only names, titles, or geography fails this proof. One case should show a reasonable refusal or no agreement. The third is a necessary negative case: absence of an opportunity must remain absence, not an authored substitute.

## Proof to request from engineering

Show one ordinary player route from an already recorded opening to a conversation, a player choice, the other person's independent response, a canonical outcome, Journal wording, and Save/Continue. Repeat with contrasting records and a different seed. Show a no-opening case that produces no offer. Identify the original record and what each person knew before the scene. Keep time on actual activity, not on opening a dialogue panel. A spoken agreement does not prove work, payment, attendance, or trust change.

For the [56-jurisdiction run already under way](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1U3VkeDxkZ1h0sF77g4kWX56Hr_wltKjE/view), capture any goal–opportunity pairing the current build naturally produces. Mark it **not observed** when the run can check but none occurs, or **unmeasured** when the run cannot check; neither status is a pass. Do not restart the sweep to manufacture an example. The [resolved QAR1-08 decision](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bZWrzjUgDql2CIo_k1ElcQ2GrBZOzC_xJs8D-JswKpE/edit) makes reusable families, supported combinations, consequential ranges, and representative outcomes the unit of owner review; each generated instance does not need individual approval.
