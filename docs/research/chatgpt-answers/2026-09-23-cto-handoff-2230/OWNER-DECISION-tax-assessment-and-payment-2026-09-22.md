# Owner decision: tax assessment and payment

September 22, 2026. For Claude and the tax/legislation teams.

**Owner decision:** “Assess everyone; zero can be lawful.” People and businesses in the simulated world are evaluated under the taxes applicable to their income, wages, property, transactions or other taxable activity. A person or business may owe $0 because no taxable base exists or because a lawful exemption, deduction or credit applies. Liability and actual payment are separate records.

**Owner decision, later clarification:** “Yes, debt persists under local rules.” When a person or business cannot pay a tax when due, the unpaid balance remains in the world. Any notice, payment plan, penalty or enforcement must follow that place’s actual applicable law. Routine cases can be summarized unless they affect the character.

## What this means for the game

**Implementation consequence proposed for engineering:** Do not charge every adult a fixed tax simply for existing. Resolve jurisdiction, tax year/effective date, payer, base and applicable rules before computing liability. Record assessment, withholding or collection, payment and any outstanding amount separately. These records should connect to a tax bill, paycheck, receipt, filing or official account when that is how the character could know. Details of filing obligations, enforcement and visibility still require the applicable law and player-knowledge rules; this owner answer does not decide them.

This decision does not certify a 2026 tax rate or invent an exemption. The 56-place plus federal research intake remains source-bound, with every unverified rule marked UNKNOWN rather than zero or permission.
