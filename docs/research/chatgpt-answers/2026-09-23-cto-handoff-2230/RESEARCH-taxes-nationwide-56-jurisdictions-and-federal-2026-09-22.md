# Taxes across all 56 jurisdictions: current rules and marked gaps

This single intake covers the 50 states, D.C. and five inhabited U.S. territories, plus a separate federal layer. It identifies official current or latest-located income and sales/receipts rules where verified. It does not yet supply every payer, base, exemption, collection date or recipient the tax engine needs. Those cells are marked UNKNOWN. Do not use an UNKNOWN as zero or copy another state's rule.

## How to read this

**YES** means the cited official source establishes a tax category. **NO** needs an affirmative official statement of absence. **UNKNOWN** means this pass did not establish the field. A state rate is not a locality's combined rate. Tax year, filing year, fiscal year, and effective date are separate; a TY2025 schedule is labeled as such and is not certified as TY2026 law. Territorial tax systems and federal filing rules have separate source requirements. The rows below are authority/intake evidence, not a complete tax-code compilation or a ready rate table.

For every jurisdiction, unless a row says otherwise, parcel-specific property bills, tax-specific excise bases/rates, unemployment insurance rates and wage bases, final revenue destinations, detailed exemptions and credits, taxpayer-specific liability, and local rates remain UNKNOWN. “Payroll” includes wage withholding only when stated; withholding is not a separate final tax liability. Tax collection, bill, payment, and unpaid balance are distinct records.

## Federal layer

Checked September 22, 2026. This layer is separate from each state, D.C. and territorial tax regime. A federal tax category is not a claim that every resident of every territory owes it in the same way. Amount owed depends on a taxpayer's situation; withholding and tax liability are different records.

### Individual income tax

- **Imposed:** YES, subject to federal residence, source, filing, exemption, deduction and credit rules. For TY2026, seven marginal rates are 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%. Single taxable-income thresholds are $12,400, $50,400, $105,700, $201,775, $256,225 and $640,600. Filing-status thresholds differ. The TY2026 standard deduction is $16,100 single or married filing separately, $32,200 joint and $24,150 head of household. These are tax-year rules generally for returns filed in 2027, not 2026 returns about TY2025. [IRS 2026 adjustments](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill); [Revenue Procedure 2025-32](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB).
- **Payer/base:** individuals' federal taxable income after applicable adjustments/deductions; exact territorial coordination, tax residence and source rules differ. [IRS Publication 570](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p570). Employer withholding and estimated tax are collection pathways, not proof of final liability. Detailed filing exceptions, credits, payment cadence and revenue destination are UNKNOWN in this intake.

### Employment taxes

- **Social Security:** Employee 6.2% and employer 6.2% on covered wages up to $184,500 in 2026. **Medicare:** Employee 1.45% and employer 1.45% on covered wages, no wage cap. Employer withholds Additional Medicare Tax of 0.9% once wages paid to an employee exceed $200,000 in a calendar year; employee liability can differ based on filing status and all wages. [IRS Publication 15 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15). Coverage and territorial exceptions need taxpayer-specific review; not every person/business owes all types.
- **Federal unemployment (FUTA):** Employer tax generally 6% on first $7,000 in wages for each employee; a credit of up to 5.4% may reduce it, subject to state credit-reduction rules. Do not state a universal 0.6% effective rate. [IRS Publication 15 (2026)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15). Territorial application and particular employer coverage are UNKNOWN in this intake.

### Corporate income tax

- **Imposed:** YES on federal taxable income of corporations subject to 26 U.S.C. §11, generally 21% of taxable income; statutory exceptions and other entity classifications apply. [26 U.S.C. §11](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=(title:26%20section:11%20edition:prelim)). Detailed territorial corporation treatment, credits, minimum-tax rules, filing cadence and recipients are UNKNOWN in this intake.

### Federal excises and other categories

- Federal excises exist across specified activities, products and services; rates and liable parties depend on the line item rather than a general rate. [IRS Form 720 instructions, June 2026](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i720). Complete item-specific rates/bases/cadence and federal estate, gift, customs, property and other tax coverage are UNKNOWN here. Do not infer absence from this intake.

### Evidence limits for the game

No single tax above can be charged simply because a person exists or lives in the U.S. Determine tax by taxable income, wages, transaction, property, business or other legal base, applicable place and time. A lawful zero liability, nonfiling, withholding, tax due, payment and delinquency are separate states. Puerto Rico, Guam, USVI, American Samoa and CNMI need the specific rules in IRS Publication 570 and their own tax sources before any federal/territorial amount is applied. This intake does not provide a complete 2026 federal code, and the missing dimensions remain UNKNOWN.

## Remaining work before a tax can be charged in play

1. Build a row per tax, government level and place: liable person/entity, tax base, actual rate or bracket by effective date, exemption/deduction/credit, collection method and due period, collecting and receiving government, official citation, and verified/UNKNOWN status. Many of those fields above are still UNKNOWN. The 56 headings establish nationwide jurisdiction coverage, not complete legal coverage.
2. Match a tax to the actual address, taxable item, wage/source or parcel. Do not use a state headline rate as a combined local amount, and do not charge a business gross receipts tax to a buyer simply because it appears on a receipt.
3. Keep tax law, assessment, withholding/collection, payment and delinquency separate in the world record. Surface only records the character could know. A lawful zero, exemption or untaxed event must not become debt.
4. For any chosen playable place, verify the particular rule and then test a real pay period, purchase, parcel bill or business filing through the saved game before calling that place implemented. This research was a document/source pass; it was not a game playtest.
