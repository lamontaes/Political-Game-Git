## Federal vacancy: primary-law research and diagnostic request

- When a President dies, resigns or is removed, the Vice President **becomes President** under the [Twenty-Fifth Amendment, §1](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-25/). A vacancy in the Vice Presidency is filled by presidential nomination and majority confirmation in both houses under [§2](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-25/). Neither provision calls for silently fabricating an officeholder.
- For the joint vacancy, inability or failure-to-qualify case covered by [3 U.S.C. §19](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A3+section%3A19+edition%3Aprelim%29), the statute identifies an eligible Speaker, then President pro tempore, then specified Senate-confirmed department heads to **act** as President, with qualifications and resignation requirements. The trigger and tenure depend on which statutory case occurred. Acting under this statute is not becoming an elected President. The [Constitution Annotated's succession overview](https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/essay/amdt25-2-5/ALDE_00013876/) summarizes the order.
- At a term boundary, the [Twentieth Amendment, §§1 and 3](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/amendment-20/) controls the January 20 start and the President-elect/Vice-President-elect failure-to-qualify cases. The 2029 observation could instead reflect a missing election, failed certification, missing seat transition, or expired term; the running record does not resolve it.
