# Research for the number stand-ins (Oct 12, 2026)

The owner asked for the research behind the game's placeholder numbers, using what's already done in Google Drive and the repo first. Four research passes covered the 111 open number stand-ins on the pillar branches (registered stopgaps whose kind marks a number or an estimate). Each pass searched Drive and the repo, including the unmerged research branches, before doing new research. Machine-readable rows: `data/research/stand-in-figures-2026-10-12.json`.

- **30 researchable:** a real-world figure exists and is given with its source.
- **37 bounded:** a modeling choice that research can only bound; the bounds and a recommended value are given.
- **44 not research:** a missing mechanic or code gap that no number fixes; the row says what's missing.

Where it came from: most existing research sat in the repo, much of it on `pool/research-roadmap` and `pool/research-roadmap-2` (P0-2 to P0-12), which are being merged to main. Drive held a few items (the packages doc, the placeholder sort, local-turnout research). The rest is new, from primary sources.

## How to apply

Each owning session applies its own rows, listed below. For a researchable or bounded row: set the parameter to the recommended value, record the source in the parameter's `source`, and close the stopgap, or narrow it to what's still estimated. Leave not-research rows to the owning session's own roadmap. Research approval is delegated to the CTO (owner, Sept 30); flag any row that looks wrong to the CTO instead of guessing.

## Decisions for the owner

- **Opening data vintage:** nothing with a 2020 reference period was public on January 1, 2021 (the 2016–2020 ACS 5-year came out March 17, 2022). Recommended: use reference-period data, the year the numbers describe, rather than only what was published by opening day.
- **One download needs permission:** the county-versus-city spending split needs the Census 2022 Annual Survey of State and Local Government Finances public-use file (about 9.7 MB).
