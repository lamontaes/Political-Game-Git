## What every kind shares

- **One theme.** The speeches read each carry one idea through: unity (2), "America at its best" (3), a unity agenda (4), "hard, not easy" and affordability (5), equity and a "decade of action" (6). Course guides say the same: one speech, one core idea, most often carried by about three main points (9, search summary). The planner picks one theme and keeps only what belongs to it.
- **Acknowledgments are short and come first.** The salutation is 1 to 3 percent of an address (2, 3, 4), a single greeting at its least (5: one word), at most about 5 percent where a mayor thanks partners (6). People are named by title ("Vice President Harris", "Chief Justice Roberts") and the list ends; it is not joined to the first idea. Guests are introduced inside the point they prove (4, 5), not in the greeting.
- **A point moves context → stand → ask.** The State of the Union (4), the State of the State (5) and the State of the City (6) build most points the same way: a figure or a story that sets the context (how many people, how long, how much), then the speaker's position, then the ask, which comes last ("Send it to my desk", "I look forward to working with"). This is Monroe's need → satisfaction → action (1). The owner's inflation example is exactly this shape.
- **Transitions are short.** Signposts ("First", "Second", "And finally"), hinges ("But", "Look", "That's why") and recaps (4, 6, 9). A transition is a chance to restate the theme (9, search summary). A lead-in that only announces the next clause is not one of these.

## What this sets in the outline rows

- Each kind's sections and their order are the rows above; `required` is the salutation and the close (every kind) and the theme (every kind with more than one point).
- The number of points is the theme's, at most three (9, rule of three); an inaugural's points are commitments with no rank opener.
- A context sentence comes from the record of the theme's subject over a span (how long, how much), once per point, and in an inaugural only as the inherited condition, never as the new President's record.
- The asks are said once each, to the level that can act, last in their point.
