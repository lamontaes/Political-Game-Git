- `tpp-screens-nov5.md`: how The Political Process lays out its budget, money, government, party, campaign and election screens, read from its install.

- `drive-and-sessions.md`: what he said about menus in Drive notes, past sessions and voice notes.
- `../audit-nov7/AUDIT.md`: all 69 player-facing screens graded against the approved look on Nov 7, with the 10 worst in order. Its screenshots stay on the CTO's Mac. Retake them from the running game when you need them.

Read them with `../MENU-LOOK.md` and `../RULINGS-nov5.md`. Where a record names `cto-notes/menus/...`, the approved part of that folder is this one (`menu-examples.html`, `img/`). The rest stays on the Mac. Where they disagree, the newest dated ruling in OWNER-DECISIONS wins.
