County law · Clark County · in force since January 1, 2025 · passed 5 to 2 on November 14, 2024
Property tax rate: 0.67% of home value
Every home in the county pays this share of its assessed value each year. The county keeps it for roads, the sheriff and the jail; the school district sets its own rate on top.
