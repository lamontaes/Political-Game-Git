## Fact packet (what happens, in substance)

- **Who asks:** the member who needs the vote (the bill's sponsor or the leader counting) asks one colleague, in person, before the roll.
- **Trigger:** a close count with a named line to clear (ten of fifteen; a veto-proof margin).
- **What the colleague weighs:** what they said before, who they answer to, what the asker can do for or to them next.
- **Answers:** agree, stall ("let me see the final text"), refuse, or relent after a favor is named.
- **The record:** the vote when it is called.
