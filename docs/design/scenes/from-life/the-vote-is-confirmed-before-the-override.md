## Fact packet (what happens, in substance)

- **Who asks:** a leader or the sponsor asks a member who has already said yes to say it again before the override.
- **Trigger:** a veto, or a promise made earlier in the term; the count has no spare votes.
- **What the member weighs:** the pressure the mayor or the other side can put on them since they promised.
- **Answers:** agree, withdraw, bargain (name what they now need), stall, or relent.
- **The record:** the roll, which may differ from the promise; the asker remembers who held.
