## Fact packet (what happens, in substance)

- **Who asks:** the person with the idea (an outside group, a member leaving, a member of the other chamber) asks a member to carry it.
- **Trigger:** a bill needs a member's name on it and the asker has none; or the player's own record of introducing bills.
- **What the member weighs:** their standing, what it costs them with the chair, whether it can pass.
- **Answers:** agree, decline, bargain (carry a narrower one), stall, or relent.
- **The record:** the bill's sponsor changes, or does not.
