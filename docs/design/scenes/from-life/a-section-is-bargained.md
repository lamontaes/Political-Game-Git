## Fact packet (what happens, in substance)

- **Who asks:** a member who needs the bill changed names one section.
- **Trigger:** an amendment in play; the bill's backers need the member's vote.
- **What the sponsor weighs:** losing the section against losing the vote.
- **Answers:** agree, refuse, bargain (trade a different section), stall, or relent.
- **The record:** the amendment text and the vote after it.
