# One electorate model (owner ruling, Oct 14, 2026)

**The model.** Everyone the world doesn't name votes through one engine: the counted residents (`src/core2/electorate.ts`). A counted voter has a record (age, traits, scheduled work, place) and a place in the town's lean order (`personLean`). On election day each decides from that record alone: whether to vote (duty, engagement, interest in the race against the cost of voting that day, the election laws in force) and whom to back (each candidate's party against their own lean, the candidate's public standing, how well known the candidate is in town, the incumbent side of their traits). Nothing is rolled. Voter groups (`voter-groups.ts`, `opening-voter-groups.ts`) and electorate blocks (`electorate-blocks.ts`) are deleted as decision paths and as stores.

**Reasons on every ballot.** Each counted resident's ballot is kept (the voting engine's `countedBallots` table, one row per contest kept by column, since a town holds thousands) with the same reasons as a named voter's: turnout reasons (duty, interest, cost), lean, the incumbent side of their traits, choice, and the field they saw; `countedBallotOf` returns it with each candidate's appeal and its parts, the engine's own reading of those numbers. Votes in a coarse place (below) are kept per place with each candidate's public reasons.

**Inputs that survive, and where each number comes from.**

- CPS November 2024 voting table 4c (`data/source/census-voting-registration`): state citizen turnout and citizen share by age band. Turnout is a check: the counted turnout in a presidential race is tested against it (tolerance `countedTurnoutTolerance`, TUNABLE). The state's all-ages citizen turnout also sizes a coarse place's eligible voters from its 2024 votes.
- The place's ACS 2020-2024 citizen share of adults (`data/place-citizenship-2024.json`), blended with the state's CPS share: counted votes are scaled by it, as they are by the felony-barred share.
- 2024 presidential vote by county (`data/county-presidential-2024.json`), the Clerk of the House's 2024 House and state results and the 2024 delegate races: each place's lean (its right share, which places its people on the lean order) and its size. Tested: with the opening's inputs, a state's count lands near its 2024 share (tolerance `stateShareTolerance`, TUNABLE).
- Candidate standing (`electorate-inputs.ts`): incumbency, experience, scandal, campaign spending, the officeholder's record, the national economy, the midterm penalty, the town's jobs and crime. Sized in research vote-share units and turned into appeal on the lean scale (on the uniform lean order a candidate's appeal moves a quarter of itself in vote share).
- Custom world politics settings (`worldSettings`): the lean shift moves the town's lean; the turnout setting scales the cost of voting.

**Campaigns reach counted residents as they do today.** Knocking fills in the counted household behind the door (it becomes full people with their own impressions); polls call the whole town, counted residents included, and record what each says from their own lean. Neither reads a group.

**Places outside the town vote on the same engine, coarser.** A race's wider electorate is a list of places: the state's counties (statewide offices, the presidency), the House district, the state legislative district's share of its state, or the rest of the town where its population runs past the counted residents. A place's eligible voters are its 2024 two-party votes over the state's CPS citizen turnout, the town's own voters set apart from its county or district. Each place votes as the town's counted voters would (`electorateSample`: every counted adult's turnout parts and rank in the lean order), re-placed on the place's own lean and weighted to its size. Its people stand on the lean order where these voters, turning out by their own readiness, would split as the place voted in 2024 (a calibration of the input: the 2024 vote is the only record of where they stand); everything that moves in play moves the vote from there: same turnout rule, same choice rule, same candidate standing. Nobody is filled in to do it. `world-races.ts` counts every seat the town doesn't vote in the same way, and a primary's wider electorate is its places' party identifiers.
