# One crime system

Part 0 of the crime plan (Account lead S3, Nov 15, 2026). It says what the one chooser is, what the one crimes table is, what takes the place of the monthly rate in `src/core2/modules/crime.ts`, and which stopgap rows each later part clears. It builds nothing.

Owner rulings it rests on: one engine per mechanic (AGENTS.md, Oct 27); no dice (a trait, money, the law and the place decide, a real rate only checks totals); one model for full people and counted groups (Oct 14); the player's only crime is corruption (the owner's ruling that retires `SG-GOV-justice-player-choices`).

## What exists

- **The chooser.** `scoreOffers` and `decisionFromScores` in `src/core2/choice.ts`. `decide` in `src/core2/modules/corruption.ts` wraps them for a yes or no with its reasons kept: the values the person weighed, their temperament, and a chosen answer beside what the engine would have said. Bribes (offer, take, tell, press, report) already go through it.
- **The crimes table.** `crimes` in the corruption module: one row per offense, with who did it, to whom or for what, the date, whether it was taken, the reasons, and who learned of it (a knowledge record `crime:<id>` per person, with its source). Justice files cases from it; the press and the director read it (`crime.victimized` is read by the director and the press).
- **A rate path.** `src/core2/modules/crime.ts` does something else: once a month, for each place, the national NCVS rate of each kind times the outcome web's factor says how many households are victimized, shared out by each household's exposure (income, tenure, household kind; age and income for violent crime) with the remainder carried. Nobody offends; the victim row has no offender. Counted households get a tally row (place, month, group). `public-safety.ts` then turns the reported ones into arrests at each offense's clearance rate as counted cases. That is the part this plan replaces.

## The one chooser

Every offense is a decision by someone through `decide`: the same function, the same kept reasons. An offense kind is a data row (what it asks of the offender's money, traits and circumstances, how a victim is chosen, how much of it is reported, its severity), never a code path per kind. A bribe is one row of that table; theft, burglary, assault and the rest are the other rows. The player has no choice of offense but corruption.

## The one crimes table

The corruption module's `crimes` table is the table: renamed and moved to a crime module that corruption, justice, public safety, news and the director read. Each row is an offense with `kind`, `offenderId` (a person or a counted group with its count: one model, two tiers), the victim (a person, household or counted group), `date`, `reported`, the offender's kept reasons, and who learned of it. The month rows the web, the voters and the views read (violent and property index, per household, reported per resident) become views over this table, computed on the day something is written, never a second record and never a daily scan.

## What takes the place of the rate path

- **Offenders decide.** On the day something changes (a person's money, a victim's exposure, a lead) the one chooser decides whether a person offends, from their traits, money and circumstances. The victim is chosen by the exposure functions that already exist (`householdExposure`, `personExposure` become the victim rule, not a rank over every household each month).
- **The national rate is a check.** The NCVS rate times the web's factor becomes the test that totals over a place's year land inside a spread of the real rate; it never decides one actor's outcome.
- **Arrests follow the offenses.** Police clear reported offenses one by one from the record (an officer, a lead, the victim's report), at the lines the town funds; the FBI clearance rates check the totals. A named offender's case is filed from their own act; a counted offense files a counted case the same way as today, from the same row.
- **Deleted in the same change that replaces them** (replace, never layer): the monthly rate producer in `crime.ts` (`runPlace`, `worldRatePer1000`, `typeFactor`, the owed-carry rows), the arrests-from-rates path in `public-safety.ts`, and every marker for the rows below.

## Rules for every part

- No new stopgap; a cut corner is a registered row, never a typed number. A chosen number is an ESTIMATED row with what raises and lowers it and a spread; real rates (NCVS, FBI clearance, BJS) are checks on totals.
- Proof is a played year in a drawn place and seed the builder did not choose (`played-world-proof`): one offense followed from the offender's decision (its kept reasons) to the victim's record, the report, the arrest, the case and the sentence, by record id, with the national rate checked at the place's total.
- Speed: nothing runs for every household every month; an offense is decided when a cause changes (a person's money or circumstance, a lead). Year 1 at three named places with and without each part, twice each side, before it folds.
- Every effect can be triggered from dev mode and traced there (owner, Nov 14).
- Each part is its own PR on the Train, built on the part before it once that part has folded.
