Status legend

- **RWC** = RESEARCHED-WITH-CITATION: the code comment names a study, statute or dataset that gives the number.
- **STAND-IN** = marked (PLACEHOLDER / SET BY HAND / GAME ASSUMPTION / UNRESEARCHED / BLANKET / ESTIMATED FROM AVERAGE / HARDWIRED) and says so.
- **UNMARKED** = a literal number with no source and no marker.
- **RWC-STRETCHED** = a real citation exists but the number applied is not what the study measured (one state's fiscal note applied nationally, a mean of ten states, an eyeballed "middle of what was found").

## 4. The 15 numbers that matter most

Ranked by how many outcomes they move and how far from bedrock they sit.

1. **cost-of-living.ts:53 `150_000` ($1,500/mo per adult)** — every household's solvency, shortfall tags, and the "can I afford it" tests all read this one national stand-in. Replace with CEX-by-region + the town's own rent.
2. **outcome-web/index.ts:638-644 `DEFAULT_SPREAD`** — every researched link without a range is jittered ±25% (±100% if contested) by an unjustified constant; touches all 97 sized links.
3. **links.json: 82 `size: null` links** — 37% of the causal web is named, cited and inert; in play those causes do nothing, which is an unmeasured "about zero".
4. **local-economy.ts:137-192 fixed `monthlyRevenueMinor` per business kind** — seeds every town's business books, owner wealth, and tax base with invented revenue.
5. **office-salary.ts:38 `6_000_000`** — the pay of every mayor, judge and civil servant outside the published table; drives player income and town payroll.
6. **town-pay.ts:232 `±15` percentile draw** — sets each worker's wage level inside the OEWS distribution by lottery rather than by anything in their record.
7. **public-budgets/rules.ts:62 `OPENING_DRAW_SD 0.05`** — every estimated budget line in every county/city opens on a log-normal draw with a spread nobody justified.
8. **macro-economy/policy.ts:295-338 (whole `MACRO_ERA_POLICY`)** — the business cycle, unemployment and inflation everyone lives under are "calibrated to the broad U.S. record rather than fitted".
9. **town-rent.ts:280 `rentRise 0.051` + housing-market.ts:80 `-0.00146/mo`** — the two housing-law effects are fixed uplifts copied from single studies (San Francisco; the middle of Auckland/Terner/São Paulo) rather than produced by the town's stock.
10. **migration/review.ts:106-112 `0.04 / 0.04`** — who leaves and arrives, hence the whole roster's turnover, is a blanket 4%; real ACS cross-county mobility is roughly half that.
11. **cannabis-sales-tax.ts:13 `40.7`** — a ten-state mean becomes every state's revenue level; violates both bedrock (mean as level) and golden rule (a research range used as the number).
12. **rules.ts:132-260 `TAX_QUESTION_EFFECTS` / `SPENDING_QUESTION_EFFECTS`** — one state's fiscal note (Oklahoma, California, Utah, Virginia) is every state's revenue/cost change; also a rule-6 problem in disguise.
13. **campaigns.ts:1318 `integer(85_000, 175_001)`, :457 `integer(0,301)`, :1652 `integer(-350,351)`** — the player's money raised, starting support and election-night result are unmarked lotteries.
14. **crisis/disaster.ts:67-110 damage/injury/death tables** — every disaster's human toll is a "first-playable balancing value" with no HAZUS or NOAA behind it.
15. **person-identity.ts:208 `48/48/4`** — an unmarked demographic split that is off by ~2.5× on the non-binary share and feeds every generated person.
