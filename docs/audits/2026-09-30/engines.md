### 0.2 DUPLICATE #1 — the 14-function apply chain exists twice

`simulation/world.ts:1425-1461` and `simulation/time-work.ts:1994-2030` (`setCurrentMoment`) nest the same 14 functions in the same order:

```
applyJudicialReview( applySpeechRetelling( applyCrisisRepairFunding( applyEnactedCourtSizes(
 applyCrisisOfficeContinuity( applyCongressLawmaking( applyFederalReform( applyArticleV(
  applyConstitutionalReform( applyPresidentialTurnover( applyGovernorTurnover( applyCongressTurnover(
   applyStateLegislatureTurnover( applyNationalTermTransitions(…)))))))))))))
```

Differences: `world.ts` passes `world.currentDate` once per call; `time-work.ts` passes `crossedFrom` per crossed midnight and **also runs the chain at every work-completion, work-progress, activity-completion and at the final target** (`time-work.ts:1609-1615`, `:1968-1980`). When the date does not change, the ten `before`-guarded functions no-op but four unguarded ones (`applyNationalTermTransitions`, `applyCrisisOfficeContinuity`, `applyEnactedCourtSizes`, `applyCrisisRepairFunding`) plus `swearInWinners` inside `applyPresidentialTurnover` (`nationwide-world/presidential-turnover.ts:1189`) run on every sub-day step. A new apply step must be added in two files; nothing enforces parity.

**Survivor:** one exported `applyDateBoundary(before, world)` in `time-work.ts` (the minute clock is the player's clock); `advanceWorld` becomes `advanceWorldMinutes(world, days*1440, …)` after `people-continuation.ts:871` and `demo.ts:663` are switched. Remove the `world.ts` copy last.
