# Beliefs

Beliefs are sparse, issue-specific, historical, and distinct from speech and behavior.

## Rules

- Absence of a belief record means unformed, unknown, or not yet relevant; it does not automatically mean neutral.
- Issue definitions use stable, expandable IDs rather than a fixed ideological checklist.
- Broad principles may influence reasoning but cannot force uniform positions across every issue.
- Private belief, public position, campaign promise, and actual behavior are separate records.
- Beliefs may evolve, contradict earlier beliefs, or remain uncertain. Changes retain historical context.
- Expertise can provide knowledge, questions, options, and arguments; it does not identify an objectively correct political answer.
- Characters act using their information and perceptions, not omniscient engine truth.
- Any internal numerical representation is hidden implementation detail, not a normal player-facing ideology meter.

The first build does not implement beliefs. Its person model and stable history references must permit later beliefs without replacing character identity or rewriting history.
