# Judicial Gameplay Workflow Kernels

Status: **Draft, headless, additive, and unmerged.** This surface is ready for
independent gameplay audit, not player-facing acceptance.

## Purpose and authority boundary

This compiler turns the largest coherent subset of the sixty researched 92G
judicial gameplay seeds into deterministic plans for mechanics already accepted
on `main`. It is an adapter over existing organizations, work relationships,
world events, evidence artifacts, scheduled activities, work items, and
relationship interactions. It is not a court, case, motion, ruling, remedy,
recusal, discipline, selection, campaign, or tenure engine.

The research inputs are:

- [`92G_JUDICIAL_GAMEPLAY_WORKFLOW_COMPLETION.md`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z9YkjtS3nWlF0YL9asCHmhlgIb4HdmkA/view)
- [`92G_JUDICIAL_GAMEPLAY_SEED_BANK.md`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1F0aE8h_TS7q2ELJM3Dz9Nry4RXrvLDj5/view)

Those documents provide source scenarios and workflow analysis. They do not
provide runtime legal authority. Official ethics material used to check the
boundary includes the [U.S. Courts ethics policies](https://www.uscourts.gov/administration-policies/judiciary-policies/ethics-policies),
the [Code of Conduct for United States Judges](https://www.uscourts.gov/administration-policies/judiciary-policies/ethics-policies/code-conduct-united-states-judges),
and the [Kentucky Judicial Ethics Committee](https://www.kycourts.gov/Courts/Judicial-Ethics/Pages/default.aspx).
The compiler does not convert that descriptive material into jurisdiction rules.

The 92L state-elective-office-identity source domain remains separately owned
and unchanged. A court organization or judicial role supplied by a caller is an
explicit binding for this workflow only; no filename, title, organization name,
source artifact, or research label can grant authority.

## Compiled contract

Each compiled kernel produces an immutable plan containing, in order:

1. one ordinary historical intake event;
2. one objective evidence artifact linked to that event;
3. one scheduled conference or response activity;
4. one player-required office work item focused on the activity; and
5. one relationship interaction linked to the event.

Application delegates to the existing canonical writers. It requires the exact
world and moment used for compilation, an existing jurisdiction, an existing
court organization, the controlled person in the principal role, active
canonical work relationships for every court-insider role, and a canonical
shared household where a spouse role is required. Canonical schedule conflict
checks remain authoritative.

Plans preserve authored decision-point keys but do not choose for the player.
They create no decision trace, legal finding, order, disposition, discipline,
case reassignment, public reaction, capacity effect, financial effect, ideology
value, moral value, ruling prediction, or probability. Each unsupported
downstream result is declared as an omission on the plan.

## Exact coverage ledger

`C` means **compiled on current mechanics**. `G1` means a canonical
case/motion/proceeding/disposition family plus effective jurisdiction rule is
required. `G2` adds recusal and case-assignment procedure. `G3` means judicial
selection/tenure and appointment, confirmation, election, campaign, or
leadership authority is required. `G4` means sourced senior-status, pension,
vacancy, and post-judicial career consequences are required.

## Integrity and acceptance

The bank self-check proves sequential `SEED-01` through `SEED-60` identity,
unique rows and definitions, exact status/definition agreement, declared
blockers on every gated row, and valid step references. Focused tests apply all
ten definitions through the canonical writers, verify exact serialization
round trips and no autonomous decision traces, exercise fail-closed role and
household checks, preserve schedule conflict behavior, and measure changed
paths against accepted `main` to protect 92L and shared engine ownership.

This wave deliberately leaves the architecture integrity audit disposition in
this additive system record: it adds no shared schema, rule registry, source
domain, UI, or persistence change, and it reopens no accepted Stage 6 semantics.
Promotion remains gated on independent judicial gameplay audit.
