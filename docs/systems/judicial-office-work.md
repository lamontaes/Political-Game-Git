# Supported judicial office work

JUD-WORK2 consumes the accepted judicial bank and compiler through existing
World records. It implements office practice, not adjudication. The shared
[delivery packet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BQTTAZlOLQBVKQpzVGPfqEH8iD5kuH08aB8nIfibamc/view)
section D authorizes this consumer. The
[92G workflow](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Z9YkjtS3nWlF0YL9asCHmhlgIb4HdmkA/view)
and [seed bank](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1F0aE8h_TS7q2ELJM3Dz9Nry4RXrvLDj5/view)
supply scenario premises; proposed downstream scores and legal consequences
are not implemented authority. The judicial-selection source tier remains
reported evidence with citations not retrieved, and has no runtime adapter here.

## Canonical behavior

`judicialOfficeContexts` resolves the controlled living principal's active dated
work relationship, exact occupation classification, court organization profile
and jurisdiction. A title alone cannot confer access. Insider roles require
active work at that organization; external roles require their actual occupation
and jurisdiction. Every response rebinds the exact original participant IDs.
Departure, changed control, ended marriage or lost co-residence refuses without
writing. Refusals return the original World, including after a due transition
would invalidate a role.

Explicit correspondence intake records an authored source event and adapts the
existing compiler's five steps: intake event, discoverable evidence, scheduled
review, work item and relationship interaction. It never creates a case or legal
finding. Reading and opening the panel are pure. Stable keys prevent repeated
intake within a work relationship.

A response performs the canonical 30-minute review, preserving earlier scheduled
commitments, then appends the chosen communication, claim, knowledge and
relationship contact. Active work completes through the existing work-state
history. The small feature-local completion writer refuses non-active work and
checks World integrity; it is not a second work store. A 20-minute principal-only
preparation activity and work item record the follow-up. Completing it creates
a private note and its evidence discovery. Requests do not infer NPC replies,
consent, attendance, personnel action or legal outcomes. Preparation does not
settle an inquiry. Both time intervals are authored design parameters.

The explicit Custom initializer uses the existing person, organization and dated
work writers. Its fictional workplace, role premises and work hours carry
`authored` provenance. It does not invent selection law, a legal term, salary
receipts, powers, marriage or household. The original generated player and life
history are retained. Existing serialization and BrowserSaveStore persist all
records without a new schema or save format.

## Presentation and integration

The feature panel and pure projection use canonical names, accessible evidence,
current activity state, work status and recorded response history. Optional person
links carry stable IDs. UI-core owns the root save callback, Work navigation and
creator. The [named handoff](../integration/jud-work2-ui-core.md) contains its exact
mechanical patch. Normal-player navigation remains awaiting integration.
`tests/judicial-office` is explicitly a developer proof, never a normal-route claim.

## Architecture and LEARN

The architecture audit preserves simulation purity, stable identity, append-only
history, dated role authority, knowledge boundaries and one canonical time/work
and persistence system. No World/barrel/source/generated-corpus change is needed.
Accepted Stage 6 semantics and existing court compiler definitions are unchanged.

Grounding review caught preparation text that assumed an unwritten staff reply.
The correction is encoded in principal-only preparation activities and private
note evidence, not a new prompt convention. Tests cover actual mutation and
reload, role/insider/spouse refusal, schedule contention and read purity. Browser
acceptance and full validation results are recorded in the delivery evidence;
automated proof does not constitute human visual or play acceptance.
