# Staged transit contract service

The ordinary supported route adds `transit-staged-service-v1` to the existing
`appropriations` family at v3. Existing configurations, order and saved lineages
are preserved. The player explicitly selects weekday or weekend added service
and an appropriation amount. The standing rural program and contract price
($100 per vehicle-service hour) are authored game content, not real Alaska
programs, balances, officials or empirical effectiveness claims.

The exact current adopted provisions and enacted outcome authorize the bounded
request. A proposal, ineffective or expired date, changed adopted term,
unestablished mandate, wrong institution, or operative repeal cannot authorize
new settlement. The clause takes effect 90 days after enactment and grants 365
days of availability. The latter duration is authored. Alaska's Constitution
provides a default delayed effective date and requires legal appropriation and
obligation authority; the shared F writer owns the acquired source validation.
See the [official constitution](https://ltgov.alaska.gov/information/alaskas-constitution/),
Article II section 18 and Article IX section 13. No universal state claim follows.

The sponsor may request and cancel; the Act assigns administration to the state
government. A sponsor request is not member-as-executive spending authority.
Two separate canonical due items represent two 14-day contract periods. Each
has a distinct alternative, baseline, operation, profile, estimate and eventual
realization. Its original adopted provision IDs and contract cost are frozen;
due settlement rereads current authority and invokes F's public payment writer.
There is one existing public account/resource-transfer substrate, not a T
wallet. Collected cash is necessary; money mentioned in legislation, an economic
metric or an estimate is not money received.

Service records contain physical paid added vehicle-service hours only. The
funding fraction is exact cash arithmetic; excluded response factors are
multiplicative identities, not assessments of capacity, compliance, uptake,
political entities or policy quality. No ridership, access, effectiveness,
opinion, ranking, score or election prediction is produced. The zero baseline
means zero _additional contract units for this unique period_, not no existing
transit service. Each scope contains a stable contract identity and period, so
concurrent appropriations cannot overwrite or sum another contract's completion.
The metric is a dated completion record evaluated at its settlement date. It does not use the
causal substrate's interval midpoint to backdate completed service.

Production saves admit only the exact shared physical contract metric and
completion mechanism definitions. Full-definition comparison rejects changed
period/curve fields and unrelated models. Newly created production catalogs
remain empty; definitions enter at the explicit service request. This admission
supplies no empirical service-response corpus or observed existing transit.

F settles a full requested period or refuses it. A paid first period followed
by cancellation of the second is partial program delivery; neither its payment
nor its service activation is removed or refunded. Repeated closed dispatch
cannot write a duplicate payment or realization. Private settlement knowledge
and explicit dated Civic Ledger publication are separate existing records.
Reading or opening the workspace cannot spend, realize, publish or advance time.

Integration owners: S owns the ordinary legislative assignment, recorded
appropriation sitting producer and exact docket
identity parser; F owns public-account receipts and payment; T owns contract
choices, request/cancellation, service realization and reports; A owns the
combined root navigation/render/callback and clock binding. Missing bindings
remain unfinished work. T neither seeds public cash nor relabels a revenue
measure as an appropriation.

## Funded route in one life (CIVIC SERVICE, 2026-09-14)

The Alaska revenue measure now has its own owner-accepted recorded sitting
(see [recorded sitting](legislative-recorded-sitting.md)), so one supplied
Alaska member can complete the whole loop with ordinary actions and the
ordinary clock: earned shop pay, the appropriation through its sitting, the tax
through its revenue sitting, both ninety-day effective dates, declared
occurrences, collection into the public account, the request, paid due
settlement and an optional dated report. The member seat is a labeled supplied
office scenario; ordinary candidacy into it belongs to NATIONWIDE
WORLD/ELECTION.

Settlement text is plain: `Delivered 1 vehicle-service hour of added contract
service for the period ending D, paid with $100.00 from the public account`, or
the period ending D `was not delivered, and nothing was paid` with F's reason.
Partial hours keep their exact decimal. The Transit surface shows each period's
meaning, the total paid and delivered, the public account's recorded cash, and
what is not modeled (ridership, travel times, access, approval). When the
account cannot cover the first period it says so before the request and links
to Taxes and public receipts; it never blocks the request.

`src/presentation/civic-funded-service.test.ts` proves the two-period paid route
across save/reopen, the refused unfunded second period with no payment or cash
change, a duplicate request, ended and Kentucky offices, and cancellation.
