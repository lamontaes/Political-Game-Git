**Specs:** M7-01 to M7-06.
**Built by:**

- OPUS-PLAY: store screenshots, the bug reporter, the shipped build.
- Every session: clears the stopgaps in its own area.
- Open work: the legal check.
- The CTO: capsule art, the store page and the FAQ draft.

**Dates (owner, Oct 11):** store page live by Friday, Oct 16. Early access two weeks later (target Oct 30), set once you've played the golden path end to end.

## What you'll get

- **Fast:** a town's year runs in under two minutes, and day to day feels instant. The world near you is simulated in full; everyone else is filled in when you click.
- **A loading screen worth watching:** up to two minutes, with the journal writing itself, a year counter with headlines, and the map filling in.
- **Small:** budgets for install size and save size, set so the game is nowhere near 8 GB and a save is nowhere near 1.6 GB. Minimum PC specs measured and listed.
- **Saves that last:** from early access on, every save loads and upgrades through one versioned path. No wipes.
- **Nothing fake:**
  - every registered stopgap is replaced, or turned into a sourced estimate;
  - estimate labels show only in developer mode, never to players;
  - nothing shows "unknown", and nothing is hard-coded;
  - the old core is deleted.
- **Nothing from the workroom:** no owner's decisions, dates or work notes ship in the game:
  - every "(owner, Oct 14)"-style note, ruling date, session name, PR number and work-order reference is removed from the game's code and data (about 240 files under `src` today);
  - code comments say what the code does, not who decided it or when;
  - the shipped build strips comments and carries no source maps, so nothing written for the team reaches a player's machine;
  - the release build refuses while any such note is found (a scan of `src`, the game's data and the built files).
- **Updates:** a Steam build every night, made in the cloud. Updates come through Steam. Proof and developer pages aren't in the shipped build.
- **Bug reports:** a "Report a bug" button, plus a prompt after a crash. Each sends its log only when the player presses send (owner, Oct 12).
- **Windows and Mac at launch** (owner, Oct 12). The Mac build is signed and notarized; the owner buys Apple's developer membership before launch.
- **Legal check:**
  - no real politicians anywhere;
  - historical quotes public-domain only;
  - real schools, places, banks and businesses cleared;
  - every data, photo and art license recorded;
  - Steam's AI-content disclosure filled in (the art is generated);
  - attribution that licenses require goes on a Credits screen under Options.
- **The Steam page:**
  - app 5376890 at $14.99;
  - your capsule art direction and tags;
  - real gameplay screenshots;
  - a short description that doesn't promise courts (they come after early access);
  - the content survey;
  - an early-access FAQ (how long, what comes next, the price after) drafted for you to approve;
  - a trailer with no AI-generated music.
