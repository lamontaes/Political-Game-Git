# M5-careers: state legislator, governor and mayor, and playing on as someone else

**Specs:** M5-01 to M5-04.
**Built by:** OPUS-GOV (careers and offices), with OPUS-PEOPLE (successors and family) and OPUS-PLAY (the desks and screens).
**Systems model:** _The Political Process_: you climb the ladder from school board to president, serve as legislator, governor or president, and retire when you choose. Our version uses the same engines at every level, with real seats, and your family carries on.

## What you'll do

**State legislator.**

- Pick a real numbered district and file at your state's filing office, the same way as piece 1.
- Campaign against the actual holder. If you win, you replace the right member at the start of the term.
- In the capitol: committees, amendments, floor votes, the governor's action, the effective date. You have two or three staff.
- Each week, you choose between being at the capitol or at home in the district.
- Your record follows you into your next election and any primary challenge.

**Governor, mayor or county executive.**

- The executive desk at full depth: set priorities with staff advice, go line by line if you want, appoint a cabinet within the office's real powers, sign or veto, and live with the results.
- A mayor's powers depend on the place's real form of government.

**Playing on as someone else.**

- When your character dies, you continue as a real, existing person connected to them. Family, a protégé or a close associate come first, and you can search for anyone eligible.
- They have their own life. They carry the family stories a child would know, weaker and secondhand. People who knew your old character treat them by your old favors and grudges.
- They inherit nothing else: no friendships, spouse, job or office.
- Death is permanent, but you can load an earlier save. You can't jump between people mid-life whenever you like.
- When you retire, you can also hand off to a connected person while your old character lives on in the world.

**Congress and president** are built at the same time on the same engines, and run in the world. You run for president on the one track every candidate runs on. Early access doesn't wait for it.

## What gets built

- The state legislator career on the M2 and M3 engines: a real seat, filing, campaign, the right member replaced, the session, staff, and the capital-or-home week.
- The governor, mayor and county executive desk at full depth, on the M3 executive engine, with each office's real powers.
- Succession:
  - choosing an existing person;
  - weaker secondhand family knowledge;
  - favors and grudges carried over;
  - nothing else inherited;
  - a look-back at the death;
  - handing off at retirement.
- Federal careers on the same engines, in the world.

## How you'll know it works

In a random state, you win a real numbered House or Assembly seat, pass a bill with colleagues, and later serve as governor, signing, vetoing, appointing and adopting a budget. When your character dies, you continue as their child, who knows the family stories, and people treat the child by your old character's favors and grudges.
