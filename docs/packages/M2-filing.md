# M2-filing: running for office (golden path step 3)

**Specs:** M2-02 and M2-03.
**Built by:** OPUS-GOV, with OPUS-PEOPLE (the people and their words) and OPUS-PLAY (the screens).
**Systems model:** _The Political Process_, where you pick a platform, file for an office and run. Our version adds the place, the people and the scene.

## What you'll do

1. You go to your place's filing office: the clerk, registrar or board of elections, whichever that place really uses.
2. **A scene plays.** Someone there asks what you're here for, and you answer with dialogue choices. They tell you what you can run for and what you need. You might be recognized, or run into a rival filing for the same seat.
3. **An overlay opens.** You pick your office, see the requirements and the deadline, and get the tutorial, which you can open again later.
4. **You say where you stand,** your platform, at the office, to a newspaper or in an interview. Voters you meet later compare it with their own views.
5. **If the office needs signatures,** you gather them like canvassing. Sometimes you're on a porch; otherwise time skips and you see who signed.
6. You file once, and you're on the ballot. If you can't run, they tell you why.

## What gets built

- Each place's real election dates and filing requirements, from data: age, residency, fee or signatures.
- Which office takes filings in each place.
- The filing scene, the overlay with its tutorial, and the platform statement.
- Signature gathering that uses the same time-and-people mechanism as canvassing.
- No campaign-finance paperwork.

## How you'll know it works

In a random place you walk into the right office, talk to someone, pick your office, state your platform, gather signatures if needed, file, and end up on the ballot.
