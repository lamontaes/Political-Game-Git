# Bank 5 mapping: the 89 situation types to scene patterns

Source: `src/director/data/situation-types.json` on ref b7440729 (89 types) against `docs/story/scene-patterns.json` on main (151 patterns).

**Rule used.** A type maps to a pattern only when the pattern's `moment` is the same human moment (same) or the nearest existing moment with a different cast or stage (close). A pattern that merely lists `kind:<key>` in `unlocks` is not a build source; those ids are in the notes column. `extends` is the strongest hint. A pattern id is never a promise that the scene's lines are written from it: close means the pattern informs the scene, not that it replaces research.

**Counts.** Same moment: 9. Close: 33. None: 47. Of the 47 with no existing pattern, 19 are now covered by a bank 5 pattern, 15 by a bank 6 pattern, and 13 are still uncovered (the office ones, for Story's helpers on the other account).
