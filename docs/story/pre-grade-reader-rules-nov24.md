## The screen rule, as he reads it

Naming the occasion is fine: "the evening of" a day everyone knows. Opening a scene on a clock time or a date fails. Say where in the activity you are instead. A line is read whole.

## Five rules from his page-1 grading

1. A parent talking politics with their kid uses everyday words and a feeling, never the policy's name.
2. A reason is one a person would say out loud in that role, not a clause from a position paper.
3. A pronoun that could mean either of two people fails.
4. A line must mean something literal.
5. A speech never turns to a new subject without a bridge.
