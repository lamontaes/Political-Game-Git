### The next day, the petition

**1.11** narration: Now the signatures: 13,319 of them, from voters here, by February 3, 2026. Have you run a campaign before, or should I walk you through it?

**1.13**   after "Yes, I know how it works. I'll handle the U.S. Senate myself." (agree), the clerk: Good. Go over each name before February 3, 2026. I only take the ones that hold up.

**1.14**   after "Not yet. Where do I even start with the U.S. Senate?" (ask), the clerk: Doors first: signatures come one neighbor at a time. Then you'll want money, and a way to reach the voters who don't answer for the U.S. Senate.

**1.15**   after "Who decided the U.S. Senate takes that many?" (confront), the clerk: That's the law's number, not mine, and February 3, 2026 is the law's day too.
