## `note`

One kind of scene, several arcs (owner, Nov 8, 9:50 voice note: 'they've gotta have like a variance. So even though there's only 5 in corruption scenes right now, you could get, I'm just making a number up, 30 different scenes within there'). A family is a purpose in purpose-arcs.json; its variants are the arcs it can play. src/director/arc-variant.ts scores each variant from the records, as an address's kind is scored (address-kinds.json): a variant's score is its base plus each term's weight times a read between 0 and 1 (`low` reads one minus the read), never below nothing, times one plus the asker's pull toward it (the mean of the personality facets listed). The highest score plays and a tie goes to the name first in the alphabet; no roll, no threshold. A read looks at the records in one of six ways: a number kept on a module's row that the cause record names (`record`), a number the cause record itself carries (`fact`), how close the two people are (`closeness`), how many earlier records of some kinds name both people, optionally only those whose facts match (`history`), the asker's money pressure (`strain`), or where the scene is set (`setting`). A variant holds the arc (state, replies, follow, again, endings: moves only, no sentence) and the purpose id its bank pieces are written under; the family's own pieces serve where it has none of its own. Ending ids the engines read downstream (took-it, put-off, left-there, said-you-would-report) keep their meaning in every variant; a variant's other endings are refusals to the engine. Every weight is TUNABLE and registered as SG-STORY-arc-variant-weights.
