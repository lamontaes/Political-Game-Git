# The separate core chooses acts and limits routine history

The prototype evaluates needs, goals, drives, traits, and emotion, then chooses available acts with shared consequences. The player follows those same rules. The focused checks comprise 142 tests across eight files. The annual warm-up missed the two-minute budget before completing a year. The playable game does not import this folder.

Before: the matched old-core busy day measured 2.14 simulated days per minute. Weekly retirement removed 134,000 outsider records, and that run measured 1.08 days per minute. These receipts do not establish annual speed.

After: the prototype has mutable ID tables and indexes, actor-specific knowledge, generated households and jobs, summarized past facts, real public institutions, tier calendars, and the shared life loop. Open assumptions remain registered and block prototype release.

## What is measured

The matched first-day median improved from 11.40 to 15.00 simulated days per minute, including initialization. All five before and after state hashes match. The incomplete annual warm-up completed 150 days in 314.986 seconds, or 28.57 days per minute across finished chunks. That is not a completed year or an annual median. The [cache receipt](receipts/prototype-day-cache.json) preserves the actual samples and hashes.

## What is checked

The [life loop](life.ts#L281) advances real calendar days. Its [chooser](life.ts#L152) uses existing trait data and records need, goal, and drive contributions. The current cache gate passed 104 state, emotion, calendar, and life checks, plus 13 tripwire checks. Earlier unchanged civic input, population, and deep-past checks complete the eight-file total.

The [state API](state.ts#L302) validates mutations and knowledge access. Payments conserve money. Memberships record requests; organizing records a plan; approaching recorded staff opens a callback. These outcomes do not establish permission, admission, or a passed law. The [civic importer](civic-inputs.ts#L107) uses actual directory identities and recorded workers.

The current API and schema are versioned. Content registries and module operations are open to data extensions. A checked extension adds a need, goal, trait, and act through data using the existing module. Routine outsiders retain results and short reasons; full traces remain for the player, supplied focus people, or observer mode.

## What still blocks acceptance

One discretionary act per activation is not a complete daily schedule. Household consumption, hiring, employer revenue, births, deaths, elections, and law effects are missing. The event-chain check supplies its initial event; a generated-world event producer remains pending. Missing January 2021 laws and offices, inherited authored job choices, and incomplete deep histories have registered gaps.

Guessed parameters are tunable. Unsupported citations were removed. The new CTO instruction requires an applicable observable or explicit P9 replay outcome for each tunable; that work remains in progress. No coefficient is calibrated. PANAS/PSS scores and ATUS activity durations have different units from these coefficients and need an output bridge.

There are 38 open stopgaps. The [release gate](stopgaps.ts#L39) refuses them. The full year, self-started annual act totals, generated causal chain, mood and stress receipt, five-year pre-run, and P9 life receipt remain in progress. The measured opening has 10,002 people and only 64 jobs, so it cannot establish workforce realism.

## Method

All execution stays in the cloud. The [old-core baseline receipt](receipts/old-core-baseline.json) preserves the measured warm samples and original receipt hashes. The prototype day used one frozen opening for La Homa, Texas, and Hidalgo County, with the full county updated daily and three warm timed runs. The incomplete year has no median. No new old-core world run was made.
