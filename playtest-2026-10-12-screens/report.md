**Run:** the /play screen on `opus-play` (head d3bc6cf8), Chromium at 1920x1080 (screenshots resized to 1400 wide).
Town: **Meridian, Idaho**, seed `play0712b`, Place field empty. Character: Emily Howard, age 18, Monday Oct 12, 2026.
(A first pass with a random seed gave Bear Creek, California, and showed the same things. Its opening started with the President shot.)
`npm run bench` was refused by the storage guard ("needs 1.0 GiB, only 0 B usable"), so I started the bench with `node --max-old-space-size=8192 --import tsx src/bench/server.ts`; it answered in under 2 minutes.
41 screenshots (01–41 plus 99, no #32). The text of each sheet is in `text/`.

## Screens

1. **Loading (01–10).** New life to opening took **51 s** (45 s in the first pass). The screen is the title card with a gold progress bar and no words. The status line (`#front-status`) stays empty the whole time, so there are no step texts to report. The seed `playtest-0712` never finished loading: I waited 9+ minutes (about 100 screenshots) before stopping it, while other seeds loaded in 43–51 s. Worth a bug: a hang with no error.
2. **Opening (11–17).** Seven shots, about 4.5 s each, 31 s total: U.S. Senators, U.S. Representative, Governor, State Senator and Representative, Mayor, then the player's father, then the player at home. The President shot was missed this time; it was there in the first pass. I watched the figure count every 0.4 s and saw no person pop in late. Each shot had all its people on its first frame.
3. **Home (18) and Journal (19).** Home is a kitchen with the player and two family members. Journal "My life" has one entry: school. It lists only the two schools, with no births, family, or anything else before 18. In the first pass it had three entries, one of them the duplicated line "My daughter was born. My daughter was born." Nothing is scrollable; it is short. Missing: the childhood history the owner wants to see.
4. **Politics (20).** A sheet with buttons (Election night (greyed), What changed, Budgets, Map, Secretary of State, County clerk). Below them it lists only seats that have a holder, with the holder's name and a Play button (6 council members, Mayor, 3 County Commissioners). No party, term start, term end or career is shown. ## What's missing or wrong, in short
- No law panel anywhere; no way to see what law you live under.
- Loading and bill drafting use raw ids (`series`, `us-policy-positions:...`), and the loading bar has no text.
- Politics shows no term/career/past-elections; no named list of offices you can file for.
- What changed stays empty after the bill is enacted; the vote shows no reasons.
- Journal shows only schools; Jobs and study is blank; Money is only cash.
- One seed (`playtest-0712`) hung the new-life call for 9+ minutes with no error.
